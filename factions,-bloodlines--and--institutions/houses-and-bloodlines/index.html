Houses and Bloodlines

Nov 17, 20262 min read

The old blood and the great houses: who holds the land, the seats and the inheritance.

At the Kessen horse fair three heralds in three sets of colors once reached the same plank bridge in the same moment, each with his house’s precedence in his mouth, and by the time the matter had been settled with staves the plank was in the river and a Yan stud-master was renting out places on the bank. Everything on this shelf is that argument at a larger size: who goes first, who says so, and who is selling seats.

  • The Archaic Bloodlines
  • The Houses of the Inner World every house of the northern shield and what belongs to it alone.
  • The Banner Houses of Kharven Six houses beneath the Ashen Crown, each holding territory, troops, and the conditional loyalty that is the north’s only currency.
  • The Southern Warlords Seven houses of the Inner World’s south, below the Moto border and beyond any crown: Yukari, Mahuo, Lambert of the Holy Sea, Yan, Ryu, Corwen and Marrick.
  • The Eight Great Houses of Kushara The Sovereign House and the Seven Pillars beneath the Kusharan Throne. Old World continental order.
  • The Ten Great Houses of Drakyssia Vaer’Kharath, “The Ten Wings Beneath the Throne.” Outer World imperial order.
