Raizan Tenrai Moto — Abilities in full

Raizan Tenrai Moto — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202625 min read

Card: Raizan Tenrai Moto · The Doom Breaker

THE LAST THREAD (終の糸 · Tsui no Ito) · Signature art

THE LAST THREAD is basically unnoticeability: the power to find the gap where nobody is looking, and be in it. He only moves when you are not looking, and he knows the morning you die. The art is named for the needle, and the north uses the one name for both. His Traits, his techniques, his Releases and his Domain are all that one act done at a different size.

Capabilities

Everything has a gap. A man’s eyes have gaps, and BETWEEN BLINKS crosses a room in them. A room has a floor of small noise, and HUSH keeps him under it. A man’s next hours have a gap, one narrow moment when every way he might live thins at once, and THE ONE STRAND finds it. A skull has a gap a finger wide where it meets the neck, and ONE NEEDLE goes up through it. A step has a gap, the place the foot was never going to reach, and the Kingstep stands there. The First Release closes every gap he might have missed, and the Second makes a field forty yards across in which nothing unlikely is allowed to happen at all.

The art runs on three lenses and an eye. Tenebra, the Hidden Shadow, is the idea of going unseen, and its operation is separation: the soul is parted from the world’s notice and kept quieter than everything around it, so his Shell drinks the light, sound and Essence that reach it and turns the little that escapes away from the watcher. The Measurewrights call it the noise floor, and say a thing cannot be found when what it sends back falls below the background of whatever is looking; they are right about how quiet he is, and the old question of what he is hiding from was never theirs. Oneirion, the Dream, is dream as a place: every way a man could go is laid out in the space of what can be thought, where no soul has an address in a room, and the Tenrai doom-sight is read on that map, which the scholars call a configuration space. Ignivale, the Spark, is the smallest beginning that pays for itself, and it closes the gap between deciding and doing until the hand has left before the shoulder has told anyone. Over all three sits the Tengan, which has no lens and needs none: it times the eyes, ranks the futures and shows the foot where to go.

It all rides one blow. He does not trade: there is no second movement in him, and he never trained one. The minute finds the moment, the eyes’ gap carries him to the nape, the breath stops, and the needle goes in with the Spark behind it, so that the read, the crossing and the thrust arrive as one thing and the wound is all a physician will find. When the blow cannot be had he does not strike at all, and what is left is the feet: the Kingstep keeps him where the answer is not, and the Forcing Stone makes a standing man move. About twenty-four parts in twenty-five of what he spends reach the world, which is his yield. The share that falls short is paid in his Core as hollowing, the Spirit’s own coin, and whatever of it would show on another fighter as glare and noise is swallowed by the Tenebra coat with everything else, and it stays swallowed until he lets the coat go.

Applications

  • Reaching a guarded man unseen and killing him with one thrust
  • Leaving a wound the physicians will miss
  • Leading a stronger fighter by the nose without once being struck
  • Working in bedchambers, rafters and ruins, the dark, quiet, Aether-thin ground the art is made for

Techniques

  • BETWEEN BLINKS - crossing a room in the gaps when eyes are blind
  • THE ONE STRAND - finding the one moment a man can be killed
  • TENGAN - the Tenrai eye the whole art runs on
  • HUSH - staying quieter than the room he stands in
  • ONE NEEDLE - the single thrust at the nape
  • FORCING STONE - a flicked go stone that must be answered
  • KINGSTEP OF THE WINNING THREAD - the foot set where the other man was about to step
  • RUINOUS COINFALL - one small chance tipped at the moment it matters
  • KAMIKAKUSHI - the First Release, the hush laid over every sense in the field
  • HITSUZEN - the Second Release and the Domain, forty yards where the likeliest thing happens

Associations

  • Tenebra, the Hidden Shadow, the idea of going unseen
  • Oneirion, the Dream, the map the doom-sight is read on
  • Ignivale, the Spark, which puts the hand in motion before the shoulder
  • The Tengan, the Tenrai blood’s eye, over all three
  • The Last Thread, the needle the art is named for
  • His aura, a black-violet so faint that it reads as the shadow of something else, which takes Oneirion’s lavender at its edges while he reads

Limitations

  • He has not slept a full night since he was twenty, and every doom he breaks brings his own last morning a year nearer
  • There is one blow in it and no second movement; when the blow cannot be had he does not strike at all
  • A packed hall or another fighter’s Domain starves it
  • The charge is small and always there for anyone who knows it: a whole room blinks at once before he crosses, he watches for a full minute before he breaks a doom, his breath stops for three heartbeats before the needle, and small things fall when the Coinfall is set
  • The shadows near him deepen and the lamps seem to burn a little lower
  • Against a higher Stage it thins from the top down: a doom that a stronger power holds will not open for him, and the Domain serves the likelier man, so against a fighter above him he keeps it shut
  • HUSH, the Kingstep and the ground still work there, and they can let a man of seventy-seven lead a fighter two Stages over him by the nose
  • It is beaten by many eyes or none, a sense that does not blink, a stiff collar at the nape, standing still, and owning nothing loose; deny him the minute and he will not work at all
  • Tenebra fails where its idea does, since a soul hides only from what it has thought of, which the Measurewrights call aspect dependence: the coat is shaped against the angles he expects, and from the one he did not plan for he is brighter than anyone in the room; held too long it saturates, and everything it swallowed comes out at once
  • Oneirion fails by projection error, a read that was true on the map and lands nowhere in the room, which is what a held doom or a man in the middle of a real choice does to him
  • Ignivale fails by preignition, the hand that fires on a feeling before he has chosen, and it is the reason the needle stays in the sleeve until the breath has stopped
  • The Trait risks he carries: he does not sleep, which is Resonance Fatigue; he will not work blind even when it would save him, which is Rigidity; and breaking a doom without watching first is his Shear Break condition

Traits

BETWEEN BLINKS (瞬き · Mabataki) · Primary Trait

BETWEEN BLINKS is basically blind spot movement: the power to cross a room only in the moments when nobody’s eyes can see. It is the art used on eyes.

Capabilities

The eye is blind while it moves. Every time a man’s gaze jumps from one point to the next, a dozen times a breath, his brain stops taking in what he sees for that tenth of a breath and paints over the gap with what was there before, and every time he blinks the world goes dark for longer, and he never notices either. Scholars call the blind jump saccadic masking: the eye cannot see while it sweeps, so the mind cuts the smear out and fills the gap with the picture from before.

Raizan moves only in those gaps. The Tengan times every pair of eyes in a room, their jumps and their blinks, and his body crosses the floor in the spaces where nobody is looking, so that he is seen standing by the door and then seen standing by the bed and never seen walking between. A one-eyed man has more gaps on his dead side, and paints over all of them. The same reading works on bodies as on eyes: in a running crowd he walks through the gaps between people.

Applications

  • Crossing a watched room without being seen walking
  • Coming up on a one-eyed man from his dead side
  • Walking through a running crowd so that the crowd is always between him and whoever hunts him and never once in his own way

Associations

  • Tenebra is its lens, under the Tengan’s timing
  • The Spirit Path does the reading, with a studied Body’s feet

Limitations

  • Its limit is the number of eyes: one man’s gaps are many, three men’s gaps overlap less, and in a crowded hall the moments when every eye is blind at once are rare, short and far apart
  • It does nothing against a sense that does not blink: a beastkin’s nose, a hound’s ears, a blind fighter’s hearing
  • A fighter who reads heat can find him in a room hotter than his body, where an old man’s body is the one shape colder than the air; in the cold among a crowd of warm townsfolk the same sense loses him
  • It is constant and nearly costless, about half a percent of his reserve a minute while he is crossing ground
  • For anyone who knows it, a whole room blinking at once is the warning
  • It is beaten by many eyes or none: surround the target with watchers who look in different directions, or put the guard out of the light and let him listen

THE ONE STRAND (運命割り · Unmei-wari) · Secondary Trait

THE ONE STRAND is basically death foresight: the power to find the single moment when a man can be killed, and strike into it. The house and the Hall still call it Doom-Breaking, and it came of the first doom he broke.

Capabilities

The Tenrai read a man’s doom: the knot of all the ways his next hours could go, drawn tight round the likeliest. Raizan reads it and breaks it. He watches a target for a full minute without blinking himself, and in the knot he finds the one strand where the man dies, and the single moment when every other strand thins at once, when the guard’s eyes are on the door, the mail has ridden up, the dog has gone out, the luck has run.

That moment is real and very narrow: every way the man might live, laid out on Oneirion’s map in the space of what can be thought, thins at once to one strand for a breath. The Measurewrights liken it to the still point between two waves that cancel, crest meeting trough so that the water does not move, and call it destructive interference; it is a good picture, and they cannot say why a man’s likelihoods should behave like water. He strikes into it, and the knot collapses onto that strand. He has broken a great many. He can see the morning now, quite clearly.

The read is made on Oneirion’s map through the Core and pulls nothing down from Fate. What it spends is his own share, the way a vow does, which is why the price is taken out of his own mornings and why it is so much cheaper in reserve than a true pull. It works by putting an ordinary blow into a moment that was already there, so it moves nothing that had no way of happening.

Applications

  • Killing a man the Tengan said could not die that year
  • Choosing the moment for ONE NEEDLE
  • Reading how a bound man will answer, which is what FORCING STONE is thrown on

Associations

  • Oneirion is its lens
  • The Spirit Path makes the read, with the walked Fate Path’s small share behind it
  • Dominion Fate, which his Fate Path opened, is its gate
  • A cigarette often burns down for a clock while he watches; one lasts a little over a minute

Limitations

  • It needs the minute of watching, close enough to read, and he will not work without it
  • It cannot find the death-strand in a man whose futures are truly open, a man in the middle of a real choice
  • It cannot read a doom that a stronger fate-sight is already holding; a Kōkan Kamigan ranks above a Tenrai Tengan, and under that eye his reading blurs
  • It costs about eight percent of his reserve
  • It also costs something he cannot get back: every doom he breaks, the Tengan shows him his own last morning a little closer, as if a year of his own futures went into the gap with the other man’s
  • The tell is the watching itself: a full minute on one man, unblinking, the spokes of the Tengan turning in his irises
  • It is beaten by being unpredictable, by standing beside someone whose eye is stronger, or by never giving him the minute

TENGAN (Eye of Fate) · Inherited Trait

TENGAN is basically probability perception: the power to see which way things are likely to go. It is the Tenrai line’s sight, the Tenrai branch of the Moto heart-eye, and the eye the whole art runs on.

Capabilities

It shows probability pressure, convergence, and the near-future paths of bodies and blades as lines of light that lean the way things are likely to go. In him it is fifty years sharper than in any Tenrai alive. It has no lens. It spends his own share and pulls nothing down.

Applications

  • Timing every pair of eyes in a room for BETWEEN BLINKS
  • Ranking a man’s futures for THE ONE STRAND
  • Showing the foot where to go for the Kingstep
  • Telling him beforehand which man is the likelier, before he opens the Domain

Associations

  • The Tenrai blood
  • No lens

Limitations

  • It shows pressure and direction and never the hour
  • It shows likelihood: what will probably happen, and anything genuinely improbable surprises him like anyone else
  • It shows him what is likely to happen to himself as plainly as to anyone, and when there is nowhere better to step he pays the likely price
  • It costs nothing to keep open and it is always open, so he has not slept a full night since he was twenty
  • The spokes in his irises show it
  • It is beaten by a ward that returns or blurs a reading, Oblivara’s or a Tenebra veil of one’s own, or by the plain unlikely act

HUSH (静寂 · Shijima) · Passive Trait

HUSH is basically presence concealment: the power to be quieter than the room he stands in. It is the art used on a room’s noise.

Capabilities

Tenebra parts him from the room’s notice and keeps him quieter than it: his footfalls absorbed, his scent thin, his Essence quieter than the lamps. Dogs do not bark at him; horses do not shy.

It hides the record as well as the man. A Reigan that reads the count of the dead in a chest found in his a gray quiet, a Tenrai of the Hall with a count of none, and in a town of carters and fishwives his chest read exactly like theirs. Tenebra thins in a crowd, but a veiled chest among two hundred empty ones needs no shadow.

Applications

  • Passing dogs, horses and guards without notice
  • Hiding the count of his dead from an eye that reads souls
  • Passing for one of the townsfolk in a crowd

Associations

  • Tenebra is its lens
  • The Spirit Path lays the hush over what he is, with a studied Body’s small share for footfall and scent

Limitations

  • Its limit is Tenebra’s own failure: it is shaped against the angles he expects, and from the one he did not plan for he is as bright as anyone, brighter, a figure suddenly sharp in the corner of a mirror or the eye of a second watcher behind the first
  • It costs nothing, and it is always on
  • Its tell is that dogs do not bark
  • It is beaten by the one angle he did not plan for

Techniques

ONE NEEDLE (一本針 · Ippon-bari) · Signature Technique

ONE NEEDLE is basically a one-hit kill: the power to end a man with a single thrust where the skull meets the neck. It is the art used on a skull, and the reason the north’s lords have worn high collars for fifty years.

Capabilities

A single thrust with the Last Thread, a needle-blade the length of a hand, up through the soft gap between the base of the skull and the first bone of the neck, the atlas, into the brainstem. The brainstem is the stalk that joins brain to spine and keeps breath and heartbeat going, so a point that reaches it stops both at once. Death is instant and almost bloodless, a single bead of blood at the nape for the physicians to miss.

Under it is the oldest habit of points: a thumb pushed against mail does nothing, and the same push behind a point finer than a surgeon’s needle is more than a steel ring or a plate of bone can bear. The force behind it is small, and the point is so fine that at its tip it goes through mail rings and leather and bone as if they were cloth; the Measurewrights call it pressure, force divided by the area it lands on. Ignivale, the spark that closes the gap between wanting and doing, puts the thrust in the hand before the shoulder has moved. He does not raise his voice for it.

Applications

  • Killing through mail rings, leather and bone at the nape
  • Killing so cleanly that a physician finds nothing

Associations

  • Ignivale is its lens
  • A studied Body Path gives the one honest thrust, aimed by the Spirit read
  • The Last Thread carries it
  • Mastered, in the Tenrai war-schools and fifty years of contracts

Limitations

  • It needs the nape bare or soft: a stiff high collar of boiled leather, a mail gorget at the back of the neck, or a back to a wall denies it
  • The steel is a limit too: a point that fine holds almost no heat of its own, and pushed through air at forge heat it passes the color where its temper lives and goes soft, so that on bone a hair above the gap it goes in a thumb’s depth and curls
  • It costs about 0.4 percent of his reserve, almost all of it spent keeping the point perfectly true
  • His breath stops for three heartbeats before the thrust
  • Before, guard the nape with a stiff high collar or a mail gorget
  • During, a turn of the head a finger’s breadth sends the needle off the gap and into bone, where it skids
  • After, he is an old man with an empty hand standing behind you, and he has never once been there long

FORCING STONE (利かし · Kikashi) · Technique

FORCING STONE is basically provocation: the power to make a standing man move, with a pebble. Kikashi is the go player’s word for a move that must be answered.

Capabilities

It is a go stone, slate or shell, flicked from the thumb at a cheekbone, a temple, the point of the jaw, with about enough behind it to crack a walnut. There is no working in it. He throws it only at a fighter whose answer he has already read: a man bound by law, vow, temper or pride to answer whatever lands on him.

The stone lands, the answer comes, and a man who has been made to move has somewhere to step to, so the Kingstep works again on a fighter who had beaten it by standing still. Each stone is a step and a palmful is a road, every answer passing through the air where an old man had just been.

Applications

  • Breaking a fighter out of standing still
  • Leading a stronger fighter across ground, a stone at a time

Associations

  • No lens and no working; the stone is aimed by the minute’s read
  • The Kingstep, which it opens the way for
  • His traveling go set, slate and shell

Limitations

  • It takes no reserve at all
  • Its price is that every stone is a blow landed and every answer is a real one, thrown with everything the other fighter has at a body one honest blow would kill
  • Against a fighter who banks what he is dealt, each stone is a coin in the other man’s purse
  • A hand going to the go stones gives it away
  • It is beaten by owing a pebble nothing: a fighter free to ignore it stands still, and the old man is out of moves

KINGSTEP OF THE WINNING THREAD (王歩 · Ōho) · School Art, Tenrai

KINGSTEP OF THE WINNING THREAD is basically predictive evasion: the power to put his foot where the other fighter was about to step. It is the Tenrai movement form, taught in the Tenrai war-schools, and the art used on a step.

Capabilities

The Tengan shows where an opponent will step, and the Kingstep puts the foot there first. In his feet it is one pace back and one to the right, the right heel carried off the floor even when he sits, and the foot comes down where nothing is going to be.

Applications

  • Keeping an old body out of the way of every blow
  • Standing where the answer is not

Associations

  • No lens, the Tengan alone
  • The Spirit Path for the read, a studied Body for the foot
  • A Tenrai school art, mastered

Limitations

  • It needs the other fighter to move: a blow, a rush, a step, anything with somewhere to go
  • It costs nothing beyond the open eye
  • Its tell is that his steps land where the other man’s next step would have gone
  • Standing still beats it, because that leaves nowhere to step to, and every Kurenai child is taught that answer at the same table as the oath

RUINOUS COINFALL (銭落とし · Zeni-otoshi) · School Art, Tenrai

RUINOUS COINFALL is basically probability manipulation: the power to tip one small chance at the moment it most matters. It is a Tenrai school art, and the one thing in his kit that reaches above his own share.

Capabilities

The lamp that falls, the coin that rolls, the latch that slips. It is a true pull on Fate, and at Fate II it brings down one thread for one thing that was already on its way: a latch already worn, a lamp already badly hung.

Applications

  • Slipping a worn latch
  • Dropping a badly hung lamp

Associations

  • The walked Fate Path, and Dominion Fate as its gate
  • Tenrai blood and fifty years of tipping small chances, with no Work behind it; blood without a Work is no grip, so his Fate stops at II
  • No lens stated

Limitations

  • It moves nothing sound
  • A single lean takes about half his reserve and has to be set beforehand, which is why he has used it rarely and why it never passed Drilled
  • Small things falling in the room give it away
  • It is beaten by owning nothing loose

Releases

KAMIKAKUSHI (神隠し · Spirited Away) · First Release

KAMIKAKUSHI is basically imperceptibility: the power to drop out of every sense in the field at once. It is the old word for a person the gods have taken, who is there and then is not, and the folk call it the Vanishing.

Capabilities

His aura drops below sight altogether, HUSH and BETWEEN BLINKS go as far as his Spirit III will carry them, and for as long as it holds there are no gaps he cannot use and no angle he forgot: even the second watcher sees nothing.

As a Spirit release it changes the reach of the line he already casts. The hush that lay over one old man is laid over every sense in the field at once, so that body heat, scent and footfall sink into the room’s own, and BETWEEN BLINKS can run on a hundred pairs of eyes and find in all their overlapping darks one gap wide enough to step through.

Applications

  • Crossing a hall full of watchers
  • Beating the second watcher and the angle he forgot

Techniques

  • Called - a single soft word in the old court tongue, for the full rise
  • Silent - weaker; it leaves his shadow behind for a heartbeat when he moves

Associations

  • Tenebra, at the top of what HUSH can do
  • The Last Thread carries its hush; taken from him, the First Release still hides him, but the needle that was the point of hiding is gone

Limitations

  • It lasts about four minutes from full
  • Everything the hush has is spent hiding the body, and nothing is left over the record: while it holds, a soul-reading eye at a finger’s distance reads his chest plainly, every killing in it
  • It hides him from watchers, never from what he owes: a working tagged to his own soul finds him wherever he stands
  • A hand laid flat on him breaks it
  • When it ends, the coat lets go of everything it swallowed at once, which is Tenebra’s saturation; he is spent and shaking, an old man again, and the shake is the window

HITSUZEN (必然 · Inevitability) · Second Release

HITSUZEN the Release is basically domain creation: the power to open his Domain and keep it standing. It opens and powers the Domain.

Capabilities

The held note is silence, deeper than the room’s. Called, it is one quiet word and the field forms as it is named.

Techniques

  • Called - one quiet word, and the field forms as it is named, out to forty yards
  • Silent - the field comes in at half the reach

Associations

  • A Spirit Release, the lead Path’s Second; because he walks Fate, the Domain it opens makes a Fate claim
  • The Domain of the same name, which it opens and powers

Limitations

  • Silent, it gives half the reach
  • What it opens is blind to whose side the odds are on

Domain

HITSUZEN (必然 · Inevitability) · Domain

HITSUZEN is basically luck negation: the power to make the likeliest thing happen every time, for everyone inside the line. It is his one idea at its largest: where BETWEEN BLINKS uses the gap nobody watches, the Domain leaves no gaps at all.

Capabilities

Opened by the Second Release out to forty yards, it presses every future in the field toward the most probable one: no lucky dodge, no blade that turns on a buckle by chance, no miracle. Whatever the odds favor, the odds deliver.

Called in the old court tongue, no louder than his speaking voice, it forms as it is named. Nothing in the room changes to the eye, and the held note is a silence deeper than the room’s. Like every Domain it is a claim laid on the ground and pulls no thread: it adds no force to anything and moves nothing that had no way of happening. It only strips the long chances out, the way a long night of dice would look if no throw ever ran against the odds.

Applications

  • Stripping the lucky dodge, the turned blade and the miracle out of a fight he is already likelier to win

Associations

  • Oneirion is its lens
  • Opened by a Spirit Second Release, its claim made on Fate because he walks Fate: a claim on conditions, authored in the Core, with Dominion Fate as its gate
  • The Second Release, which opens and powers it
  • The Tengan, which tells him beforehand which man is the likelier, the only reason he has ever dared open it

Limitations

  • It serves him only while he is the likelier man
  • It is blind to whose side the odds are on, and it makes a stronger enemy surer of winning
  • Its upkeep is paid at his Core’s rate, and it lasts about two minutes from a full reserve
  • It reaches forty yards, and half that when opened silent
  • It is beaten by being the likelier side outright, by stepping out of the forty yards, or by making one truly free choice inside it, an act no reading could have leaned toward, which the field cannot press because it has no weight to press
