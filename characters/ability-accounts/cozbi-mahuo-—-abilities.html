Cozbi Mahuo — Abilities in full

Cozbi Mahuo — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202621 min read

Card: Cozbi Mahuo

THE TABLE FOR THE DEAD

THE TABLE FOR THE DEAD (제사상 · Jesasang) is basically paradox manipulation: the power to make one place where a thing both is and is not, and to hold it there until the world gives way. He lays a place at his table for somebody who is not there, and they come and sit down. It is his signature art, and his Ability and his three summoning arts are this one idea. It has made him one of the great summoners the Continuum has ever recorded, the one called the God of Summons.

Capabilities

Every piece of it is the same act at a different size. A place set at a table for a dead woman is that act done with a cup. THE SEAM is that act done with the air, a point that is a sink and a source together. A Founding is that act done with a person: someone who never existed is given a place to exist in first, and then stands up in it. A compact is a wild will that is free and held at once. A conscript is a soul that has died and is still standing in the room. It is also why he speaks of his dead in the present tense; that habit and the art are one thing, and the Continuum entered it at Principality under its own name, Antithesis.

Summoning is a three-plane crossing, a vessel shaped on the Physical Plane, an identity donated on the Soul Plane and a recognition held on the Plane of Fate, and he pays at all three. He is a Spirit Path summoner, so the work is authored and mostly spent through the Essence Core, at the Ferryman’s rung, where the Veil opens and the dead know a man by name. The vessel is grown by Eidolyn, the Shaping Mind, whose idea is a thing imagined so hard that it stands up and has to answer for itself, and whose operation is coagulation: what is imagined is set solid along the pattern the maker holds in mind. Frost ferning across a windowpane along the ice already there is the nearest picture of it, and the Measurewrights call it templated growth. In Eidolyn the pattern is his intent, and what he holds clearly comes out as real hair and bone and a beating heart. The self is carried by Anima Spirare, the Soul’s Breath and the Mahuo house current, whose idea is the breath of the whole, a body that remembers being whole and answers a small hurt with everything it has, and whose operation is fermentation, the whole quickened round one small start, so a small donated self catches and fills the whole vessel. Physicians know the shape of it from blood, which clots as a cascade in which each step sets off many of the next, and takes up breath faster the more it already carries. On the Plane of Fate he has no grip at all, no blood and no blessing, so every compact and every charter is written out of his own share, the way a vow is, and pulls nothing down. Under all three stands Mortalis, the Black Gate, whose idea is ending looked at squarely and understood as a passage with its own law, and whose operation is dissolution, a thing’s hold on what it is loosed lawfully and in order. He knows that gate as a road, and works its edge the other way, keeping the spending going in what should have stopped. The Measurewrights call a living body a structure held far from equilibrium by constant spending, and death the spending stopping.

Every summoner pays twice: once in Essence for the form and again for the self that animates it, a piece of their own Core lent out and strained. Cozbi pays the first in his own coin and the second in other people’s, harvested soul-fragment spent as if it were his, and founds beings with cores of their own besides. That is why he can field a host where other masters field a squad, and it is, in plain terms, the crime the Zettari–Mahou Accord names. Every other summoner in the Accord’s register works from a roster. He works from a gap: the Accord’s list of sealed forms has been out of date for four hundred years, and he drafts to the problem. The Commission’s assessors gave up trying to list his forms after the third year, and one of them wrote in the margin of the file, in a hand that was not entirely steady, that he founds a being the way a host lays a table, for exactly the guest who is coming.

Applications

  • Fighting at the length of his table, through whoever he has seated at it
  • Putting a founded hero or a drafted wall where the fight needs it faster than legs could
  • Taking the ground from under whoever his guest is about to strike
  • Opening the plate a breath before the guest’s edge arrives, so that the cut that kills is somebody else’s and the wound it goes through is his
  • Fielding a host where other masters field a squad

Techniques

  • ANTITHESIS - the idea in a point of air, a sink and a source together
  • THE FOUNDING - the idea given a body: someone who never existed, given a place to exist in and standing up in it
  • THE BOUND WILD - a wild will that is free and held at once
  • THE CONSCRIPTS - souls that have died and are still standing in the room

Associations

  • Antithesis, his own registered law, which runs on no Wellspring
  • Eidolyn, the Shaping Mind, grows the vessel along the pattern of his intent
  • Anima Spirare, the Soul’s Breath and the Mahuo house current, carries the self into the vessel
  • Mortalis, the Black Gate, keeps the spending going in what should have stopped
  • The Spirit Path does the work, through the Essence Core; the Fate Path, which he walks, carries the compacts and charters; his Body is only Studied
  • His best ground is the south’s own: the iron hills and salt pans, and any place where the dead have soured a site
  • He has no Releases, though his Stage has long since opened both the Releases his Spirit road allows, and he carries no weapon
  • His guests, compacts and conscripts work at their own Grade and add nothing to his
  • He says every name at a host’s volume, pleasantly, as if announcing the next course, and will explain any of them at length and with real pride

Limitations

  • Every guest is one more thread out of his own chest, paid for every second it stands
  • He does not sleep, cannot keep food down on campaign, and his own table is wearing him to bones
  • He has no blow for any of it to ride; it rides his guests’ blows
  • Nine parts in ten of what he spends reach the work, which is his yield, and the lost tenth is paid in his Core, showing as no more than a faint warmth at his collar
  • The charge can always be read, and he does not trouble to hide it
  • His resolve holds every seam, leash and thread, so the way to beat him is to strike the host and not the guests
  • It is also beaten by counting the threads, collapsing the boundary, finding the line the charter forgot, or staying beyond the gradient
  • Mortalis fails by its flaw: a soul that has stopped fearing the gate has stopped holding out against it, and he cools toward the boundary he stands at, which the Measurewrights call equilibration
  • Anima Spirare fails by its flaw: the whole answers a small hurt with everything it has and cannot answer with less, so when it loses the place of the hurt it answers everywhere at once until he bleeds from the gums, the nose and the eyes; the Measurewrights call it disseminated activation
  • Eidolyn fails by its flaw: a thing imagined hard enough to stand up stands up exactly as it was imagined, so every flaw in his memory is cast into the body, which the Mahuo founders called the defect of the template and feared more than any enemy
  • Antithesis fails by turning inward

Ability

ANTITHESIS (상극 · Sanggeuk) · Ability, his registered law

ANTITHESIS is basically attraction and repulsion: the power to hold one point where a pull and its exact opposite are both true and neither yields. It is the one phenomenon his whole architecture is built around. Everything he borrows from other souls is coin; Antithesis is the root current of his own.

Capabilities

Sanggeuk is the old word for two forces that overcome each other, as water quenches fire. In Cozbi it is no contest between two things. It is one place where a thing and its contradiction are both true, and Cozbi is Antithesis. He holds a single point in the air beside him where one claim has two opposite faces at once: an attraction and its exact negation, a sink and a source sharing one location.

The two claims are laid one over the other there, and because neither yields they do not cancel; they answer at different reaches. The pull carries far and the push stays close, so matter far out feels mostly the pull and matter close in feels mostly the push. The Measurewrights draw it as a potential and its opposite falling away with distance at different rates, and reach for the way two grains of anything draw together across a gap and shove apart when pressed too close.

What breaks a thing in it is being made to answer both claims at once, one face of it drawn in and the other driven off. Plate is made to stand against a push that comes all one way and has no answer to being drawn two ways along its own thickness, so it parts along its weakest line. The Measurewrights call it a tidal gradient and the parting shear: the same difference from one side of a thing to the other, they say, that raises a tide and, close to a heavy enough body, pulls a moon to rubble. It is active and rooted, his own and neither conscripted nor borrowed, and it scales with his resolve and not his reserve, which is why an opponent who has beaten every borrowed soul on his structure has not yet met it.

Applications

  • Parting armor that has nothing to resist with, since it is split and not pushed
  • Taking a blade out of a hand, or pulling a charging fighter onto his guard
  • Flinging a host’s bodies where he needs them, and moving his own constructs faster than their legs
  • Removing ground from under a fighter, collapsing a stair, or closing a gap without moving
  • Holding a founded core together

Techniques

  • THE SEAM (이음새 · Ieumsae) - a pinprick in the air at his shoulder that swallows the light and shines at once; everything else he does is hung on it
  • THE CONTRADICTION (모순 · Mosun) - named for the old story of the spear that pierces any shield and the shield that stops any spear; where the seam’s pull and push overlap, a breastplate is not dented, it is parted like bread, and at the smallest scale a thing that meets its own negation is let go entirely, which the Measurewrights call annihilation, in the loose sense, and reckon as stored mass-energy set free
  • TIDES (조류 · Joryu) - the same two claims used gently: anything between him and a held seam is dragged, then thrown, so the sword leaves a hand and goes to him, the charging man arrives early and off his feet, and his own guests cross the yard thrown
  • THE FOLD (접기 · Jeopgi) - a seam drawn out along a line, a door that opens in one place and closes in another, so the stair a fighter stood on is somewhere else and he is a pace nearer without having moved; it scales with how much space the seam is stretched across

Associations

  • His own law, registered by the Continuum at Principality; no Wellspring
  • The Spirit Path: held in the Core as a registered law, written there, and what it does to matter leaves by the Shell
  • THE FOUNDING, whose cores it holds together
  • His resolve, which it scales with

Limitations

  • He can hold one seam comfortably and a few with strain
  • It has no price in reserve, and it tears at whatever holds it: the seam’s two faces pull on his own attention, and a flinch is a collapse
  • Its tell is in the light: it bends toward the point and away from it at once, so the seam looks like a pinprick that both swallows and shines
  • The air tastes of struck flint, and there is a faint ring in the ears that the Measurewrights put down to a pressure gradient
  • When he is angry the light bends at his shoulder with no seam called
  • A seam is held by concentration, so a fighter who strikes at his resolve and not at the seam makes it lapse
  • Distance beats the gradient; stay beyond its reach
  • It fails by turning inward: a point that holds both claims will, if his hold slips, hold both claims in him

Techniques

THE FOUNDING (건국 · Geonguk) · Signature Technique

THE FOUNDING is basically life creation: the power to make a small world with a wall round it and a person inside it who has a heart of their own and knows what they are for. It is the Mahuo house art and runs in the blood.

Capabilities

A summon at this level is a founding and no projection. He creates a small Realm, a micro-world with its own boundary, and in it a being that exists as a semi-independent presence with a core, a shell and an attraction layer of its own. It thinks, adapts, feels and persists beyond his immediate attention. It is the art behind eidolons that are people.

He calls it with one quiet word in Common, “Summoning,” and it is a building more than a calling, in three steps anyone watching can follow: the boundary, then the core, then the charter. The near-white of his aura sharpens at its fringe to arterial red, like red glass held up to the sun, a black-violet seam opens under it where Eidolyn runs, and the boundary of the little Realm unfolds with the seam’s own bent light round its edge, because it is Antithesis that holds a founded core together. A founded heartbeat is heard from the wrong side of a door, with a fine crystal ticking under it as the shape is laid down.

Stage XII is the minimum for the art, and Stage XIV is the usual gate for genuine autonomy. He founds autonomous beings at XIII because Antithesis gives the founding a core that holds without recognition from a Hollow Throne.

Applications

  • A warden to hold a gate, a general to hold a field, a scholar to answer questions
  • A second self in a fight: a being that fights as the hero it was written to be, with judgment he does not have to supply, which leaves his attention for the point at his shoulder
  • Dari, the elders’ nine-tailed fox of the crossings, founded in the Post’s yard and chartered to follow and protect his nephew Hiromi, serve him, and report every great change

Techniques

  • The Boundary (경계 · Gyeonggye) - the Realm is a cavity with a wall, a doorway of a place where there had been no place; a being needs somewhere to be real, so he builds the place first, and inside the wall his law holds
  • The Core Analog (핵 · Haek) - a point of his own Crystal pressed into the middle of it the way a potter presses a thumb into clay to start the hollow, giving the being a core, shell and attraction analog of its own
  • The Charter (칙령 · Chikryeong) - the being’s purpose, written into it in the moment of its making; it obeys its charter and not his word of the moment, and a charter written from a story carries the story whole

Associations

  • The Spirit Path, with the walked Fate Path for the Realm’s claim and the charter
  • Eidolyn makes the body, Anima Spirare the core, and Antithesis holds it
  • The Mahuo blood: a child of the house can raise a crude one
  • He came to it by living it

Limitations

  • Founding takes about a third of a percent of his reserve, and keeping a founded being lasts about ten hours from full
  • The keeping is one more thread from his chest to its heart, so a guest who stays is a weight he carries every second
  • He founds one, or two with strain, and only when a fight deserves it
  • The being’s coherence is his, and if the Realm’s boundary cracks, the strain lands on him
  • A founded being cannot leave the wall without him, which is both leash and shelter; outside it the being is a stranger
  • Whatever he forgot to write in the charter is a gap in the being
  • Every flaw in his memory is cast into the body: a child’s crude founding shows a wing a hand too long where the memory was surer of one side
  • Dari never once did a thing the way she was asked, and he wrote her that way knowing it; her upkeep is a thread down the Kessen road, less than he recovers and one more thing to carry
  • Its tell is a small Realm unfolding around a boundary, with a being that has a core of its own standing in it
  • It is beaten by collapsing the boundary, outlasting the founding’s stability, or finding the line the charter forgot
  • It is the most dangerous form, because a founded being with enough internal coherence begins to resist dissolution: he has made a person and cannot always unmake it

THE BOUND WILD (야생의 결박 · Yasaeng-ui Gyeolbak) · Signature Technique

THE BOUND WILD is basically contract binding: the power to bind a living power by a sealed compact and call it by its seal. He binds beasts, Well-born creatures and the great creatures of the Bestiary’s Summoned and Bound.

Capabilities

Binding ties the creature’s state to a leash he holds, so that it answers his hand the way a hawk answers the glove; the Measurewrights call it a coupled system. A compact he has sealed is powered by the creature’s own Essence, so he pays only the leash. Unlike his drafts, a sealed compact gets cheaper each time it answers, because recognition accumulates, so his oldest compacts cost him almost nothing and answer almost at once. He is the rare drafter who keeps a standing roster alongside an infinite catalog.

A seal of his house color opens on a great beast’s brow before the binding sets, and it arrives already knowing how to be what it is. It scales with the creature’s power and his resolve, not his reserve.

Applications

  • Calling a war-beast, a storm-creature or a ward-guardian by its seal
  • Keeping the great beast of the white mantle, the oldest compact he wears, alive on the Roster; the fur is its rent, shed each winter under the compact’s terms
  • Pressing many presences into one worse thing

Techniques

  • The Compact (계약 · Gyeyak) - written on the Plane of Fate with the creature’s own name as the address and a price both sides can read
  • The Roster (명부 · Myeongbu) - his living list of sealed compacts, each with a price and a clause
  • The Leash (고삐 · Gobi) - his will holding the creature’s will down
  • SPIRIT SINGULARITY (정령 합일 · Jeongnyeong Hapil) - the whole table pressed into one guest: where lawful practice restricts it to drafted forms, he compresses bound presences into one core entity carrying the aggregate, and one thing gets up that is worse than the sum was

Associations

  • The Fate Path, Walked: the compact is written on the Plane of Fate out of his own share; Spirit holds the leash
  • No lens; the compact is its own instrument, a binding with a call
  • The creature’s own Essence, which powers it
  • He came to it by living it

Limitations

  • The leash takes about 60,000 EU a second, falling with every answering; the creature’s own Essence pays the rest
  • The compact’s price is paid in what the creature wants, and the great ones want things that cost
  • A bound will tests its leash, so a second great binding at once strains his attention
  • In SPIRIT SINGULARITY the constituent presences do not survive as themselves, so he does it with drafted forms and conscripts and never with a bound creature he respects
  • Its tell is the seal of his house color opening on the creature’s brow before the binding sets
  • The compact runs on the creature’s own Essence, so exhausting it breaks the compact
  • A fighter who strikes at his resolve while his attention is on the leash, and not at the beast, is hitting the real weak point
  • It fails with a creature that decides the price was unfair

THE CONSCRIPTS (징집 · Jingjip) · Signature Technique

THE CONSCRIPTS is basically soul summoning: the power to field a host of forms on threads spun from other people’s souls. It is the art the Accord names without qualification, and he is called the reason the art is regulated.

Capabilities

It is legion and Infinite practice combined, on a foundation of harvested soul-fragment in place of lawful draft. He fragments his Core output into threads, each thread anchoring one call, and the Essence he spends on the call is not all his own. Some of it is taken from people who were not asked. Mortalis keeps the fragment this side of the Gate, and Eidolyn gives it a form.

Black-violet threads run out of his chest by the dozen, and at the end of every one stands something that used to be somebody. Most people cannot see the threads. Mahuo blood can, a little: very fine, like the lines a spider lets out into the wind, dozens and more than dozens, going off through the walls of the house in every direction. It is active, mass-produced and economical.

Applications

  • Standing forms at Apprentice to Journeyman rank: an eidolon with tactical intelligence, an ancestral echo, a scout or a wall
  • Ending a long fight richer than he began it
  • A form that hardens when he is resolute

Techniques

  • The Harvest (수확 · Suhwak) - he draws soul-fragment, the small residue of Essence a living soul sheds, from willing and unwilling sources over years, and folds it until it stops being a draft and becomes a thread bent to the shape of his will; a hundred unwilling sources can give him a hundred threads
  • The Thread (실 · Sil) - one thread anchors one standing form, and each is a separate load on his body
  • Axis Binding (축 결박 · Chuk Gyeolbak) - a form’s traits tied to one of his own Spirit Axes shift as his own state does; the most responsive technique in the art
  • Echo Manifestation (잔향 · Janhyang) - remnants of dissolved forms are gathered and folded into new ones

Associations

  • The Spirit Path, as a call with a binding
  • Mortalis for the fragment kept this side of the Gate, Eidolyn for the form
  • The Zettari–Mahou Accord, which names it as the crime
  • He came to it by living it

Limitations

  • Each form takes about 150,000 EU to raise and 25,000 EU a second to keep
  • The souls inside are not paid for, which is the crime
  • Each thread is a separate load on his body: a drafter at his scale does not sleep normally, cannot digest properly in a campaign and is the thinnest man in the camp
  • Axis Binding is the least deniable technique in the art, because damage to the form reaches him as a change in himself, and the form falters when he is not resolute
  • Forms destroyed by dissolution leave nothing for Echo Manifestation to gather
  • Its tell is the threads, and the people near him feel a draft where their own warmth should be; when he is tired the threads shake, and where they leave his chest it looks as though it hurts
  • The threads are readable: anyone with comparable Perception can count them, so his exact commitment is visible before the first form lands
  • Cut his line of attention and the Feed lapses, leaving only what the Binding alone will hold
  • It fails at the seam in a structure built from many borrowed souls, which a Harmonia-trained ear can find
