Yukazuri Moto — Abilities in full

Yukazuri Moto — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202613 min read

Card: Yukazuri Moto

HOLD STILL

HOLD STILL is basically tranquility inducement: the power to make a body hold still, and to hear in the quiet exactly what is wrong with it. It is the surgeon’s art, said in northern Common, and three of her Traits are this one idea. The name is her own two words, the ones every man she ever sewed heard first.

Capabilities

All three pieces are one act at three sizes: make it hold still, listen, and take only what is dead. STILL WATER stills the room. THE HUM listens to what the stillness leaves audible. WHERE IT’S DEAD stills her own hands and face and puts the listening to work on a wound.

It rides no blow, because there is no blow in her. It rides the needle. The room comes down first, so the man lies still; the ear goes to him second, so she knows what he is not saying; the hands go in third, and by then the three are one movement that takes less time than it takes him to finish swearing at her.

She did not design any of it. It grew around the work, the way her Crystal did, and it is the reason a girl of thirteen with a reserve a grown practitioner would laugh at ran the surgeons’ line of an army.

Applications

  • Getting a wounded man to lie still for the needle
  • Knowing what he is not saying before the hands go in
  • Taking the dead part of a wound and nothing else

Techniques

  • STILL WATER - stills the room
  • THE HUM - listens to what the stillness leaves audible
  • WHERE IT’S DEAD - stills her own hands and face and puts the listening to work on a wound

Associations

  • Somnalis, the Hush, and Anamnesis, the Remembering, which she has never harmonized
  • The Spirit Path carries the gift that hears, and the Body Path, studied, carries her hands
  • The field surgeon’s trade
  • THE MARK is the red line’s and not the art’s
  • She has no named technique, no Release and no Domain; the nearest she came to a Domain was a surgeons’ tent where everyone breathed slower

Limitations

  • It has never been pointed at anyone: its three pieces are Traits and not techniques, and nothing in it is called, charged or aimed
  • It is aimed at nobody, so anyone with a blade can simply walk up to her
  • It costs almost nothing in reserve. The price is the art’s own: it only works beside the dying, so she has to be where they are
  • Whatever she shut away to keep her hands steady comes due in the hours when she is not working
  • The visible charge is small and a room learns it fast: she comes close, her own out-breath goes long and slow, the frost-pale of her aura takes a faint rose, and the tin comes out of the apron pocket before she has said a word
  • It is beaten by noise and fear louder than she is, by a man who has decided, and by a shut mouth and a low, even breath
  • Each piece fails in its own way

Traits

STILL WATER · Passive Trait

STILL WATER is basically a calming aura: the power to slow the breath and the pulse of everyone within a few paces of her. It is always on, and her brother stopped noticing it years ago.

Capabilities

A frightened body breathes high and short, up under the collarbone, and a body breathing like that keeps itself frightened. The heart quickens on every in-breath and eases on every out-breath, a swing the scholars call respiratory sinus arrhythmia, and the long nerve that does the easing they call the vagus, so a man taking quick gulps of air never reaches the part of the breath that would slow him. Breath is also the one such rhythm a person can be led in, and people fall into step with a slower breather beside them without noticing they have. That is the plain half, and any calm surgeon has a little of it.

The other half is Somnalis, folk name the Hush. Somnalis is the Wellspring of the near branches, the law that what has not happened yet already has weight and can be held as a spread instead of a single answer, and its bearers are souls at ease in not knowing. Panic is the opposite condition: a body that has picked the worst branch out of the spread and is already living in it. What runs off her into the air within a few paces is that ease, laid through her Core onto the souls nearest. Under it the frightened body lets go of the one doom it had settled on, the breath comes down from the collarbone to the belly, the pulse follows the breath, and the shaking stops.

Applications

  • Quieting a shouting yard when the salve tin comes out of the apron
  • Getting a man who was thrashing to lie down for the needle
  • Calming a whole room when her fingers are on the wound, which is when it is strongest
  • It works on anyone, friend or prisoner or dog, and on her brother most of all, who felt it through a door before he had turned to see her and never once in his life wondered why

Associations

  • Somnalis, the Hush, at first contact, met at the Veyrath ditch
  • The lens met her where it pools, on ground where many plans cross and every one could still go either way: two hundred men in a ditch, each of whom might live or might not
  • The Spirit Path, laid through the Core on the souls nearest

Limitations

  • Its reach is a few paces
  • The lens’s own limit, the one the scholars write as Return, brings a body back toward its own rest and no lower: it cannot put a man under and it cannot slow a heart past resting
  • It cannot stop a bleed, only the panic over the bleed, which matters because a panicking man pumps himself empty faster
  • A man who has decided is not running too fast, so there is nothing in him for it to return; a hall full of men who have already decided is the place it does least
  • It costs almost nothing, a share of her reserve too small to feel and less than she recovers standing still, and it never stops
  • Something over a third of what she spends arrives as that work; the rest goes off as the pale light almost nobody sees and a slight coolness in the air about her
  • Its tell is everyone near her breathing slower
  • Noise and terror louder than she is beat it
  • Somnalis fails by ensemble collapse, the spread taken for a single answer: if she ever told a man he would be fine, she would be choosing one branch for him out loud, the ease would turn false on the spot, and his body would know it before he did
  • So she never says it. She says to hold still, which is true on every branch

THE HUM · Primary Trait

THE HUM is basically psychometry by ear: the power to hear the record a thing or a person is carrying. It is Anamnesis leaking through her Perception, a gift she was born with, before she has a name for it.

Capabilities

Everything keeps its past, and that is the whole idea of Anamnesis, the Remembering: a fired pot holds every dose it has taken since the kiln, and a body, a soul and a sworn thing keep theirs the same way. Its operation is separation, the kept past parted from the thing that kept it and read, and the Measurewrights’ favorite example is basalt, which they say holds the direction of the field it cooled in. In her it comes as sound. She bends her ear to a thing the way she bends over a man’s chest and closes her eyes for it, and when they open they have gone very dark, the blue nearly black.

So a seal-token that sits under a house’s name has a note she can hear across a yard at night, like bees in a wall, a sealed one has a shape she can feel like a coin in a pocket, and a man’s decision is on him before he opens his mouth. She does not have to touch the thing. Under it sits the plain trade skill that any good surgeon has and she has to a rare degree: where the breath sits, what a thumb does on a table edge.

Applications

  • Hearing every token in a post hum, all the way across a yard at night
  • Reading her brother’s dead Stark token: not broken, not dead, only looking straight at him and unable to see anyone there
  • Reading the Stark token in his closed fist as sealed, a good seal, old wall and bright light
  • Hearing a man decide
  • Hearing a lie in the breath, high and short, right up under the collarbone

Associations

  • Anamnesis, never harmonized, a leak through her Perception
  • The Spirit Path
  • Her Perception and Empathy, which price it
  • The surgeon’s habit of reading breath

Limitations

  • A record is all it is: it hears what has been laid down and nothing that has not
  • It hears and does not act
  • What she hears she then has to reason out like anyone else: it took her three days of listening to a post full of tokens to work out that they were listening for a house name, and that she and her brother had none one could find
  • It costs nothing she notices
  • Its tell is her head bent to listen
  • A man who keeps his mouth shut and his breath low and even has laid nothing down for her to hear, so the counter is to say nothing and breathe evenly
  • Anamnesis fails by thermal resetting, a careless reader wiping the record in the act of taking it; it has never happened to her because she has never pulled, only listened, and a trained hand grabbing at what she hears would risk it

WHERE IT’S DEAD · Secondary Trait

WHERE IT’S DEAD is basically surgical precision: the power to find exactly where the living part of a wound stops, with hands that do not shake. It formed at the Veyrath ditch at twelve, two hundred men on her knees.

Capabilities

A wound heals from tissue that is alive, and dead tissue left in it feeds the rot that kills the man a week later; cut too far and he loses what he could have kept. The whole craft, which the scholars call debridement, is finding the line, and the line shows at the edges: living flesh has color, bleeds bright when touched and draws back from the knife, and dead flesh does none of the three. Her rule for it is this: you don’t cut what you can sew; you don’t sew what has to come off; you look; you find where it’s dead; you take that, and nothing else; and then you close what’s left, and let it live.

That rule is no Wellspring’s. It is a break in her Core from the Veyrath ditch that healed into a tool. While there is work in front of her, the fear is shut behind it, her hands are steady, her face does nothing at all, which is what it does when she is frightened, and everything she is goes into the look.

Applications

  • Taking the dead part of a wound and nothing else, and closing what is left
  • Keeping her hands steady and her face blank while she works
  • It became the first lesson of her brother’s Work of Ruins, and the law of the oath he swore before the Seat

Associations

  • A healed break in her Core, with no Wellspring under it
  • The Body Path, studied, for the hands, under a Spirit Path’s look
  • Plain field surgery: the cut and the stitch are no working

Limitations

  • It takes no reserve
  • It holds only while there is work in front of her, and it steadies her for a wound and for nothing else
  • It has no say over what happens to her when her hands are tied
  • Its tell is the stillness, the hands gone steady and the face gone blank at the same moment, which the people who know her read as the sign that it is bad
  • She pays afterward, in the hours when she is not working, and she does not show that either
  • It is beaten by taking the work away from her
  • The Core’s rule fails when the work stops: what it was holding back arrives in the hours afterward, all at once, and she lets nobody see it

THE MARK · Inherited Trait

THE MARK is basically bloodline marking: the power to write the red line’s name true, in the one hand the registers cannot read. It is blood and no working, and it stands apart from HOLD STILL because it belongs to the red line and not to the surgeon.

Capabilities

The Kōkan mark is a long down-stroke, a hook, a crossing and a small closed loop like a knot in a rope, a little like a closed eye. Her brother’s hand could never draw it. Hers always drew it true, on frost, in camp dirt, on the bottom of her salve tin, in her own blood on her palm to steady her hands. At eleven she drew it in spilled lamp oil on the stamping floor and it burned blue for a moment before the oil went out.

It is the red line’s name, and in her it answers. It does nothing but tell the truth about whose she is, which she did not know until her brother told her, and she answered, “so that’s whose it was.”

Applications

  • Telling the truth about whose she is
  • Steadying her hands, drawn in her own blood on her palm
  • Held up on her palm in her own blood in the yard of the Foot, it put six hundred people on their knees without a word from her

Associations

  • The Kōkan blood, the red line
  • No Wellspring, no Path and no Lens; it is a name, drawn by hand
  • Her brother, who told her whose it was

Limitations

  • It costs nothing: it spends nothing and pulls nothing down
  • It only speaks to people who can already read it, and those people lie about what they saw
  • A Zettari surveyor at the Marrow went white when she drew it on his frosted window, wiped it off with his sleeve and lied to her about it, with his breath gone short and high under the collarbone
  • Its tell is the hand: four strokes, the same every time, on frost or tin or skin
  • That same silence is what beats it: a register that will not enter it and a clerk who will not say its name
