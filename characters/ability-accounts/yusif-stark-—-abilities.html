Yusif Stark — Abilities in full

Yusif Stark — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202611 min read

Card: Yusif Stark

THE DOG THAT DOESN’T BARK

THE DOG THAT DOESN’T BARK is basically presence concealment: the power to never let anything show, not the cut before it lands, not the cold, and not the secret. It is his signature art, one idea, and it is in the line about his cold: he keeps it the way a man keeps a dog that does not bark. Each ability below is that same quiet in a different part of him: the feet, the edge, the mouth and the blood.

Capabilities

A Stark is supposed to be seen coming. The snow stiffens, the timber silvers, the breath of everyone in the room shows, and the house likes it that way, because a border that announces itself gets crossed less often. Yusif took the announcement out of everything he had. His cold stops at his hands. His sword gives no notice. His face gives none. The one thing he knew that mattered, he kept behind his teeth for sixteen years and died with it there.

A barking dog tells you where it is and how frightened it is; the one that says nothing is the one that has already decided.

Applications

  • Cutting with no notice given
  • Keeping the Stark cold from announcing him
  • Keeping his face empty
  • Keeping one secret for sixteen years, to the death

Techniques

  • LET YOUR FEET DO IT - the quiet in the feet, a body that fights before the head decides
  • THE THING NOBODY WROTE DOWN - the quiet in the mouth, one promise kept
  • STARK RIME - the quiet in the blood, the cold held to his hands and blade
  • THE LONG CLEAN LINE - the quiet in the edge, one cut with nothing shown first

Associations

  • The Body Path, Mastered, does the feet, the line and the cold
  • His Walked Fate holds the thing kept; nothing is routed through his Studied Spirit
  • The Stark cold, the house’s inheritance and no Wellspring, is the lens for the rime and the edge
  • The feet are drill made into law, and the thing kept is his own sworn word

Limitations

  • Every clean line chills his own steel toward breaking
  • The thing he keeps can never speak up for him

Traits

LET YOUR FEET DO IT · Primary Trait

LET YOUR FEET DO IT is basically muscle memory combat: the power to fight below thought, the body moving before the mind decides. “You’re thinking. Stop thinking. Let your feet do it.” He lived it, and it is what he put into a salt-cutter’s boy with an ash stick from the age of eight.

Capabilities

The plain account is the one any drill-yard knows: a thing done ten thousand times stops needing the head. The scholars call it automaticity, which only means the body has the whole movement stored and runs it without asking, the way a man walks downstairs in the dark. It is faster than choosing, because choosing takes time and the stored movement takes none.

It is also quieter. Nearly every warning a fighter gives, the glance at the target, the shoulder that turns a hair early, the breath caught before the swing, is the mind getting ready and the body leaking it. Fencing masters call that leak a tell, and a blow that carries one is said to be telegraphed. Take the deciding out and the leak goes with it, so there is no moment of making up his mind for anyone to see.

In Yusif this stopped being a habit and became a law of his Shell (the body-side layer of the soul, the part that turns what is inside a person into force in the world). While it runs, his Reflex reads a step high and his cuts come without a tell. The boy’s body still moves before he decides, every time.

Applications

  • Answering a blow faster than choosing allows
  • Cutting with no warning on the cut
  • Teaching it, until a child’s feet stop asking the head

Associations

  • The Body Path, as a strike, through the Shell
  • Drill made into law
  • The Kharven breath, in through the nose until the ribs ache and out through the mouth until nothing is left
  • The boy he taught with an ash stick

Limitations

  • It answers what the body has met before
  • A fight that needs a new idea needs the mind back, and the instant the mind comes back, the step and the silence both go
  • It costs nothing, because nothing is being made or thrown
  • It has no tell, and that is the point
  • What anyone watching sees before it starts is the one piece of ritual he allowed himself, the breath, and then a face with nobody at home in it
  • A fight he has to think his way through beats it: ground he has never stood on, a weapon nobody has shown him, a question asked in the middle of the exchange
  • Scholars call that the centipede’s trouble, after the creature that walked perfectly until somebody asked it which leg went first

THE THING NOBODY WROTE DOWN · Secondary Trait

THE THING NOBODY WROTE DOWN is basically interrogation immunity: the power to keep one unwritten promise so that neither fear nor pain gets a word of it out of him. It formed the morning after the cellars, when he quit Gimbzo and never rode with him again.

Capabilities

A contract is not the paper; it is the thing both sides know that nobody wrote down, and he kept one for sixteen years: the boy’s blood, his mother’s road, the name. This is the Fate he walks, and it is small Fate, the kind anyone can make: a vow, with terms, that the soul itself holds him to.

Nobody witnessed it and it needed nobody. His sheet carried the line where only he could read it. A kept vow is a bond, and a bond that is fed every day for sixteen years sets hard. What it does for him is narrow and absolute: while he keeps it nothing gets it out of him, not fear and not pain.

Applications

  • Keeping the boy’s blood, his mother’s road and the name behind his teeth
  • Holding his silence under fear and under pain

Associations

  • His Walked Fate, as a binding
  • His own sworn word, kept sixteen years
  • The morning after the cellars

Limitations

  • Its limit is the keeping: it is a silence, and a silence cannot defend itself except by staying silent
  • It cannot argue, cannot explain him to his own house, cannot tell his nephew why he rode away
  • It could not ask anybody for help on the road where he died
  • It takes no reserve, and it buys him no step
  • Its tell is that he says nothing at all, and when the question comes close his hand goes flat on the nearest child’s head, or would
  • To beat it, ask him in front of the one he is keeping it from
  • He will walk away rather than lie to the boy, and a man who has to walk away has been moved

STARK RIME · Inherited Trait

STARK RIME is basically personal cold: the power to carry the Stark cold on a short lead, as frost on his knuckles and along his blade and no further. It is an inheritance of the house and no Wellspring, and his nephew’s whitens a whole yard.

Capabilities

The cold in the Stark blood does one thing: it moves heat out, from the warm thing to the cold one, through him and down through his heels into the earth, which can take more than any man can give. Near a Stark, heat leaves everything, timber silvers and snow stiffens.

In him it ran quiet, a rime on the hands and the blade and no further, the way a man keeps a dog that does not bark. Holding it that close is harder than letting it run. The cold wants out the way water wants downhill, and a Stark who is angry or afraid whitens the room without meaning to. Yusif’s never got past his wrists in front of two children in all the seasons he came down, and that tells you what he was like inside.

Applications

  • A thread of cold along the edge in THE LONG CLEAN LINE
  • Frost on his own knuckles thick enough that a wound he has taken does not show in his hands
  • Standing in a room without whitening it

Associations

  • The Stark cold, the house’s inheritance, with no Wellspring behind it
  • The Body Path, as a shaping
  • THE LONG CLEAN LINE, which it runs along
  • Ulf Stark, his nephew, who lets the same cold run

Limitations

  • The cold moves heat and cannot make any
  • Where everything around him is already frozen hard it has little heat to take
  • Against a real fire it loses
  • At rest it takes no reserve
  • Its tell is that his breath stops steaming
  • Heat and calm beat it

Techniques

THE LONG CLEAN LINE · Signature Technique

THE LONG CLEAN LINE is basically sharpness manipulation: the power to put the whole of him behind one cut along one line, with nothing wasted and nothing shown first, and then it is over. He lived his way to it and has it Mastered. The boy has it now, and nobody alive calls it by this name but him.

Capabilities

The visible charge is small and it is all there is: the rime on the flat of the blade draws in toward the edge, a white thread running from the cross to the point, and his back heel settles. Then the body goes in order. The hips turn first, the shoulders follow, the arms come last, and each joins while the one before is still moving, so that the speeds add and the edge arrives carrying all of them. A carter cracking a whip is doing the same thing with leather.

Along that same line he sends the working. He pulls Aether (mana) up through his heels out of the ground he stands on, packs it raw in his Shell as Charge, and puts the whole of it, the coat a practitioner ordinarily wears spread thin over body and blade, into the last finger’s width of the edge for the length of one stroke. At his rung a Body fighter commands matter as an object, which means the thing his hand is on does as it is told, and what he tells the sword is to be an edge and nothing else.

That is why the cut does not ask the steel. The market blade is only a ruler laid down for the line to follow. What parts the harness is the Charge along it, B-Grade on the edge from a man whose hands read a mean of 125.

Applications

  • Parting worked harness that stops the plain steel of a market sword
  • Ending an exchange in one stroke

Associations

  • The Body Path, as a strike
  • STARK RIME, a thread of it along the edge
  • LET YOUR FEET DO IT, which takes the warning off the cut
  • The road sword, a plain market blade he has never bonded

Limitations

  • It is one cut, and a man who sees the line can step off it
  • There is no second stroke folded inside the first
  • Each cut takes about two percent of his reserve
  • A little over three fifths of what he spends gets to the edge, which is his yield, and the share that burns in his Shell comes off as cold, because he is a Stark and that is the shape his furnace takes
  • The cold goes into the nearest thing, which is the sword, and steel chilled hard enough stops bending and starts cracking; smiths call the point where it turns the brittle change, and a blade on the wrong side of it breaks like a pane
  • Every clean line leaves his sword a little nearer to glass, which is the true price of the art and the reason he wears through a blade every couple of years and leaves the old one with a farrier
  • A blade that has had too many winters of it may be the thing that gives
  • Its tell is one cut, no flourish, with the white thread on the edge and the back heel settling before it
  • To beat it, do not be where the line ends: break the measure as the heel sets, or crowd him so close the hips have no room to turn
