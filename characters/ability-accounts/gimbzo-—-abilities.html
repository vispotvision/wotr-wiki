Gimbzo — Abilities in full

Gimbzo — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202626 min read

Card: Gimbzo

THE WHOLE BURNING

THE WHOLE BURNING (全焼 · Zenshō) is basically all-out destruction: the power to hit everything as hard as he can, with a breaking that never stops where he hit. It was his signature art, and his Traits, his techniques, his Releases and his Domain are this one idea at different sizes. The eye it aimed by and the Work it ended with were the Kōkan blood’s and not his own making; he was the one who put his fire behind both.

Capabilities

Every piece of it is the same act: commit everything, and let nothing give. It runs on three lenses, an eye, and a fire that is none of them. Fractura, the Crack, is the idea that everything has a place where it will go, and a soul that has become that idea finds the place and sends the ending to it: the blow goes to the fault, and the thing comes apart along a line that was already in it, where the same blow sent to sound material would only shake it. The Measurewrights call it stress concentration and will show you on a cracked tile how the load crowds at the crack’s tip; their figure for how much is good, and they have no idea why a soul can find the tip. Coagulatio, the Seed, is the idea of settling round a seed, and its operation is coagulation. In his Shell the whole of what he could raise set around one grain, the striking knuckle, at one instant, so that nothing was early and nothing was spread: a thing gathered to one point is all of it in one place. A hammer drives a nail that a hand’s push never would, and that is the comparison every Kōkan yard uses. Judicium, the Scale, did one job in him, which was to rank: every fault in a field listed worst first. The limit sat at the contact: one point, one instant, and nothing held back to steer with afterward.

It all rode one blow, and the blow was real: bone and iron and the weight of a locked body arriving first. Coagulatio set it to a point as it landed, so the first damage was the plain one, the thing struck staved in at the place it was struck. Fractura carried what was left out along the target’s own faults, so the second damage was the thing coming apart on lines that were already in it. Then the Work of Ruins took hold, if there was rot there to take hold of, and ended what was already ending. What followed was weight and nothing stranger. When one member of a structure fails, the load it carried falls on its neighbors, and neighbors that were sound enough for their own share are not sound enough for two, so the failure walks. Builders call it progressive collapse, and it is why a gatehouse follows its gate and a street follows its wall.

The fire has no lens. Seven parts in ten of what he spent reached the thing he struck, which is his yield, and the other three burned in the furnace of his Shell and came off him as heat, light and noise, and at his rate of flow those three parts were enough to run snow off a hillside. A cleaner soul at his Stage would have burned low and quiet, and the Kōkan before him were not hot at all. The vow of the black shut the red line’s own Wellspring-heat out of his Crystal, so his furnace had no hearth to bank it in and burned in the open, and the Overchannel the vow fed pushed more through the Shell than the Shell could pass. What the north called the King’s Fire is a Body soul’s furnace with its door off: what burns when a man spends everything, every time, and does not care what the spending costs. It is real heat, it burns real things, and he shed almost none of it away from himself.

Applications

  • Breaking walls, gates, keeps and anything massive, brittle and already carrying a load
  • Ranking a field by where it is weakest and going to the worst of it first
  • Breaking a levy line with the weight of his coming, before he reached it
  • Ending a rotten thing so that everything leaning on it goes after it
  • Fighting past his own ceiling on a vow
  • Laying four hundred yards of ground where everything struck either holds or breaks

Techniques

  • AS HARD AS HE COULD - a blow that is all of him
  • FOREST FIRE - an intent so whole that it arrives before he does
  • THE GOLD AT THE EDGES - an eye that ranks a field by where it is weakest and aims him at the worst of it
  • THE BLACK - a vow that lets him spend past his own ceiling
  • SHINGAN - the Moto sight the gold sits in
  • HAMETSU NO GŌ - what happens when a blow like his is given a thing that was already ending
  • TETSUTSUI - the body locked into one piece behind one knuckle
  • ENJŌ - the First Release, which sets his own body alight with it
  • NOBI - the Second Release and the Domain, which lay the same refusal over four hundred yards of ground
  • SHŌDO - the Third Release, which burns down everything in reach that can be burned, and he is in reach

Associations

  • Fractura, the Crack, finds the place a thing will go and runs the blow out along its faults
  • Coagulatio, the Seed, sets the whole of what he can raise around one knuckle at one instant
  • Judicium, the Scale, ranks a field worst first
  • The Body Path does the blow; the Fate Path, which he walked, carries his Pressure, his vow and his Domain
  • The Kōkan blood gives the gold in the eye and the grip on the Work of Ruins
  • He had no bonded weapon; he struck with his hands and with whatever the field put in them

Limitations

  • He could throw nothing less than everything, at twice the rate a man of his Stage should spend
  • The breaking took whatever stood nearest him: his line, his Crystal, and the woman who loved him
  • There is no hidden charge anywhere in it: his intent goes out ahead of him, the black of his aura cracks and shows ember-red, the ground breaks around what he is aiming at before he has thrown anything, and his eyes go gold at the rims
  • For the set the whole body locks, heels to fist, for one breath in which he is planted and cannot turn
  • His ground is the hard kind; loose sediment, marsh and open water starve Fractura and Coagulatio both, and the last field of the war was mud
  • A sound thing with no fault in it gives the Work nothing and takes only the hit, and a thing that will not stand gives the hit nothing either
  • Against a higher Stage it thins from the outside in: the Pressure does nothing to a soul that outweighs his, the field falls to a stronger Domain, and a fighter who can read him has three moves to be elsewhere, which leaves only the blow
  • It is beaten by reading him, which anyone can, and being somewhere else; by giving way, or carrying the blow through the body into the ground; by standing where nothing is rotten; or by putting the Kōkan red on him and taking the vow out from under it
  • Coagulatio fails as the many seeds: start more than one grain setting at once and the reserve thickens in the arms with no way out, which is why he never learned a second blow, a combination or a feint
  • Fractura’s sight has no outward-only setting, and force sent into a thing that will not take it comes home along the striker’s worst fault, which in him ran from the breastbone to the back of the left eye
  • Judicium ranks whatever is put on it, and when he put his own line on it, it ranked them
  • Heat that is not shed stays, and a body run past its ceiling for forty years is a field that has been burned every spring and has nothing left under the ash

Traits

He carried two Trait risks. AS HARD AS HE COULD hardened into the only thing he knew how to do, which is Rigidity. His Shear Break condition was wearing red before he had made it worth wearing, which he never did.

AS HARD AS HE COULD · Primary Trait

AS HARD AS HE COULD is basically all-out force: the power to put the whole of himself into every blow. It is a commitment made in the Core before the body moves.

Capabilities

Before he ever tried anything, his soul committed all of itself to it. Every blow he threw carried the whole of the force he could raise at that instant, Coagulatio drawing it to one point and Fractura running it out along the target’s faults, and nothing he struck took a partial blow. Against a thing that stood and took it, his Penetration counted a full step higher on the hidden scale.

It could not be argued with from inside. By the time he might have chosen a lighter hand, the whole reserve had already set around the knuckle. The doubled spending is the same fact seen from the purse: a fighter who feints pays for the blows he lands, and he paid in full for every blow he began.

Applications

  • Breaking whatever stands and takes the blow
  • Putting the full weight of a strike behind the Work of Ruins
  • Folding iron gauntlets like tin, one pair to a blow

Associations

  • The Body Path, Mastered, in the form of a strike
  • Coagulatio sets the force to one point, and Fractura runs it out along the target’s faults
  • TETSUTSUI is the blow it was built to throw, and ENJŌ raises it as far as his Body IV will carry it

Limitations

  • He could not throw anything less: no feint, no test and no blow held back
  • His reserve emptied twice as fast as a man of his Stage should have spent it
  • It has no tell, because he never once disguised a blow
  • Its limit is the target’s willingness to stand: a thing that gave way, rolled, or let the blow pass through it into the ground took far less than he sent
  • What is committed must arrive somewhere, and when the thing in front of him declined it, the force often went back into him
  • It is beaten by yielding, so that the blow spends itself on nothing
  • It hardened into the only thing he knew how to do

FOREST FIRE · Passive Trait

FOREST FIRE is basically killing intent: the power to be felt coming before he is seen. It is the same commitment as the blow, made visible.

Capabilities

His Shell put out his intent ahead of him the way a fire puts out heat ahead of the flame. A soul that has already decided has nothing left to hide the decision behind, and a Shell as thin and lucid as his passed intent as cleanly as it passed force. His Pressure rode out on it, so that a levy line a field away felt the weight of a thing already coming and already certain, and most men broke before he arrived.

Naiser Yukari, meeting the son, thought only that the Kōkan King had fought like a forest fire, and the north had been saying it for forty years before that. His son reads as a page with no ink on it. Gimbzo read like a forest fire.

Applications

  • Breaking levies before he reached them
  • Emptying a wall with nothing more than the black coming over a rise

Associations

  • The Fate Path, Walked: Aura Pressure and presence, his by right of the Path and unchosen in how they show
  • No lens; it is a claim, carried on his Pressure
  • His thin, lucid Shell, which passes intent as cleanly as force

Limitations

  • It cost him nothing, and gained him a great deal in Pressure
  • His intent was readable three moves out by anyone with a reading sense; Shigure Tenrai, who stood behind him twenty years, said so
  • No ambush he ever planned came off, and he stopped planning them
  • Anyone who could read him and keep their nerve could be somewhere else when the blow arrived, and that is how it is beaten

THE GOLD AT THE EDGES · Inherited Trait

THE GOLD AT THE EDGES is basically weakness detection: the power to see where everything is weakest, listed worst first. It is the Kōkan blood in the Moto heart-eye.

Capabilities

The gold is the first seam of the Kamigan, the crown eye that takes what the other eyes see and ranks it, and in him it never went further than the seam. What it added to the common Shingan was order. Where any Moto sees every fault in a wall at once, he saw them listed, the worst at the top, and went to the top of the list.

In forty years Shigure never once read the red line in his eyes, in a fight or out of one, even the night he went down into the cellars after it. At the end, at Shimane, his eyes went gold to the edges.

Applications

  • Finding a gate’s rotten hinge above its sound one
  • Picking the weak company out of a field of Zettari
  • Knowing who in a hall will break first

Associations

  • The Kōkan blood; it spends his own share and pulls nothing down
  • Judicium does the ranking, on top of SHINGAN’s Fractura sight
  • The Kamigan, of which the gold is the first seam

Limitations

  • It resolved fault and load and ranked them; it could not show him intent
  • It could not show him that what he was ranking was his own house: a scale weighs what is set on it and has no opinion about whether it should have been
  • Holding his Shingan open with it lasted near fourteen minutes from full, and at the end it was open all the time
  • Held open without rest it paid out as heat behind the eyes and the gold creeping outward from the rims
  • Its tell is gold coming up through the gray of his eyes
  • It is beaten by giving it nothing whole enough to read

THE BLACK · Secondary Trait, a vow

THE BLACK is basically oath empowerment: the power to fight past what his body can bear, bought by swearing off the red. He swore it before his captains after what the Recension made of the red line.

Capabilities

He swore that he would not wear the red line’s color until he had made it worth wearing again, and he wore black lacquer, black lacing and black everything for twenty years. The vow bound his Attraction Layer to a red he would not touch and fed the Overchannel that let him fight past his ceiling: a step of Overchannel inside the two-step stack. The step it bought was real, and was most of how a body of sixty stood in front of the Zettari for the war’s last four years.

A vow’s price shows on the body at once, and this one showed as the fire. The red line’s heat, shut out of the Crystal by his own word, had no hearth to sit in and burned in the open, so the man who would not wear red burned red at every seam when he pushed. It was never broken. He died in black.

Applications

  • Buying a step of Overchannel for twenty years
  • Standing in front of the Zettari at sixty

Associations

  • A vow, small Fate, spending his own share and pulling nothing down; a binding laid on himself, which anyone may swear
  • No lens
  • The Overchannel it feeds, and the King’s Fire it sheds
  • The black harness, which in twenty years never showed one thread of red

Limitations

  • It was paid in a Crystal that never once in twenty years let the red line’s own Wellspring-heat into him cleanly
  • Its tell is that there is no red on him anywhere
  • Its terms are its counter: put the Kōkan red on him, and the vow breaks and takes the Overchannel with it
  • The terms were never read to an enemy, because his captains were the only witnesses and none of them would have dared, so the one clean counter went the whole war unused
  • A red sash thrown over his shoulders would have done what forty years of spears could not
  • Wearing red before he had made it worth wearing was his Shear Break condition

Techniques

SHINGAN (心眼 · Shingan, the heart-eye) · Bloodline Faculty, Moto

SHINGAN is basically structural perception: the power to see where everything is carrying its weight. It is the Moto sight, inherited, and his was the Kōkan Shingan with the gold in it.

Capabilities

Any Moto can see where a wall wants to fall; the gold in it is the Kōkan’s alone. His was wide and loud and always aimed outward. Aimed outward, it gave him the gate’s worst hinge and the company that would run.

Applications

  • Reading where a wall, a gate or a company will break first

Associations

  • The Moto blood; a bloodline eye gives no Path
  • Seen in him through his Fractura
  • The Kōkan gold in it opens the road to the Kamigan
  • THE GOLD AT THE EDGES, which ranked what it saw and in the last years held it open

Limitations

  • It could not see intent, and it could not see itself
  • It never once in sixty years turned round to show him the crack that ran from his own breastbone to the back of his left eye
  • It cost him little until the last years
  • Its tell is the eye going still
  • A sound wall, a fresh company or a fighter with nothing flawed to offer gives the eye a blank page and leaves him only his fists

HAMETSU NO GŌ (破滅の業 · the Work of Ruins), the Obsession face · Bloodline Art, Hataraki

HAMETSU NO GŌ is basically ruin inducement: the power to end what is already failing, and in his hands the ending ran on past it. The Kōkan had Hataraki from the Head of Fate; Gimbzo had the Work of Ruins and nothing that tempered it.

Capabilities

A Work is an inherited grip on one function, and this one’s function is ending: what is already failing, at the seam where it fails, and nothing sound. That is why the price is so small. He reached for nothing that was not already on its way, the weight gathered in the fault did the carrying, and the grip only found the seam and held it while the blow went in. His Dominion Fate was nothing, so the Work in his hands never once closed on a sound thing.

The Attraction face ends only what is already dead and stops at the edge of the rot. The Obsession face ends what was rotten and then what was near the rot, and it does not stop, and that was the face he worked in. The house’s law is that a long life in one grip becomes a long life in its worse face, and the bearer does not notice.

With all of AS HARD AS HE COULD behind the strike, the dead thing did not merely come apart along its fault. It went all at once, everything that had been leaning on it took its load in the same instant and failed in its turn, and what leaned on that failed after. No thread was pulled for any of it. The Work ended one rotten beam, and plain weight and a king who would not close the edges ended the granary.

Applications

  • A gate, and the gatehouse after it
  • A wall, and the street under it
  • A granary at Hiroe, with the children still inside

Associations

  • The Kōkan grip, inherited; no Wellspring
  • Fate where the grip takes hold, at Fate III, the Spinner’s rung, with the Work of Ruins for his grip, and the breaking done by Body through Fractura
  • His Dominion Fate against the target’s Persistence and Continuity, and his was nothing
  • AS HARD AS HE COULD, which put the full weight behind it
  • SHŌDO, which is this face given a whole field

Limitations

  • The Work itself closes only on what is already failing; a sound thing gives it nothing
  • It took about two percent of his reserve a strike, and the cost to the field was without limit
  • Its tell is the ground cracking around what he aimed at, not only under it
  • It is beaten by standing where nothing is rotten, or by having someone close the edges faster than it can run
  • It has no failure of its own beyond the face: a breaker ends what was rotten, then what was near the rot, and then does not stop, and by then the people who could have told him were in the cellar

TETSUTSUI (鉄槌 · Tetsutsui, the Iron Hammer) · Signature Technique

TETSUTSUI is basically an absolute punch: the power to land one blow with the whole locked body behind one knuckle. It is the art’s one blow, and he came to it by living it.

Capabilities

The lock is the technique. A loose body loses a blow at every joint, a little in the ankle, the knee, the hip, the spine, the shoulder and the wrist, each one a soft link that takes up force in bending. With every one of them braced, the ground under his heels and the knuckle at the far end are the two ends of a single iron bar, and the whole mass of him arrives at once, with the earth behind it for an anvil.

Coagulatio then does to the Charge what the lock has done to the bones, setting all of it around the one grain at the one instant, so that nothing is spray and nothing is early. It hit like a ram in a gate. The ground behind his heels was found cratered after, because the bar pushes back as hard as it pushes forward.

Applications

  • Breaking a gate the way a ram does
  • Folding the iron hands like tin, one pair to a blow
  • Thrown inside NOBI, at a thing forbidden to give, it was the blow the whole art was built to deliver

Associations

  • The Body Path, Mastered, in the form of a strike
  • Coagulatio sets the force to one instant at one point
  • AS HARD AS HE COULD, which means there is never a lighter one
  • The iron hands, black-lacquered gauntlets that never lasted

Limitations

  • It took about five percent of his reserve and delivered force at his Penetration of 440, and he could throw one every turn until the reserve gave out
  • The whole body sets before the strike; the set takes a breath, and in that breath he is planted and cannot turn
  • A fighter who read him, and anyone could, was out of the line before it landed
  • During it, the answer is to give way, roll, or carry it through the body into the ground
  • After it there is a breath when everything he had is spent forward and nothing is left behind it

Releases

He called all three with one word, “Moyase,” burn it, in the Kōkan’s old tongue, roared. Called silent they came weaker, and he never once called them silent. He had no bonded weapon.

ENJŌ (炎上 · Enjō, the Burning) · First Release

ENJŌ is basically a heat aura: the power to set his own body alight with the fire he sheds. The north called it the King’s Fire.

Capabilities

On the word the release opens the furnace door. The three parts in ten that a quieter soul would bank and let burn slowly come out through every seam of the aura at once. The black of his aura splits along every seam and the ember-red comes up through it, and heat pours off him so that snow runs and lamp-flames lean away for twenty paces. AS HARD AS HE COULD rises as far as his Body IV will carry it. In ENJŌ it is his own body that will not give.

Applications

  • Raising AS HARD AS HE COULD as far as his Body IV will carry it
  • Bringing heat for twenty paces round him, as weather and not as a weapon he aims

Associations

  • The Body Path: the transformed body, opened at Stage IV
  • No fire lens; the heat is his own furnace, unbanked
  • The black harness, heat-crazed down both arms from it

Limitations

  • It lasted a little under eleven minutes from full
  • The body under the harness ran hot enough to craze the lacquer down both arms
  • The heat cooked him first, because he did not shed it
  • Cold and distance beat it, and so does its own heat: a fight that keeps him burning and keeps him at arm’s length is a fight he loses to his own skin
  • Called silent it would have come weaker

NOBI (野火 · Nobi, the Field Fire) · Second Release

NOBI as a Release is basically field projection: the power to send the fire out of himself and into the ground. The north called it the Wildfire.

Capabilities

As a release it changes where the refusal lives. In ENJŌ it is his own body that will not give, and in NOBI the ground is told the same thing. The fire leaves him and runs out across the ground to four hundred yards, and the field burns without burning anything, and nothing in it can give way. It opens and powers the Domain of the same name.

Applications

  • Opening the Domain and paying for it

Associations

  • The NOBI Domain, which it opens and powers
  • ENJŌ, the same refusal held in the body

Limitations

  • It lasted about four and a half minutes from full
  • Called aloud it formed as it was named, in about six seconds
  • He never once called it silent, and by the rule of such things it would have come at half the reach

SHŌDO (焦土 · Shōdo, Scorched Earth) · Third Release

SHŌDO is basically a suicidal attack: the power to burn down everything in reach that can be burned, himself among it. He called it twice in forty years that anyone lived to tell.

Capabilities

The fire goes inward as well as out, and everything within the field and within him burns down to what will not burn. It is the Work’s Obsession face given the entire field at once. Where a strike of HAMETSU NO GŌ ends one rotten thing and lets the weight walk, SHŌDO lays the ending on everything inside NOBI together, and the only things left standing are the ones with no fault in them at all.

Applications

  • Ending everything that can be ended in reach

Associations

  • A Third Release, opened at Stage IX
  • HAMETSU NO GŌ, whose Obsession face it is
  • NOBI, whose field it fills

Limitations

  • It emptied him in about forty seconds from full, and his own body’s reserve was among what it ended
  • By the law of the stack it fills the whole two-step cap alone, with nothing left over for the vow to add
  • A thing with no fault in it at all is left standing
  • He had faults: the first calling he survived whole, and the second ran home along the worst line in him, breastbone to left eye
  • The second calling fractured his Crystal along the line his son carries, and it stayed Fractured

Domain

NOBI (野火 · Nobi, the Field Fire) · Domain

NOBI as a Domain is basically law imposition: the power to lay down ground where nothing is allowed to give way, so everything struck in it either holds or breaks. Nobi is the old word for the fire set in early spring to burn the dead grass off a field, and the name is the whole of what he thought he was doing to the north.

Capabilities

A field set alight runs ahead of the wind, and nothing standing in it is spared because it is green. Inside NOBI his doctrine outranked the ground. Like every Domain it is a claim laid on the ground and pulls no thread: it adds no force to any blow and moves nothing that had no way of happening. What it takes away is the give.

A struck body ordinarily spends most of a blow in moving, in the flex of a shield arm, the step back, the roll of a shoulder, the slide of a boot. The field forbids every one of those, so all of a blow’s work is done on the thing struck and none on the air. Every blow struck inside it, his own and everyone else’s, landed whole, with no yielding, no rolling with it and no passing a load down into the earth.

Called aloud it formed as it was named, the red running out along the ground from his feet in a ring like fire through stubble, some four hundred yards out from him, with no flame in it anywhere and the roar of one over all of it. For forty years it was the most terrible ground in the north to fight on.

Applications

  • Breaking a levy line that cannot recoil, cannot buckle and re-form, and cannot be driven back a pace and hold there: it stands or it bursts
  • Giving TETSUTSUI a target that is forbidden to give

Associations

  • The Fate Path, Walked: a claim on conditions, authored in the Core and opened at Stage VIII
  • No lens of its own; his doctrine is the law
  • The Second Release opens and powers it
  • Its reach and hold are read from his Dominion Radius and Sovereignty

Limitations

  • It held about four and a half minutes from a full reserve, and took about six seconds to form
  • It served him only while he was the harder thing in the field
  • The field’s law has no exception for the man who laid it: inside NOBI he cannot give way either, his own blows cannot be pulled, and a blow that lands on him lands whole
  • A stronger Domain beats it, and so does breaking his reserve or his attention
  • There is one lawful road through it: do not give way, and pass the whole weight through your bones into the ground, which obeys the field’s law to the letter
  • His son learned that kneeling under the Lawbell; Gimbzo never did
