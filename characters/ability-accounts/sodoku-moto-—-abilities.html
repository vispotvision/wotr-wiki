Sodoku Moto — Abilities in full

Sodoku Moto — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202611 min read

Card: Sodoku Moto

AKAI ITO (赤い糸), the Red Thread

AKAI ITO is basically Threads of Fate Manipulation: the power to make the threads of fate visible as red string, and to tie them together or cut them apart. It is his signature, the one idea his Traits, his moves and his blade each express in their own way.

Capabilities

There is an old saying in the south that two people meant to meet are tied at the little finger by a red thread nobody can see, and that the thread may tangle and stretch but never breaks. Sodoku’s soul has taken that saying for its law. The idea he works by is the oldest idea any marriage-broker or peacemaker ever lived on: whatever has truly met stays met. Its operation is conjunction, two things joined into one and the joining set down where the world can weigh it. A soul that has become that idea stops seeing people and things as separate; it sees the threads between them first, the way a weaver looking at cloth sees the warp before the pattern. When he calls the art, the threads come out of hiding as fine red strings in the air of the room, and for as long as he holds it, everyone there can see what he sees.

What reaches the world is Attraction Force, the binding principle under every vow, every lineage and every Domain, the thing that lets one presence acknowledge another and keeps that acknowledgment true. Sodoku’s blessing lets him lay his hands on it more freely than his blood alone would allow. He can tie two threads, and then the two things they belong to must meet: a blade and a throat, an arrow and its mark, a lost man and the road home. He can loosen a knot, and two things meant to meet miss each other by a hair. And because a fighter’s allies, his Domain and his own Wellspring are all held to him by the same force, Sodoku can cut those threads too, and a man who had friends, ground and power a moment before stands alone in the world.

The glyph’s limit is the road. He can only tie what already has a way to meet. Two things with nothing between them, no road, no reason, no chance, cannot be knotted, because Fate moves only what already has a way of happening. The flaw follows from the idea: a knot has two ends, and he is always holding one. Everything he ties runs a thread through his own soul, and everything he cuts is cut a little in him as well. Its contrary is Obsession Force, recognition gone to possession, which is his own brother’s road; and a soul severed from everything, which gives him nothing to tie.

Applications

  • Making a blow, an arrow or a meeting arrive where it must
  • Making a blow meant for him or his miss by a hair
  • Drawing a whole field’s threads to one crossing
  • Tying an enemy’s fate to a single ending
  • Cutting an enemy away from allies, ground and power
  • Feeling across any distance whether someone he loves is in danger
  • Reading every bond in a room, who is tied to whom and how hard

Techniques

  • EN-MUSUBI - the principle as sight and instinct, always on
  • INOCHIZUNA - every bond he holds become a lifeline, waking when one of them is in danger
  • THE UNCLOSED REACH - his blessing, the cap lifted off his reach
  • THE KATANA - the blade he carries, and the road the art rides into a fight
  • DEAI - his blade tied to a target’s thread
  • SURECHIGAI - a knot loosened so a blow misses
  • TSUJI - every thread on the field drawn to one crossing
  • MUSUBIME - an enemy’s fate tied to one ending
  • HANARE - one bond cut
  • HITORI - every ally in reach cut away
  • KODOKU - cut from everything

Associations

  • Benediction, the Grace, makes a bond freely given weigh as a real thing, so the knots he ties for those who love him hold hardest; Coagula, the Mending, brings scattered things back to one table
  • The Fate Path does the tying and cutting through the Attraction Layer; the Spirit half lets him read the souls on the ends of the threads
  • The Kōkan blood opened his Fate early, and his blessing, a grip, lifts its cap
  • His brother Sonzai is the contrary he loves; Tenma spreads along the threads he ties
  • His father’s beads, jade and iron, are in his hand whenever he pulls

Limitations

  • He can tie only what already has a road between it; a meeting with no way to happen cannot be knotted
  • He is one end of every thread he touches, so anyone who reads fate well reads everything he holds, and can pull on him through it
  • He feels every bond he cuts, and cutting someone he is tied to cuts him too
  • At Fate I, 0.60 to 0.70 of what he spends on a real pull arrives, and every pull ties a knot, and the knot comes due as luck turned against him, in a strap that snaps or a plan that fails for no reason anyone can find
  • The tell: his father’s beads come into his hand, the three ring-lines of his eye tighten, the air round his hands warms, and the red threads show themselves a breath before he moves
  • His aura is the warm red-gold of the Grace, with a gray-amber seam of the Mending under it
  • It is countered by a soul with no bonds, by a severing art that cuts the thread before he ties it, by possession that refuses to let a thread be read, and by reaching him before the threads show
  • Under strain it fails the way a weaver’s loom fails, by tangling: he ties more than he meant, and two things he wanted apart find themselves knotted together

Traits

EN-MUSUBI (縁結び), the Tying of Fates · Primary Trait

EN-MUSUBI is basically Node Manipulation: the power to see where things are going to meet, and to tie, tighten or untie those meetings. It is his Primary Trait, always on, and AKAI ITO starts from it.

Capabilities

Sodoku sees the world as threads of fate crossing each other, and wherever two cross there is a knot, a node: a sword and a throat, a cart and a bridge, two people who are going to meet at a gate. He sees those knots before anyone else does. He can pull two fates together so they meet, tie a knot so tight the meeting cannot be avoided, or slip one loose so two things miss each other by a hair. He never forces anything; he only ties what already has a road between it, so the world never feels pushed. It simply seems to have been going that way all along, which is why they call him the Author of Fate.

Applications

  • Knowing which door someone will come through, and being there
  • Arranging that two people meet, or never quite do
  • Tightening the knot between his blade and an opening
  • Seeing a betrayal coming as two threads drawing together that should not

Associations

  • Coagula, the Mending, whose old habit is scattered things coming back to the same table
  • His Dominion Fate and Gnosis Perception, the two Sub-Stats he leans on hardest

Limitations

  • Always one end of the knot; when a bond he tied breaks, he feels it go
  • It reads only the threads; what a person chooses on the end of one is still their own

INOCHIZUNA (命綱), the Lifeline · Secondary Trait

INOCHIZUNA is basically Bond Empowerment: the power to grow stronger whenever someone he is tied to is in danger. It woke the night Mizuyi was taken, when her thread went south pulled tight and he felt every inch of it go.

Capabilities

Every bond he holds is a lifeline. When someone on the end of one is threatened, he feels their thread at any distance: which way it runs, whether it is fraying, whether they are hurt or afraid. While they are in danger his body and his power rise to meet it, faster, harder to stop, his knots pulling tighter. The more people he stands for, the stronger he gets.

Applications

  • Following a captive’s thread across a country
  • Fighting above himself with his family behind him
  • Knowing the moment a loved one is hurt, and roughly where

Associations

  • Benediction, the Grace, which makes the lifelines of those who love him run strongest
  • His Harmonics Axis and Empathy, where bonds are held

Limitations

  • The line runs both ways: their fear and pain come to him through it
  • If someone on the end of a lifeline dies, he feels that too
  • It wakes only under real danger to someone he is truly tied to

THE UNCLOSED REACH · Blessing

THE UNCLOSED REACH is basically Limitless Potential, given: the power to keep climbing the ladder of Fate past where his blood would stop him.

Capabilities

The Moto blood opens Fate early and, with no Work behind it, stops it at the second rung. Sodoku was born with the cap lifted, as far as his Stage allows and never past it. On his window it reads as the giver wrote it:

[Blessing: The Unclosed Reach]
[Your reach upon what binds will never close.]

Limitations

  • A blessing can lie, because the giver wrote the line
  • It lifts the ceiling and nothing else; every rung is still climbed, and every pull still paid for

Techniques

THE KATANA · Blade (all swords)

THE KATANA is basically Swordsmanship: Sodoku can use any blade, and he fights with the katana.

Capabilities

He was taught the sword in the yard at Kharven-Seat like every Kōkan child, and he fights with a katana in the house’s way: a long two-handed draw, an even, unhurried guard, and a cut that comes out of the draw itself. He moves before he decides to, which on a sword reads as a blade already moving when the other man’s foot is still in the air. The sword is the road his art rides. He does not need a heavy cut, because DEAI has already decided that the cut arrives; he needs only a clean one, and he keeps it clean. He handles a straight sword, a saber or a long knife well enough to win with them, but the katana is the blade his hands go to.

Associations

  • DEAI ties the edge to the opening before the cut leaves the scabbard
  • His Dexterity Finesse and Reflex, and his father’s way of keeping the edge off the scabbard wood

Limitations

  • He is a boy of thirteen with a boy’s arms; a grown swordsman can beat his guard by weight
  • His art places the cut; it does not make the cut heavier

DEAI (出会い), the Meeting · Move of AKAI ITO

He ties his blade to a target’s thread, and the cut has to arrive. Dodging only changes where it lands. Its limit is the road: a target with no way to be reached, behind a wall that will not open, cannot be met. Its price is a small knot, which comes due within days.

SURECHIGAI (すれ違い), Passing By · Move of AKAI ITO

He loosens the knot between an attack and its target, and the blow misses by a hair. It works on what is aimed at him or at anyone he is tied to. A blow that was never going to miss by much, a wide swing or a falling roof, cannot be slipped far enough.

TSUJI (辻), the Crossroads · Move of AKAI ITO

He pulls every thread on the field to one point. Enemies collide, and arrows meet arrows in the air. It is a large pull for a boy of his rung, and it leaves a knot he will be paying off for a season.

MUSUBIME (結び目), the Final Knot · Move of AKAI ITO

He ties an enemy’s whole fate to one ending, so every move they make leads back to it. It is the move his brothers’ arts land on: Sonzai’s calamity cannot miss a man tied to one spot.

HANARE (離れ), Apart · Move of AKAI ITO

He cuts one bond, and an ally cannot reach them: a shield-man stumbles, a messenger takes the wrong road, a Resonant partner loses the other’s rhythm.

HITORI (一人), Alone · Move of AKAI ITO

He cuts every ally in reach, and help always arrives too late. He feels each cut, and if any of those allies were his own, he loses them too.

KODOKU (孤独), Solitude · Finisher of AKAI ITO

He cuts them off from everything, their own power included. Their Domain collapses, their Wellspring goes silent, and they stand in the world as if nothing has ever known them. It is the one he cannot take back: nothing cut this way comes back to the one who lost it.
