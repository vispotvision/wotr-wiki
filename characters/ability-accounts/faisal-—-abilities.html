Faisal — Abilities in full

Faisal — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202621 min read

Card: Faisal

I DO NOT LET THINGS FALL · Signature art

I DO NOT LET THINGS FALL is basically oath embodiment: the power to give a kept promise back its shape, so that the shape bears weight. Every promise anybody ever kept is a great gold hand, and he catches the whole world with them. The Midreach Choir calls the act Visualization, the First Technique, and his Traits, his techniques and his Domain are all that one act done at a different size.

Capabilities

An oath sworn and kept is a real thing. It stays written in the outer layer of the world after the words are gone, the way a footprint stays in clay after the foot has lifted, and a Midreach Celestial can read that record and make it stand up: a promise kept becomes a hand, a gate held becomes a wall, a deathbed vow becomes a light that will not go out. It is no illusion. What stands up has exactly the weight of what was sworn. The Choir gives the art to every Oath-Keeper at the Singing, the way a mortal child is given a tongue, and in most of the Choir it is a modest art, the oaths of one hall or one battlefield, a door that will not open. Faisal carries every oath sworn on honor under the Lawbell for eleven thousand years and every one sworn in the Material for seven hundred more, so in him the same clerk’s art fills a sky.

This is the Fate Path, the hardest one, worked through the Attraction Layer, the outermost of the soul’s three layers, the one that holds a soul’s share of fate. His lens is Order: the Choir’s Order Magic, the law the Lawbell Sang into the Host, and no Wellspring. Order does one thing under all its uses. It makes things agree, with their word, with their nature and with each other. It can settle particles that have been shaken apart back into their places the way a shaken sieve sorts sand from gravel, lay the panic in a fighting heart flat as still water, or come down on a field as the weight of every kept promise on the backs of those who mean to break one. He pulls Aether (mana) out of the place he stands in like anyone else, and his anchored Crystal turns it to Essence (spiritual energy), so about seventeen parts in twenty arrive as gold, which is his yield, and the rest is paid on his Layer as the knot every pull ties, though all anyone standing near him feels of it is a honey warmth.

He does not ride a blow of his own, because he throws none. A hand takes the stroke on its palm and holds it there, and the stroke is not stopped in the hand; it is passed along, because Order makes the weight agree with the ground it is going to. Masons know the shape of it from the arch, where weight is not resisted in any one stone and runs down through all of them to the footing. A kneeling body is the best footing a man has, low and wide and braced in three places. A pressure meant to crush him ran through him into the plain and left a fathom of new stone under his knee. Against disorder the hands do better than hold, because ordering it is what feeds them. They close on a grain of chaos and set it in its place, as a weaver lays a dropped thread back into the warp, and they grow brighter for every grain.

The bigger works are the same law. THE STRAIGHT LIGHT is Order made of light: one breath twisted until every strand of it agrees, so that the light keeps one step and falls in rods in place of spreading. The Measurewrights call it coherence, and say ordinary light is a crowd, every wave at its own pace, which is why it spreads. The rods converge on one seed point, and order spreads from the seed the way ice races across a pond that was cold enough to freeze and only wanted a first crystal.

Applications

  • Catching what falls, from a bird to a world
  • Taking a blow and passing its force into the ground
  • Holding an enemy still without striking him
  • Setting disorder in order, grain by grain
  • Making a spoken word weigh what it was sworn to weigh

Techniques

  • VOW-LIGHT - the warm heavy gold he gives off, under which a lie is hard to say
  • I DO NOT LET THINGS FALL, the Trait - the soul’s reflex that puts a hand under whatever is falling
  • THE BOUND FLAME - the kneel under the weight of every oath in the world
  • FAIETH - the Thousand Hands, the kept oaths given back as gold palms
  • THE STRAIGHT LIGHT - a move of the art itself: one long breath twisted until every strand of it agrees, and the sky cracks from rim to rim and lets down gold light in rods as straight as plumb lines, under which a whole world finds lying hard to think
  • THE AXIOM OF THE KEPT - one premise set under everything, with four works called under it
  • TALGRIM - choosing the order in which things happen
  • DIVINE FIST · PARU 86 · COMBINATION - every open hand closed, once, at the price of the Crystal
  • OTHSAL - the Domain, the record itself opened and hung in the air to be read

Associations

  • Order, the Choir’s Order Magic, the law the Lawbell Sang into the Host, is the lens, and there is no Wellspring in it
  • The Fate Path, mastered, worked through the Attraction Layer
  • The Midreach Choir, the Oath-Keepers, who call the act Visualization
  • Vow-Cant, the script with no grammar for a lie, and Parunic, the script the Archons’ lattice is read in; when he writes glyphs, on the Axiom’s works and on the fists, each one tells the working what it is for
  • He has no Release, because a Celestial’s Crystal was anchored and not grown, and no bonded weapon; he has carried no weapon since the Bell cracked
  • His aura is Vow-Light, a warm heavy gold like late sun through honey, with a pale silver line where his wings meet his back

Limitations

  • Only what was sworn and kept has any weight to give, so the hands can catch, hold, set down and press, and have nothing in them that strikes first
  • A man who promises nothing gives OTHSAL nothing to bind
  • Vow-Cant has no grammar for a lie, so he cannot feint, bluff or hide what he is about to do
  • Every hand he puts out is a hand that is not under the Bell, and the great works are paid in pieces of him that do not come back
  • THE STRAIGHT LIGHT takes about half of what he came down with, in one breath
  • The pale silver line between his wings is the gauge, and it thins as he spends
  • Because there is no Wellspring in it, there is no glyph on the plain hands and no Wellspring failure
  • A watcher sees it first: he sets his fingertips together before his breast and breathes in, and a ring of Vow-Light turns round him at the height of his heart, once, twice, faster, with a bell’s note held under water
  • Then the hands come, out of his shoulders and back when the catch is his soul’s own reflex, out of the ground on long gold arms when he sends them, with a rushing sound like a tide
  • The air goes warm, heavy and very clear, liars cough, grass and dust lie flat away from him, and corrupted ground under the ring goes dull and stiff and stops spreading
  • It is beaten by saying nothing and promising nothing, by making him choose between two falling things, by swearing two oaths of equal weight that cannot both be kept, or by making him stand and keeping him standing
  • The named failure is the Midreach Paradox, the old wound of his Choir: two oaths of equal weight that cannot both be kept, sworn where he can hear them, are an instruction his Crystal cannot resolve, and every hand stops where it is and the Domain seizes with him inside it
  • He broke one such seizure by binding himself in Vow-Cant as the difference between every impossible pair and paying for both out of his own substance, which is an answer and a ruinous one
  • Order is made against Chaos and cannot outlive it: the one who embodies the other thing is his exact weight, feeds his hands and is fed by them, and can never be beaten by this art, only met
  • One thing was seen and put down unused: THE STILLED WORLD, a single rule written under every law, no oath may contradict itself; it would have won the plain and ended every creature’s freedom to change its mind, and him with it

Traits

VOW-LIGHT · Inherited Trait

VOW-LIGHT is basically truth inducement: the power to make a lie hard to say. It is what every Celestial’s Aether carries, and in him it comes at a Grand Angel’s weight.

Capabilities

It is a heavy, warm-pressure radiance under which a spoken lie becomes physically hard to say: a labored breath, a tightening throat, a headache that will not lift until the speaker takes it back. In the Midreach it reaches further than in any other Choir and compels truth across the whole room rather than discouraging it in one man. People near him find themselves wanting to tell him things.

Poured out at the scale of THE STRAIGHT LIGHT it stops being a room’s discomfort and becomes a world’s: under a whole sky of it a falsehood is hard to think, let alone say, and a thief forgets why he meant to pretend.

Applications

  • Making a room tell the truth
  • Drawing a confession nobody asked for
  • Making up the gold of the hands of FAIETH

Associations

  • Order is its lens, the Host’s sung law, with no Wellspring
  • The Fate Path
  • His Celestial nature, the Midreach Choir

Limitations

  • It makes a lie cost and does not detect it; a man willing to pay can still lie to him, once
  • It costs nothing and takes no reserve
  • Its tell is that people near him want to confess
  • It is beaten by saying nothing at all

I DO NOT LET THINGS FALL · Primary Trait

I DO NOT LET THINGS FALL, the Trait, is basically reflexive rescue: the power to have a hand under anything that falls within his reach before he has decided anything. “It is the only thing I was ever made to do.”

Capabilities

His soul does it before it does anything else. Whatever is falling within his reach, a man off a cliff, a boy out of his own body on the Skeldmere, a city wall, a world, a gold hand is already under it. The catch is a reflex of the soul and no choice: a lark that dropped out of the sky was in a gold palm before it had fallen the height of a man. It has a reach, too. When a city came down a hundred leagues off, a dozen hands rose out of his shoulders and back of their own will and hung open in the air with nothing in them.

While he is catching, his Anchoring and Fortitude read two steps high on the hidden scale and nothing he holds can be torn out of his hands. He also hears every oath sworn on honor, even at the edge, even under the Bell’s weight; he heard Muken’s at the woodpile and Yukazuri’s last one in the Shirogane house, and every word of both was true.

Applications

  • Catching a bird, a boy, a city wall or a world
  • Holding a thing so that nothing can tear it out of his hands
  • Hearing every oath sworn on honor, and whether it was true

Associations

  • Order is its lens, with no Wellspring
  • The Fate Path: a kept oath called up as a hand
  • The catch fills his cap of two steps by itself

Limitations

  • He can catch, and hold, and set down; he does not strike first, and a thing that is not falling he can only stand in front of
  • The catch takes no reserve
  • Its tell is that a hand is already under you
  • It is beaten by making him choose which of two falling things to catch

THE BOUND FLAME · Secondary Trait

THE BOUND FLAME is basically burden bearing: the power to carry the weight of every oath in the world so that it does not come down on what is underneath. It began when Malakar cracked the Lawbell.

Capabilities

A cracked Bell could no longer hold the weight it was built for, and that weight was every oath sworn under it for eleven thousand years. Faisal went to the edge of creation, to the place where the threads run, took the weight onto his own shoulders, and has knelt there ever since. While he kneels, the oaths of the world have somewhere to rest. Malakar has gone to the edge more than once to break him, and he is still kneeling.

The kneel is a working posture and no penance: one knee, one flat hand and the bowed back make a road for the weight to go down. His body goes to it by habit, even landing.

Applications

  • Giving the oaths of the world somewhere to rest
  • Passing a crushing weight through his body into the ground
  • Teaching the same road: he taught it to Muken as the Work of Bearing Load

Associations

  • Order is its lens, with no Wellspring
  • The Fate Path, the load carried through his own body
  • The Lawbell, whose weight it is
  • TALETH, the Axiom’s first work, which is the same thing as the kneel

Limitations

  • Its limit is the kneeling itself: standing, he is only borrowing time from the weight
  • It costs everything he does not spend elsewhere, and has for seven centuries, which is why he comes to Muken only when he is needed and when he can, and why what kneels at the edge does not go far from it
  • Its tell is that he does not stand up
  • It is beaten by making him stand, and keeping him standing

Techniques

FAIETH · the Weight Kneeling · the Thousand Hands · Signature Technique

FAIETH is basically giant hand constructs: the power to fill the air with great gold hands that catch, hold and press. It is a Host-name, the kneeling and the weight; mortals who have seen it call it the Thousand Hands.

Capabilities

Every oath he carries is a hand that was given, and he gives them back. The air around him fills with hands of solid gold Vow-Light, colossal, a man’s height from wrist to fingertip, open-palmed and held out, hundreds of them at once, rushing out from him in a tide like a wave breaking sideways. Each hand carries what it catches into the ground the way his own knees do.

Turned on an enemy, they come in from every side, open, and close, pressing the weight of every kept oath down onto one body from all directions at once until it cannot move, cannot breathe, and is held. The ground under the target cracks in a ring and the target goes to its knees, the way he has knelt for seven hundred years.

Applications

  • Catching what falls and setting it down
  • Taking a blow on the palm and holding it there
  • Closing on an enemy from every side and pressing him to his knees
  • Feeding on disorder: what the hands close on they set in order grain by grain, and they come away brighter and heavier than they went in

Associations

  • Order is its lens, with no Wellspring
  • The Fate Path: the kept oaths called up as hands
  • Vow-Light, which the hands are made of
  • Mastered

Limitations

  • The hands only hold, catch and press; there is no fist among them that strikes first
  • He has never used them to kill
  • Keeping them out takes about two tenths of one percent of his reserve a breath, about five hundred breaths from a full reserve
  • Its tell is the air filling with gold palms, open, held out
  • It is beaten by the Choir’s old wound: present him two oaths of equal weight that cannot both be kept, and every hand stops where it is

THE AXIOM OF THE KEPT · Technique, with four works under it

THE AXIOM OF THE KEPT is basically axiom manipulation: the power to set one premise under everything where he stands, so that everything built on it follows. The premise is: what is kept, is.

Capabilities

He says one word, “Axiom,” and white script comes up across the back of his right hand. It answers an enemy who feeds on correction. A steersman who corrects late overcorrects, and each correction swings wider, so he stops correcting and sets a single premise as the first stone of the law where he stands. Everything built on it follows, and nothing downstream needs correcting. The script is Parunic, and each glyph tells the working what it is for.

Applications

  • Passing a load through him into the ground
  • Starving a storm
  • Voiding a contract made under the knife
  • Running one consistent line back to its source

Techniques

  • TALETH · the Tally’s Weight ([Th] Foundation, [Grv] Gravity, [Ur] Balance) - the oldest thing he knows, older than the Thousand Hands: a load need not stop in him, it can pass through him into the ground, and it is the same thing as the kneel
  • DAHLREN · the Walked Boundary ([RenB] Boundary, [Ur] Balance) - makes the fast and the slow round a storm agree; a storm lives on difference, a fast current beside a slow one, and with the differences leveled it has nothing to tear at
  • SARNRUTH · the Seal Entered ([Sel] Measure, [Va] Law, [Vo] Voice) - weighs the intent in a contract by the oldest rule of contract: a yes given under the knife carries no intent, binds nobody, and is entered void
  • OTHANTHA · the Unbroken Line ([Len] Line, [Ho] Order, [Ir] Continuum, [Ed] Edgetruth, [Ora] Truth) - every path is tried at once and only the kept one stands, the one consistent line running back to its source; the Measurewrights tell it as the principle of least action, the paths that disagree cancelling crest against trough

Associations

  • Order is its lens, with no Wellspring, written in Parunic glyphs
  • The Fate Path
  • The kneel and THE BOUND FLAME, which TALETH is

Limitations

  • It holds only where he stands
  • Each use puts a little of him into the foundation for good; the first took two fingertips, which went clear as glass
  • Its tell is the one word, “Axiom,” and white script running down the back of his right hand
  • It is beaten by making him go on paying

TALGRIM · the Order of Precedence · Technique

TALGRIM is basically event rearrangement: the power to choose what order things happen in. It is the quiet art under every other art of the Midreach, tal the tally kept and grim the order in which things are entered, and the thing about him the Host feared more than his hands.

Capabilities

Every event is a member of a set, and every set can be arranged. He cannot unmake a verdict or stop a thing happening, and he can decide which falls first. It works the way an army breaks step on a bridge: the same blows in an order that never falls in time with itself.

Applications

  • Breaking the step of his own shaking Domain: the same true verdicts in a sequence that never fell in time with itself

Associations

  • Order is its lens, with no Wellspring, written [Len] Line, [VaM] Moment, [Ho] Order
  • The Fate Path

Limitations

  • It changes order and nothing else
  • At a Domain’s scale it costs the rearrangement by hand of a hundred thousand moments a breath, and the small vessels of his eyes, which burst and run
  • Its tell is the rhythm of the room going ragged and blood running from the corners of his eyes
  • It is beaten by patience: a patient enemy learns any new rhythm

DIVINE FIST · PARU 86 · COMBINATION · Last Technique

DIVINE FIST · PARU 86 · COMBINATION is basically a sacrificial final attack: the power to close every open hand at once, for the first time in eleven thousand years. It can be thrown only by a Grand Angel who has broken his own Crystal with his own hands.

Capabilities

It is called in three breaths, clearly and without raising his voice: “Divine Fist.” “Paru Eighty-Six.” “Combination.” Breaking the Crystal pulls everything he carried into his body at once, with the hope of mortals poured in where the Crystal was. Every palm of FAIETH folds into a fist, Parunic script comes up across the knuckles in white fire, and the fists take up the fourteen stations of the Archons’ lattice, so that the whole field is one sentence of law written in three dimensions, eighty-six strokes long, all of them pointing in.

The first comes straight down the center line. The second, the one open hand in the sentence, rises from behind and hauls toward the first. The other eighty-four close from every angle and herd the spent chaos back in on its author.

Applications

  • Herding spent chaos back in on its author

Associations

  • Order is its lens, with the hope of mortals poured in where the Crystal was
  • The Fate Path: the hands called, and the sentence bound
  • FAIETH, whose palms become the fists
  • Parunic script and the fourteen stations of the Archons’ lattice

Limitations

  • His own refusal held even here: the fists closed only on one who had already struck first
  • Its price is the Crystal and everything after
  • Its tell is every open palm closing at once, and he has to break his own Crystal to do it
  • Its counter is the plainest he has, which is not to strike first

Domain

OTHSAL · the Position Held · Covenant Domain

OTHSAL is basically oath enforcement: the power to make every spoken promise physically binding on the ground it covers. It is the art turned inside out, the record itself opened and hung in the air to be read, the only Domain the Midreach Choir has ever grown, and the thing that made him a person rather than an instrument.

Capabilities

He has no Release to open it with, so it is opened by a spoken call, his hands drawn apart: “Domain Expansion. OTHSAL.” It forms as it is named, petal behind petal. The oaths he carries come out of him as script: lines of gold Vow-Cant, fine as hair and bright as wire drawn hot from a die, every oath ever sworn on honor and kept, unfurling from one point in long curving ribbons like a lotus opening, until the whole space around him is written in them. It takes up every word already spoken on the ground it covers and hangs them in the script like banners, so that a boast made an hour before it opened is a promise inside it.

Inside it, every spoken commitment becomes visibly, physically binding. The air thickens a little each time an oath is sworn, and breaking one parts with a snap like a cord, which everyone present hears whether they speak the language or not. Vow-Cant has no grammar for lying, so nothing false can be written there at all.

Applications

  • Raising whoever swears inside it and keeps it one step in Oath and Anchoring, for as long as they keep it
  • Knocking an oath-breaker to his knees by the weight of what he dropped
  • Turning a boast made before it opened into a promise

Associations

  • Order is its lens, with no Wellspring
  • The Fate Path, mastered: a claim on what a spoken word is worth
  • Vow-Cant, the script it is written in
  • It was grown out of him over eleven thousand years and was never lent

Limitations

  • It binds what is sworn and nothing else; a man who promises nothing walks through it untouched
  • It takes about one percent of his reserve to open, and a slow bleed while it stays open
  • A kept oath inside it lifts him too, but adds nothing to him while he is already catching
  • A thing grown out of him is not separate from him: what shakes the petals shakes his ribs
  • A Domain that is broken returns its held verdicts to the one who grew it, so whatever breaks it breaks his body along the lines it grew from
  • Silence walks through it
  • Its deeper counter is the one every Midreach Celestial fears: two oaths of equal weight that cannot both be kept, sworn inside it, seize the Domain and him with it
