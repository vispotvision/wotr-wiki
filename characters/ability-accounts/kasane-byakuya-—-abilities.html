Kasane Byakuya — Abilities in full

Kasane Byakuya — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202621 min read

Card: Kasane Byakuya

HALF A STEP AHEAD

HALF A STEP AHEAD (半歩先 · Hanpo-saki) is basically attack prediction: the power to see where a blow is going to end and to be standing there already, between it and her king. It was her signature art, and her Traits, her techniques and her Releases are this one idea at different sizes. The oath that pointed all of it at one man is the Byakuya house’s.

Capabilities

Every piece of it is the same act: be at the end of the road before the thing that is traveling down it. A blow is a road, a man’s next movement is a road, and a hall with a king in it is a hundred roads that all have to pass one place. It runs on two lenses, an eye and an oath. Fixatio, the Knot, is the idea of commitment, the bond with no way back, and its operation is coagulation, what is bound set past melting. Scholars set it beside a resin that has cured: a wax can be melted and poured again forever, but a cured resin has grown bridges through its whole body and will char before it flows. When she set her feet, the lens cured her stance into the stone, and for that breath she and the floor were one piece. That is what makes stepping in front of a blow worth doing. A blow carries a moving weight, and a moving weight is stubborn: it is never lost, only handed on, which the Measurewrights call momentum. An unanchored guard who catches a great blow is thrown backward into the man she is guarding, and has saved him nothing. An anchored one hands the whole of it down through her legs into the foundations, and the king feels a wind and hears the floor ring.

Judicium, the Scale, is the idea of truth told apart, discernment that knows a thing by the mark it cannot help leaving, and its operation is separation. Every substance shows its own lines to a trained light, and nothing can show another’s. The lens turned her Forecast and Vigilance on a room the same way, and what came back was the room ranked, worst first, with one thin arc-blue line laid on the threat that would reach the king. The Moto heart-eye added where a body’s weight was gathering before the limb had moved, since every real blow is paid for in advance by a shift of the hips and the back foot that the striker cannot hide. Between the two she did not need to see the blow. She saw the bill for it.

It all rode on blows, hers and other people’s. The guard’s step is a blow received on purpose, and the cut is the same read spent the other way. Nine parts in ten of what she spent reached the work, which is her yield. The lost tenth burned in the furnace of her Shell and left her as heat under the lacquer and as the red in her aura.

Applications

  • Getting in front of a blow aimed at the one she guards, and handing its force down into the floor
  • Ranking every threat in a room, worst first
  • Cutting a man at the place his own movement is carrying him to
  • Seeing where a road, a man, a marriage or a war ends
  • Stepping out from the end of a search before it arrives

Techniques

  • THE RED BEFORE THE WHITE - the house’s oath, which aims all of it at one man and his children
  • UNMEI NO SAMURAI - a body that is already in the way, in the one place every road has to pass
  • DOWN THE ROAD - the read turned on a life, which saw where her marriage went
  • THE GUARD’S STEP - standing at the end of a blow
  • AKAITO - cutting where a man’s next movement ends
  • SHINGAN - the Moto eye that confirms the nearest body
  • FŪIN - the same step taken ahead of a search
  • AKAZONAE - the First Release, which makes the stepping free

Associations

  • Fixatio, the Knot, holds her where she sets her feet and holds the blow where it meets her
  • Judicium, the Scale, ranks every threat in the room and marks the one that will reach the king
  • The Body Path leads: the foot, the lacquer and the cut, the full force of it at contact
  • The red harness of the Samurai of Fate, and the guard’s blade of the red chair, her bonded weapon, both left in the white house
  • Her ground is a guard’s ground: Fixatio loves worked stone, deep foundation and mineral rock, and Judicium wants dry air and a clear line of sight, so a king’s stone hall was the best ground in the world for her, and so was a salt mine

Limitations

  • It only ever puts her ahead of what is coming at someone else: every blow she stops lands on her
  • It has no answer at all when the one she guards is holding the knife
  • Fixatio’s glyph is Topology, which rules only what is joined to what; it can make her and the ground one thing and cannot make that thing stronger than her lacquer and her bones
  • Judicium’s glyph is Measure, which ranks only what is there to be read, so an end a man has not chosen yet has no line on it
  • Fixatio hates open water and anything that moves underfoot, and Judicium goes half blind in fog or behind an iron grille; on a ship’s deck in a sea mist she would have been a very fast woman with a sword and little more
  • The charge is small and always the same, and anyone who knows it can read her: her eyes go past the man in front of her, the near-white of her aura draws in and the red under it brightens along the edges, and the silk-cord note tightens
  • Under her feet the floor takes a faint amber shine for a breath, with a sweetish sharp smell of resin, and those nearest feel a prickle on the skin and a taste of hot copper as the Scale’s line goes out
  • Against a higher Stage the read fails first, because a stronger soul’s movement leaves fainter lines and its Pressure fogs the Scale; the step still arrives, and then the question is only whether her body behind its lacquer can live through what it stepped in front of
  • It is beaten by making the king the knife, by selling her a feint she believes, by changing your mind in the middle of your own movement, by coming at him from where she cannot stand, or by sending more than one body can meet
  • Fixatio fails as Irreversibility: what is set stays set, and a binding placed in error is a permanent feature of what it was placed on
  • Judicium fails as spectral overload, every line in view reporting at once until none can be ranked

Traits

She carried three Trait risks. The oath outliving the king it was sworn to is her Rigidity. Her Shear Break condition was the king as the knife, which happened. Her Reflex and her Oath both sit on the fracture line of her Stage.

THE RED BEFORE THE WHITE · Inherited Trait

THE RED BEFORE THE WHITE is basically oath embodiment: the power to be bound, body and soul, to the protection of one man and his children. It is the house’s and not her art: every Byakuya child has it said over them, and her sister carries the same words.

Capabilities

It was said over her at nine with her grandmother’s hand on her head: purity before power, peace before the blade, and the red line before the white, until the end of the red line. It bound her Attraction Layer to the Kōkan king and his children, and near him, keeping it, nothing frightened her and nothing got past her.

What it does for the art is aim it. A vow is small Fate, spent out of the swearer’s own share, and this one told her soul whose road she was standing at the end of, so that the step and the read both took their bearing from one man and his children.

Applications

  • Aiming the step and the read at the king and his children
  • Keeping her unafraid at his side

Associations

  • The Byakuya house and her grandmother, who taught the oath over the lamp
  • The Fate Path, Walked: a vow, kept on her own share and pulling nothing down, in the form of a binding
  • No Wellspring; the lens is the house’s oath

Limitations

  • It gave her dominion over the protection of the king and the king’s children, and it had nothing to say when the king himself was the one holding the knife
  • Its terms named the king as the one protected and never as the one protected against
  • She was strong enough to have stopped him in the cellars, and her blade would not leave its scabbard against him, and that is the whole of why she walked
  • Its tell is her lips moving over the words of the oath
  • It is beaten by making the king the knife
  • It has no Wellspring and so no Wellspring’s failure; its failure is its own terms

UNMEI NO SAMURAI (運命の侍 · Unmei no Samurai, the Samurai of Fate) · Primary Trait

UNMEI NO SAMURAI is basically bodyguard power: the power to be, always, the body between her king and whatever is coming. It is the idea itself, before any technique is made of it.

Capabilities

The Samurai of Fate are the guard of the red chair, every one of them a Byakuya or raised by the Byakuya, red lacquer for the red line and white lacing for the white line that kept it. She stood at the Kōkan king’s shoulder in every hall he ever walked into, half a step ahead and to his left, and her soul did that before it did anything else.

Fixatio cured the place into her the way it cures a stance into stone, so that the half step ahead and to the left was where her soul rested when it was doing nothing else, and leaving it was the thing that took effort. Her Reflex and Anchoring held a step higher on the hidden scale while he stood behind her. The step is the lens doing what it does for any anchored thing.

Applications

  • Standing at the one place every road to the king has to pass
  • Holding Reflex and Anchoring a step higher while he stands behind her

Associations

  • The Body Path, shaped by Fixatio
  • The Byakuya guard of the red chair
  • AKAZONAE, which lets this Trait off its limit

Limitations

  • It lends the step only while there is someone behind her to be anchored for; with nobody at her back it is a woman standing oddly in an empty hall
  • It guards from outside; it cannot stand between a man and what is in his own ear
  • It takes no reserve; it cost her nothing, and in the end everything
  • Its tell is where she stands: always half a step ahead of the king and to his left
  • It is beaten by coming at the king from where she cannot stand

DOWN THE ROAD (道の先 · Michi no Saki) · Secondary Trait

DOWN THE ROAD is basically destiny perception: the power to look at a road, a man, a marriage or a war and see where it ends. She could always see further down a road than the rest of them, and it formed into a law the night she understood where Gimbzo’s road was going.

Capabilities

A road has many ends, and Judicium lays its line on the one that the weight of everything already on the road is carrying it to, the way a trained eye picks the one flawed link in a coat of mail. The Measurewrights would call it reading composition from signature. Her sister said it more plainly: she saw things coming that the rest of them did not believe until they were standing in the door.

It is a read and never a pull. It moves nothing, brings no thread down, and costs her no reserve, which is why a soul on her low rung of Fate Mastery, Fate II, could hold it. The night it formed into law she sent four lines to her sister by runner, telling her to stay home, and walked into the salt with the boy before anyone knew she was going.

Applications

  • Seeing the end of a movement, a mood, a marriage or a war
  • Leaving before anyone else understands there is something to leave

Associations

  • Judicium does the ranking
  • The Fate Path, Walked, landing as a read through a Studied Spirit’s eye: a small use that opens no gate
  • Her Forecast, at the top of what her Stage holds
  • AKAITO, which is this Trait at the length of a sword, and SAIGO NO MICHI, which is this Trait made law

Limitations

  • It shows her the end of a road and gives her no way to change it, only to step off
  • It shows an end and offers no other, and the night it formed into law it showed her a cellar
  • It takes no reserve
  • Its tell is that by the time anyone else understands, she has already packed
  • It is beaten by a road whose end she cannot see

Techniques

THE GUARD’S STEP (先の先 · Sen no Sen) · Bloodline Art, the Byakuya guard

THE GUARD’S STEP is basically hyper interception: the power to move before a blow is thrown, so that it lands on her and not on him. It is the Samurai of Fate’s first lesson, taught on the Byakuya training ground.

Capabilities

The training ground’s name for it is the old one, sen no sen, the moment before the moment. A blow is paid for in advance by the hips and the back foot, and the guard learns to move on the payment. Her Reflex and Celerity put her across the line of a strike between one breath and the next, and the blow met the guard’s lacquer and not the king.

What Fixatio adds is the arrival. Her feet cure to the floor in the instant she lands, so that she and the foundations are one piece, and the blow’s weight, which is never lost and has to go somewhere, goes down through her into the stone and not back through her into him. The read, the crossing, the feet curing to the stone and the lacquer turned to meet the edge are one movement, and the wound is the one she chose over his.

Applications

  • Taking a blow meant for the king on red lacquer
  • Getting in front of any blow in the north, if she sees it coming
  • Stepping for nothing while AKAZONAE holds

Associations

  • The Body Path, shaped by Fixatio and aimed by the read
  • The Byakuya guard, who taught it
  • The red harness, which takes the edge at the breast and the right shoulder, where it has been relaid in patches

Limitations

  • It is a movement and a guard, and it deals no wound
  • Each step took about half a percent of her reserve, the price of the cure, set and broken again, and nothing she noticed until she had done it a thousand times
  • The blow still has to be taken on the harness and survived
  • It answers only a blow she sees coming; a feint she believes moves her out of the way of the real one
  • Its tell is that she is already moving
  • Before it, the counter is to show her a blow she will believe; during it there is nothing to be done, since she is already there
  • After it she is rooted for the length of a breath, and a second blow from another quarter finds her still set

AKAITO (赤糸 · Akaito, the Red Thread) · Signature Technique

AKAITO is basically trajectory prediction with a blade: the power to make one cut at the place a man has not reached yet. It is DOWN THE ROAD at the length of a sword.

Capabilities

She does not cut where the man is. She looks past him, down the road of his next movement, to where it ends, Judicium ranks the end that will reach the king, and she cuts there, so that the man arrives at the end of his own movement and the blade is already in the place he arrived. An archer leads a running deer the same way, loosing at the place it will be and trusting the deer to keep its appointment.

The cut is a plain cut and the damage is a blade’s, driven by her Penetration and a long arm of gray-bright steel. What the reserve buys is the force laid along the edge and the certainty of the place. Because the man is still arriving when the steel is already there, his own speed is added to hers, and the faster he came the worse it is for him. Both damages are real: the edge opens what an edge opens, and the blow follows it down the line of the one cut, laid along an edge and not thrown wide, and takes what is behind that.

For the length of the cut a thin red line hangs in the air from the scabbard’s mouth to the end of the man’s movement, with Judicium’s arc-blue fringing it. She never called it aloud, and the Hall never saw the blade out at all.

Applications

  • Ending a threat with one cut from the scabbard and back
  • Turning an attacker’s own speed against him
  • Beating Instrument plate

Associations

  • The Body Path: one cut from the scabbard, in the form of a strike, aimed by the read
  • Judicium picks the end
  • The guard’s blade of the red chair
  • She came to it by living it

Limitations

  • It took about six percent of her reserve
  • It cuts the end of a road she can see; a man who changes his mind in the middle of his own movement, or does not know himself where he is going, gives her an end that moves
  • It fails against Storied plate
  • A guard who cuts second cuts once
  • Its tells are her eyes going past the man to where he will be, and the red line, which is the whole of the warning
  • It is beaten by a road whose end changes

SHINGAN (心眼 · Shingan, the heart-eye) · Bloodline Faculty, Moto

SHINGAN is basically initial movement sense: the power to see where the next blow’s weight is gathering before the limb has moved. It is the common Moto sight, inherited, held low and quiet in her.

Capabilities

Every real blow is paid for in advance by a shift of the hips and the back foot that the striker cannot hide, and the heart-eye shows her that weight as it gathers. The Scale ranks the room, and the eye only confirms the nearest body in it.

Applications

  • Confirming the nearest body’s next blow

Associations

  • The Moto blood’s eye; no lens, and it spends her own share and pulls nothing down
  • Judicium, which does the ranking it only confirms

Limitations

  • In her it never passed Drilled, because she never needed it to

FŪIN (封印 · Fūin, the Salt Seal)

FŪIN is basically self-sealing: the power to shut her own soul so that there is nothing left to find. It is the last piece of HALF A STEP AHEAD: she saw where his search would end, and was gone from the end of it before he got there.

Capabilities

The Byakuya guard has never let the seven lines see what stands at the king’s shoulder, because the guard’s strength known is the guard’s strength planned for. GLASS was her sister’s; Kasane hid the plain way, by never once letting anything reach the king that she would have had to show herself to stop. When she walked into the salt she could not hide that way any more. A Crystal like hers changes the air round it: frost goes strange, flames lean, the fate-readers feel it from a valley off, and Gimbzo was looking for her with every eye he had.

So on the third gallery of the Orrel Cuttings, with the boy asleep in a salt niche, she turned Fixatio inward and tied the knot round her own Shell, three turns and a fourth no Byakuya had tied before. The Crystal shut to the lowest rung of the Aether ladder, the Dormant, sealed and silent, with no stat output above Hollow and nothing for a reader to find.

Fixatio’s flaw is that commitment cannot tell a true binding from a mistaken one, because the way back is closed for both alike; the Measurewrights call it irreversibility, and every other working of hers courts it as a risk. The Seal is that flaw chosen on purpose and used as the working: a knot whose undoing no longer exists as a place she could reach, which is the whole of why a fate-reader a valley off found nothing to read. Salt rock is deep, still, mineral ground, the kind Fixatio loves best, and the third gallery cured it harder than any hall floor would have.

Applications

  • Vanishing from every eye a king could send after her
  • Keeping her son hidden with her for sixteen years

Associations

  • Fixatio, turned inward on her own Shell, on the Body Path
  • The Byakuya knot, with a fourth turn of her own
  • The salt rock of the Orrel Cuttings
  • Her vow to herself, on the third gallery, never to be found

Limitations

  • It cost everything she was, at once, for good, and it has no clean undoing
  • She never tried to undo it, and cut salt at a mortal’s strength for sixteen years
  • She dropped her own humming token down a dead shaft, because a thing that hums can be heard
  • It was never an enemy’s to find; what beat it was sixteen years of salt dust in a mortal woman’s throat, and she died of salt-throat at forty-two with the Seal still tied

Releases

The only thing she ever called aloud was the one word, “Mae,” before. She was a guard and a legend and she did not shout.

AKAZONAE (赤備え · Akazonae, the Red Harness) · First Release

AKAZONAE is basically protection mode: the power to make the guard’s place true of every step she takes, so that nothing she steps in front of costs her anything. The word is the old one for a company armored all in red, and the release is the harness worn on the soul.

Capabilities

Called, it is the one word, said no louder than she says anything. The red comes up through the white of her aura like blood through a bandage, the silk-cord sound of the harness lacing becomes a thousand cords drawn tight at once, and the field goes the color of old blood. Reflex, Anchoring and Celerity strike a step high on the hidden scale. THE GUARD’S STEP costs her nothing while it holds, the cure which cost half a percent each time setting and breaking for nothing.

People in a hall with her have said afterward that they could not remember her moving, only that she was in front of each thing in turn.

Applications

  • Stepping in front of everything, for free, for four minutes
  • Raising Reflex, Anchoring and Celerity a step

Associations

  • The Body Path: the flesh and the blow transformed, through Fixatio
  • UNMEI NO SAMURAI, the Primary Trait let off its limit
  • THE GUARD’S STEP, which it makes free

Limitations

  • It lasts four minutes from full
  • Called silent it comes weaker, the red showing only at her throat and wrists
  • The red stays in her face for a day afterward, the lost tenth of four minutes’ spending, the furnace’s heat with nowhere to have gone under the lacquer
  • Time beats it
