Léonard Lambert — Abilities in full

Léonard Lambert — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202611 min read

Card: Léonard Lambert

COUNT TRUE

COUNT TRUE (Numera Vere, the house words in the Court’s Latin) is basically numerical precision: the power to have already counted you, your army and your next move, with a count that is never wrong. It is his signature art, and it is the two words on the Lambert banner, which his soul took for its law. Every ability below is that law working at a different size.

Capabilities

A true count is a count made before the thing happens, said out loud, and never softened. The Lamberts have sat at the Sea’s bench beside the cleric for as long as the Sea has had courts, counting what the testimony is about.

The blood counts a blow before it is thrown, the eye counts a field before it is fought, the list counts the men who gave a false number, and the sword pays out the answer. He calls nothing by a technique’s name. His call is the number, said politely, with a tap of the pommel for every thousand.

Applications

  • Counting a blow before it is thrown
  • Counting a field before it is fought
  • Counting the men who gave a false number
  • Telling a general his own numbers in front of his captains

Techniques

  • ONE BEFORE - the blood’s count of a blow before it is thrown
  • COUNT TRUE - the eye’s count of a field, said aloud
  • THE STRUCK LIST - the count of the men who gave a false number
  • THE SALLOWGATE ANSWER - the sword that pays out the answer

Associations

  • The Lambert inheritance and the house words are the lens; there is no Wellspring behind any of it
  • The Body Path does the Answer and carries the blood’s lean on his Reflex
  • The Spirit Path does the count and the list
  • The Lamberts’ seat at the Sea’s bench, beside the cleric

Limitations

  • The count judges the number and never the reason
  • The Answer takes about one and a half percent of his reserve each time
  • The rest costs no reserve and is paid in people, and there are four notches under his pommel to show it

Traits

ONE BEFORE · Inherited Trait

ONE BEFORE is basically intent perception: the power to start his answer a hair before the blow it answers. Across the south the blood’s gift is called the Lambert count, or the bench, and it is the thing in the Lambert blood the house does not discuss.

Capabilities

A great many Lamberts are born with Crystals that never wake and fight anyway, reading a blow before it is thrown with no faculty to read it with, so that a spectator calls it luck and a practitioner calls it impossible. Léonard woke, and the old gift woke with him. Every answer he gives begins before the delivery it answers, by a margin so slight it looks like chance, and men he has fought swear he struck first. While he is facing a blow with a will behind it, his Reflex reads a step high on the hidden scale.

The Measurewrights (the Guild’s scholars of measurement) have an account, which the house has never confirmed. A blow with a will behind it is decided before it is thrown. The body makes ready first: the weight settles on the driving foot, the breath sets, the shoulder loads, and all of that begins a good part of a heartbeat before the hand moves, which is what fighting men mean when they say a blow was telegraphed. Under the body, the deciding itself comes earlier still. A trained eye reads the shoulder. The scholars say the Lambert blood reads the deciding, the will at the moment it commits, which is why an unwoken Lambert with no trained faculty can do it at all, and why the south calls the gift the bench that reads a blow before the shoulder commits to it.

Lamberts who carry the gift tell it the other way round: the count sets the shoulder’s first commitment against ten thousand blows it has seen before. Both accounts are told at Sallowgate, and the house confirms neither.

Applications

  • Answering a blow before it is thrown
  • Putting the Sallowgate Answer in place so early that it looks like the first stroke
  • Reading a lie told knowingly, since it has a will behind it

Associations

  • The Lambert blood, an inheritance of the house and no Wellspring
  • The Body Path, as a lean on his Reflex
  • THE SALLOWGATE ANSWER, which it puts ahead of the blow

Limitations

  • It reads intent, and nothing else
  • It is slower against a thing with no will in it: a falling wall, a beast in a panic, a working that runs by itself
  • Where nobody decided anything there is nothing to read early, and he is left with only his ordinary quickness
  • It costs nothing, because nothing is made or thrown, and he no more spends reserve on it than a man spends reserve on hearing
  • Its tell is that he moves a hair before you do
  • A blow with no intent behind it beats it

COUNT TRUE · Primary Trait

COUNT TRUE is basically calculation mastery: the power to look at a field and know its number, men and horses and remounts and the days of grain behind them. It is the house words, become his soul’s law.

Capabilities

He knows the number of any field he can see, and when it matters he says it out loud, in front of everyone, and does not soften it. A field is a set of sums that have to close. So many men eat so much bread a day, so many horses so much fodder, a wagon carries a known load and a road passes a known number of wagons between dawn and dark, and every figure in a return leans on three others. A liar changes one figure and leaves the others standing, and the sum stops closing. Most men cannot hold enough columns at once to feel that.

Léonard’s soul keeps all of them, which is what his Retention is for, and reads where they are going, which is what his Forecast is for. So while he is counting, no lie told about the field gets past him. His Analysis and Forecast read a step high on the hidden scale, and a false return feels wrong in his hand like a cased weight, the hollowed and plugged kind a cheating merchant keeps for his scales, which looks right and lifts wrong.

It is a Trait of his own soul with no Wellspring behind it, a reading on the Spirit side, of what a report means and whether it is true.

Applications

  • Saying a field’s number aloud, never rounded, with one tap of the pommel for every thousand
  • Catching a false return
  • Telling how long an army can last on the grain behind it

Associations

  • The Spirit Path, as a reading
  • His Retention and his Forecast
  • The house words, and the bench-house at Sallowgate where he learned to count beside the clerks

Limitations

  • Its limit is sight: he counts what he can see and what he is told
  • A field he cannot see or a report never made is a hole in the sum, and a sum with a hole in it he will not call a count
  • It has no purchase at all on the men and the grain themselves
  • It costs nothing
  • Its tell is the saying: he gives the number aloud and taps the pommel
  • A field he cannot see beats it: night, fog, a reverse slope, a column that never came onto the roll

THE STRUCK LIST · Secondary Trait

THE STRUCK LIST is basically interrogation intuition: the power to ask for a count twice, so that a man who lies the second time knows exactly what he is doing. It formed the night he struck his own cousin from the house.

Capabilities

A Lambert who gives a false count is struck from the house, and the house keeps the list. Léonard has struck four names in his time, and one of them was his cousin, whom he loved, and who lied about two hundred sacks of grain to keep a village fed through a bad winter. Since then, when a man reports to him, he asks for the count twice, and the second asking carries the weight of the list.

A first answer can be habit, pride, hope or plain bad arithmetic, and a man can give it without ever deciding to lie. The second question takes that away. The man has now been told the number matters, by someone whose own cousin is on the list, and whatever he says next he has chosen.

The house clerks add that a lie told knowingly has a will behind it, and will is the one thing the Lambert blood has always been able to read.

Applications

  • Getting a true count out of a man who reports to him
  • Turning a careless wrong number into a chosen one

Associations

  • The Spirit Path, as a reading
  • The house list of struck names, and the four notches on the underside of his pommel
  • The Lambert blood, which reads the will behind a knowing lie

Limitations

  • It judges the number and not the reason
  • A true reason wrapped around a false number is still a false number, and the list has no column for mercy
  • It takes no reserve
  • Its tell is the second asking, in the same courteous voice, with his thumb on the underside of the pommel where the four notches are
  • A man who gives him a true count, however bad, beats it, because there is then nothing on him for the list to weigh

Techniques

THE SALLOWGATE ANSWER · House Art, Lambert · Signature

THE SALLOWGATE ANSWER is basically counter mastery: the power to turn his blade before yours has left its line, take it on the flat a hand from your guard, and ride down it into the wrist, the elbow or the throat. It is the Lambert counter-cut, and every child at Sallowgate, woken or not, learns it by this name on a wooden copy of the house sword’s balance.

Capabilities

It is one movement with no flourish. The visible charge is small and it is always there: the wrist turns, the gray blade rolls a quarter in his hand, and the pale gold of his aura draws down off his shoulders and lies along the steel as one thin white line.

The place it meets you is the mechanism. A swung sword turns about the hand that holds it, so the point travels fast and far while the steel beside the guard has barely begun to move. An answer that starts early enough can meet the blade there, a hand from the hilt, where it is still slow and has almost no force in it yet, and from there the wrist is a hand’s width away and the elbow and the throat lie on the same line. Any Lambert child can do it against a slow cut. In him, with ONE BEFORE and Glory’s Reflex behind it, the answer arrives so early that men he has fought swear he struck first.

The spending rides the cut. He pulls Aether (mana) up out of the ground he stands on, packs it raw in his Shell (the body-side layer of the soul) as Charge, and the Shell lays it along the edge as the same coat of aura that covers his body, drawn to one place. So two things land together: the sword’s own cut, placed where the harness opens, and the coat on the edge, which is what lets a plain gray blade bite a worked guard.

Applications

  • Taking an attacker’s wrist, elbow or throat off his own blow
  • Biting a worked guard with a plain gray blade

Associations

  • The Body Path, as a strike
  • Sallowgate, where it is taught, and where he has it Mastered
  • ONE BEFORE and Glory’s Reflex, which put it ahead of the blow
  • The house sword, balanced close to the guard for the turn of the wrist the Answer needs

Limitations

  • It is an answer: he has to be asked first, and a man who will not attack him gives him nothing to answer
  • Each Answer takes about one and a half percent of his reserve, about 66 Answers from full
  • About two thirds of what he spends reaches the steel, and the rest leaves him as heat, which is why in the cold he smokes like a horse
  • Its tell is the blade turning in his hand before yours leaves its line
  • To beat it, feint with the whole body, so that the intent he reads is false, and then strike with no thought at all
  • A real decision to throw a false blow reads to the blood as a real blow, the Answer is paid out against it, and the stroke that follows comes off drilled habit with no fresh deciding in it for him to read
