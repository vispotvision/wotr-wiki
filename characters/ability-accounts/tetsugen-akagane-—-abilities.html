Tetsugen Akagane — Abilities in full

Tetsugen Akagane — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202629 min read

Card: Tetsugen Akagane

THE COPPER DRUM

THE COPPER DRUM (銅の鼓 · Akagane no Tsuzumi) is basically heartbeat manipulation: the power to take hold of a beat and keep it. He has held his own heart at twenty-five for forty years, and every heart near him beats when he says. It is his signature art, worked in the Akagane Blood and Heart Arts, and his Traits, techniques, Releases and Domain are all this one idea done at a different size.

Capabilities

He took hold of his own heart first, and kept it at one hour for forty years. With a hand on a man he takes hold of that man’s heart or his blood. With an arrow he takes hold of it at sixty paces, to listen. Released, he stops keeping his own hour and lets the true beat out; released again, the beat becomes a field and every heart in it takes his step; and at the last he keeps or does not keep one named heart. That is the whole ladder: the idea, the transformed body, the world obeying it. The two readings that sit beside it, the long read of what another man would do and the ledger of every heart he ever fed, are the same habit turned on minds and on debts. He puts his own heart in the other man’s place, and he keeps count.

It runs on two lenses. Anima Spirare, the Soul’s Breath, which his house calls the Breath of the Living, is the idea of the breath of the whole: a body that remembers being whole and answers a small hurt with everything it has, exactly where the hurt says. Its operation is fermentation, the whole body quickened round one small signal, and a soul that has taken the idea into its Shell carries that quickening in its own blood and can lend it to another body’s. The Measurewrights explain it by the clotting of blood, a cascade in which each woken factor wakes many of the next, and their figure for how large an answer a small signal draws is good; they have no idea why the body remembers being whole. The Akagane turned the idea from the wound to the heart. A heart is a muscle that fires itself: a small knot of tissue at its top sets the pace, a wave of current runs down through the muscle, the muscle clenches, and for a moment afterward it cannot fire again while it resets. That beat is a small signal that commands a whole body, which is exactly the kind of signal the Breath of the Living answers, so a man who has harmonized with it can listen to the signal, hold it, hurry it, or drown it. Verdantia, the Green, is the idea of growth without end, a self with no final size, the tissue that stays young because it never stops being made, and in him it does one job: it holds the body round the heart to the hour the beat was taken.

He reads Body IV, the rung of law, and still works by contact, the habit of the rung he learned on, which is why almost everything he does needs his hand on the thing: the palm on the breastbone, the hand flat on the chest, the thumb on the vein, the copper head in the flesh. Only the Domain lets go of that, because inside a Domain the field itself is the hand. It all rides one blow, and the blow is a clinch. He lets the first strike come and takes it on a folded forearm, clamps the striking arm against his own ribs, and lays the palm on. Then the same hand is already on the chest for the Warrant, or the thumb is already at the throat for the Blood Road.

Applications

  • Keeping his own body, and the outward face of his Crystal, at the hour he took the copper chair
  • Stopping, holding or keeping another man’s heart with a hand on him
  • Moving the blood in a body he has his thumb on
  • Listening to one heart from across a field, once an arrow has bitten
  • Setting the beat of every heart in a field
  • Reading what another man will do, and keeping count of every heart he ever fed

Techniques

  • THE COPPER HEART - his own heart held at one hour, and his body and Crystal face with it
  • IN HIS PLACE - the same habit turned on minds: what he would do in the other man’s place
  • THE SENNEN BOOKS - the same habit turned on debts: the heartbeat of everyone his granaries fed
  • AKA-TE - a palm on the breastbone in the one instant that breaks a heart’s rhythm
  • HEART-WARRANT - a flat hand that holds a heart still, or keeps a failing one going
  • KETSURO - a thumb on a vein that sends the blood where he wants it
  • KITSUNE-YA - an arrow whose bite lets him hear that heart at range
  • TOSHI-GAERI - the First Release: he lets go of the hour and the true beat comes out
  • KODŌ - the Second Release and the Domain: the beat becomes a field
  • SHINZŌ-DOME - the Third Release: one named heart, stopped by the field’s law

Associations

  • Anima Spirare, the Soul’s Breath, his governing Wellspring, gives him the heart’s own current: what the Akagane keep and what they can stop
  • Verdantia, the Green, holds the body’s renewal at the hour it was taken
  • The Body Path holds the heart and the blood, and the Spirit Path, which he walks, holds the long read and the books
  • The Akagane Blood and Heart Arts, learned at his grandfather’s hands
  • He bonds no weapon; the horn-handled knife is for meat
  • Living ground feeds him: both Wellsprings favor living biomass, temperate air and the nearness of blood, so a granary town, a full hall, a wood or a battlefield serves him well

Limitations

  • The art has purchase on living blood and a beating heart and on nothing else, so the dead and the made are outside it
  • Almost all of it needs his hand, his thumb or his arrow on the target; only the Domain lets go of that
  • It never stops charging him: about a hundredth of his reserve every hour of his life, asleep or awake, and every large use is paid in years that come into the body and stay
  • About a fifth of what he spends never reaches the world, which is his yield, and burns in the furnace of his Shell as blood-heat: a flush under the pale skin, palms hot enough to feel through a coat, and, when he is pushing, the snow round his feet melting pink
  • Sterile ground, extreme cold and soil already drained of Essence starve him; a frozen field is the poorest footing he can have, and he lost the worst day of his youth on the ice under the Grey Teeth
  • The charge is always readable, and at rest it is the slow copper pulse in his aura keeping time with his heart
  • As his reserve falls it shows: at half the copper pulse slows, at a quarter gray comes in at his temples and lines at his eyes, and at a tenth sixty-one years begin to arrive
  • Against a higher Stage the art thins at the grip and holds at the timing: a stronger Sovereignty contests the field at its edge and in its owner’s own chest, a stronger Hemostasis pushes the hand off the heart and holds its own blood where it is, and a heart held still or run ragged by its owner’s own art gives him no window
  • It is beaten by starving him or catching him asleep, by keeping his hands and his arrows off your skin, by giving him a heart he cannot read, a body in great heat or a thing with no heart at all, or by striking the drum, since the field beats to his own heart and falls when it is struck
  • Anima Spirare fails by its own flaw: the whole answers a small hurt with everything it has and cannot answer with less, so held past his reserve in the field or on the Blood Road, with no sense left of where, his own blood starts answering every heart he is holding, clots where nothing is cut, and leaves him with blue lips, a white face and nothing left to close the wound that matters; the Measurewrights call it disseminated activation
  • Pushed to the end, that is a Crystal Fracture Event along the Sennen road, and his own heart stops with the field
  • Verdantia fails by its own flaw, a self with no final size that goes on growing when nothing feeds it, which the Measurewrights call etiolation: starved, drugged or knocked senseless, the hold on the hour goes thin and the years come in at once, and the longer the lapse the more of them stay
  • THE COPPER HEART has run without rest for most of his life, which is Resonance Fatigue
  • Letting the hold lapse long enough that the years stay is his Shear Break condition
  • His Longevity stands at 725, past the fracture line, and the hold is now the thing keeping the Crystal in one piece

Traits

THE COPPER HEART (銅の心 · Akagane no Kokoro) · Inherited Trait

THE COPPER HEART is basically suspended aging: the power to keep his own heart, body and Crystal at one chosen hour. It is the Akagane keeping of a heart, turned on himself the year he took the copper chair.

Capabilities

Anima Spirare holds his heart to the beat it had that year, and Verdantia holds the body round it to the hour the beat was taken: the hair black, the face smooth, the eyes clear, the hands quick. He looks twenty-five, and has for most of his life; the seven lines have watched him sit the copper chair forty years without aging, and do not ask. It keeps the body and never what has been done to it. The scars on his hands stayed, and so did every year of what he did, which he carries like an old man and is tired like one.

A Crystal is a body too, so the same hold keeps the outward face of his Crystal at the density it had that year. That is the Veil. Every assessor who has read him since has read a Stage VII Refraction practitioner at Level 190, and been right about the face and wrong about everything under it. He kept it there because Gimbzo burned whatever in the north was strong enough to say no to him, the Akagane granaries at Hiroe among it, and a Fox who looks harmless lives to sit in council.

Applications

  • Keeping a sixty-one-year-old body at twenty-five
  • Holding off bleeding, wounds and aging
  • Passing for a Stage far below his own
  • Keeping his own heart beating through SHINZŌ-DOME, when it stops with the other

Techniques

  • THE VEIL - the same hold laid over his Crystal, so that it reads as Refraction; it costs nothing more

Associations

  • Anima Spirare for the beat, Verdantia for the hour
  • The Body Path, which he has mastered
  • Inherited with the Akagane blood and its Blood and Heart Arts
  • TOSHI-GAERI, which is this hold let go on purpose

Limitations

  • Its limit is the reserve: starved, drugged or knocked senseless, the hold slips, and the years he has been keeping off come into him at once, gray into the hair, lines into the face and a sixty-one-year-old heart laboring in a young man’s chest, until he can take hold again
  • The longer it lapses the more of the years stay, and with them the Veil goes, and anyone looking sees all of it at once
  • It costs about one percent of his reserve an hour, every hour, asleep or awake, for as long as he lives
  • The slow copper pulse in his aura keeps time with his heart, which no young man’s aura does, and that gives it away
  • He has called TOSHI-GAERI in front of witnesses, and the Veil is gone for everyone on the Kessen road who looked
  • It is beaten by starving him, or catching him asleep

IN HIS PLACE · Primary Trait

IN HIS PLACE is basically behavior prediction: the power to know what a man will do by asking what he himself would do in that man’s place. It is the art’s habit turned on minds.

Capabilities

His soul reads a field by asking what it would do if it were the other man, and it is very rarely wrong. He says so out loud. Forecast and Analysis turn forty years of provisioning, levies and ledgers into the long read. The Keld were bought, with no tithe, seed grain and no Moto boot on their snow, because that is what he would have offered in Karogane’s place; Karogane would not need to win, only to walk out between the lines and shout.

Applications

  • Reading what an enemy commander will do before he does it
  • Working out what a bought man was offered

Associations

  • The Spirit Path, which he walks
  • No Wellspring: forty years of ledgers and the long read

Limitations

  • Its limit is himself: he reads every enemy as a man like him, and a man who would not do what Tetsugen would do, for better or worse, is invisible to it
  • Old Harald Keld moved four hundred riders through a whiteout so close to his column that he heard their horses blowing, in the second year of the war, and he never found out how
  • The Sin of Wrath is not buying anything, and Tetsugen cannot stand in a furnace
  • It costs nothing
  • The phrase gives it away: “That’s what I’d have offered, in his place”
  • It is beaten by an enemy who is not him, one who would not do what he would

THE SENNEN BOOKS · Secondary Trait

THE SENNEN BOOKS is basically life-sign tracking: the power to feel the heartbeat of everyone who ever ate from an Akagane granary, wherever they are. It formed at Dissonance along the Sennen road, and it is the art’s habit turned on debts.

Capabilities

He signed for every sack that fed the columns, in his own hand, with his own seal, knowing what they would do, and Anima Spirare entered every heart that ate from his granaries the way the books entered every sack. He can feel, faintly, at any distance, the heartbeat of anyone who was ever fed from an Akagane store, and whether it is steady, and when it stops: three hundred Starks at the woodpile; the Kurogane tenants, forty-one of them stopped; a Zettari border town that ate Akagane grain the winter before the columns came.

Applications

  • Knowing where the people he fed are, and how their hearts are beating
  • Feeling each one stop

Associations

  • Anima Spirare, through the Spirit Path
  • The Sennen road, the open fracture in his Core that left it

Limitations

  • It is a ledger and no reach: it tells him where those hearts are and how they are beating and lets him do nothing to them at range
  • It keeps him awake, and it costs nothing he can put down
  • His scarred hands turn palm up, as if there were writing on them
  • It cuts the other way too: a reader who counts a man’s dead finds every one of these hearts stacked in his chest, thousands of them, and anything that burns by the weight of what a man has done will go for him before anyone else in the room
  • It has nothing on a man who never ate his bread, who owes him nothing and was never fed by him

Techniques

AKA-TE (赤手 · Aka-te, the Red Hand) · Signature Technique

AKA-TE is basically fibrillation inducement: the power to stop a heart with an open palm, by landing it in the one instant of a heartbeat when a touch breaks the rhythm. It is the plainest piece of the art and the deadliest.

Capabilities

In every heartbeat there is a window, a few hundredths of a second while the heart’s lower chambers reset their charge for the next stroke. A blow over the heart inside that window, and only inside it, throws the whole rhythm into a quivering fibrillation: every fiber of the muscle firing on its own, none together, a heart that shakes and pumps nothing. Physicians call it commotio cordis, the agitation of the heart, and it is what kills a boy struck in the chest by a ball. It bruises nothing and breaks nothing, which is why it leaves no mark, and left alone it is death inside three minutes.

The Akagane have known about the window for a thousand years. Tetsugen steps into the clinch, goes quiet, feels the other man’s heart through his own with Anima Spirare, counts it, and puts his open palm on the breastbone in the window, every time, with the Breath of the Living behind it to hold the quiver once it starts. He says the name very low. Both harms are real and separate: the palm lands hard enough to bruise a breastbone or crack one, a blow the Measurewrights reckon at about a terajoule, and the timing stops the heart under it whether the breastbone breaks or not.

Applications

  • Dropping a man where he stands, with no mark on him
  • Stopping a heart whose skin banks every blow struck at it as heat, since the blow needs almost no force
  • Striking a frightened or furious heart more easily: at two hundred beats a minute the window comes round three times a second
  • Finishing a count that a Fox Arrow has already started, without needing the clinch to begin it

Associations

  • Anima Spirare, through the Body Path: the palm aimed by a listening heart
  • Clinch fighting, from the Akagane yard and his grandfather
  • KITSUNE-YA, which lets him start counting from range
  • HEART-WARRANT and KETSURO, which the same hand is already placed for

Limitations

  • Its limit is the rhythm: a heart he cannot catch, too fast, too irregular, or held still by its owner’s own art, gives him no window
  • A sternum armored in worked plate takes the blow and passes no shock to the heart
  • He must be close and listening first, and the strike is a palm, which can be caught
  • It takes about one percent of his reserve
  • The tell is the quiet before: he steps into the clinch and goes quiet, listening
  • Afterward the man’s own Hemostasis and his friends’ hands can shock the heart back into time if they are quick and know how; a spark of the target’s own lens firing inside the breastbone does it, the same thing a surgeon’s fist does
  • It is beaten by a heart he cannot read, one whose rhythm he cannot catch

HEART-WARRANT (the Akagane warrant) · School Art, the Blood and Heart Arts

HEART-WARRANT is basically heartbeat suspension: the power to hold a heart still between one beat and the next with a flat hand, or to keep a failing one going. The Akagane can keep a heart, and they can stop one, and this is the school art that does both.

Capabilities

With his open hand flat on a man’s chest, Anima Spirare finds the heart’s current and holds it still: no wave of current, no stroke, and a brain that gets no blood for six seconds goes dark and drops its body. A man whose heart is held for six seconds falls, and for three minutes does not get up. The same hand, gentler, keeps a failing heart beating, and that was its first use and its oldest. Inside KODŌ he needs no hand.

It is the art the Hall thinks of first when a lord is found dead with his heart stopped and no mark on him, which is how a needle in the nape was once laid at the copper line’s door.

Applications

  • Dropping a man in six seconds without wounding him
  • Keeping a failing heart beating
  • Holding a heart from across the field, inside KODŌ

Associations

  • Anima Spirare, through the Body Path
  • Taught, in the Akagane Blood and Heart Arts
  • SHINZŌ-DOME, which is this warrant read by the field

Limitations

  • It is fought by the target’s own Hemostasis and heart-ward: a strong practitioner whose blood is his own can push the hand off his heart, at a cost
  • It takes about three percent of his reserve to take hold and 25,000 EU a second to keep it, more than an hour from full
  • The tell is the open hand coming up flat, the way he lays it on his own chest when he swears, with the rings taken off first
  • It is beaten by never letting him touch you

KETSURO (血路 · Ketsuro, the Blood Road) · Application of Anima Spirare

KETSURO is basically blood manipulation: the power to send the blood in a body where he wants it, with a thumb on a vein. It is what the Copper Heart does with his own blood, done to someone else.

Capabilities

It works on the vessels and not the pump. He narrows them from inside to shut a bleed, pushes the blood away from a wound until it can be stitched, or keeps a crushed limb’s flesh alive for the hour it takes to reach a surgeon. Or he drives it, raising the pressure in every small vessel of a man’s eyes and brain and lungs at once until their walls give. In the old speech the word also means a way cut out through an enemy’s lines, and the Akagane surgeons who named it meant both.

Applications

  • Shutting a bleed from the inside
  • Starving a wound of blood until it can be stitched
  • Keeping a crushed limb alive on the way to a surgeon
  • Bursting the small vessels of the eyes, brain and lungs together

Associations

  • Anima Spirare, through the Body Path
  • Lived, and drilled as a surgeon’s art of the Akagane
  • KITSUNE-YA, which is this thumb sent out on an arrow

Limitations

  • It needs contact: his thumb must be on skin over a vein
  • A man whose Hemostasis outranks his reach holds his own blood where it is
  • It takes about one percent of his reserve to open and about 30,000 EU a second to hold
  • The tell is his thumb finding a vein, at the wrist or the throat
  • Held past his reserve it fails by the Breath’s own flaw, the whole answering everywhere at once, his own blood clotting where nothing is cut
  • It is beaten by keeping his hands off you

KITSUNE-YA (狐矢 · Kitsune-ya, the Fox Arrow) · Application of Anima Spirare, the war-bow of the copper line

KITSUNE-YA is basically heartbeat tagging: the power to mark a body with an arrow and hear its heart from across a field afterward. The Fox is the first of the Akagane beasts, and this is the house’s hunting art.

Capabilities

He draws the tall bow below its center, and his aura gathers round the copper broadhead in the shape of a fox’s mouth, red-bronze, bright arterial red at the lip, the long narrow jaws open and every tooth a needle of light. Where the head goes in, the jaws close on the blood and hold, and they go on holding after the shaft is pulled out, burned off or melted in the hand. The bite is Anima Spirare seated in living blood, and from it his listening runs up the veins to the chest, so that he hears that heart at any range on the field and can count it. By the time he closes he has already been counting it for half a minute.

It is contact made at range: KETSURO’s thumb and AKA-TE’s quiet, done from sixty paces. The arrow itself is an ordinary arrow with a soft head. It holes flesh, tendon and leather, folds like tin on worked plate or a hardened breastbone, and bites anyway if it breaks skin. The shafts are copper for one customer, but soft copper serves the art too, since the head folds and stays where a steel one would pass through.

Applications

  • Hearing and counting one heart at any range on the field
  • Starting the Red Hand’s count before he has closed
  • Hitting the knee and the neck at sixty paces

Associations

  • Anima Spirare; the Body Path for the shaft and the Spirit Path for the listening
  • Taught, in the Akagane yard
  • The war-bow of the copper line and the copper shafts

Limitations

  • It needs blood: a thing with none gives the fox nothing to hold
  • A man whose Hemostasis outranks his reach can push the listening out of his veins as he would push off the hand
  • Each shaft takes about half of one percent of his reserve, and the bite then holds for nothing
  • It is seen and heard coming: the mouth round the arrowhead, and a shaft that screams in the air and flies warm as blood, so anything with a sense for heat knows
  • The soft head fails against plate
  • It is beaten by letting it miss and never catching it, because catching it is touching it

SHINGAN (心眼 · Shingan, the heart-eye) · Bloodline Faculty, Moto

SHINGAN is basically load perception: the power to see where a thing carries its weight. It is the common Moto sight, inherited with the blood, and it does not run on THE COPPER DRUM.

Capabilities

The eye reads load, and men by their load. In him it is the long weighing look, the look of a man looking at a horse he has been told is unbreakable.

Applications

  • Weighing a thing, or a man, by where the load sits in it

Associations

  • The Moto blood’s eye, with no Wellspring behind it and no Path

Limitations

  • It does not read a man with nothing to weigh
  • Held wide it draws about 3,000 EU a second
  • The long weighing look gives it away

Releases

TOSHI-GAERI (年返り · Toshi-gaeri, the Years Returned) · First Release

TOSHI-GAERI is basically limiter removal: the power to let go of the hour he has kept and fight as what he really is. The word is “Kaese,” give it back.

Capabilities

The face the Copper Heart has held over his Crystal for forty years falls away, and the Sovereign density under it comes up all at once through the copper. The slow pulse in his aura becomes a drum, the red-bronze goes red as fresh blood in a basin, the smell comes off him, copper and something sweet like the air after a thunderstorm, and the room feels a Stage it did not know was in it.

With it the years come. The black curls go iron gray from the temples back all at once, as if frost had run through them, a soldier’s furrows come in at the gold eyes and the mouth, the knuckles knot, and the snow round his feet goes pink. For as long as the Release holds he fights as what he is, sixty-one, at Dissonance, with Hemostasis, Regeneration and Grapple striking a step high.

Applications

  • Fighting at his true Stage
  • Raising Hemostasis, Regeneration and Grapple a step
  • Called silent, a smaller rise: the face slips only a little and the gain is half

Associations

  • Anima Spirare and Verdantia, let go, through the Body Path
  • THE COPPER HEART, whose hold it drops
  • KODŌ, which opens on the released pulse

Limitations

  • Some of the years stay: for every minute he holds it, about a year comes into the body for good when he takes hold again, and a man who has kept himself at twenty-five for forty years has done the sum
  • It lasts about four minutes from full
  • It ends the Veil for whoever sees it: whoever sees it has seen him
  • It is beaten by time

KODŌ (鼓動 · Kodō, the Heartbeat) · Second Release

KODŌ the Second Release is basically field projection: the power to turn the drum in his aura into a field. It opens the Domain of the same name.

Capabilities

Released, the slow copper pulse in his aura has become a drum, and the Second Release opens the field on it. He is usually on one knee with his good hand flat on his own chest when he says the word, and the field comes up from the ground, copper-red at its edges, felt through the soles and the backs of the teeth before it is heard.

Applications

  • Opening and powering KODŌ the Domain
  • Making the ground SHINZŌ-DOME needs

Associations

  • Anima Spirare, through the Body Path
  • TOSHI-GAERI, whose drum it takes up

Limitations

  • Held past its limit it is a Crystal Fracture Event along the Sennen road, and his own heart stops with it

SHINZŌ-DOME (心臓止め · Shinzō-dome, the Heart Stopped) · Third Release

SHINZŌ-DOME is basically death by decree: the power to stop one named heart by the law of the field, with no hand on it. It is the Akagane warrant read by KODŌ.

Capabilities

Inside KODŌ he names one heart, aloud, the way the Akagane warrant is read, and that heart stops between one beat and the next, and stays stopped. HEART-WARRANT can be pushed off by a strong man’s blood. This cannot, because inside the field the heart’s beat is his to keep or not to keep, and it stops by law and not by force.

His own heart stops with the other for as long as he holds it, kept by the Copper Heart alone. That is the only reason he can do it at all.

Applications

  • Killing one named enemy anywhere inside the field
  • Stopping a heart whose owner’s blood would push off the hand

Associations

  • Anima Spirare, through the Body Path, by the field’s law
  • KODŌ, which it can only be called inside
  • HEART-WARRANT, which it is at Domain size
  • THE COPPER HEART, which keeps him alive through it

Limitations

  • One heart, inside the field, named aloud, and it cannot be called twice in one field
  • It takes a quarter of his reserve at once
  • His own heart is still for as long as he holds it, and every second of that puts years on him that stay
  • A Sovereignty stronger than his holds against it, and so does an Oath and Integrity that will not let the body go
  • A body with no heart gives it nothing
  • It is beaten by killing him in the seconds his own heart is still

Domain

KODŌ (鼓動 · Kodō, the Heartbeat) · Domain

KODŌ the Domain is basically heartbeat synchronization: the power to make every heart within about a hundred and twenty meters beat to his. It is Anima Spirare at Domain-law, and the one piece of the art that needs no hand.

Capabilities

It is the Breath of the Living with the ground for its body. Inside the field his doctrine is the ground’s law, and the field answers every heart in it as one whole body answers a hurt, with his own heart for the signal: Anima Spirare lays his beat under every heart, and each one takes it, the way men marching fall into step with a drum without choosing to. He sets the tempo. The Measurewrights call it entrainment, one rhythm pulling its neighbors into step, and they can time it to the beat; they cannot say why a heart that has never met him keeps his.

His allies’ hearts he steadies, holds and keeps. A steadied heart pumps evenly through shock and blood loss and does not race itself to death in heat or terror, so a man run through stays on his feet and a man bleeding out goes on bleeding slowly enough to be carried. His enemies’ he slows, a beat at a time, until too little blood reaches the brain, the sight grays and the knees go, which is how a field of men falls down without a mark on them. It lays a second field inside whatever field already stands, and a heart galloping in heat or fear stumbles once and catches his step.

Applications

  • Keeping wounded allies on their feet and alive long enough to be carried
  • Dropping a field of enemies without a mark on them
  • Calming a heart that is racing itself to death
  • Using HEART-WARRANT without a hand, and calling SHINZŌ-DOME

Associations

  • Anima Spirare, through the Body Path: the Shell’s hold laid over a field
  • The Second Release, which opens it

Limitations

  • The price is the drum itself: the field beats to his own heart, and anything that strikes his heart, or startles it, or stops it, collapses the field at once
  • Every heart he holds slowed is a weight on his own, which he feels as an old man feels stairs, and the gray at his temples goes white while he holds them
  • It holds about two minutes from full
  • A practitioner of his Stage with stronger Sovereignty or Hemostasis contests it at the edge and in his own chest
  • The dead and the made have no heart to hold
  • A body running a high fever, or standing in great heat, races its own heart harder than he can slow it
  • It holds the heart and nothing else: in an oven it keeps a man’s heart from racing itself to death and does nothing for his brain, which cooks first
  • Held past his reserve his own blood starts answering every heart he is holding, and pushed to the end his own heart stops with the field
  • It is beaten by contesting it, by striking the drum, or by bringing something with no heart
