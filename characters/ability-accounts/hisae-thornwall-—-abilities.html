Hisae Thornwall — Abilities in full

Hisae Thornwall — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202612 min read

Card: Hisae Thornwall

SMILE AND PAY

SMILE AND PAY is basically deception mastery: the power to let them count you bought, and keep the door. It is her signature art. She smiled and paid for forty years, and the whole time she was keeping a back door open.

Capabilities

It is one idea, and it took her forty years to say it out loud. A Byakuya girl was sent north to seal a peace, into a house that sat under Tenrai taxes and Tenrai factors and Tenrai silver, and on her wedding night an old forester told her that Thornwall kept a road open through the wood for a king who had not come. She thought him a sentimental old fool. Then she spent forty years being the thing that kept his road safe.

The smile is the keeping, the door is what was kept, and the pike is what comes through the door when the crow arrives. None of the three is a Wellspring’s work. She has harmonized no lens for them. They are a habit of the soul, a secret of her husband’s house and a drill of its foresters, and each takes its limit and its failure from itself.

Applications

  • Being counted bought by the people she means to turn on
  • Keeping a road open for a king who had not come
  • Sending the pikes through it when the crow arrives

Techniques

  • SMILE AND PAY - the keeping, a small dry smile with everything behind it
  • THE BACK DOOR - what was kept, the way in nobody uses
  • THE THORNWALL PIKE - what comes through the door

Associations

  • No Wellspring and no lens: a habit of the soul, a secret of the house and a house drill
  • The Spirit Path carries the smile, as one meaning written over herself
  • The Fate Path carries the door and the Command; the door and the pikes are no working
  • THE RED BEFORE THE WHITE, which is no part of the art and the reason for all of it

Limitations

  • A waiting does nothing until the crow comes
  • It cost her forty years behind shut doors, and her own name with them
  • The door, once used, belongs to everybody

Traits

SMILE AND PAY · Primary Trait

SMILE AND PAY is basically emotion concealment: the power to hold everything behind a small dry smile, so that no reading of her face or her aura gives up what she means. Her soul learned it sitting in Thornhaven under Tenrai taxes, Tenrai factors and Tenrai silver for forty years, smiling and paying, while the Tenrai counted Thornwall bought.

Capabilities

A face gives a person away in a particular manner. What somebody truly feels arrives on the face first, for well under half a second, before the face they mean to show can be laid over it, and a sharp eye across a table lives on those flickers. An aura leaks the same way: it brightens and leans toward what its owner wants. In her the flicker is not there to catch, because the dry smile is already in place before the feeling comes, and a face that is already doing one thing has no slack left to do another.

She walks the Spirit Path a third and more of her practice, and that Path works on what a thing means; she uses it on one thing only, herself. Her Core (the innermost layer of the soul, where identity sits) holds a single plain meaning over everything else, a provincial lady who likes gold thread and pays what she is asked, and that is the page any reader gets, whether he reads with his eyes or with his Gnosis (the stat of perception and understanding).

While she keeps the smile her Feint reads a step high on the hidden scale. Her aura, the near-white with its red line, stays banked so low under it that a factor across the table saw a woman and no practitioner at all.

Applications

  • Sitting across a table from Tenrai factors and giving nothing away
  • Passing as a provincial lady with a weakness for gold thread
  • Hiding a practitioner’s aura from a reader

Associations

  • The Spirit Path, as one meaning written over herself by her Core
  • Her own table at Thornhaven, across from Tenrai factors
  • The red-and-gold bodice and the icicles at her ears

Limitations

  • It is a waiting, and a waiting does nothing on its own; it needs a crow to come to Thornhaven
  • When the crow came and said that the red line lived, she had the Tenrai factor put in her cellar the next morning and called out the pikes, and the smile had done all it would ever do
  • It costs nothing she would mention, because nothing is made or thrown and no reserve leaves her
  • What it truly cost was forty years, and her own name
  • Its tell tells a watcher nothing: the corners of the dark red mouth lifting a very little, the heavy lids coming half down, the small hands folding, the icicles at her ears hanging dead still
  • The Tenrai found nothing that beat it, because they read her face for forty years and never once read her house
  • The smile hides what she means and cannot hide what she does
  • A man who counted the pike-shafts in the Thornhaven lofts, or asked the foresters why they cut the same empty rides every winter and every spring, or simply walked the Thornwood east until it ended, would have had all of it without looking at her at all
  • A reader whose Perception outranks her Ward by a full step on the hidden scale, with her raised Feint counted against him, sees a banked aura for what it is

THE BACK DOOR · Secondary Trait

THE BACK DOOR is basically weakness detection: the power to look at ground for the way nobody uses. It formed the night she married into the house, when her husband’s grandfather told her why the Thornwall foresters kept the corridors cut.

Capabilities

A stronghold is safe for exactly as long as the things its safety depends on stay true, and its keepers stop looking at those things long before they stop being true. That is the oldest lesson in siege work, that a pass is turned by the goat track its defenders forgot.

She is a Fate Path soul, and Fate is the Path of what has to be so before anything can happen; her Forecast, the best thing in her, goes to that and nothing else. She pulls no thread and moves nothing, which is why it is free. She only looks at a map, a wall or a bargain and finds the one condition everybody has agreed to stop checking.

She already knows where one is: the long cuts through the Thornwood, walled in by trees so old you cannot see the sky, and the stair behind the frozen fall. Thornwall cut them two hundred years ago, when the Kōkan still sat the red chair and Thornwall was their forester, and they run from Thornhaven to the foot of the Seat’s eastern face, where the rock is lowest. The Seat’s main gate had been the only way up the rock for three hundred years, so every eye, ward and garrison on the rock faced south. In the case of the Seat she did not even have to find the door, because she had been handed it on her wedding night and kept it.

Applications

  • Finding the low place in a rock face, the ride through a wood, the hour every eye is on another road
  • Reading a map, a wall or a bargain for the condition nobody checks
  • Bringing pikes to the foot of the Seat’s eastern face by the Thornwood corridors and the stair

Associations

  • The Fate Path, through her Forecast; it is no working
  • Her husband’s grandfather and the foresters’ marks
  • House Thornwall’s corridors, kept cut two hundred years
  • THE THORNWALL PIKE, which goes through the door

Limitations

  • A back door works once; after the Seat, everybody knows, and the Thornwood rides are now on every map in the north
  • It costs nothing
  • Its tell is that she looks at a map for the way nobody uses, and her finger rests where the map is blank
  • A man who already knows where the corridors run beats it
  • One sentry at the head of the stair, or one forester who talks, and twelve hundred men are strung out in single files in a wood with a cliff at the end of it

THE RED BEFORE THE WHITE · Inherited Trait

THE RED BEFORE THE WHITE is basically oath empowerment: the power to stand unafraid near the Kōkan blood. It is the oath of the white house and no part of the art SMILE AND PAY, though it is the reason for all of it.

Capabilities

It is the Byakuya oath, said over her as a girl in the white house: purity before power, peace before the blade, and the red line before the white, until the end of the red line. An oath is the small end of Fate, and this one was laid on her Attraction Layer (the outermost layer of the soul, where bonds and vows take hold) before she was old enough to weigh it. It binds her to the red line and binds the red line to nothing. Near the Kōkan blood her Anchoring holds and nothing frightens her.

She kept it over the lamp every night for forty years in Thornhaven with the doors shut, and spoke it aloud in the Hall of Verdicts beside Kiyome, the old woman and the not-so-old one, so softly only the front of the Hall could hear. The smile, the door and the pikes were forty years of keeping this one sentence alive where nobody could hear it.

Applications

  • Holding her nerve and her Anchoring near the Kōkan blood

Associations

  • The Byakuya oath, the white house’s inheritance, with no Wellspring behind it
  • The Fate Path, as a binding
  • The Kōkan, the red line it binds her to
  • The oath lamp at Thornhaven, over which she kept it

Limitations

  • The limit is the oath’s own: it guards the king, and has nothing to say when the king is the knife
  • It costs nothing to keep, and it cannot be set down
  • Its tell is her lips moving over the words, with the doors shut
  • To beat it, make the king the knife

Techniques

THE THORNWALL PIKE · House Art, Thornwall · Signature

THE THORNWALL PIKE is basically pike formation command: the power to bring twelve hundred pikes on at a walk as a hedge of ash and iron that horse will not go into, or to send them thirty at a time up a stair and over a wall nobody was watching. Every Thornwall pikeman is taught the step and the hedge; only the Lady knew where to send them.

Capabilities

The pikes come on in gray felt, in step, to the slow beat of a drum, with the thorn-horn sounding behind them every hundred paces. The charge is the sound, and it is meant to be heard: a drum too slow to be anything but deliberate, the horn marking each hundred paces, and then the block itself coming out of the white, gray felt and iron caps, each banner with its dark red thorn-sprig, forty men wide and thirty deep with eighteen-foot ash shafts leveled.

The reason it works is in the horse and in the arithmetic. A horse will gallop at a man; it will not run itself onto a point it can see, and a shaft that long puts the iron of the first four or five ranks out past the front man’s chest, so that a rider coming in meets five points before he reaches one face. A horseman’s whole weapon is weight at speed, and a hedge that holds its shape gives him nothing to spend it on but the ash.

So the art is all in holding the shape, and that is drill and will together: every man at one pace so no gap opens, which the drum gives them; every man certain the man beside him will stand, which the lord gives them. Her Command keeps the step and her Forecast picks the ground. She does not shout and does not need to. A Stage V soul with her Dominion (the stat of a soul’s command over what stands around it) riding at a walk beside the block is felt down every rank as a steadiness, the same plain weight that bends the attention of lesser souls toward her, turned here to holding twelve hundred frightened men to a drumbeat.

Applications

  • Stopping horse with a set hedge
  • Holding ground and taking walls
  • Going up a stair thirty at a time, on her quiet order: “Not all at once. Thirty at a time.”

Associations

  • House Thornwall, which teaches every pikeman the step and the hedge
  • The Fate Path, for the Command; it is no working
  • Her Command, her Forecast and her Dominion
  • The drum and the thorn-horn, which do her shouting
  • THE BACK DOOR, which says where to send them

Limitations

  • Its limit is the hedge: it holds ground and takes walls, and it is slow
  • It cannot chase, and it cannot turn quickly
  • A block caught strung out on the march or half formed is twelve hundred men carrying long poles
  • It costs the house its pikemen, and her nothing she would call a working
  • Thornwall pikes are among the ninety-one names the north paid for the Seat beside Rob Stark’s
  • Its tell is a drum, and the thorn-horn every hundred paces
  • Horse on open ground before the hedge is set beats it
  • So do bolts and arrows from beyond the reach of the ash, which a slow block in felt with no plate anywhere can only walk through
