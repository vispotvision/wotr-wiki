Vaashuuk Snorr-Khal — Abilities in full

Vaashuuk Snorr-Khal — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202628 min read

Card: Vaashuuk Snorr-Khal · The Sin of Sloth

NOTHIN’ GETS DONE

NOTHIN’ GETS DONE (Vaross · Dok-Snorr, striking-at, asleep) is basically viscosity manipulation: the power to make the world go thick as tar, so that the harder you swing the slower you go and nothing anybody starts near him ever gets finished. It is his signature art, and his Traits, techniques, Releases and Domain are all this one idea: things stop before they are done.

Capabilities

The Sleep Crown he slept on for a year is Hypnather, the idea of change that comes only at rest, the soul setting itself in order in the dark while nothing is asked of it, and its operation is coagulation: left at rest, a thing slowly sets into an order it never had time to reach. A year asleep on its Core set his Crystal that way, never finished, always settling, and he does to a room what it did to him. His claim lays the air down to rest, and air at rest thickens and goes on thickening, like a sleeper sinking, until it has stopped flowing for any purpose that matters. It sets like a glass. The Measurewrights call the settling structural relaxation and say he drives the air toward the glass transition; they have good figures for how thick, and no idea why a goblin’s nap does it. The pitch in the philosophers’ funnel at Stannvaard is the picture he uses himself: it is flowing, and it drops once every ten winters.

Rest will not be hurried, and that is why the thickness stiffens under a blow. A slow hand asks little of the rested air and passes; a fast one asks to be let through at once, which is the one thing rest refuses, so the glass barely resists a slow hand and fights a fast one harder the harder it comes. Stir cornflour into water and a slow spoon passes through soup while a punch lands on a brick; the scholars call that shear thickening, and the glass behaves the same way. He never strikes anyone, and he does not need to. The blow that does not land goes somewhere, and where it goes is back up the arm that threw it. That is how the art rides a blow: the other fighter supplies the blow, and the glass returns it.

He moves through all of it at his ordinary amble because of Abython, the Abyssal Pulse, the idea of peace with the abyss, whose operation is dissolution: the soul’s difference from what surrounds it is loosed into it until nothing is left between them. A fish in a river feels no current, because it is carried at the river’s own pace; he has made his peace with his own thick air in the same way, keeps nothing of himself set against it, and so it does not drag on him. The charge is the yawn, and nobody has ever missed it. His jaw cracks, his eyes screw shut, one claw goes politely over his mouth, and the drowsy lavender round him thickens and goes level like a bath filling. He pulls Aether up out of whatever ground he happens to be lying over, the Crystal refines it into Essence, and the claim goes out through his Attraction Layer with almost nothing lost on the way.

Applications

  • Slowing everything that moves through a stretch of air
  • Turning a fast blow back on the one who threw it
  • Setting air hard as a pane, a plug or a bed
  • Undoing motion, ending heat, and stopping anything new from starting
  • Running his own body slow and cheap
  • Staying off every slate, since his fields only stand and wait

Techniques

  • TORPOR - the glass turned on his own body: he runs as slow and cheap as a dormouse in winter
  • DOK - the fracture under all of it: nothing he does is ever finished
  • DROWSE - the leak: everyone near him yawns
  • TAR TIME - the yawn that thickens a sphere of air
  • THE SNOOZE-GLASS DROP - one TAR TIME banked in a glass bubble, opened with no yawn
  • THE CHAISE - a slab of glassed air he lies on
  • THE WAITER’S WAVE - a hand that sets one piece of the glass harder: a pane, a plug, a slab
  • THE INFINITE DO-OVER - the field rocked back and forth so every motion comes home
  • PLAN B - the Crown forced, so pieces of the field stop all the way down
  • FEATHERBED - THE CHAISE grown large: the bed, and TORPOR at its deepest
  • THE LIE-IN - the One, his only Release, which counts as his Second, and the Domain: nothing new can start

Associations

  • Hypnather, the Sleep Crown, a Spirit Wellspring he was baptized in at its Core, makes the glass
  • Abython, the Abyssal Pulse, a Wild Wellspring, matches him to his own thick air
  • Nyxial, the Nocturne Flame, a Spirit Wellspring, holds heat for him
  • The Fate Path, which he has mastered, lays the fields; the Spirit Path, which he walks, turns the Crown on minds and on his own Crystal
  • Self-taught by sleeping on a Wellspring
  • He bonds no weapon and does not carry anything heavy; the Snooze-Glass rides at his shoulder
  • He does not shout these. Shouting is work. A name comes out of him on the back end of a yawn, in Vaross or in Common, whichever is less effort, and he raises his voice only to heckle and to complain

Limitations

  • His fields do not kill. They stand in the road and wait, and whatever dies in them is finished by its own speed or by somebody else’s hand
  • He cannot finish anything, his own kills, oaths and loves included, and he cannot start anything fast
  • Every working that wakes him properly costs him the only thing he values, which is lying still
  • The yawn is the tell, and DROWSE carries it ahead of the working, so every jaw in fifty paces has caught it by the time the air begins to change
  • About six parts in a hundred of what he spends never reach the world, and on a Fate soul that share ties a knot; his are too small for him to notice
  • Each piece fails in its own way: a dropped field lets go of everything it held in the same instant, with him standing in the middle of it; a fire held against under Nyxial comes back out of him all at once; and TORPOR lasts only as long as the Crystal holds him there
  • Against a higher Stage the art fails from the outside in: a stronger Sovereignty takes the Domain back hall by hall, a working that cancels a law the air is carrying cuts a path through TAR TIME, a glyph of unmaking shatters a pane from the inside, and a judge who reads what a soul has decided it is finds in him nothing to read
  • He has never trained an eye for anything fast, and he will not see it
  • He has no other Release: the Fate road gives a soul the One and no more
  • It is beaten by hitting him before the yawn; after it, by coming slow and breathing slow, running hot, turning one way, counting the Snooze-Glass, staying between the pieces of PLAN B, walking out of the Lie-In, or cornering him until he has to finish something
  • A judge who reads what a soul has decided it is can rule on the hole: it can crack his hold on the Sleep Crown and part the tether between his Crystal and its Wellspring in one cut, and then the Crystal leaves him and TORPOR’s long low pressure lets go like a diver hauled up too fast

Traits

TORPOR (Vaross · Snaal) · Primary Trait

TORPOR is basically hibernation: the power to idle his body and his Crystal at a floor most creatures reach only in winter sleep. It is the Sleep Crown turned on himself.

Capabilities

A dormouse in winter drops its heart to a few beats a minute and burns almost nothing, and he lives there. Everything in him runs slow and cheap: poisons and venoms work through him at a crawl, bleeding is sluggish because there is so little pressure behind it, his reserve barely moves when he is not using it, and when he sleeps under Hypnather he recovers three times as fast as he would awake. His blood is dark as plum wine and comes slowly, welling in a cut and hanging there in a fat bead, and he mends at the same sleeping pace: a cut hand draws to a pink seam in an afternoon.

It is a held state, a body kept at low pressure by the Crystal the way a diver is kept by the weight of the sea.

Applications

  • Slowing poison and venom to a crawl
  • Barely bleeding from a wound
  • Refilling his reserve three times as fast asleep
  • Spending almost nothing at rest

Associations

  • Hypnather, through the Spirit Path, written on his own Crystal
  • FEATHERBED, which takes it as deep as his Fate II will carry it

Limitations

  • It all rests on the Crystal’s hold: every wound he takes clots and pinches at a sleeping heart’s pace only for as long as the Crystal holds him there
  • A body kept at a low pressure for ninety years has never learned to bear an ordinary one
  • He wakes slowly: from a cold start his reactions lag, his thinking is foggy for a few breaths, and he could not run if he wanted to
  • A torpid animal is the easiest kill in the forest for anything quick, and he is the same
  • The tell is that he never hurries, even when he should
  • It is beaten by speed and surprise: hit him early, hard and fast, before he has yawned and before he is awake, and the whole sheet is a slow, soft goblin in a big coat

DOK (Vaross · Striking-At) · Secondary Trait

DOK is basically perpetual incompletion: the power to never have finished anything, so that nothing can be pinned on him. It belongs to no Wellspring. It is a fracture in his Attraction Layer, the layer of the Crystal that holds a soul’s share of fate.

Capabilities

In Vaross a doubled consonant marks a finished act: dok is to strike at, dokk is to have struck. Vaashuuk lives in the undoubled form. When the bellows-gang died he had done nothing, and the Plane of Fate wrote that down as exactly what it was. The fracture was written the night he lay down and let the roof come, and it is held open by an Obsession Force with nothing to grip. Since then nothing he does ever closes. His acts stay open in the Attraction Layer, and any working that needs a completed act to anchor to slides off him like rain off oiled cloth.

It is also the wall round his whole art. Whatever dies in one of his fields is, on his slate, nothing at all.

Applications

  • Showing a soul-reading no killing, because he has never completed one; the Kurenai eye read his chest and found a clean slate
  • Giving an oath-binding no oath to grip, because Oath 1 holds nothing
  • Leaving a grudge nothing banked against him, because he has never landed a blow that finished; the Wrath’s grudge had no blow of his to burn by
  • Leaving a working that sets a spoken promise no end to find; a Yukari smoke-working neither bent his promise nor set it

Associations

  • The Fate Path, with no Wellspring: a fracture feature of his own Attraction Layer, held by Obsession Force
  • The one grip his soul won for itself, lying in the coal-store, with no blood and no blessing behind it
  • The cave-in at the bellows-gang, which left an act open for good, and every act after it
  • THE WAITER’S WAVE, whose squash it stops half-set

Limitations

  • The price is total: he cannot finish anything either. He cannot complete a kill even when he wants to; he cannot keep a promise, finish a task or hold a love to its end, and every job he is given has to be the kind that someone else finishes
  • It shows in a fight as a hand that stops: a slab he begins to set on a chest goes half hard and will go no further, while he frowns at his own palm
  • Every verb he speaks is unfinished, and near a working that sets true speech the padding fell off his boast and what was left was “I’ll just start.”
  • A judge who reads what a soul has decided it is finds the same hole from the other side, and a hole can be ruled on
  • It is beaten by making him finish something: corner him with no way out but to strike to completion, or bind him to a deal he must close to survive, and the moment the act closes the Trait goes thin and every reading, oath and grudge that slid off him sticks at once

DROWSE (Heavy Air) · Passive Trait

DROWSE is basically sleep inducement: the power to make everything near him drowsy without trying. Hypnather leaks off him the way heat leaks off a stove.

Capabilities

Within about ten yards of him people yawn, eyelids grow heavy, attention drifts and reactions slow by a hair. Flames lean low and burn smaller, dust settles faster, a stirred cup stops swirling sooner, and an untrained guard on a long watch near him will be asleep inside the hour. It travels wherever his glass does, a seam in a wall included. It carries the yawn before the working arrives.

Applications

  • Stealing a beat between seeing and cutting, with one caught yawn
  • Putting a guard on a long watch to sleep
  • Reaching through a wall by a seam

Associations

  • Hypnather; the Fate Path for the reach, and the Spirit Path for what it does to a mind
  • It is unshaped, a leak and no working

Limitations

  • It costs nothing and he cannot switch it off, which makes him terrible company at parties and useless as a sentry
  • Everyone near him yawning is the tell
  • It is beaten by anything that wakes the body: noise, pain, cold water, a stimulant, or a practitioner whose Ignivale or whose plain fury keeps the fire lit

Techniques

TAR TIME · Signature Technique

TAR TIME is basically motion slowing: the power to thicken a sphere of air until everything moving through it crawls. It is the art at its plainest.

Capabilities

He yawns, enormously, and the air inside a sphere forty yards across, out to a hundred and twenty when he bothers, lies down to rest and starts to set. Everything that has to travel through space slows with the air: steam, smoke, a fire’s spread, a thrown weapon, a working’s flight, and the salts that carry a signal down a nerve, so a body inside it thinks at its own pace and moves at the field’s. Rest will not be hurried, so a slow push passes almost freely and the faster the blow, the thicker the world it meets. A bird flung at his face like a spear folded its own neck against the air a hand from his nose.

A small one needs only a small yawn, and can glass the air round a single fist and leave the arm hanging there mid-haul. The field also keeps what falls in it. Blood, thrown clods, breath and rice-mist hang where they stopped. He walks through it at his ordinary amble, because Abython equalizes him to it.

Applications

  • Slowing a thrown spear to a walk
  • Stopping steam, smoke and the spread of fire
  • Slowing a working in flight
  • Catching one fist in the air
  • Giving THE WAITER’S WAVE and THE INFINITE DO-OVER the glass they work in

Associations

  • Hypnather, with Abython for his own passage
  • The Fate Path for the field and its reach
  • THE SNOOZE-GLASS DROP, which is one of these banked overnight

Limitations

  • The glass is rest laid on whatever travels through it, and heat is restlessness, the one thing in a thing that will not lie down, so heat is untouched and keeps building, and a rest that is stirred goes soft; the Measurewrights say the glass slows motion through space and never the shaking of a thing’s smallest pieces where they stand, which is what they take heat to be
  • When he drops it, everything held in it comes loose at once, and he is standing in the middle of it
  • It takes 0.3 percent of his reserve a second and holds 5 minutes 33 seconds from full
  • The tell is the big yawn, long, loud and obvious, and then the air going glossy and steam ceasing to rise
  • What hangs in the field tells a watcher exactly how far the glass reaches, and anyone out of the forty yards when the yawn ends is outside it
  • A working that cancels a law the air is carrying, as the Yukari Null-Light does, cuts a path of plain air through it no wider than the blade, and a blow can come down that path at its own speed
  • It is beaten by moving slowly, which the field barely resists, by running hot, because heat will not lie down and thins it fast, or by stepping out of the radius

THE SNOOZE-GLASS DROP · Signature Technique

THE SNOOZE-GLASS DROP is basically stored casting: the power to bank one whole TAR TIME in a glass bubble and open it later with no yawn. It is the one way he has found to skip the yawn.

Capabilities

Overnight, while he sleeps, he lets a full TAR TIME’s worth of glassed air settle into the brass-banded glass bubble that floats at his shoulder, held ready. He drops the bubble and the field opens around him with no yawn and no warning. A fresh field is whole and cold, and it closes any hot channel that has been bored through the old one.

Applications

  • Opening a field on an enemy who was waiting for the yawn
  • Closing a hot channel bored through the old field
  • Catching a fighter already at full speed inside a thinned patch, who meets the new glass at exactly the speed she brought

Associations

  • Hypnather, through the Fate Path, banked in the glass overnight
  • The Snooze-Glass, a made thing he did not make
  • TAR TIME, which is what it holds

Limitations

  • It needs the Snooze-Glass
  • It takes four percent of his reserve, and it is one charge; the bubble needs a full night’s sleep to refill
  • After it he is back to yawning like everybody else
  • The tell is the bubble dimming as it falls
  • It is beaten by counting it: if it has dropped once today, it will not drop again

THE CHAISE · Technique

THE CHAISE is basically levitation: the power to lie down on nothing and drift wherever he leans. He spends most of his life on it.

Capabilities

He sets a slab of air under himself into glass and lies on it, held afloat by Abython, the same peace with the air that lets him walk through his own field. It carries him wherever he leans, at a walk. When he rolls off it, it sags into a puddle of clear syrup and is gone.

Applications

  • Travel without standing up
  • A bed anywhere

Associations

  • Hypnather for the slab, Abython for the float, through the Fate Path
  • FEATHERBED, which is this grown large and called aloud

Limitations

  • It holds his weight and nothing much more
  • Any real heat makes it sag and drip like toffee left in the sun
  • It costs so little it barely counts, a fraction of a percent of his reserve an hour
  • The tell is that he is lying on nothing
  • It is beaten by heating it until it drips

THE WAITER’S WAVE · Technique

THE WAITER’S WAVE is basically air solidification: the power to set one small piece of the glass harder with a lazy hand. It is a second claim made inside a standing TAR TIME.

Capabilities

He lifts his good hand off his knee, palm out, like a waiter waving away a plate, and a yard of the glass in front of it sets clear and hard as a window with no frame, the light running round its edges in a thin bright line. The pane rings when struck like a wine glass flicked with a finger, and whatever hits it meets it at exactly the speed it brought. A boy who charged one broke his nose and brow on it.

The plug is subtler and crueler. A breath is a thing that has to travel, down the long branching pipe of the throat into the chest, and the air a hand’s breadth from a mouth, laid down as thick as pitch, is rest that will not be hurried. A whole heaving chest cannot draw it: the harder the chest heaves the harder the rest refuses it, and the less comes down. The Measurewrights say the push a pipe needs rises in strict proportion to the thickness of what goes down it.

Techniques

  • The pane - a flat palm lifted, and a yard of glass sets hard as a window in front of a face
  • The plug - one claw closed slowly into the palm, like a man pinching out a candle, and the air at a mouth goes toward pitch
  • The squash - the palm laid flat an inch off a breastbone, beginning a slab that would stop the ribs rising; it has never been finished

Associations

  • Hypnather, through the Fate Path
  • TAR TIME, which it needs standing
  • DOK, which stops the squash

Limitations

  • It needs a standing TAR TIME, and is paid inside it
  • The tell is the hand, and it is a slow hand
  • The plug stiffens only as fast as the breath pulls on it, so a fighter who breathes slowly, in for six and out for six, breathes straight through
  • Heat breaks a pane, and so does a working that unmakes or cancels the glass at the touch; a glyph of unmaking shatters one from the inside
  • The squash has been begun at least twice and has always stopped half-set, because pressing a rib cage flat would be a finished act and DOK does not permit him one
  • It is beaten by coming slower than the glass stiffens

THE INFINITE DO-OVER · Technique

THE INFINITE DO-OVER is basically motion reversal: the power to make every back-and-forth motion in his field undo itself on the way home. It is the only thing he works at.

Capabilities

He does not yawn. The hum that sits under him all day goes quick, a steady beat like a man working a bellows handle, and the lavender round him swells and shrinks with it. He takes the glass that is still his past pitch, into a rest so deep that nothing in it keeps any going of its own: a thing moves exactly as far as it is pushed and no further, so if the push is undone the motion undoes itself, all the way back to the start. The Measurewrights say nothing in it has any momentum left. The Stannvaard philosophers show it with three drops of dye in corn syrup between two glass cylinders, smeared away to nothing by four turns of the inner cylinder and gathered back into three round drops by four turns the other way.

He sets his whole field rocking in time with his hum, one way and back, and every motion that goes out and returns is undone on the return stroke. He alone moves on.

Applications

  • A pike strikes and unstrikes the same air
  • A breath goes out and comes back
  • A rising girl rises and sinks
  • A line of fire runs out and is drawn home

Associations

  • Hypnather, through the Fate Path
  • TAR TIME, which it needs standing
  • Bellows-work, the skill from the bellows-gang that he swore he would never use again, and which is in the hum

Limitations

  • Only back-and-forth motion is caught: a thing that keeps turning one way, a spinning blade, a burning auger boring forward on its own twist, is never brought back, because its motion has no return in it
  • Anything turning one way goes through and takes the loops apart behind it
  • Heat is restlessness and goes where it likes, and no rocking can gather it home again
  • It is the hardest he has ever worked: the reserve goes in long pulls, his wounds bleed faster with every stroke, and sweat stands on his brow
  • The tell is the missing yawn, the hum gone quick as a bellows stroke, and the sweat
  • It is beaten by anything that turns one way, and by heat

PLAN B · Technique

PLAN B is basically absolute stillness: the power to stop pieces of the world all the way down, so that fire poured into them simply ends. It is the other yawn, and it is what happens when he forces the Crown instead of resting on it.

Capabilities

He puts two claws in his mouth and yawns again, slowly, and the yawn goes on and on with the corners of his mouth drawn back past his cheeks, and the look over the claws is not lazy at all. The Sleep Crown’s change comes only at rest, and a soul cannot say when it is finished. He hurries it anyway, with what he calls a little spark of enthusiasm, and patches of the field arrive all at once at the stillness rest only ever approaches: in those pieces the rest goes all the way down, so even the restlessness inside a thing where it stands lies down, and heat is that restlessness. Inside a piece nothing stirs, so nothing is hot and nothing is cold. The Measurewrights say the shaking of a thing’s smallest parts has stopped there.

Behind the stillness stands Nyxial, the Nocturne Flame, the idea of the even flame, a self that holds its level whatever is poured into it. The pieces take the fire and he carries it, and Nyxial’s flaw is that a level held against a load keeps everything it refused to feel, so it comes out of him when the load lifts. Hypnather’s named failure is interrupted relaxation, a rest broken before it has finished, which locks the thing worse ordered than before, and PLAN B is that failure used on purpose. That is why the stillness never comes as one whole. It comes scattered and malformed, in faceted pockets sealed off from each other along flat planes whose angles do not agree, with plain air between them.

Applications

  • Ending fire: poured in, it has nothing to stir and ends there, the way water ends at the bottom of a well
  • Putting out a Domain built on trapped heat, in holes from its edge inward
  • Hanging a falling drop of molten stone as a dull black bead, or a tongue of fire as a twisted violet sculpture
  • Holding a hand caught in a piece, which is there and cannot be felt or moved

Associations

  • Hypnather, forced, over Nyxial
  • The Fate Path for the reach, with the Spirit Path forcing the Crown
  • It is also called the second yawn

Limitations

  • It comes in scattered pieces he does not place, with plain air between
  • The planes cut through steam, stone and light alike, and whatever solid thing they pass through breaks along them like a frozen lake along its cracks, so they break the ground and drop him along with everyone else
  • The fire he carries comes out of him all at once when the working lifts
  • It hurts him as much as it hurts anyone: he goes gray round the mouth, sweats, shakes, and is spent after
  • It wants a little spark of enthusiasm or it does not take
  • The tell is two claws in his mouth and a yawn that does not end
  • It is beaten by staying in the plain air between the pieces, and it empties him

FEATHERBED (the Lie-Down) · Technique

FEATHERBED is basically deep rest: the power to sink into a bed of glassed air where poison, bleeding and tiredness all but stop in him. It is THE CHAISE grown large and called aloud.

Capabilities

His eyes go fully heavy-lidded, the lavender shimmer thickens into a slow halo of hanging droplets, and the air under him gels into a long clear bed that carries him wherever he drifts, at a walk. TORPOR strikes as deep as his Fate II will carry it.

Applications

  • All but stopping poison, bleeding and fatigue in him
  • Riding out a week without spending down
  • Silent, it is only the bed; called, he sinks into it with a sigh and the whole of it blooms

Associations

  • Hypnather, with Abython’s float
  • The Fate Path for the bed, the Spirit Path for what it does in him
  • TORPOR, which it deepens

Limitations

  • It sips about 0.2 percent of his reserve a minute, under his recovery, and he can hold it for a week
  • Its counter is TORPOR’s: inside FEATHERBED he is at his slowest to wake

Releases

THE LIE-IN · the One

THE LIE-IN, the One, is basically domain opening: the power to open and pay for his Domain. It has no other use.

Capabilities

The held note is that slow ringing pane of glass, and the field the lavender of a sleepless dawn. It opens the Domain in one long yawn.

Applications

  • Opening and powering THE LIE-IN the Domain

Associations

  • Hypnather, through the Fate Path

Limitations

  • It holds 3 minutes 20 seconds from full
  • Held past its limit it is a Crystal Fracture Event, which would be the first hard thing that ever happened to his Crystal
  • Afterward he runs Overgrown until he sleeps, and he sleeps at once

Domain

THE LIE-IN (Vaross · Thaal-Snorr) · Domain

THE LIE-IN the Domain is basically beginning suppression: the power to make everything new, within a hundred and fifty yards, cost three times as much to start. It is the same law as the glass, laid on beginnings.

Capabilities

It is the Sleep Crown laid on beginnings. To begin, a thing has first to get up out of rest, and a thing that runs hot is already half up: a damp log on a cold hearth only smokes, and a coal from a hot fire lights whatever it is laid on. The Domain is a claim laid on the ground, on what has to be so before anything can begin, and what it says is that everything in it lies deeper and must climb three times as far to get up. The Measurewrights call it a raised activation barrier. He calls it sing-song and half asleep, the Vaross first and then the Common.

What is already moving keeps its pace, slowly, and anything that wants to begin has to climb three times as far. The law makes no exception for what he would rather spare, since he does not aim it. Its cruelest reach is one he has never aimed: a heart begins every beat from nothing and a chest begins every breath, and a body with no reserve left to pay the raised price stutters on both.

Applications

  • A fresh working costs three times its price to light
  • A fire already burning sinks to embers, and a new one will not catch; a fox-fire will not catch in it
  • A nerve fires late
  • A blade swung from stillness starts as if through mud
  • A dying heart, which has to begin every beat again from nothing, climbs the hill late and weaker each time

Associations

  • Hypnather, through the Fate Path
  • The One, the Fate road’s only Release, which counts as the Second and opens and powers it
  • His Sovereignty of 300, which holds it

Limitations

  • The same rise is charged to him: he cannot start anything quickly in it either, and he never does
  • It burns about half a percent of his reserve every second it stands, 3 minutes 20 seconds from full
  • A working already running when it opens keeps running, and so does anything already in motion
  • It is beaten by heat: the claim makes everything climb three times as far before it can begin, and a thing that runs hot is already half up, so a fighter who runs hot enough lights anyway, and a Domain held against a furnace burns through his reserve fast
  • A stronger Sovereignty contests it from its edge and takes the ground back hall by hall
  • Bodies can simply walk out of it, slowly
