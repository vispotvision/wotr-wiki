Muken Moto — Abilities in full

Muken Moto — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202640 min read

Card: Muken Moto

THE SPLINTERED WORK

THE SPLINTERED WORK is basically shatterpoint mastery: the power to see where anything is ready to break, and to finish the job. It is his signature, the one idea his Trait, his Works, his Releases and his Domain each express in their own way, said in northern Common with the Kōkan’s old words beside it.

Capabilities

A salt cutter reads a face for where it will give before he sets the pick, and Fractura, the Crack, runs that habit through his Perception. Fractura’s idea is that everything has a place where it will go, and its operation is separation: a thing is parted along the one place where it was always going to part. Nothing fails at its full strength; a thing fails at a flaw it already had, and a soul that has become that idea sees the flaw the way a cutter sees the soft seam in a face. The gold lines he sees over walls, bodies and formations are every place where a thing is ready to go, and Judicium, the Scale, whose idea is truth told apart, ranks them worst first. Smiths and masons, and the Measurewrights after them, call it stress concentration, and will tell you that the strain at the tip of a crack is many times what the sound stone beside it carries, and the sharper the tip, the worse; their figure is good, and they have no idea why a soul can see the tip.

Every made thing has a note as well, the pitch it rings at when struck, and a thing that is ready to break already sounds it: a cracked thing has two notes, the steady one of the sound part and a sour wavering one from the crack. He finishes the job by giving the thing its own ending in time with that note, each push adding to the last, until it parts at the place where it was always going to part. The strength that breaks the thing is the thing’s own, and Muken only gives it the way. The scholars call the note a natural frequency and the finishing resonance, and the comparison every Kharven child knows is a swing that goes higher when it is pushed in time; it is a good comparison, and it says nothing about why the crack answers him.

The Works of Hataraki are how his blood takes hold of this. They are no spells. Each is one inherited grip on one job of Fate, and the blood gives the grip and nothing else. He uses them the way most of his house fights: the grip finds the thing and names it (this is dead, this is whole, this is crossing, this is too much), and the work itself is done by his body and his blade. It rides a blow plainly. The Eye picks the place, the edge goes into it with everything his arm has, and the note goes in behind the edge, so the cut does its own harm and then the crack runs on from the cut.

Applications

  • Reading where a wall, a body, a line of men, a road, a promise or a war will give
  • Ending what is already dead without harming what stands next to it
  • Running the worst flaw in one chosen thing
  • Standing under a load by giving it a road to the ground
  • Closing a wound from the mending side, by its living edges
  • Giving him one chance in one place against a higher Stage

Techniques

  • SEAM-SIGHT - the principle as sight, always on
  • DEBT RECOGNITION - the same ranking, worst first, turned on the people in the room
  • SHINGAN - the Moto eye, SEAM-SIGHT held open
  • DEAD NOTE! - a dead crack’s sour note held through one long breath until the dead thing comes apart
  • THE WAY SHE DID IT - the knowledge from the mending side, the edges of a wound held on their living note until they close
  • OPEN HAND - the grip on a soul’s thread, held or let go
  • JIKAN NO SHIGOTO - a quiet Work that follows a cause back a knot at a time
  • CHISHIKI NO SHIGOTO - a quiet Work that hears what a man hums under his face
  • DIDN’T CRACK! - the same knowledge turned round, a load given a road through him to the ground
  • THE WHOLE HAND - all six grips closing together, priced as all six
  • FURE! - the blade as a tuning fork on one target, the way a singer can break a glass by holding its note
  • EVERY ROPE IN THE CITY - the same ring with no choosing in it, every note at once

Associations

  • Fractura finds the flaw and does the breaking; Judicium, the Scale, ranks the flaws; Petralon, the Foundation Stone, carries a load down
  • The Body Path does the work, through his body and blade; Fate is where each Work’s grip takes hold
  • The Kōkan blood gives the grip on the Works of Hataraki, and Faisal taught him six of them in the Plane of Fate
  • Tsugigane, his bonded sword, carries HIBI’s note and, in the silver thread of its join, his sister’s
  • The Kharven breath, which every Work and the release start on
  • He says every name low and plain; the marks on the names are how the north repeats them

Limitations

  • He can work only at a seam that is already there; a whole thing gives him nothing to see and nothing to hold
  • He stands at Knotwright, Fate IV, the fourth rung of his Fate Mastery; the hall where the table was cleared saw him at Weaver, an overreach, once, at a price, and no Moto has ever found the pull light
  • His Dominion Fate wins the contest and never lowers the price, so he cannot afford to force a Work on anything that pushes back
  • Dead things, willing wounds and loads on his own bones do not push back, which keeps the small uses small; reaching for something further from likely, the price climbs like a wall
  • About a fourteenth of every spend burns in his Shell’s furnace instead of reaching the thing: the heat behind the left eye, the tremor in the sword hand, the bell in his back teeth
  • His Eye will not switch off at the supper table, and he sees where everyone he loves will break
  • Each time he forces it the gold seam widens a thread and does not close
  • Breaking the oath he swore by his honor, that no Moto marches on Zettari land under his rule, is his Shear Break condition
  • Anyone watching sees it coming: the Kharven breath, in through the nose until the ribs ache, and the gold seam in the left iris widening
  • His aura’s gray goes nearly black while its seams brighten to forge-gold, and lamps and cups shiver before anything breaks
  • Against a higher Stage it gives him one chance in one place, because the strength that breaks the thing is the thing’s own
  • It is countered by bringing him nothing already flawed, by damping the note with cold, noise or a flat hand, by taking the ground away from DIDN’T CRACK, or by reaching him before the breath is out
  • Under strain it fails in Fractura’s own manner, which the Measurewrights call self-inspection: the bearer is shown his own flaws first, and a blade held too long on another thing’s note finds its own crack
  • Driven by rage it fails the other way, as runaway crazing, ending what was rotten and then what stood near the rot, and it does not stop by itself

Traits

SEAM-SIGHT · Primary Trait

SEAM-SIGHT is basically weakness detection: the power to see where anything is carrying its weight and where it will give. It is his Primary Trait, always on, and THE SPLINTERED WORK starts from it.

Capabilities

Before he ever knew it had a name he read a salt face for the place it would give before he set the pick, and his soul still does that before it tries anything else. Within about fifty paces he sees where a made thing, a body or a line of men is carrying its weight, and where that weight has gathered in a crack, as fine gold lines laid over the world like crazing in old glaze. He sees them without trying, and his own worst flaw is always the one he sees first.

It reads more than stone. A man’s locked loads show as colored bands, the way the colors run in a sheet of stressed glass held up to the sky. It shows where a road, a promise or a war would give. It can read every stance an opponent might take in the next breath, as a field of seams so thick it looks like light.

Applications

  • Finding a locked arm, or a knee that takes all the load
  • Finding a wall built new over an old frost-seam
  • Finding the feud running down the middle of a clan wedge
  • Reading where a road, a promise or a war would give
  • Reading every stance an opponent might take in the next breath

Associations

  • Fractura, read through the Body Path by the eye his blood gave him
  • Judicium ranks what it shows, worst flaw first
  • The cutter’s habit from the salt face, lived and never taught
  • Held open it is SHINGAN

Limitations

  • It reads only load and fault, so a thing truly whole, freshly forged or simply soft gives him nothing
  • It shows where a man will break, never what the man means
  • It reaches about fifty paces
  • It costs nothing he notices at rest, and a slow heat behind the left eye under strain
  • It does not switch off, and it has run hot since the war and will not rest, which is Resonance Fatigue
  • The heat and the widening gold seam in his left iris give it away
  • It can be blinded: when Fate collects, the Eye fills with blood and sees nothing
  • It is countered by offering nothing flawed, or by moving faster than a fifty-pace read can follow

THE UNWRITTEN NAME · Inherited Trait, the Kōkan blood

THE UNWRITTEN NAME is basically nameless existence: the power to go unfound by anything that looks a man up by his name. It is blood and no working, and it belongs to the red line and not to THE SPLINTERED WORK.

Capabilities

No book in the world has his name in it. The Kōkan wrote their name in a mark no register in the inner world can read, and Muken carries that blood without the mark ever having been entered against him. Every Nalūn seal-token, every Moto fate-read and every working that finds a person by the house written over them looks at him and finds a page with no ink on it. Ulf Stark’s token went cold and silent in his palm, and Shigure Tenrai’s fate-rings flickered and slid off him on the stair.

It has another side. A grip nobody ever wrote down is a grip nobody can call in, so the Kōkan hold on the Works is neither loan nor gift.

Applications

  • Staying unread by fate-sight, token-reads and name-workings
  • Wearing THE WHOLE HAND over it like a hand over a lamp, so his thread runs smooth where he stands and nothing there can find a seam in him until he lets the mask go
  • Keeping the Kōkan hold on the Works where nobody can call it in

Associations

  • The Kōkan blood, the red line of the Moto
  • It has no Path, no Lens and no Form
  • THE WHOLE HAND, which can be laid over it as a mask

Limitations

  • It hides him only from things that read by name and lineage; plain eyes, a blade and a good guess see him as well as anyone
  • It costs nothing in Essence, he cannot turn it off, and it does not grow
  • No token will ever hum for him, and no house seal will bind under his name until the Kōkan mark is set on it by hand
  • Tokens going dead in his hand give him away
  • It is countered by fighting him with your eyes open and your fate-sight shut

DEBT RECOGNITION · Secondary Trait

DEBT RECOGNITION is basically threat identification: the power to feel who in the room means him harm and who wants something from him. It is his Secondary Trait, left by the shore.

Capabilities

It formed in the three days on the Seat road after the shore, when every man he looked at was either bringing his sister back or keeping her from him. Judicium, the Scale, marks the people within about sixty paces who mean him or his harm, and the people who want something from him. He feels it the way a nose marks smoke: a pressure in the Eye, ranked worst first, and his head turns before they move.

It is strongest against intent that is clear and loud, and it goes quiet against a man who truly wants nothing. It marked Amelia Greymane as a solid, quiet weight, and that is all it could tell him.

Applications

  • Marking the people who mean him or his harm, worst first
  • Marking the people who want something from him

Associations

  • Judicium, the Scale, reads it
  • It lands through the Attraction Layer
  • Lived, never taught

Limitations

  • It has a reach of about sixty paces and no more, and a man waiting outside it is outside the Eye as well
  • It marks appetite as readily as malice, and a king who reads everyone at his table as a debt stops sitting at tables
  • It is hardening into reading every face as an account, which is Rigidity
  • It takes a little of his reserve while it runs hot, and almost nothing when he is calm
  • His head turning toward a threat a moment before the threat moves gives it away
  • It is countered by wanting nothing and standing very still, or by waiting outside sixty paces

STONES ON A CAIRN · Passive Trait

STONES ON A CAIRN is basically influential presence: the power to make a room stop and listen when he means what he says. It is a Passive Trait, left by the cairn on the ridge.

Capabilities

Since the cairn his voice comes lower and slower when he means it, each word set on the last like stones laid one on another, and the Attraction in it carries. Cups tremble, lamps on their chains swing all together, and people who were talking stop. Where there are no lamps the room still answers: frost on the walls trembles all together when he draws breath to speak, and the pillars hum back.

Kiyome Byakuya heard it in the death-house and thought it the first true Moto voice she had heard in her life.

Associations

  • It lands through the Attraction Layer, on his voice, as a claim at its smallest
  • Lived, never taught

Limitations

  • It rides his voice and his conviction, and does nothing when he does not mean what he says
  • It carries as far as a hall carries a low voice, and dies in a battle’s noise
  • It costs nothing
  • The lamps give it away
  • It is countered by noise and by distance

Techniques

SHINGAN (心眼 · Shingan, the heart-eye) · Bloodline Faculty, Moto

SHINGAN is basically structural sight: the power to read where everything is carrying its weight. It is the common Moto eye, and in him it is SEAM-SIGHT held open.

Capabilities

It woke on the Foot’s stair. In Muken it came with something the other lines do not have: a hairline seam of gold running out from the amber fleck low in his left iris. Shigure named it on the spot: any Moto brat can see where a wall wants to fall, and the gold in it is Kōkan. The seam is the first of the Kamigan, the crown eye that takes what all the other eyes see and ranks it.

The seam has torn wider since, and the crown eye has shown itself. A vow tore the seam and left a line of blood down his cheek, and the gold has come into both eyes. With Bara’s knowledge in him and the boy gone out of the body, both eyes have burned one even gold from lid to lid with no seam, ring or pupil, and Naiser Yukari had no name for it but Kamigan. Under THE WHOLE HAND both irises craze gold from edge to edge and bleed. When Fate collects, the gold goes out and both eyes go red through iris and white together, the red of the line of the red chair.

Applications

  • Reading load and fault in matter, bodies and formations

Associations

  • The Moto blood gives the eye, and the Kōkan gold in it opens the road to the Kamigan
  • Fractura, read through the Body Path
  • SEAM-SIGHT, the Trait it holds open

Limitations

  • It cannot resolve intent, a lie, or anything with no structure
  • Held open it takes a little under eight percent of his reserve a minute, some eleven and a half minutes from full to Starvation
  • It is heat behind the eye for as long as it is held
  • Forced past its depth, the seam widens by a thread and never closes
  • The gold seam gives it away
  • It is countered by giving it nothing flawed, or nothing it can see

DEAD NOTE! (破滅の業 · Hametsu no Gō, the Work of Ruins) · Bloodline Art, Hataraki

DEAD NOTE is basically resonant destruction: the power to end what is already dead. It is one of the six Works of Hataraki, and the one his blood leans to. Not to be confused with FURE, which uses the blade to ring one chosen thing at its worst flaw.

Capabilities

The Work of Ruins is his blood’s grip on one job of Fate, the ending of what has already begun to end, and Fractura does the breaking through his body. A dead crack, with the rot already through it, rings sour and wavering against the steady note of the sound stone around it: the thing is already telling the world where it will go. Muken finds that sour note with the Eye, takes it in the Kharven breath and holds it through one long breath and nothing else. The thing’s own readiness to end does the rest, until the dead thing comes apart along its own fault.

Nothing sound beside it is touched. The strength that breaks the thing is the thing’s own, which is why it takes so little from him and why it finds only what was already dying.

It can touch a living man, inside his oath, where a death done to him was never finished, because that crack is already dead. The ending that runs it is all the target’s own, whatever his Stage.

Applications

  • Bringing down rotten rope, timber and stonework without harming what stands next to it
  • Ending the lying ward-stone over the Seat’s gatehouse from half a mile away
  • Finishing a death that was begun and never completed, in a living man

Associations

  • The Kōkan grip on the Works of Hataraki, taught by Faisal in the Plane of Fate
  • Fate is where the grip takes hold, and Fractura does the breaking through his body
  • SEAM-SIGHT finds the note, and the Kharven breath carries it
  • His Dominion Fate is set against the target’s Persistence and Continuity, and a dead thing cannot meet it

Limitations

  • It works only on what is already dead, and he has sworn by his honor to crack nothing else
  • A rope’s worth of rot takes about two percent of his reserve; a thing held at a distance takes a fifth or more
  • The breath gives it away: in through the nose until the ribs ache, out through the mouth until nothing is left
  • Noise, damping, cold or a hand laid flat on the struck thing breaks the note
  • Bringing him nothing already dead leaves it nothing to end
  • Driven by rage it ends what was rotten and then what stood near the rot, and it does not stop by itself

THE WAY SHE DID IT (純潔の業 · Junketsu no Gō, the Work of Purity) · Bloodline Art, Hataraki

THE WAY SHE DID IT is basically healing: the power to close a wound by listening to its living edges. It is one of the six Works of Hataraki, and the breaker’s knowledge from the mending side.

Capabilities

Purity establishes a thing in its proper shape and holds it there. He learned it by learning to look at the edges of a wound and never the wound, the way his sister did. He lays his palm flat over the hurt, says nothing, listens for the living edges, and holds their note until they close along the shape they already had. The closing is done by the living flesh itself.

It can run without being called, along a shape already laid round him. A harness laced in the old order is a shape, and the Work finds the shape in the cords and runs along it, so the plates press broken ribs together and a greave draws a kneecap round and holds it like a splint.

Purity has an Obsession face, which purges whatever does not fit the shape. That is the Shirogane way.

Applications

  • Knitting his own broken ribs, the cut across his side, his forearm and his palm
  • Shutting a long fresh cut clean, without hiding the scar
  • Pressing broken ribs together and splinting a kneecap through a harness laced in the old order

Associations

  • The Kōkan grip on the Works of Hataraki, taught by Faisal
  • Fate is where the grip takes hold, and the living flesh does the closing
  • His sister, whose way of looking at a wound it is

Limitations

  • It works only on what is living and willing, and Faisal forbade him to do it to a man who has not asked
  • Old harm the body has already set crooked it will not touch, because the proper shape is now the crooked one: the wrist from the stamping floor, the shin from the Veyrath ditch, his knuckles
  • A fresh wound takes about five percent of his reserve and a quiet minute
  • The flat palm and the silence give it away
  • It is countered by hurting him faster than he can listen

OPEN HAND (精霊の業 · Seirei no Gō, the Work of Spirit) · Bloodline Art, Hataraki

OPEN HAND is basically soul anchoring: the power to feel a soul’s thread, and hold it or let it go. It is one of the six Works of Hataraki, the Work of what lies between life and death.

Capabilities

He can feel a soul’s thread where it runs, and hold it, or open his hand and let it pass. He held his sister’s on the road out without knowing he was doing it. In the Plane of Fate he let her go, and wept for the first time since he was nine.

It finds a living thread even in a dead hall: a bond to the dead, burned nearly through and still there.

Applications

  • Holding a soul’s thread on the crossing between living and dead
  • Letting a thread pass
  • Finding the one living bond in a dead place

Associations

  • The Kōkan grip on the Works of Hataraki, taught by Faisal
  • Fate is where the grip takes hold, on the crossing between living and dead

Limitations

  • It needs a thread that is still there to hold
  • It cannot call back a thread that has gone, nor keep one that will not stay
  • It costs little in Essence, and a great deal otherwise
  • His closed hand gives it away
  • It is countered by distance and time

JIKAN NO SHIGOTO (時間の仕事 · the Work of Temporality) · Bloodline Art, Hataraki

JIKAN NO SHIGOTO is basically retrocognition: the power to follow a cause back, a knot at a time. It is one of the six Works of Hataraki, and a quiet one.

Capabilities

Temporality is the Work of cause and consequence. Holding one thread, he can follow it back along what made it, a knot at a time. He followed his sister’s death back that way to the Shirogane’s thousand-year wound of standing second. He uses it only to follow.

Applications

  • Tracing a death back along what caused it

Associations

  • The Kōkan grip on the Works of Hataraki, taught by Faisal
  • Fate is where the grip takes hold
  • THE WHOLE HAND, which can read past a knot this Work stops at

Limitations

  • It shows causes and changes none
  • A long thread takes long hours
  • Each knot takes about one percent of his reserve, and leaves a heavy head
  • A knot where the cause has gone dark, or has been hidden, stops it every time
  • It is countered by a thread too tangled to follow before the moment passes

CHISHIKI NO SHIGOTO (知識の仕事 · the Work of Knowledge) · Bloodline Art, Hataraki

CHISHIKI NO SHIGOTO is basically emotion detection: the power to hear what a man is humming under his face. It is one of the six Works of Hataraki, and a quiet one.

Capabilities

Knowledge reveals what a thing is. He hears what a man is humming under his face: fear, grief, appetite. He uses it only to hear.

Associations

  • The Kōkan grip on the Works of Hataraki, taught by Faisal
  • Fate is where the grip takes hold

Limitations

  • It reveals and does not act: it will tell him Kagenori Tenrai is grieving without telling him what to do about it
  • Each reading takes about one percent of his reserve
  • His head tipping as if listening gives it away
  • It is countered by a man who has gone quiet inside

DIDN’T CRACK! (仕事の壁 · Shigoto no Kabe, the Work of Bearing Load) on BARANSU NO SHIGOTO (バランスの仕事 · the Work of Balance) · Bloodline Art, Hataraki, and its Application

DIDN’T CRACK is basically force redirection: the power to stand under a load and send it into the ground. Balance is one of the six Works of Hataraki, and the wall, Bearing Load, is what he makes of it.

Capabilities

It is the breaker’s knowledge turned round. A cutter who can see where a load gathers can see the road it wants to take, and a load need not stop in him if he gives it a road to the ground. Petralon, the Foundation Stone, whose idea is the foundation, to become what other things stand on, carries it down through bone and stance as a sound pillar carries a roof. He learned it kneeling beside Faisal under the Lawbell’s weight.

Applications

  • Carrying even a seven-Stage Pressure through him into the ground
  • Standing in front of something that means to come down on everyone behind him

Techniques

  • BARANSU NO SHIGOTO - the Work under it: a load passes through him and goes down into the ground
  • SHIGOTO NO KABE - the wall: he plants both feet, Balance takes the blow through his bones into the earth, and he does not move

Associations

  • The Kōkan grip on the Works of Hataraki, taught by Faisal
  • Fate is where the grip takes hold, and his body does the carrying through Petralon

Limitations

  • It needs ground under him that will take the load; airborne, braced on ice, or on a floor that will not take it, the weight has nowhere to go
  • Against a load that comes from every side at once, or from below, it has no path
  • A floor that holds only what its owner lets it hold gives the load nowhere to go, and every blow stops in his body
  • Balance takes about three percent of his reserve to open, and a slow bleed while held
  • The wall takes about four percent to set and a steady drain to hold, some eight and a half minutes from full
  • It cracks his bones a little every time, which shows as hairline cracks down both forearms from wrist to elbow
  • Whatever he sends down the ground has to keep, and enough loads open a hollow under the stone until the hill goes out from under him
  • He kneels, or plants both feet and stops moving, and that gives it away
  • It is countered by taking the ground out from under him, making him move, or hitting him from where the ground is not

THE WHOLE HAND (全体性の働き · Zentai-sei no Hataraki, the Work of Totality) · Bloodline Art, Hataraki; the Kōkan’s own

THE WHOLE HAND is basically destiny perception: the power to close every Work on one thread at once and read the thing whole. It is the crown of the other Works and the Kōkan’s own, tied to the Kamigan.

Capabilities

Each Work is a grip on one job: what is failing, what ought to stand, what is crossing, what is due, what a thing is, what is overdone. A hand can close only one way at a time, which is why every Moto who ever lived held one grip well and the rest by rote. Totality is the hand itself. All six close on the same thread together, and the thread is read whole: what it is, what it is due, where it fails and where it holds, what it carries and where it is crossing to. A thing seen that way has no surprises left in it and needs no predicting, and he has stopped trying to read Fate ahead of itself, as the rest of his house does and buries its children for.

He sits inside the run of things and sees where each action goes down into its consequence, smooth where it is smooth and broken where it meets a fault. A complete thing can also be stepped into, the way a man steps into a river at the place where the water slides past itself in layers with no eddies in it. The scholars call that laminar flow: smooth water carries a stone without tumbling it.

It first came uncalled. The one bell in his teeth split into seven notes, the six Works and over them a seventh he had never heard, which gathered the rest into one chord, and everything in front of him went to glaze: every crack already in the air, the ground and the man, shown all at once as one total thing he can hold. Now he calls it low and plain, the way a man says grace over a bowl: “Zentai-sei no Hataraki.”

Applications

  • Reading a thread whole, past a dark knot that stops JIKAN NO SHIGOTO
  • Watching where a working sent across hundreds of miles of sky will run out of consequence, so that it ends there without his lifting a hand
  • Finding his own thread, where he is and where he is due, and letting the flow carry his place in the world down the smooth part and through a pocket left open in the Veil
  • Laying it over himself and THE UNWRITTEN NAME as a mask
  • Keeping it closed in the left hand for the length of a fight, holding a thousand mended threads steady while the right hand stays on the sword

Techniques

  • SPLIT AND RESOLVE - two words on one short falling cut with Tsugigane: “Split” is Ruins, every crack in the thing running at once, and “Resolve” is Purity, every piece sent to its proper end
  • SPLIT AND RESOLVE, how it works - a thing heated and quenched for centuries has one place where the whole of it is waiting to go, as a glassman’s drop laughs at a hammer on its bulb and goes to powder if the thin tail is snapped; he finds that place and cuts there
  • SPLIT AND RESOLVE, what it leaves - the thing shatters from the cut outward into pieces no bigger than rice grains, and the world keeps the line of that cut, still called Tsugigane’s Resolve, so a later cut can fall on it

Associations

  • The Kōkan blood, with the other Works in hand
  • Faisal withheld it and never taught it, until Muken could stand before seven lines, a dozen Banner Houses and a Zettari prince and want every one of them to stay exactly what they are and still be one thing together; then it came by itself
  • The Kamigan, the crown eye
  • Every move of UNLIMITED WORKS OF FATE is THE WHOLE HAND closed on one purpose

Limitations

  • It follows cause down into consequence, so a thing whose arriving has been cut loose from its going gives the hand nothing to close on, and he does not see it come
  • The step through the Veil needs an opening already there; a Body walker cannot step through the Soul Plane as a Yukari does, so this is the long way
  • It is priced as all six Works at once, far above what his rung of Fate Mastery, Fate IV, can pay for, and it is paid in blood from both eyes and the nose and in bells that go from ringing to aching
  • With the reserve short it goes through the floor of his reserve into his own substance, a debt the Crystal does not refill
  • The charge shows: the Kharven breath, held, then the left iris crazing gold like an old cup, then the right, then heat, pressure and a thin line of blood from each eye
  • His aura does not spread but draws in tight round the body, more seam than stone, six notes sound in his teeth as one chord, and the gravel under him beds lower
  • When Fate calls the debt in it stops like a mill stopping, six bells gone to one low note under the stone
  • Its Obsession face is the hunger to gather everything into one hand; his father reached for it, and that was what the cellars were
  • That is no longer only a warning: with the red in his eyes and the rules twisted in his fist he has felt his own mouth and jaw set into his father’s, the year before the cellars, and not stopped
  • It is countered by putting the result ahead of its cause, by breaking his hold while both hands are full, or by being so whole that the reading finds nothing to use

UNLIMITED WORKS OF FATE · Legendary art, Bara’s own, given as knowledge; counted in Acts

UNLIMITED WORKS OF FATE is basically fate manipulation on credit: the power to do every one of the first god’s jobs at once, and the bill always comes. It is a legendary art, Bara’s own, given to him as knowledge and not as a blessing, and its moves all run on the one idea below.

Capabilities

Bara wrote the Works before time to keep the world running. In the Plane of Fate Bara laughed, called the boy the White Wolf and the scavenger of Fate, a man who could see the flaws in any possibility and pick it clean, touched his forehead, and gave him the knowledge of his Ultimate Techniques. It is knowledge and no blessing, so nothing pays for it but him.

He opens it himself: every Work he has, all at once, with no limit set on what they are for except the one he sets as he names the move. That is the name, and he chose it alone, knowing Yusif would have laughed. Each move is THE WHOLE HAND closed on one purpose, so the charge is the same breath and the same crazed gold, and he speaks the name of the art aloud before the name of the move.

Several moves lean on roots. They come down from where the Plane of Fate leans closest and go into stone like roots into wet soil, and they take a standing load into ground far deeper than one man. They also fill him, and not by drawing. Purity knows the proper shape of his reserve, which is full, Balance knows what went out of it and where, and the roots make his Crystal the place the flow of Fate wants to go, so it comes in by itself. Foresters say a tree does the same, that it does not suck water up but packs its root with what it has gathered until the water outside cannot stay outside and pushes in, and they call it root pressure; it is a good comparison and a forester’s word, and it is not the reason.

Applications

  • Cutting a man whose skin seals wounds by drinking the force of a blow
  • Calling in the claim on a person’s past
  • Lifting a seal that has been pressed on a soul
  • Mending a person’s broken threads, and tying body back to spirit
  • Catching a collapse and setting it into a floor, a wall and a stair
  • Carrying a standing load into the ground through the roots
  • Refilling his reserve through the roots, to about 95 percent, on account

Techniques

  • CLEAVE - a cut with no force in it at all, falling on the line of an old cut because the world keeps the mark of a cut; a skin that seals wounds by drinking a blow’s force as heat has nothing to drink, and bleeds like any man’s
  • CLEAVE, as a real stroke - the rising diagonal from the hip with Tsugigane, which treats the body, the Shell, the Core and the layer where a soul’s share of fate is tied as the next thing along one line, the way a good cut goes through a bundle of bamboo, the mat behind it and the post behind that
  • CLEAVE, what it leaves - a seam into the Plane of Fate, the color of nothing, and whatever energy touches the seam is taken out of the world, chaos and order alike; he says it low, and the last of a set he shouts
  • NADI - one committed stroke allowed to run down every line it might have taken, ten seams, twenty, five hundred, and then closed back into the one; Temporality knows the cut is due, Knowledge knows what it is, and Balance lays its weight along every other course the motion could have run
  • FIRST ACT: INFINITE CLEAVE - the space round him curves and it cuts along every road the target could take, so every step away from him lands nearer, and a fist thrown straight down a bent road passes a hand’s width wide
  • INFINITE REACH - the world folds toward one target like cloth toward a pin, and a gold blade no longer than a forearm, the shape of the cutter’s chisel he carried at eight, comes down the grain of every seam in every stance the target might take, including the ones it has not chosen yet
  • SABA NO ROSA - the Reckoning of Roses: the sky grows roots, each rose a deposit and each thorn an unpaid debt, and the claim on a person’s past is called in a deposit at a time, the past staying true while the present grip on it thins like a spoon of salt in a river
  • CLEANSE - the roots go white, and a seal pressed on a soul lifts in flakes and spreads along them across the whole Plane until it is everywhere and therefore nowhere, like a drop of ink in a still lake
  • FUSION - gold thread off his fingers splices every broken strand of a person end to end, the cutters’ long splice, thicker at the mend and stronger there than anywhere else; it wakes the threads they never knew they had, and ties body back to spirit
  • WEAVE, then RESOLVE - Ruin lets the dead rubble fall, Purity holds the whole things in their shape, Balance turns each weight sideways to brace the next, and everything falling is knotted over and under its neighbor into a net; the second word pulls the net hard into a floor, a wall and a stair, the way a wet rope goes hard when the knot is pulled tight

Associations

  • Bara, the First Epoch, who gave it with a touch on the forehead in the Plane of Fate
  • THE WHOLE HAND, which every move is, and the six Works under it
  • Tsugigane, which puts a smith’s edge into CLEAVE
  • NADI is the old readers’ word for a channel, and SABA NO ROSA has its own page among the spells
  • INFINITE CLEAVE is the Stannvaard philosophers’ saying, that a thrown stone tries every road at once and goes by the one that costs least, made into a blade
  • He calls it “Unlimited Fate Works”

Limitations

  • It runs on credit: every share is entered against his name in Fate, and nothing comes down out of Fate that Fate does not mark
  • The bill always comes, and it has come once already and taken the Eye
  • When Fate collects it takes everything below a quarter of his reserve, the floor under it and the place under the floor, with blood from both eyes, the nose and the ears, his hearing muffled and the Eye blind
  • The gold seam in his eye tears a thread wider with every share
  • Every use runs on the gift or on borrowed sap, and none of it on his own rung
  • A move that leans on the roots dies with the roots: cut above the ground, they hang gray and the sap stops where it stood
  • Fire that has stopped telling fuel from air will eat the roots, though there is always more root
  • Used out of the body, paid by the gift, he comes back vomiting blood, his aura’s gold dim as brass, his teeth chattering
  • A crossing and FUSION together took him down to about 40 percent of his reserve, with blood from eyes and nose and the left knee loose; WEAVE costs more than FUSION
  • NADI at five hundred seams takes him past the half and past the quarter in one stroke with nothing refilling
  • SABA NO ROSA costs half a percent of his full reserve a rose
  • A thing that has bitten off its own thread has no knot for CLEAVE to part, and a thing with nothing tied gives it none either
  • He names the art aloud before every move, so it is heard coming
  • It is countered by owing nothing and carrying no old cut, by cutting the roots above the ground or taking the ground away, or by breaking his hold before the mends set

Releases

FURE! (ひび · Hibi, the Hairline) · First Release

FURE is basically shattering: the power to ring one thing at its own note until its worst crack runs. It is his First Release, which the north calls the Crack, and Tsugigane, his bonded sword, carries it.

Capabilities

He calls “Fure,” shake, the word the Kōkan used before it was taken from the line in the cellars at Halcrest, and Faisal gave it back. On the call the blade begins to ring at the natural note of one thing before him, a single blade, a bone, a plate of armor, an iron hand, and holds it there. Fractura and the Eye find that thing’s worst flaw, the place where it was always going to part, and the held note keeps telling the thing where it will go until the crack runs there on its own. The blade is the tuning fork, the way a singer can break a glass by holding its note, which is the comparison the north makes.

One target, one crack. Called silent, the blade only hums and the crack runs slow. The bond carries the call: torn from his hand and lying paces away, Tsugigane still rings on “Fure.”

Applications

  • Breaking a single blade, a bone, a plate of armor or an iron hand
  • Passing over a crack in plain sight to take a hidden one, such as a cold shut, a seam where the metal never fused in the casting
  • Ringing Tsugigane from paces away, out of his hand

Associations

  • Fractura, through the Body Path, as a strike
  • SEAM-SIGHT finds the flaw, and the Kharven breath comes before the call
  • Tsugigane carries HIBI’s note and, in the silver thread of its join, his sister’s
  • The word a blade of his house answers to while it sleeps is Asami

Limitations

  • It needs a flaw; something whole gives it nothing
  • It needs a blade to carry the note
  • A blade held on a note too long finds its own crack
  • It takes about eight percent of his reserve to wake and a fast drain to hold, a little under two minutes from full
  • The cost shows as heat behind the eyes and a tremor in the sword hand that grows with each new note he tunes to
  • The Kharven breath before it gives it away
  • It is countered by killing the note with cold, padding or a hand flat on the struck steel, by changing what he must read before he can hold one pitch, or by bringing nothing flawed

EVERY ROPE IN THE CITY (ひび割れ · Hibiware, the Crazing) · Second Release

EVERY ROPE IN THE CITY is basically a destructive field: the power to ring everything around him at once and find every hairline flaw in it. It is his Second Release. It woke once, it has never been called, and Faisal has forbidden it.

Capabilities

The single crack becomes a web. The blade rings at the note of everything around him at once, and the rings go out, felt in the long bones and the back teeth, finding every hairline flaw in whatever they pass. It is FURE’s ring with no choosing in it, every note at once, and that is the whole danger.

It woke once, out of him, in the rage after his sister, and crazed the snow of the Foot’s yard and the ends of the woodpile. Faisal named it, and forbade it until he can do to every rope in a city what he did to one rope and leave every sound one standing. He re-closed the crazed snow by holding its note, which is the only thing about it he has mastered.

It presses up under him and he refuses it. He can let it rise as far as his throat and set it down the way a cutter sets down a full load at the top of the fourth gallery, carefully, so it does not spill. A third waking lies under it, and Faisal will not speak of it.

Associations

  • Fractura, through the Body Path, as a field of the same ring
  • FURE, the same ring on one target
  • His Domain, which this release opens and pays for
  • Faisal, who named it and whose condition gives it its name

Limitations

  • Faisal’s word has not been lifted
  • The reserve is there now, but called in grief it would fracture the Crystal along its grief
  • With no choosing in it, it would crack everyone he loves along their flaws
  • He is the counter: his oath, and Faisal’s word
  • From outside it is countered as FURE is, and by distance from everything he loves
