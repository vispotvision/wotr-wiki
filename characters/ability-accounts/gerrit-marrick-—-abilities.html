Gerrit Marrick — Abilities in full

Gerrit Marrick — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202614 min read

Card: Gerrit Marrick

THE LONG SPEAR

THE LONG SPEAR is basically collective strength: the power to gather a whole signed rank into one body and put all of its weight behind one point. The Fields gave his art the name of his office, because by the time he was elected nobody could tell the two apart. Every ability below is the one rank made into one body at a different size.

Capabilities

The Shoulder is the house’s art, and anyone who signs a Marrick contract has it: a rank under one contract, touching at the shoulder, shares its load, and a blow that lands on one man goes down the file and into the ground. Gerrit runs it backward. Where the Shoulder sends the file’s load down, the Long Spear sends it forward, up the shafts and into the man at the point, who is him, and out through one spearhead.

The idea is Coagula’s: gathering the broken. A rank is sixteen scattered men, and under him they come back into one body without ever melting, each man keeping his own shape, his own boots and his own bad back. Its operation is coagulation, the scattered pieces set into one thing. What the one thing does is lean. Sixteen men lean on the drum, and their weight and their coats come up the ash to the front, and Gerrit is the front.

Coagula’s flaw comes with it. A body gathered without melting keeps every gap between the pieces, and the seams between the men are where the next break starts. An enemy who knows the Fields hits the rank between the fourth man and the fifth, where the coat is thinnest and the lean has to cross from one shoulder to another, and the Long Spear comes apart there, all at once, into sixteen ordinary men.

The price is the house’s price, doubled. Everyone pays. When the Shoulder spreads a blow, sixteen men keep the injury; when the Long Spear throws one, sixteen men keep the recoil, the same sprain in the same right wrist, and old companies at Hotwell walk with the same limp because of him. The Measurewrights have an account of it, spreading the impulse over a greater mass and then gathering it again, and they have filed a complaint with the Moot that it does not balance. Gerrit has asked them what they are paid.

Applications

  • Breaking a gatehouse with one file
  • Breaking a cavalry charge, and then the cavalry
  • Throwing a whole afternoon of blows back at the man who dealt them
  • Standing a Stage X off with sergeants

Techniques

  • THE CORNER - the coat run down the whole file
  • THE SETTLING - every blow sent down through him into the ground
  • TO THE HOUR - the contract held against chance
  • SIXTEEN DEEP! - one file’s lean through one spearhead
  • HEAVE! - the ground gives back what it took
  • THE WHOLE WALL! - several companies behind one point
  • THE DRILL SQUARE - the Domain where everyone keeps step

Associations

  • Coagula, the Mending, deep: the rank gathered into one body
  • Petralon, the Foundation Stone, governing: the load taken and sent down
  • Transference, the Give-and-Take, trace: the coat moving down a file
  • The Shoulder, the house’s art, which anyone who signs may have
  • The Body Path carries all of it

Limitations

  • It needs the rank: signed, touching, in term. Alone, he is a big man with a Common spear and a Reflex of D
  • The contract ends at the hour named, and the Long Spear ends with it
  • The seams between the men are where it breaks
  • Everyone pays, in the same bone
  • It does nothing at all against a working on the soul, because no one ever shared a soul’s hurt with a shoulder

Traits

THE CORNER · Passive Trait

THE CORNER is basically aura extension: the power to run his coat down a whole signed rank. On the Fields a corner is the one fighter at the outside angle of a file who can take a blow, “shining for the whole file,” and Gerrit has been a corner for forty years.

Capabilities

A corner’s coat runs along the rank thin as paint on the back row. His does not go thin. It holds at full strength sixteen ranks deep, and thinly along a whole Wall, so that the unwoken back rank of a company stands in a share of something none of them could make for themselves. The gray comes up on the back of every helmet in the file at the same moment, which is how the enemy knows the Old Man has arrived.

Applications

  • Making a file of farmers into a wall
  • Holding ground a much higher Stage would like to walk through

Associations

  • Petralon; the house’s Shoulder
  • The Body Path

Limitations

  • It needs a rank under one contract, shoulders touching
  • Cost: Charge from him alone, about 1,800 EU a second for a file; near an hour and three quarters from full on bad ground, and the whole afternoon on beaten earth, where he draws back nearly as fast as he spends
  • Tell: the gray on every helmet at once
  • Counter: break the file, or go past the Shell

THE SETTLING · Primary Trait

THE SETTLING is basically force redirection to the ground: the power to send every blow that lands on him, or on any signed man touching him, down through his bones and into the earth. It is Petralon’s foundation, which becomes what other things stand on.

Capabilities

Every blow that lands goes down. It goes through his skeleton and his boots and into the ground, and the ground keeps it: the earth under a square he has held all afternoon has a settled look, cracked in lines as straight as a rule. That is what HEAVE! draws on. It also settles him. He is a hand’s breadth lower in the hips than he was at forty, his stance sinks a little after every fight and does not come all the way back up, and one day, the Fields say, he will plant his feet and not be able to lift them.

Petralon’s flaw is the flaw of every foundation. A foundation bears whatever presses down on it, and nothing ever asked it to hold on. Pull him, and he parts.

Applications

  • Taking blows that would kill a Stage IX and staying up
  • Storing a day’s load in the ground for HEAVE!

Associations

  • Petralon, the Foundation Stone
  • The Body Path

Limitations

  • It needs ground under it: a deck, loose sand, open water or a drop takes nothing
  • Cost: he sinks a little with every fight
  • Tell: the earth under the square settles and cracks in a straight line
  • Counter: a pull. Lift him, drag him or take the ground away, and a foundation does not give gracefully

TO THE HOUR · Secondary Trait

TO THE HOUR is basically oath binding: the power to make a signed contract hold against chance until the hour it names. It is forty years of kept contracts, which are vows and the only Fate a Marrick has; a vow binds without a rung, and it pulls nothing down.

Capabilities

Anyone may make a vow, and a kept one binds without a rung on any ladder, so a Marrick who keeps his reads no Fate Mastery at all and is held by them anyway. Gerrit has kept every contract he has ever signed, to the hour, on both sides of every war, and while one of them is in term, nothing his company depends on gives by chance. The strap does not snap. The drumhead does not split. The pay wagon arrives. A Fate fighter who reaches for what the Wall depends on finds every strap held by forty years of kept words, and has to pull against all of them.

Applications

  • Keeping a Fate fighter’s hands off the company’s straps and drums
  • Being on time to the minute, every time, for money

Associations

  • A kept word: a vow, which binds without a rung
  • The contract, which is the law

Limitations

  • The instant the hour passes, it is gone, and so is the Shoulder
  • Cost: a knot for every town he has watched burn because it was not noon
  • Tell: he checks his feet at noon, every day, contract or no contract
  • Counter: a pull made after the hour, or anything that is not chance

Techniques

SIXTEEN DEEP! · Signature Technique

SIXTEEN DEEP! is basically force concentration: the power to put one file’s whole lean through one spearhead in one thrust.

Capabilities

He shouts it, the file leans on the drum, and over a breath and a half sixteen men’s weight and coats come up the ash to him and go out through the point. Alone his thrust lands at his Penetration of 220; with a file behind it, it lands a full step higher on the hidden scale, and it breaks a gatehouse. The Keld who took one at Hessel Down was found forty yards behind his own shield.

Applications

  • The barbican at Oxley, in one afternoon
  • A Keld shield wall at Hessel Down
  • A Stage X who has stopped in front of the rank to gloat

Associations

  • Coagula; the Long Spear
  • The Body Path

Limitations

  • One file, one thrust
  • Cost: about 3% of his reserve, near 354,000 EU, and sixteen sprained right wrists
  • Tell: “Sixteen deep!” and the lean
  • Counter: not to be where the file is pointed; or to take it on a slope, so the lean runs off downhill

HEAVE! · Technique

HEAVE! is basically damage reflection: the power to gather back up out of the ground every blow his square has sent down into it, and throw them out of his spear together.

Capabilities

All afternoon the blows have gone down through THE SETTLING into the earth under the square. A foundation only bears; it gives nothing back. What gives them back is Coagula. The day’s blows lie scattered in the ground like the pieces of a broken pot, and the Mending gathers scattered pieces into one body without melting them, each keeping the shape it had while broken. At sundown, or whenever he likes, the ground cracks open in a straight line from the square to his boots, and the whole day comes up through him over two or three breaths, every blow still the shape it was dealt in, and goes out through the spearhead at the man who dealt most of it. The Fields consider it the funniest thing the Captain does, and the Midmarch kings do not.

Applications

  • Ending a siege at sundown
  • Answering a high-Stage fighter who has spent the day hitting the rank

Associations

  • Coagula, which gathers what Petralon only bore; THE SETTLING
  • The Body Path

Limitations

  • Only what went down under him today, on this ground
  • Cost: about 5% of his reserve, near 590,000 EU, and every man in the square feels the day’s blows a second time
  • Tell: the crack running through the ground to his boots
  • Counter: move him off the ground he stood on; fight him on a deck

THE WHOLE WALL! · Technique

THE WHOLE WALL! is basically army-scale force concentration: the power to put several companies’ lean behind one point, with himself as the point.

Capabilities

It takes time, four or five breaths. The drums of every company in the Wall stop together, and the lean goes along the files and up to him the way water runs to the low corner of a field. Anyone watching can see it coming, and nobody who has seen it arrive has had to watch twice. It is on record four times.

Applications

  • The curtain wall at Harl’s Keep, on the third try
  • A Grandmaster’s Domain, once, at the Long Barrow

Associations

  • Coagula and Transference
  • The Body Path

Limitations

  • It is slow, and the lean must cross every seam in the Wall to reach him
  • Cost: about 8% of his reserve, near 943,000 EU, and every soul in the Wall breaks the same bone, the left collarbone by custom, since Wick Bottom
  • Tell: every drum stops
  • Counter: strike before the lean arrives, or hit the Wall from two sides at once, so the force has nowhere to go

Releases

SHOULDER! · First Release

SHOULDER! is basically body hardening: the power to make the house’s first order his own body. The call is “Shoulder!”, the first order of any Marrick fight.

Capabilities

The gray comes up on him hard and square, with corners, and his Vitality and Dominion read a step high on the hidden scale, though nothing in him passes the roof of his Stage. Every rank in earshot closes up without being told, because it is the word they have closed up on since they were nine.

Applications

  • Every contract that runs to noon

Associations

  • Petralon; the Body Path

Limitations

  • Cost: about 30,000 EU a second, near six and a half minutes from a full reserve
  • Tell: everybody on the field heard him shout it
  • Counter: a working on the soul

DRUMS! · Second Release

DRUMS! is basically domain activation: the power to open THE DRILL SQUARE. The call is “Drums!”

Capabilities

Every drum in reach takes up his beat, the enemy’s included, and the Domain opens around him. At Refraction it only lit the Domain’s seed; from Transcendence it opens the whole square. With SHOULDER! under it, his Vitality, Resilience and Dominion stand two steps over his line.

Applications

  • Opening THE DRILL SQUARE

Associations

  • Coagula; the Body Path

Limitations

  • Cost: about 60,000 EU a second; with SHOULDER! under it, 90,000, near two minutes from a full reserve
  • It fills the cap with the First
  • Counter: anything that breaks the Domain

OLD MARGET’S CART! · Third Release

OLD MARGET’S CART! is basically immovability: the power to take the whole load of every signed rank in reach into his own body and become the thing nothing goes past. The call is “Old Marget’s cart!”

Capabilities

The cart has stood broken at the ford since 631, because Old Marget wrote that the hire stands as long as the cart stands, and nobody has dared find out what happens if it moves. Released, Gerrit is that cart. The ground sinks a foot in a square around him, the penny in his circlet rings, and every blow on every signed man in reach comes to rest in him and goes down. His Vitality and Resilience read two steps high, and his feet do not move again until he lets it go. Nothing gets past him while the clause stands.

Applications

  • The bridge on the Tolly Wash
  • The barrow-field, when the ghouls came up in step
  • The last minute before the hour

Associations

  • Petralon; the Body Path

Limitations

  • He cannot walk out from under it either
  • A pull parts him, sudden and total
  • Cost: about 110,000 EU a second, near a minute and three quarters from a full reserve; it fills the cap alone
  • Tell: the ground sinking in a square, and the penny ringing
  • Counter: drag him, lift him, or take the ground away

Domain

THE DRILL SQUARE · Domain

THE DRILL SQUARE is basically rhythm imposition: the power to make everyone inside a place keep step. The Fields say a Captain’s Domain is a drill square, and his is the one they mean.

Capabilities

DRUMS! opens it. The ground goes ochre and black in squares like the stubble at Musterfield, his drum sets the beat, and every foot inside comes down on it, the enemy’s too. Coagula gathers scattered things into one body, and in here the enemy is gathered into the rank’s body as well, which means he marches. The rank always knows where he will be on the next beat, and so do the spears.

Applications

  • Making a skirmisher march into a file
  • Making a cavalry charge arrive on the beat

Associations

  • Coagula; the Body Path; DRUMS!

Limitations

  • It needs feet: a flier, a man who will not walk, or a working that runs by itself passes through
  • Counter: another Domain with a law that refuses it, or a deaf man, which has happened once and is not discussed
