Gyuro — Abilities in full

Gyuro — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202638 min read

Card: Gyuro

THE INNER WAY (내도 內道 · Naedo, “the way inside”)

NAEDO is basically inner world manipulation: the power to live so deep inside himself that nothing in the world can reach him, and to carry anyone who reaches him and asks down into Rooms where nothing can reach them either. It is his signature, the one idea his Traits, his moves, his Releases, his Domain and his Realm each say at a different size, counted in Rooms, and spoken slowly in the plain speech of the hill country, each syllable given the same weight.

Capabilities

In the hill tongue nae is the inside and do is a road walked so long it has become a rule, and a house there is measured in kan, the space between four posts. A poor man’s house has three. Thaloré’s idea is feeling given its true weight, and its operation is dissolution: a thing is let down through the whole of the soul until it rests at the depth its weight earns. The old saying of that water is that a soul under it takes the shape of what holds it. What held Gyuro for fifty years was a shell, a pen and a flood-door, and so his soul took the shape of a house. He lives at the bottom of it, where every feeling he has ever had has sunk and settled, and where no light from the yard comes down.

The house has ninety-nine Rooms, the most a subject may keep, and the Core of him is ordered as they are, each bay lying deeper than the last with the Crystal in the deepest. The shell is the outer wall and nothing more. Fixatio, whose idea is that what is bound stays bound, is on every door: a door he has shut is set past melting, and the way back through it stops existing until someone opens it from inside. Petralon and Orrenthal are in the wall, the one sending a load down and the other sharing it through the whole frame, and Pyraeon is the banked fire under the floors, the flare given up and the heat kept. The work goes by the Spirit road, Essence through the Core, laid on souls and on what things mean. It touches matter only through the shell, by the Body road he walks beside it.

So a thing that hunts the self, a Pressure, a read, a drain, a compulsion, a curse laid on a name, must come down through every door between the yard and wherever the master is sitting, and it stops at the first door that was not opened to it. A blow is simpler. It lands on the wall, the whole frame takes it, the ground under him takes what is left, and none of it goes in. A soul one Stage above him reaches the outer Rooms and can make them ring. A soul two Stages above could walk in, and few souls living stand there. The Measurewrights say the weight on a thing under water depends on how far down it sits and nothing else. They have the water right. They have no account of why it parts for a knock.

Because it does part. One who reaches him and asks is taken in, and the asking is the only key the house has. It may be a word, a knock on the shell, a hand held up, or a child holding on. It must come from the one to be carried, and it must be real. Fear counts, and despair, and so does a hidden purpose: a killer who truly wants in is truly asking. A compelled word does not count, since that is the yard speaking and the soul silent, and no one may ask for another. The sleeping, the drugged, the senseless, the gagged and the infant too young to hold on cannot ask, and he cannot carry them. A load is another matter. He will bear a river, a wall or a siege for anyone who asks it of him, and the people under it climb out on their own feet.

Applications

  • Going so deep that no Pressure, read, drain, curse or compulsion reaches him
  • Carrying the soul of anyone who reaches him and asks, hunted, cursed or dying, into a Room
  • Bearing a load no body should bear, a flood-door, a river, nine days of an Archmaster’s blows, and sending it down
  • Setting the whole of a borne load down in one blow, once
  • Laying his law over a house and its yard
  • Taking other people’s Domains in as Rooms of his house

Techniques

  • EMPTY YARD - nothing kept on the surface, always on
  • THE CARRIED HOUSE - the Tortoisekin shell, his by birth
  • HEARD - every real asking reaches him, wherever he is
  • BEARING - a blow on one point borne by the whole frame
  • GOING IN - the master goes down Room by Room, shutting the doors behind him
  • COME IN - one who reaches him and asks is carried into a Room
  • THE GREAT BEAM - a held load sent down through him into the ground, for days
  • ALL WHO KNOCKED - everyone who asked inside his yard, taken in at once
  • SETTING DOWN - everything he has borne put into one blow, once
  • MAY I COME IN - the last movement of the inner way, which he has never made
  • AT HOME - the First Release: at home in the yard and in every Room at once
  • I WILL BEAR IT - the Second Release: whatever is laid on a guest is borne by the whole house
  • THE ASKING GATE - the Domain: no one is carried who has not asked
  • THE NINETY-NINE ROOMS - the Realm: other fields held as Rooms of the house

Associations

  • Thaloré is his home lens, and both his depth and the heaviness of any room he sits in come from it. Fixatio binds the doors and the rule. Petralon and Orrenthal are the shell. Pyraeon is the fire under the floors, patience kept as a fire is kept
  • The Spirit road does the work, at the rung its schools call Ferryman, the soul that carries others across and holds many. The Body road, walked beside it, carries the shell and gives his field its claim on matter
  • The Tortoisekin blood gives the shell, the long life and the drawing-in. The art is his own, and no bloodline’s instrument
  • His Class has had five names, Jimkkun the porter, Munjigi the doorkeeper, Jipjigi the housekeeper, Daedeulbo the great beam and Jipjuin the master of the house, and each left him a move. His students shorten the last to Juin, the one the house belongs to
  • Naedomun at Gwibong, which outsiders call the Turtle School. The words over its door are “The shell is the house. The master is inside.”
  • It began at the Gate under the Greaves pit, where a starving Wellspring drained every keeper on the stair and found nothing on a bearer’s surface to take

Limitations

  • The held house costs him almost nothing. From full he could hold it eleven days empty and five with every Room taken. Whatever he does past holding is dear: one committed exchange takes between a hundredth and a thirtieth of everything he has, and at full output he is empty in about three minutes. Rest gives back a tenth a minute
  • It opens only to an asking, and only at his reach. An asker must touch his hand or his shell, or stand within thirty paces of him. An enemy who knows him fights beside those who cannot ask, or does the work somewhere he is not
  • It cannot imprison. Nothing is dragged in, an enemy least of all, and a guest leaves when the guest wishes. He cannot keep anyone, as he could not keep his sister Ohra
  • A house is opened from inside. A guest is past the outer wall and may open any door on the way down, and the road from the yard to the master then stands open behind them. The surest way to reach him is to be invited, and he honors a real asking from a hostile hand
  • The refusal is set in the house under Fixatio and cannot be undone, even if it is wrong. To go on denying the truth beneath it, that rescue done to a person is the same hand as sale, is his Shear Break condition
  • He tells himself he waits for the one he wronged out of respect, and he waits because he fears being refused a second time. While he believes the first, every great art of the way runs a little under his Stage
  • He is slow and has no range. Whatever will not come to him can wait him out, and whatever strikes from past thirty paces is outside his law
  • His one true seam is a pull. A foundation bears what presses down on it, and nothing ever asked it to hold on, so a lift, an uprooting or a drawing-out parts him. The Measurewrights call it tensile exposure
  • His worst contrary is Abython, which stands against his home lens and his shell together and matches pressure away until he has no load to bear. Nyxial and Cymorath answer him too, and Fractura reads the seams of a shared frame
  • Dry ground and thin air starve the home lens, a moving deck loosens the doors, an iron cage or a thick fog troubles the frame, and soaking cold puts out the floors. A dry iron cell in winter is the worst room in the world for him
  • The tell is the room. The air thickens and goes faintly blue, voices drop, and the cyan lattice along the joins of his plates lights or dims, one pane for every guest he carries
  • Run too long it fails in Thaloré’s manner. The weight he has laid on the room presses back when the support within gives way, and he is crushed under his own house. The Measurewrights’ word for it is implosion

Traits

EMPTY YARD (빈 마당 · Bin madang, “the empty yard”) · Primary Trait

EMPTY YARD is basically psychic immunity: the power to keep nothing on the surface for anything to read, grip or talk round. It is his Primary Trait, always on, the lesson of the Gate made into his nature.

Capabilities

A swept yard tells a visitor nothing about the family. Under Thaloré each feeling is let down to the bottom the moment it is felt, so nothing floats where a caller could see it, and Orrenthal joins the whole soul in common, so that no part of him holds an opinion the rest does not share. One who reads him finds still water and a shell. A worker of minds, an inscriber or a patient talker goes looking for the part of a man that already half agrees, and in him there is no such part; he is the hardest soul in any room to argue with, because nothing in him argues back.

It was this that saved two hundred and six people at the Greaves pit. The Wellspring under the stair had stopped knowing its keepers and begun to take from them, and it took from every soul that had anything on its surface. It passed over a bearer with his back to a flood-door for seventy-one hours and found nothing there to eat.

Applications

  • Going unread and unmoved by mind-work, inscription and persuasion
  • Walking where a hungry Wellspring drains everyone else
  • Sitting through any argument without any part of him changing sides

Associations

  • Thaloré and Orrenthal, on the Spirit road, read at the height of his mastery there
  • Lived and never taught: seventy-one hours on the flood-door
  • Opened downward, it becomes GOING IN

Limitations

  • It costs him nothing. It is part of the held house
  • Everything he feels sinks to its true weight and none of it comes back up. No one has seen him weep, and the grief that would have shown presses on the room instead; people who sit an evening with him go home tired and cannot say why. Comfort reaches the surface and stops there. No one has comforted him in four hundred years
  • A reader much his better at reading can still get through
  • It is no armor. A spear has no interest in what lies on the surface
  • It is not empty while AT HOME stands
  • The tell is a room growing heavy and quiet around a man who shows nothing

THE CARRIED HOUSE (진 집 · Jin jip, “the house borne on the back”) · Inherited Trait, the Tortoisekin blood

THE CARRIED HOUSE is basically organic exoskeleton: the power to carry a shell that is a second skin and a roof at once. It is blood and no working, and it belongs to his people and not to NAEDO.

Capabilities

Every child of the Carried House is hatched under one: a domed and plated shell whose scutes wear pale with the years, a long corded neck that draws back into the collar, a heart that beats slowly and a body that mends slowly, and five centuries in which to use them. Buyers priced the line as carrying stock for exactly these things, since a Tortoisekin will stand under a load no rope is trusted with and will outlive the man who bought him.

All of his people have the shell. Only Gyuro made a house of it. The reflex of drawing head and limbs in under a blow is the seed the whole inner way grew from, and the plate that cracked at the Gate and healed crooked is the one fault in the wall. A wound set wrong stays as it was set, and so every Room in the house carries that same crooked line.

Applications

  • Bearing ordinary loads no rope would be trusted with
  • Drawing head and limbs in under a blow

Associations

  • The Testudine line, the Carried House
  • The Labor Provision, which prices this body as stock
  • It has no Path, no lens and no Form

Limitations

  • It costs nothing and does not grow
  • It is slow and heavy, poor at climbing, dangerous to be turned on its back, and slow to heal
  • The crooked plate is the weak place, and everything built on the shell inherits it

HEARD (들었소 · Deureosso, “heard”) · Passive Trait, left by Principality

HEARD is basically danger detection turned to pleas: the power to hear every real asking made anywhere near him. It is the price Principality took from him, the last of his privacy, and he cannot shut it.

Capabilities

An asking is a knock, and a house hears its own door. When the world entered his law it left the door standing open in him: every real asking made within a day’s walk of where he sits comes to him clear, and nothing else comes at all. He knows who asks, from where, and for what. The harder half is that he knows who did not. The drowning man too proud to call is plain to him, and so are the penned who have stopped hoping, each one a silence with a shape. A compelled plea reaches him as a silence wearing somebody’s voice, and he is not fooled by it.

He throws nothing to do this. The askings come to him, as a great soul’s country carries his doctrine back to its source. His answer still goes only as far as his hand.

Applications

  • Knowing who in a valley wants to be carried
  • Knowing that a compelled asking is none
  • Knowing which of the dead never asked

Associations

  • Thaloré, on the Spirit road, and the law the world entered at Principality
  • His one word for it, said to whoever has finally reached him: “Heard.”

Limitations

  • It never switches off. A day’s walk of fear reaches him every night, and what it costs him is sleep
  • He hears far and reaches near. He can answer only those who come to him, or those he can walk to at a tortoise’s pace
  • It can be flooded. A city in terror asks with ten thousand voices at once; he hears each of them and can answer almost none, and a panic raised on purpose drowns him

Techniques

BEARING (지다 · Jida, “to bear on the back”)

BEARING is basically impact absorption: the power to take a blow on one point into the whole frame, so nothing gathers and nothing breaks. It is the move his first Class gave him, when the soul named him a porter.

Capabilities

Jida is to bear a thing on the back. It is also to lose, and it is what the sun does at evening, and his school teaches all three meanings together. Orrenthal’s idea is a heart held in common, a frame whose every part owns the goods and the debts of the whole, and its operation is conjunction: the blow is joined to all of him. Petralon lies under it and hands down what is left. A maul that ought to split one plate spends itself through every plate at once, and the remainder goes into the earth he stands on. He bends under it, and he comes back.

This is Body work, raw Aether packed in the shell and told where to send a force. A small part of every spend burns in the shell’s own furnace, and his plates run warm to the hand after a long day of it. It is how he stood for nine days on the Kneeling Hill under the blows of a man a Stage above him.

Applications

  • Standing under an Archmaster’s blows for as long as the man cares to throw them
  • Shielding whoever stands in the shell’s shadow, asked or unasked, since a load is no person and bearing is no carrying

Associations

  • Orrenthal and Petralon, through the Body road he walks
  • THE CARRIED HOUSE, and a century and a half in the Porters’ Hall

Limitations

  • It runs inside the cost of the held house. A blow taken costs him nothing more, and the toll is the warmth in the plates
  • It bears what presses and what strikes. A pull, a lift or a grapple that uproots goes straight past it
  • Petralon settles. His stance sinks a little under every great load and does not always rise again. He stands a hand shorter than the day he left the pens, and the floors of his own house are stone because the wooden ones gave
  • Struck along the seams between its parts, the frame fails at the seam while the bulk still stands, and the crooked plate is the first seam
  • The tell is dust sagging toward the ground about his feet, a smell of stone and hot metal, and a faint metal sheen on the skin
  • It is countered by one who reads seams, by a hold that lifts, and by an iron-caged room

GOING IN (들어가오 · Deureogao, “I go in”)

GOING IN is basically inner world internalization: the power to go down Room by Room, binding each door behind him, until nothing that hunts him can follow. It came with the Class name Doorkeeper, and it is the first thing his school teaches.

Capabilities

It is a rite, done slowly and in order, one door at a time. Thaloré takes him down, and at each threshold Fixatio sets the door: once shut it is fixed past melting, and the way back through it is gone until a hand opens it from within. The outer Rooms stop a Pressure or a read. Deeper, a blow meant for the soul finds only wall. Deeper still, a claim laid on his name arrives at an empty house and has no one to serve. At the ninety-ninth nothing reaches him that has not first found him, and nothing living has.

His body sets as he goes. The shell goes on bearing whatever is on it, and from outside he is a stone tortoise with the world on its back. His students count their own progress by the doors, and “she has opened the Seventh Room” is the highest praise the school gives.

Applications

  • Outlasting a duelist of souls, a worker of minds, a soul-drinker or a curse
  • Sitting out a siege inside the shell
  • A seclusion of years

Associations

  • Thaloré for the depth and Fixatio for the doors, on the Spirit road
  • EMPTY YARD opened downward

Limitations

  • Going deep is nearly free. It runs at the cost of the empty house, which he could pay for eleven days
  • The deeper he goes, the less of him is in the yard. His senses dim, his speech falls to single words, and from the deep Rooms he cannot act in the world at all unless AT HOME is standing
  • No light from the yard reaches the deep Rooms, so he never knows how long he was down until he sees the sky. He came out of eleven years of seclusion believing one winter had passed
  • A door bound in error stays bound, and Fixatio will not let him open it from the wrong side
  • The tell is the head drawing into the collar, the amber eye half closing, the breath slowing to a few in a minute, and the lattice dimming pane by pane like a house putting out its lamps
  • It is countered by waiting, which is a fool’s choice against a tortoise, or by harming what he loves in the yard while he is below

COME IN (들어오시오 · Deureoosio, “come in”)

COME IN is basically habitable anatomy of the soul: the power to take one who reaches him and asks into a Room of his house, where nothing can reach them. It came with the Class name Housekeeper.

Capabilities

He never says come with me. He says come in, and the guest makes the crossing, with the asking as the rite’s one offering. It is done by touch, a hand on the shell or his hand over theirs. Under Thaloré the guest’s soul takes the shape of what holds it and is let down into the house, and the body stays where it stood, slack and breathing slowly like a tortoise in winter. Since 702 the Room it comes to has a warm floor. Pyraeon’s banked heat runs under every Room the way a hill house is fired from beneath, and people who have sat there since remember the warmth before anything else.

Nothing follows a guest in. A curse, a Pressure, a possessing thing, a wound of the soul and the pull of a dying body all stop at the wall. A dying guest may wait there while a mender works on the flesh, or wait for a goodbye. The dead stay dead: if the body fails, the guest cannot go back out, and he carries them across the Veil himself, as a Ferryman does. On the third night of the Nine Days a stock boy crossed the yard, laid a hand on the shell and knocked, and was taken in with the blows still falling. It is the school’s favorite story.

Applications

  • Sheltering the hunted, the possessed or the cursed until the danger has passed
  • Holding a dying soul while a mender works on the body
  • Letting a grieving soul sit somewhere warm for a while

Associations

  • Thaloré, and Pyraeon since 702, on the Spirit road, at Ferryman

Limitations

  • The asker must reach him. Outside ALL WHO KNOCKED he takes no one in at a distance, and no one can ask for the senseless or the infant
  • A guest costs almost nothing to keep, a hundredth part of the empty house, so a full house of ninety-eight costs twice what an empty one does. A dying guest costs ten times a living one. The ninety-ninth Room is his own
  • The bodies stay in the yard and can be killed there. An enemy who knows him strikes the empty bodies
  • Thaloré binds what it holds. Every guest he has carried is tied to him for life, and he feels each one’s worst grief as his own. A guest walking to their Room sees what the house is built of, so he has no privacy from anyone he has carried, and every guest who leaves knows the way back in
  • After the guests have gone the floors stay warm and go on eating his reserve for hours, down where he does not notice it
  • The tell is two words, a body going slack under his hand, a smell of peat smoke, and one more pane lit in the lattice. Anyone watching can count his guests
  • It is countered by killing the bodies, by keeping askers from his hand, and by a guest who opens a door

THE GREAT BEAM (대들보 · Daedeulbo, “the great beam”)

THE GREAT BEAM is basically weight redirection: the power to hold a load for days by handing it down through himself into the ground, and pushing nothing back. It is the move, and the name, his fourth Class gave him.

Capabilities

The great beam of a house carries the roof to the posts, and the posts carry it to the earth, and the beam does this for three hundred years without once pushing upward. The same word means the one a family stands on. Petralon’s idea is the foundation, the thing other things rest on, and its operation is coagulation: the bearer is fixed in place, set to bear and never to rise. Thaloré takes the weight into the soul at its true measure and Petralon passes it down. He fights nothing. The load only goes through him on its way to the ground.

That is why it can last. At the Bearer’s Bend a river stood behind him eleven fathoms high for a day and a night, green as glass, while a whole district climbed past beneath it on its own feet. From one breath to the next he was never doing more than a Journeyman does. When he let the river go it went around him, and it bends there still, and the gorge wall keeps a smooth hollow six fathoms across where the weight went into the stone.

Applications

  • Holding a flood-door, a river, a failing gallery or a falling roof for days
  • Being the place where an army’s weight, a Domain’s weight or a mountain’s goes down

Associations

  • Petralon, with Thaloré carrying the weight in the soul, through the Body road
  • The Gate, the Bearer’s Bend and the Kneeling Hill
  • Gwibong, stone under load on a height, is the best ground he has

Limitations

  • It is the held house at its purest. A river costs him little more than an empty house does, and today he could hold one for the better part of nine days from full. At the Bend, a far smaller man, he lasted the day and the night and was empty at dawn
  • It needs ground beneath him to take the load. On a deck, on silt, on loose sand or above a drop the weight has nowhere to go
  • It answers pressing and never pulling. Lift him from his ground and the whole load comes loose at once
  • Each great hold sets him lower for good, as BEARING does
  • The tell is dust sagging down, a slow deep settling in the stone under him, and nothing else moving
  • It is countered by Cymorath, which slips the load, by Abython, by bad ground, and by lifting him

ALL WHO KNOCKED (두드린 이 모두 · Dudeurin i modu, “all who knocked”)

ALL WHO KNOCKED is basically mass inner world internalization: the power to take in at once everyone inside his yard who has asked. It is the move of his last Class, the Master of the House.

Capabilities

A house holds many. Here Orrenthal’s conjunction is turned from blows to people, all held in common under one roof, and Thaloré lets them down together. Every soul within thirty paces of him that has asked goes into a Room in the same breath, and the lattice lights from crown to tail, every pane at once. It is COME IN said once for a whole courtyard, and it is the only time he takes in anyone his hand has not touched.

Applications

  • Emptying a yard of the hunted in one breath
  • Taking a company’s souls out of the path of a working that kills souls

Associations

  • Thaloré and Orrenthal, on the Spirit road
  • COME IN, of which it is the full size

Limitations

  • Only within thirty paces, and only those who asked. The house is full at ninety-eight
  • Each guest costs what a guest of COME IN costs
  • The tell is the whole lattice lit together and a yard full of bodies going slack in one moment. It tells any watcher exactly where the souls have gone
  • It is countered as COME IN is, and more cheaply, since the bodies lie all in one place

SETTING DOWN (내려놓소 · Naeryeonoso, “I set it down”)

SETTING DOWN is basically absorption release: the power to put everything he has borne into one blow, at one place, once.

Capabilities

The beam lets go. Everything he has sent into the ground through the days of a bearing comes back up through him into a single stroke, laid on by contact or sent through the earth he stands on. Petralon’s foundation is worked the other way, by separation, the whole borne load parted from its bearer and sent to one place, and since 702 Pyraeon keeps the heat of it, so that a blow at the dusk of a long bearing lands exactly as heavy as the whole bearing was. He does not strike first, and he adds nothing of his own. The blow weighs what was put on him.

On the ninth day at the training yard north of Verath he struck once, on the stone alone, years before the ember woke in him. The hill sank six fathoms with the yard and the school upon it, and it sits level now, and the Accord’s own survey calls it the Kneeling Hill.

Applications

  • Ending a siege of blows with one blow
  • Putting a held river, a wall or a field down somewhere other than the valley beneath it

Associations

  • Petralon, and Pyraeon since 702, through the Body road
  • THE GREAT BEAM and BEARING, its only sources

Limitations

  • It gives back only what was laid on him. A fighter who never strikes him gives it nothing, and a short fight gives it little
  • It takes about a third of everything he has, on top of the bearing
  • It passes through the house on its way out, and the crooked plate cracks again each time
  • Thaloré gave the load its true weight, his griefs along with it. When the load goes, one grief he carried goes into the ground with it, and he can never feel that one at its weight again: a dead friend he can name and can no longer mourn
  • Afterward the ember smolders under him for hours, eating reserve and flesh, and for a day the shell takes nothing well. That day is the window
  • The tell is the lattice going from dim to blazing down the whole shell in an instant, thin smoke at every seam, and bright cracks along the plates. Whoever reads it has one breath
  • It is countered by giving it nothing, by breaking off before the load is full, by lifting him from his ground, and by Catharsis, which makes him set it down early and without aim

MAY I COME IN (들어가도 되겠소 · Deureogado doegesso, “may I come in?”)

MAY I COME IN is basically inner world manipulation turned around: the power to ask to be carried into another’s house. The doctrine of his own school names it the last movement of the inner way. He has never made it.

Capabilities

The master knocks. That is all the doctrine says, and nobody alive knows what follows. Every other movement of the way has him inside and the world at the door; in this one he stands in somebody else’s yard with his hand raised, and the art is in another’s keeping. He has not asked anyone for anything since the pens.

Applications

  • None that anyone has seen

Associations

  • Thaloré, on the Spirit road
  • Every other movement of the way, turned to face the other direction

Limitations

  • He has never done it
  • It needs another soul to answer, and that soul may refuse

Releases

AT HOME (집에 있소 · Jibe isso, “I am at home”) · First Release

AT HOME is basically inner world externalization: the power to be at home in the yard and in every Room at once, so that the master acts in the world without leaving the house. It is his First Release, found at Flourishing.

Capabilities

The Spirit road’s first door asks for the self said aloud and true, and he opens it with two quiet words. The lattice lights over the whole shell, and GOING IN stops costing him the yard: he sits in the ninety-ninth Room and stands on his feet in the same breath, the slow body in the world with all the depth of the house behind its hands.

A Spirit soul’s First lengthens its reach on other souls, and his own reach on a soul has always been the length of his arm. So it runs through his palms and his shell and nowhere else. A soul he lays a hand on is shown the door: its Pressure, its coat and every working it was holding are put back inside it for the space of a breath, and the struck man finds his art shut up in him like a guest who has been walked politely to the gate. It moves no stone and breaks no bone. The blow that follows is another matter, and it is usually someone else’s.

Applications

  • Fighting from the deep Rooms with his body in the yard
  • Showing a soul the door by touch

Associations

  • Thaloré, on the Spirit road, with the texture of the Body road he walks
  • EMPTY YARD and GOING IN, held together

Limitations

  • It empties him in about half an hour from full, and each palm laid on a soul is a committed exchange besides, a hundredth to a thirtieth of all he has. Run long it hollows him: colors go flat near him, then a word slips, then a name
  • Contact only. He must put his hand on the soul he means, and he is slow
  • The self is said aloud and true, so while it stands the yard is not empty and he cannot lie. His face can be read. No lie will leave his mouth, and a plain question gets the truth or a silence anyone can read
  • The door holds for one breath and no longer
  • The tell is the two words, then the lattice lit from crown to tail
  • It is countered by staying out of his hands, by striking the body, which is no tougher for it, and by asking him questions

I WILL BEAR IT (지겠소 · Jigesso, “I will carry it”) · Second Release

I WILL BEAR IT is basically damage transferal: the power to have whatever is laid on a guest borne by the whole house. It is his Second Release, found at Refraction, the deepest a Spirit soul has, and it opens and powers his Domain.

Capabilities

The second door on the Spirit road asks for a meaning borne for someone else. It is the future of jida, and it is a promise. Orrenthal’s conjunction joins the guest’s load to the house in common, Thaloré gives it its weight, and Fixatio fixes it where it lands. Within thirty paces of him, whatever is laid on anyone who has asked him is taken to have been laid on the house: a curse, a wound of the soul, a Pressure, a grief, the meaning of a debt.

A Spirit soul’s Second rules meanings and leaves the room looking as it did. But he walks the Body road as well, and that gives the claim a hold on matter, so a blade swung at a guest is taken as struck on the shell, and BEARING receives it. The guest does not flinch. The old tortoise three paces off rocks on his feet and comes back.

Applications

  • Taking a company’s wounds onto the shell
  • Lifting the weight of a curse from the one who asked, and keeping it
  • Opening the Domain

Associations

  • Orrenthal, Thaloré and Fixatio, on the Spirit road
  • The Bearer’s Bend, where the river was a load and the district walked

Limitations

  • With the Domain it empties him in about thirteen minutes from full, and in nine if AT HOME is standing too
  • Only for those who asked, and only within thirty paces
  • What he bears for another stays bound to him. The guest walks out free of it, and he keeps the scar, the curse’s term written against his own name, and the grief. Two centuries of other people’s worst hours sit in his plates
  • The tell is that nothing in the room changes. The blow at the guest lands on him
  • It is countered by striking the unasking, who stand outside it, by striking from past thirty paces, and by harm already done, a poison swallowed or a wound already given

Domain

THE ASKING GATE (청문 請門 · Cheongmun, “the gate of asking”)

THE ASKING GATE is basically sanctuary domain: the power to make the house and yard around him obey one law, no one is carried who has not asked. The first character of its name means to ask and also to invite, and the name holds both.

Capabilities

A house is opened from inside, and within his gate no soul is taken to be anyone’s load. Thaloré lays the meaning on the ground and Fixatio sets its terms as hard as written law; the operation is coagulation, each soul fixed in its own vessel past melting. It is a field of the Spirit, which rules what things are taken to mean, with a claim on matter from the Body road beneath it, and it is small: thirty paces, a house and its yard.

Inside it nothing carries off, drags, compels, reads, drains, possesses or takes a person who has not asked. A collar bound by a working goes slack on the neck. A compelling voice speaks into empty yards. A chain will not haul a body, and a hand that tries to drag a man finds that the man will not leave the place where he is standing. Every guest in it stands under I WILL BEAR IT. Called in a fight it is as dear as the Release that powers it. Laid as a house is held, with nothing pushed, it costs what the house costs, and his own hall at Gwibong has stood inside it for a century and a half.

At Principality the world entered that law in its own book, and it stands now whether he does or not. A law of the Spirit works on what people take to be so, and it puts no chain on anyone’s arms. On the day he crossed a kingdom fell quiet for one held breath, and afterward everyone in it found they agreed about something they had never once discussed. In the hill country round Gwibong nobody lifts a fallen man without asking him first. A child in its mother’s arms has asked by holding on. The senseless lie where they fell until someone wakes them, and that country’s physicians are the best wakers in the world. No drover there can bring himself to drive stock that has not asked. The Accord’s clerks file him as the Standing Exception and do not write down why.

Applications

  • Making a hall where no one can be taken: a sanctuary for the hunted, a yard no slaver can work in
  • Breaking working-bound collars, compulsions and claims on a soul inside it
  • Holding a company of askers under I WILL BEAR IT

Associations

  • Opened and powered by I WILL BEAR IT. It is NAEDO’s meaning laid on the ground
  • The hall at Gwibong and its yard stand inside it
  • The law registered in his name at Principality covers a kingdom. His own field has never grown past the yard

Limitations

  • The law binds everyone inside it, himself first, and Fixatio keeps it from being changed. He can end the field and cannot alter its terms. Nobody can carry the unasking inside it, so the sleeping, the senseless and the infant cannot be moved by anyone. Set a fire round a sleeping child within his gate and his own law holds her in it: he must end the field, or she must wake and ask
  • No one can carry him off inside it unless he asks, and he has never asked. If he falls senseless there, he lies where he fell
  • A guest within can open it, and a hostile asker is a guest
  • It forbids no killing. A blade kills a man who has not asked, since killing is no carrying
  • Everything past thirty paces is outside it, the bowman on the wall included
  • He cannot put the registered law down. The dead of that country who lay where they fell are his
  • It yields to a field two Stages above his
  • The tell is almost nothing: a chain going slack in the hands of the man hauling on it, and the air thickening toward blue

Realm

THE NINETY-NINE ROOMS (아흔아홉 칸 · Aheunahop kan, “ninety-nine bays”)

THE NINETY-NINE ROOMS is basically inner world internalization at its last size: the power to take other people’s Domains in as Rooms of his house, still running, their owners inside them, and to have their law read as a guest’s law. It is his Realm, reached through the Second Release grown whole.

Capabilities

Ninety-nine is the largest house a subject may keep. A Realm of the Spirit rules what the fields within it are taken to mean, and its operation is conjunction, two meanings joined in one vessel and each kept whole. His is the house that receives any field as a guest. A Domain that stands inside his thirty paces keeps its law entire, and that law is read in the sense of the house: it holds over its own master and over whoever asked to be inside it, and over no one else. A rival’s field becomes a Room, and the rival is the one who must stay in it.

He found the door on the Kneeling Hill. For nine days he held the Domain of Corvane Hask, the owners’ champion and a Stage his better, inside his own, and he understood its law wholly, exactly as its master meant it, because he had lived fifty years under that law in the pens. His own law stood around it and did not change by a word. Held as the house is held, the Realm costs what the house costs, and he can keep it up from morning to night.

Applications

  • Shutting an enemy’s Domain inside its own walls, so that it rules no one who did not walk in
  • Holding allied fields together as rooms of one house
  • Standing every field in a fight under one roof, if the fight comes into his yard

Associations

  • The Second Release grown whole, and the Domain’s law at its last size
  • Thaloré, on the Spirit road

Limitations

  • It holds only fields that stand inside his thirty paces. A field kept off at range is never his guest
  • For a day and a night after he lets it go his power sits badly on him, spilling and ill governed
  • A reading needs something to read. Silence, the unrecorded and a field with no name give it nothing to hold, and a field built on a meaning he has never truly understood is not held at all. He has never understood the carrying of an unasking person as anything but sale, so a field whose law is rescue by force is the one field he cannot hold whole
  • It counts against the cap on his own boosts, as the Domain beneath it does
  • Null-Light’s Mugan, the Unseeing, is its direct contrary, and so is any soul that will not be read
  • It yields to a field two Stages above his, and to hold a field is not to outrank it
  • If it breaks, it breaks with everything inside it, the held fields included
