Tenma Moto — Abilities in full

Tenma Moto — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 20268 min read

Card: Tenma Moto

NAGASHI-BINA (流し雛), the Floating Dolls

NAGASHI-BINA is basically Sympathetic Magic: the power to tie a person to a paper doll, so that whatever happens to the doll happens to them. It is his signature, the one idea his Traits and his dolls each express in their own way. Tenma does not fight with his hands at all; the dolls do the fighting.

Capabilities

In the old country, families fold small paper dolls and rub them against their bodies to draw their impurities into the paper, then set the dolls afloat on a river to carry everything away. The idea Tenma works by is older than the rite: things that have touched go on touching. A lock of a man’s hair, his name spoken over a folded shape, a drop of his blood on paper, and the thing becomes a door to him. Its operation is distillation: what one tuned vessel holds rises out of it and comes down in the other, with nothing poured between them.

What reaches the world runs on both his Traits. Through KEGARE, anything he puts into a doll spreads into the person tied to it: a wound, a sickness, a curse, sleep, fear. Through KAESHI, a doll can take what was meant for someone he loves and send it back along the thread to whoever did it. He folds them small and white from plain paper, and the moment one is tied, a thread runs between doll and person that only he can see. On the field he looks like a twelve-year-old playing with paper. People laugh until the first doll tears.

The glyph’s limit is the tie: a doll works only on someone it has been truly tied to, by a touch, a hair, blood or a true name, and a person he has never touched and cannot name is out of its reach. The flaw follows from the idea: what touches goes on touching both ways, so every doll is tied to him as well as to its target, and everything it carries passes through his hands first. Its contrary is purity, the Work that washes a thing back to what it ought to be; and severance, the cut that leaves nothing for a thread to run along.

Applications

  • Hurting a man by hurting the doll tied to him
  • Drawing a wound, poison or curse out of someone he loves and sending it elsewhere
  • Taking a killing blow on a doll instead of a brother
  • Putting a whole guard to sleep through one doll tied to their captain
  • Laying a slow curse with a straw doll and iron nails

Techniques

  • KEGARE - the principle as instinct, always on: whatever he carries spreads
  • KAESHI - whatever is done to him goes home to whoever did it
  • KATASHIRO - a doll tied to a person
  • NAGASHI - a wound or curse drawn out into a doll and sent away
  • MIGAWARI - a doll taking a killing blow in someone’s place
  • WARA NINGYŌ - the straw doll and the nails

Associations

  • Mortalis, the Black Gate, gives KEGARE its death-touch; Transference, the Give-and-Take, is the road his dolls’ threads run along
  • The Fate Path holds the ties; the Spirit half reads the soul on the other end
  • The Kōkan blood gives him Hataraki, and he carries it further than the house meant it to go
  • Sodoku ties the threads and Tenma spreads along them
  • The red hood, and paper always between his fingers

Limitations

  • He does not fight with his hands, and his body is a child’s; a man who reaches him before a doll is folded can kill him
  • A doll needs a true tie: a touch, a hair, blood or a true name
  • Every doll is tied to him too; if a doll is burned before he is done with it, whatever it held comes back to him
  • Whatever he spreads passes through him first, and he carries a little of every sickness he has ever started
  • At Fate I, 0.60 to 0.70 of a real pull arrives, and every pull ties a knot
  • The tell: paper in his fingers, a doll folding itself faster than hands should fold, and a thin violet thread in his black-red aura running off toward someone
  • His aura is the venous black-red of the Black Gate, the color of blood that has stopped hurrying
  • It is countered by fire, by purity, by a severing that leaves no thread, and by getting to him before he folds
  • Under strain it fails as contagion does, by spreading where he did not send it, to whoever is nearest the doll, the target, or himself

Traits

KEGARE (穢れ), the Defilement · Primary Trait

KEGARE is basically Ontopathogenesis: the power to infect what a thing is, so that whatever he carries spreads from one thing to the next like a sickness. It is his Primary Trait, always on, and NAGASHI-BINA runs on it.

Capabilities

In the old belief, kegare is the impurity that clings to anyone who touches death and passes to whoever touches them. Tenma’s soul works the same way. Everything is tied to everything else by fine threads of fate, and he can send whatever he holds along them: a wound, rot, frost, fire or poison, or health and strength; a working, a curse or a blessing; fear, sleep, courage or an oath’s hold. One touch starts it, and from there it travels through touch, bonds and shared ground. At its extreme it stops being something a person has and becomes what they are: a man becomes stone, a curse becomes who someone is, a Domain turns to rot from the inside.

Applications

  • Spreading one soldier’s courage down a whole line
  • Spreading one enemy’s wound to every man beside him
  • Rot through a wall from a single touched stone

Associations

  • Mortalis, the Black Gate; Transference, the Give-and-Take
  • His Harmonics Synergy and Projection, and his Vitality Filtration, which lets him carry what he spreads

Limitations

  • He is always the first one infected
  • It travels only where there is a connection; a soul cut off from everything cannot catch it
  • The Work of Purity washes it out
  • Which Hataraki Work it grows from is not known

KAESHI (返し), the Return · Secondary Trait

KAESHI is basically Damage Reflection: the power to send whatever is done to him back to whoever did it.

Capabilities

Tenma’s soul is woven into everything around him, and KAESHI turns that around: when someone hurts him, the hurt follows its own thread home. Cut him and the cut opens on the swordsman, in the same place. Poison him and the poisoner sickens. Curse him and the curse goes back to whoever laid it. It wakes while he is under attack, and it is strongest when the attack is meant to kill.

Applications

  • Making any attack on him cost the attacker the same wound
  • Turning MIGAWARI’s torn doll into a blow sent home

Associations

  • His Resilience Fortification and Continuity, which keep a curse from staying in him

Limitations

  • He feels all of it first, in full
  • A blow strong enough to kill him outright still kills him, and then kills the one who struck it

Techniques

KATASHIRO (形代), the Effigy · Move of NAGASHI-BINA

He folds a doll and ties it to a person, by a touch, a hair, a drop of blood or their name spoken over it. From then on, what happens to the doll happens to them. A doll keeps its tie until it is burned, drowned or torn through, or until he unties it.

NAGASHI (流し), the Floating Away · Move of NAGASHI-BINA

He pulls a wound or a curse out of someone into a doll and sets it loose, sending it to whoever he chooses. It is the healing side of his art, and the cruel side, in one fold.

MIGAWARI (身代わり), the Stand-In · Move of NAGASHI-BINA

A doll tied to someone he loves takes a killing blow for them. The doll tears, and through KAESHI the blow goes home to whoever struck it. He can keep only a few of these tied at once, and his brothers each carry one.

WARA NINGYŌ (藁人形), the Straw Doll · Finisher of NAGASHI-BINA

It comes from the old curse ritual of nailing a straw doll to a shrine tree in the darkest hour of the night. He nails one, and the person tied to it feels every nail. It is slow and certain, and it is the one thing he does that his mother has asked him never to do in the house.
