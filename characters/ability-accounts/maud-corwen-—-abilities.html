Maud Corwen — Abilities in full

Maud Corwen — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 20269 min read

Card: Maud Corwen

THE WARDEN’S KEY

THE WARDEN’S KEY is basically ley line manipulation: the power to open and shut the ground’s supply of Aether to everyone standing on it. It is the house’s Hours in the hand of the Warden, and her own soul has grown around it until the soul’s name for her is the ring itself.

Capabilities

A practitioner pulls Aether from the world around her, and where she stands decides how fast she fills. A worked pool has a bronze ring at its lip, the ferrule, and whoever holds the ring decides who gets how much and when. Everyone else in the world treats that as plumbing. Maud holds the key to every ring on the chain from the Wellheads to the southern sea, and on the Hotwell she does not need the key.

The idea is Solfatara’s: pressure let out forever and never saved. The Wellheads have been venting since before the empire, and her soul vents with them, steadily, so that she stands in a fight the way a hot spring stands in a field. Its operation is sublimation, what presses given off as fume and never stored. Phreatis lies under it, the Waters of Memory: the pools remember everyone who has bathed in them, and she reads what they remember the way a keeper reads the tundish.

Closing a ring takes nothing from anyone. A closed Core takes no one’s reserve; it stops the refill, and nothing happens to you until you need more, and you always find out late. Anyone who has been truly dangerous in a long fight has been dangerous because they were filling as fast as they spent. On Maud’s ground, they were filling because she let them.

The flaw is the Fume’s. A head of Aether does not go away when the gate shuts. Interrupt the release and the pressure finds its way out through whatever is weakest, and when she shuts a ring by hand the weakest thing is usually her. Phreatis has its own: deep water does not ask what is sinking into it, and the pools remember the false as readily as the true. The house reads the forks, and the forks ring true.

Applications

  • Ending a sect’s siege of the Cask Yards by turning a key
  • Selling an hour on the book to the highest bidder
  • Fighting a long fight on her own ground against anyone at all
  • Passing a warning from Brightwater to the sea faster than a horse

Techniques

  • SOAKED - raised in the fat
  • THE HUM - every ring on the chain, felt like a pulse
  • THE BOOK - names that fill as if they owned the place
  • NEVER DRINKS - the Tongue turned inside out
  • SLACK WATER! - one terrace gone lean in ten breaths
  • ON THE HOUSE! - the ring thrown wide for one breath
  • READ THE TERMS! - the keeper’s Command
  • CASK! - a circle of fat ground, carried
  • THE KEPT POOL - nothing fills here but by the book

Associations

  • Solfatara, the Fume, deep: pressure let out and never saved
  • Phreatis, the Waters of Memory, governing: the pools remember
  • The Guild lease, which is the authority; the key, which is the instrument
  • The Body Path carries it, with the keeper’s drift into the shallow end of Fate

Limitations

  • Leased ground with a ring on it, and nowhere else; ten miles from a ring she is what her Stage says
  • A closed ring stops the refill and takes nothing; a man who arrives full and finishes fast never notices her
  • The pressure behind every closing rises, and is owed, and something breeds in a ring kept shut too long
  • Deadwater kicks back through the key, and her left ear is already half gone
  • The whole of it stands on a piece of wax nobody can find

Traits

SOAKED · Inherited Trait

SOAKED is basically enhanced recovery on home ground: raised in the pools from birth, she fills far above her Stage on rich ground, and her Regeneration and Filtration run high. Every Corwen raised in the fat has it. On lean or ordinary ground the gift stops, and every Corwen in a foreign war has written home to say the air there is thin.

THE HUM · Inherited Trait

THE HUM is basically network awareness: she feels every ring on the chain the way she feels her own pulse, and passes turnings and warnings down the assay forks faster than a horse. Off the ground the silence is a physical thing, and she does not sleep.

THE BOOK · Primary Trait

THE BOOK is basically selective empowerment: the power to make a named person fill on her ground as if they owned it.

Capabilities

An hour sold to a name: a sitter on the book fills as if she owned the place, and anyone else on the terrace gets a trickle. Any keeper of ten years can keep the book at her own lip. The Warden can enter a name at any lip on the whole chain by speaking it into the forks, and the pool hums a note higher when she does.

Limitations

  • Leased ground with a ring on it
  • Cost: a name entered is a name owed, and she keeps every hour she has ever given away
  • Counter: step off her ground, or buy your own cask

NEVER DRINKS · Secondary Trait

NEVER DRINKS is basically contaminant detection: the power to tell by smell alone which pool water came from and whether it has been cut. The house’s old Tongue tasted the water. She gave up tasting at fourteen and has never drunk from the pools since, and what the Tongue would have told her she has learned to take off the steam instead.

Limitations

  • She has given up the Tongue, and everything it would tell her that the forks do not
  • Tell: she lifts a cup and sets it down full

Techniques

SLACK WATER! · Signature Technique

SLACK WATER! is basically energy cutoff: the power to turn one terrace lean in ten breaths. It is the house’s Deadwater, taken by hand.

Capabilities

“Time, ladies and gentlemen, please,” in the voice of someone closing a public house, and a quarter-turn of the key with the ring in reach. The ground under one terrace goes lean in ten breaths. Nobody on it refills. She gives the warning every time, because the house does, and the ten breaths are the only chance anyone gets.

Limitations

  • Ten breaths’ warning, given every time
  • Cost: about 4% of her reserve, and the kick back through the key as a scald up the arm and a hum to the bone
  • Counter: finish the fight in ten breaths

ON THE HOUSE! · Technique

ON THE HOUSE! is basically energy surge: the ring thrown wide for one breath. Everyone on the book fills, every guard’s coat flares blue, and the pool behind her jumps as the head drops.

Limitations

  • One breath; anything standing in the pool is scalded
  • Cost: about 2% of her reserve

READ THE TERMS! · Technique

READ THE TERMS! is basically contractual command: a keeper’s Command with the key raised. Anyone on the book feels the line of their terms on their window in the middle of whatever they are doing, and the line says what they owe her. It is a poor moment for it.

Limitations

  • Only those on the book
  • Cost: about 1% of her reserve
  • Counter: never buy an hour from a Corwen

CASK! · Technique

CASK! is basically portable terrain: a sealed cask of Wellhead water stove in underfoot, and for a minute a circle a few paces across is fat ground, and she fights in it.

Limitations

  • A minute, a circle, one cask
  • Counter: let it run out

Releases

KINGFISHER! · First Release

KINGFISHER! is basically body enhancement: the house’s First Release, blue steam off the skin. Her Harmonics and Vitality read a step high on the hidden scale. The steam shows exactly where she is.

  • Cost: about 18,000 EU a second; on her ground she refills faster than that

HOTWELL! · Second Release

HOTWELL! is basically domain activation: the power to open THE KEPT POOL. With KINGFISHER! under it, her Harmonics and Vitality stand two steps over her line.

  • Cost: about 30,000 EU a second; with KINGFISHER! under it, 48,000
  • It fills the cap with the First

DEADWATER! · Third Release

DEADWATER! is basically energy nullification: the power to become the ring. The call is “Deadwater.”

Capabilities

She stops being the keeper of the ring and becomes it. Every pool within her reach closes through her body at once, and the head of pressure that has nowhere to go goes into her. For as long as she holds it, nobody near her refills but by her, and her Harmonics and Resilience read two steps high on the hidden scale. The hum stops for a mile, and people look round for it.

Limitations

  • The pressure rises behind her the whole time, and when she lets go it comes out through whatever in her is weakest
  • Cost: about 60,000 EU a second, and the back-pressure, which no one counts in EU
  • Counter: outlast her

Domain

THE KEPT POOL · Domain

THE KEPT POOL is basically resource monopoly: the power to make a place answer to her book.

Capabilities

Its law: nothing fills here but by the book. The ground goes wet and steaming under everyone’s boots, the hum comes up, and no one draws a breath of Aether from it that she has not entered. Those she has entered fill as if they owned the place. When she lets it go, the pressure behind it lets go too, and the pool jumps.

Limitations

  • The head rises the whole time it is open; held too long, something begins to breed in it
  • Counter: a Domain that brings its own supply, or a man with a bottled Draft and a short plan
