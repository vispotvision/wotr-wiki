Amelia Greymane — Abilities in full

Amelia Greymane — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202617 min read

Card: Amelia Greymane

THE STONE IN THE WALL

THE STONE IN THE WALL is basically force redirection: the power to put herself between a blow and somebody else, and send the blow into the ground. It is the Greymane signature art, and her Traits, her techniques and her release are this one idea done at a different size. The short way to say it is that if you stand behind her, nothing gets to you.

Capabilities

The Greymanes raise their children to see themselves as stones in a wall, and a stone in a wall does one thing: it takes the weight that comes onto it and hands it down to the stone beneath. Her Wellspring is Petralon, the Foundation Stone, the one under the Ridgewall, and its idea is the foundation: to become what other things stand on, the load taken, carried and sent down, at the price of slowly settling. Its operation is coagulation, the soul fixed as the foundation, set to bear and never to rise, and Petralon sets her Shell in that idea along the road the weight takes. Masons know the shape of it from the arch, which turns a span into a queue of stones each pressing on the next, so that a cathedral roof arrives at its footings as nothing but weight pushing straight down; the Measurewrights call the pressing compression and the road a load path, and they have the road right.

A blow that lands on her shield is a weight laid on a foundation, and a foundation’s whole office is to send the weight down. Her Shell, the body-side layer of the soul, lines up the boss, the forearm, the shoulder, the spine, the hip, the knee and the heel so that the weight finds bone stacked on bone all the way down and is never held by a muscle or by a joint bent sideways. It leaves her through the heels and the ground takes it: the paving cracks in a ring, or the earth settles an inch, and dust and grit sag toward her boots.

The condition is the strange part, and it is the art’s own. It arms only when someone is behind the shield. It is a soul that learned to stand by standing in front of other people, and with nobody to guard it has nothing to stand for, and goes quiet.

Applications

  • Standing between a blow and somebody else
  • Sending the weight of a blow down through bone into the ground
  • Carrying a line’s fear the way she carries a line’s blows
  • Buying heartbeats for whoever is behind her against a higher Stage

Techniques

  • THE GREY WALL - the idea in one body
  • ELEVEN NAMES - the idea turned on fear and bad news, which are loads like any other
  • NO HOOKS - no working at all, only what a stone in a wall is like to look into
  • THE HOOK AND THE HEW - the wall reaching out
  • THE GREY FILE - the same idea in thirty bodies, rim to rim
  • SHIELD-HANDS - the shield-line’s first lesson done with nothing on the arm
  • STONEFAST - the transformed body, the stone set in its bed

Associations

  • Petralon, the Foundation Stone, the Wellspring under the Ridgewall and the house’s for generations, hands every load down through her and into the ground
  • The Foundation glyph that seats on Petralon imposes a geometry and reinforces a load path
  • The Body Path carries the shield and the axe; the Fate Path she walks carries the line, the man on her left and the man on her right
  • House Greymane and the Shieldwardens, who gave the pieces their names
  • She shouts none of them: the file is called as an order and the release as a report
  • She has no Domain yet, and when one comes it will be the ground itself obeying the same sentence

Limitations

  • It only works for somebody else: with nobody behind her it goes quiet, and she does not know what she is
  • That is why she would not leave the cedar wood when everyone else was sent out of it, and why the one word that moved her was “please”
  • It needs structural mass under her: bedrock, a paved road, a wall-walk, the ridge. It hates mud, loose sand and soft even ground, and on those her heels have nothing to hand the weight to
  • It promises exactly as much as the ground under it can bear, and the working holds until the footing goes
  • Against a higher Stage the art does not win. It stands, and what it buys is heartbeats for whoever is behind it
  • Somewhat under half of what she spends reaches the work, and the rest burns in the furnace of her Shell and comes off her as heat, which is why the rust and gold rises off her shoulders like heat off a forge and a shoulder set against hers is warm
  • An opponent sees all of it coming every time: the feet go apart, the weight settles square over both, the knees bend, the shield goes down in front of whoever is behind her, and the aura comes up red-brown with a gold edge over the fading ring of a struck shield-rim. What he cannot do is go through it to the person behind
  • Every fight sinks her a little deeper into her own stance
  • It is beaten by taking away whoever is behind her, by pulling her, lifting her or fighting her on ice, by letting go of the hooked shield, or by breaking the file’s end and rolling it up
  • Petralon fails by its own flaw: a foundation bears whatever presses down on it, and nothing ever asked it to hold on. Pulled, lifted or with the ground taken out from under her, she does not give gracefully. She parts, the column unstacks all at once, and the next load lands on joints and steel, on the hairline in the second lame of the left pauldron and the shoulder under it. The Measurewrights call it tensile exposure
  • The slower failure is the settling, the price the Wellspring names for never cracking and the Measurewrights call creep: her stance sinks a little with every fight and does not come all the way back up, and she is slower to turn each year than the year before

Traits

THE GREY WALL · Primary Trait

THE GREY WALL is basically guardian empowerment: the power to stand harder when there is someone behind her. She took up the shield when nobody wanted her to, and her soul did that before it did anything else: stand in front.

Capabilities

It is THE STONE IN THE WALL in one body. She steps in front of you before she has thought about it, and whatever was coming for you lands on her shield and goes into the ground. While there is someone behind her shield, her Anchoring and Fortitude read one step high on the hidden scale, and Petralon carries what hits her down through her heels.

The guarding does not need the shield on her arm, only her body in the way. She can plant the shield in front of the one she guards and stand at it, her ruined hand coming back to catch a sleeve and hold him behind her.

Applications

  • Taking on her shield what was meant for the person behind her
  • Guarding with the shield planted and her own body in the way
  • Holding the guarded one behind her by the sleeve

Associations

  • Petralon, which carries the blow down through her heels
  • The Body Path
  • The round file shield

Limitations

  • With nobody to guard, the Trait goes quiet, and she does not know what she is with nobody behind her
  • She can choose to leave it: she will walk out from in front of the one she guards to drag a fallen man off burning ground, with the shield left standing where she set it, and be burned for it, out there with nobody behind her
  • It costs nothing she counts
  • Its tell is that she steps in front before she has thought about it
  • It is beaten by taking away whoever is behind her

ELEVEN NAMES · Secondary Trait

ELEVEN NAMES is basically fear absorption: the power to keep fear out of the line beside her by keeping the count of her dead. It formed on the north face of the Thornwall, the second night, when thirty-one Shieldwardens held her section and twenty walked off it.

Capabilities

Torvald, Ebbe, Hamund, the two Ashcroft lads, and six more: she says the eleven every night before she sleeps, because she is frightened that if she misses one night she will forget one and he will be gone like he never stood there. While she keeps the count, her line does not break. Her Command holds the men beside her where they stand, and fear does not get into the file.

Fear is a load, and this is where hers goes. Whole companies come to rest on one name, and the panic that should have run along the file goes down through the one who holds the count into the ground. She is that name for twenty Shieldwardens. It moves nothing and pulls no thread. It is a bond kept, the way a vow is kept, and what it spends is her own share.

Applications

  • Holding the men beside her where they stand
  • Keeping fear and bad news from running along the file
  • Keeping the eleven from being forgotten

Associations

  • The share of her that walks the Fate Path
  • Petralon, away from its home
  • Her Command

Limitations

  • It takes no reserve, and it costs her at night
  • It is grief that will not move: she cried for a week when her mother died and then stopped, and did not cry again for six years
  • Its tell is her lips moving at night before she sleeps
  • It is a count and no guard, so nothing in a fight counters it
  • An enemy who knows the art does not try to frighten her line. He pulls her out of it sideways, and finds out what the twenty do without her

NO HOOKS · Passive Trait

NO HOOKS is basically absolute honesty: the power to give a read for hidden want nothing to find. It is no working at all, only what she is.

Capabilities

A read for want, the Debt Recognition Field opened on her the way it would be on a stranger in a yard, comes back with nothing to mark: no appetite, no hostility, nothing with a hook in it, only a solid, quiet, load-bearing weight like a stone in a river. She wants what she says she wants, and says it. Fields and reads that look for hidden want find nothing on her, because there is nothing hidden.

Applications

  • Coming up empty under fields and reads that look for want or hostility

Associations

  • No Path, no Wellspring and no Form; it is what a stone in a wall is like to look into

Limitations

  • It takes no reserve and has no Wellspring under it, so it has no Wellspring’s failure either
  • Its failure is its own: she cannot lie or bluff, and has stopped trying
  • A fighter who cannot feint is read by anyone with eyes, and every blow she throws is the blow she looked like she was going to throw
  • Its tell is that she says the thing
  • To get past it, simply ask her

Techniques

THE HOOK AND THE HEW · Signature Technique

THE HOOK AND THE HEW is basically guard breaking: the power to pull a man forward off his feet and out of his guard with the axe, and cut what the pull has opened. It is the wall reaching out, and it works because she is the one thing in the exchange that does not move.

Capabilities

The bearded war-axe does two things, and she does them as one. The beard goes over the rim of a shield, or behind a knee, or round a lance-haft, and she pulls with her whole weight from the heels. A pull is the one load Petralon does not answer, because nothing ever asked a foundation to hold on, so she does not pull with the arm at all. She sets her Anchoring through the heels and lets her whole weight fall back like a counterweight on a rope, with the haft as the rope. The man’s shield is levered off his body over its own rim, his weight goes forward past his toes with his feet still behind him, and for that half-step nothing under his collarbone is braced against anything.

The edge lands there, on collarbone or neck or arm, before he has his feet back. Both damages are real and separate: the pull wrenches the shield arm and takes his balance, and the hew lands on a body that could not stiffen to meet it. The collarbone is the strut that props the shoulder off the chest, and on a body pulled forward with nothing braced beneath it, it breaks clean under an edge that mail alone would have turned. The hooked spike at the top finishes what the edge did not.

Applications

  • Levering a shield off the man behind it
  • Hooking a knee or a lance-haft to bring a man forward
  • Breaking the collarbone, opening the neck or taking the arm on a body that cannot brace
  • Finishing with the hooked spike

Associations

  • Petralon, for the set of her heels only; the pull and the hew are honest steel
  • The Body Path
  • The bearded war-axe, which she taught herself when her father said no
  • Drilled, and learned by living it

Limitations

  • It takes about three percent of her reserve to set her Anchoring through the pull and the hew
  • The hook needs a rim, a limb or a haft to catch
  • Its tell is the axe-beard going over the rim
  • A man who lets go of his shield when he feels the beard takes the pull out of it
  • A shield with no rim to catch gives it nothing

THE GREY FILE · School Art, Greymane

THE GREY FILE is basically shield wall formation: the power to lock a line of shields into one thing that takes each blow down through every pair of boots at once. It is the school art of the Greymanes, taught, and she held the Thornwall’s north face with it.

Capabilities

Greymane shields lock rim to rim, and a line of Shieldwardens stands like the ridge it is named for. The file is the same arch as the art’s, laid on its side. The iron lip on each Greymane rim locks into the next, so a blow on one shield is shared sideways into its neighbors and goes down through every pair of boots in the line. She calls it as an order, numbers first.

Applications

  • Holding a wall face or a section against an assault
  • Sharing one shield’s blow across the whole line

Associations

  • Petralon
  • The Body Path, with the walked Fate Path’s share for the line
  • The Greymane yard, where it is taught, and the iron lip on each file shield

Limitations

  • Its limit is its ends: an arch stands only while something holds its two ends, which masons call the abutments, and a file has none
  • The man on the end has one neighbor where the rest have two, and the load that reaches him has only him to go through
  • The file pays for it, and she does not
  • Its tell is the shields locking rim to rim
  • It is beaten by breaking the file’s end and rolling it up

SHIELD-HANDS · House Drill, Greymane

SHIELD-HANDS is basically sacrificial blocking: the power to put her own hands between a blow and her heart, and let them be spent. It is the yard’s drill for a Shieldwarden caught without a shield.

Capabilities

The hands come down crossed in front of the breast, palms out, a wall of two hands, and take what was aimed at the heart. It is the shield-line’s first lesson done with nothing on the arm: put something of yours between the blow and the thing that must not be hit, and let it be spent. No working is shown in it.

Applications

  • Taking a strike meant for the heart when she has no shield
  • Crossed against a claw strike meant to take her heart out, held by the throat with burned hands, the claws came through the palms and reached her side, a rip as long as a thumb over the bottom rib on the left, and no deeper

Associations

  • House Greymane’s drill, taught in the yard
  • No Wellspring and no working

Limitations

  • It takes no reserve; its price is the hands
  • Flesh is not a shield and stops only what it can be spent on
  • Its tell is the hands crossing
  • A point fine enough to go through a palm keeps going

Releases

STONEFAST · Setting the Stone · First Release, Greymane

STONEFAST is basically immovability: the power to lock her whole body into one column that a blow cannot knock back. It is the house release, called aloud as one flat word, the way she gives a report.

Capabilities

She sinks her heels, and Fixatio and Petralon lock every joint from the shield-boss down to the heel into one column, so that a blow’s weight goes down through her into the ground and the ground does not give. As a Body release it transforms the flesh and what a blow does to it: set, she is one stacked column from boss to heel, and a blow has to move the ground to move her.

A stroke from a fighter many Stages over her, which the road under her could not take, came out through the air onto her shield and down the column, and STONEFAST held it, and her heels never left the ground. The ground left with her, plowed back eight paces in two long furrows, still locked from the shield-boss to the heel. The art did everything it claims. The road was the weak stone, and she was not.

Applications

  • Standing under a blow that would otherwise knock her back
  • Holding in front of someone against a far stronger fighter for as long as the ground lasts

Associations

  • Petralon, with the Fixatio the house’s release names
  • The Fixatio is the house’s lens, carried in its drill and never harmonized by her, so it fires at the away rate
  • The Body Path
  • House Greymane, whose release it is

Limitations

  • It takes about four percent of her reserve to set, and it holds as long as she does not move her feet
  • The feet are its limit: set, she cannot step
  • Its tells are the rust-and-gold aura coming up off her shoulders like heat off a forge, and dust and grit settling in a ring round her boots
  • It promises the heels and nothing past them: when the ground itself gives, she goes with it still locked, upright and whole, and stops where the ground stops
  • It is beaten by pulling her, lifting her or fighting her on ice
  • Ice, a lift or a pull takes the footing away without breaking anything, and then the column has nothing to stand on and comes apart at once
