Thibault Le Lambert — Abilities in full

Thibault Le Lambert — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202616 min read

Card: Thibault Le Lambert

LA RÉPONSE BLANCHE

LA RÉPONSE BLANCHE (the White Answer; Responsio Alba in the Court’s Latin) is basically preemptive counter: the power to answer an attack before it has been decided, with one cut that arrives exactly where it was meant. Every ability below is that one answer at a different size.

Capabilities

Every Lambert child at Sallowgate learns the Answer, the counter-cut that turns in the hand before your blade has left its line. Nobody in the house is taught to attack, because the Answer has to be asked first. Thibault is the Lamberts’ Answer carried to Emanation, where a Trait stops being something a soul does and becomes the law of the ground around it. Near him your will is counted the moment it forms, before your breath has set or your shoulder loaded, and the answer leaves his low guard on that count. By the time your body has begun to obey you, the cut is already in your throat.

The idea is a reply that comes before the question. Its operation is Aurevane’s sublimation: the answer is raised clear of everything that would cloud it, so it arrives meaning exactly what your blow meant, returned. Nothing in it starts with him. That is why the eyes of the great lines never saw him coming. The Moto Shingan, the Zettari Everlasting Eye and the Yukari Ketsumyōgan all read what a man is about to do, and what Thibault is about to do is decided by the man looking at him. A seer who looks into his next move sees his own blow coming back.

The price comes from the same idea, and it came due at Refraction, the first time his answer moved before the question. A man who answers everything before it is asked has lost the power to ask. He cannot petition, propose or pray, and he has not asked anyone anything in thirteen years. The Veil-Saints had ordained him at twenty, and a priest who cannot ask cannot pray.

The Measurewrights have their own account, which the house confirms no more than it confirms theirs for Léonard. In their reckoning the deciding comes before the body’s readiness by a good part of a heartbeat, the Lambert blood reads the deciding, and a Stage XII Shell at a yield of 1.00 loses almost nothing between the deciding and the cut. One of them timed it at Alabaster with a water clock and entered the margin as a fifth of a heartbeat. Thibault told him it was a seventh, and the clock was slow.

Applications

  • Killing a duelist before his first blow has been decided
  • Killing a seer through the eye that reads him
  • Breaking a charge by answering the first tenth of it
  • Ending a brawl before the first punch lands

Techniques

  • CROC BLANC! - the single cut he is named for
  • À L’ŒIL! - the cut that answers a reading
  • LA DÎME! - the answer to a field
  • AVANT LA QUESTION - the Trait that counts a will as it forms
  • LE RÉPONS - the Domain where every question is answered

Associations

  • Aurevane, the True Light, governing: what he sends arrives exactly as meant, and the light does not flatter
  • Judicium, the Scale, deep: the true will told apart from the feint wrapped around it
  • The Lambert inheritance, the Computus in the blood, and the Sallowgate Answer taught on a wooden sword
  • The Body Path carries all of it

Limitations

  • An answer needs a question. A man who means no harm to him or to anyone in his charge, or a thing with no will in it, gives the art nothing to answer
  • The mule, the falling wall, the dead man’s trap, the panicked horse and the working that runs by itself all reach him as they would reach any man of his Stage, and his body is a Lambert’s
  • Aurevane’s light shows him plainly, so he is the easiest man on any field to find, and every crossbow on the wall knows where to look
  • Pushed too far, the glare goes out through his eyes and burns the road it leaves by; the eyes go first
  • He cannot begin anything that asks: a question, a petition, a prayer

Traits

ONE BEFORE · Inherited Trait

ONE BEFORE is basically intent perception: the power to start his answer a hair before the blow it answers. It is the house’s own Trait, the Computus in the body, the same in him as in Léonard and in every woken Lambert.

Capabilities

His answer begins before the delivery it answers, by a margin so slight that a spectator calls it luck. Against a blow with a will behind it, his Reflex reads a step higher than the slate says. It costs nothing, since nothing is made or thrown, and it cannot be sensed, because it comes from no working.

Applications

  • Moving a hair before every blow
  • Reading the shoulder, the breath and the foot in the order a body cannot hide

Associations

  • The Lambert blood; no Wellspring
  • The Body Path

Limitations

  • It reads a will and nothing else, and is slower against anything with no will in it
  • It tells him what a body is about to do and makes him no faster
  • Tell: he moves a hair before you do
  • Counter: a blow with no intent behind it

AVANT LA QUESTION (Before the Question) · Primary Trait

AVANT LA QUESTION is basically intent precognition: the power to count a will the moment it forms, before the body has begun to obey it. ONE BEFORE reads the shoulder; this reads the deciding.

Capabilities

It woke at Refraction, the first time his answer moved before the question, and at Emanation, where a soul’s Traits become the law of the ground around it, it became the Lambert count made law. Within about thirty paces of him, any will turned on him or on anyone in his charge is counted as it forms, whether or not the man has begun to move, and whether or not he has told himself yet. It is what lets the White Answer move before it is asked.

It reads the true will and not the show. A feint with the whole body is still a man deciding to feint, and Judicium parts the feint from the will beneath it, so the answer goes to the intent and never to the performance. The house’s oldest counter, to feint with the whole body and then strike with no thought, works on Léonard and does not work on him, because the decision to feint is itself a will turned on him, and he answers that before the thoughtless blow can follow it. It reaches past him to anyone in his charge: a will turned on the men he stands over is turned on him, as far as the blood is concerned.

Applications

  • Counting every will in a room that is turned on him
  • Telling a real threat from a bluff before the bluff is finished
  • Knowing which of the men around a table has decided

Associations

  • The Lambert inheritance, made law by Emanation
  • Aurevane, which shows the true thing plainly, and Judicium, which tells it apart
  • The Body Path

Limitations

  • A will must form. He cannot read what nobody means
  • It reads the will turned on him and not the man behind it: a hired blade’s will is read, and the man who paid for it is not
  • Price: it took from him the power to ask, at Refraction. He cannot begin a question, a petition or a prayer
  • Tell: his eyes go pale and still and settle on the place on you where the decision will come out
  • Counter: the mule, the falling wall, the dead man’s trap, the working that runs by itself, and the man who truly comes to him with nothing in his hands

LA DERNIÈRE ONCTION (the Last Unction) · Secondary Trait

LA DERNIÈRE ONCTION is basically soul guiding: the power to send the men he kills across the Veil whole. It formed in the Rimward plague years, when he was a Veil-Saint, and it stayed when the Court cut his name from the roll.

Capabilities

The passage of the dead on the Sea’s ground has not gone right since the working that drowned the low country, and the Veil-Saints are paid to sit with the dying so the soul crosses without fragmenting. Thibault still says the commendation, in the old Latin, over every man he kills, with two fingers on the brow before the body is cold: Proficiscere, anima, go forth, soul. It is a command and not a petition, which is why he can still say it. The South knows what follows, and it is half of why the White Fang’s name is said the way it is: his dead do not walk.

The idea is Thaloré’s dissolution, a feeling let down through the whole soul to its true depth, where the soul takes the shape of what holds it. For the length of the crossing what holds the dead man is Thibault. The soul goes across in his shape, whole and closed, with no loose edge for a ghost to rise from, a dying curse to ride out on, or an art that feeds on the dead to take hold of.

Applications

  • Ending a fight with no haunting after it
  • Denying a necromancer or a soul-eater the men he has killed
  • Sitting with the dying of Rimward, who still send for him

Associations

  • Thaloré, the Heavy Heart, the ground of the Sounding Walk
  • The Spirit Path, a Stage behind the Body
  • The Veil-Saints of Rimward

Limitations

  • Only the men he kills himself, and only if he speaks the commendation before the body is cold
  • Cost: about a tenth of one percent of his reserve for each soul, and the soul’s full weight for the length of the crossing. A bad death presses on the Core, and if his footing gives under a heavy one, the weight goes down through him instead of across
  • Tell: Proficiscere, anima under his breath, and two fingers on the dead man’s brow
  • Counter: a soul taken before he can reach it, or ground and Domains that hold the dead

Techniques

CROC BLANC! (White Fang!) · Signature Technique

CROC BLANC! is basically preemptive strike: the power to kill a man with one cut before he has decided to strike. It is the cut he is named for.

Capabilities

When your will to strike him forms, the cut has already left the low guard. It rises along the line your blow would have taken and goes into the throat, or the wrist, or wherever on you the decision was going to come out. Aurevane puts every ounce of it on the span of skin it was meant for, so the cut is an S-Grade edge on a target the width of a finger. The men who have watched it describe one white flash, seen and gone, like a fang in a dark mouth, and that is where the name came from.

Applications

  • The end of a duel
  • The end of an assassin
  • The end of anyone who decides

Associations

  • Aurevane, the Sallowgate Answer, AMEN
  • The Body Path

Limitations

  • One man, one will, one cut. It does not answer a second man in the same heartbeat
  • Cost: about seven tenths of one percent of his reserve, near 4.4 million EU, spent in under a second; about a hundred and forty from full
  • Tell: the white flash, one clean high tone, and nothing before it
  • Counter: a blow with no will in it, such as the mule, the trap or a bolt loosed on a timed order; or a Fate fighter who removes what the cut depends on before it leaves the guard

À L’ŒIL! (To the Eye!) · Technique

À L’ŒIL! is basically perception counter: the power to answer a seer’s reading with a cut into the eye that read him. It is how the White Fang killed the great lines’ seers.

Capabilities

To the blood, a reading is a will turned on him, because a reading is a question. He answers it. A seer who looks into his next move sees only his own blow coming back, and while he is still looking, the point of AMEN goes into the eye that read him. The Shingan, the Everlasting Eye and the Ketsumyōgan all read what is coming, and what was coming was the answer to their reading.

Applications

  • Killing a seer at a blade’s reach
  • Blinding a reader who would not die of it
  • Teaching a whole army’s scouts not to look at him

Associations

  • Aurevane and Judicium
  • The Body Path

Limitations

  • Only an eye that reads him, and only within the reach of the blade or a step
  • Cost: about four tenths of one percent of his reserve, near 2.5 million EU
  • Tell: he meets the reader’s eye before the reader has finished looking
  • Counter: look away; send someone else to read him; read him from out of reach

LA DÎME! (The Tithe!) · Technique

LA DÎME! is basically mass counter: the power to answer the first tenth of a charge before the rest of it has finished deciding.

Capabilities

A tithe is the tenth the Church takes of everything, and he takes it of a charge. Against a rush or a volley he answers the wills turned on him in the order they formed, until a tenth of them have fallen, all inside one long breath of about six seconds. The other nine of every ten are left standing to see it. Most charges have stopped by then.

Applications

  • Breaking a charge with its own front rank
  • Breaking a volley by killing the archers who had decided first

Associations

  • Aurevane
  • The Body Path

Limitations

  • A tenth and no more; the rest are left alive
  • Cost: about six percent of his reserve, near 38 million EU, over one long breath; rarely used twice in a fight
  • Tell: he says “la dîme” quietly, as a priest says it at the collection
  • Counter: men who do not mean it, conscripts driven from behind, and crossbows loosed on a timed order, with no will in the loosing

Releases

BLANC DE NEIGE (Snow-White) · First Release

BLANC DE NEIGE is basically light-clad enhancement: the power to bring his aura up hard and white over the skin and read faster than he already does. The call is “Blanc.”

Capabilities

The white of his hair goes hard and lit from the roots outward, like frost on a pane at dawn, and the aura comes up close on the skin, hard-edged, with no soft part anywhere on it. His Reflex and Forecast read a step higher while it holds. Called silently, it rises less and lasts longer.

Applications

  • Holding the Stair against a crowd that has all decided
  • A duel with a Grandmaster who reads as fast as he does

Associations

  • Aurevane, which he touched on the snow above the shelf at Stage IV and took for good at Stage VIII
  • The Body Path; AMEN carries none of it

Limitations

  • Cost: about 1.2 million EU a second, near nine minutes from a full reserve
  • The light shows him to every crossbow on the wall
  • Counter: Tenebra, fog, any room caged in iron

LA CHAIRE (the Pulpit) · Second Release

LA CHAIRE is basically domain activation: the power to open his Domain. The call is “En chaire.”

Capabilities

A ring and a triangle of hard light stand behind his head like the halos the Court paints on its saints, AMEN’s edge goes white, and LE RÉPONS opens around him. At Refraction it only lit the Domain’s seed; from Transcendence it opens the whole of it. With the First Release under it, it carries him two steps over his sustainable line.

Applications

  • Opening LE RÉPONS

Associations

  • Aurevane; AMEN, whose edge whitens
  • The Body Path

Limitations

  • Cost: about 2.6 million EU a second; with the First under it, 3.8 million, near two minutes and three quarters from a full reserve
  • Tell: the halo behind his head
  • Counter: anything that breaks his Domain

LA MÂCHOIRE (the Jaw) · Third Release

LA MÂCHOIRE is basically total counter: the power to answer every will turned on him within a sword’s reach from two sides at once. The call is “La mâchoire.”

Capabilities

The white closes round him in the shape of a jaw, and AMEN’s edge is the lower teeth. Inside its reach a will turned on him is answered by the blade and by the light together. It fills the two-step cap by itself. It is on record three times.

Applications

  • A ring of Zettari who have all decided at once
  • A room where every man in it means it

Associations

  • Aurevane and Judicium
  • The Body Path; AMEN carries the lower teeth

Limitations

  • Cost: about 4 million EU a second, near two and a half minutes from a full reserve
  • It leaves no room in the cap for anything else
  • Tell: the light takes the shape of teeth
  • Counter: a man who comes in with no will at all, or a blow from outside the jaw’s reach

Domain

LE RÉPONS (the Responsory · Responsorium) · Domain

LE RÉPONS is basically answer domain: the power to make a place where every question raised is answered, and he is the answer. A responsory is the part of the liturgy where the cantor calls and the choir replies, and he named it for that.

Capabilities

LA CHAIRE opens it, and at Emanation it holds itself once open. The ground inside goes white, as the Walk goes wet under a true oath, and the light in the place comes from no lamp. Inside it, no will turned on him or on anyone in his charge goes uncounted at any distance, and no blow struck in anger reaches anyone before his answer reaches the striker. On open ground it runs about a hundred paces across.

Applications

  • Holding a gate, a hall or a stair
  • Ending a riot without drawing
  • Fighting more men than LA DÎME can answer

Associations

  • Aurevane
  • The Body Path; the Second Release opens it

Limitations

  • A will must form inside it; a working that runs by itself passes through
  • Counter: another Domain with a law that refuses his, a Fate claim on the ground, or a Zenith
