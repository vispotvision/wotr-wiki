Kimizu Moto — Abilities in full

Kimizu Moto — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 20268 min read

Card: Kimizu Moto

KAGAMI (鏡), the Mirror

KAGAMI is basically Attack Replication: the power to read anything completely and give it back. It is his signature, the one idea his Traits, his moves and his rapier each express in their own way.

Capabilities

In the old stories a mirror shows what a thing truly is, and a demon that looks into one sees its own face and cannot pretend any longer. The idea Kimizu works by is the oldest idea any scholar or appraiser ever lived on: nothing is what it says; everything is exactly what it is. Its operation is separation, a thing parted from what it claims to be until the true parts show at the edges, each in its own color. A soul that has become that idea cannot look at anything without reading it.

What reaches the world is the Moto’s Work of Knowledge, held further than the house has seen it held, and run through Judicium, the Scale. Through YOMI he reads a person, a sword or a technique down to the last stitch: what it is made of, what job it was made for, how strong it is, and the one loose stitch where it will come apart. Through ISSHUN he has all the time he needs to do it, between one heartbeat and the next. Then he answers with exactly what they showed him: their own technique, their own weakness, their whole art played back at them. Fighting Kimizu is fighting a reflection of yourself, held by a ten-year-old who has already seen how it ends.

The glyph’s limit is his own strength: a mirror gives back the shape of a thing, not its weight, so every copy runs at Kimizu’s own power and not the original’s. The flaw follows from the idea: a mirror cannot close its eyes, and he cannot stop reading, so every face, wall and wound tells itself to him all day. Its contrary is concealment, the art of leaving no mark; and the thing that is exactly what it says it is, which gives a mirror nothing to show.

Applications

  • Reading anyone’s window, which nobody is supposed to be able to do
  • Copying a technique after seeing it once
  • Finding the one weak seam in armor, an art, a person or an argument
  • Breaking a disguise or an illusion by reading what is under it
  • Playing an enemy’s whole art back at them, flaw included
  • Learning anything faster than anyone

Techniques

  • YOMI - the principle as sight, always on
  • ISSHUN - the world gone still while his mind runs
  • THE RAPIER - the blade he carries, and the point his reading arrives through
  • KANTEI - a full read of anything
  • UTSUSHI - a technique copied after one sight of it
  • HOKOROBI - the strike at the one loose stitch
  • MASUKAGAMI - the enemy’s whole art at once, played back

Associations

  • Judicium, the Scale, whose idea is truth told apart and whose readers read weakness the way the lettered read text
  • The Spirit Path does the reading; the Fate half holds his grip on the Work of Knowledge
  • His Gnosis, already at the top of what his Stage allows
  • His father sees where a thing will break; Kimizu sees why it was built that way, and how to build it again
  • Tenma, who stands in front of him while he reads

Limitations

  • His copies run at his own strength, and his Ardency and Vitality are a child’s; he copies the clever moves, never the heavy ones
  • Copying a bloodline art or a Fate art fails or hurts him badly
  • He knows what a thing is, not what a person will choose
  • He cannot stop reading; after a big read his eyes bleed and he goes flat and empty the way the Moto do after a verdict
  • The tell: his eyes go still on one point just past the person, the arc-blue shows in them with thin lines of color at the edges, and he smiles a moment before he answers
  • His aura is the arc-blue of the Scale, and it shows mostly in his eyes
  • It is countered by concealment that leaves no mark, by brute force he cannot copy and cannot stop, by a thing that is exactly what it says it is, and by keeping him reading until he burns out
  • Under strain it fails the way a reader drowns: too much at once, and he cannot tell which of the hundred things he has read is the one that matters

Traits

YOMI (読み), the Reading · Primary Trait

YOMI is basically Information Analysis: the power to read anything completely, find its weakness and copy what it does. It is his Primary Trait, always on, and KAGAMI starts from it.

Capabilities

“Read” is the Moto house’s own verb for a person, and in Go and shogi yomi is a master seeing the board many moves ahead. Kimizu does both. He looks at a person, a sword, a wall, a technique or a Wellspring, and it opens to him like a book. At its height it is the Work of Knowledge at full strength: he reads a thing’s true name aloud, and it can no longer pretend. Disguises fall, illusions break, and a lie stops working in front of him. Yomi is also the old name for the land of the dead, and the house has noticed.

Applications

  • Reading windows, weaknesses and true names
  • Catching a lie as it is told

Associations

  • Judicium, the Scale; the Work of Knowledge
  • His Gnosis Perception and Analysis

Limitations

  • He cannot stop
  • Bleeding eyes and the Moto’s flatness after a big read

ISSHUN (一瞬), the Instant · Secondary Trait

ISSHUN is basically Accelerated Thought: the power to think so fast that the world goes still around him.

Capabilities

When Kimizu is in danger, his mind leaves the world behind. A falling sword hangs in the air, an arrow sits frozen in flight, and a man’s face is caught halfway through deciding what to do. In that stillness YOMI has all the time it needs, and he weighs every choice and picks the best one between one heartbeat and the next. To everyone watching he simply answers instantly and perfectly.

Applications

  • Reading a whole fight before it moves
  • The perfect parry, the perfect riposte, chosen at leisure

Associations

  • His Gnosis Cognition and Forecast, and his Dexterity Reflex

Limitations

  • His body does not speed up with his mind; he has time to choose, not more time to act
  • When the instant ends he comes out shaking, with a pounding head and a bleeding nose
  • Held too long or called too often, it costs him a full day before he can read again

Techniques

THE RAPIER · Blade (all swords)

THE RAPIER is basically Swordsmanship: Kimizu can use any blade, and he fights with the rapier.

Capabilities

He learned every sword the yard at the Seat would hand him, read each master who taught him, and settled on the rapier because it rewards exactly what he has and forgives what he lacks. A rapier is a long, light blade made for the thrust, fought at a careful distance from the other blade, where everything turns on measure and timing and very little on strength. He keeps the point in line, waits at the edge of the other man’s reach, and answers the attack with a thrust into the opening he read before it opened. ISSHUN gives him all the time in the world to choose the moment; YOMI tells him where the point should go; HOKOROBI is usually where it goes. He can handle a katana or a heavier sword well enough to win a lesson with it, but against a grown man’s weight only the rapier keeps him alive.

Associations

  • HOKOROBI rides the point; UTSUSHI lets him borrow a master’s footwork after watching it once
  • His Dexterity Reflex, and ISSHUN’s stillness

Limitations

  • A ten-year-old’s reach and a ten-year-old’s arm; a man who closes the distance and bears down on his blade beats him
  • A thrust that finds the stitch still needs enough behind it to go in

KANTEI (鑑定), the Appraisal · Move of KAGAMI

A full read of anything: what it is made of, what it was made for, how strong it is, and the window nobody else can see. It takes a moment of stillness and leaves a dull ache behind the eyes.

UTSUSHI (写し), the Copy · Move of KAGAMI

In Japanese an utsushi is a faithful copy of a master’s sword or painting. He copies a technique after seeing it once and uses it himself, at his own strength.

HOKOROBI (綻び), the Loose Stitch · Move of KAGAMI

Hokorobi is a seam starting to come apart. He strikes the one weak stitch in anything: armor, an art, a person, an argument. On the field it is usually his rapier’s point.

MASUKAGAMI (真澄鏡), the Clear Mirror · Finisher of KAGAMI

A masukagami is a perfectly clear mirror. He reads the enemy’s whole art at once and plays all of it back at them, the way they would have used it, flaw included. It burns so hot that afterward he cannot read anything for a day.
