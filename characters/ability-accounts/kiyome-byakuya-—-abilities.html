Kiyome Byakuya — Abilities in full

Kiyome Byakuya — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202635 min read

Card: Kiyome Byakuya

THE LAMP AND THE KNOT

THE LAMP AND THE KNOT (灯りと結び · Akari to Musubi) is basically clear sight and binding: the power to see the seam in everything, and to make whatever she ties stay tied. It is her signature art, and her Traits, her techniques and her Releases are this one idea done at a different size. The art counts its releases in Turns, for the three quick turns of the cord. When the third turn is made the knot is tied, and she is the knot.

Capabilities

Every piece of it is one act: find the seam, and hold. The Lamp finds. The Knot holds. A reading laid on her has a surface it must cross, a room has a seam in the moment a hand goes to a hilt, a working has a seam where its law was told where to act, a body has an inside, and a moment has a state. She meets each one the same way, and then the same idea goes bigger: the idea, the transformed body, the world obeying it. It all rides one blow. The Knot stops the thing for a breath, the Lamp shows the seam in it, and the edge goes to the seam: bind, read, cut.

The Lamp is Luminalis, the hidden light, whose idea is that seeing what hides and hiding what shows are one act run in two directions, and whose operation is separation: what is seen is parted from what is there. Her Shell does both. Looking out, it reaches under the surface to the mechanism, the warmth of a man behind a screen, the old break under the skin, the weld under the polish. Looked at, it turns the look back carrying only what the surface holds up, a mirror from that side only. The Measurewrights set it beside two facts about light: that most of what a thing gives off is light the eye was never built for, and that clear glass, slanted far enough from a ray, stops passing it and sends all of it back, which they call total internal reflection.

The Knot is Fixatio, the idea of commitment, the bond with no way back, and its operation is coagulation, what is bound set past melting. Scholars set it beside a resin that has cured: a wax can be melted and poured again forever, but a cured resin has grown bridges through its whole body and will char before it flows, because the softer state is no longer anywhere it can go. What she binds is held the same way, by the other arrangement having stopped being reachable, and force has nothing to do with it. Like any practitioner she pulls Aether (mana, in the common word) out of the ground she stands on, refines it in the Soul Crystal into Essence (spiritual energy), and drives it out through whichever layer the working lands through.

Applications

  • Returning every reading of her level
  • Holding a peace in a room past the point it should break
  • Parting a working at the join where it was put together
  • Stopping one thing for a heartbeat, for an ally’s arrow or her own edge
  • Lighting a body through and reading everything inside it at once
  • A draw that is over before its own sound arrives
  • Showing every seam, hidden wound, veil and lie on a field to everyone standing in it
  • Standing between the Kōkan heir and every blow meant for him

Techniques

  • THE WHITE KNOT - the Knot in its first and smallest form, tied across the seam of a room
  • BROKEN AND MENDED - the Knot turned on herself
  • THE RED BEFORE THE WHITE - the house’s oath, which the Third Turn makes law
  • GLASS - the Lamp run backward, turning a reading back at her surface
  • HAKKŌ - the Byakuya draw, laid along the one straightest line
  • KAGAMI-WARI - the Lamp turned on workings, cut at the join
  • MUSUBI - the Knot tied on a moment where it stands
  • SHINGAN - the Moto eye, with the Lamp in it
  • SHIROBANE - the lamp thrown ahead of her, lighting a body through
  • YUKIAKARI - the First Turn: her body made the lamp, glass gone to a prism
  • HAKUREN - the Second Turn: a hall of it, a hundred and fifty meters with no shadow in it
  • KŌKAN NO MAE - the Third Turn: the knot tied round her own body, between the heir and whatever is coming for him

Associations

  • Luminalis, the Lamp, her governing Wellspring; its glyph is Depth and its Titan is Nysera, who keeps the line between what is seen and what is there
  • Fixatio, the Knot; its glyph is Topology and its Titan is Atla’zon, who decides whether Essence stays inside the shape it was poured into
  • Fate, her mastered Path, carries the oath and the knot; Spirit, Walked, carries the reading of a room and the lamp; Body, Studied, is the draw, one movement trained ten thousand times behind a closed door
  • The Byakuya guard’s school, taught behind a closed door and never shown
  • Her katana, her bonded weapon, which carries HAKKŌ’s channel in its steel and the memory of an ancestor’s knot in its guard

Limitations

  • The Lamp’s glyph shows her how a thing is put together and never what the thing will choose, and it tells her nothing about what to do with what she sees
  • The Knot’s glyph freezes one small grid of outcomes for one short window: one thing, a thing she can see and name, in the state it was already in, for as long as she keeps paying; it holds an arm and never a will
  • She cannot hide in her own light and she cannot put down what she has tied: the oath is the crack in her Crystal and the thing holding the crack shut
  • Luminalis favors dry air, a clear line of sight and ground that carries a charge, and hates dense fog and iron-caged rooms
  • Fixatio favors worked stone and deep foundation, and hates open water and anything that moves under the feet
  • The Hall of Verdicts on a dry morning is the best ground she will ever stand on, and a steaming wood in a thaw is the worst
  • About twenty-three parts in twenty-five of what she spends reaches the work, which is her yield; what shows of the lost share is the cold rainbow at the edge of her aura and a faint clean warmth with the smell of fresh linen and of air after a storm
  • Mastery is quiet, and hers is nearly silent: the only sound is the glass at her temples
  • The charge is always there for anyone who knows the house: three quick turns before the white cord and before the white light that copies it, her eyes leaving the owner before KAGAMI-WARI, the split shadows and ringing beads before SHIROBANE, the violet line before HAKKŌ
  • At half her reserve the beads at her temples stop clicking and hang still; at a quarter the cold rainbow at her edges goes out; at a tenth the near-white flickers to gray and she has to put a hand on something to stand
  • At full output she would empty in about twenty seconds, and nobody has ever seen her spend at full
  • Against a higher Stage the art thins from the Knot down: MUSUBI holds a stronger Sovereignty for a heartbeat and no longer, and the Hall is dimmed from its edge inward
  • GLASS still reads level there, because it is a surface and no contest; KAGAMI-WARI still parts any working that has a join, whatever its weight, and still does nothing at all to a Trait; the draw does not care about Stage, it cares about the weather
  • Luminalis fails by birefringence: the Shell splits its own output into two paths that travel at different speeds, as some clear crystals show two images of one candle, and a complex working arrives out of step with itself, the cut in one place and its light in another
  • The shadows that split in two near her are that fault at its smallest and most harmless, and they are the warning to stop stacking workings
  • Fixatio fails by irreversibility: a binding placed in error stays placed, and she carries the largest example in the north in her own chest, an oath that outlived the reason for it and could not be put down
  • It is beaten by wet air, rain or a grounded iron for the draw, by a working with no seam, by a will stronger than her Sovereignty, by two blows inside one breath, or by setting the heir against his own good and making her choose

Traits

THE WHITE KNOT · Primary Trait

THE WHITE KNOT is basically peace inducement: the power to tie her own sword shut and have the peace she walked in with hold better than it should. It is the second half of her art in its first and smallest form: a thing tied so that the way back is a cut somebody has to make, and answer for.

Capabilities

A Byakuya ties it when she goes into a house where she means to keep the peace, whatever comes: three quick turns of the white cord round the guard of the sword and the mouth of the scabbard, so that the blade cannot leave its sheath without the cord being cut. Fixatio holds the knot to the oath sworn over it. Fixatio’s cure is the law under it, and the Topology glyph is its limit, since the glyph freezes only one small grid of outcomes: the cord joins her blade to its sheath and her word to the room.

She untied it at the Foot, slowly, as if the movement had been waiting in her body for sixteen years, and gave the cord to her sister’s son. It is Tsugigane’s sageo, its scabbard cord, now. It was always his. Her blade has been free to leave its scabbard since that day, and nobody but Tetsugen and Hayato understood what that meant. The knot survives in her as a working, MUSUBI.

Applications

  • Bringing tempers down in a room
  • Taking hands off hilts
  • Making a parley last

Associations

  • Fixatio, the Knot
  • The Fate Path: a bond tied on a thing and on a room
  • The oath sworn over the knot
  • Her katana, whose guard carried an ancestor’s knot for three hundred and twelve years
  • MUSUBI, which is the knot as a working

Limitations

  • It holds her own peace and her own sword; it cannot make another man keep his, and it has no say over a hand that is not hers
  • It takes about two percent of her reserve to tie and nothing to keep
  • The three quick turns of white cord round the guard and the scabbard mouth are its sign
  • It is beaten by cutting the cord, and the cutting costs the cutter, because the Dawi who made her sword felt her ancestor’s knot in the steel for three hundred and twelve years and walked four hundred miles when it came undone
  • The cord itself is gone from her sword now

BROKEN AND MENDED · Secondary Trait

BROKEN AND MENDED is basically self-restoration: the power to break where the whole room can see it and be whole again inside a breath. It formed at the Verdict of Six, when her voice shook in the Hall for the first time, and it is the Knot turned on herself.

Capabilities

When something strikes her the fine silver rings in her eyes turn once, very slowly, and her composure breaks, visibly, and mends in a breath. The breaking is the Trait as much as the mending, and the mending is real. Her Crystal has been cured, broken and cured again so many times that mending is the shape it sets in. Stability holds her through it.

At Dissonance it holds her Crystal too. The fracture of the four lines opens a hair every time she breaks and closes every time she mends, which is why she can stand at Stage XI without the Crystal running away from her.

Applications

  • Taking a blow to her composure and coming back whole
  • Holding a Dissonance Crystal that would otherwise run away from her

Associations

  • No lens: it is the Crystal’s own habit
  • The Spirit Path, Walked: the Core mending its own break
  • Her Stability
  • The Lamp’s half of the bargain, since nothing in it is hidden

Limitations

  • Nothing in it is hidden: the room sees the break, and that is the price of a mend that is real
  • Past that it costs nothing she shows
  • It mends one break at a time; a second blow inside the breath finds her still open, and a second break she does not always mend
  • The silver rings in her eyes turning once give it away
  • It is beaten by striking twice before she mends

THE RED BEFORE THE WHITE · Inherited Trait

THE RED BEFORE THE WHITE is basically oath empowerment: the power to stand stronger and steadier beside the one she is sworn to guard. It is the Byakuya oath to the Kōkan heir. It is the house’s and not her art: every Byakuya child has it said over them, and her sister carried the same words to the red chair.

Capabilities

She stands between the red king’s child and anything that comes for him, and he owes her nothing back. The oath is spoken over every child of the house at nine, with a grandmother’s hand on her head, and it is in the blood as much as in the words: her Attraction Layer is bound to the Kōkan heir and his seat. Near him, keeping it, her Oath and Ward stand a step higher on the hidden scale, fear reaches her late, and her hands do not leave the work.

It is a vow, which is small Fate, kept on her own share with no thread pulled down for it, and that is why it costs no reserve. Fixatio is only what has made it impossible for her to put down. For sixteen years it bound her to nothing, a red line she believed had ended in the cellars, and she kept it anyway. The pressure of keeping it with nothing at the end is what drove her up through the Stages alone in the white house: an oath with no object is a load with no ground under it, and her Crystal grew to carry it.

Applications

  • Guarding the Kōkan heir with her Oath and Ward a step higher
  • Keeping her hands on the work when fear should have reached her
  • Giving KŌKAN NO MAE the sentence it makes law

Associations

  • The house’s oath, with no Wellspring behind it
  • The Fate Path: a vow, kept on her own share and pulling nothing down
  • The Kōkan heir and his seat
  • Fixatio, which has made it impossible to put down

Limitations

  • It binds her to the boy and does not bind the boy to her; she can serve him and cannot command him
  • It obeys as well as serves: when the heir orders her, her lips move over the words and she goes, because she cannot command the red line and the red line can command her
  • When the king himself is holding the knife, as Gimbzo was, the oath has nothing to say, and her blade will not leave its scabbard against him
  • It costs nothing to keep and everything to break
  • Her lips moving over the words give it away
  • Its Rigidity is the oath outliving the reason for it, as it did for sixteen years
  • Setting the red line behind the white is her Shear Break condition
  • Her Oath stands at 725, past the fracture line of Dissonance; an oath broken now would break the Crystal with it
  • It is beaten by setting the heir against his own good and making her choose

GLASS (玻璃 · Hari) · Passive Trait

GLASS is basically reading reflection: the power to send every reading of her back level, carrying only what she holds up to it. It is the Lamp run backward, and in the old tongue of the house the word is hari, glass.

Capabilities

There is something so still and so clean in her that wards go over it like water over glass and find nowhere to catch, and a reader’s threads lie flat off her when she speaks the truth, which is always. Luminalis, the seeing and the hiding that are one act, runs here backward: it sets her Shell against any look laid on it the way clear glass slanted far enough from a ray stops passing it and sends the whole of it back, so a reading returns from her surface carrying only what the surface holds up. A ward is a look with a hook on it and fares no better.

At Dissonance it also does the thing the Byakuya have used it for since the house began: it returns any reading level, and level is whatever she holds up to it. Because the trick is all surface, it is no contest of reserve. What decides it is her Ward against the reader’s eye, and at 660 there are very few eyes in the north that outrank it.

Under Gimbzo the house’s custom became something more: he put his own blood down the cellars for being strong enough to tell him no, and a Byakuya at Dissonance in the white chair would have gone down after them. So she let GLASS do what it does. Every Measurewright, Hall assessor and fate-reader has entered her at Level 160, Stage VI, an Expert, with a Shell newly emerged, since she was fourteen.

Applications

  • Letting wards and readings slide off her
  • Showing every assessor a lesser woman than the one standing there
  • Keeping the Byakuya guard’s true strength unknown to the seven lines

Techniques

  • THE VEIL - GLASS as the house has always used it: every reading of her comes back as whatever she chooses to show, which since she was fourteen has been a Stage VI lady with sense and no reach

Associations

  • Luminalis, the Lamp
  • The Spirit Path, Walked: the surface of the soul, turned against a look
  • Her Ward, which decides it
  • The Byakuya guard’s custom of never letting the seven lines see what stands at the king’s shoulder, because a guard whose strength is known is a guard whose strength is planned for

Limitations

  • It costs nothing, and the Veil costs nothing
  • It hides nothing she chooses to show and protects nothing she is not
  • Plain eyes see her perfectly, and she cannot lie through it, because she does not lie; she has never once said she was Stage VI, she has only let people read it
  • The Veil’s limit is the body: plain eyes see a woman whose hands do not shake when they should, and whose draw, if anyone ever saw it, would end the pretense at once
  • When she calls YUKIAKARI the Veil is gone and cannot be put back in front of anyone who saw it
  • Every reading of her coming back level is itself the sign
  • It is beaten by a plain question asked in plain light

Techniques

HAKKŌ (白光 · White Light) · Signature Technique

HAKKŌ is basically a lightning draw: the power to lay a charged line through the air and bring the blade out along it faster than its own sound. It is the Byakuya draw, and the school’s working more than her own: the guard teaches the draw on Judicium, and the sword carries the channel in its steel.

Capabilities

The guard teaches the draw on Judicium, the Scale, whose idea is truth told apart and whose operation is separation, and in the draw the thing told apart is one line of air: from the scabbard mouth to the place the cut will end, the air along that line is parted into charged pieces and set apart from the air round it, a road laid before anything travels down it. A storm lays its road the same way, more slowly. A faint leader feels its way down in steps and leaves a thin road behind it, and then the return stroke runs back up that road many times faster than the leader came down. The Measurewrights say the air along it is heated several times hotter than the face of the sun before it has time to move aside, and that air that hot and that sudden can only burst outward, and the bursting is thunder.

Her left thumb cuts the scabbard mouth, and Judicium lays the road. For that breath a hair-fine violet line hangs in the air. That line is the leader. Her blade is the return stroke. The cut is over before the sound of the draw arrives, and the thunder is the air closing behind it. She does not call it; the thunder does.

It rides the blow completely. The cut is an honest cut by a Very Rare blade, and the stroke goes in with it, so the wound is a sword wound that has also been struck by lightning from the inside, parted and burned shut along the same line. It lands along a line a few inches wide, about a kiloton and a quarter by the Measurewrights’ reckoning. Inside her Hall the violet line is laid along one of the seams that already show.

Applications

  • Ending a fight in one movement, before the blow lands on her
  • A cut that parts and burns shut along the same line
  • Following a seam the Hall has already lit

Associations

  • Judicium, the guard’s school’s lens for the draw, carried in the sword’s steel and never harmonized by her
  • The Body Path, Studied: one cut from the scabbard, with the full force of it at contact
  • The Byakuya guard’s school, which taught it
  • Her katana, which carries the channel
  • YUKIAKARI, under which the violet line shortens to half a breath

Limitations

  • The violet line hangs for a full breath before the cut, and anyone with the eye can read it like a signpost and see exactly where she is going to cut
  • It takes about four percent of her reserve
  • Afterward the thunder is loud enough to stun anyone near it who did not see it coming, her allies included
  • The fault is the road’s own: what she parts out of the air is a storm’s road, and it keeps a storm’s habit of taking the easiest way to ground, so water, rain, wet earth or a grounded piece of iron near the line is an easier road than the one she laid
  • The channel wanders to it, and the blade, which only follows, either goes with it or arrives without it
  • A cut that loses its channel arrives at the speed of a very fast woman, which is still very fast
  • Along the line the blade beats Instrument plate; in the wet, or against Hallowed plate, it is only a very good sword
  • She has said so herself: her light wants dry air, and in steam as wet as a bathhouse the channel would wander like a drunk on a road and arrive wherever the water pulled it, perhaps in her own feet; in such air she fights with the spear
  • It is beaten by wet ground, rain or grounded metal; a grounded piece of iron thrust across the line takes the stroke

KAGAMI-WARI (鏡割り · the Mirror Split) · Bloodline Art, Byakuya

KAGAMI-WARI is basically spell cutting: the power to find the join where a working was put together and cut it there, so it falls in two clean halves. It is the Lamp turned on workings.

Capabilities

The Byakuya Meigan sees hidden seams and the clean structural breach, and in her Luminalis turns that on workings as well as on steel and bone. A working is a law and a place the law was told to act, and the join between the two is the only part that is neither. She looks at a technique instead of at its owner, finds the seam where its boundary condition meets its law, and cuts there, and the working comes apart along its own join, like a mirror that falls in two clean halves.

The school’s Seam-Gospel Cut, Nuime-kiri, lays a blade along a working’s join by feel. Hers is that cut with the Lamp behind it, so the join is seen. She calls it, clear and quick, “Kagami-wari,” and any edge will carry it once the eye has found the join, the sword or the side-blade of a spear.

Applications

  • Splitting a working of any weight, so long as it has a join
  • Splitting GŌKA at the seam where the fire is placed

Associations

  • Luminalis, through the Meigan, the Byakuya eye
  • The Spirit Path, Walked: a working read, and parted at its join
  • The school’s Seam-Gospel Cut
  • Any edge she is holding

Limitations

  • It parts and does not unmake: the law lets go, and whatever the working had gathered is still there, loose, and falls where it is, and she is standing next to it; a fire split a third gathered is a third of a fire on the ground
  • It takes about two percent of her reserve, and a heartbeat of looking that she cannot spend on anything else
  • A working must have a seam: a pure physical blow has none, and a working that is all one law with no boundary has none
  • A Trait, which is the soul’s own law and no boundary at all, gives her nothing to cut; she can do nothing to ENSHŌ
  • Her eyes leaving the owner and going to the working give it away
  • It is beaten by a working with no seam, or by a fighter who never lets her stand still long enough to look

MUSUBI (結び · the Knot) · Application of Fixatio

MUSUBI is basically immobilization: the power to tie three turns of white light round a thing and stop it where it was. It is what the white cord did to her own sword, done to anything she can see.

Capabilities

Three quick turns of white light go round a thing, and Fixatio holds it where it was, in the state it was in, for as long as she keeps paying, like a fly in amber. She calls it in one word, “Musubi,” and the knot sets with an amber flash in the white. The hold is a cure and no grip: for that breath the other arrangement of the thing, the hand an inch further on, is nowhere it can go.

Fixatio’s hold is contested by Sovereignty, hers against that of whatever she has tied. A heartbeat is still a heartbeat. She ties it to buy one, for an ally’s arrow or her own edge, and she will tie it again.

Applications

  • Stopping a wrist, a raised blade or a fist in the air
  • Holding a door, or a fire’s front
  • Catching a working in the moment it fires
  • Buying one heartbeat against something stronger than she is

Associations

  • Fixatio, the Knot
  • The Fate Path: a condition tied on a thing she can see and name
  • Her Sovereignty, which the hold is contested on
  • THE WHITE KNOT, which it survives from

Limitations

  • What she binds must be a thing she can see and name, and she can hold a man’s arm and not his will
  • She can hold one thing at a time
  • It takes about three percent of her reserve for each turn of the breath
  • It is paid in her own arms: when the thing tied is stronger the strain goes down into her hands
  • A practitioner whose Sovereignty outranks hers pulls out of the knot at a cost to both of them; against a stronger Sovereignty it holds one heartbeat, cracks, and goes to white dust
  • A contested knot splits her fingernails one after another on whatever she is holding, and each knot she ties again opens a fresh split in her fingers
  • The three turns of light give it away
  • It is beaten by a will stronger than her Sovereignty, or by a thing she cannot see

SHINGAN (心眼 · the heart-eye) · Bloodline Faculty, Moto

SHINGAN is basically structural perception: the power to see where a thing carries its weight, and what is working under a face. It is the common Moto sight, which in her line is the Meigan, the Eye of Revelation.

Capabilities

It shows as fine silver rings in her eyes. In her the sight is held level and quiet, the rings lying as still as the face of a deep well, so that the moment they turn is the moment everyone in the room learns something has struck her. With Luminalis it reads beneath a face to the mechanism under it as well as where a thing carries its weight.

With the Lamp in it, it shows her the gold line the Kōkan heir’s own eye lays across the air, because the gold in that eye is the surface of something, and what lies under a surface is hers to see.

Applications

  • Reading where a thing carries its weight
  • Reading what is working under a face
  • Seeing the gold line the Kōkan heir’s eye lays across the air
  • Seeing every seam her Hall lights, as a fine bright line

Associations

  • The Moto blood, which gives the eye and an early grip on the Thread ladder, and never a Path
  • No lens of its own; Luminalis sharpens it
  • It reads, spends her own share, and pulls nothing down

Limitations

  • It cannot read a man who is truly still inside
  • It does not tell her what to do about what she sees
  • It is a slow, steady spend while it is held wide, about ten hours from full
  • At rest the rings lie flat and silver as winter ice; when they turn, the room knows she has been struck

SHIROBANE (白羽 · the White Feathers) · House Working, Byakuya

SHIROBANE is basically scanning light blades: the power to throw a silent fan of feathers made of light that open a body a finger deep and light it through. It is the house’s oldest working, and the same lamp thrown ahead of her.

Capabilities

The Byakuya beast is the Owl, and the working has the Owl’s shape for the same reason the Owl hunts as it does: nothing hears it come. A fan of white planes goes out ahead of her, a hundred, two hundred, each the shape of an owl’s flight feather and as long as a forearm, so thin it has no edge to look at, only a plane of light with a cold rainbow burning along its rim. They make no sound in the air and cross eighty paces in the time a candle flame takes to bend.

Each feather is light held so hard and so narrow that where it touches it boils the surface off into vapor, the way a burning-glass holes a leaf; the Measurewrights call that ablation. It does not cut as steel cuts, and it goes exactly as deep as she lets it, a finger’s depth into the muscle of a belly wall and no further.

The wound is beside the point. In the instant a feather is inside a body Luminalis lights that body through, the way a hand held in front of a candle shows the shadow of its bones, and she sees everything in it at once: a scalded lung, the pace of a heart, the heat banked in the tissue, the open fracture in a Crystal with every lesser crack running into it. Then she arrives behind the feathers, at a run faster than eyes follow, already knowing where everything is, so that a watcher thinks the last feather folded itself into a woman. She does not call it.

Applications

  • Reading the inside of a body at eighty paces
  • Finding a hidden wound, banked heat or a cracked Crystal before she closes
  • Covering her own arrival

Associations

  • Luminalis, the Lamp
  • The Spirit Path, Walked: projection, force at range, sent to read
  • The Byakuya house and its beast, the Owl
  • YUKIAKARI, out of which the Feathers go when it comes up uncalled

Limitations

  • A fan takes about one percent of her reserve
  • It wounds shallow by design, and what it shows she must still act on with something else
  • Fog, steam and smoke scatter the planes into glare before they arrive, and a mirror-bright surface throws them wide
  • Before it, every shadow near her splits in two and the beads ring, and a white fan opens ahead of her without a sound
  • It is beaten by bad air, or by a body that drinks heat, closing every seam the feathers open and keeping their heat for itself

Releases

YUKIAKARI (雪明かり · Snowlight) · First Release, the First Turn

YUKIAKARI is basically light embodiment: the power to stop looking through the Lamp and become it. She calls it “Akari,” light, and the name is the old word for the light that snow gives back at night, enough to walk by with no lamp lit.

Capabilities

Glass passes light and shows nothing of it. A prism is the same glass cut so that the light comes out sorted, every color laid beside the next, and the Release is that cut made in her: the Lamp stops being something she looks through and becomes something she is.

The glass of her aura goes to a prism. The near-white breaks into every color at once round her edges, the glass beads at her temples ring where they used to click, and the halo-ring at her crown throws a pale gold circle on the floor round her feet. Reflex, Finesse and Perception strike a step high on the hidden scale for as long as it holds.

It can come up in her uncalled, as it did a thousand mornings alone behind the closed door, with the beads ringing at the very edge of hearing and every shadow split in two, and the White Feathers go out of it.

Applications

  • Striking a step high in Reflex, Finesse and Perception
  • Shortening HAKKŌ’s violet line to half a breath
  • Sending out SHIROBANE

Associations

  • Luminalis, the Lamp let off its limit
  • The Spirit Path, Walked, with the Studied Body’s edge under it: the body made the lamp
  • HAKKŌ and SHIROBANE, which it sharpens
  • A Walked Spirit Release, past the one Release her Fate lead brings; it stands because the frozen road saw it

Limitations

  • Called silent, the prism stays inside the glass and the gain is half
  • It lasts about four minutes from a full reserve
  • Afterward her hands shake for an hour
  • Its price is the Veil: once anyone has seen it, the Stage VI lady is gone for good in front of them
  • It is beaten by everything that counters HAKKŌ, and by patience

HAKUREN (白廉 · the White Hall) · the One, the Second Turn

HAKUREN, as a Release, is basically domain activation: the power to open her Domain in one breath. It is her Fate lead’s one Release, the One, opened at Refraction and counting as her Second, and it opens and powers the Domain.

Capabilities

Called, the field forms as it is named. The note of the struck crystal bowl rises under the beads and holds, and the field opens.

Associations

  • HAKUREN, the Domain it opens and powers

Limitations

  • Silent, it comes in at half the reach
  • Held past its limit it is a Crystal Fracture Event along the line of the four letters, and she has never held it past its limit

KŌKAN NO MAE (Before the Red) · the One’s last stage, the Third Turn

KŌKAN NO MAE is basically harm interception: the power to make every blow meant for the heir land on her instead. It is the oath made law, and the third turn of the cord.

Capabilities

The One’s last stage makes one Trait’s stated effect local law, and here the effect is the oath’s own sentence, the red before the white. Fixatio binds her body to the place between him and the blow, wherever the blow comes from, and THE RED BEFORE THE WHITE lifts her Oath, Ward and Anchoring to the top of what her stack will hold, so that nothing in the field reaches him except through her.

For forty seconds the Hall has one seam in it, the line between the heir and the blow, and she is tied across it. Every blow that would have landed on him lands on her instead, and is entered against her. The Hall’s white takes Fixatio’s amber at its edges while it holds, and the sweet resin smell comes up through the clean one.

Applications

  • Keeping the Kōkan heir untouched by anything in the field
  • Lifting her Oath, Ward and Anchoring to the top of her stack

Associations

  • Fixatio, on the house’s oath
  • The Fate Path, Mastered: a binding made local law inside the Hall
  • THE RED BEFORE THE WHITE, whose stated effect it makes law, and that effect alone
  • HAKUREN, the Hall it must be called inside

Limitations

  • It can be called only with the Kōkan heir behind her, inside her White Hall
  • It lasts about forty seconds from a full reserve
  • The price is plain, and she said it at nine: what would have killed him is owed by her, and if it is enough, it is paid
  • Fixatio’s own failure is the price too, since a binding placed stays placed: she cannot step out from in front of a blow she would rather dodge, and the guard who was built never to be hit takes every one
  • Its limit is the oath’s own: it guards him from outside, and it has nothing to say if the knife is in his own hand
  • It is beaten by making her choose between him and his good, or by outlasting forty seconds

Domain

HAKUREN (白廉 · the White Hall) · Domain

HAKUREN is basically a revealing territory: the power to make a hundred and fifty meters of ground where nothing has a shadow and nothing has an inside she cannot see. It is the Lamp at its largest: where her eye finds one seam, the Hall shows every seam to everyone.

Capabilities

The One, counting as her Second, opens it in one breath, with Luminalis as the law of the ground. Every surface is lit from all sides at once with a clean near-white light that throws no shade. Veils, Letheveil concealments and Tenebra darks come apart in it like fog in a lamp. Every seam in every working, every weld in every blade, every place a body is carrying a wound it has hidden, shows to her Shingan as a fine bright line, and to her allies as a faint one. A lie spoken inside the field leaves the speaker’s mouth with a visible tremor in the light round it, because Luminalis shows the mechanism under a face, and a lie is a mechanism.

The school’s Glass Cathedral Sight, Hari no Dō, makes one room transparent to one reader for a breath. This is that art grown into a place and held. Called, it forms as it is named, on the note of the struck bowl, and the first thing anyone inside notices is their own feet, standing on a floor with no shadow under them. Like every Domain it is a claim laid on the ground and pulls no thread. The characters of its name say white and without stain, and the north has always heard a hall in it.

It adds no force to any blow and moves nothing. It only takes away the dark, and in a fight between people who rely on being misread, that is enough.

Applications

  • Stripping veils, concealments and darks from a whole field
  • Showing every seam, weld and hidden wound to her and her allies
  • Making a lie shake the light round the mouth that told it
  • Laying HAKKŌ’s violet line along a seam already lit
  • Giving KŌKAN NO MAE the ground it is called on

Associations

  • Luminalis, the Lamp
  • The Fate Path, Mastered: a claim on conditions, authored in the Core
  • The One, counting as her Second, which opens and powers it
  • The school’s Glass Cathedral Sight, grown into a place
  • Her Shingan, which reads what it lights

Limitations

  • It reaches about a hundred and fifty meters, and lasts about two and a half minutes from a full reserve
  • It adds no force to any blow and moves nothing
  • The law is total: light does not sort friend from enemy, and neither does the Hall; she cannot hide in it either, and it shows her allies’ secrets to her enemies as plainly as theirs to her
  • Its weak side is its Dominion, a mean of 145.71 with a Sovereignty of 195: a practitioner of her Stage with stronger Sovereignty contests the ground and dims the field from its edge
  • A Domain that is itself made of light, or a heat bright enough to drown it, washes it out
  • It is beaten by a stronger Sovereignty dimming it from the edge, by a brighter light or a great heat to drown it, or by fighting her with nothing hidden
