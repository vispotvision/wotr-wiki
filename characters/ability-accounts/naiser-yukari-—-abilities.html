Naiser Yukari — Abilities in full

Naiser Yukari — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202642 min read

Card: Naiser Yukari

THE RECORD OF BOTH HOUSES

THE RECORD OF BOTH HOUSES (両家の記録 · Ryōke no Kiroku) is basically record manipulation: the power to read what is already there, enter it, and make the entry hold. Whatever you say or owe near him goes into the book, and what is in the book comes true. It is his signature art, and his Traits, his techniques, his Releases and his Domain are this one idea done at a different size.

Capabilities

A house record does three things to a promise. It reads it, it writes it down, and after that it will not let the promise be anything other than what was written. His soul does those three things to people, to ground, to smoke, to wounds and to himself, and his two bloods are the two clerks who do the work. Two pendulum clocks hung on one beam fall into the same swing over a night, each nudging the other through the wood. The scholars call it entrainment, and the old clockmakers who found it could not stop it. His Crystal is the beam. The Yukari half, Ashura’s sight in his father’s line, sees every Aether thread in a room as a line. The Lambert half, his mother’s ledger, forecasts a blow from the shoulder’s first commitment by setting it against every blow it has ever counted. While the two swing together the sight gives the count something to count and the count tells the sight which thread matters. This part has no Wellspring behind it. It is inheritance and labor.

Three lenses shape the rest. Like any practitioner he pulls Aether (mana, in the common word) out of the ground he stands on, refines it in the Soul Crystal into Essence (spiritual energy), and drives it out through the Shell. He is a Spirit fighter with a Walked Body, so the Core authors everything and most of it lands on souls and on meaning, with enough left in the hands to carry a cut. Anamnesis, the Remembering, is the idea that everything keeps its past, and its operation is separation: the past a thing has kept is parted from it and read, as the residue is parted from the vessel that held it. A soul that has become that idea can touch a thing and read it, so under it a soul’s lineage, a stone’s history and a bone’s first shape are all pages. It reads and it never writes. The Measurewrights compare it to fired clay, which keeps the dose of its kiln and gives it back as light when it is heated again; their instruments can read the dose, and none of them has ever read a lineage. Fixatio, the Knot, is the lens that enters. Its idea is commitment, and its operation is coagulation: what is bound is set past melting, because the way back has stopped existing. A line set into the ground holds without him, a sentence set into smoke stands in still air, and a declaration made in his hearing sets with the padding fallen away, because a commitment takes only what was truly meant. The Measurewrights call it a thermoset cure, a resin whose cross-links leave no other shape to go back to, and their tables of cure times are the best in the four quarters. Ascensio, the Ladder of Ascent, is the idea of the ladder, the whole height climbed by steps with no single step large enough to break you, and its operation is distillation, the same work passed up again and again by degrees. It is how six Stages came to him quietly. The Measurewrights draw it as an inclined plane.

On a blow, the art rides inside the cut and never replaces it. The count chooses the line and the sight chooses the instant, the cut is real steel through real tissue, placed to the finger’s width, and the sum of what each man spent is known to him before the blade is clear.

Applications

  • Reading a blow before it is thrown, and tracking several fighters at once
  • Making oaths, truces and offers bind at the strength they were spoken
  • Showing a lie where the other man can see it
  • Bringing up everything unsettled in an opponent’s blood at once
  • Closing ground to anyone who has not decided what he is
  • Holding his own wounds at what was read
  • Striking the account of every exchange, and leaving the moment the sum turns
  • Calling every promise ever entered in the hall due at once

Techniques

  • TWIN-BLOOD HARMONY - the two bloods keeping one count, the clerks themselves
  • THE ENTERED WORD - what is said near him sets
  • THE BREATH’S CONTINUANCE - Ashura’s blood, which slows ruin and clears his senses
  • KAGE NO KEIFU - the record read out of another man
  • YUKARI NO MON - the record entered into ground, as a line
  • RYŌKE NO KATA - both bloods at full swing for one exchange, closed by SEISAN
  • KEMURI NO HAN - a sentence entered into smoke
  • NULL-LIGHT - the house’s instrument, as far as he carries it
  • MIZU NO UTSUWA, MIMAMORI, SENGAKI, YOMIAGE, KIZU-YOMI, MUSAN, IMI, MITSU NO MA - the moves found on the Aerne ridge, each called once
  • THE TWIN MANTLE - the First Release, the two halves of him shown at once
  • THE CORRIDOR BETWEEN HALLS - the Second Release and the Domain, the record given a floor
  • THE RECORD OPENS - the Second Release’s last stage, the whole archive read aloud at once

Associations

  • Anamnesis, the Remembering, reads the record a thing keeps of its own making
  • Fixatio, the Knot, enters what is read and holds it
  • Ascensio, the Ladder of Ascent, laid his Stages over a long road
  • The Spirit Path does the work, on souls and on meaning, and the Walked Body carries the cut
  • The Yukari blood gives the sight and the Lambert blood gives the count, and neither has a Wellspring behind it
  • The unnamed longblade, his bonded weapon, narrow and severe, carried more as punctuation than ornament
  • He does not shout any of it: a name is said once, low, in the house’s old Japonic, usually round the leaf, and the room goes quiet enough to hear it

Limitations

  • It reads and enters only what is already there: what was said, what is owed, what a thing was made as
  • The labor of keeping the two bloods agreed, the Equilibrium Tax, is charged against every great working he does
  • The book’s own price: his word sets too, the mistaken ones included, and people stand near him the way they stand near a record being written
  • What he binds stays bound, including mistakes; Fixatio’s named failure is over-cure, a rigidity that cannot bend even to save him
  • Ascensio’s failure is the debt: a ladder makes the height bearable and never makes it lower, every step is still owed, and he arrives everywhere owing the difference; at Stage XII he owes six
  • About a ninth of what he calls strays, and it comes off him as plain warmth at the fingertips, on the cord of the hilt or the paper of the leaf
  • Under a load too big to carry clean the heat shows in the thread-marks, which light silver, go violet at the edges, and redden the skin over them
  • Each of his arts costs a fixed share and lands entire; at full output he would empty in about 71 seconds, and nobody has ever seen him open at full
  • The charge always shows: his breath and heartbeat fall into one count, the two low notes of his aura drift together until they are one with a long hall’s echo under them, and the air round him dims as if somebody had gone down a corridor turning the lamps low, the gray of him going black-violet at the edges like black silk drawn over a blade
  • Smaller workings add their own sign: the shadow stretching for KAGE NO KEIFU, the dimmed line for the Gate, the smoke standing still for the Verdict, a small dry click as Fixatio sets
  • Against a higher Stage it thins in a known order: the forecast goes first, because a stronger fighter does things the ledger has never counted
  • THE ENTERED WORD and the Verdict keep working there, since they run on what is said and not on rank, and the Corridor still asks its question, though a soul far above him answers it and walks through at a cost
  • What he has left then is the hall’s doors, the account, and the choice to leave when the sum turns
  • It is beaten by saying nothing and swearing nothing, by driving his two bloods apart with ugly formless pressure or with grief, by hitting early before he settles, or by simply outlasting the form

Traits

TWIN-BLOOD HARMONY (双血調和 · Sōketsu Chōwa) · Primary Trait

TWIN-BLOOD HARMONY is basically bloodline synchronization: the power to have two inheritances keep one count, so that he is never where the blow goes and every cut of his arrives like something already decided. It is the two clerks of the art themselves.

Capabilities

Two inheritances that share one soul either tear it or learn to keep one count, and his learned. The Yukari current, Ashura’s sight in his father’s line, is wide and deep and always reaching for every thread in the room. The Lambert blood from his mother is exact and counting, the blood that reads a blow before it is thrown. They pulled against each other through his childhood, and then over years, through the beam of his own Crystal, they began to answer, the way two pendulum clocks hung on one beam fall into the same swing over a night, each nudging the other through the wood.

It is labor and inheritance, with no Wellspring behind it: the two bloods hold each other in his Core, and his Crystal is the beam they hang from. The Measurewrights call it entrainment and say coupled swings lock together through any shared medium; the yard says only that the clocks agree. The count sharpens the sight and the sight gives the count something to count. The count can read an attack worst first and answer it with breath alone, in for six and out for six. The count lays the next minute out in columns and the sight lays it out in threads, and he can read a whole field from both at once. His window names the state in plain words, AGREED at a hair’s width, then ONE COUNT.

Applications

  • Moving before a blow lands, so the opponent sees only a man who is never where the strike goes
  • Tracking several fighters at once and knowing which of them is committed
  • Never being surrounded by a crowd
  • Seeing where one duelist’s next line runs
  • Reading a whole field from the count and the sight together

Techniques

  • The Clocks Agree (同調 · Dōchō) - while the two swing together every action arrives less like a choice than an inevitability, and Finesse, Economy and Penetration run a step above what the numbers read
  • The Count Reads (算読 · Sandoku) - the Lambert half reads a blow before it is thrown by comparing the shoulder’s first commitment against thousands of earlier ones
  • The Sight Holds (糸視 · Itomi) - the Yukari half sees every Aether thread in the room, each person’s current as a line

Associations

  • The two inheritances, Yukari sight and Lambert count, with no Wellspring behind them
  • The Spirit Path reads, and the Walked Body’s hands land it
  • It scales with his Coherence and his Gnosis, and is bound to his two bloods agreeing
  • RYŌKE NO KATA and THE TWIN MANTLE, which are this Trait at full swing

Limitations

  • It is passive and constant and costs nothing to keep, which is why it is also the thing an opponent attacks
  • The forecast is made from old blows: it is accurate until the opponent does something no one has done, and blind to a blow nobody has thrown before
  • When the bloods disagree it fails all at once
  • Its failure is Rigidity: he will not lean toward either blood even when leaning would save him
  • Its cost is the Equilibrium Tax: every great working draws on the labor of keeping the bloods agreed, so a long fight turns his advantage into upkeep
  • His breath and heartbeat falling into one count, and the two notes of his aura drifting together, give it away
  • It is beaten by a rhythm that drives the bloods apart: a ragged, ugly fighter who cares nothing for form, or grief
  • That counter has been proved on him: one sentence about his daughter sent the Yukari reach one way and the Lambert count the other, the bloods tore, and everything under them came up at once

THE ENTERED WORD (録言 · Rokugen) · Secondary Trait

THE ENTERED WORD is basically declaration binding: the power to make a thing said near him come out plainer than it was meant, and then set so it cannot be taken back. His house enters every promise made in its hall in the Yukari record, and his mother’s house keeps its counts true or strikes the liar from its rolls, and between the two his soul learned that a word said is a word kept.

Capabilities

Fixatio is commitment, and what it binds stays bound because the way back has stopped existing. His soul has lived inside that idea so long that it reaches past him into what is said in his hearing: near him, declarations set. Reality listens a little harder when he decides what something is. The Measurewrights call it a thermoset cure, cross-links that leave no other shape to go back to.

A commitment takes only what was truly meant. The padding a lie needs does not set and falls away, so a boast spoken within twenty paces of him comes out as the bare thing under it, and a true threat is entered whole. Smoke near him bends once at a lie and straightens for the truth. An offer he makes aloud stands where he left it until it is taken or refused, and a vow of vengeance sworn beside him sets as plainly as his own offers do.

Applications

  • Making oaths taken in his presence bind more tightly, and betrayals leave deeper scars
  • Hearing a grievance stated more plainly than the speaker meant
  • Turning a negotiation into a record being written
  • Naming a truce that holds against a broken sword, a grudge or a bribe
  • Leaving an offer standing until it is taken or refused

Techniques

  • Setting (定着 · Teichaku) - declarations made near him set, and the other shape is no longer anywhere they can go back to
  • Plain Speech (平語 · Heigo) - a man who brings him a grievance hears himself state it plainly, because the extra words that would have hidden it do not set; the oldest of his tools
  • The Binding Truce (縛休戦 · Shibari Kyūsen) - he offers terms and they bind both sides at the strength of the Setting; breaking it costs the breaker, visibly, in the body

Associations

  • Fixatio, the Knot, does the curing
  • The Spirit Path, by writing
  • It is passive and social, bound to speech, and it scales with his Sovereignty and his Oath
  • The Yukari record and the Lambert rolls, where the habit came from

Limitations

  • It works only on what is said within his hearing
  • It takes no reserve
  • It runs on his own mouth as well: he cannot dress his own case up, and a sentence of his can stand in the air as a gray skein the wind will not take, a weight on his own chest until it is kept
  • Its failure is over-cure: he cannot go back on his own word, including the mistaken ones
  • Its price is nearness: people do not stand near him as near an ordinary man, but as near a record being written
  • Oaths spoken near him coming out plainer than the speaker meant give it away
  • It is beaten by saying nothing; a man who makes no declaration gives it nothing to set

THE BREATH’S CONTINUANCE (続息 · Zokusoku) · Inherited Trait

THE BREATH’S CONTINUANCE is basically decay resistance: the power to have ruin reach him slowly, and to hear the true note of a room through any noise. Ashura’s blood runs thinner in him than in his father, and it still lengthens the line of him and the life in it.

Capabilities

Damage reaches him more slowly than it should, so he stays composed under a wound that would have broken a faster man. He is hard to rush toward ruin. The same blood filters his senses, stripping noise from what he perceives.

The Breath is also a pipe. A Yukari Crystal runs down into the Soul Plane the way a root runs down to the water table, and a Yukari at an empty reserve does not stop: the spirit keeps being fed from below. The body is not built for that flood. When his reserve reaches nothing, soul sickness sets in under four parts in a hundred, and the plain matter of his hands begins to lift away in bloodless gray flecks while the soul inside stands whole. He has held that pipe shut all his life, and opening it on purpose is the last thing he would choose.

Applications

  • Staying composed under a wound
  • Reading a room’s real signal through a crowd, a glamour or a haze
  • Living longer and younger than most
  • Being fed from below when the reserve is empty, at the body’s cost

Techniques

  • Slow Ruin (緩壊 · Kankai) - a strange resistance to spiritual decay; it has no Form and is only how he is made
  • The Filtered Sense (濾感 · Rokan) - refined sensory filtering that strips noise from what he perceives

Associations

  • Ashura’s blood, with no Wellspring behind it
  • The Spirit Path, by reading, for the Filtered Sense
  • It is passive, slow and lengthening

Limitations

  • It slows ruin and does not stop it
  • It takes no reserve
  • Its price is the long view: he will outlive most of the people who stood near him, and knows it
  • The tell is that he does not hurry, ever
  • The body is not built for the pipe: at an empty reserve soul sickness sets in and the matter of his hands lifts away
  • It is beaten by being quick: hit him in the first moments, before he has settled into the slow, certain pace that is his best ground

Techniques

KAGE NO KEIFU (影の系譜 · Genealogy of Shadow) · Signature Technique

KAGE NO KEIFU is basically lineage reading: the power to reach a man with his shadow and make everything in that man’s blood he never settled stand up at once. It is the record read out of another person.

Capabilities

He lets a layered shadow-current run through the other man’s Aether, and Anamnesis reads it as it goes, the way a scholar reads a record. Everything keeps its past, a soul most of all, and Anamnesis parts that past from the soul that kept it so it can be read; the shadow-current is the hand that does the parting. It pulls every dormant tension, contradiction and lineage fracture in that soul up to the surface at once.

It is active and borne by contact. He has done it to other men a hundred times.

Applications

  • Bringing hesitation, backlash or a loss of form to an unstable enemy
  • Muddying the tempo of a disciplined one, who feels every unresolved inheritance in him together

Techniques

  • The Reading (読取 · Yomitori) - the shadow-current reads the soul’s stored history and pulls what is unresolved to the surface
  • The Surfacing (浮上 · Fujō) - what the reading does to the man once it is up

Associations

  • Anamnesis, the Remembering
  • The Spirit Path, by reading
  • He came to it by living it

Limitations

  • It needs contact through the Aether, a crossed blade or a held gaze
  • It takes about thirteen percent of his reserve
  • It surfaces only what is truly there, so against a soul with nothing unresolved the shadow-current comes back with nothing in its hands
  • Against a soul of pure disorder it would go looking for something unresolved, find nothing it could hold, and come back empty
  • His shadow stretching toward the other man gives it away
  • It is beaten by a soul with nothing unresolved in it, which is rarer than gold, or by a ward that returns a reading
  • It can be done to him, with a flat hand and a sentence, and he knows it by the feel

YUKARI NO MON (縁の門 · Gate of the Bound Line) · Signature Technique

YUKARI NO MON is basically boundary creation: the power to lay a line of dimmed air across the ground that asks whether you are enough of a person to cross it. It is the record entered into ground, and the seed of the Corridor’s Threshold.

Capabilities

He sets a threshold across the field, a line of pressure that marks the territory of his will, and Fixatio commits it to the ground. A thing committed has no way back to being otherwise, so once set the line holds without him. Those who cross without enough force of self feel resistance in body and spirit at once. It is less a wall than a declaration.

Inside the Corridor the same line is called as SHIKII and sets as a sill.

Applications

  • Denying an area to anyone without enough force of self
  • Marking the territory of his will

Techniques

  • The Line (線 · Sen) - the cured line itself, which holds without him
  • The Declaration (宣言 · Sengen) - what the line says to whoever means to cross it
  • THE CORRIDOR: SHIKII (敷居 · the Threshold) - one quiet word inside the hall and the dimmed line sets into a black sill under your feet, and whatever you are made of is asked what it is

Associations

  • Fixatio, the Knot
  • The Spirit Path, by writing
  • THE CORRIDOR BETWEEN HALLS, which is its fixed home
  • He came to it by living it

Limitations

  • It runs the length of a line he can walk
  • It takes about twenty-two percent of his reserve, and it holds while he stands
  • The air along it dims, as if a lamp had been turned down only there
  • It is beaten by crossing it with enough self, with a will that has already decided what it is, or by going round its ends

RYŌKE NO KATA (両家の型 · Form of Both Houses) · Signature Technique

RYŌKE NO KATA is basically waste-free combat: the power to fight one exchange with nothing wasted and nothing readable, and to strike the account at the end. It is the two bloods brought into full synchronized expression, the Yukari sight and the Lambert count swinging as one clock.

Capabilities

His strikes become ruthlessly efficient, his steps hard to read, his defense almost complete. It takes the waste out of a draw the yard already taught him, the count choosing the line and the sight choosing the instant, so the edge lands its whole weight on one point. It takes out waste and leaves the force as it was, and in a duel that is his answer to a stronger opponent.

Because the count is exact, every exchange ends with an account struck: what he spent, what the other man spent, who is ahead. He leaves the fight the moment the sum turns. At the close of the form he calls SEISAN and the account is struck: the cut is real steel through real tissue, placed to the finger’s width, and the sum of what each man spent is known to him before the blade is clear.

Applications

  • Answering a stronger opponent in a duel
  • Placing cuts to the finger’s width
  • Knowing who is ahead at the end of every exchange, and leaving when the sum turns
  • Under the Twin Mantle, placing six cuts in one pass, shoulder, belly, both thighs, both heels and the crest of the hair, each where it would cripple and not kill, all six opening together after he was back where he began

Techniques

  • The Form (型 · Kata) - the efficient strikes, unreadable steps and near-complete defense
  • The Reckoning (清算 · Seisan) - the account struck at the end of every exchange
  • RYŌKE NO KATA: SEISAN (清算 · the Reckoning) - the called cut that closes the form: said aloud, the name brings the form up with its whole rise, the draw goes out flat from the hip at the height of a raised forearm, and a thread of black runs down the edge and cuts another man’s law in two along the plane of the blade

Associations

  • The two inheritances, with no Wellspring behind them; Fixatio sets the account
  • The Spirit Path for the count and the sight, the Walked Body for the cut
  • The yard’s draw, taught under his father
  • NULL-LIGHT, whose thread SEISAN carries, and which has cut a law of slowness in two that way
  • THE TWIN MANTLE, which lifts it

Limitations

  • It lasts the length of one exchange
  • It takes about twenty-nine percent of his reserve, a little over a quarter of everything he carries, and SEISAN is paid inside it
  • Held too long or too often, it is where the Equilibrium Tax comes due
  • The two notes of his aura becoming one give it away; SEISAN shows as a black thread running down the steel from guard to point
  • On plain matter SEISAN’s thread is only an edge
  • It is beaten by the long fight: outlast it, and the clocks start to drift

KEMURI NO HAN (煙の判 · Smoke Verdict) · Signature Technique

KEMURI NO HAN is basically law creation: the power to speak a sentence through smoke and have it be law inside that smoke until the leaf burns out. The habit was his before the art, a pause in every argument, and Fixatio took the habit and made it law.

Capabilities

He lights the rolled leaf from a coal, and what he says through the smoke is entered. An exhaled breath of smoke shows where the air goes, and Fixatio commits it there, so the smoke stands in a skein that does not drift. It records the sentence spoken through it, and the sentence binds those inside it until the smoke clears.

He has breathed the smoke round three people without turning his head, and it set into three thin shells a hand’s breadth off their skins under the sentence “Nothing inside this smoke sleeps.” A drowse laid on the whole road ran round the shells like water round a stone, and the Stage-gap weight a Sin laid on the three stopped at the skin of smoke, because the smoke had already been told what its air was for.

Applications

  • Binding everyone inside the smoke to one spoken sentence
  • Showing a lie where the other man can see it
  • Snaring one person in a fight until he answers
  • Sheltering people from a drowse or from a stronger fighter’s weight

Techniques

  • The Skein (筋 · Suji) - the cured smoke that stands and does not drift, holding the sentence
  • The Bend (曲 · Magari) - the smoke bends once when a lie is told inside it, the same bend that tells him when he himself is lying; a lie detector the other man can see, which is the point
  • The Cord (縄 · Nawa) - a verdict set as a cord round one target, “you will not cross this floor until you have answered me,” held for as long as the leaf burns; a snare for a person and not for a body
  • The Shell (殻 · Kara) - the smoke breathed round people as thin shells under one sentence; a sip of his reserve

Associations

  • Fixatio, the Knot
  • The Spirit Path, by writing
  • The rolled smoke-leaf and its coal, which it is bound to
  • He came to it by living it

Limitations

  • It is short, and lasts as long as the leaf
  • It takes about eight percent of his reserve
  • It breaks if what he said becomes false
  • A shell’s price is the sentence itself: it binds those inside until the last wisp parts
  • The smoke from the leaf standing still gives it away
  • It is beaten by making what he said false: win the fight, change the terms, take the floor; a cord is only as strong as its sentence

NULL-LIGHT (無光 · Mukō) · Bloodline Instrument, as far as he carries it

NULL-LIGHT is basically supreme observation: the power to know a thing so completely that it has no shadow left, and a thing that well known cannot be changed behind his back. It is the Yukari house’s instrument, Ashura’s own way of seeing, under the law to be known is to be held, and what he keeps of it is a fragment.

Capabilities

Its law is to be known is to be held, and two acts of looking come out of it. A thing can be found by the light that does not come back from it, which is knowing without touching, the null reading. And a thing known closely enough, with no gap in the knowing, does not get to change behind the knower’s back, because it is held. The scholars call that the Zeno hold, after the watched pot. It gives no fire and moves no pebble. Its whole work is on souls, on workings and on what a thing is.

Laid along an edge, the fragment is the mirror of whatever law it meets: it knows the working whole, and a working known whole is held to its own opposite and lies down along the plane of the blade, so the steel goes through plain air at its own speed. The scholars draw it as a hump sent down a rope from each end, one flicked up and one flicked down, which leave the rope flat where they meet, and call it destructive interference.

With the Mantle and the Corridor both standing, the fragment can be pushed through the Corridor’s whole length and comes out of him as a blackness that covers the ground. Nothing under it casts a shadow, and the only shadow left is his own, stretching toward the target. Trained fighters go to their knees from the presence alone. With one word, “Begone,” he draws in the spiritual world first, where there is no hindrance on the scabbard: the cut runs down the threads to the other man’s Crystal before the steel has moved, the way a hammer blow travels through an iron rail faster than through the air, and the blade comes out after it, late. The Sin of Sloth died of it.

Applications

  • Cutting another man’s working in two along the plane of a blade
  • Knowing a man’s hurts, lies, Traits and next step without his feeling a thing
  • Turning the reading on himself
  • Cutting a Crystal through its threads before the steel moves
  • Holding the air along his edge still, so the blade meets no drag
  • Keeping his Crystal’s waste as a coal to spend later

Techniques

  • The Thread on the Edge - a thread of black from guard to point, blacker than shadow, with nothing on the steel to reflect the sky; it rides SEISAN as the mirror of the law it meets, and is enough for one plane, one edge, one stroke
  • The Hall Made It Long - the fragment pushed through the Corridor’s whole length, and the draw made in the spiritual world first on the word “Begone”
  • Mushō, the Null Reading - the one Regard he holds, by his own window’s word; it goes outward by habit, and he has turned it on himself, which the house had never written down
  • What the Whole Light left - something of Zenkō, the Whole Light, still in his eyes: air he looks at along his edge cannot move, and the waste his Crystal throws off can be cured by Fixatio into a violet-black coal that hangs behind his left shoulder until he spends it
  • Shin’en (心縁 · Affinity), the threshold only - the house’s name for a soul whose mastery of its own Essence is so complete that it shapes the Crystal’s form and attributes at will; he has stood in its doorway, black-eyed and shadowless, the Null worn as a skin, with four parts in a hundred of his reserve left

Associations

  • The Ketsumyōgan’s inheritance, with no Wellspring behind it
  • Ashura, whose way of seeing it is
  • The Spirit Path, by reading; it climbs on his Spirit Mastery
  • RYŌKE NO KATA, whose SEISAN carries the thread and pays for it
  • THE TWIN MANTLE and THE CORRIDOR BETWEEN HALLS, which make it long
  • Fixatio, which cures the coal
  • YOMIAGE, MIMAMORI and MUSAN, which use the null reading

Limitations

  • No Regard has been set down for him, and he told his daughter on the causeway that he did not have the gift his mother had
  • It gives no fire and moves no pebble
  • The thread answers only a working it matches, and on plain matter it is an edge and nothing more; its price is folded into RYŌKE NO KATA
  • The Hall Made It Long needs both Releases already paid for, follows only threads the Threshold has already cracked, and gets nothing to follow from a soul above the Corridor’s cutoff
  • A Resilience Ward answers Mushō as it answers any read, and a man who truly changes his mind in mid-stride walks out of it
  • Shin’en is not held; no Yukari has held it since Ashura walked
  • Its price is the Long Look: what he has held stays known to him for life, and to look away is to let all of it slip at once
  • Shadows go out of the ground near him, his own last, and that gives it away
  • Nothing arrives at the target, so no ward stops the knowing
  • What stops him is a working with no signature to match, a soul sound enough to answer the Corridor, or a spear in the body, which Null-Light has never turned

Associations

Limitations

THE TWIN MANTLE (双衣 · Sōi) · First Release

THE TWIN MANTLE is basically a power mode: the power to wear his aura as a black silk coat, show both halves of himself at once, and not be seen moving. It is TWIN-BLOOD HARMONY struck at the full of its strength.

Capabilities

His aura settles over him like a second coat: black silk over the shoulders, silver-violet at the seams, and for a breath the two halves of him show, the Breath’s long line and the Lambert count laid one over the other like two pages held to the light. He calls it in two low words, “Sōi… Nimaime,” and nobody sees him move.

The ground shows it instead, after he has gone through: a straight trench lifted out of the frost with nothing in it, a wave of pressure a breath late, and afterimages of a man in black silk fading like breath on a mirror. With the Corridor called on top of it the silk darkens to the color of a well’s bottom.

Applications

  • Moving faster than anyone watching can follow
  • Lifting RYŌKE NO KATA, which under it has placed six cuts in one pass
  • Standing under the Corridor, so that NULL-LIGHT can be made long

Techniques

  • The Second Page (二枚目 · Nimaime) - TWIN-BLOOD HARMONY strikes at the full of its strength, and the second page of him shows

Associations

  • The two inheritances, with no Wellspring behind them
  • The Spirit Path, landing through the Walked Body, by striking
  • The unnamed longblade, which carries the Mantle’s edge
  • THE CORRIDOR BETWEEN HALLS, which can be called on top of it

Limitations

  • It is called with the house’s name, spoken, or silent and weaker; silent, the second page does not show
  • It is short: about forty seconds from full, one fight’s worth
  • Afterward he is spent, and the quarrel in his blood is loud for an hour
  • With the Corridor standing, the Mantle keeps eating at his reserve the whole time the hall stands
  • The aura becoming a coat gives it away
  • It is beaten by outlasting it

THE RECORD OPENS (開録 · Kairoku) · the Second Release’s last stage

THE RECORD OPENS is basically obligation enforcement: the power to bring every promise ever entered in the hall due at once. It is the whole archive read aloud, with Fixatio holding the reading as law, and it is the Second Release’s last stage, for a Spirit soul carries two Releases and no Third.

Capabilities

He speaks the line from his Telling stone, Master the innards, and the outer world is yours to command, and the Yukari record opens: every promise ever entered in the hall, every debt counted on the Lambert bench, brought due at once inside the Corridor. Those who have kept their oaths find them a weight lifted. Those who have broken one feel it land, in body and spirit, at the size it was made. Nothing is invented. It is a reading of what is already owed.

It has never been called. With nothing left, he has asked the record a smaller thing and it answered: the Soul Plane rose over the Corridor as the hall’s own voice.

Applications

  • Lifting the weight of kept oaths from those who kept them
  • Landing every broken oath on its breaker at the size it was made
  • Holding every man in the hall to what he swore

Techniques

  • The Roll (点呼 · Tenko) - the reading of what each person in the hall already owes
  • The Cure (硬化 · Kōka) - Fixatio sets the reading as law for the length of the release, so a man cannot undo what he swore

Associations

  • Fixatio over Anamnesis
  • The Spirit Path, as a rite
  • His Telling stone, whose line opens it
  • THE CORRIDOR BETWEEN HALLS, which it can only be called inside

  • It can be called only inside the open Corridor
  • It is short and heavy: about twelve seconds
  • It costs the Corridor’s price and then the Equilibrium Tax, and afterward his bloods quarrel for a day
  • His own promises hold hardest of all
  • The line spoken aloud, and the record’s pages turning in the air, give it away
  • It is beaten by standing on no oath, by owing nothing, or by outlasting the twelve seconds

Domain

THE CORRIDOR BETWEEN HALLS (狭間の廊 · Hazama no Rō) · Domain

THE CORRIDOR BETWEEN HALLS is basically a judgment territory: the power to make the ground round him a hall where everything you owe gets heavier, and where anyone without enough self cannot arrive. It is the record given a floor, and it formed round him at Emanation without being called.

Capabilities

Space round him behaves like a place between houses, between vows, between names. Those caught in his strongest pressure report a corridor between ancestral halls, judged by both and owned by neither. It stands up as a double row of square cedar posts gone red-brown and resin-dark, the posts of the Enclave’s long hall and of the Lambert bench-house at Sallowgate laid one over the other, with a door of cedar at one end and a door of iron-bound oak at the other, and the smell of old paper under the cedar smoke.

A threshold belongs to the houses on either side of it and never to the one standing in it, and the only question it asks is by whose leave. That is the law of the whole Domain: Fixatio commits the ground to the judgment of both houses, and the measure the judgment takes is a self. “Enough self” is the cutoff. A fighter who has decided what he is goes down the hall like a bell’s low note. One who has not is below cutoff, and below cutoff a thing does not slow down in the hall; it fails to arrive, so his force, his speed and his workings die a pace past the line. The scholars have their own account of it, a waveguide, a passage that carries only the modes it was built for and lets everything below its cutoff fade in the first paces, and they are right about how far the fading reaches and never about why a self is what it measures.

It reads the relations a rival law is made of, one thread at a time, and each thread that has no self behind it fades from the far end toward the near. With nothing left to spend, it can form again uncalled: the dimness goes out from two paces to six, the pillars stand up, and a hand that pushes to shut it with no leave from either house dies below cutoff. He calls himself a threshold that asks. The space that formed where the two bloods meet is the narrowest place in him, and it is where the Domain grew.

Applications

  • Killing the force, speed and workings of anyone who crosses without enough self
  • Reading what every person inside owes, and laying that weight on them
  • Fading a rival law thread by thread
  • Giving THE RECORD OPENS the hall it is called in
  • Giving NULL-LIGHT a length to run down

Techniques

  • The Threshold (敷居 · Shikii) - anyone crossing the line without enough force of self feels it as resistance in body and spirit at once, as if entering a doctrine that has already judged them unready, and the blow dies in the first pace past it
  • The Two Benches (両席 · Ryōseki) - the corridor is judged from both ends, the Yukari hall and the Lambert bench; every grievance, debt and unresolved inheritance a person carries is read, and the heavier the account, the heavier the air on them
  • The Length (廊の長 · Rō no Naga) - a hall and no field, about two hundred paces, with two doors, one at each end
  • THE CORRIDOR: SHIKII (敷居 · the Threshold) - called inside it, the dimmed line sets into a black sill under one man, the Two Benches read his whole account at once, and the pull his nature was built on is asked what it is and can crack like glaze on a pot plunged cold

Associations

  • Fixatio, with Anamnesis reading the accounts
  • The Spirit Path: a judgment on souls authored in the Core, landing through a small use of the Studied Fate as a claim on the ground
  • His Attraction Layer, which carries territorial durability and is why the Corridor forms round him without being called
  • YUKARI NO MON, whose fixed home it is
  • The Second Release, which calls it

Limitations

  • Its radius stays short, about two hundred paces
  • It costs what a Domain of his rank costs and is held for minutes; begun inside another’s Domain it costs three times as much to start
  • A fighter above cutoff walks through, at a cost
  • The Two Benches tell him what a person owes and never what they will do
  • A guest may leave by either door
  • It is only as sound as the judgment of both houses, and a man who has settled with both is below nothing
  • A line of dimmed air, then pillars appearing in the dark at the corridor’s edges, and the two notes of his aura becoming a long hall’s echo, show it coming
  • It is beaten by crossing with enough self, or by walking out by a door
