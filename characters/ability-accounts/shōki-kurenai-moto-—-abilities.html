Shōki Kurenai Moto — Abilities in full

Shōki Kurenai Moto — Abilities in full

Nov 28, 202637 min read

Card: Shōki Kurenai Moto · The Sin of Wrath

THE BOOK THAT WILL NOT CLOSE

THE BOOK THAT WILL NOT CLOSE (未済の帳 · Misai no Chō) is basically revenge empowerment: the power to keep everything done to him as fire with the doer’s name on it, and to pay every bit of it back. It is his signature art, and his Traits, techniques, releases and Domain are this one idea at different sizes. The eye it reads by is the Kurenai blood’s and the closing stroke is the Moto house’s; everything else is his.

Capabilities

A headsman closes the register. That was his whole trade for twenty-two years: the verdict kills, the hand ends it, and the book shuts on the name. At the block the Hall made him the thing the book was shut on, and his soul answered with the one sentence it has held ever since, that what is done to him is never finished. The art is that sentence worked out at every size. A blow is an entry, so he takes it in. An entry has an author, so he keeps it under a name. A debtor has a record, so he reads it. An account comes due, so he pays it into the body of whoever owes it. The ink never dries, so heat leaks off him day and night. That is the whole ladder: the idea, the transformed body, the world obeying it.

It starts where every practitioner starts. He pulls Aether (mana, in the common word) out of the ground he stands on, packs it raw in his Shell as Charge, shapes it there by his Wellsprings’ lenses and drives it out unrefined, the Body’s way. His Shell is the corrupted kind the ladder-keepers call Rot-Born, which amplifies every working and corrodes a little with each one. Of everything he spends, seventy-one parts in a hundred reach the thing he means them for, which is his yield. The other twenty-nine burn in the furnace of his Shell and leave him as heat, which is poor for a Grandmaster, and that share is no side matter: it is the oven he lives in, and at full output the Measurewrights reckon it in terawatts.

Four Wellsprings shape it, and each does one job. Tarturon, the Fire of Reckoning, is the payment: the idea of reckoning, a fire that burns on what is owed and that nothing can smother once it is lit, so the same touch burns a butcher to the bone and only scorches a child. Exuroth, the Trial Flame, is the trial by fire, burned and plunged and burned again, and three centuries of it have set his Shell hard enough to carry loads that would shatter another vessel. Vantabriel, the Night, is the keeping, the night that puts nothing down: what strikes him does not fade. Ignivale, the Spark, is the smallest beginning that pays for itself, so in him the gap between wanting and burning is a hair wide. It rides a blow plainly, and both damages are real. The cuff is nine kilograms of black iron on the end of an arm that strikes at the top of what a body can deliver, and that lands first, as bone and crater. The fire goes in with it: Tarturon seeds the fire in whatever the iron touched, the count sets how hot it burns, and the account pours in after if he chooses to spend it.

Applications

  • Taking a blow as heat, and sealing the wound it opens
  • Keeping each blow under the name of whoever threw it, and paying it back to that one person
  • Counting the killings a person carries, and burning them by that count
  • Setting a fire inside a body or its kit that needs no air
  • Breaking steel, stone or bone with two touches and a blow
  • Closing a fight inside an oven that no heat can leave

Techniques

  • ENSHŌ - the entry: a blow that lands is taken in as heat
  • ONNEN - the keeping: the heat is banked under the name of whoever dealt it
  • TSUMI-YOMI - the reading: the count of a debtor’s killings
  • SHŌNETSU - the ink that never dries: the heat that leaks off him day and night
  • HAKANASHI - the sentence itself, the two Traits fused into one law
  • GŌKA - the payment, made by the fist into the body of whoever owes it
  • YAKI-IRE - the quench, in his palm, on someone else’s steel
  • SENTENCE OF SEVERANCE - the Moto house’s closing stroke
  • THE HELL FIRE - the kept heat of the Register standing up as fire
  • TATAMI-ABI - the whole oven folded down to one point
  • SHAKUNETSU - the First Release, the open book worn as a skin
  • ABI - the Second Release and the Domain, a field where nothing spent is allowed to leave
  • ENMA-CHŌ - the Third Release, the Hall itself on the morning of the sentence, with him holding the pen

Associations

  • Tarturon, the Fire of Reckoning, a Wellspring of Fate, is the payment
  • Exuroth, the Trial Flame, is the quench and the temper
  • Vantabriel, the Night, taken at the block, is the keeping, and gives his aura its black-violet rim
  • Ignivale, the Spark, is the low barrier between wanting and burning
  • The Body Path does the work; his Walked Fate Path carries the name on each debt, the weight the fire burns by, and the Domain’s claim
  • The Kurenai blood gives the eye, which has no Wellspring
  • The Moto house gives the closing stroke
  • The Hall Irons, his bonded weapon, which are also what he hits with
  • The headsman’s trade, twenty-two years of closing the register

Limitations

  • He can put nothing down: the fever never breaks, he does not sleep a full night, he cannot forgive, and a long fight he does not finish cooks him from the inside
  • Twenty-nine parts in a hundred of every spend burn in his Shell’s furnace and leave him as heat
  • Hot ground fills him faster and cold, wet ground slower, and his Rot-Born Shell corrodes a little with every working
  • Anyone who knows it sees the charge: at rest the air shimmers and frost dies in a ring around him, and when he means it he breathes in like a bellows, the Covenant Scar flares black-violet through his shirt and the Hall Irons go from blue-black to white
  • The amber-red of his aura brightens to an overdriven filament with its dark rim thickening on the side of whoever owes him most, and the roar of a blast furnace stands under everything he does, with iron ringing in it
  • It is beaten by cold and water, by harm that carries no force, by a chest with no killing in it, by one small blow from the side to drag him off his target, or by a crew that loads the bank past its quarter line on purpose
  • Tarturon fails as runaway: a reckoning has no end it can recognize, every burning adds to what is owed, and his anger has the same shape, so a heavy enough record fires him before he has chosen and he wastes himself on the first butcher in the room
  • Exuroth fails as quench cracking, the Measurewrights’ name for a trial that cannot tell an ordeal from a ruin: burned and plunged for three centuries without rest, he is loaded like a drop of tempered glass, and cold water on hot skin shatters the glaze
  • Vantabriel fails as saturation: the night keeps everything and puts none of it down, the bank holds a quarter of his reserve above his maximum, and past that line the excess goes into the soul that holds it
  • Ignivale fails as preignition, the working that goes off on a feeling

Traits

He carries three Trait risks. Resonance Fatigue is the fever after every fight, days long. Rigidity is absolute, every blow answered. His Shear Break condition is one sustained outward look, Asami, held.

ENSHŌ (炎症 · Inflammation) · Primary Trait

ENSHŌ is basically kinetic absorption: the power to take a blow as heat instead of as harm. He was the hot child of a cold line, and his soul did this before it ever tried; it is older than any of his Wellsprings.

Capabilities

A blow is an entry, and his soul took every entry in long before it learned the word: what lands on him is kept, and nothing of it is given back. So a blow neither rebounds nor tears. The whole of it stops in the tissue under it and becomes heat, the way a dead-blow hammer, filled with loose shot, swallows its own recoil. The Measurewrights measure how much of a collision comes back as bounce and call it restitution, a dropped ball of glass returning nearly all of it and a ball of wet clay none; they say his Shell drives it toward nothing, call what is left a perfectly inelastic collision, and point out that a struck anvil warms. Their figure for the heat is good, and they have no idea why a soul should refuse to give a blow back.

The spot flushes, swells, burns and hurts, the four old signs of inflammation all at once, and the heat cauterizes the wound as it opens, so a cut that would bleed a man white closes in a hiss. The heat stays in him and does not radiate, and Exuroth’s trial-hardened Shell and his Tolerance let him carry it. Vantabriel then keeps it and the Reigan signs it.

Applications

  • Walking into a blow, letting it land, and being hotter than he was
  • Closing a cut as it opens
  • Swallowing the heat YAKI-IRE’s second touch pulls out of a thing
  • Feeding the grudge bank

Associations

  • The Body Path, shaping what strikes him
  • His own soul’s first habit, carried on Exuroth’s trial-hardened Shell
  • His Tolerance, which lets him hold the heat
  • ONNEN, which keeps what it swallows
  • SHAKUNETSU, under which it strikes as hard as his Body IV will carry it, and ENMA-CHŌ, inside which it becomes local law

Limitations

  • It acts only on harm that arrives as force or heat; cold, poison, starvation of air, soul-work and Nihiloth negation pass straight through it
  • It banks the force of a blow and cannot bank its timing, so a palm timed to the beat of his heart can stop it; Ignivale firing into his own chest starts it again
  • Whatever does not strike as force goes past it entirely: a note can open the old crack at his nape along the line of the block, and a cut that carries no weight gives it nothing to drink
  • Its cost is thermal debt, as the Measurewrights call it: everything he keeps is owed back out, and if he cannot spend it or vent it, it leaves through the nearest organ, so a long fight he does not finish cooks him from inside, and he drinks like a horse after every one
  • It shows: struck flesh glows cherry-red and steams, and his blood hisses where another man’s would run
  • Cold beats it: water, ice and saturated cold ground pull the banked heat out of the struck tissue faster than the Trait can hold it
  • A fighter who never trades blows and bleeds him by attrition with things that carry no force gives it nothing to eat
  • Running water is the worst of it: the skin of steam that lets water ride on a very hot surface holds until the current tears it away, then the flood boils against him and pulls the tissue heat out, the glaze of his First Release shatters, and he bleeds without a hiss

ONNEN (怨念 · Grudge) · Secondary Trait

ONNEN is basically damage banking: the power to keep every blow he swallows under the name of whoever threw it, and to pay it back to that one person, all at once. It was forged at the block.

Capabilities

What ENSHŌ swallows, ONNEN keeps. Vantabriel is the night that keeps, enduring by carrying everything that was lost with none of it put down, and its operation is coagulation: what is taken is fixed in the soul and never given back. In him nothing is ever put down. The Measurewrights call it hysteresis, a thing whose present state is the sum of its past, as iron stays magnetized when the lodestone is gone, and they time how long such a thing takes to let go; in him it never does. So the heat does not decay, and his Reigan tags each portion with the soul that dealt it, which makes the bank a ledger of who owes what. It is always on and it never forgets; every insult that lands as force is entered.

He can spend a portion only against its author, where Tarturon burns it by the moral weight of the debt and it burns with the force of the original blow returned. The bank sits in Vantabriel’s keeping and not in his flesh, so cold that takes every bit of heat his tissue holds leaves the blows entered in the bank untouched. The name on a portion is a bond, and a bond finds its owner: a man hidden from every eye is found by the rim of the aura leaning toward him, because he can hide from watchers and not from what he owes.

Applications

  • Holding up to a quarter of his maximum reserve above that maximum, 82,663,922 EU
  • Pouring a debtor’s whole account into GŌKA
  • Finding a hidden man by the lean of the aura’s rim
  • Banking from every hostile act in the field inside ABI, regardless of whom it struck
  • Fueling THE HELL FIRE, which burns on what the man in front of him owes

Associations

  • Vantabriel, the Night, for the keeping, and Tarturon for the spending
  • The Body Path for the heat kept, with his Walked Fate Path’s share for the name on it
  • The Reigan, which signs each portion
  • ENSHŌ, which fills it

Limitations

  • A portion can be spent only against the one who dealt it
  • A full bank is fever: it runs him at a heat that would kill a man and makes him sleepless
  • Past a quarter the excess has nowhere to go but into the structure holding it, which is a Crystal Fracture Event
  • It shows as the black-violet rim of his aura thickening toward whoever has hurt him
  • Give him nothing to bank: strike from range with harm he cannot turn to heat, or never strike him at all
  • Or overfeed him on purpose, several fighters loading him with more than he can spend, until the bank saturates and the overflow cracks his own lattice; it works, and until it works every blow is a coin in his purse
  • A Catharsis or Lethegraal practitioner who can lay hands on him can hurry the bank into settling and drain it, which he will kill to prevent

TSUMI-YOMI (罪読み · Sin-Reading) · Inherited Trait

TSUMI-YOMI is basically sin detection: the power to look at a breastbone and count everyone that person has ever killed. It is the Kurenai branch of the Moto heart-eye, the Reigan, hardened in him into a Trait.

Capabilities

The Kurenai Reigan reads curse anatomy and soul structure, and in the line’s headsmen it reads the record every killing leaves in the Attraction Layer, scar on scar, the way fired clay keeps the dose it has absorbed. He cannot look at a chest without counting what it holds, and the count is the moral weight his Tarturon fire burns by. A target with a long record takes GŌKA at full; a target who has never killed takes it at its base and no more.

The count comes to him as a number and he says it aloud, and calls the person by it. He can read a count out one entry at a time, with the place and the year of each. He reads by sight of the chest, and his heat-sense is a separate thing that the eye does not need.

Applications

  • Setting how hot his fire burns on a given person
  • Reading a count aloud, entry by entry, with the place and the year of each
  • Giving SENTENCE OF SEVERANCE its judgment, already made

Associations

  • The Kurenai blood’s inheritance, with no Wellspring; the blood gives the eye and a grip on the Work, and never a Path
  • It reads the Attraction Layer and pulls nothing down
  • Tarturon, which burns by the count
  • Ignivale, whose low barrier lets a heavy count fire him

Limitations

  • It reads only what has been done, never what is about to be done, and it reads the dead as nothing
  • He cannot stop reading, and a record heavy enough fires his heat before he chooses, which is how he wastes himself on the first butcher in a room while the real threat walks behind him
  • It shows as ring-lines and red capillary light in the left eye, with his gaze on the breastbone
  • A soul with no killing in it leaves him counting nothing and burning at base
  • So does any ward that returns or quiets a reading, Oblivara’s retroreflection or a Tenebra veil
  • A Tenebra hush holds only while its holder spends it on the record; spent on hiding the body instead, the record comes through plain

SHŌNETSU (焦熱 · Scorching Heat) · Passive Trait

SHŌNETSU is basically heat radiation: the power, which he cannot switch off, to make any place he stands into a forge. The Hell of Scorching Heat is where he lives, carried with him.

Capabilities

It is his furnace burning in the open and his bank’s leakage together, with no lens of its own. At rest he sheds as much heat as a smithy forge in full blast, so snow will not lie within ten yards of him, frost melts off the ground he crosses, iron in a room with him grows too hot to hold bare, and the untrained sweat, then sicken, then go down with heat-stroke inside the hour. Fighting at full output twenty-nine parts in a hundred of everything he spends burn off him, terawatts in the burst by the Measurewrights’ reckoning, and the yard turns to an oven, the air shimmering, wood scorching black, stone spalling.

It has a worse face. Let go on purpose and without flame, the heat goes out of him as a bruise-colored haze with the rage riding in it, and in every body it reaches the blood answers it the way blood answers a wound, everywhere at once: a fever that will not break, flesh swelling and burning, glaze-crack lines rising on the skin, the body’s own defense turned on the body. It goes from house to house; a Kharven border village on the western shield died of it, all but one girl in the last house past the bridge and a white-eared dog.

Applications

  • Turning a room into a forge and a yard into an oven
  • Sensing who is near by their warmth
  • Killing without flame, house by house

Techniques

  • Heat-sense - he feels warm bodies the way other men see lamps, and in a room hot as a kiln a veiled body shows as the one shape colder than the air; a warm crowd blinds it
  • The fever-storm - the physicians’ name for the heat let go on purpose as a bruise-colored haze, which turns every body it reaches against itself

Associations

  • The Body Path, unshaped
  • No lens of its own: it is the furnace of all four Wellsprings, burning in the open, and the leakage of the bank

Limitations

  • It costs him concealment and company: he cannot hide from anyone who can feel heat, he cannot be near anything he wants to keep, and he cannot be silent the way the Kurenai walk
  • It shows wherever he goes: snow will not lie within ten yards of him
  • A warm crowd blinds the heat-sense
  • Distance beats the forge and the fever-storm alike, and so does cold, wet ground that drinks the heat
  • Practitioners with Tolerance or Abython’s equalization stand in it unharmed
  • A fight on a frozen lake or in a winter river runs on terms he hates

HAKANASHI (墓無し · The Unburied) · Compound Trait, ENSHŌ and ONNEN

HAKANASHI is basically unfinishable existence: the power, and the curse, that nothing done to him can be finished, his death included. It is ENSHŌ and ONNEN fused into one law.

Capabilities

At the block he went into Asami and looked inward instead of out, the Shallow-Seen Unburied the Moto scholars refused to name, and then, at Gisei, he burned the bond to his wife for the fuel to break the irons. The two Traits fused into one law in that moment, the sentence he held as she died: what is done to me is never finished. Under that law ENSHŌ and ONNEN no longer switch off, Obsession Force has the whole of him, and the Crystal’s Overgrowth has made his body the shape of the law, which is why the Church files a Moto beside the First Eon’s demons.

The law reaches further than blows. A death is a thing done to him, so it cannot finish: the Hall could not execute him, thirteen Kurenai could not, and a blade he turns on himself is a blow he must answer first, which has ended every attempt. A lost limb is unfinished too. A stump driven into the floor of his own Register is rebuilt by the kept heat from the bud outward, the way a salamander regrows a leg.

Applications

  • Answering every blow that lands
  • Outliving execution, thirteen Kurenai, and his own hand
  • Regrowing a lost limb from kept heat, inside his Register

Associations

  • His Walked Fate Path, binding two Body Traits
  • No Wellspring: it is the law of his own Core, held by Obsession Force
  • ENSHŌ and ONNEN, which it keeps from ever switching off
  • ENMA-CHŌ, whose floor the stump is driven into

Limitations

  • It is the Rigidity Effect at its absolute: he must answer every blow that lands, from anyone, before he does anything else
  • He cannot forgive anything, because forgiveness would discharge the whole bank at once into his own structure
  • Regrowing a limb costs the last of his reserve, a spike of fever and a lung filling with blood
  • It shows in that he answers every blow
  • That rigidity is the counter: a fighter who lands something small on him from the side pulls his answer off whoever he was killing, and a crew can drag him by the nose across a field this way while his clock runs
  • The law can shear: true Asami looks outward, and anything that could turn his eyes outward and hold them there long enough would leave the Unburied nothing to grip

Techniques

GŌKA (業火 · Karmic Fire) · Signature Technique

GŌKA is basically sin burning: the power to set a fire inside whatever his fist touches that burns without air, on the weight of the target’s own dead. It is the payment, the account coming due.

Capabilities

He breathes in like a bellows, the Covenant Scar on his chest flares black-violet, the Hall Irons go white, and he puts a fist on the target. On contact Tarturon seeds a fire in the struck body or kit, and it is the fire of reckoning: it burns on what is owed, the soul’s own calcination turned outward, and a fire that burns on a debt asks nothing of the air. It burns at about two and a half thousand degrees under water or under sand and cannot be smothered, since water only turns to steam and bursts, and because every burning adds to what is owed it runs away until the fuel is gone. The Measurewrights liken it to a smith’s thermite, a metal and a metal’s rust ground together so the powder holds both the fuel and the air, and they are right about the heat and wrong about why it will not go out.

Against a target with a long record read by TSUMI-YOMI he may pour that target’s whole ONNEN account into the same blow, as high as his Stage lets any working of his climb. Poured in whole, on a long count with a full account behind it, it boils a man’s blood in every vessel he has. Against a soul with no killing in it, it runs at base and nothing more. At base it lands at SS, a blow the Measurewrights put at about six and a half kilotons, and with a full bank behind it near twenty-one.

Applications

  • Burning a body or its kit from the inside, under water or sand
  • Paying a debtor’s whole account back in one blow
  • Boiling a man’s blood on a long count

Associations

  • Tarturon, the Fire of Reckoning
  • The Body Path, by contact, with his Walked Fate Path’s share for the weight it burns by
  • TSUMI-YOMI, which sets the count, and ONNEN, which holds the account
  • YAKI-IRE, whose second touch can drink its heat home again

Limitations

  • It needs contact; the fire is placed, never thrown
  • The bellows breath needs a sound chest and a clear beat: with a lung torn and a forehead in his face, a full Karmic Fire goes into the floor
  • At base it costs about twelve percent of his reserve
  • Past the reserve it costs the rage itself: the bank spent is gone, and for one turn after he is cooler and slower than he can stand
  • The bellows breath, the flaring scar and the white irons are a full beat of warning
  • Before it lands, stepping out of reach or putting anything in the way that he will hit first spends the working on the wrong thing
  • While it burns, the fire lives in what was struck, so shedding the struck pauldron or cutting out the burning flesh ends it, ugly as that is
  • Afterward, the cool turn that follows is the window, and every crew that has ever hurt him took it there
  • The fuel is the price paid in, and the working has a seam where the fire meets the law that tells it where to go; a working that looks at a technique instead of its owner can find that seam while the fire is still gathering and split it
  • Split that way, what falls away goes up as steam, the Measurewrights’ tons where their kilotons were owed, because the full price was never paid

YAKI-IRE (焼入れ · Quench) · Signature Technique

YAKI-IRE is basically thermal shock inducement: the power to heat a thing with one touch, drink the heat back out with a second, and break what is left with a third. The name is the swordsmith’s word for the moment the blade goes into the water.

Capabilities

His first touch drives heat into one spot of steel, iron, stone or bone until steel glows cherry-red above its change point. His second touch on the same spot pulls the heat back out through his palm in an instant, ENSHŌ swallowing it. It is Exuroth’s trial by fire laid on someone else’s steel, burned and plunged with no rest between, and the thing comes out the way the trial’s flaw says it must: hard, brittle and split along the lines of its ordeal. Every swordsmith knows the steel side of it: steel cooled too fast to settle goes hard as a file and as easy to snap, and a slow reheat gives the toughness back. Stone and bone go the same way, the outside shrinking faster than the inside when it is chilled in an instant, and the difference tearing it; the Measurewrights call the hard steel martensite and the tearing thermal shock. The third blow shatters what the first two prepared. On bone it runs the same: a forearm heated through old hairline cracks and quenched crazes like glaze and breaks under a chop.

The second touch will drink any heat, his own fire’s included. Laid on a man already burning with GŌKA, it draws the Karmic Fire’s heat back through the palm as fast as the other hand pours it in, so the fire stays inside the man instead of bursting outward and its warmth comes home to him, burning a man’s sins for warmth. Lift the hand and the fire leaves through the man all at once.

Applications

  • Breaking steel plate and mail, stone or bone
  • Holding Karmic Fire inside a man and drinking its warmth
  • Refunding a little heat to the bank with each cycle

Associations

  • Exuroth, the Trial Flame, whose trial by fire it is
  • The Body Path, shaping, riding a strike
  • ENSHŌ, which swallows the heat on the second touch
  • The Hall Irons, which beat steel plate and mail once it has had its two touches

Limitations

  • It does nothing to wood, leather, cloth, soft flesh or ductile metal: copper comes out of a quench softer and not harder, which the Akagane copper line has known for a thousand years
  • Each cycle costs one and a half percent of his reserve
  • It shows as a cherry-red handprint that goes black with a hiss on the second touch
  • Never let him touch the same place twice
  • Temper the struck piece by heating it gently before the second touch, which restores its toughness
  • Or fight him in copper, bronze and leather
  • Running cold water turns the art on its owner, quenching his own glazed skin until it shatters

SENTENCE OF SEVERANCE · House Art, Moto

SENTENCE OF SEVERANCE is basically judgment striking: the power to bring one cut down with the whole weight of a judgment already made. It is the Moto house’s concluding principle, taught, with no Wellspring.

Capabilities

Every Moto uses it on a target already revealed. He uses it on everyone, because TSUMI-YOMI has already judged them. It is a stroke and wants an edge, and for three centuries his edge has been his hands. Give him an edge, even one taken off a guard as he throws her down, and the headsman’s grip finds it at once: one cut, falling, with the whole of the judgment in it.

Applications

  • Closing on anyone he has counted, which is everyone
  • Putting a headsman’s stroke through any edge that comes to hand

Associations

  • The Moto house’s inheritance
  • TSUMI-YOMI, the reading it falls on
  • The headsman’s grip

Limitations

  • It wants an edge, and his hands have had to serve
  • The stare comes first and gives it away
  • A target his reading cannot see gives it nothing to fall under

THE HELL FIRE · Technique

THE HELL FIRE is basically stored-heat eruption: the power to stamp, split the floor of his Register, and stand its kept heat up as a wall of fire that needs no air. It is the Third Release doing by the acre what GŌKA does by the fist.

Capabilities

Inside ENMA-CHŌ the edge lets no heat out, so everything ever spent in the kept morning is still there, lying in the black polish of the floor like coals under ash. A stamp opens a seam in that floor and three centuries of kept heat stand up through it as a sheet or a ring of Tarturon fire, white-orange at the root, ember-orange in the body, black-violet along its crown, carrying its own breath and wanting no air.

Applications

  • Raising a sheet or a ring of fire across the hall
  • Opening the same seams for himself, to feed a wound

Associations

  • Tarturon, fed from what ENMA-CHŌ has kept
  • The Body Path, shaping
  • ONNEN, whose portion it burns on

Limitations

  • It works only inside his own Register and only against a soul he holds an account for
  • It spends only what is already banked or kept: it burns on what the man in front of him owes, so using it spends that man’s ONNEN portion and leaves him cooler for it
  • The stamp and the seam show it coming
  • Go through it fast, which is a flash burn and not a charring
  • Have given him nothing to spend, bring cold, or stay out of his Register

TATAMI-ABI (畳み阿鼻 · Abi Folded) · Technique

TATAMI-ABI is basically heat concentration: the power to haul his whole Domain in through the Irons and squeeze the oven down to one point at the tip of whatever he is holding. It is what he does with ABI when it is about to fall.

Capabilities

When ABI is about to fall, the kept heat is supposed to leave at once as a blast. He folds it instead. The Hall Irons carry part of the boundary, so he hauls the wall in by his wrists, and heat that cannot cross a wall has to go where the wall drives it: toward the one door left open, the Irons, the way a bellows squeezed shut sends every breath it holds out through the nozzle. The wood behind the shrinking dome goes cold and its fires go out.

Everything the oven held pours into him and down whatever is in his hand, and there the Work of Ruins, alone in him since the block and bent by Obsession toward ending whatever it is pointed at, presses it into a single point, hot past violet, that ends what enters it. The air screams going in, and an eye that reads flaws finds none in it.

Applications

  • Throwing the point as a spear
  • Driving it point-down into the ground so that its heat pays for the opening of ENMA-CHŌ, and the Third Release opens like a book from its spine without waiting on his reserve

Associations

  • Tarturon, bent by Hametsu no Gō, the Work of Ruins
  • The Body Path for the heat, his Walked Fate Path for the Work that points it
  • ABI, whose heat it is, and the Hall Irons, the door it goes through
  • It needs his Dominion and the Irons on his wrists

Limitations

  • It spends only what the Domain has already kept, and the whole Domain is spent to make it
  • It shows on the arm that holds it
  • It is unmistakable: a red dome drawing in toward his wrists like glass back into a blowpipe, with frost following it and the wood going cold
  • Be gone before the point is made
  • Or break an Iron, or get the Irons off him, since they are the door the heat goes through

Releases

The Hall Irons are his bonded weapon. They carry the First Release’s heat and part of ABI’s boundary; taken off him, the boundary of his Domain falls inward a third of its radius.

SHAKUNETSU (灼熱 · White Heat) · First Release

SHAKUNETSU is basically ember embodiment: the power to stand as fired glaze with ember light in every crack. It is the open book worn as a skin, and the north calls it the Burning.

Capabilities

At Stage XI the First Release no longer needs calling; it is how he stands, and it never ends while he is awake. His skin crazes like glaze on fired pots, ember light in every crack across the forearms and the chest, the hair lifts white off his skull, the chain stubs on the Irons glow, and ENSHŌ strikes as hard as his Body IV will carry it. Released silently it is simply him. Called aloud, “Shakunetsu!”, at the start of a fight he means to win, it flares to his full color and roars.

As a Body release it changes the body, and after three centuries on the slab in the Pit it found a new face. Called in that face, his right hand splits along the knuckles into black scale edged in violet light, the palm folds into two jaws, the fingers become long teeth white-hot at the points, and where the wrist had been there is a furnace throat: a dragon’s mouth on the end of his arm, the old crown beast of the Moto, which no Kurenai was ever permitted to wear.

Applications

  • Raising ENSHŌ as far as his Body IV will carry it
  • Pouring heat from the maw as a wave
  • Letting the same heat out slowly, through every crack of the glaze at once, when something stops him moving fast

Associations

  • A Body release
  • The Hall Irons, which carry its heat
  • ENSHŌ, which it lifts

Limitations

  • It takes about half a percent of his reserve a minute, under what he recovers at rest
  • The glaze is fired skin and breaks like it: a quench shatters it, and banked heat fires it shut again
  • Everything that counters ENSHŌ counters it
  • It never stops, so he is never fresh, and his fever is always running

ABI (阿鼻 · Without Interval) · Second Release

ABI the release is basically domain opening: the power to stand the oven up with one word. It is a field where nothing spent is allowed to leave.

Capabilities

Called, it is one word in the old court tongue, “Abi!”, and the dome stands as it is said. Its standing note is the blast furnace with its doors thrown open, and the field is the color of a kiln’s mouth. It opens and powers the Domain of the same name, in one turn.

Applications

  • Opening and powering the Domain

Associations

  • The Domain ABI, which it opens
  • The Hall Irons, which carry part of the boundary

Limitations

  • Held past its limit it is a Crystal Fracture Event along the old line of the block
  • Afterward he runs Overgrown until he has slept, and he does not sleep well

ENMA-CHŌ (閻魔帳 · Enma’s Register) · Third Release

ENMA-CHŌ is basically law imposition: the power to keep the morning of his sentence open as a place, and to set his own law over the world’s inside it. It is the Hall itself with him holding the pen.

Capabilities

Enma is the judge of the dead in the old stories, and his register is the book in which every deed is written. Called aloud, shouted in the court tongue, “Enma-chō!”, the field shows what it is: the Hall of Verdicts on the morning of the sentence, kept open. Kiln light stands in the windows and does not move, the floor is black polish, the seven chairs are empty, and the block is there with its stain. The field takes Vantabriel’s black-violet at its edges, and its deep note sounds under the furnace. Anyone inside is standing in his one morning, and an eye that reads flaws hears every line of the hall ring with the same dead note as the crack at his nape.

Its law is his, set over the world’s. Inside it ENSHŌ becomes local law two steps over its base for that effect alone, so no blow lands on him as force at all: all of it arrives as heat and is banked. A floor pushes back on whatever stands on it exactly as hard as it is pushed; that is one of the world’s oldest habits, and the only reason anything stands. In the Register the floor keeps that bargain only for what he allows, so it carries a man’s weight and will not take a blow handed down into it, and every technique that sheds force into the ground stops in its user’s body.

Its far edge is already folded, so where something else has breached that edge he can bend the fold onto the breach and throw a man out of the hall through it, back into the world.

Applications

  • Taking every blow as heat to be entered
  • Stopping any technique that sheds force into the ground
  • Reading a count aloud, entry by entry, and taking a price for each
  • Holding THE HELL FIRE in its floor
  • Throwing a man out of the hall through a breach
  • Opening on borrowed heat, which TATAMI-ABI’s can pay for

Associations

  • Vantabriel, whose black-violet stands at its edges
  • ENSHŌ, made local law
  • THE HELL FIRE and TATAMI-ABI
  • HAKANASHI, which regrows a limb from its floor
  • The Hall Irons

Limitations

  • It lasts about fifty seconds from a full reserve
  • Its limit is the bank: everything poured into him goes toward the quarter-reserve saturation line, and a crew that loads him deliberately past it inside ENMA-CHŌ cracks his Crystal with their own blows
  • Cold, poison, soul-work and Nihiloth negation pass through it as they pass through the Trait
  • Throwing a man out costs that man’s whole account and another third of the hall
  • It shrinks as it is hurt: one Iron broken on a flaw in its casting drops the boundary inward a third, and the light of the kept morning moves

Domain

ABI (阿鼻 · Without Interval) · Domain

ABI the Domain is basically heat trapping: the power to close a hundred and fifty meters of ground into an oven that no heat can leave, his own included. The deepest of the hells is the one without a pause.

Capabilities

His Domain is his doctrine laid on the ground as its law: nothing spent is allowed to leave. The Second Release opens it in one turn, out to a hundred and fifty meters, and inside the field heat cannot cross the boundary by any road. The Measurewrights call such a wall adiabatic and quote the oldest law in their book, that nothing spent is lost and only goes somewhere; here the ground has taken his doctrine for its own, and there is nowhere for it to go. So every working spent inside, what his furnace burns, what his enemies’ workings shed, every blow’s heat, stays in and the temperature only climbs, second by second.

A dome that size holds some seven million cubic meters of air, and at fighting output the air inside climbs a few degrees every second, so that the unwoken go dry-skinned, then confused, then into seizure. Wet ground makes it worse, since steam stops sweat from cooling anyone.

Applications

  • Cooking everyone inside a little more each second
  • Filling the grudge bank from every hostile act inside, regardless of whom it struck
  • Softening thickened air the way warmth loosens pitch, so he can move again
  • Drawing the whole field in by his wrists instead of letting it fall, which is TATAMI-ABI

Associations

  • Tarturon over Exuroth
  • Opened by a Body Second Release, its claim laid on the ground through his Walked Fate Path, with a Body Path’s law of heat inside it
  • His Dominion, with his Path gate holding Sovereignty under 276
  • The Second Release, which opens and powers it
  • The Hall Irons, which carry part of the boundary

Limitations

  • It is a crude Domain on a Dominion of 1,595: bodies can walk out across the boundary even though heat cannot
  • The law is symmetric: his own heat stays too, so a long Domain cooks him past his Tolerance as surely as anyone
  • It lasts two minutes and forty seconds from a full reserve
  • When it falls the stored heat leaves all at once as a blast from the boundary inward and outward
  • With the Irons taken off him the boundary falls inward a third of its radius
  • Walk out across the edge
  • Contest the ground with stronger Sovereignty and Gravity, as any practitioner of his Stage with both can, eroding the field from its edge
  • Or be heat-proofed or Abython-equalized and outlast two minutes forty in the oven, waiting for the field to fall on its owner
