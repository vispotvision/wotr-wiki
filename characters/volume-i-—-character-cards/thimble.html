Thimble

Thimble

Nov 28, 20263 min read

Thimblerunner and lookout on the great bridge at Kingsbridge · Human · Accord, Kingsbridge · as of the dawn after Lady Day 715 IC
EpithetThimble · the market’s own name for her, after the smallest thing on Ned’s tray; nobody on the bridge uses the name she was given. to Ned she is the best runner on the bridge, as the brass rose is the best thimble on it
ConceptA runner so small the market named her for the smallest thing on Ned’s tray, paid one thimble an errand.
Stat linea child, unwoken · no Stage and no rank (Seed)
Level not known · unclassed; the unwoken are offered none
no Path, so no Mastery · no lens
Combat grammarnone: a child
Aura and soundnone; an unwoken soul shows no coat. What a crowd gets of her is a small shape going through it at knee height · sound: tin thimbles knocking together in the pouch as she runs
How she movesat a run, mostly: up and down nineteen arches with the pouch bouncing on her chest, under elbows and round cart tails, where a grown runner has to wait for the press to open.
Stacknone; no reserve to spend and nothing to layer
Kita pouch of thimbles round her neck · the piece people talk about first: the pouch itself, her whole wage since Michaelmas; Ned’s Lady Day thimble was plain tin and she looked at it with contempt before it went in; the one she wants is not in it
Voiceshe holds out her hand for what she is owed · speaker type: a bridge child who runs errands · “You owe me a thimble, for running.”
