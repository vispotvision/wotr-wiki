Ketil BrandssonAccord bounty hunter of the Wainburgh
Human, Rimland timber stock, Corridor-born
the Iron-to-Salt Corridor, Marrowhold side
as of the open sea-gate at Yeomjeon, the night of Monday, September 27, 715 IC
Epithetthe Gleaner
the bounty boards and the Corridor halls say it of a man who lives on what others killed; it is wrong the other way round, since he wounds and tracks for days and others take the kill at the end of his trail, and he has stopped correcting it
ConceptAn old hunter who holds whatever he touches and cannot be moved off the ground he stands on, as long as it stays dry.
Stat lineStage III Ascension · Journeyman (Clouded)
Level 29 · Refined · unclassed until Glory
Body III Mastered, unread · Lenses: home
yield (η) 0.40, the Stage’s own figure
Combat grammarVerdict: he roots, reads the other fighter to the bottom, closes every line but one and kills in the one he left; Wainburgh bill-work behind it
Auracolor: dark rust and red earth, the hue of the ground he holds to
texture: a smoke that comes up off his forearms and trails from the head of the bill, thickening at the grip
feel: dry earth and rust; steam comes off him with it in cold air
the room: loose grit and salt dust creep in across the ground toward his boots, as filings creep to a lodestone
How he movesweight on the left leg, always; the right foot set down like a man testing ice and never given a push; rooted, the left foot before the right a pace apart, knees settled, the bill laid across the left side; beats a point aside with the back spike or the flat and gives no ground; the hook goes over a haft a foot behind the head and the hands close
Stacksustainable to the Stage III roof · cap two steps on the hidden scale over the sustainable figure · layers in play: none
Bonded weaponnone (the bill is kept, not bonded)
Kita padded jack and a coat he has slept in, Wainburgh cords faded to the color of an old bandage, and the piece people talk about first: the bill, seven feet of ash with a hook, a blade, a top spike and a back spike, Common
Voice”It’s my beast. Qui vulnerat sequitur. Who wounds, follows.”
speaker type: an old wagon-escort sergeant of a drovers’ road
Next gateStage IV Flourishing, held from Level 30 · Catalyst: the first genuine Spirit Axis bond
