Dhaerin Valorin

Dhaerin Valorin

Nov 28, 202610 min read

The Warden of Lines · Headmaster of the Scriptorium · Leader of Vaeloris

“Everything is held by where it ends. Tell me where it ends, and I will tell you what it costs to move it.”

I. Identity

Full Name: Dhaerin Valorin

Age: 38

Race / Lineage: Human · pureblood Valorin, the Scripted Soul Lattice bloodline

Faction: House Valorin · Headmaster of the Vaeloris Alchemical Scriptorium · Leader of Vaeloris, a rich, crowded village of great buildings and magic-filled land and caves, which hides from no one

Current Location: Vaeloris Village, Inner World

Family: Younger brother of Malex Valorin · brother of the late Rheana Valorin · uncle of Xhem Valorin, Rheana’s son · husband of Sienna Valorin, who lies in a magical hibernation · father of Lyria Valorin (12)

Status: Active

Catalyst Event: His first self-written glyph, as a child, was a straight line.

II. Soul Architecture

Stat line: Stage XIII Principality · Archmaster (Starred, as a Lattice reads it) · Level 480 · State not known · Warden of Lines · Fate lead, Ø · Spirit second, V · Lenses: home + 4

Level: 480; it holds Principality (401) and would hold Zenith (420), and no Residual Strain builds in him short of 500

Temperance Stage: XIII · Principality · Archmaster · stat ceiling 1,200

Soul: No Soul Crystal. A Scripted Soul Lattice grown to the depth a Crystal would call Starred, written in ruled lines instead of script: a surveyed soul, every region bounded and titled.

Path: Fate, leading (ownership, claims, recognition, price); Spirit, second (definition; where a meaning stops)

Mastery: Fate Ø, the lead (a Valorin cannot read Fate) · Spirit V, Ferryman · Body not walked, and an unwalked Path reads nothing (what he works on matter goes through his foci)

Class: Warden of Lines, evolving with his lead Mastery

Reserve: about 3.7 billion EU at full (log₁₀ EU = 3.4202 + 0.012812 × 480); a Fate-led bar reads as Essence.

Standing: a little stronger than his brother Malex. He never says so; Malex never lets him forget it.

III. Wellspring Harmonizations

  1. Oblation (Fate) · the Offering Flame; what is given up returns as force, never one grain more. Every boundary he moves is paid here.
  2. Fixatio (Medium) · a line he draws sets and stays set.
  3. Luminalis (Medium) · the line between what is seen and what is there; sees edges, hides the ones he wants hidden.
  4. Mortalis (Medium) · thresholds and completion; sees a boundary through to its proper end.
  5. Nihiloth (Wild) · the boundary between something and nothing; for his last art only, and seldom. Forms: Scriptia · Harmonia.

VI. Physical Force

Vitality 720, Ardency 1,000: thin in the body like every Valorin. He stops talking and starts drawing.

VII. Aether Flow

Essence through a surveyed lattice; every boundary moved is paid through Oblation at exact par. Domain upkeep about 1.6 million EU a second.

VIII. Traits

Lineage (Valorin Family page): Scripted Soul Lattice · Primordial Scriptia · Harmonic Translation Engine · Living Spell Memory · Arcane Vessel Sovereignty · the Cistern of Unspoken Lines · Scribed Flesh · the Inner Grimoire · Errata · the Distributed Soul.

His own (each feeds his boundary arts):

The Surveyor’s Eye Feeds every boundary art. Sees boundaries: of bodies, wards, claims, Domains, and between what a soul owns and what it only holds. Cannot move what he has not seen. Chaos and the undefined show nothing.

Consideration Feeds every boundary art and the Last Line. No boundary moves without a price; he always knows it exactly and pays it from what is truly his, returned as force at par. Cannot spend another’s claim; every payment leaves a hollow open until the bargain closes.

The Headmaster’s Bounds Feeds Terminus and the Walled Garden. Every ward, wall, door and boundary-stone of Vaeloris and the Scriptorium is written to his name; there, lines obey him first and cost a fraction. Abroad he pays full price.

Threshold-Walker Feeds Inter Est and Limen Apertum. Stands on the line between two states and belongs to neither for a breath. Fails when a working fills both sides at once.

IX. Domain

Hortus Conclusus, the Walled Garden · complete Principality Domain. About 12 km natural; the whole valley of Vaeloris on his own ground. Upkeep about 1.6 million EU/s; collapses at about 14% reserve, a little over half an hour from full. “Inside, every line is mine: what is in and what is out, what is owned and what is only held, what may cross and what may not.” Ruled silver lines cover everything; every edge shows. Behaviors: Partition · The Gate · Title Shown · The Price Board · Confinement · Restored Edges. A greater sovereignty contests it line by line; chaos walks through its walls.

X. Techniques

Boundary Manipulation. Everything is held by where it ends; he draws, moves, opens, closes, joins and restores those lines in Valorin Scriptia. No boundary moves without consideration.

  • Terminus, the Drawn Line · a boundary nothing crosses without his leave, in space or in concept; holds by his Sovereignty; price rises with what it holds back.
  • Divisio, the Line Through · parts a thing along a drawn line: a wall, a ward, a fused working, a curse from its host. Through a living body: trusted least, used last. Lines through greater claims do not take.
  • Confinium, the Shared Border · overlaps two boundaries into one; both must be his or consent.
  • Limen Apertum, the Opened Threshold · a door where a wall was; between his marked boundary-stones, here and there share one threshold. Only between marked stones.
  • Lex Finium, the Law of Limits · sets a working a limit: not beyond this line. Greater sovereignty pushes it back.
  • Inter Est, Between · on the line between two states, belonging to neither, for a breath.
  • Restitutio Finium, the Borders Restored · puts a broken boundary back: a breached ward, a torn Veil seam, a soul’s edge after possession. Restores the line, not what passed through.
  • Ultima Linea, the Last Line (Nihiloth) · draws the boundary between something and nothing beneath a thing and lets it fall to the far side. Oblation demands exactly what is unmade, and both times he has drawn it, the price was memory, which in a Valorin is the architecture of the soul.
    • First, at twenty-four: something nameless was beginning to be in a hollow under the staff gardens. He unmade it before it finished. Price: every memory of his own childhood before the age of nine. He knows his early years only as Malex tells them.
    • Second, the night Rheana died: her soul sickness could not be cut from her while she lived, because in a living soul the sickness and its edge were the same line. Price: every memory of his sister’s last year. He cannot remember her dying; Malex remembers for both of them.
    • Sworn never to draw it under a person. Archon and Titan boundaries are not his; symmetric null. Limits: metaphysical, not absolute: he cannot redraw his own boundaries or raise his own limits. Chaos has no edges. Greater claims push back. Overpaying ends in cavitation.

XI. Spirit Axes

Sienna Valorin his wife, Lyria’s mother; in a magical hibernation. He visits every evening after the Scriptorium closes; the line around her room is the one he walks first.

Lyria Valorin daughter, 12; the boundary he would pay any price to hold. She has grown up with her mother asleep.

Malex Valorin older brother and best colleague; a healthy brotherhood: they disagree about everything, drink together on the bad nights, and stand behind each other in every room that matters. Malex remembers two things Dhaerin paid away: his childhood, and Rheana’s last year.

Rheana Valorin younger sister, Xhem’s mother; died of a soul sickness; both brothers mourn her.

Xhem Valorin nephew, Rheana’s son; now teaching at the Scriptorium under him.

Vaeloris his village, written to his name.

XII. Resistances

Possession, intrusion, soul-edits: very high (Ward 1,100; surveyed soul). Spatial attacks and folds: high. Structured magic: high. Weak to chaos and the nameless and to dream logic. Deepest danger: overpaying (cavitation); two hollows are already held open. Counters: formless or ever-changing attacks, greater sovereignty, distance from his marked thresholds, being made to choose whose boundary to hold.

XIII. Physical Description

Six foot two, the tallest of the three siblings; broad-shouldered and straight-backed, like a surveyor who walks his own lines. Warm Kushian brown skin a shade darker than Malex’s; thick white Valorin hair, medium-length, worn loose and a little tousled, falling around his ears as if he has just pushed it back with one hand and forgotten it; a short white beard and mustache, a little rough at the edges. Pale royal violet eyes, the same as his brother’s and his nephew’s; ruled lines cross his irises when he reads a boundary. His face rests in a faint, amused half-smile, as if he has already worked out the price of whatever you are about to ask. Lattice markings in straight ruled lines along hands, forearms and collarbones. A deep violet headmaster’s robe edged in silver, properly closed, and the leader’s chain of iron keys and brass rulers. Smells of cedar, sealing wax, chalk dust and cold iron. People tend to stop where he stands.

XIV. Psychology

Core wound: he leads a village written to his name and could not keep a line around his own sister; in a living soul, the soul sickness and its edge were the same line. His wife sleeps behind a line he cannot open. Flaw: he prices everything, including what should not have a price; fair, and hard to love. Desire: keep Vaeloris, the Scriptorium, Lyria and Xhem safe; a school where nobody pays more than they owe. Voice: measured, formal, exact; he finishes every sentence he starts. Calls Malex’s work vandalism with footnotes; Malex calls his accounting.

XV. Equipment

The Chain of Vaeloris · leader’s chain; each brass ruler is cut from a village boundary-stone and is a marked threshold, so Vaeloris is always one step away. The Measuring Rod · black iron surveyor’s rod with silver rule marks; draws his lines. The Ledger of Prices · bonded grimoire recording every price he has paid; two pages sealed in black wax record the prices of the Last Line, not the memories.
