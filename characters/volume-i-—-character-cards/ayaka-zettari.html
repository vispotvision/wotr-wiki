Colorful Flower · Princess of Kushara

I. Identity

Full Name: Ayaka Zettari

Alias: Colorful flower (her mother’s name for her)

Age: 8

Race / Lineage: Human · Zettari and Yukari; the most Yukari of the children

Faction: House Zettari, Kushara. Youngest child and only daughter of Sovereign Dougou Zettari and Naevra Yukari.

Status: Alive

Family: Father Dougou; mother Naevra; brothers Ma’Kovu (15), Rashani (14), Rengai (12) and Zuberi (10, the heir). Uncle Naiser Yukari.

Current Location: The Sovereign House, Kushara Crownlands

Catalyst Event: Born with the Ketsumyōgan, the Yukari eye.
