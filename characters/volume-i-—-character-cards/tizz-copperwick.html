Queen Tizz Copperwick the FourthTinker Goblin queen of Tollgarth, the castle-town shut across the one good pass in the Harl Hills
Tinker Goblin
Accord, the Hundred Crowns of the Midmarch
as of the Tollgarth gate and the Struck Bushel, mid-April 715 IC
Epithetthe Copper Wick
off her own seal, a candle with a crown on its flame; the Tollgarth road says it fondly and means two things by it, that she lights every room she walks into and that a goblin queen burns down fast
ConceptA tiny goblin queen with one arm of her own and one of her own design, who shouts her machines’ names and will not take light money.
Stat lineStage not known, but woken: colors lit at will
Level not known · Class not known
Path not known · yield (η) under a half through the arm, by Ned’s eye at the windlass
Combat grammarnone; her machines and her arm
Aura and soundcolor: the clean hot blue at the root of a solder flame, close to the skin like a second coat, crackling like fat in a pan
her eyes go dark all at once when she shuts them and blaze when she draws
smell: hot metal, burned vitriol and scorched paper, strong enough to taste
the Long Arm’s sound: the ratchet’s tooth and pawl
How she movesquick and small, the right arm thin and burned, the made left arm lifting what no goblin could
Bonded weaponnone; the Long Arm is built into her shoulder and runs on her own runes
Traitsnone; what she has by blood is her people’s: the Spliced Runes cut into one limb, eyes of a hundred windows, and the Tinker temper, which has more pressure in it than the frame can hold
tell: the air goes tight, the smells sharpen, the key in her elbow turns by itself
counter: two paces back and a bucket chain, which all Tollgarth knows
Kita black leather coat to the knee with too many pockets, black copper-toed boots with soles as thick as a loaf, her second-best crown, and the piece people talk about first: the Long Arm, Mark Four, “Rare, if it’s anything”, never put on a beam

speaker type: a showman queen and engineer with a voice for a fish market or a battle

THE LONG ARM, MARK FOURSignature art——Lens the Spliced Runes of the Tinker Goblins, her people’s craft and her own cutting · Form Makenone writtenShe built a ratchet into her own elbow, so every beat of her temper buys one click and nothing she wins can slip back.The wheel’s own: it cannot move a thing by less than one tooth or faster than one beat, and it goes one way only until the key is turned back.—Heat: less than half the pull reaches the crank; the copper tempers straw to purple-blue, the enamel blisters, she sweats and breathes a breath to a tooth, her colors thin. And the fuel is her temper, which is the thing she has to mind to see forty.By the wheel’s own law it is slow, a tooth to a breath and three turns of a key first, so be finished before she is wound, or lift the pawl and everything it has kept runs back at once. BY THE INCH!Move of THE LONG ARM, MARK FOUR————Boots planted, the copper hand shut on the work, and the arm turns a tooth at a beat with a pulse of blue down the runes each time, the pawl keeping every tooth, so a goblin of three foot five lifts the four-ton grille of her own gate and never once holds it.—blue pulses down the runes, a tooth of the elbow ratchet at each beat, the copper tempering straw to purple-blue—by the wheel’s own law it is slow, a tooth to a breath, and a lifted pawl gives back all it kept—
THAT’S A DEED!Move of THE LONG ARM, MARK FOUR————One short turn of the key and the copper thumb goes dull red from nail to knuckle; on wax it is the Queen’s seal, and nobody has yet asked what it is on a hand.—one short turn of the key, blue in the neck scars for a breath—THAT’S A DEED! is one thumb on a short arm; stay off the end of it.—
MIND YOUR TEMPERMove of THE LONG ARM, MARK FOUR————Every window of her eyes goes dark, the key goes back one full turn against the ratchet, the wheel runs down, and the room gets to keep its roof. She does not shout this one.—every window of her eyes goes dark at once and the key goes back a full turn—none wanted; everybody in the room is hoping it works—

XII. Physical Description

FeatureDescription
HeightThree foot five in her boots (the saying is a boast about the boots).
Skin and faceSkin the soft gray-green of a weathered copper roof, sharp-boned and long-chinned, a little hawk’s nose, long ears with copper rings run up both edges.
Eyes and teethMultifaceted eyes of hot electric blue, each a dome of a hundred windows like a dragonfly’s; a great many very small teeth.
Right armHer own right arm thin and quick, burned shiny from fingertips to elbow, solder under every nail.
The made left armThe made left arm: black enamel over copper chipped at the four knuckles, a crooked solder seam down its length, a saucer-sized ratchet wheel with its iron pawl at the elbow and a brass wind-key standing out of the joint like a clock’s, a rune-cut copper socket at the shoulder whose marks run on into her skin as pale blue scars up her neck.
