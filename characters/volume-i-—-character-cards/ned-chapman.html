Ned Chapmanchapman (peddler) of the Kessen road; Initiate of the Mercers’ hall at Kingsbridge, on its pack roll; sutler of the Short Measure Company at a copper a day
Human, Midmarch commons
Accord (Northern English stratum)
as of the drumhead on the Hem road, Saturday, May 22, 715 IC
Epithetthe Warrior Merchant, coined by Hodge Toller at his toll-board on Lady Day 715 and charged two copper for; a minstrel’s song of it (wrong in every detail) runs from the Struck Bushel at Tollgarth to the Rolling Strike; Amabel Breakspear calls him Nobody’s, from his own “Nobody’s yet.”
ConceptA peddler whose hands can feel who owned a thing before him, and who means to be the greatest merchant in the world and a warrior too.
Stat lineUnwoken · no Stage, no rank (Seed)
Level 9 · Capped (named so by Wolstan Kemp’s finger) · no class
no Path walked, no Essence · no lens
Combat grammarVerdict in the making: he wins by knowing (a read, a measured length, a cried price), never by force he does not have
Aura and soundnone: he is unwoken and shows no color
the knack’s sound, heard by him alone: thriff, a dry riffle like ledger pages
smell: old paper and attic dust
How he movesA drover’s staff, one lesson deep: the drover’s hold, the low guard, the falling quarter stroke, the rake. He carries the staff upright in the left hand as a yardwand first.
Stacknone: no Essence to stack
Releasesnone: unwoken and Capped; a First Release opens at Stage IV, and he has no Stage · bonded weapon: none
Kita russet chapman’s coat with its right skirt and left sleeve gone, a half-tray of a deal-box lid hung on his belly by hemp rope, the ash measuring-staff with the ell burned in and a bunch of every ribbon he sells at its head, and the piece people talk about first: the scarlet-and-ribbon look of him, a maypole that learned to walk (everything he owns is Common)
Voice”Pins! Pins by the paper and needles by the one, laces, tapes and points, the best thread south of the Brine, and a good word for free! Buy the thread, friend, the word’s worth nothing, and I’ll prove it!“
speaker type: a market crier and pack-peddler, a patter man who closes every deal with “Done”

THE SLOW MINUTEMove of GUESSING WHOSE IT WAS————Skin on a used thing, turned to the light like any buyer pricing it, and in a minute he has the hand, the height, the reach, the years and the care, whether it bit flesh, its hardest load, and how the last hand left it. Never called aloud: to the whole bridge it is a chapman making up his mind.—————
