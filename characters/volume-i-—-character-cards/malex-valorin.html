Malex Valorin

Malex Valorin

Nov 28, 20269 min read

The Redactor · Professor of Refusal · the Last Lecturer

“If a phenomenon cannot explain why it is still occurring, I see no academic reason to permit it.”

I. Identity

Full Name: Malex Valorin

Age: 36

Race / Lineage: Human · pureblood Valorin, the Scripted Soul Lattice bloodline

Faction: House Valorin · Professor at the Vaeloris Alchemical Scriptorium, under his brother Dhaerin as headmaster and alongside his nephew Xhem (Scriptia diagnostics, reconstruction theory, foreign-system analysis, magical failure architecture, phenomena jurisprudence)

Current Location: Vaeloris Village, Inner World

Family: Eldest brother of Dhaerin Valorin, headmaster of the Scriptorium and leader of Vaeloris · brother of the late Rheana Valorin · uncle and colleague of Xhem Valorin, Rheana’s son · married to Helaena Valorin, born Garyen · no children

Status: Active, teaching

Catalyst Event: His lattice wrote its first glyph on its own when he was five and has never stopped writing since.

II. Soul Architecture

Stat line: Stage XIII Principality · Archmaster (Starred, by a Crystal’s reckoning) · Level 460 · Refined · Redactor · Spirit Mastered, V · Fate Walked, II · Lenses: home + 4

Level: 460; it holds Principality (401) and would hold Zenith (420), and no Residual Strain builds in him short of 500

Temperance Stage: XIII · Principality · Archmaster · stat ceiling 1,200

Soul: No Soul Crystal. A Living Ink Soul, a self-editing palimpsest, grown to the depth a Crystal would call Starred. Partly self-writing: once a problem is framed, the lattice keeps testing and revising while his attention is elsewhere.

Path: Spirit, leading (meaning and make-up: deconstruction); Fate, second (standing and admission: refusal)

Mastery: Spirit V, Ferryman · Fate II, Walked, with no grip by blood or blessing to lift it past II · Body none: he walks no Body road, and works matter through his foci

Class: Redactor; it evolves as his Spirit Mastery rises

Reserve: about 2.06 billion EU at full (log₁₀ EU = 3.4202 + 0.012812 × 460). The Cistern of Unspoken Lines makes each unit work harder than a Crystal-bearer’s.

III. Wellspring Harmonizations

  1. Judicium (Wild) · validity, recognition, consequence, standing.
  2. Fixatio (Medium) · anchoring; makes refusals hold and reconstructions stay rebuilt.
  3. Coagula (Wild) · gathering the broken without melting; the engine of reconstruction.
  4. Transference (Fate) · moves burden, property, Essence, inscription and function between holders.
  5. Anamnesis (Medium) · reads the structural memory of a thing’s earlier shape. Forms: Scriptia · Harmonia.

VI. Physical Force

Vitality 650 and Ardency 1,040 by the numbers: he can fight physically if he must, and seldom should. He would rather find out why an attack works and remove the reason.

VII. Aether Flow

Essence routed through a self-writing lattice; the Cistern pays out first; the Domain costs about 1.3 million EU a second to hold.

VIII. Traits

Lineage (Valorin Family page): Scripted Soul Lattice · Primordial Scriptia · Harmonic Translation Engine · Living Spell Memory · Arcane Vessel Sovereignty · the Cistern of Unspoken Lines · Scribed Flesh · the Inner Grimoire · Errata · the Distributed Soul.

His own (each feeds his arts, and each reads at his lead Mastery, Spirit V):

The Redactor Feeds Scholia Anatomica and Palimpsest Restitutio. Sees a working as separate clauses: Wellspring mechanism, Essence input, material form, Soul authorship, Fate recognition, Formation, environment, resonance, consequence. Removes one and leaves the rest. Undefined, paradoxical or fast-changing systems resist.

Nihil Admittitur Feeds the technique of that name and Margin Exclusion. After full diagnosis, a Three-Plane refusal: this phenomenon is not admitted here. The source, the caster and the Wellspring survive; the manifestation stops. He cannot cleanly refuse what he cannot define, and only where he has jurisdiction.

Professor’s Margin Feeds Scholia Anatomica and the Last Margin. Repetition teaches him: record, compare, invariants, then hidden dependencies and failure points. Learns nothing from what never repeats.

Living Footnotes Feeds every technique. Autonomous annotations investigate unfamiliar magic while his attention is elsewhere. They read, they do not act; a flood of unrelated workings swamps them.

Beside his nephew: Nihil Admittitur refuses a manifestation inside his jurisdiction after full diagnosis; Xhem’s Nullgrammar withdraws the Continuum’s recognition of one working anywhere, at once. Malex calls Nullgrammar “the student’s version, and the better one.”

IX. Domain

The Last Margin (Aula Negata) · complete Principality Domain. About 9.5 km natural, 40 km prepared. Upkeep about 1.3 million EU/s; collapses at about 14% reserve or catastrophic Formula Saturation, a little over twenty minutes from full. Doctrine: “What enters the lesson submits to examination. What cannot justify its continuation may be refused.” Everything inside acquires annotations describing how it functions; the Domain exposes grammar. Behaviors: Automatic Annotation · Structural Separation · Palimpsest Restoration · Margin Exclusion · Contradiction Exposure · Living Footnotes. It does not make him all-knowing.

X. Techniques

Scholia Anatomica, the Dissection of the Written Thing · separates a phenomenon into its working parts; about 80,000 to over 20 million EU; backlash Formula Saturation.

Palimpsest Restitutio, Reconstruction of the Lost Page · rebuilds damaged physical or magical structures from what survives; cannot invent a history that never existed; does not reverse time; wrong assumptions get rebuilt in.

Nihil Admittitur, Phenomenon Rejected · a single violet line crosses the phenomenon and it stops; about 2 million EU minimum; needs a defined phenomenon, full diagnosis and Three-Plane standing; backlash contradiction pressure across the lattice. Students called it being expelled from class.

XI. Spirit Axes

Helaena Valorin (born Garyen) his wife; alive; the strongest bond he has.

Dhaerin Valorin younger brother, headmaster of the Scriptorium and leader of Vaeloris. A healthy brotherhood: they disagree constantly, drink together on the bad nights, and stand behind each other in every room that matters. Dhaerin’s scholarship is cost and recognition; Malex’s is mechanism. Malex remembers a thing Dhaerin paid away for the Last Line: Dhaerin’s own childhood before nine. He tells it whenever asked.

Rheana Valorin their younger sister, Xhem’s mother; died of a soul sickness. Both brothers still mourn her, and neither says her name lightly.

Xhem Valorin nephew, Rheana’s son; once his student, now his colleague. “Yes. Try not to ruin him with the observation.”

His students frightened of him and devoted; the room is always full.

XII. Resistances

Structured magic he has read: very high (Ward 1,050). Possession and Crystal manipulation: very high, usually irrelevant. Severance and continuity attacks: high (distributed lattice). Weak to memory attacks, dream logic, Void and anti-continuity, probability instability. Known counters: speed before diagnosis finishes, ever-changing mechanisms, floods of unrelated workings, Formula Saturation, losing his foci, the genuinely nameless, and threats to his family, which lower his patience rather than his power.

XIII. Physical Description

Six foot; lean and deceptively strong. Thick white Valorin hair, straight, swept back from his brow and tucked behind his ears, a few strands always falling loose; a full white beard trimmed close at the cheeks; heavy white brows. Pale royal violet eyes, usually half-lidded; when something has earned his attention they narrow into a hard, unblinking glare that every student he has taught learns to dread. Warm Kushian brown skin; mature luminous lattice markings that sometimes annotate unfamiliar magic on their own. A long black high-collared coat with a burgundy mantle over one shoulder, the family’s color, which Xhem wears too; worn looser than the Scriptorium allows. Smells of wine, parchment, cedar and ink. Usually looks like a professor dragged out of a comfortable chair, wineglass in hand, until the glare arrives.

XIV. Psychology

Core wound: the lattice has never given him a quiet mind since five, and the grief for Rheana has not made it quieter. Flaw: wine and avoidance; he knows exactly what it is and has not stopped. Desire: to teach the next generation to ask on what grounds before they cast, and to keep Xhem from being ruined by being called a prodigy. Loop: noise, wine, quiet, avoidance, more wine. Under threat the laziness goes first and the wine second; then he starts removing things from the problem. He sees the same habit starting in his nephew with a different leaf, and has not decided how to say so without sounding like a hypocrite.

XV. Equipment

The Final Margin · scriptwood staff, dark silver rings, violet focus stone; widens his diagnostic jurisdiction and steadies refusals (Judicium · Fixatio). Errata · his living grimoire of failures (Anamnesis · Coagula). Wine · ordinary; the quantity is not.

XVI. Temperance Record

I Murmuring: first self-writing glyph at five. II Welling: lattice corrects formulae before he notices. III Ascension: first spontaneous deconstruction. IV Flourishing: rebuilt a Scriptorium ward without schematics. V Splintering: family, scholarship and his first wine collide. VI Glory: chose to teach, young. VII Refraction: understanding grants no moral permission. VIII Transcendence: the Last Margin first opens. IX Invocation: Judicium recognizes him as an adjudicating intelligence. X Realization: true Three-Plane deconstruction. XI Dissonance: some things should not be rebuilt, even when they can be. XII Emanation: autonomous annotations appear. XIII Principality: the Living Ink Soul stabilizes; the Domain matures. XIV Zenith: not reached; he currently has students.
