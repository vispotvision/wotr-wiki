Human

as of the fire on the Hem road, Saturday, May 22, 715 IC
EpithetThree-Copper Kit
the Short Measure’s own name for him, cried down the baggage line whenever the bow comes up; the Gedge twins say it as a boast and the levies say it as a warning, and the joke under it is that nobody has ever seen him waste the three copper
ConceptA deadpan crossbowman who prices every bolt before he shoots it, and is never once cheap about it.
Stat lineStage III Ascension · Journeyman (Clouded)
Level not known, nor the Crystal’s State · unclassed until Glory
Body III Mastered, unread · Lenses: home
yield (η) about 0.40, the Stage’s own figure
Combat grammarBlade: he waits for the opening, the thing standing still, and takes it once
Aura and soundcolor: a smoky steel gray with fine darker lines running through the smoke the way rope runs over a pulley, held close to the skin like breath on a cold morning, close and still
nearest Ascensio’s signature
signature sound: a faint dry creak like a rope taking up on a pulley
Pressurehe keeps his crown off on purpose so a room does not go heavy; the only sign on the Furlong was that the chain-men never once looked at him
How he movesa long, lean, quiet man who does everything slowly, as if each thing cost money and he did not mean to pay for any of it twice; spans by hand with the stirrup under his boot; lifts the bow a hair and calls the price
Stacksustainable 175, the Stage III roof · cap two steps on the hidden scale over the sustainable figure · layers in play: none
Bonded weaponnone (the crossbow is kept, not bonded)

speaker type: a veteran sellsword marksman who talks in prices and orders
