Bartram Ellwand

Bartram Ellwand

Nov 28, 20265 min read

Bartram Ellwandjourneyman Mercer factor at Kingsbridge · Human · Accord, Midmarch · as of the first-Thursday sitting of the Mercers’ Court, May 6, 715 IC
EpithetClean Hands · the pack roll’s name for him, off the wall benches, for a factor who has never once touched the goods; he likes it and thinks it is praise
ConceptA warm, funny salesman with spotless hands who will price anything, the living or the dead, and means to sink the chapman who talks like him.
Stat lineStage not known, rank not known
Level not known · whether he is woken is not known · unclassed, none shown
no Path known, no art and no colors known · Lenses: none known
Combat grammarnone known; his tools are price, paper and the room
Aura and soundnone shown
How he moveslike a man with a hall to sell to and no blow to throw: he stands, spreads both clean hands in the air before the Court and keeps them there while he talks, turns and opens the hands toward the man he is pricing with a lovely warm open smile, shakes his head over him slowly and fondly, bows a little to the benches and deeper to the Court, lets his eyes rest one moment where he means the cut to land, and sits; no guard, no stance and no footwork are shown
Stacknone shown; nothing to stack
Kita plum broadcloth coat far too good for his silver, and the piece people talk about first: that coat, three gold of cloth by Ned’s price, Rare, which on a Journeyman is a claim
Voice”I’ll price anything, Masters. The living or the dead. It’s all goods.” · speaker type: a big-room auctioneer and factor, Ned’s own patter turned round to face into the room

WantMarion Thurlow’s seat on the Court of Assistants (Marion: six years he has wanted it, and he will make her the Master who sponsored a fighter and Ned the fighter, at Midsummer, on a tray)

XII. Physical Description

Feature

Hands

Description

The cleanest hands Ned has ever seen, spread in the air before the Court as he talks: nails pared and buffed, no ink, no wax on the thumb, no scar, no callus from any rope, strap or staff; the hands of a factor who has never in his life touched the goods.

XIII. Psychology

FieldValue

Voicewarm, quick and funny; it goes round a hall as if it had a hand on every shoulder; the count and the joke that buys the next breath; calls men “cousin” and the Court “Masters”; makes the benches laugh on purpose
Sample lines”Taken, cousin! Taken, before witnesses!” (Ned’s own closer, taken for his) · “Ten pieces. Every one with its paper, Masters. Every one priced to the copper. Some of them honest.”

The plum coatplum broadcloth, the dark red-purple of a ripe plum’s skin (the herald-painters’ murrey; “the dyers called a fortune”), woven too wide and fulled dense and blind-faced so no thread of the weave shows, a soft nap that drinks the light; cut close and long to the knee, the edges unhemmed because broadcloth that good does not fray; twelve silver-gilt buttons, a little mulberry leaf on each———three gold of cloth by Ned’s price
