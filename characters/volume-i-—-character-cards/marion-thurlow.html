Marion Thurlow

Marion Thurlow

Nov 28, 20266 min read

Marion ThurlowMercer factor at Master standing (Very Rare goods), on the Court of Assistants of the Mercers’ hall at Kingsbridge · Human · Accord, Midmarch · as of the back stair of the Mercers’ hall, Thursday, May 6, 715 IC
EpithetWashers · the pack roll’s name for her, off the wall benches, after the word she has said to half of them without looking up; nobody on the height has ever said it where she could hear, and to her face she is Master Thurlow
ConceptA small, exact old merchant who can hear what’s in your purse, and who put a chapman’s orphan on the roll and won’t say why.
Stat lineUnwoken · no Stage, no rank (Seed)
Level 14 · Class none; the unwoken are offered none
no Path walked · no lens
Combat grammarnone; she fights with the ledger and the Court
Aura and soundnone: unwoken
How she moveslike a woman with a ledger to keep and no blow to throw: she sits very upright three places down from the Clerk with the book open under her right hand, and she does not look up, not for a jingled purse and not for a rival’s eyes resting on her; the pen moves a line and stops, and the stopping is the whole of her flinch; her thumb worries the wax of the token while she reads; the spectacles go on to read and come off to look at you; on a stair she comes down slowly with a hand on the rope rail and stops one step above a taller man so that her pale eyes are level with his, says “Walk with me” and walks nowhere; when the matter turns hard her voice goes down and never up; no guard, no stance and no footwork are shown
Stacknone; unwoken, nothing to stack
Kita plain black gown of good wool that cost more than everything Ned owns and looks as if it cost nothing, horn spectacles on a silver chain, and the piece people talk about first: the Master’s silver token of the Mercers (numeral VI, the bale and yard), its green wax rubbed to a smear by twenty years of her thumb
Voice”When I see you doing well, Initiate, I will enter it. Today I hear washers. Sit down or go.” · speaker type: a senior guild factor and court assistant who speaks like a ledger closing
Classnone; the unwoken are offered none, and what she has is a guild title, which is the hall’s trust and not the soul’s offer

X. Spirit Axes

PersonBondAxis weightWhat flows across it
Ned Chapmansponsored him onto the pack roll at fifteen, when nobody else on the height would sponsor a chapman’s orphan off the Kessen road, and has never told him why; heard him ask how a man holds both silvers and warned him off the sword; on the back stair warned him that Kemp’s bond is an indenture, what the hall rule does once read against a name, and that Ellwand means to sink them both; told him to stop selling the ribbons, heard “That’s the price I like”, and let him go——
Jasper Sealeythe Clerk she has outlasted three Masters with——
Bartram Ellwandher rival in office; has wanted her chair six years; she reads his plan: to make her the Master who sponsored a fighter onto the roll, and Ned the fighter, at Midsummer on a tray before the Court——
Wolstan Kempthe old enemy across the square; she has read his bond paper on the Court three times in twenty years, when men who signed it came to get out of it——
