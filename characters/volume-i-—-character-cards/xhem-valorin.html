Xhem Valorin

Xhem Valorin

Nov 28, 202610 min read

The Young Inkbinder · He Who Hears Magic Before It Speaks

I. Identity

Full Name: Xhem Valorin

Titles: The Young Inkbinder · the Vaeloris Prodigy · the Boy Without a Crystal

Age: 16

Race / Lineage: Human · House Valorin, the Scripted Soul Lattice bloodline

Faction: Vaeloris Village, Inner World, after the Sundering. Vaeloris Alchemical Scriptorium · wand-forges · Grimoire Conservatory. Sponsored by the House Valorin elders.

Family: Son of the late Rheana Valorin, who died of a soul sickness · nephew of Malex Valorin and of Dhaerin Valorin, headmaster of the Scriptorium and leader of Vaeloris · cousin Saella of the crimson branch

Status: Active

Catalyst Event: At nine he opened a sealed family grimoire that had rejected every child before him, collapsed for three days whispering unfinished formulae, and woke to say they were not curses, they were questions.

II. Soul Architecture

Stat line: Stage IX Invocation · Master (Fire) · Level 275 · Refined · Inkbinder · Fate leading, II · Spirit second, Walked, IV · Lenses: home + 2

Level: 275; it holds Invocation (215) and would hold Realization (230), and no Residual Strain builds in him short of 300

Temperance Stage: IX · Invocation · Master · stat ceiling 625

Soul: No Soul Crystal. A Scripted Soul Lattice with the same three faces, grown to the depth a Crystal would call Fire.

Path: Fate, leading (permissions, recognition, bonds); Spirit, second (comprehension and memory; gates a Stage late); Body only as far as his hands and tools.

Mastery: Fate II, Drawn, the lead, with no grip by blood or blessing to lift it past II · Spirit IV, Shepherd, Walked a rung behind · Body not walked, and nothing reads there; his hands work through his foci

Class: Inkbinder (evolves as his lead Mastery rises)

Reserve: about 8.8 million EU at full (log₁₀ EU = 3.4202 + 0.012812 × 275); a Fate-led bar reads as Essence. The Cistern of Unspoken Lines puts him on par with Crystal-bearers of his Stage.

Inner Grimoire: his since Stage VIII; the lattice is its own focus.

Releases: not known.

The climb: Stage III at fifteen to Stage IX at sixteen, every crossing lived on the Fate road.

III. Wellspring Harmonizations

Triune Core: Anamnesis (Medium; magical memory) · Fixatio (Medium; holds formulae and seals) · Ascensio (Distillation: separating a working into its parts).

Network: Transference (Fate; moves a working’s standing: Lent Script, Translation) · Judicium (names the weakest clause) · Luminalis (diagnostic light) · Coagulatio (gives ink a body) · Absolution (vents corruption).

Forms: Scriptia · Alchemia · Harmonia.

VI. Physical Force

Thin: Vitality and Ardency at means of 70 and 55. Scribed Flesh has begun writing his bone and sinew, and he still will never trade blows with a warrior. His force is in Ward 480, Analysis 450, Fluency 420 and Sovereignty 400.

VII. Aether Flow

Essence routed through the lattice as living language: intent into formula, formula into structure, structure into effect, through grimoires, wands, staffs, inks, catalysts and circles. Banked lines in the Cistern pay out first.

VIII. Traits

Lineage (Valorin Family page): Scripted Soul Lattice · Primordial Scriptia · Harmonic Translation Engine · Living Spell Memory · Arcane Vessel Sovereignty · the Cistern of Unspoken Lines · Scribed Flesh · the Inner Grimoire · Errata · the Distributed Soul.

His own (each feeds his arts; every Trait reads at his lead Mastery, Fate II):

The Grammar Heard Feeds Errata, Nullgrammar, the Translator’s Hand. Hears a working’s grammar as it forms, its sequence, pressure, intent and weakest clause, in a third of a breath to one breath. Overloaded by chaos, the nameless and the truly alien.

Grimoire Symbiosis Feeds Stored Workings and the Silent Page. Vellum-Wake as an organ: pages hold longer and fire cleaner, and it opens to the page he needs. Countered by book-burning, page-sealing wax, anti-script wards, theft.

The Translator’s Hand Feeds Translation and Lent Script. After one or two looks, rebuilds a simple-to-middling foreign working at four to six tenths of its strength; every working Nullgrammar strikes out leaves its shape in Vellum-Wake as a study page. Bloodline locks, Domains, miracles and chaos resist.

Wand-Hand Precision Feeds Wand-Script Bolt and the Struck Line. A mark within a finger’s width at forty meters. Countered by hand injuries, tremors, fear, disarming.

Mystic Lock Feeds the Seal. What he seals stays sealed while his written name holds it; only his hand or a greater authority reopens. Holds the structured, not a battering ram.

Margin Notes Feeds Errata and Lent Script. Writes small annotations into a working mid-cast, his or a consenting ally’s: lengthen, quiet, quicken, narrow. Each note costs; it never changes what a working fundamentally is.

IX. Domain

The Unwritten Library. Inside it every working must be written down to exist, and he can read everything written. About thirty meters, young. Every working legible to him at once; Nullgrammar at half cost; exceptions to his Silent Pages free. Smell of ink, paper, candle smoke and rainwater; footsteps like turning pages; lies hang as smudged text. Holds about a minute before Lattice Shear. Chaos stays illegible; a Domain of greater authority overwrites it.

X. Techniques

Featured creation: Nullgrammar (anti-magic). Every clean working is held up by recognition: the Continuum recognizes the caster’s intent, read off the Attraction Layer. Nullgrammar is Fate work: through the Grammar Heard he reads a working’s authorization, through Errata he writes one clause into it on the Fate plane, backed by his Sovereignty: this was not permitted. The Continuum withdraws recognition; the working comes apart into loose Aether, a shimmer and a smell of wet ink. The caster’s spend stays spent.

  • The Struck Line · by wand bolt; strikes out one working (a bolt mid-flight, a ward, an enchantment, a curse) to about forty meters; cost rises with the working’s Grade against his own Stage.
  • The Silent Page · a torn page of Vellum-Wake unfolding into a field of about ten meters; nothing new is cast inside unless it passes his clause; holds one to three minutes; exceptions written by name, his own spells included.
  • The Seal · written onto the thing itself; seals a ward, door, cursed book or Essence channel shut for hours to days (Mystic Lock).
  • The Unwritten Word · spoken, the whole lattice behind it; strikes one clause of a Domain’s law out of a pocket two or three meters across for a few breaths; risks Lattice Shear. Cannot: unpermit what was never permitted (Obsession-held workings); touch chaos or the nameless; stop momentum (a charge keeps its weight, a stone still falls); take hold against sacred blessings, Archon-scale workings, or Domains far above him; choose its targets inside the Silent Page.

Other arts: Errata: Correction (blunt, redirect, delay or invert a structured working) · Vaeloric Formula: Unbind (loosen a binding’s anchor clause up to his Stage) · Living Ink Barrier (a ten-meter script wall holding to around A-grade strikes) · Wand-Script Bolt (forty meters; can carry the Struck Line) · Translation · Lent Script (with consent) · Stored Workings (about a dozen prepared pages) · Ink Familiars (minutes now) · Silver Page Solvent (purges curse residue, poison-magic, corrupted ink).

XI. Spirit Axes

The Spirit of Written Memory and the Spirit of Alchemical Refinement; a faint, unborn Axis to the future idea of Magehood as a universal discipline. Vellum-Wake, his first true grimoire. His mother Rheana, dead of a soul sickness; he wears her medallion. His uncles Malex and Dhaerin, who see her face in his.

XII. Resistances

Crystal-targeting arts, possession and Crystal corruption: very high (no Crystal; Distributed Soul). Structured magic: very high (Errata, Nullgrammar, Ward 480). Memory attacks: his greatest danger. Speed and closeness, raw force, chaos and the nameless, Obsession-held workings: weak.

XIII. Physical Description

Five foot eleven, slim and long-limbed, long-fingered, narrow through the shoulders, and denser than he looks. A sharp jaw, high cheekbones, and a mouth that rarely bothers to smile. Warm Kushian brown skin, the same line as his uncles; faint gold and violet alchemical stains on his hands; luminous script along his fingers, wrists, forearms, throat and temples that changes with every new understanding. White Valorin hair in loose, heavy curls to his shoulders, usually falling across his brow and never quite combed. Royal violet eyes so pale they read almost blue in daylight, under heavy lids that make him look permanently unimpressed; when he casts, rings of red-violet Vaeloric script rotate around each pupil and the iris goes liquid like ink shot with light. Ink-burn lines on the right wrist. Out in the village, a deep burgundy mantle thrown over one shoulder and a gold high-collared tunic beneath; on a fine gold chain, his mother’s medallion, a gold roundel worked with a lion’s face. In the laboratory, violet scholar’s robes with ink-stained sleeves. Always the satchel belt of vials, chalk, pages and spell tags. Smells of black ink, lavender root, heated copper, old paper and rainwater in glass.

XIV. Psychology

Curious, obsessive, soft-spoken, brilliant; fights like a student solving a dangerous equation before it kills him. Wound: praised for brilliance before he knew whether he deserved it. Flaw: obsession that looks like curiosity. Desire: that no child is ever locked out of magic by birth. When afraid he gets more technical. He lives with the irony that his greatest work is a lock; he keeps Nullgrammar’s full formula in no book but his own lattice. “Magic does not hate us. It only refuses to answer questions asked poorly.”

XV. Equipment

Vellum-Wake · bonded grimoire of white bark paper, black hide and silver thread; holds his Silent Pages, stored workings and Nullgrammar’s study pages. The First Wand of Vaeloris · blackwood, moonbone sliver core, a strand of his hair in the handle; returns when called. The Seven-Vial Belt · solvent, ink, silver-root, binding chalk, bitter flame salt, memory oil, blank charm tags. Rheana’s medallion · a gold roundel worked with a lion’s face on a fine gold chain; his mother’s, worn since she died. A staff, half-written; maker and name not known.

XVI. Temperance Record

I · woken by the sealed grimoire at nine. III · crossed on stabilizing his first original wand formula. IV–IX · lived on the Fate road, from fifteen to sixteen.

XVII. Fracture Log

  • The Sealed Grimoire (age 9).
  • The Moonbone Wand Trial (age 13).
  • The Failed Living Ink Barrier (age 14): comprehension is not capacity.
  • The year of six Stages (15 to 16); writes Nullgrammar.
  • Finds the Unwritten Library. Costs he carries: Formula Headache, Over-Translation, Arcane Drift, Lattice Shear, Memory Burden.
