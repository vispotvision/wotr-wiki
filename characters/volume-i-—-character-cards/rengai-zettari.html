Rengai Zettari

Rengai Zettari

Nov 28, 20262 min read

Third son of the Sovereign of Kushara

I. Identity

Full Name: Rengai Zettari

Age: 12

Race / Lineage: Human · Zettari and Yukari; he takes after his mother’s house

Faction: House Zettari, Kushara. Third child of Sovereign Dougou Zettari and Naevra Yukari. Declined the heirship.

Status: Alive

Family: Father Dougou; mother Naevra; brothers Ma’Kovu (15), Rashani (14) and Zuberi (10, the heir); sister Ayaka (8). Uncle Naiser Yukari.

Current Location: The Sovereign House, Kushara Crownlands

Catalyst Event: Born with the Ketsumyōgan, the Yukari eye.

Stage, Level, Path and Crystal not known.

VIII. Traits

The Ketsumyōgan (the Binding Fate Eye) · Unlike his elder brothers he was born with the Yukari eye, which sees what everything is tied to, and with his mother’s house’s abilities, Null-Light among them. Which of them he has trained is not known.

His Father’s Wrath · In a fight he has Dougou’s anger.

IX. Domain

None.

X. Techniques

The Zettari arts · He grew up a martial artist and is mastering every one of the Zettari fighting arts, the Way of Weight first among them, and works hard at the Iron Bison. Named techniques not known.

XI. Spirit Axes

Dougou (father) Has told him he may join his elder brothers’ training when he turns thirteen. Rengai thinks this is unfair.

Naevra (mother) He favors her in looks. She pulls his ear when he hits his brother.

Ma’Kovu (brother) Rengai calls him a jack of all trades. They can argue for a full minute on two words.

Zuberi (brother) Teases him about having to wait. Rengai punches him for it.

Ayaka (sister) Teases him worse.

Rashani (brother).

XII. Resistances

Not known.

XIII. Physical Description

Height: Five foot ten. Eyes: Silver. Hair: Dirty blond, falling to his shoulders. He favors his mother.

XIV. Psychology

Impatient to fight and quick with his fists. Sulks when he is kept out. Proud of how hard he trains. The rest is not known.

XV. Equipment

Fighting wraps of the Way of Weight.

XVI. Temperance Record

Not known.

XVII. Fracture Log

  • Born with the Ketsumyōgan.
  • Declined the heirship of Kushara.
  • Told he may join the elder brothers’ training at thirteen.
  • Sails north with his family on the first ship to cross the seas of the Division.
