Jasper Sealey

Jasper Sealey

Nov 28, 20264 min read

Jasper SealeyClerk of the Mercers’ hall at Kingsbridge, thirty-one years · Human · Accord, Midmarch · as of the first-Thursday sitting, May 6, 715 IC
EpithetOld One-Note · the wall benches’ name for him, for the single note he sings every entry on; the minster’s version is kinder, the canon the hall kept
ConceptThe hall’s clerk with a voice for a choir and a quill behind each ear, who has not crossed a name off in a year.
Stat lineUnwoken as far as is known · no Stage, no rank
Level not known · unclassed, none shown
no Path walked, and an unwalked Path reads nothing · Lenses: none
Combat grammarnone
Aura and soundnone shown; what fills the hall is his voice, one long note that goes up into the hammer-beams and stays there, without his ever raising it
How he moveshardly at all: he sits at the end of the long table with the roll open before him and does not look up to ask for a word against; the left quill comes down, writes and goes back behind the ear, the right hand blots, and the right-hand quill stays where it is; he reaches under the roll for the hall’s book and opens it without looking for the place; when something is wrong in the room his left hand stops on the roll, and when it is settled the hand moves again; no guard, no stance and no footwork are shown
Stacknone shown; nothing to stack
Kita quill behind each ear: he writes with the left and crosses names off only with the right
Voice”Solutum. In registro relatum.” · speaker type: a guild clerk and precentor who sings the roll on one note
