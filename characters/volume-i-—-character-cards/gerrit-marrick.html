Gerrit MarrickCaptain of the Long Spear, elected head of House Marrick and of the Ochre Fields · Human, of the Long Fields · Hammerford · as of Spearmoot in the week of the midsummer Moot, about 732 IC
Epithetthe Spear of Hope

ConceptHit his line and you hit all of them, and he hits you back with all of them at once.
Stat lineStage IX Invocation · Master (Fire)

Combat grammarPercussion; the Long Fields rank, sixteen deep, with himself at the point of the file
Auracolor: Petralon’s heavy gray, the color of what everything else stands on, run through with Coagula’s amber-gray seams like a wall patched in yellow mortar · texture: settled, dust-heavy, a barley-floor in August · shape: a square, always; it goes out from him in a block with corners and fills the rank around him · feel: the smell of wet stubble and lamp-black, the ground under him sinking a little as the color comes up, and the men nearest him standing closer together without meaning to
How he moveshe never fights alone if he can help it; he stands at the point of a file, a long ash spear leveled two-handed over the shoulder of the man in front, weight sunk into the back leg and both boots planted like fence posts; he thrusts once, on the drum, from the hips, with the whole file’s lean coming up the shaft behind him; he gives no ground, because the ground has given up arguing with him; alone and hurt he backs to a wall and sets his feet and lets you hit him until the ground under him has taken enough to pay you back with; between blows he talks, loudly, mostly about money
Stacksustainable Vitality 1,900, Resilience 1,650 and Dominion 1,500 (Fortitude 520, Absorption 420, Command 450), Tempering 1,000 and Harmonics 900, the rest lower · cap two steps on the hidden scale over the sustainable figure · layers: SHOULDER! for one, OLD MARGET’S CART! alone filling the cap; every signed rank’s coat is outside help and stacks past it
Bonded weaponnone; a plain ash spear, shaved true and replaced every season, because the spear is not the point and he will tell you so
Kitplain black half-plate with an ochre sash, every buckle mended, over a high-collared coat of black wool worked across the breast in gold scroll-braid; a thin gold circlet across the brow with one round boss, which is the piece people talk about first and which he calls a receipt
Voice”I’ve fought for your father and I’ve fought against him, lad, and I’ll tell you what I told him both times. Rates go up at noon.” · speaker type: a loud, scarred, funny old mercenary captain who has buried everyone he ever liked and sent their widows the money on time

Value

A body built to be hit and a voice built to be obeyed: Fortitude 520 and Absorption 420, Command 450, Gravity 330 and Anchoring 300, Synergy 280 so the file’s bells ring true around him. Everything that would let him fight alone is low. With Dexterity 560 and Gnosis 420, a quick man who catches him without a rank finds a Stage IX who cannot see him coming and cannot get out of the way. The trouble is that the quick man has never yet caught him without a rank.
