Yeom SeolhwaAccord bounty hunter of the Wainburgh, come home
Human, raker-born
Yeomjeon, in Sum-gol
as of the open sea-gate at Yeomjeon, the night of Monday, September 27, 715 IC
Epithetthe Quickset
the bounty boards from Marrowhold to the Salt Quay; Ketil coined it in the Wainburgh yard. A quickset is a hedge grown from live cuttings, and quick in his tongue is both fast and alive: the halls hear “fast”
ConceptA raker’s daughter who feels every way already open, through a guard or across a salt field, and lets her spear and her feet go down it.
Stat lineStage III Ascension · Journeyman (Clouded)
Level 22 · Refined · unclassed until Glory
Body III Mastered, unread · Lenses: home
yield (η) 0.40, the Stage’s own figure
Combat grammarBlade: spear-work pass by pass, short thrusts and shorter recoveries, measure and the open gap; Wainburgh spear behind it
Auracolor: sky blue in spirals
texture: a thin blue smoke winding off the spearpoint in curls, never straight
feel: cold high air, like a mountain pass in March; the hair at her temples lifts in a wind nobody else feels
the room: before her greatest stroke the reeds lean toward her line, all of them, and the air smells of hilltops
How she movesthe spear low, its point going round in a slow loose ring that never closes and never rests; eyes gone soft, resting on no part of the man in front of her; flat-footed and light, along lines no sane foot would choose, sinking nowhere; strips her boots and fights in linen wrappings on her own ground
Stacksustainable to the Stage III roof · cap two steps on the hidden scale over the sustainable figure · layers in play: none
Bonded weaponnone (the spear is kept, not bonded)
Kita quilted coat of the southern cut with new Wainburgh cords at the throat, the Accord’s silver token at her belt, and the piece people talk about first: the spear, nine feet of ash with a leaf head that takes a shaving edge and the assay mark on it, Common
Voice”Not here. Here we say good water.”
speaker type: a young champion duelist come home to her own people’s ground
