Dougou Zettari

Dougou Zettari

Nov 28, 202610 min read

The Iron Bison · He Whose Stillness Is the Warning

As of sixteen years after the Division of the Worlds.

I. Identity

Full Name: Dougou Ozumu Zettari

Alias: The Iron Bison · Little Bison (Malakar’s name for him) · Baba (to his children)

Race / Lineage: Human · Zettari, the Path-Born Sovereigns of Aegor; royal line of Kushara · Vessel of the Titan Atla’zon, the World-Binder

Faction / Rank: Sovereign of Kushara. Every house of Kushara has sworn fealty to him.

Status: Alive

Family: Father Shaka-Al Zettari (dead; gone into the Paths). Wife Naevra Yukari, sister of Naiser Yukari. Five children: Ma’Kovu (15), Rashani (14), Rengai (12), Zuberi (10), Ayaka (8).

Heir: Zuberi, his fourth son, whom he calls Dawn Guard. The three elder brothers declined the heirship; Zuberi embraced it.

Current Location: The Sovereign House, Kushara Crownlands, rebuilt to his own design for his family

Origin: Born the vessel of Atla’zon, who bears the law of gravity and the burden of order. The Titan’s pressure has sat in his body since birth; the Iron Bison is the seal that lets a human frame hold it.

Catalyst Event: The Akar Road. He hung in Malakar’s chains and watched his father beaten to death, then was left alive on purpose. The Paths took him in while he was dying, healed him and sent him back.

II. Soul Architecture

Stat line: Stage VI Glory · Expert (Faceted), at the Division of the Worlds · Level not known · State: sealed by the Iron Bison · Class not known · Body primary, Fate secondary, Mastery not known · Lenses: home + 6, with Atla’zon’s Strain outside the Sixty

Temperance Stage: Stage VI · Glory · Expert, at the Division of the Worlds.

Level: not known

Path: Body, primary. Fate, secondary, as in all the royal Zettari

Crystal: Zettari three-layer Crystal; Faceted by its Stage at the Division; Mastery not known

Crystal State: Sealed by the Iron Bison. All seven seals burst at the Sevenfold Release; how far they have been rebuilt in sixteen years is not known.

Standing with the Paths: Path-Acknowledged. One of the very few Zettari to walk the Paths and return alive

III. Wellspring Harmonizations

Fixatio Primary. The seal.

Anima Spirare Engine. The harder the sealed body pulls, the more readily the soul provides.

Coagulatio Densifier. Compressed Essence laid down in layers.

Chthonica Column. Roots the axis crown to root.

Phreatis Circulation. All seven chambers breathing as one.

Monolithion Support. The monument body.

Terranova Support. Living foundation, recovery under load.

Atla’zon’s Strain The core. A Titan’s power, not one of the Sixty. Titanic compression and world-binding pressure seated in a human body; the reason the seal was necessary in the first place.

VI. Physical Force

Figures not known. Feats known: stopped a falling dome with his body in the plaza at Kushara; killed eleven hundred of fifteen hundred demonic N’Kharu on the Akar Road; ran across the sky to the Aerne mouth on claimed air; struck Malakar two hundred miles with one Black-Heart Bison Break; with the Three Halls lent to him, emptied all seven chambers through one fist and obliterated Malakar’s body. Sixteen years on: stands in a ring with one hand behind his back while his two eldest, both master swordsmen, fail to touch him.

VII. Aether Flow

A sealed vessel. External leak near nil by design, so Essence-readers take him for dormant or untrained. Internal cycling not known.

VIII. Traits

Vessel of Atla’zon He carries the World-Binder’s pressure-law in a human body.

Sealed-World Physiology Essence and feeling convert to stored weight instead of radiating.

True Everlasting Eyes Pale silver-gray eyes ringed in gold since the Paths. Reads the script of the world, the Wellspring a person draws on, and the threads between people. Read of Intent: feels the will to strike a breath before the strike. Limit: needs structure to read.

Fault-Sight (Brow Chamber) Reads strain, footing, breath and the weak seam in a body or a wall.

Load-Bearing Will Takes another’s Load onto his own body and carries it into the ground.

Sovereign Footing Ground he stands on is harder to take from him.

Wellspring Sovereignty Reads Wellsprings at a glance. Whether the Spring Arts have opened to him in sixteen years is not known.

IX. Domain

Not known. At the Division he had none, only its seed, a pressure the house calls the Ground That Confesses: near him movement grows expensive and weak structures show their strain.

X. Techniques

Elite master of the Zettari arts. Sixteen years of rule and practice have made him the standard his house measures itself against.

Matla (three of the Nine Practices at the Division). Form, the First: makes and changes matter. Anti-Form: the unbinding of substance. Dominion, the Fifth: Material Jurisdiction. Paid under the Law of Weight. Any Practice gained since is not known.

Njia ya Uzito, the Way of Weight. Mtiririko wa Kimya (the Silent Flow), the Breaking Palm, the Sarmad Fracture.

The Iron Bison (his version: the Iron Tree).

  • Nanatsu no Fūin · Sevenfold Seal. Closes the whole vessel. About fifteen in a hundred of full reserve. Cannot be held while a chamber vents.
  • The seven branches · Iron Fist, Kick, Elbow, Knee, Shoulder, Head, Step. Three to ten in a hundred each. Contact and a grounded stance.
  • Kokushin Gyūha · Black-Heart Bison Break. The apex: one sealed chamber into one point of contact. About eight in a hundred.
  • Ozumu no Katachi · Form of Accumulated Crush.
  • Juuretsu · Binding Weight.
  • Tetsu no Arashi · the Iron Bison’s Charge.
  • Kokushin Shissō · Black-Heart Stampede. Every chamber emptied into the legs at once.
  • Nanatsu no Kaihō · Sevenfold Release. All seven chambers into one point of contact. Done once, against Malakar, with the Three Halls in his spine.
  • Shinsei Tetsugyū · True Iron Bison, the apex state. Whether he has reached it is not known.

Borrowed, not his: Mitsu no Ma, the Three Halls, lent by Naiser Yukari and Muken Moto at the Division and returned after the blow.

XI. Spirit Axes

Naevra Yukari (wife) An alliance marriage neither chose, made real under the baobab. Carried north with the Inner World at the Division; with him in Kushara now, mother of his five children.

Ma’Kovu (son, 15) The eldest. Origin Eye, power over Names, master of the single katana. Favors his father.

Rashani (son, 14) Origin Eye, power over Origins, master of two katana. Favors his father.

Rengai (son, 12) His mother’s eyes and face, his father’s wrath in a fight. Training in every Zettari art. May join his elder brothers’ sessions at thirteen.

Zuberi (son, 10) Dawn Guard. His heir. The most Zettari of the children.

Ayaka (daughter, 8) The image of her mother. He spoils her. She wants to be a fighter.

Shaka-Al Zettari (father) Killed by Malakar on the Akar Road. His body turned to sand and went into the Paths.

Revari (kinsman) Last of the eastern branch line. Hanged on the gate of the Sovereign House.

Naiser Yukari (brother-in-law) Lent him the soul strand at the Division.

Muken Moto Dougou killed his father, Gimbzo Kōkan Moto, at seventeen. Muken swore to fight him when the snow went, no armies. What became of that oath in sixteen years is not known.

Malakar The enemy. Obliterated at the Division and stopped, not dead. Kushara was retaken from his forces.

Atla’zon The Titan whose burden he carries.

Theluji, Seraph Their standing sixteen years on is not known.

The Paths Healed him, opened his eyes, trained him in Dominion.

XII. Resistances

Structural / Impact: Very high while sealed.

Reading and detection: Very high; a sealed body emits nothing.

Root disruption: Low. Lost footing or a blow through an open Root drains the column into the ground.

Void and anti-recognition fields: Low.

Contamination: Low.

Emotional breach: Lowest. Love, grief and kinship flood the Heart chamber, and he now has a wife and five children.

Known Counters: Take the ground away, strike the open vent, contaminate the loop, or reach him.

XIII. Physical Description

Height / Build: Six foot nine. Monolithic, load-bearing.

Skin: Dark brown. Eyes: Pale silver-gray, pupils ringed in gold. Hair: Long, heavy black locs with gold cuffs, gray now at the temples.

Scars: Slash scars across the chest; a round scar at the center of the chest from Malakar, closed by the Paths. An old break in the jaw that clicks when he smiles.

Dress for war: An open black-and-gold royal warrior’s robe, bare chest beneath.

The House-Bound Dress: A blend of Zettari and Yukari clothing. Long dark haori-style coat worn open; wide hakama-style trousers and plain cloth shoes (Yukari). Black and gold (Zettari). High collar closed with Yukari bond-knots in Zettari gold thread. A thick braided rope belt with two tassels: his Cord of Becoming braided the Yukari way from a gold strand and a silver-white one, knotted for what he has paid, with five bright beads for his five children. Fighting wraps at the wrists.

XIV. Psychology

Core Wound: He was a sealed vessel before he was old enough to choose to be one. Then he watched his father die while he hung in chains, and was left alive on purpose.

Flaw: Turns every feeling into stored weight and calls it mastery.

Desire: To keep what he rebuilt: his house, his family and Kushara. To be ready the day Malakar comes back.

Strength: Patience, doctrine over impulse, violence that accumulates. Carries other people’s weight unasked.

As a father: Strict about the rules of the yard, never raises his voice, and is soft on his daughter. Six people get past the seal now.

Foils: Muken Moto; Malakar.

Voice: Quiet, severe, patient. Few words, plain ones. “Death will have me when it earns me.” To his sons: “Language.” “Again.”

XV. Equipment

None. His body is the only implement he trusts. He wears his Cord of Becoming as a belt.

XVI. Temperance Record

I–IV: not itemized.

V · Splintering: held through the war and the fall of Kushara.

VI · Glory: crossed on the Akar Road, mid-battle.

Since: not known.

XVII. Fracture Log

  • Birth. Seeded as Atla’zon’s vessel.
  • The war. Kills Gimbzo Kōkan Moto at seventeen.
  • The Akar Road. Kills eleven hundred demonic N’Kharu; Malakar chains him, kills his father and blows a hole through his chest.
  • The Paths. Healed; True Everlasting Eyes; two months of Dominion.
  • The burial. Kills Tariq-Nal, the Sin of Pride.
  • The Trident. Saves Naevra from Malakar’s beam.
  • The Division of the Worlds. His fist meets Malakar’s above the Aerne and the one land breaks into four. With the Three Halls he obliterates Malakar, who is stopped and not dead.
  • The sixteen years. Takes the Kushara Crownlands back from Malakar’s forces. Makes every house swear fealty and becomes Sovereign. Rebuilds the Zettari homeland to his own design. Has five children with Naevra and names Zuberi his heir.
