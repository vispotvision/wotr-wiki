Ma'Kovu Zettari

Ma'Kovu Zettari

Nov 28, 20269 min read

The Namecutter · Eldest son of the Sovereign of Kushara · Bearer of Sujimon

I. Identity

Full Name: Ma’Kovu Neno Zettari (Neno, Swahili “word,” given by his mother)

Called: the Namecutter (Class) · loose cannon (Rashani) · jack of all trades (Rengai)

Age: 15

Race / Lineage: Human · Zettari and Yukari, a perfect blend of both houses

Faction: House Zettari, Kushara. Eldest child of Sovereign Dougou Ozumu Zettari and Naevra Yukari. Declined the heirship (now Zuberi’s).

Status: Alive

Family: Brothers Rashani (14), Rengai (12), Zuberi (10, heir); sister Ayaka (8). Uncle Naiser Yukari.

Current Location: Kharven-Seat, Inner World, with his family’s embassy to the Moto

Catalyst Event: Declining the heirship of Kushara, the decision that carried him across to Stage VI, Glory, lived.

II. Soul Architecture

Stat line: Stage VI Glory · Expert (Faceted) · Level 160 · Refined · Namecutter · Body Mastered, I · Fate Walked, unread · Lenses: home + 3

Level: 160; it holds Glory (115), and no Residual Strain builds in him short of 200

Temperance Stage: VI · Glory · Expert · stat ceiling 400

Soul Crystal: Faceted

Path: Body, leading; Fate, second (its gates open a Stage late)

Mastery: Body I, Muridic · Fate unread, Walked a rung behind a lead of I; a grip by blood (his power over Names)

Class: Namecutter; it evolves as his Body Mastery rises

Reserve: about 295,000 EU at full (log₁₀ EU = 3.4202 + 0.012812 × 160)

Releases: none found yet

III. Wellspring Harmonizations

Fixatio (Medium) · names he carves set and stay set.

Anamnesis (Medium) · reads and calls up a thing’s older names.

Judicium (Wild) · truth; reads weakness like text.

Oblivara (Fate) · his Fate lens and the reach of his erasures.

VI. Physical Force

Read off the numbers: Finesse and Penetration at 300, the rest of his output two steps or more below them on the hidden scale. A sword master at fifteen; neither he nor Rashani has ever landed a blow on their father.

VII. Aether Flow

Body-led: raw Aether packed in the Shell, Body I. His name-work is Fate: pulled through the Attraction Layer and paid from his own share. Gives off nothing a reader can feel when he chooses, the first thing his father taught all his sons.

VIII. Traits

Every trait feeds one of his Name Arts, Sujimon, the Origin Eye or the sword, and every Trait reads at his lead Mastery, Body I.

Jina la Kweli · The True Name Feeds Kusoma Majina, Pigo la Mwisho and every name-cut. Reads the true name of what he looks at, at a glance. A name he has read whole counts as the precise knowledge his deeper arts demand, so Name-Cutting, Erasure and the Last Blow go in without a guess. Line of sight; hidden, warded and Archon-scale names blur; the nameless show nothing.

Upanga Ni Mimi · The Blade Is Me Feeds every art of Sujimon and the Unseen Wave. A name-cut made through the blade costs him less than the same cut made bare; the Unseen Wave holds its edge further out; the blade cannot be named away from him. If Sujimon’s name is ever cut, he feels it as a wound.

Jicho la Jina la Kwanza · The Eye of the First Name Feeds the Origin Eye, Echo and Renaming. Reads the first name a thing was given in one breath, the name Echo calls up and Renaming has to write over; a short look no longer costs him blood. A long or deep look still does.

Ulimi Mkali · The Sharp Tongue Feeds Kutaja and Kufunga kwa Jina. Names he speaks aloud carry Command Weight: the Calling holds for a second breath, Binding by Name lasts longer, and a barb aimed at a man’s name lands where he means it. Backfires on the proud, and does not know when to stop.

Jina Lisilovunjika · The Unbroken Name Feeds Jina la Kuazima and Upanga Ni Mimi. He knows his own name to the bottom: he can wear a Borrowed Name far longer before he starts losing his own, and name-workings, glamours and possessions turned on him slide off more often than not. Guards his name, not his body.

Origin Eye (Iso Lomsuka) Born with it. Shows him the first name a thing was given, the name still holding it under every later one.

IX. Domain

None. A first flicker is possible at Stage VII, a Domain from Stage VIII. Sujimon’s Name Domain is its ceiling.

X. Techniques

The Name Arts (without the sword).

  • Kusoma Majina · Reading Names · a name’s full shape at rest: what it holds, what holds it, its first name.
  • Kutaja · The Calling · speaks a thing by its true name so it must answer for one breath (two with Ulimi Mkali).
  • Kufunga kwa Jina · Binding by Name · a name with a command holds a thing fast for a few heartbeats; less against the stronger.
  • Kuachilia · The Release · speaks a person’s true name to loosen what binds them; loosens, does not cut.
  • Jina la Kuazima · The Borrowed Name · wears a name (Swift, Iron, Unseen) for a while; held too long, he forgets his own. Limits: he must know the name; the nameless are beyond him; every art is paid from his own share; turning it on himself is how a name-worker forgets the real one.

Swordsmanship and Sujimon’s arts: see XV.

XI. Spirit Axes

Dougou (father, teacher) He favors him; has never touched him in the yard.

Naevra (mother) The one he fears most; gave him his middle name.

Rashani Always at his side; promised to marry the Moto girl for him, then meant it.

Rengai Calls him a jack of all trades.

Zuberi Picked up the heirship he set down.

Ayaka Stole his lemon tart from the air.

Naiser Yukari Uncle; Ma’Kovu means to go where Rashani’s training goes.

Sonzai Moto Rival. Future Commander? · blood boy. Ma’Kovu wants to fight him.

Sodoku Moto The heir who did not bite at the gate.

Mizuyi Moto Promised to Rashani; taken south by Cozbi Mahuo.

Cozbi Mahuo Said he thought about Ma’Kovu first.

XII. Resistances

Identity and glamour effects: good (Jina Lisilovunjika). Name-workings against him: resisted by his own name’s depth. Pain: fair (Threshold). Thin where a boy who has never been badly beaten is thin: Tempering, Harmonics and Resilience. Known tell: he looks where he is going to cut. Known flaw: he does not know when to stop.

XIII. Physical Description

Six foot five, long in the arm and leg, lean and quick. Light brown skin, silver eyes, neck-length black hair streaked white that the wind keeps undoing. His father’s face on a longer frame, and a mouth half a step from a grin. Left hand always resting on Sujimon’s guard. Abroad he wears the royal dress of Kushara: a long black coat worked in gold thread, gold at collar and wrist, and a Cord of Becoming with few knots yet.

XIV. Psychology

Proud, loud, quick and funny; a slick mouth and a slick way with words. Tests people with small baits and calls it testing the waters. Wants the Iron Bison, one blow on his father, and a fight with Sonzai Moto. Fiercely loyal: first back up the stair when the harbor went up, first into a doorway for his brother. When he means it, his father’s flat low voice comes up under the slick one. “The blade is me. Wherever I go, it goes.”

XV. Equipment

Sujimon · the Absolute Edge, the Blade of Names

A Yukari-forged katana of plain gray steel with a pale temper line, bound to his power over Names.

Governing rule: Sujimon answers only to its wielder; its edge has no limit of its own, but reaches exactly as far as his Stage and his numbers. Every name-cut is a working paid from his reserve.

Properties: The Absolute Edge (cuts materials, constructs, wards; can cut a defense’s name; his Penetration at 300) · The Unending Edge (never dulls, chips or wears).

Name arts of the blade: Name-Cutting · Name Carving · Erasure · Name Fusion (most likely to backlash) · Resonance · Echo · Severing Destiny (among his costliest) · Renaming (not resurrection) · Endless Flow (with the Origin Eye; temporary; permanent Wellspring shut-off follows the Spring Arts, Stage VIII) · The Unseen Wave (range his Stage’s).

The four strikes: Kupasua Pazia, Tearing the Veil (a crack, not a door, at his Stage) · Kata Kisichoonekana, Cut the Unseen (bonds cut can be retied) · Pigo la Mwisho, The Last Blow (needs the true name; at or below his Stage and his numbers; fails on hidden names and the nameless) · Pigo Lisilo na Mipaka, The Blow Without Borders (empties him).

Name Domain: every name visible and rewritable without a stroke; from Stage VIII, not yet his.

Limits: answers to its wielder; needs the name; every cut paid; protected and nameless resist; it does not choose its target; backlash on wrong names or bad fusions; paradox leaves it nothing to grip.

XVI. Temperance Record

I–V: lived, not itemized. VI · Glory: lived, on the decision to decline the heirship of Kushara.

XVII. Fracture Log

  • Born with the Origin Eye; named Neno.
  • Declines the heirship; crosses to Glory.
  • Trains with Rashani against his father under the baobab.
  • Sails north on the first ship across the seas of the Division.
  • At the gate of Kharven-Seat, reads Sonzai’s slipped hitch and mocks him to his back.
  • Back up the stair as the harbor burns; meets Sonzai at the great doors.
  • Declares he will go south; ordered silent by his father. Owed a talk with his mother.
