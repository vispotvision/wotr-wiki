Rashani Zettari

Rashani Zettari

Nov 28, 202610 min read

The Rootreader · Second son of the Sovereign of Kushara

I. Identity

Full Name: Rashani Asili Zettari (Asili, Swahili “origin,” chosen by his father)

Called: the Rootreader (Class) · nephew (Naiser)

Age: 14

Race / Lineage: Human · Zettari and Yukari, a perfect blend of both houses

Faction: House Zettari, Kushara. Second child of Sovereign Dougou Ozumu Zettari and Naevra Yukari. Declined the heirship (now Zuberi’s).

Status: Alive

Family: Brothers Ma’Kovu (15), Rengai (12), Zuberi (10, heir); sister Ayaka (8). Uncle and teacher Naiser Yukari.

Promised to: Mizuyi Moto, taken south by Cozbi Mahuo.

Current Location: In training under Naiser Yukari’s household, with Hiromi Mahuo as his yard teacher alongside Sodoku Moto

Catalyst Event: At sea, three weeks out of Kushara, he said I’ll marry her about the Moto girl and meant it. That decision carried him across to Stage VI, Glory, lived.

II. Soul Architecture

Stat line: Stage VI Glory · Expert (Faceted) · Level 155 · Refined · Rootreader · Spirit Mastered, I · Body Walked, unread · Lenses: home + 2

Level: 155; it holds Glory (115), and no Residual Strain builds in him short of 200

Temperance Stage: VI · Glory · Expert · stat ceiling 400

Soul Crystal: Faceted

Path: Spirit, leading; Body, second (its gates open a Stage late)

Mastery: Spirit I, Listener · Body unread, Walked a rung behind a lead of I · Fate none: he walks no Fate road; he sees threads, does not pull them

Class: Rootreader; it evolves as his Spirit Mastery rises

Reserve: about 255,000 EU at full (log₁₀ EU = 3.4202 + 0.012812 × 155)

Releases: none found yet

III. Wellspring Harmonizations

Anamnesis (Medium) · everything keeps the record of its own making, and he reads it.

Rebirthine (Wild) · destruction turned into rebuilding; a new beginning out of an old ending.

VI. Physical Force

Read off the numbers: Perception at 280, his blade Sub-Stats at 200 to 240, most of his output two steps or more below Perception on the hidden scale. A master of two swords at fourteen; neither he nor Ma’Kovu has ever landed a blow on their father.

VII. Aether Flow

Spirit-led: Aether refined in the Crystal into Essence and spent through the Core. Gives off nothing a reader can feel, and neither do his arts (Mtupu).

VIII. Traits

Every trait feeds one of his arts, his eyes or his blades, and every Trait reads at his lead Mastery, Spirit I.

Kuona Nyuzi · Seeing the Threads Feeds Kuona Asili and Kukata Mzizi. Sees every bond as his mother’s people do, the Yukari half of his blood showing early. He can follow any thread he has seen to its far end, and aim his Origin Arts along it: find where a working began with its caster out of sight, or cut a hold at its root from across a hall. Sees, does not pull; aiming along a thread costs more the longer it is.

Jicho la Mwanzo · The Eye of the First Moment Feeds the Origin Eye and Mwanzo wa Pigo. Reads where a strike begins in the space of one breath; a short look no longer costs him blood. A long or deep look still does; a blow with no single beginning gives it nothing.

Mizizi Miwili · Two Roots Feeds the two blades and Mkasi. Each katana can carry a different Origin Art in the same exchange: the left cuts a working from its root while the right sets a grip back to its beginning. Two arts at once cost both at once; held past a few exchanges it empties him fast.

Kumbukumbu ya Mwanzo · Memory of the Beginning Feeds Mkono wa Mwanzo and Kubadili Mwanzo. What he sets back stays set longer; the holder must work to take hold again. An origin he has read fully once, he keeps, and touching it again costs less Essence. Keeps only what he has read whole.

Mtupu · The Empty One Feeds every Origin Art and the two blades. Gives off no heat, pressure or Aether, and neither do his arts: a working he touches does not flare, so its maker does not feel it until it is done, and his sword tells never show in his aura. Under real strain the emptiness slips and everything shows at once.

Origin Eye (Iso Lomsuka) Born with it. Reads the first moment of a thing: the stair where a choice was made, the hand that tied a knot.

IX. Domain

None. A first flicker is possible at Stage VII, a Domain from Stage VIII.

X. Techniques

The Origin Arts (Spirit, through the Core, paid in Essence; every one needs precise knowledge of the beginning touched):

  • Kuona Asili · Seeing the Source · traces anything to where it began: a working to its caster, a thread to its far end, a made thing to the mind that made it.
  • Mkono wa Mwanzo · The Hand of the Beginning · sets a thing back where it began; a grip becomes the resting hand it started as. Does not tear: the holder can take hold again. Done on the possessed maid’s hand at Kharven-Seat.
  • Kukata Mzizi · Cutting the Root · severs a working from its source for a moment; the root grows back, faster the stronger the source.
  • Kubadili Mwanzo · Turning the Beginning · changes where a small, recent thing he knows exactly began, and so what it is. A cut, not a life, at his Stage; costs far more than the thing changed.
  • Asili Yangu · My Own Source · returns himself to his own beginning to shrug off a curse, possession or borrowed hand. Too far back and he loses what he has grown into, his vow included. Limits: precise knowledge; what never began, or has stepped outside its own making, cannot be touched; reach is his Stage’s; it loosens, it does not break.

The two blades:

  • Mkasi · The Shears · two blades closing from opposite heights, ribs and back of the knee.
  • Ukingo · The Rim · running the ring’s edge to come in behind the off hand.
  • Mwanzo wa Pigo · The Root of the Blow · with the Origin Eye, cuts at the moment an opponent’s strike begins. Fails against a fighter with no single commitment, or chaos with every beginning at once.

XI. Spirit Axes

Ma’Kovu Always beside him; Rashani is the hand on his shoulder.

Dougou (father) Turned a look of pride on him the night of the vow.

Naevra (mother) He has her sight without her eye.

Rengai Held by the collar when he wants into fights.

Zuberi Caught Rashani’s tell from the veranda.

Ayaka Gets his warmest smile.

Naiser Yukari Uncle and now teacher.

Mizuyi Moto Promised on paper; he held her hand while the dark took her. He means to ask her a third time.

Sodoku Moto Seen and told so, kindly; apologized for his brother and thanked him for her hand. Now his yard partner.

Sonzai Moto Not wrong, only early. Rashani has promised to tell him every morning that Mizuyi’s thread still holds.

Hiromi Mahuo His yard teacher. Rashani fixed his collar in the carriage, and teases him about Senna.

Cozbi Mahuo Predicted him: a good man always goes to the girl.

XII. Resistances

Identity attacks: fair (Integrity, Ward). Being read: hard; he and his arts give off nothing. Pain: fair. Thin in Tempering and Dominion. Known tell: a breath drawn before the left sword moves. Known flaw: he trusts his first good reading of a thing.

XIII. Physical Description

Six foot three, a hand shorter than Ma’Kovu, lean and light on his feet. Light brown skin, jet-black eyes that go deeper when he reads an origin, black hair streaked white reaching his back, tied back to fight. His father’s face and jaw. A smile that arrives before he has decided to let it. Abroad he wears the royal dress of Kushara: a long black coat worked in gold thread, gold at collar and wrist, and a Cord of Becoming with few knots yet.

XIV. Psychology

Sly and kind at once. Watches; takes the long way round a house before he sits down in it. Picky, by his father’s word; Mizuyi was to his taste. Goes to the person in distress, quietly, without being asked. His voice goes level and quiet when it matters, and carries further than shouting.

Vow (entered in Naiser Yukari’s truth-smoke, binding): “I am going to bring Mizuyi home.”

Answer (entered in the smoke on the road to the Enclave): what he means to pick up in place of Kushara is freedom from his own beginning: to choose where he starts, and to begin again when he must, and the same for others.

XV. Equipment

Two Yukari katana of plain gray steel, named the morning he first drew them against Hiromi Mahuo:

  • Enishi (縁) · the left blade. Japanese, the bond that ties two lives together whether either chose it. For his mother, who sees every thread in a room before she sits down.
  • Mbuyu · the right blade. Swahili, the baobab tree. For his father, and the old tree in the yard at home where Dougou trained his sons every morning. At a host’s table he leaves them in his room.

XVI. Temperance Record

I–V: lived, not itemized. VI · Glory: lived, at sea, on the decision to marry the Moto girl.

XVII. Fracture Log

  • Born with the Origin Eye and the thread-sight; named Asili.
  • Declines the heirship.
  • At sea: I’ll marry her. Crosses to Glory.
  • At the gate of Kharven-Seat, sees Sodoku’s maneuver and tells him so.
  • Follows a distressed thread into the great hall; frees Mizuyi’s arm from the possessed maid’s grip.
  • The ribbon takes her anyway; he gives her cut hair to the queen and tells the king she is alive.
  • Vows in the smoke to bring her home; accepts Naiser’s teaching.
  • On the road south, answers Naiser in the smoke: freedom from his own beginning.
  • Names his blades Enishi and Mbuyu and draws them against Hiromi Mahuo.
