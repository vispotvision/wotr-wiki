Larkin Gedgesellsword of the Short Measure Company, a twin (the green hat) · Human, Kingsbridge · Accord, Midmarch · as of the Hem road, Saturday, May 22, 715 IC
Epithetthe Green Hat · half of the Gedge Boys, which is what the twins call themselves in every song they make up; Kit Quarrel reads the pair of them aloud as the Bell-twins. Larkin would rather be the Wolf-Halver of the Hem, and nobody has said it yet but him
ConceptA loud twin who starts every boast and makes it bigger, and fights back to back with his brother as if they shared one saw.
Stat lineStage III Ascension · Journeyman (Clouded)
Level not known, nor the Crystal’s State · unclassed until Glory
Path not known · Lenses: home
Journeyman’s fighting token (numeral three) · yield (η) about 0.40, the Stage’s own figure
Combat grammarBlade, paired: two edges crossing in one opening from either side
Aura and soundcolor: a warm red-gold like a loaf crust when the oven opens, the same as his brother’s, round arms, shoulders and sword · Wellspring: Benediction (Kit: “one spring between them, the blessing kind, that keeps no count”) · between him and Parkin a red-gold thread with a soft low chord under it and a click of binding · smell: new bread and church incense · impact sounds of the pair: tinng, zhakk
How he movesback to back with Parkin, cutting blind because he feels his brother’s edge through the thread; his own finish steps in on the left foot, turns the wrist over and drives the point down under the ear at the base of the skull
Stacksustainable 175, the Stage III roof · cap two steps on the hidden scale over the sustainable figure · layers: the Resonant Pair’s thread (outside help from his pair)
Kita green hat that cost more than the cord, and a plain Common hanger with its grip wound new in green cord to match it
Voice”That’s two wolves’ worth, I’m counting it twice!” · speaker type: a Kingsbridge sellsword and teller of tall tales who wants a song

XII. Physical Description

Feature

Build

Description

The twins are the same man twice: a long-jawed, loose-limbed young Kingsbridge man with a big nose and a big laugh, told apart only by their hats; Larkin’s is green.

XIII. Psychology

FieldValue
Speaker typea sellsword boaster
Voicestarts every boast and inflates it, roared as he cuts (“Twenty wolves came down on the Short Measure’s baggage… every one of them the size of an ox”, “the finest captain in the whole wide world and the Hem besides”, “a hundred wolves”, “the biggest wolf in the Hem”); adds the numbers up; tells the ginger-oatcake joke on his brother; glosses the Stages with pride (“Flourishing… The fourth Stage, chapman.”)
