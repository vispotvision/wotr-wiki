VIII. Traits

Every Trait reads at her lead Mastery, Spirit III.

TSUKUMO (付喪 · 九十九), the Spirit in Things · Primary Trait

TSUKUMO is basically Spiritual Infusion: the power to wake the light in things and to take it apart. In the old tales a tool that serves a hundred years wakes up with a spirit of its own. Senna does not wait the hundred years. She sees the innate light in everything, made or living, the way her father hears its hum, and she can read in it what a thing is made of, how strong it is, and where it is flawed. She can pour her own spirit into an object and wake its light, so a kunai cuts a working or strikes a soul, and a rope or a door holds against things it never could before. She can draw a working’s light into her Core and decompile it, taking it apart into its pieces until she knows how it was built. Once it is apart she can borrow one piece and set it into her own things for a little while, or play the whole light back reversed, as her null-light, so it lies down against the original and cancels it. She can only cancel what she has fully read, and on plain matter a kunai is a kunai. The null-light takes light from everything near her, her own fingers first, and every working she decompiles leaves its light sitting in her Core. Take in too much and her own color starts to go.

ISHI-KAZOE (石数え), the Stone Count · Secondary Trait

ISHI-KAZOE is basically Presence Concealment held by a count. The Yukari count is an old family art, a way of measuring a breath and a heartbeat to the beat. Senna’s is her own version, built for one blood, and she counts down. Breath by breath and beat by beat she slows herself, and as she slows, her own light dims, until a reader looking straight at her sees a stone where a girl should be. While she is counted down no one can read her, track her by her light, or feel her coming, and anything that answers to motion or pressure, like the Sin’s glass that stiffens against whatever strikes it fast, lets her walk through because she is too slow to wake it. She reads every light in the room and gives back none of her own. The price is all her speed. Counted down she moves like someone wading, her lips go pale and cold, and gray creeps in at the edges of her sight. Make her hurry and the count breaks all at once, every bit of her light coming back in a single beat, where anyone watching can see it.

The house instrument

Null-Light, Ashura’s own, climbs on her Spirit Mastery. Its price is the Long Look: what she has held she keeps knowing for life, and when she looks away everything held slips at once. Which Regard she holds is not known. The canceling light she threw on the Kessen road is TSUKUMO’s.

X. Techniques

KARASU (烏), the Crow · Signature Technique

Her aunt Naevra’s kunai are plain folded steel with cedar handles, a ring at the pommel and a crow punched into each blade, and they carry no working at all. Senna wakes one. She pours TSUKUMO into the steel until the crow stamped on it opens its eyes, and it comes off the metal as a living spirit of black light, a crow the size of a hawk with her violet in the sheen of its wings. It flies at her word and sees what she would see. It carries her reading and her null-light anywhere she sends it, so the Yukari, called the crows of the threshold, fight again with the family’s own bird, woken out of a blade from the family’s own forge. When she calls it home it folds back into the steel, and the kunai drops into her hand still warm. The crow is her light, so whatever it suffers she feels, and a wing broken in the field is an arm gone numb at her side. It cannot fly while ISHI-KAZOE has her counted down; she chooses, every time, between hiding and the bird.

  • MIHARI (見張り), the Lookout · The crow scouts ahead and reads for her at a distance. Whatever it sees, she can read with TSUKUMO as if she stood there.
  • TSUIBAMU (啄む), the Peck · The crow strikes a working and tears out a beakful of its light, then carries it home to her Core to be decompiled.
  • KAZAKIRI (風切り), the Wind-Cutter · The crow folds its wings and dives as a streak of null-light, canceling a working she has already read along its whole path.
  • KAERI (帰り), the Return · Any kunai of hers in the crow’s reach comes home: the crow catches it in flight and turns it back to her hand.

The null-light

Her canceling light, played back reversed down a spinning kunai. First landed on the Kessen road after a year of failing at it every evening.

XII. Resistances

  • Gnosis 1,584, Perception 286, Ward 187: she reads before she is read, and reads are hard to land on her.
  • Counted down: while ISHI-KAZOE holds she cannot be read or tracked by her light.
  • Hurry: make her rush and both traits break, since TSUKUMO needs the time to read and the count needs her slow.
  • Unmatched workings: a working she cannot read whole has nothing for her null-light to match.
  • The crow: what hurts it hurts her.
  • Body: Vitality 441. The house is lean and breakable, and she is of the house.

XIII. Physical Description

Hair: Violet, worn long in a braid and tied back hard at the nape with a gray cord.

Eyes: Gray.

Bearing: Sits straight with her hands folded. The Yukari composure was a coat cut too big for her as a child, and she meant to grow into it.

Carries: Two kunai on rings, and the habit of catching one on her finger and letting it spin twice before it stops.

XIV. Psychology

How she moves: Slow when she is reading and very fast when she has finished. Counting under her breath is the tell.

Voice: Quiet and exact, her father’s habit of putting a sentence away where she can find it again.

Underneath: She refuses a yawn in company and swallows it whole. She practiced a thing every evening for a year and failed every evening, and when it finally came on the Kessen road the composure fell off her for one instant, and what was under it was a girl who knew what she had done.
