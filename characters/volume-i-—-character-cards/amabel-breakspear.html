Amabel Breakspear

Amabel Breakspear

Nov 28, 20269 min read

Amabel Breakspearcaptain of the Short Measure Company, a free company of forty lances
Human, a Kingsbridge cooper’s daughter
Accord, Midmarch
as of the fire on the Hem road, Saturday, May 22, 715 IC
Epithetthe Long Tab, sung by Larkin Gedge in the fight; owed by eleven kings, by Parkin’s count
ConceptA big laughing axe-captain who puts every swing she doesn’t land on her axe’s tab, then pays the whole bill in one cleave.
Stat lineStage IV Flourishing · Adept (Clouded); Larkin’s word: “the fourth Stage, chapman”
Level not known · State not known · unclassed until Glory
Body IV Mastered, unread below Glory · Lenses: home (Tarturon) + 0; yield (η) about 0.5 on Tarturon: half her pull burns in her Shell’s furnace and comes off her as heat
Combat grammarPercussion: she banks the work of the fight on the haft and pays it out at once when the account is full
Aura and soundcolor: a hot fox-orange, red at the edges and pale gold at the heart, smoke still fraying at the edges but clearer than Kit’s, extended into Arrears from shoe to bit
Wellspring: Tarturon, reckoning-fire, a Fate Wellspring that burns on what is owed, off its home on a Body fighter and so dearer: “not an axe-woman’s spring by nature, so it charges her double” (Kit)
smell: smith’s scale and burned salt
feel: dry heat with no fire
signature sounds: ssstik (a swing banked), PAKKOOM (the bill paid)
Pressureshe holds most of her crown in near her own levies; its edge is heat in an unwoken man’s teeth at a few yards
How she movesa long easy walker’s stride, coming down the road at the pace of a cart that has decided where it is going; the long axe worked down a line of carts in full swings, banked or finished; between banked swings she finishes with the spiked shoe (a jab below the eye; a straight drive into the chest) and the bit (a short chop), and takes jaws on the pauldron
Stacksustainable to the Stage IV roof · cap two steps on the hidden scale over the sustainable figure · layers in play: the banked tab on the haft (nine is full)
Releasesnone; she has not called one
Bonded weaponnone; ARREARS carries the tab because her colors are run out into it from shoe to bit
Traitsnone; the whole kit is one art, THE LONG TAB
Kitflaked half-plate in the company’s black-and-yellow checks over another company’s red, a yellow sash stuffed with the company’s debts, and the piece people talk about first: ARREARS, the long axe across her back, Uncommon by her own mouth, nine scorched tally bars down its haft
Voice”Nine, chapman! Nine on the haft and she’s full! It’s called a TAB!“
speaker type: a free-company captain with a battlefield voice that was never told to come down

THE LONG TABSignature art——Path Body · Lens Tarturon, a Fate Wellspring worked away from its home · Form Strikenone writtenEvery swing she doesn’t land goes on her axe’s tab, and when the tab is full she pays the whole bill in one cleave.Nine on the haft is full (her brag); ten and the haft burns, and her with it (Kit).———Stage: IV · yield (η): about 0.5 on Tarturon, half of every pull burned in her Shell’s furnace as heat · RUNNING TAB!: one bar a banked swing, each stopped a forearm short · nine bars: full, the ninth being the held windup · ten: the haft burns, and her with it · SETTLE UP!: pays all nine at once: one alpha dog halved neck to belly, the third cart’s near thill split, the road cracked a yard each way · Sound: a short hiss as the swing stops dead
RUNNING TAB!Signature Technique; Move of THE LONG TAB————A full swing stopped dead a forearm short of the target, and a bar of fox-orange light burns across the ash where the swing went: one more on the tab, up to nine.—A full swing stopped dead a forearm short, a notch of fox-orange light burning across the ash.Every bar is an enemy left standing; while banking she is slower on every turn, forearms shaking, colors reddening and drawing in, face scarlet.Every banked swing is a wolf not stopped (a blow not landed): don’t wait for the bill, press her while the bars are lit and she is slow.Sound: a short hiss as the swing stops dead
SETTLE UP!Signature Technique; Move of THE LONG TAB————The axe drawn back over the right shoulder, the ninth bar lighting in the windup, and every banked swing let go in one stroke that goes through the wolf, the cart shaft under it and the road.—The axe drawn back over the right shoulder, hips wound, the ninth notch the held windup.Paying the bill empties her: after, her colors gone out, sick in the road, forearms blistered red from wrist to elbow, hands shaking too much to write without steadying, face gray-white with red spots; the haft, wrapped in a wet cloth, still steams.Come straight into the ninth windup: it opens her from chin to knees and she cannot move until the swing is spent; slow to wind (Ned’s read).Sound: a long hiss, then one cracking blast
