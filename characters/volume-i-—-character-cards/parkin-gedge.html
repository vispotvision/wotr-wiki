HALF AND HALFMove of HALF AND HALF————Back to back in the ditch with the red-gold thread pulled bright as wire between their shoulders, Larkin cuts high at the head and Parkin, turning behind him blind, cuts low under the chest of the same wolf in the same instant, and it comes apart in two pieces that go on kicking. Or Larkin goes in at the head to make it look and Parkin, behind it, brings one short cut down across the back of the neck to the spine. Parkin counts it as one wolf either way. Larkin shouts it as HALVES! and is told that Halves sounds like a pie.—————
