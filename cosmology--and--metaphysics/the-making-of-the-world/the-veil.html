The Veil

The Veil

Nov 28, 202620 min read

The Veil is a condition with no address: the state of being between planes, the moment of transition where the ordinary separation between matter, soul, and fate becomes navigable.

When the grain factor Bardolph Mowle of Salt Cross buried his wife, he hired a Veil-thread singer named Merrin Two-Bodies to fetch him her last word, and he held the wake on the green above the Salt Cross plague pits, where three thousand had gone into the lime in a single summer and the ground ran Thin (a place where the fold between the Physical and the Soul Plane wears close). He paid for a striped pavilion, forty musicians and a table of roast swans with their feathers put back on, and he invited the county, because he wanted it witnessed. Merrin sat down in a gilded chair with a cup in her hand, sang her thread until her coat ran black-violet at the edges, and crossed. Her body stayed behind in the chair, upright and smiling slightly, while the band played and the swans went round. A crossing runs under a minute at the threshold’s edge, and she was gone for four. At the fifth, Mowle’s nephew, who was young and well into the wine, took her by the shoulder and shook her to see if she was shamming.

A crossing cannot be aborted fast. She came back too hard and too shallow, and something came with her: a split in the air above the swan table, tall as a barn door, deep violet along its lips, with a color at its center on which no two witnesses have ever agreed. That was a rupture, a channel struck clean through the Veil, and it stayed struck. Out of it, down onto the good linen, condensed the unresolved content of three thousand plague dead, in the shape of Echo Beasts (what crosses a rupture and sets on this side like a precipitate), each with the character of what formed it and none of its mind, big as carts and bent double, wet in the lungs, and very frightened. The nephew died first, and a third of the band. Four Concordia practitioners rode in from the Guild hall at Ambrel through the night in their sea-blue cloaks, and they sang in relays for three days to re-insulate the channel, and two of them went home to bed for a month. Merrin lived, with more Soul Drift counted against her in one afternoon than most of her trade collect in a career. She went back in the next spring, on the advice of a school I will come to below, and Mowle never did get the word. Measurewrights have argued for sixty years over whether the shake struck the channel or the pits were going regardless, and the argument has the great merit of being unwinnable.

In the bracket towns a crossing is called stepping out, a Thick zone is a wall, and someone who has done too much of it has gone thin. A Guild roll says what follows, and every clause of it is in that afternoon.

What the Veil Is

Everyone calls it a wall, and everyone is thinking of the part they have bumped into. The Veil is the skin of the lower realities. The Physical Plane and the Soul Plane both lie inside it, and every realm anyone has ever named is a region inside it. It has no address because there is nowhere it is not: it is everywhere, and thinner in some places.

Picture a wineskin with a pleat stitched down the middle, so that one skin makes two chambers. Wine in either chamber is still inside the skin, and a person can press a finger against the pleat from one side and feel a good deal of what is on the other. That gives the two surfaces that matter.

  • The inner fold is the pleat, running between the Physical and the Soul Plane. It is what a crossing crosses, it is thick or thin by the place, and everything below about Thin zones, ruptures and Drift (the loosening a soul takes from each crossing) is an account of this one surface.
  • The outer face is the skin itself, where the lower realities meet Fate. Nothing is on the far side of it but what both sides depend on, which is why nobody has mapped it and why “beyond the Veil” means something quite different in the mouth of a thread singer and a Moto. A thread pulled down from Fate comes in through the outer face, and the old books call that surface the Astral Fold.

The Plane of Fate is therefore outside the skin, if it is anywhere, and the Measurewrights who say “perhaps” at this point have earned it. The Titans bear what is inside and carry a law out to the edge of it, and a Wellspring whose kind is Medium is one at home on the skin itself, which is why it is at home on all three.

Origin

Origin · Sylorin, the Ferryman of Etherea, governs the Ethereal Flow that runs through the Veil, and Aetherica literature names the Veil the Spiritual Stratum. The first known method of Veil-thread weaving was developed through song.

Practitioners · A Moto said to have worked the deep Veil layers to fracture spiritual continuity across battlefields; the Veil-thread singers; the Bound Watchers at the outer edge of the Astral Fold, who sit in judgment over oaths and Temperance rites; the Veil Pilgrims, who never fully returned.

The lens

The idea Correspondence: two things alike enough and near enough pass to one another, and nothing is coerced. A crossing is a soul tuned so close to the far side of the fold that for a minute it is like enough to be there. Transference lends the exchange, Anamnesis the place’s memory of itself, Oneirion the grammar of a room that is also a dream.

Thickness How much of a place refuses to be like the far side: dense matter, close-set wards, Silence, a city’s noise. A thick crossing costs what it does because the soul has to make itself like something that is not like it, and there is no moderately hard way to do that.

The price Drift is the price the idea sets. Every time a soul makes itself like the far side a little of that likeness stays and never comes back, and enough of it and the Physical Plane stops being home.

The flaw A likeness forced instead of found. A rupture is a correspondence struck by force through the fold, and a forced likeness does not close of its own accord. It is put out by taking away what feeds it, never by pushing harder, and until then the far side’s unresolved content comes through it and sets on this side as Echo Beasts.

The Measurewrights’ account

The guild’s natural philosophy of the Veil follows, as their theory. Their figures are good and the table below runs on them; they have never once said why.

Phenomenon The Veil is a condition with no address: the membrane where one plane’s law softens into another’s. Crossing its inner fold is evanescent coupling, a wave leaking through a barrier it cannot travel in and resuming on the far side if the barrier is thin enough. Where its outer face touches the Plane of Fate, which stands above both lower realities and is no reality, what lies past it is less a far side than the thing both sides depend on.

Law Transmission falls exponentially with thickness, T ≈ e^(−2κd); ruptures follow dielectric breakdown (an insulator failing into a self-sustaining channel); Drift follows per-cycle fatigue, damage summed per crossing until it reaches one.

Limit Thickness is a property of the place, not the practitioner, and cost rises by e^(2κΔd): there is no moderately hard crossing.

Terrain Thin zones gather near Wellspring sites (Cores, in common speech), worn ritual sites and mass-death sites; Thick comes from dense material, close-set wards, Silence, or a city’s noise drowning the Soul Plane’s deeper frequencies.

Rupture (a Veil rupture: a channel struck through the Veil, not a Shell Rupture or Rupture Event) · A struck channel runs at a far lower field than it took to strike and stays struck until re-insulated: it is put out by removing its current or cooling it, never by pushing harder.

Echo Beasts · Unresolved spiritual content crosses a rupture and condenses on the Physical side like a precipitate, keeping the character of what formed it and none of its mind.

Energy · Coupling moves what is already present; the practitioner pays for the match, priced by thickness. A rupture was paid for once by whatever struck it and has run free since; containment is the expense.

Metaphysics

Aether The Veil is the Aether stratum’s own geography. A Thin zone is Residue, carries ambient Resonance, and deafens Harmonists; ambient noise inverts saturation, so Thick zones protect mortals, while a rupture risks a Crystal Fracture Event for anyone standing in it.

Wellspring Transference, the Living Exchange, primary: two Crystals in tune and near exchange Essence along a gradient, with coupling falling off sharply with distance; it strengthens Harmonics Synergy, Projection and Tempering Conversion.

Wellspring Anamnesis, the Wellspring of Remembrance: objects store their history readably; it reads a location’s Mnemata, sharper and more erasive in Thin zones.

Wellspring Oneirion, the Wellspring of Dream: a working acts on the manifold, not the room; it is Bifold Perception’s second grammar.

Secondary Somnalis, Eidolyn, Tenebra and Mortalis attach to particular operations.

Inherited failures Transference gives toward whoever has less and never asks who is giving, so it empties the practitioner into a depleted ally and couples every stabilized spirit back to them; Anamnesis’s reading writes itself over what it came to read and erases it; Oneirion’s working, true in the space of what can be thought, lands nowhere when it comes down into a room. The Measurewrights call these reverse coupling, thermal resetting and projection error. Any of them can be induced from outside.

Theory The Residue Position: Thin zones, Mnemata, Echo Beasts and Drift are all residue. Correspondence governs lawful passage: two things alike and near enough, nothing coerced.

Essence All three layers radiate through the medium: the Shell’s Resonant Tone, the Core’s emotional frequency, the Attraction Layer’s recognition hum; the Attraction Layer is the oscillator and dissolves in transit. The Crystal looks Faceted from Splintering through Refraction, Fire from Transcendence through Realization, and Flawless at Dissonance and Emanation.

School An instrument-makers’ school reads the Veil as a stationary medium, so Drift is a displacement one can navigate back from; it maps Thin zones superbly, but its remedy, go back in, is another cycle, and it graduates Veil Pilgrims.

Mechanism

Glyph Passage (Auren) as the boundary that moves, laid under Frequency (Selhar) for the tuning the crossing needs.

Boundary Where the practitioner’s operation is resident moves, never the barrier: the body stays on the Physical Plane while awareness, Essence circulation and Crystal operation shift into the threshold.

Effect Two renderings of one room at once: surfaces and light, with frequency, emotional density, temporal depth and relational topology over them. Buildings carry their history, people their Trait haloes, ground its grief and prayer.

Gates None below Refraction but exposure: passive unease for all, involuntary sensitivity at Splintering (B). Bifold at Refraction (A), short crossings at Transcendence (S), sustained Bifold at Invocation (S), sustained liminal work at Realization (SS), carrying others at Emanation (SSS).

Containment A significant rupture cannot be sealed by one practitioner at any Stage or Temperance; it takes Concordia-level practice re-insulating the channel by sustained harmonic projection.

Drift Each crossing couples the far side back to the practitioner and loosens their anchorage by an increment that never returns; the Physical Plane grows less convincing, then stops being home.

Drift Scale Loosened (0 to 24 points), no outward sign; Thinned (25 to 49), bodily sensation dulls; Unmoored (50 to 74), the Veil feels realer than the body; Adrift (75 to 99), returns come slow and incomplete; Veil Pilgrim at 100.

Failure Field fault: a Thin zone brings contamination, memory bleed and involuntary Bifold; a Thick zone costs exponentially more and muddies readings; at a rupture the planes collide, with no interface between them.

Structural Drift is a structural fault paid in installments; the Veil Pilgrim, patient enough to guide the lost and too thin to remember wanting a body, is the fault completing.

Bleed The Shell’s furnace burns off what does not arrive, as heat and light: 30 to 40 percent at Refraction, 20 to 30 at Transcendence to Realization, 10 to 15 at Emanation, around a body that is not moving.

Counterplay

Tell A practitioner visibly handling two perceptual grammars at once; on a crossing, an unattended body exactly where it was. Drift has no tell at any one crossing; only the count of entries reads it. A rupture’s tell is Echo Beasts.

Limits No controlled operation below Refraction; no solo rupture sealing; for the crossing art, no entry into the Soul Plane or Plane of Fate, only the threshold, and the body left behind every time; cost set by where the practitioner stands.

Fails against Thick ground or a city; Silence, wards and anti-Harmonia fields; harm to the unattended body, since a crossing cannot be aborted fast; repeated lawful reasons to cross, each one counted in Drift.

Carried Anyone ever carried through a crossing is already counting Drift, told or not; a soulguard cannot decline a soul.

The Bodily Road

Everything above is the crossing art, and the crossing art leaves the body in the chair. The world is wider than the art. The planes can be entered by the living, in the flesh, rarely and at a high price, and the Guild keeps a short roll of those who did it beside a much longer one of those who set out.

Ysolde Carrow is the entry every clerk knows. She was a thread singer of Realization standing with a coat of plum and silver and the highest fee in the Rose Coils, and in 668 a grain widow paid her nine hundred gold to go down to the bottom of a Whirlpool and fetch back a drowned son, in person, since a word was no use to her. Six men held the rope. It went slack in the fourth hour. Carrow walked up the stair eleven months later by the hall’s book and eighty-eight minutes later by her own count, in the same coat, holding the cut end, at ninety-one points of Drift on a scale that stops at a hundred. She has not tasted salt since, and she cannot say which hand she writes with. She gave back the nine hundred. The son is still where he was, because the dead stay dead, and no one on either roll has ever brought one home by this road.

Three roads are on the roll.

  • Thin ground at its worst. A mass-death site or a worn ritual ground where the wall has come so close that a whole person can be pushed through it, on a rare night, at a place nobody has learned to choose.
  • The bottom of a deep dungeon. The Deeps of an old dungeon thin the Veil and the Heart thins it further, so whoever has killed their way that far stands nearer another plane than any singer in a gilded chair. The Well’s law holds the door, and what guards the Heart generally stands in it.
  • A high-Stage art. A handful of arts, taught in no hall, carry the body where the crossing art carries only the awareness. The roll credits them to people at Emanation and above, the floor for carrying others, and it lists each one by owner and by what became of the owner.

Why it costs what it costs. It is the same likeness on a far worse account. A whole body, with all its weight and the Crystal seated in it, is a great deal more to make like the far side than an awareness and a shift of Essence, and the Measurewrights put it as transmission falling exponentially with what has to pass. Nobody has ever found this to be a harder crossing. It is a different order of expense, and no reserve makes it cheap. All three roads run through the inner fold. The outer face has a shorter heading on the roll and very few names under it, since a body past that surface has nothing to stand on.
