Nov 28, 202616 min read

The fourteen Archons as the Master Codex files them

One card to each Archon: epithets, domain, law, the form it shows in the heavens, its glyphs and Wellsprings, its signature working, its edict, what it is at its best and its worst, its bonds, what it did in history, who worships it and who serves it. The long account of each is on its pair’s page under The Fourteen Archons.

Urion

The Balancer of Scales; The Equilibrant; The Scaled Judge; The Steady Flame

DomainBalance, Judgment, Equilibrium, Temperance, Law of Exchange
DesignationOrder Magic / Judicaris
LawLaw of Equivalence: the metaphysical axiom that every act generates counterweight.
Celestial formThe Meridian Pair — twin-star system (one golden, one azure) in perfect rotational harmony
Glyphs[Ur] Balance · [Sel] Measure · [Va] Law · [Hael] Flame
WellspringsAnamnesis, Exuroth, Fixatio, Ignivale, Judicium, Lethegraal, Mirithane, Petralon, Thaloré
Signature workingThe Unbending Scale
Full accountThe Primordial Dyad — Order and Chaos

“Every force seeks its equal. To forget this is to invite collapse.”

Constructive expression. Lawful recognition: sees the true weight of every act, intention, and imbalance, and declares the correction required.

Corrupt expression and cost. Paralytic stasis: forced symmetry that refuses to permit necessary imbalance even when reality demands it.

Bonds and alignments. Triad of Law with Maelor (Memory) and Iesara (Insight). Primordial Dyad opposite Uurgath. Adversarial to Veyra, whose impulses distort measured flow. Mutual respect with Wyther, though Motion’s refusal of rest creates constant friction.

Historical influence. Manifested the First Concord Writ during the Great Spirit War; forebears of the Arbitration Division.

Cults and worship. Flame-lit equilibrium courts; Rite of Equal Breath; mirror trials.

Heralds and servants. Ember Magistrates; Tir Vaen the Golden Arbiter; Auran Lexicons

Uurgath

The Devourer of Forms; The Black Mouth; The Shatterfather

DomainDisruption, Entropy, Decay, Ruin, Erasure of Pattern
DesignationChaos Magic / Nihilaris
LawLaw of Unmaking: that every structure is unmade once it has exhausted its purpose, which the Measurewrights file as entropy.
Celestial formThe Ecliptic Maw — a dark pulsar whose devouring corona eclipses neighboring stars
Glyphs[Uur] Disruption · [Kael] Ruin · [Thru] Hollow · [Vorn] End
WellspringsAnamnesis, Chthonica, Coagulatio, Dissolution, Fixatio, Fractura, Ignivale, Judicium, Lethegraal, Materia Primordia, Mirithane, Nihiloth, Petralon, Pyraeon, Rebirthine, Verdantia
Signature workingLast Rites of Uurgath
Full accountThe Primordial Dyad — Order and Chaos

“All forms lie. I grant them truth through their end.”

Constructive expression. Lawful ending: the necessary severance of what has overstayed its design.

Corrupt expression and cost. Recursive annihilation: hunger that devours not to make room for creation but to satisfy the void’s own appetite.

Bonds and alignments. Primordial Dyad opposite Urion. Arch-enemy of Maelor, whose remembrance defies his hunger. Feared even by Auren, who calls him ‘the After of After.’ Hated by Elyndra. Occasionally courted by Veyra and Wyther, who know his gifts cost more than they promise.

Historical influence. Age of Erasure; silenced the Archon Sirael of Bloom from memory and starlight; catalyst of the Withering Age.

Cults and worship. Subterranean ritual silence; ash-blackened tongues; unwriting sacrifices.

Heralds and servants. Nihil Choir; Thren Ka; The Graven Maw

Thalen

The Architect of Form; The Maker of Edges; The Geometer

DomainStructure, Space, Geometry, the Permission for Shape to Exist
DesignationForm Magic / Formaris
Celestial formThe Hexagonal Crown — six perfectly equidistant stars visible only through Essence-tuned instruments
Glyphs[Th] Foundation · [Al] Edge · [En] Lattice · [Cal] Craft
WellspringsFractura, Materia Primordia, Petralon, Terranova, Verdantia
Signature workingThe Hexagonal Oath
Full accountPair I — Form Against Time

“All chaos is only structure unremembered.”

Constructive expression. Consecrated structure: architecture that persists because reality recognizes its rightness.

Corrupt expression and cost. Imprisonment in form: tyranny of shape that refuses growth, change, or organic deviation.

Bonds and alignments. Paired with Valen as Form Against Time. Respected by Maelor, since archives require structure. Trusts Urion. Contrasts Wyther, whose every storm tests his work. Reveres Auren. Opposed by Uurgath. Kinship with Elyndra where growth wraps stone without breaking it.

Historical influence. Adamant Vaults built to his ratios during the Withering; Forgewrights devised Soul Crystal housings and stabilizer frames.

Cults and worship. Powdered-iron geometry; Six Angles of Foundation; pillar-centered domes.

Heralds and servants. Stone Choir; Borun the Maker’s Hand; Guild of Measurewrights

Valen

The Clock of Ages; The Chronarch; The Hourless King

DomainTime, Sequence, Duration, Causality, the Rhythm That Must Be Followed
DesignationTime Magic / Chronaris
Celestial formThe Pendulum Arc — a binary orbit so regular that civilizations calibrate calendars to its eclipses
Glyphs[Va] Moment · [Len] Line · [Cro] Cycle · [Ae] Era
WellspringsAnamnesis, Cymorath, Lethegraal, Petralon
Signature workingCovenant of the Instant
Full accountPair I — Form Against Time

“Every moment is a covenant.”

Constructive expression. Preserves sequence, history, causality, and meaningful progression.

Corrupt expression and cost. Loop-prison: recursive inevitability, frozen causality — events unable to move beyond one wound, one vow, one decisive moment.

Bonds and alignments. Paired with Thalen as Form Against Time. Kin to Maelor: Maelor keeps what happened, Valen keeps when. Distrusts Wyther, whose upheavals tangle timelines. Quiet sorrowful respect for Auren. Rivals Uurgath. Distant necessary accord with Zhaeren.

Historical influence. Sealed the Temporal Wound of Dorrosfenn; Chronometric Citadel; the Vector Drift incident.

Cults and worship. Sand offerings; stellar timekeeping; Clockroads pilgrimages.

Heralds and servants. Chronolyths; Arel the Repeater; Pendulum Choir

Maelor

The Keeper of Memory; The Silent Witness; The Silver Archivist

DomainMemory, Continuity, Record, Ancestral Recall
DesignationMemory Magic / Mnemoris
Celestial formEidhras’ Lantern — a pale argent star with six satellites representing the layers of recollection
Glyphs[Ma] Recall · [Lei] Binding · [On] Light · [Ir] Continuum
WellspringsAnamnesis, Eclipseron, Ignivale, Judicium, Luminalis, Materia Primordia, Mirithane, Rebirthine, Transmutatio
Signature workingThe Silver Archive Opened
Full accountPair II — Memory Against Horizon

“Nothing is lost, only waiting to be remembered.”

Constructive expression. Lawful preservation: the faithful keeping of what deserves to endure.

Corrupt expression and cost. Memory-hoarding: refusal to let the past rest; remembrance curdles into imprisonment, nostalgia into a cage.

Bonds and alignments. Triad of Law with Urion and Iesara. Paired against Zhaeren as Memory Against Horizon. Kin to Valen. Rivalry with Wyther, whose flux threatens record stability. Forbidden connection to Uurgath. Bound by solemn accord to Auren, for remembrance grants death meaning.

Historical influence. Founded the first Parunic Codex; sealed memories into Echo Vaults during the Withering, later uncovered by the Guild Accord.

Cults and worship. Vault-Spires; mirrored-water recitations; memory-thread offerings.

Heralds and servants. Aelthir the Chronicler; Mnemon Priests of Velthaeir; The Lamented Choir

Zhaeren

The Infinite Horizon; The Vastwalker; Keeper of the Silent Depth

DomainSpace, Distance, Infinity, Direction, The Unreachable
DesignationSpace Magic / Vastaris
Celestial formCrown of Distant Suns — fourteen stars stretched so far apart they appear unmoving across millennia
Glyphs[Zh] Space · [Ae] Vastness · [Ren] Boundary · [Oth] Path
WellspringsAbyntheus, Dissolution, Eclipseron, Luminalis, Materia Primordia, Transmutatio
Signature workingThe Unreachable Made Near
Full accountPair II — Memory Against Horizon

“The distance between two souls is the truest measure of their courage.”

Constructive expression. Connects: maps lawful paths between distant places, enabling the journeys that drive civilizations forward.

Corrupt expression and cost. Tyranny of distance: isolation so absolute no recognition can bridge the gap, no voice can carry, no traveler arrives.

Bonds and alignments. Paired against Maelor as Memory Against Horizon: the Horizon dissolves certainty into possibility while Memory crystallizes it. Distant necessary accord with Valen. Kinship with Wyther, whose winds carry travelers across the distances Zhaeren defines.

Historical influence. Stabilized the Outer Gates; defined Concord Gate geometry; the Fracture of Rovann.

Cults and worship. Walking meditations; sky vigils; vast halls without center.

Heralds and servants. Starstriders; Aes Volen; Hollow Lamps

Irath

The Flame of Will and Dominion; The Dominion-Bearer; The Crown Unbent

DomainWill, Dominion, Power, Authority, Assertion of Self over Chaos
DesignationDominion Magic / Imperaris
LawLaw of Sovereign Will: the principle that Essence obeys conviction.
Celestial formThe King’s Heart — a blinding amber star at the center of the constellation Tyrant’s Crown
Glyphs[Ir] Will · [Ath] Command · [Dom] Dominion · [Krn] Crown
WellspringsCoagula, Fixatio, Ignivale, Judicium, Sublimatio
Signature workingThe Unbowed Crown
Full accountPair III — Will Against Desire

“He who commands himself commands the world.”

Constructive expression. Lawful sovereignty: leadership earned through clarity of purpose, accepted by those who recognize its righteousness.

Corrupt expression and cost. Tyranny: the crushing imposition of one will upon all others regardless of dignity, autonomy, or right to exist.

Bonds and alignments. Twin Flames with Veyra, productive rivalry rather than cooperation. Mutual respect with Urion, for law is will disciplined. Hostility toward Uurgath. Distant kinship with Thalen, both believing order must be built. Refuses alliance with Auren, for to end is to surrender.

Historical influence. Forged the Concord Hierarchies; raised the Dominion Spires; the Throne Wars of Zarn.

Cults and worship. Unbowed Spine rite; molten gold sigils; vertical forge temples.

Heralds and servants. Ash-Crowned Legion; Kharos the Bound; Ember Monoliths

Veyra

The Flame of Desire; The Red Seraph; She Who Burns the Path

DomainDesire, Passion, Will, Creation through Longing
DesignationDesire Magic / Ardoris
LawLaw of Becoming: the truth that yearning shapes form.
Celestial formThe Heartflare — a blood-red star encircled by an amber halo synced to living magnetic patterns
Glyphs[Ve] Desire · [Ra] Flame · [Leth] Blood · [Asa] Creation
WellspringsAbyntheus, Anima Spirare, Cymorath, Dissolution, Exuroth, Ignivale, Judicium, Mirithane, Verdantia
Signature workingThe Heartflare Given Shape
Full accountPair III — Will Against Desire

“Desire is the only proof of life.”

Constructive expression. Sacred creation: the love that builds worlds, the ambition that drives civilization, the yearning toward highest potential.

Corrupt expression and cost. Consuming fixation: desire that devours its object, mistaking destruction for devotion and possession for love.

Bonds and alignments. Twin Flames with Irath. Drawn to Wyther, whose storms feed her flame. Respected by Iesara, whose clarity she envies but seduces. At odds with Urion, for law dulls desire. Feared by Maelor, since her passion rewrites what was. Tempted by Uurgath.

Historical influence. Ignited the Covenant Revolt; birthed the first free Wellspring Cities and the catastrophic Ashen Floods.

Cults and worship. Flame dances; Lovers’ thread-binds; Veyric Wine visions.

Heralds and servants. Emberdaughters; Vaelith the Lover-Saint; House of Ashen Hearts

Iesara

The Veiled Seer of Insight; The Mother of Sight; The Whisper Beyond the Mirror

DomainSight, Perception, Clarity, Truth Beyond Surfaces
DesignationInsight Magic / Luminaris
Celestial formA prismatic star cluster (devotionally called The Seraph’s Eye) whose light refracts differently per observer
Glyphs[Ie] Perception · [Sar] Reflection · [Lum] Light · [Vei] Depth
WellspringsEclipseron, Letheveil, Luminalis, Oneirion, Somnalis, Tenebra, Verdantia
Signature workingThe Prism That Does Not Blink
Full accountPair IV — Insight Against Dream

“To see clearly is to suffer what clarity demands.”

Constructive expression. Wisdom: the gift of seeing rightly and sharing that sight with those who can bear it.

Corrupt expression and cost. Madness of omniscience: the compulsive need to see everything, unable to stop perceiving or unsee what it has found.

Bonds and alignments. Triad of Law with Maelor and Urion. Sister-bond to Kaetra, the Dialectic of Perception: one reveals by light, the other by reflection. Closest kin to Selhar. Gentle rivalry with Wyther. Pity and quiet fear for Uurgath.

Historical influence. Shaped the Seer Orders; saved the Dream Wellspring from an Echo Cascade.

Cults and worship. Blindfolded Reflection; Lysdra incense; prism temples.

Heralds and servants. The Seven Veils; Vel Orathiel; Order of Prismatons

Kaetra

The Dream-Weaver of Veils; The Veil-Singer; Father of Illusions

DomainDream, Illusion, Vision, Truth Obscured Through Beauty
DesignationDream Magic / Oneiris
Celestial formA nebulous constellation that never appears the same twice, yet is always unmistakably hers
Glyphs[Ka] Dream · [Et] Mirage · [Ra] Emotion · [Vl] Veil
WellspringsCymorath, Eclipseron, Exuroth, Hypnather, Ignivale, Luminalis, Mirithane, Oneirion, Somnalis, Verdantia
Signature workingThe Mask That Loves You Back
Full accountPair IV — Insight Against Dream

“Truth wears the mask that love or fear demands.”

Constructive expression. Sacred vision: the dream that reveals what waking sight cannot reach; healing illusion granting a moment’s peace.

Corrupt expression and cost. Prison of false reality: the dream that refuses to release its dreamer; illusion so perfect truth becomes the unreal thing.

Bonds and alignments. Sister-bond to Iesara, the Dialectic of Perception. Intrigued by Selhar, whose tones she turns to lullabies. Opposed by Urion, whose clarity dispels her veils. Courted by Veyra, whose passions feed her dreamscapes. Ancient truce with Auren, for in dreams the dead still whisper.

Historical influence. Founded the Mirror-Veil Accord; cloaked Rovann’s cities during its Fall; her arts birthed the Phantom War.

Cults and worship. Lucid sleep halls; Lunareth incense; Veil-Thread offerings.

Heralds and servants. Somnari; Eilune the Veiled; Mirror-Owls

Wyther

The Unbound Current of Motion; The Ever-Turning Wheel; The Tempest That Remembers Not

DomainMotion, Change, Evolution, Momentum, Freedom from Stasis
DesignationMotion Magic / Kineris
LawLaw of Continuance through Change: the metaphysical truth that stagnation is death and movement is the soul’s proof of life.
Celestial formThe Spiral Verge — a shifting tri-star system leaving spiraling plasma trails that redraw nightly
Glyphs[Wy] Motion · [Noq] Wind · [Ser] Renewal · [Eith] Flux
WellspringsCataclysm, Coagula, Fixatio, Ignivale, Judicium, Lethegraal, Mortalis, Orrenthal, Petralon, Somnalis, Terranova, Thaloré
Signature workingThe Storm That Remembers Not
Full accountPair V — Motion Against Stillness

“Only that which moves endures.”

Constructive expression. Evolution: the lawful progression from what was to what must be, the wind that carries seeds to fertile ground.

Corrupt expression and cost. Chaos without purpose: compulsive need to destroy every stillness, the storm that tears apart what it should merely reshape.

Bonds and alignments. Paired against Auren as Motion Against Stillness, eternal opposition. Cosmic Heartbeat with Urion: not war but conversation, producing the oscillation between stability and change. Perpetual friction with Maelor. Admired by Veyra. Kinship with Zhaeren.

Historical influence. Birthed the Aetherstorm Era — restarted atmospheric Wellsprings, preventing Essence rot in the Outer Realms.

Cults and worship. Storm-peak offerings; Whirling Step rites; suspended-shrine rings.

Heralds and servants. Galeborne Choir; Eira Vanthel; Shatterwings

Auren

The Keeper of the Still Gate; The Quiet Judge; The Last Shepherd

DomainDeath, Stillness, Passage, Closure, Repose
DesignationDeath Magic / Mortaris
LawLaw of Transition: the truth that nothing truly ends, but all must pass through stillness.
Celestial formA dim white dwarf at the heart of Thanaris, ringed by the unmoving Shroud Sea
Glyphs[Au] Death · [Ren] Passage · [Thal] Silence · [Vor] Return
WellspringsAnima Spirare, Cataclysm, Cymorath, Ignivale, Mortalis, Nihiloth, Somnalis, Tenebra, Terranova, Verdantia
Signature workingThe Still Gate’s Mercy
Full accountPair V — Motion Against Stillness

“All paths lead to the Still Gate. Only peace walks through it.”

Constructive expression. Peaceful passage: the mercy of a death that remembers the life it concludes.

Corrupt expression and cost. Tyranny of premature ending: compulsive need to silence what still lives — stillness spreading as extinction, not peace.

Bonds and alignments. Paired against Wyther as Motion Against Stillness. Cycle of Life and Death with Elyndra, the only Archonic partnership both call kinship rather than alliance. Solemn accord with Maelor. Mutual acknowledgment with Urion. Resented by Veyra. Feared by Uurgath.

Historical influence. Ended the War of Seven Dawns; established funerary law and soul-reclamation doctrine.

Cults and worship. Silent candles; Gatebearer dreams; salt and bone dust on still water.

Heralds and servants. Pale Sentinels; Sere Antha; Choir of Hollow Bells

Selhar

The Voice of Harmony; The Resonant Bridge; Keeper of the Nine Chords

DomainSound, Resonance, Harmony, Frequency, Emotion Through Vibration
DesignationHarmony Magic / Resonaris
LawLaw of Resonant Equilibrium: the principle that all things, living or dead, possess a frequency that seeks harmony with its environment.
Celestial formLytherion’s String — a shimmering triad of stars whose tone shifts with the moral state of the world
Glyphs[Se] Song · [Lh] Frequency · [Ar] Accord · [Chw] Choir
WellspringsJudicium, Mirithane, Orrenthal, Tenebra, Thaloré, Verdantia
Signature workingThe Chord That Remembers Its Shape
Full accountPair VI — Harmony and Renewal

“In every sound, the world remembers its shape.”

Constructive expression. Communion: the shared frequency that binds communities across distance and time.

Corrupt expression and cost. Enforced unison: erasure of individual voice in service of a single dominant tone, silencing dissent by drowning it.

Bonds and alignments. Paired with Elyndra as Harmony and Renewal. Closest kin to Iesara, for sound and sight reflect each other’s truths. Respects Wyther, whose winds carry his song. Distrusts Uurgath. Often mediates between Urion and Veyra, their discord his testing ground.

Historical influence. Rite of Choral Concord founded Harmonia; ended the Siege of Epphrenean Spire by harmonizing three Domains.

Cults and worship. Choral Alignment; Wellspring-glass instruments; silent echo hymns.

Heralds and servants. The Harmonics; Ril the Chanter; Choir of Nine

Elyndra

The Verdant Pulse of Life; The Bloommother; The Everbreathing Heart

DomainLife, Growth, Renewal, Vitality, Fertility
DesignationLife Magic / Vitalaris
LawLaw of Renewal: the truth that existence perpetuates itself through death and rebirth.
Celestial formThe Living Crown — an emerald-gold nebular bloom that pulses as if inhaling
Glyphs[El] Life · [Yn] Bloom · [Dr] Vitality · [Vael] Renewal
WellspringsAnima Spirare, Dissolution, Exuroth, Luminalis, Materia Primordia, Nihiloth, Oblivara, Oneirion, Rebirthine, Somnalis, Verdantia
Signature workingThe Wheel That Turns Again
Full accountPair VI — Harmony and Renewal

“What grows must die; what dies must bloom again.”

Constructive expression. The sacred cycle: trust that death is not final, that what falls feeds what rises.

Corrupt expression and cost. Uncontrolled growth: the Plague of the Living Flesh — Essence that multiplies without limit, consuming what it should nourish.

Bonds and alignments. Paired with Selhar as Harmony and Renewal. Cycle of Life and Death with Auren, absolute codependence and true kinship. Symbiosis with Selhar and Kaetra. Friendly tension with Irath. Absolute loathing for Uurgath. Rides with Wyther, for wind spreads life’s seeds.

Historical influence. Verdant Exhalation reversed Uurgath’s entropy floods; schisms erupted over weaponizing life itself.

Cults and worship. Life Circles; blood to roots; vine pilgrim test.

Heralds and servants. Verdure Choir; Arathen the Root-Singer; Green Mirrors
