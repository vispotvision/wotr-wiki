Pair IV — Insight Against Dream

Pair IV — Insight Against Dream

Nov 28, 20266 min read

Old Gil Mossgill was the best fiddler in the Vale and he died in the hard end of Year 592 with the roof unpaid, and his widow Elspet took the roof money to the back room of an inn and hired Lisabet Kettering, an Oneiris worker with rose in her hair and violet in her coat, to make the wake worth having. The whole parish was at the barn by sundown, and at the first cup the air went soft and pink as the inside of a shell, the lamps grew halos, and there was Gil in the middle of the threshing floor in his wedding shirt with the fiddle under his chin. He played for three hours. He played the Long Reel and the one about the reeve’s daughter, and every note went in under the ribs, because Lisabet’s art works from what the listener has already got, and the parish had sixty years of him. He danced his widow down the length of the barn. Old men who had hated him for decades were crying with joy and shouting up requests.

In the middle of the Reel a young woman stood up at the back, a novice from the prism temple at Lindholt with bits of glass and water-vial knotted in her cords, whose name was Anwen and who had come for the free food. Her art was Luminaris and she had no more choice in the matter than a window has. She said, in a high, clear, wretched voice, that Gil was on the trestle with a coin on each eye, that nobody was playing anything, that the woman by the door had the roof money in her apron, and that she was very sorry. They threw her out in the snow. The widow paid Lisabet double to go on till morning. And next day, when the barn was cold and the roof was still unpaid, Elspet Mossgill walked to the temple and asked for Anwen, who came and sat with her and told her the truth about the money, the debts and the man. For a year after that she kept them both, the weaver on feast days and the seer on rent days. Neither has forgiven her or the other, or Gil’s fiddle, which the one could make sing and the other could tell you was cracked.

The sister-bond. Iesara reveals truth through clarity; Kaetra obscures truth through beauty. Their relationship ensures that truth is never so harsh it destroys the observer, and illusion is never so perfect it replaces reality entirely.

IX. Iesara, the Veiled Seer of Insight

Insight Magic: Luminaris

DomainSight, Perception, Clarity, Truth Beyond Surfaces
Celestial FormA prismatic star cluster whose light refracts differently depending on the perceiver’s Essence, revealing truth calibrated to each observer’s capacity to bear it.
Associated Glyphs[Ie] Perception · [Sar] Reflection · [Lum] Light · [Vei] Depth

Divine Edict · “To see clearly is to suffer what clarity demands.”

Iesara governs the act of seeing past surfaces to the mechanisms beneath. Her seers prevented the erasure of the Dream Wellspring by decoding an Echo Cascade that threatened to consume it, and even Archons sought her counsel before engaging in cosmic conflict. Her temples are prisms of glass and water, where sunlight refracts endlessly, symbolizing perception’s infinity.

Luminaris: The Magic of Clarity and Revelation

Luminaris is the Archonic magic of absolute perception, the stripping away of falsehood, disguise, and self-deception to reveal the naked truth of a thing’s nature. A practitioner of Luminaris perceives at a depth that ordinary senses cannot reach, reading the Essence signatures (the mark left by Aether refined in a Soul Crystal) of objects, the emotional histories of places, and the hidden architectures of Domains and wards. Every magic below runs two ways: through the Attraction Force (the law of recognition, the binding that lets one presence acknowledge another and endure) and through the Obsession Force (the same reach gone to fixation, unable to let go).

At its lowest expression, Luminaris grants heightened sensory clarity, the ability to detect illusion, and the capacity to read intention through body language and Essence fluctuation. At its highest expression, Luminaris becomes the power to reveal cosmic truth, to perceive the Plane of Fate’s lattice directly, and to see the full causal chain that connects every act to its ultimate consequence.

Through Attraction Force · Luminaris becomes wisdom, the gift of seeing rightly and sharing that sight with those who can bear it.

Through Obsession Force · It becomes the madness of omniscience, the compulsive need to see everything, the horror of a mind that cannot stop perceiving and cannot unsee what it has found.

Connections · Forms the Triad of Law with Maelor (Memory) and Urion (Balance). Gentle rivalry with Wyther (Motion) for embodying instability of perception. Holds pity and quiet fear for Uurgath (Disruption), the blind destroyer of knowing. Sister-bond to Kaetra (Dream), for one reveals by light, the other by reflection.

X. Kaetra, the Dream-Weaver of Veils

Dream Magic: Oneiris

DomainDream, Illusion, Vision, Truth Obscured Through Beauty
Celestial FormA nebulous constellation that appears differently to every observer, never the same twice, yet always unmistakably hers.
Associated Glyphs[Ka] Dream · [Et] Mirage · [Ra] Emotion · [Vl] Veil

Divine Edict · “Truth wears the mask that love or fear demands.”

Kaetra governs the liminal space where truth and illusion become indistinguishable. Her followers created the Mirror-Veil Accord, allowing communication through shared dreamscapes when Concord Gates collapsed. During the Fall of Rovann, her intervention cloaked entire cities in perpetual twilight, saving thousands by convincing invaders the land was already ruin. Her arts birthed the Phantom War, where soldiers fought shadows believing them real.

Oneiris: The Magic of Veils and Visions

Oneiris is the Archonic magic of shaped perception, dream-architecture, and the deliberate obscuring or reshaping of truth through visionary overlay. A practitioner of Oneiris reaches into the Veil’s liminal medium and weaves constructs from the raw material of dream, memory, and subconscious association, and calling the result an illusion is roughly as fair as calling a house a pile of wood.

At its lowest expression, Oneiris generates reality-linked illusions charged with authentic psychic weight, bends perception layers to disguise surfaces, and infuses imagery with emotional resonance so convincing that the observer’s own heart confirms the illusion as real. At its highest expression, Oneiris becomes the power to construct entire dreamscapes that function as habitable realities, shared spaces where the laws of waking give way to the grammar of the subconscious.

Through Attraction Force · Oneiris becomes sacred vision, the dream that reveals what waking sight cannot reach, the healing illusion that grants the suffering a moment of peace.

Through Obsession Force · It becomes the prison of false reality, the dream that refuses to release its dreamer, the illusion so perfect that truth becomes the thing that seems unreal.

Connections · Sister-bond to Iesara (Insight), one reveals by light, the other by reflection. Intrigued by Selhar (Harmony), whose tones she turns to lullabies. Opposed by Urion (Balance), whose clarity dispels her veils. Courted by Veyra (Desire), whose passions feed her dreamscapes. Holds ancient truce with Auren (Death), for in dreams, the dead still whisper.
