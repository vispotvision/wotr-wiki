Pair III — Will Against Desire

Pair III — Will Against Desire

Nov 17, 20266 min read

The Siege of Brannock had reached its fifth month in Year 380 when the city’s whole defense came to rest on two people who had never met. Lord Marshal Halloran Steel-Lip held the walls. He was an Imperaris man, his coat the amber of a king’s seal, and he held three thousand starving soldiers at their posts by the plain force of his word: they could not desert because he had said so, and their knees would not bend until he said otherwise. Down in Sugar Lane, Mistress Joan Fenwick, confectioner, had a thimbleful of Ardoris in her hands, inherited from an aunt, and it had always made her saffron buns sell well, because you could not eat one without wanting something. With the Marshal’s blessing she took the last saffron in Brannock and the last of the flour and set out to bake one bun for every man on the wall, for morale. She doubled the working, for the occasion. It was the worst mistake and the finest baking in the history of the quarter. The carts went up at dawn, crimson-gold, smelling of every kitchen anyone had ever been small in. Three thousand men took one bite, and they wanted to go home.

They wanted it in a way that lit the walls. The ramparts came up red from end to end, one long smoldering line, with the amber of the Marshal’s will pressing down over it, and for the space of a slow breath the two colors stood against each other while grown men wept on their feet and could not move and could not stop. Then Steel-Lip’s nose and eyes began to bleed, his word gave way like a rotten stair, and the entire garrison of Brannock went over the wall with a howl. Nobody had ordered an attack. They were going home, the enemy camp was in the way, and the besiegers, who had been promised a starving city, looked up from their breakfast at three thousand men who would have walked through a fire to see their mothers. The siege ended by nine. Four hundred died on the wall and in the camp, nine hundred were never seen in Brannock again because they kept walking, and the Marshal, who never gave another order, and Mistress Fenwick, who never again baked anything but plain rye, were both voted the freedom of the city and have a statue each, facing in opposite directions.

The Twin Flames. Irath and Veyra share the elemental correspondence of Fire, but their relationship is productive rivalry rather than cooperation. Where Irath builds thrones, Veyra lights the fires that either illuminate or burn them.

VII. Irath, the Flame of Will and Dominion

Dominion Magic: Imperaris

DomainWill, Dominion, Power, Authority, Assertion of Self over Chaos
Celestial FormThe King’s Heart: a blinding amber star at the center of the constellation Tyrant’s Crown, whose burning lances form a crown-shaped corona during realm alignments.
Associated Glyphs[Ir] Will · [Ath] Command · [Dom] Dominion · [Krn] Crown

Divine Edict · “He who commands himself commands the world.”

Irath governs the Law of Sovereign Will, the principle that Essence obeys conviction. His dominion defines hierarchy within Wellsprings and soul structures, ensuring that belief, far ahead of blood, determines ascension. His influence forged the Concord Hierarchies, establishing order among the Guild Divisions when rebellion threatened unity. His devotees raised the Dominion Spires, resonant constructs amplifying commands across battlefronts.

Imperaris: The Magic of Command and Sovereignty

Imperaris is the Archonic magic of imposed will, hierarchical enforcement, and the crystallization of personal conviction into metaphysical authority. A practitioner of Imperaris declares her truth so absolutely that Essence itself (Aether refined in the Soul Crystal, the soul’s own substance, which a fighter holds and spends) reorganizes to serve that declaration. Every magic below runs two ways: through the Attraction Force (the law of recognition, the binding that lets one presence acknowledge another and endure) and through the Obsession Force (the same reach gone to fixation, unable to let go).

At its lowest expression, Imperaris amplifies personal Essence through conviction, compels elemental obedience, and shields the caster’s Essence from external control. At its highest expression, Imperaris becomes the power to enforce hierarchical stability upon an entire Domain, declaring that within this space, one will reigns supreme and all other wills must submit or shatter.

Through Attraction Force · Imperaris becomes lawful sovereignty, leadership earned through clarity of purpose and accepted by those who recognize its righteousness.

Through Obsession Force · It becomes tyranny, the crushing imposition of one will upon all others regardless of their dignity, autonomy, or right to exist on their own terms.

Connections · Mutual respect with Urion (Balance), for law is will disciplined. Rivalry with Veyra (Desire), for desire without discipline becomes ruin. Hostility toward Uurgath (Disruption), who erases what will creates. Distant kinship with Thalen (Form), both believing order must be built, not begged. Refuses alliance with Auren (Death), for to end is to surrender.

VIII. Veyra, the Flame of Desire

Desire Magic: Ardoris

DomainDesire, Passion, Will, Creation through Longing
Celestial FormThe Heartflare: a blood-red star encircled by an amber halo whose pulsing radiance beats visibly even through dense cloud, syncing with the magnetic patterns of living beings.
Associated Glyphs[Ve] Desire · [Ra] Flame · [Leth] Blood · [Asa] Creation

Divine Edict · “Desire is the only proof of life.”

Veyra governs the Law of Becoming, the truth that yearning shapes form. Every mortal impulse, every divine ambition, every song sung from want reverberates through her domain. She is the cause of innovation and downfall alike.

Her followers ignited the Covenant Revolt, breaking the Concord’s emotional bindings to reclaim the right to feel without sanction, birthing both the first free Wellspring Cities and the catastrophic Ashen Floods where passion-driven rituals destabilized whole Domains.

Ardoris: The Magic of Longing and Creation

Ardoris is the Archonic magic of passion made manifest, the power to transform yearning into physical and metaphysical change. A practitioner of Ardoris burns with a desire so focused that reality reshapes itself to accommodate their longing.

At its lowest expression, Ardoris kindles creative inspiration, stirs emotional resonance between souls, and amplifies the Essence of those who pursue their ambitions with genuine fervor. At its highest expression, Ardoris becomes the power to create through sheer want, to bring into existence things that have never been by desiring them with sufficient metaphysical intensity, bridging the gap between what is and what must be.

Through Attraction Force · Ardoris becomes sacred creation, the love that builds worlds, the ambition that drives civilization forward, the yearning that pulls souls toward their highest potential.

Through Obsession Force · It becomes consuming fixation, the desire that devours its object, the passion that mistakes destruction for devotion and possession for love.

Connections · Drawn to Wyther (Motion), whose storms feed her flame. Respected by Iesara (Insight), whose clarity she envies but seduces. At odds with Urion (Balance), for law dulls her desire. Feared by Maelor (Memory), since her passion rewrites what once was. Tempted by Uurgath (Disruption), whose void she sometimes mistakes for hunger.
