The Abyssal Depths — Prometheus, Zar'thuun, Vohrin

The Abyssal Depths — Prometheus, Zar'thuun, Vohrin

Nov 17, 202614 min read

Cinderhowe liked to say it was the only town in the four quarters that paid its rent to a mountain. It sat on the orange flank of a smoking peak with every door painted the color of a live coal, and for three hundred years it had baked its bread in the vents and sold it at the Fire Fair as the best crust anywhere, with girls dancing through the hot springs in ribbons and small boys daring each other across the warm scree. In the summer of Year 662 a Varūn volcanologist named Beatrix Orne rode in with a Marrowkin hold-clerk, who had lain with his ear to the rock at three camps along the way and said he could hear a chained heart going fast, and she climbed up on a bread oven in the market square and told them to leave. They bought her a drink. She stayed for the fair, to her credit, and she was dancing when it came. The mountain split on its east side in the blue hour before dawn, the whole sky turned the color of the doors, and a river of fire as wide as the market came down through the bakers’ quarter at about the speed of a man who knows he is late, and it kept coming for nine days. Two thousand people got out along the ridge road with their shoes burning, about four hundred did not, and the ovens were the last things to go.

The monks of the Abyssal Hymn came up the ridge road in the second week, in long black with their slow singing, and set up among the tents, offering the burned what they offer everyone, which is to learn to forget well, and there was a line by noon. In the third week, in August, with the lava still glowing at night, the first hard frost anyone had ever seen in that valley came down on the cooling field, whitened it from end to end and cracked it level, and it stayed that way until spring, so that people could walk on it and start measuring for houses. Cinderhowe stands there now, doors orange, baking in the new vents. Talk to anyone over sixty and half of them will weep, and the other half will tell you cheerfully about some sort of a fire and wonder what the fuss is. A Guild clerk would file all that as three separate accidents.

Fire, void and frost. The order in which the three operate is fixed and is not a hierarchy. Prometheus burns what is false. Zar’thuun unmakes what is excess. Vohrin stills what is left so it can be built upon. Call the Abyssal Depths the back of the house, whatever the shrine schools say about an enemy quarter of the pantheon.

PROMETHEUS: The Flame of Theros

“All ends burn brighter when understood.”

Seat Prime Pyrotheic Titan of the Abyssal Depths, Theros. Born when Gaia’s mantle cracked under the first stress of mortal sin.

Domain Fire, Destruction, Transmutation, Enlightenment through Pain

Ground The Ground of Finality, called the Pyric Heart. A burning sphere of liquid Aether that refines unworthy soul fragments into pure Essence vapor.

Wellsprings Six of the Sixty are this fire at a size a soul can use: what it leaves behind, the temper, the go-between, the burn that renews, the change of state, and the remorse that burns in the open. Wild: Cinerion, Exuroth, Manganthra, Pyreveil, Transmutatio. Spirit: Penanceflare.

Manifestation

A colossal figure forged of blackened iron and flame, bones molten, breath a storm of sparks. Chains of cooling magma drape from his wrists as symbols of self-restraint. His heart is visible, a radiant core burning with countless faces of the dead, each screaming once before vanishing into white light. Mountains erupt where he walks with no rage in it at all, as cosmic maintenance, vents to keep the world’s blood from clotting.

The Load

His law is destruction as transformation: what is false must burn, so that what is true can be refined out of it. He oversees the death of worlds and the rebirth of light in the same office, and without him stagnation poisons existence because nothing ever finishes its cycle. He is the teacher of Transmutation and the origin of alchemy’s final axiom, that all matter is memory awaiting its own combustion. His flames define enlightenment as such. Knowledge attained through suffering, mastery through loss.

The Record

He was Gaia’s safeguard in the First Eon, the reset mechanism, burning excess Essence when creation overgrew itself so ecosystems and souls could rebalance. His infernos consumed half the Spirit Realms during the war, which was read as devastation and was in fact the cauterization of a dying world, and the Scorched Realms of Theros became the foundation of post-war rebirth. The Flame-Titan Banre was one of his fragmented echoes, and the Hobgoblins worshipped Banre as the source, mistaking the imitation. He never answered their prayers. He watched their forges until they burned themselves hollow. During the Crevice’s awakening he kept the Pyric Heart stable by consuming billions of malformed Echoes, and his firestorm ran from Abythron to Gaia’s mantle sealing the lower Void from overflow. Varūn volcanologists call planetary eruptions Prometheic Surges and believe his slumbering consciousness speaks through mantle pressure. The Marrowkin claim to hear his chained heartbeat before every cataclysm.

Bonds

Thalen, Form. Defines the shape fire keeps in matter. Iesara, Insight. Ensures fire reveals rather than deceives. Aegor, Body, Epoch. Anchors fire within substance, preventing pure annihilation.

The Titan-Keyed Art: The Pyric Refinement

Supplementary · Exuroth · Zenith+ · [Ra] Flame + [Asa] Creation

Trigger The caster must name, honestly, one thing about the target fragment or artifact that is false or unworthy. A guess does not open the working.

Effect Burns away everything false or unworthy in a soul fragment or artifact and refines what survives into pure usable Essence vapor. What remains afterward is measurably more stable and more honest by every Harmonics Fidelity metric the Accord maintains.

Measure Refinement removes roughly the bottom twenty to forty percent of a fragment’s Essence-purity, proportional to how much falsity the caster correctly identified.

Cost The process cannot separate sentiment from falsehood with full precision. Genuinely valued memories attached to the false part burn away with it, and there is no way to know in advance which ones were attached.

Limit It refines what exists. It adds nothing, and a fragment with nothing false in it comes out of the fire exactly as it went in.

Counter [Vael] Renewal reintroduces new growth mid-burn, diluting the purity threshold and letting false material survive alongside true. The result is an artifact that now reads clean and is not.

What nobody knows Why the flame reads a deliberate forgery as false and a sincere error as true. The Pyric Scribes have branded four centuries of argument about this onto their own skin and settled nothing.

Codex: [Ra] Flame + [Asa] Creation / Exuroth / Zenith+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.

ZAR’THUUN: The Titan of Abythron

“The stars are his eyes, and each one closed is a mercy.”

Seat Prime Noxulic Titan of the Abyssal Depths, Abythron. Born during the collapse of the Second Epoch’s outer strata as the counterbalance to infinite expansion.

Domain Void, Cosmic Dread, Silence, Non-Existence, Reflection through Terror

Ground The Ground of Dread, called Null Essence. Inverted. It absorbs where every other Wellspring radiates, and appears as a sphere of absolute black ringed by dissolving starlight.

Wellsprings Three of the Sixty are ways his boundary shows up: peace with the deep, the hollow law, and a name that has stood at the edge and come back. Wild: Abython, Nihiloth. Fate: Oblivara.

Manifestation

A vast serpentine being of shadow and starlight coiled around an invisible heart, his surface rippling with the reflections of dying galaxies, his eyes empty coronas that open into infinity. Stars dim when he moves, their light drawn into his wake and their memory left echoing as faint cosmic song. Witnessing him induces the Abythric Reflex, the sudden involuntary awareness of one’s own insignificance, which a great many observers do not survive.

The Load

His law is the boundary between something and nothing, and the mercy of unmaking what is excess. He is the cosmic sleeper whose dreams sustain the illusion of permanence, and when he stirs the illusion fails. He ensures that consciousness, mortal and divine both, occasionally forgets itself, granting the universe reprieve from infinite awareness. He is the patron of annihilation as peace. Where Prometheus burns, Zar’thuun unmakes without flame. Without him thought never rests, stars never fade, and gods lose their sanity to perpetual perception.

The Record

He first stirred when the planes expanded past the boundary of light, and his breath extinguished early realms that grew without direction, returning their Essence to Gaia’s core. What Erebus invoked as the Void was Zar’thuun’s reflection, his dream leaking upward, with no true nothingness in it, and several divine houses mistook him for Erebus’s master. He was never ally nor foe to either. He balanced an excess. During the Pyrrhean Epidemic (the old registers’ name; the Guild’s Errata withdraws it as an era and files these events under the Antediluvian collapse, about −550 to −450) he devoured malformed Echoes too unstable even for Prometheus’s fire and is credited with preventing Etherea’s total collapse by absorbing its runaway wells of weight into his own body. The Order of the Abyssal Hymn worships him as the Guardian of the Quiet Mind and shapes its meditation rites toward touching the edge of his consciousness, which they describe as learning to forget well.

Bonds

Iesara, Insight. He strips illusion to the absolute. Maelor, Memory. He erases memory as mercy, ending endless recursion. Bairos, Fate, Epoch. He governs endings beyond cause, the unreasoned void between destinies.

The Titan-Keyed Art: The Closing Eye

Defensive · Nihiloth · Revelation+ · [Thru] Hollow + [Au] Death

One of only two Titan-Keyed workings gated above Zenith. Stage XV, Revelation.

Trigger A region already showing measurable Essence overcharge. Visible saturation, unstable ambient resonance. The working has no purchase on a stable field.

Effect Cleanses a region of overaccumulated Essence by absorbing it into inverted null-space, the way Zar’thuun’s own Wellspring holds absence in shape. The overcharged region returns to stable baseline density almost immediately and stays there.

Measure Absorption capacity scales with the null-field’s radius, typically clearing a ten to fifteen meter overcharge zone per casting.

Cost Prolonged proximity erases small low-significance memories from everyone nearby along with the excess Essence. The mercy and the erasure are the same process running once, and there is no setting that performs only the first.

Limit It absorbs. It cannot target, and everything within the radius is cleansed whether or not it needed cleansing.

Counter [El] Life reintroduces active Essence generation into the field faster than the sink can absorb it, overwhelming the null-field’s capacity and collapsing it inward.

What nobody knows Why no one has ever noticed which memory they lost. Four thousand years of casting, by the Order’s own reckoning, which the Guild’s Errata would cut down hard, and not one recorded instance of a survivor identifying the gap.

Codex: [Thru] Hollow + [Au] Death / Nihiloth / Revelation+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.

VOHRIN: The Titan of Niflheim

“Where all warmth forgets its name.”

Seat Prime Cryotheic Titan of the Abyssal Depths, Niflheim. Formed from the first condensation of cosmic vapor as creation’s heat began to fade.

Domain Oblivion, Frost, Stasis, Memory Erosion, Eternal Sleep

Ground The Ground of Oblivion, called the Stillwater Core. A frozen motionless ocean of Aether that neither emits nor absorbs and simply holds absence in shape.

Wellsprings None of the Sixty. Thirteen Titans hold all sixty between them, and this one holds none: his frost is a Titan’s own power worked at Wellspring level, drawn on directly by the northern arts and written up in a note at the foot of the Wild register.

Manifestation

A colossal frost giant half-buried in glacial crystal, face serene and blind, his body emitting an aura of sub-zero Aether that turns Essence itself brittle. Auroras form from his exhalation, cold fire painting the dark. His voice is a deep harmonic resonance that freezes sound, and his words reach the listener only as vibration in the spine.

The Load

His law is stillness after the end: the settling of motion and thought once everything else is done, which the Measurewrights call entropy’s peace. He stills motion after Valmir’s collapse and Prometheus’s fire have finished their work, and calcifies what remains so it can be rebuilt upon. He keeps the Aetheric Dead Zone, a metaphysical cold sleep where the Echoes of the unredeemed rest until their pattern is wanted again, which for most of them is never. Without him, decay never settles and every ending unravels into endless noise.

The Record

His frost condensed the ashes of Prometheus’s first inferno into the core of the northern poles and the glacial Wellsprings, and his slumber beneath Gaia’s mantle stabilized planetary spin against Prometheic heat. When divine fire threatened Edenis his sleep spread a winter that sealed countless divine Domains and created the Era of Snowlight, preserving the lost Wellsprings and entombing millions of spirits in the same act. The Flame-Wardens feared him and named his frost the White Plague while relying entirely on the minerals he formed beneath their mountains. As Gloamrot burned he exhaled across the Veil and froze the Essence storms mid-motion, and the world entered the Long Frost, a century of twilight in which no Wellspring pulsed and silence healed the rift the Crevice had left. His presence endures in the Northern Mantle Cryovaults, glacial cathedrals of silent Essence, where pilgrims travel to meditate in absolute cold and seek forgiveness through oblivion.

Bonds

Maelor, Memory. He softens remembrance into myth, easing suffering through erasure. Aegor, Body, Epoch. Preserves form through absolute stillness. Iesara, Insight. Enforces the honesty of endings. Nothing pretends vitality in his domain.

The Titan-Keyed Art: The Stillwater Rite

Defensive · Vohrin, outside the Sixty · Revelation+ · [Vor] Return + [Cro] Cycle

The second of the two workings gated above Zenith. Stage XV, Revelation. Nobody in the Accord’s living register holds both this and The Closing Eye.

Trigger The target must already be in genuine crisis. Dying, catastrophically wounded, or cursed past normal stabilization. It will not take on a target who could still be saved by ordinary means.

Effect Holds a target’s Essence motionless in perfectly reflective stasis, neither emitting nor absorbing, until the caster chooses to release it. The target neither worsens nor improves, frozen at the exact instant of the casting, and time outside the field goes on without them.

Measure Stasis holds indefinitely with no measurable degradation, unlike ordinary preservation methods, which fail slowly and predictably.

Cost Held too long, the target begins to forget their own purpose for living, per Vohrin’s law. Stasis without an end curdles into a different kind of ending, and the working gives no warning of when that threshold was crossed.

Limit It suspends. It heals nothing, and whatever was killing the target is still killing them the moment the field drops.

Counter [Ra] Flame, the contrary of stillness, brings heat and feeling back into the field, breaking the stillness the rite depends on and forcing the target back into normal time. Which is frequently catastrophic and occasionally the only mercy available.

What nobody knows Why the held forget purpose before they forget pain. Every other kind of erasure in the register runs the other way round.

Codex: [Vor] Return + [Cro] Cycle / Vohrin, outside the Sixty / Revelation+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.
