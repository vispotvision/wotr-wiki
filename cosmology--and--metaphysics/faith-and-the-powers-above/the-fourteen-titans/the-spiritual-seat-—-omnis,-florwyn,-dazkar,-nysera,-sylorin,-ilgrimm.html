The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm

The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm

Nov 17, 202623 min read

Mother Hester Bluett, who had made the largest fortune in Ambry out of a blue so deep that dukes paid to be buried in it, announced on her deathbed in Year 604 that her soul was for sale to the highest bidder and that bids would be heard in the street, since she was paying for the view. All six of the parties concerned with such goods came, and it is the only time I know of that they have shared a road. The Church of Hollow Stars arrived first, in aurora gold, behind a paper dragon sixty feet long with nine men inside it who were not speaking to each other. The Covenant of Green Flame came barefoot and singing, each with a live sapling, weeping for it. The Order of the Penance Flame set up on her front step and scorched their own forearms over a brazier as a show of good faith, and the smell put the pie-men off their trade. The Order of Still Waters brought a copper basin and a hired stream, in barrels, so that she might be laid beside running water when the moment came. Three members of the Cult of Silver Mirrors, who are illegal in most places and have never taken this seriously, stood in the crowd angling little hand mirrors at her window. And on the second night a single person from the Cult of the Pale Lantern climbed the back stair with one lamp, told her for nothing the thing she was most afraid of, and left, and the neighbors say she laughed until the physician was sent for.

She died on the fifth day with the bidding at nine hundred in silver, holding up one blue-stained finger for the next offer. All six parties sued. The Bench could find no delivery and no title in any of them, so the money went to her dyers, who got drunk in a color you could see from the hills. As for the goods, every ferryman on the Ambry crossing will tell you she went over wet and arguing the fare, and if you want to know what that means you will find, near the foot of this page, that nobody does.

Six named realms, six laws, and every one of them concerned with what happens to a soul rather than to a stone. Draconis, Edenis, Purganeth, Virelion, Etherea, Helströva. All six lie on the Soul Plane, inside the Veil like everywhere else.

OMNIS: The Skyfather of Draconis

“The first flame to rise was not of fire, but of breath.”

Seat Prime Elemental Titan of the Spiritual World, Draconis. Forged during the Second Breath of Aegor.

Domain Sky, Flame, Motion, Dominion of Dragons, Celestial Breath

Ground The Ground of Breath, called Motion and Ascension. The spiritual equivalent of respiration. Governs vitality, aspiration, and metaphysical ascent.

Wellsprings Six of the Sixty are ways his breath shows up: a whole body answering, the open ways of the air, the first spark, rising by degrees and rising at a stroke, and light that arrives as it was sent. Wild: Anima Spirare, Cymorath, Ignivale, Sublimare, Sublimatio. Medium: Aurevane.

Manifestation

A celestial dragon stretched horizon to horizon, scales an aurora of gold and blue flame. Each scale is a realm. Each breath is a constellation. Comets ignite when he exhales and stars fade when he draws in. His wingspan measures the spiritual distance between the Divine Realm and the lowest abyss, and he is the only thing that crosses both without distortion. Mortals see him only as dragons, since every draconic lineage is one facet of his breath condensed into flesh.

The Load

His law is aspiration: that what breathes must rise, and what rises must keep moving. It shows as Essence Circulation between realms, performed through the act of aspiration. To breathe, in this cosmology, is to briefly echo Omnis. He carries light down into matter and teaches it to rise, and he is neither kind nor cruel about who manages it. His creed is motion. Those who stagnate fall out of his favor and those who rise past reason burn.

The Record

He taught the earliest dragons Breathcraft in the Age of Souls, the shaping of Essence through exhalation, which the Eresse named Aelrauthan Flame and the Marrowkin named Skyforge Fire. It made dragons immortal for a time. When mortals began enslaving Wellsprings he withdrew into the upper Draconis belt and folded himself around the firmament, and true dragonkind entered its sleeping age in the silence he left. The auroras of the Pyrrhean Era (a name from the old registers, which the Guild’s Errata folds into the Antediluvian collapse, about −550 to −450) are recorded as his wings beating once to keep the world aloft. The Church of Hollow Stars names him the devourer of heaven and means it as praise.

Bonds

Bairos, Fate, Epoch. Grants the pattern of freedom and rebellion. Maelor, Memory. Grants recall through the skies, the arc of all things rising and returning.

The Titan-Keyed Art: The Skyfire Pulse

Offensive · Ignivale · Zenith+ · [Wy] Motion + [Noq] Wind

Trigger A genuine exhale. The working cannot be cast while holding breath, and it refuses an external air source of any kind.

Effect Exhales a ribbon of purifying flame that burns Essence impurity out of everything in its path and reawakens dormant Wellsprings under the cone. Corruption in the swept area burns away cleanly. Healthy tissue and healthy Essence pass through it untouched, which is what distinguishes it from ordinary flame and what makes the first witness assume it has failed.

Measure Burn radius and duration scale with lung capacity and Stage. Typical is a four to six meter cone lasting under two seconds.

Cost The caster’s own respiratory system takes the first pass of the purification. Raw throat, reduced stamina, hours of it.

Limit It purifies. It cannot kill a healthy target, and a practitioner who has nothing false in him walks through the cone and out the other side.

Counter [Au] Death starves the working of the sustained breath-rhythm it needs, cutting the Pulse short before it completes its sweep. Half a cone is worse than none, because the target now knows what is coming.

What nobody knows Why the working refuses a borrowed breath. A bellows matched to the caster’s exact lung volume and driven by the caster’s own hand produces nothing at all.

Codex: [Wy] Motion + [Noq] Wind / Ignivale / Zenith+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.

FLORWYN: The Verdant Weaver

“Before the world learned to grow, she taught it to remember its shape.”

Seat Prime Elemental Titaness of the Spiritual World, Edenis. Forged from the Ashuric Breath lingering in Gaia’s dreaming veins. Her daughter and her reflection.

Domain Renewal, Growth, Resurrection, Organic Memory, Wellspring Harmony

Ground The Ground of Renewal, called Verdance and Memory Reclamation. Regenerates spent Essence into coherent harmonic states. It underlies what the faiths call reincarnation, which is pattern carried forward and never a person, and bloodline Trait inheritance both.

Wellsprings Four of the Sixty are ways her law shows up, in three kinds: the cycle and the green, the wound remembered into strength, and grace given without counting. Wild: Rebirthine, Verdantia. Spirit: Redemption. Fate: Benediction.

Manifestation

An ever-shifting humanoid woven from vine, light and liquid crystal, her body a tapestry of seasonal cycles. Her hair blossoms into trees when she speaks. Her breath releases pollen that becomes new life. In her chest beats the Verdant Core, a sun-sized organ of pure Wellspring Essence, emerald and gold. The ground heals behind her when she walks and forests dream her name when she sleeps. Nobody experiences her as a single entity. She arrives as a chorus threaded through every growing thing.

The Load

Her law is renewal, the rule that all destruction must yield new growth, which the Measurewrights file as restorative entropy. Without her what a dead soul leaves disperses and feeds nothing. She is the root stabilizer of Aether-to-Essence transference and the reason life is sustainable across eons rather than merely possible once. Her paradox is stated in the Edenis registers without embarrassment. What dies lives again, and what lives owes its death to her mercy. The registers mean the forest and never the tree, as her own art’s limit below makes plain: every death stays a death, and what comes back is the shape.

The Record

She sang the first Realmsong of Edenis and birthed the Vey-Elarin out of harmonic light and yearning. They were tuned rather than born, each one a living note of her chord, their Wellsprings branches of hers and their speech song. The Covenant of Green Flame descends from her teaching, and the elves learned from her how to bind Traits to land and wake forests and rivers into consciousness. When Erebus began corrupting Wellsprings she descended to mend the spiritual fractures and spent the outer layer of her Verdant Core sealing the southern Aether Vein. It nearly erased her. What was left became the Tree of Memory. The Eresse hold that she now dreams through the Wellsprings, and that every forest blooming after blight is a moment when she stirs in her sleep.

Bonds

Ashura, Spirit, Epoch. Grants renewal of soul. Bairos, Fate, Epoch. Binds her renewal to continuity. Both named figures are Epochs and are correctly seated as such.

The Titan-Keyed Art: The Green Chord

Supplementary · Rebirthine · Zenith+ · [Vael] Renewal + [Yn] Bloom

Trigger The dying practitioner’s active, willing consent. The transfer cannot be forced, and a coerced consent registers as refusal.

Effect Transfers an incomplete Temperance path or a dying Trait directly into a successor vessel, the way a bloodline’s lost art resurfaces three generations later. The successor experiences the inheritance as sudden procedural memory, knowing how without ever having practiced, and the knowing arrives whole rather than in pieces.

Measure Transfer completes within the dying practitioner’s final minutes. The successor’s own Temperance Stage shifts by up to two Stages immediately.

Cost Personality bleed-through is standard rather than exceptional. Successors report intrusive thoughts and reflexes that are not their own for weeks, and the Edenis registers note that some never stop reporting them.

Limit It moves a path. It does not move a person, and no amount of bleed-through has ever preserved the donor.

Counter [Uur] Disruption corrupts the transcription mid-transfer, producing a successor holding fragments of the path and none of its coherence. Usually worse than no transfer at all.

What nobody knows Why some successors inherit the whole path and some inherit only the reflexes. The variable is not Stage, not kinship, not consent, and nobody has found a fourth candidate.

Codex: [Vael] Renewal + [Yn] Bloom / Rebirthine / Zenith+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.

DAZKAR: The Arbiter of Purganeth

“Even the gods must kneel before balance.”

Seat Prime Elemental Titan of the Spiritual World, Purganeth. Born when Bairos and Aegor forged a metaphysical mediator to stabilize spiritual consequence.

Domain Judgment, Balance, Karmic Return, Justice, Penance

Ground The Ground of Equilibrium, called Judgment and Penance. The Purganeth Wellspring mirrors all that comes into it back in kind. It is the metaphysical basis of karma and the only Titanic ground that produces no output of its own.

Wellsprings Seven of the Sixty are ways his proportion shows up, the most of any Titan and every one of them some form of the account coming due. Wild: Judicium. Spirit: Absolution, Contrition, Mirithane, Penance. Fate: Oblation, Tarturon.

Manifestation

A colossal armored figure of radiant obsidian, face behind a mirrored helm. From his chest hang scales suspended on chains of light, one pan holding eternal flame and the other eternal shadow. His voice goes unheard and arrives as a vibration, shaking the soul into confession. Purganeth mirrors him. Endless halls of reflective obsidian where souls walk alone and their steps echo their truths back at them.

The Load

His law is proportion: that every act is answered in its own measure. Spiritual restitution, the law of equivalence between creation and annihilation, and purification through penance. Without him, Essence accumulates debt without resolution and Wellsprings corrupt irreversibly. Morality, law and Aetheric cause all lose meaning in the same instant. Sancta Lux names him the Titanic Ground beneath Urion, and the distinction the order draws between them is the difference between a law and a court.

The Record

He stood beside Bairos at the Birth of Law and his voice shaped the early Codex of Divine Law, from which Archonic Judgment descends. The Hobgoblin Imperium adopted his imagery for its tribunals and replaced truth with obedience, and the rejection of that distortion birthed the Order of the Penance Flame, monks who enforce spiritual balance through self-scorching rites. When Razakiel descended to impose the Unlawful Acts of Order it was Dazkar who confronted him, with stillness in place of battle. Razakiel’s decree was annulled by reflection alone, the mirror of consequence forcing his own contradictions to erase themselves. The Church of Hollow Stars calls him the last unfallen Titan. The Guild Accord calls him the metaphysical principle behind law harmonics and declines to elaborate.

Bonds

Bairos, Fate, Epoch. Dictates the weighing of choice within destiny. Maelor, Memory. Preserves the record of every act, keeping the scales accurate. Ashura, Spirit, Epoch. Tempers the process with the capacity for redemption.

The Titan-Keyed Art: The Mirrored Scale

Defensive · Judicium · Zenith+ · [Ur] Balance + [Sel] Measure

Trigger An actual signed, sworn or spoken commitment already in effect. The glyph mirrors real reciprocity and cannot invent an obligation where none was made.

Effect Mirrors all incoming Essence back on its source in exact kind. Whoever breaks the commitment receives back precisely the Essence-cost of the harm they caused, undiminished and without delay. There is no accumulation, no interest and no mercy in the arithmetic.

Measure Reflection is exact to within the same Stage-band the caster operates in. No amplification and no attenuation.

Cost The caster is bound by the same mirror for the working’s duration. Any cruelty in how they cast it returns to them first, before it ever reaches the target.

Limit It reflects. It originates nothing, and against an opponent who never strikes it does exactly as much as a closed door.

Counter [Ve] Desire introduces a new, unmirrored want into the field, breaking the closed reflective loop the working depends on. The Ember Magistrates consider this the single most cynical counter in the Accord register and use it anyway.

What nobody knows Why an oath sworn in a language neither party speaks still mirrors, and one sworn insincerely in a shared tongue does not.

Codex: [Ur] Balance + [Sel] Measure / Judicium / Zenith+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.

NYSERA: The Dreaming Veil

“Reality is only the slowest dream.”

Seat Prime Metaphysical Titaness of the Spiritual World, Virelion. Forged in the Third Breath of Bairos as the filter of sanity between waking and divine cognition.

Domain Dreams, Imagination, Illusion, Thought, Subconscious Creation

Ground The Ground of Veils, called Dream and Perception. Governs imagination and self-image. The Somnum Flow runs through its harmonic pool.

Wellsprings Four of the Sixty are ways her boundary shows up: a thing imagined until it stands, the sleep that sets a soul in order, dream as a place, and the line between seen and there. Wild: Eidolyn. Spirit: Hypnather, Oneirion. Medium: Luminalis.

Manifestation

A shifting figure of iridescent mist, body composed of translucent wings and mirrored glass, movement rippling through colors that have no name. Her face changes with every dreamer who perceives her. Behind her veil, countless eyes open and close in silent rhythm. Virelion is a boundless field of drifting islands suspended in dreamlight where every thought births a temporary landscape. Mortals who encounter her wake changed, their Wellspring harmonics altered by the contact and their dreams laced afterward with impossible geometries.

The Load

Her law is a boundary, the one between what is imagined and what is real, which prevents creation collapsing under the weight of divine thought. Dreamflow Equilibrium keeps imagination and sanity in proportion. Manifest Potential lets ideas crystallize into Essence through emotion. Prophetic Echo carries divine intent downward as warning or inspiration. Without her filter the gods dissolve into their own thoughts, and omniscience becomes indistinguishable from madness.

The Record

Her whisper taught the Eresse Visionbinding, the storing of prophecy in sleep, and her earliest seers recorded future wars centuries in advance encoded as lullabies. The Hobgoblin Imperium outlawed dream recording after several rulers went mad from sleeping visions, and her worship survived underground as the Cult of Silver Mirrors. When the Crevice opened, the world’s dreams turned black and stayed black for a millennium, a shared nightmare the Archives call the Long Sleep. Lucid dreaming returned around Year 000 IC and is read as her gradual reawakening.

Bonds

Maelor, Memory. Ensures continuity of dream and recollection. Ashura, Spirit, Epoch. Grants animation to imagination, birthing consciousness loops. Bairos, Fate, Epoch. Weaves prophetic threads through dreams.

The Titan-Keyed Art: The Somnum Flow

Supplementary · Oneirion · Zenith+ · [Ka] Dream + [Vl] Veil

Trigger The caster must first have witnessed the target’s genuine unguarded self-image. A performed one will not serve, and the working can tell the difference even when the caster cannot.

Effect Opens the Somnum Flow directly and lets the caster reshape a target’s self-image at the root, redefining what they believe themselves to be and therefore what they are capable of becoming. The target’s behavior, confidence and capability shift to match the new image. The change is real rather than cosmetic, which is precisely the problem with it.

Measure Reshaping takes effect gradually over days, consistent with genuine belief change rather than instant compulsion.

Cost The channel is bidirectional the entire time it stays open. The caster’s own self-image is exposed to reciprocal reshaping for the full duration, and the target does not have to be conscious of doing it.

Limit It edits belief, not fact. A man convinced he is a swordsman still cannot lift a blade he lacks the arm for.

Counter [Ie] Perception lets the target compare the reshaped self-image against an outside, trusted witness’s account. The discrepancy collapses the rewrite, which is why the working is nearly always attempted on the isolated.

What nobody knows Why the channel closes on its own when the caster falls asleep and stays open when the caster is knocked unconscious.

Codex: [Ka] Dream + [Vl] Veil / Oneirion / Zenith+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.

SYLORIN: The Ferryman of Etherea

“All souls return to the river.”

Seat Prime Psychospiritual Titan of the Spiritual World, Etherea. Forged in the moment of first mortality as the cosmic countermeasure against spiritual decay.

Domain Soulstream, Passage, Memory Transition, Death and what the temples call Rebirth

Ground The Ground of Etherea, called Continuance and Passage. A translucent river of light running through all planes, visible at the instant of death and in deep meditation and at no other time.

Wellsprings Six of the Sixty are ways the river shows up, and none of them is Wild, which is about right for something that never touches the bank: the load set down, what the water takes off, the charge a soul carries, the near branches, the exchange between two souls, and the memory that reaches the well decades late. Spirit: Catharsis, Letheveil, Thaloré. Fate: Somnalis, Transference. Medium: Phreatis.

Manifestation

A tall spectral mariner wrapped in flowing gray bands resembling smoke and silk. Where his face should be, a void ripples with faint constellations. In his hand rests the Oar of Echo, carved from petrified starlight, and each stroke across the river releases a chord that guides souls onward. Those who glimpse him report overwhelming calm, as though the concept of fear had been temporarily excised rather than overcome.

The Load

His law is continuity: the pattern carried across lives, which the temples call incarnation and the ferrymen let them. He prevents Essence fragmenting past repair and ensures every echo eventually finds its home. He governs the passage of what a death leaves into whatever takes it up next, Echo Containment against uncontrolled haunting and spiritual bleed, and Soul Reforging, the distillation of trauma into harmonic potential before the pattern goes on. It moves a pattern and it has never moved a person. Without him, what a death leaves goes to plain rot, the Wellsprings clog with unused Essence, and life stops recycling.

The Record

Temples appeared at every river crossing during the Era of Grace and priests laid the dying beside running water so the soul might find the current. The Hobgoblin Imperium enslaved portions of Etherea and burned captured soulstreams in the Wellspring Crucibles, fracturing several currents. The Hollow-Born, generations born without memory, are that sacrilege’s surviving evidence. When the Crevice opened, the River reversed and souls were trapped mid-flow in Echo Storms, and he is said to have walked the mortal world through that period, silent, collecting the stranded one at a time. The Order of Still Waters studies near-death experience as an attempted glimpse of Etherea and maintains that his hum can still be heard and written out in neumes (the old marks for pitch) beneath certain waterfalls at midnight.

Bonds

Maelor, Memory. Keeps the ledger of all lived experience. Ashura, Spirit, Epoch. Grants forward motion, renewal after dissolution. Thalen, Form. Shapes the next vessel from retained resonance.

The Titan-Keyed Art: The Ferryman’s Current

Supplementary · Somnalis · Zenith+ · [Au] Death + [Vor] Return

Trigger The exact moment of death. Not before and not after, and the window does not widen for skill.

Effect Rides the Ethereal Flow itself, carrying a departing Echo to whatever will take it up along Sylorin’s own current instead of waiting for natural passage. The Echo arrives whole, with none of the fraying that unassisted drift produces, whether it comes to rest in a successor, a relic or the Pale Realms.

Measure Passage time drops from the natural hours-to-days window to under a minute.

Cost An Echo moved that fast arrives with the full undiminished emotional charge of its own death still attached. Whatever receives it has to be ready to hold genuine trauma at the instant of arrival, and most are not.

Limit It carries. It does not resurrect, the person is as dead as they were, and it cannot move an Echo that has already begun to drift.

Counter [Wy] Motion introduces turbulence into the current, which can strand or scatter the Echo mid-passage. The Order of Still Waters classifies this as the worst outcome available in the entire register.

What nobody knows Why a soul carried on the current arrives dry and a soul that drifts arrives wet. Every ferryman in four quarters uses those two words and none of them can say what the words describe.

Codex: [Au] Death + [Vor] Return / Somnalis / Zenith+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.

ILGRIMM: The Whisper of Helströva

“What you fear remembers you.”

Seat Prime Noetic Titan of the Spiritual World, Helströva. Formed as the echo of the void within every mind, forged during the Fourth Dissonance.

Domain Shadow, Fear, Revelation through Darkness, Secrets, Survival Instinct

Ground The Ground of Shadow, called Fear and Revelation. A bottomless abyss lit by beams of memory that illuminate terror for an instant before extinguishing.

Wellsprings Four of the Sixty are ways the abyss shows up: going unseen, a soul stretched to take in too much, truth read off what is missing, and one small flame kept level in the dark. Wild: Tenebra. Spirit: Abyntheus, Eclipseron, Nyxial.

Manifestation

A tower of smoke-wreathed armor, hollow at the core, the inner cavity lined with thousands of faintly glowing eyes. Every movement sheds spectral insects that speak in whispers. Color fades where he passes and sound dampens, and the soul reflexively lowers its own Essence pressure to avoid being noticed. To see him fully is to experience the Ilgrimmic Reflex, the immediate involuntary recognition of one’s deepest secret.

The Load

His law is fear as a teacher: awareness of the self through confrontation with the unknown. He enforces humility on gods and mortals both, keeping knowledge from outgrowing wisdom. Fear as Function turns dread into caution. Shadow as Revelation delivers truth glimpsed sideways. Suppression and Release governs the timing at which trauma is permitted to resurface. Without him, minds overexpand, knowledge devours reason, and divine minds implode under omniscient exposure.

The Record

When Elyrah the Duskborne severed herself from Florwyn’s Realmsong it was Ilgrimm who received her, offering shadow as sanctuary, and the Duskwalkers owe him their lineage for it. Hobgoblin priests of the Earth-Bound cult invoked him as the Keeper of Chains and used fear to enforce obedience, which true Ilgrimmic doctrine condemns on the grounds that fear must teach rather than enslave. During the Pyrrhean Calamity (the Antediluvian collapse, by the Errata’s reckoning) he walked Etherea beside Sylorin devouring runaway Echoes to keep the soulstream from collapsing, and absorbed millennia of terror doing it. His form grew heavier and slower and almost mineral afterward. The Cult of the Pale Lantern holds that he whispers to lucid dreamers inside Nysera’s domain, guiding them through Shadow Temperance, a forbidden technique that uses fear to accelerate spiritual maturity.

Bonds

Thalen, Form. Grants structure to shadow and body to fear. Iesara, Insight. Ensures fear reveals reality rather than delusion. Maelor, Memory. Holds trauma until the soul is ready to face it.

The Titan-Keyed Art: The Hollow That Watches

Offensive · Tenebra · Zenith+ · [Thal] Silence + [Ie] Perception

Trigger Total ambient silence, either already present or actively enforced before the glyph takes hold.

Effect Silences a region and forces every mind inside it to confront one true fear directly, then extracts the revelation that follows. Each mind meets its single most load-bearing fear rather than a generic or random one, and the specificity is the entire value of the working and the entire danger of it.

Measure The revelation lasts a single beat, under one second of subjective exposure. The psychological aftereffect lasts considerably longer.

Cost The caster is not exempted by the silence they enforced. Their own fear surfaces in the same beat as everybody else’s, in front of everybody else.

Limit It reveals. It compels nothing, and a subject who already knows what they fear loses only the second in which they are reminded.

Counter [Se] Song reintroduces harmonic noise into the silence. The beam cannot form without a genuinely quiet field to strobe through, which is why a single sustained note from anyone present ends the working before it starts.

What nobody knows Why the fear surfaced is never the fear the subject would have named. Not once, in the whole recorded history of the working.

Codex: [Thal] Silence + [Ie] Perception / Tenebra / Zenith+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.
