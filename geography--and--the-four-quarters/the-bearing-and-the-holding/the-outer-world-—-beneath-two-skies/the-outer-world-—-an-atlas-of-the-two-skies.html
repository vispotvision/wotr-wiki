The Outer World — An Atlas of the Two Skies

The Outer World — An Atlas of the Two Skies

Nov 17, 20265 min read

Say itthe OW-ter WURLD

The Land Beneath Two Skies, laid out band by band · Vampire North, Dragon South, the March between

In one breath: a continent that runs long from north to south and warms the whole way down, split evenly between a young empire of night in the cold north and an old empire of fire in the hot south, with a frontier band of mortal kingdoms across its middle where the veils give out and the sun comes back.

How to use this sheet. It stands in Year 715. The bands and the named corners (Northeast, Southwest, Southeast) are fixed by The Geography of the Outer World, which governs. Where this atlas puts a place west, center or east inside its band, it is this sheet’s reading, and the governing page wins if the two ever disagree.

The Sheet

Read it top to bottom as north to south. Every band south of the line gets hotter, brighter and poorer in Aether; every band north of it gets colder, darker and richer.

Who Holds What

The two empires hold the continent in even halves, each half directly or through its tributaries.

  • The north is Dreadvalis. Everything from the Frostfang down to the Red Marches lies under the Blood Throne in Bluvest, held directly in the Veiled Provinces and through Grimhold Deeps, Vel Sarith and the tithe kingdoms beyond. A northern border follows the veil: a village is Dreadvalis land if a veil hangs over it or a Tithe Hall stands in it.
  • The south is Drakyssia. Everything from Akhazar Gate down to Zyraxia lies under the Dragon Sovereign at Kra’Vorrak, held through the Ten Great Houses and their tributaries. A southern border is layered: a mortal king on the ground, a Wyrm below, a House on the mountains, a Wyvern wing above, the Sovereign over all.
  • The March is both. Across the middle runs the Twilight March, where both systems run at once and the Dusk Line moves south in winter and north in summer.

About the Painted Atlas

The painted World Atlas on the Four Quarters page shows the Outer World in its older arrangement, from before the even split was written: Drakyssia, the Drakhal Spine and Kra’Vorrak spread down the west side of the pan, the Frostfang Cordillera and the Last Archipelago drawn at its southern tip, and no Dreadvalis, Bluvest or Twilight March at all. This sheet is the corrected layout until the painting is redone. The other three quarters on the painted sheet are unaffected.

For a repaint. One brief, kept with the sheet so whoever paints it next has it:

  • Keep the Outer World as the southern pan of the whole sheet, in the house atlas style.
  • Inside it, put the cold at the top and the heat at the bottom: the Frostfang and the Archipelago at its northern edge nearest the Central Expanse, the Ash Wastes and Zyraxia at its southern tip.
  • Shade the northern half under a blue-violet veil, with the Moonlight Forest silver-white; leave the southern half in open gold and ash-red, with the volcanic Spine down its west side.
  • Draw the Twilight March as a broad green band across the middle, with the Dusk Line as a dotted line that does not quite meet either coast.
  • Label, at minimum: Bluvest, Morvayne, Grimhold Deeps, Vel Sarith, the Great Grass Sea, the Five Rivers, Akhazar Gate, the Golden Escarpment, Kra’Vorrak, the Veloran Rainsea, the Eastern Sun Coast and Zyraxia.
