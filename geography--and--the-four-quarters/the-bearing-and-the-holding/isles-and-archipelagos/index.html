Isles and Archipelagos

Nov 28, 20261 min read

The island lands off the shores of the one great land, lying between the quarters: the Eighty Isles and the volcanic Expanse. One of them buried four delegates in a kelp debate, and the other sent a Guild magister home with a fine mat and no answer. Neither has ever had a king, and both will tell you so before you have finished tying up your boat.
