Equilibrium Draught

Liquid Tonic · T2 · Ur, Me

WellspringJudicium (Wild)
OperationSeparation

Ingredients. Distilled Wellspring water, scale-seed, balanced salt-pair

Method. Brew on twin flames under Eq Equivalence; bottle in mirrored glass.

Effect. Temporarily steadies emotions and Aether output, resisting panic and surges.

Risk. Long-term reliance flattens affect; harder to reach peak emotional Resonance.

How it works.

  • The idea. Truth told apart. Discernment that knows what a thing is made of, and whether it is lying, from the mark it cannot help leaving.
  • The operation. Separation. A thing is parted from what it claims to be, the true told apart from the false by the mark it cannot help leaving.
  • In the maker. Ur, Me fix what it may do, and the bench work keeps the separation going until the maker’s hold on the idea sets in the matter, so it does: temporarily steadies emotions and Aether output, resisting panic and surges.
  • Price. The maker’s reserve at the bench; the Risk line is the taker’s price.
  • Flaw. Discernment tells everything apart and cannot choose to leave anything untold. Off guard, every object in view reports what it is made of at once, and the reader loses the power to put any of it first. Pushed past the point where a reading only reads, telling a thing apart becomes taking it apart: the read starts to pass judgment, which can turn away force or at the very top end a life, and since it has no way to look at one thing only, it takes the reader’s own memory in the same stroke.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Emission and absorption spectroscopy.
