The Material Index · T0–T4

The Material Index · T0–T4

Nov 17, 202615 min read

T0 through T4 · the ground, the bulk supply, and the first instrument grades.

The longest suit ever heard in the Dorrosfenn basins was fought over a barge. The wheat-growers of the Upper Fenn had spread Marrowchalk on their fields for sixty years, at a price they considered their birthright, until the sect of the Chalk Hall set up on the far bank in robes the green of old copper and began outbidding them by the load for the same rock, for purposes of their own. The growers sued. Mother Yevane of the Chalk Hall came to the first hearing with her witnesses walking two by two behind her: thirty-seven dead wheat-growers in their good coats, who had between them spread more Marrowchalk than any man living, and who stood in the well of the court with their hats in their hands while she asked the magistrate which of them had a complaint. The gallery started bringing lunch. Four of the widows started bringing cases of their own. It ran twenty-three years and ended on the ruling that a barge has two sides, which is how the chalk has been loaded ever since: grain on the left, grave on the right, same bench, same rock, same price, and a barge-master who has bought three vineyards.

I start this half of the index with a barge because this is the half people go to law over. Nobody has ever sued for a T9 material. Everything below is what a person actually touches between waking and supper, the wall and the roof lead and the stew salt and the iron on the man who came to collect the rent, set out in the Accord’s tier order (T0 for what fails at ordinary handling, up through T4, the first instrument grade) with the quarter it comes from and the way it lets you down. Bearing is what a material does under load. Holding is what it does under Essence, the refined power a practitioner spends. Resonance, Kind and Domain name the Wellspring it sympathizes with, its kind, and the branch of natural philosophy the Measurewrights file it under, which tells you how much and never why.

T2 · Common Durable

The material the world is actually built out of.

MaterialSourceResonance / Kind / DomainWhat it is
Greystone Bearing · Stone · NorthQuarrylands near Kaetra—Standard fortification stone of four kingdoms. Minimally resistive to magic, entirely adequate against everything else. Why the north builds gray where the west builds pale
Shieldgneiss Bearing · Stone · NorthBasement outcrop across the whole quarterPetralon (weakly) · Wild · MechanicsBearing exceptional, Holding negligible. Inert to nine Wellsprings in ten. An array laid on the northern shield stays exactly where it was put for a century, which is why the Accord sites its great arrays here
Stillband Ore Bearing · Metal Ore · NorthBedded across the western shield—Banded iron, magnetite alternating with gray chert in finger-width layers. The backbone of every bridge in the quarter. The chert bands scatter Essence, so inscriptions bleed off over a season
Chainsalt Holding · Mineral · NorthThe salt country of Nalūn, bedded haliteFixatio · Medium · Material ScienceThe most important dull material in the four quarters. Holding deliberately nil, an Essence dielectric. Gaskets, isolation collars, separator plates in every serious array; food preservation; currency bars. A salt bar is worth a silver mark and can be eaten. Fails in standing water
Forge-Iron Bearing · Metal · NorthHuman Kingdom of Altherion—Bears magnificently and holds almost nothing, which is exactly why it arms the line infantry of six kingdoms and appears in no ward-work anywhere. Specification unchanged in three hundred years
Slagbloom Bearing · Metal · WestAncient spoil, western foreland and tin districtsAnamnesis (unwelcome) · Medium · Abstract/EntropicInscriptions laid on it read doubled, because the metal was worked and marked once already and something of the earlier marking survives the second melt. The same behavior a Crystal shows at a Threshold, in a substrate. A melt cools into order around whatever survived the heat, and in reworked spoil that is the prior marking, which the new inscription then has to grow around rather than replace. Engravers charge extra for a rough seat in metal for the same reason the halls cap a rough-seated practitioner at Tier Four. Superstitious foremen will not have it underground
Palegrit Limestone Bearing · Stone · WestPlatform carbonate across the central west—Ashlar, lime for mortar, and flux for every iron furnace in the quarter. Three uses, one quarry. The same rock gives the west its sinkholes and its habit of losing rivers underground
Kirkyard Lead Bearing · Metal · WestGalena in the limestone belt, worked with the silver—Roofing, coffin lining, pipe and sling bullets, and Essence shielding for every assay house and isolation room in the west. Attenuates Essence more effectively per unit thickness than anything else in common supply. The men who smelt it do not last
Greaves Tin Holding · Metal · WestCassiterite, lode and stream-workCoagulatio · Wild · Material ScienceBronze, solder, and the tinning of array contacts. A tinned contact does not corrode, and contact corrosion in a working array is a slow, invisible, cumulative failure. Unglamorous, essential, and running out
Attercrop Vitriol Holding · Liquid Reagent · WestDrainage of abandoned workingsDissolution · Wild · Abstract/EntropicThe same fluid that killed the Aerne valley writes the Accord’s own codices. Mordant fixing ritual ink to organic substrate. Blinds on contact with the eye and does so without warning, since it does not burn the skin at working dilution
Veinwood Plank Holding · Wood · WestMountain trees of Varun—Semi-hardened grain, briefly enchantable. Standard stock for ritual shields across three kingdoms
Alchemelt Brick Bearing · Synthetic Stone · EastArtisan guilds of the arcMaterial ScienceVolcanic ash ground with burned lime and water. Sets hard, sets fast, and sets underwater, which is why the east builds deep-water harbors while western harbors silt shut. Will not span
Redcrust Laterite Bearing · Mineral · EastWeathered flanks, wet eastern seaboard—The red caprock. Useless as stone, useless as soil, and the feedstock for Cloudmetal. Everyone knew the metal was in it; only Caedor was willing to commit the array capacity
Roughgold Holding · Metal · EastPlacer bars, black-sand riversBenediction, SublimatioWon by hand, by anyone, with no permission from anyone, which produced the eastern quarter’s characteristic political condition and the Great Inflation of the Voyager Era. Ecclesiastical and oath work; bullion
Ember Flint Holding · Stone · EastForaye—Survival fire-starting. The single most widely distributed Essence-active object in the eastern quarter by simple count
Trapstone Bearing · Stone · SouthFlood-basalt terraces, western interiorCoagula, Fixatio · Wild · Material ScienceColumnar basalt splitting naturally into six-sided posts that need almost no dressing. The Braqthian standing-stone tradition exploits its resonance; the west has never bothered to study it
Greenskin Serpentine Bearing · Stone · SouthOphiolite belt, eastern coastal ranges—Carving stock. In certain pods the stone is fibrous, and the fibers separate into a dust so fine it does not settle. Twenty-year onset lung scarring, invariably fatal. Enforcement has posted no writ and the quarries have raised no objection to the absence
Marrowchalk Holding · Phosphorite · SouthBone-beds, Dorrosfenn and EppreneaMortalis, Rebirthine · Medium · BiochemistryDoubles a wheat yield as fertilizer and is the primary reagent of every necromantic practice in the quarter. The same bench, the same rock, the same barge. Fertilizer and necromancy cannot be distinguished at the point of sale because they cannot be distinguished at the point of extraction
Coilhide Leather Bearing · Beastkin Hide · SouthIroncoils, native to Braqth—Training armor and duel-school gloves. Common enough at home to be worthless and expensive enough abroad to be worth shipping
