The Material Index · T5–T9

The Material Index · T5–T9

Nov 28, 202611 min read

T5 through T9 · what requires a forge-array, a rite, or a corpse, and what lies past that.

Margrave Corvane spent a third of a year’s revenue on the chandelier, because he wanted the Feast of the Long Truce remembered. Four houses who had been burning each other’s orchards for thirty years were to sit down at one table under his roof, in their colors, and over that table he hung a ring of Whiteshard as wide as a mill wheel, which his agent had recommended for its cold luster and its resistance to holy corruption. It hung dark through the soup. Then the Margrave rose with his cup and said that he had always counted these men his brothers, and the chandelier lit the hall like noon on a snowfield.

Lord Aumbrey, who was quick, turned to his neighbor and told him the wine was excellent, and it blazed again, and the whole table understood what it was eating under. For a quarter of an hour it was the finest entertainment anyone present had ever attended, four houses paying each other elaborate compliments and howling with laughter at the glare, and then somebody’s eldest son asked somebody’s uncle a plain question about the orchards at Tern Hollow. Sixty-three people died in that hall, and there was ample light to do it by. The truce had lasted until the fish. The chandelier still hangs there, and the Corvanes have been trying for ninety years to sell it to a buyer who means what he says.

The row for Whiteshard, in the table below, says all of this in one clause, and it said so then. Past T4 these materials have behaviors, they have sources, and several of them have been somebody. The Ledger sets all of that down with perfect candor for anyone who reads to the end of the line before paying.

These tiers carry no Provenance Grade column (the mark of where a stock came from) and several rows are rendered stock. The Bearing and Holding indices grade what a material does. They have never graded what it was.

Grade IV or above by their own source lines: Bloodsilver, from the heartveins of Ascended humans; Drakehide Steel, smelted from slain elder drakes; Veilbone and Sunsinew, whose sources are described and not classed; and Red Ecliptite, which is Archon death.

The Standing Index prices on provenance. This index describes behavior. A buyer reading only this one is being told exactly what the thing does and nothing whatsoever about who it used to be.
