The Five Beastkin Lineages

The Five Beastkin Lineages

Nov 28, 202632 min read

Soul architecture, stat frameworks, metaphysical identity, culture, and historical record for the five documented major lineages.

The Merry Alderman was a fat yellow coasting cog out of Altherion with a cargo of oranges and palm oil, and she went onto the Gannet Rocks in the spring gale of 619 IC with five beastkin in her crew, and the deposition is the best thing in the port archive. The cook, a fox-spirit the crew called Ginger, had come up on deck two hours before the strike to tell the master she could smell old wrecks to leeward, a great many of them, and he had told her to see to the soup. When the hull opened, the Catkin topman Nessa Quickstep came off the mast and was on the first rock before the spar finished falling, and she crossed the black gaps between the rocks with a line in her teeth, appearing a stride ahead of where she had just been, without anyone seeing her get there. The four Tovey brothers, Lupine deckhands, went into the surf together with that line and swam it in one stroke, four bodies keeping one time, while burst jars turned the sea around them the color of a sunrise and ten thousand oranges came bobbing up through it on the swell.

Down in the flooding hold the ship’s carpenter, an old Bearkin named Brannoch, had put his back under the cracked keel-beam, and there he stayed, with the water at his chest and the whole hull working on his spine, for the six hours it took thirty-four people to climb out over him. Somebody had to go down at dawn and tell him he could stop. Out on the rock the purser, a Scalebond notary named Zsevrin, sat with the rain running off his scales and observed that the Bishop’s Daughter had struck here on the same tide two hundred and twelve years earlier, and that the people who went left along the ledge had lived and the people who went right had not, and he said this once, clearly, to anyone who wished to consider it. The master went right, with the strongbox. Young Wil Tovey, torn off the line in the dark and thrown up alone on the far stack, was found at sundown sitting quite still with nothing wrong with him, and told his brothers he had been too quiet for too long, and they carried him home between them.

Then came the salvage court at Altherion, which divided the crew’s share as the sea-law required. Brannoch and Zsevrin were paid. Under the Labor Provision the cook, the topman and the four brothers could show no free-holder papers, and the clerk therefore entered them on the roll with the oranges, as recovered cargo, value to the owner. Zsevrin sealed the record himself, and it is his hand that notes in the margin that he found the ruling clarifying.

Five people, one bad night, and five entirely different souls doing the only thing each of them was built to do. Here are the builds.

On terms. The stat columns are the Guild’s measure of what a lineage adds to or takes from a practitioner: Vitality for wounds, Ardency for output and the punch through worked armor, Gnosis for knowledge and the read, Dexterity for measure and reaction, Harmonics for attunement and the pull of Aether from the ground, Resilience for what a working does to the Crystal, Tempering for control and how long a working can be pushed, Dominion for spatial authority and Pressure. An Untrained lean is the tilt a lineage gives the three layers of a soul before anyone has trained it, and it never sets Mastered or Walked (The Three Paths). Body Mastery sets how much of what a practitioner pulls reaches a working that lands through the Shell. A Wellspring is one of the sixty types of Aether, each working on the Soul Crystal in its own way. A glyph is a unit of Parunic, the script that writes boundaries onto laws. A Trait is a power bound into the soul, and a Domain is the territory in which a high-Stage practitioner’s own law holds. Dissonance is what a soul does when a Stage breaks it instead of raising it.

Feline: Catkin

Shadow-Leap Stalkers · Emotive Kinetics

Descended from the ancient Clawbound Accord formed during the Watcher’s Eclipse. When the celestial lattice first fractured, spirit-leopards and desert nomads fused beneath a period of absolute darkness, trading permanent physical stability for a soul-lattice optimized entirely for reflex and shadow-walking. They are built for the moment between moments.

Spiritual lineage · Nyzara, the Silent Pad. Archonic guardian of foresight and emotional poise, whose heirs inherit her mastery of the blink-space between physical actions and her ability to weaponize stillness before movement.

Soul architecture

In the blood · Their souls function as Kinetic Reservoirs: raw emotion stored in muscle memory, released as Aetheric force through physical acceleration. Where most practitioners channel Aether deliberately, they embody it, and then they move.

Untrained lean · Body 62% / Spirit 38%. Their Aura lays no claim to the space around it. It is the absence of spatial presence, the way a room changes when something very fast has just passed through it.

Body Mastery · unread below Glory; I through IV standard above it, by Stage and Level; V (Radiant) for senior shadow-walkers with sustained Somnalis resonance. Their Body rung tends to read lower than their practical capability because burst, precise, contained output does not saturate ambient Aether the way volume-heavy practitioners do. Assay forks have cost a lot of people a lot of money over this.

Primary fracture point at Stage V Splintering. The emotional reservoir architecture resists the soul cracking open that the Stage requires. Catkin who pass Stage V cleanly tend to pass it violently, and emerge with elevated Domain formation instincts they weren’t expecting.

Stage VII Refraction stall: the Stage requires the soul to perceive itself from outside, which the emotional architecture resists. Catkin at Stage VII frequently mistake the external self-perception for external threat and respond accordingly.

Dissonance expression: the emotional reservoir detonates. A single overwhelming release of accumulated stored emotion that either transforms the practitioner or dissolves them. Survivors describe it as finally feeling something they had been storing for years, all at once.

Metaphysical identity

Wellsprings · Somnalis primary: shadow-walking is a Somnalis expression made physical; the blink-space between moments is Somnalis territory. Tenebra strong secondary. Ignivale accessible, the moment of emotional ignition that converts reservoir to output. Aurevane and Luminalis suppressed, −30% all stats in Radiant Domains: their architecture is built around the space between light sources, and light itself is no friend of it.

Glyphs · Strong: Wy Motion, Fi Passion, El Illusion, Sa Silence. Rejected: Ko Order, Ky Structure. These require the linear Gnosis chains the architecture suppresses, and contact produces feedback rather than working.

Traits · Awakening clusters around kinetic enhancement and shadow expression: movement blurs, after-images that persist slightly too long, the first involuntary shadow-step, appearing two feet from where the practitioner was standing with no visible transit. Ascension moves through speed → shadow-embodiment → absence. At high stages, senior practitioners stop walking through shadow and become temporarily indistinguishable from it.

Overgrowth risk · Reservoir saturation. A Catkin who stores too much Aetheric emotion without releasing it develops overflow: involuntary emotional detonations, technique misfires, Stage V risk elevated to acute.

Domain law. Shadow Domains. Light deepens rather than extinguishes: every shadow in the territory has depth, a quality of there being more room in the dark than the architecture should allow. Entities inside experience elevated reaction-time pressure: their own Dexterity feels slightly slower, as if the Domain is running faster than they are.

Governing principle: the moment before movement as sovereign law. Within the territory, Catkin have first-action authority over any exchange. No technique is involved. The Domain is expressing the practitioner’s nature as the ground’s own law.

Culture and record

The Clawbound Trial · A rite in which the young practitioner must shadow-walk through territory their clan elder has anchored with Tenebra-resonance. Speed will not save them: the test is whether the practitioner can read the shadow-map the elder has written and follow it without detection. Both emerge with a permanent bond marker in their Harmonics architecture. The bond is always mutual; the elder accepts a burden too.

The Emotional Covenant · Before significant action, Catkin formally acknowledge the reservoir they are bringing to the event. Nobody prays. It is closer to inventory. The warrior stands still, catalogs what they are carrying emotionally, and decides consciously whether the reservoir is ready or should be managed first. They are the only known culture to treat pre-combat emotional assessment as a formal discipline.

Settlements · Shadowed Ridge · the Dusk Quarter of Altherion · Nightveil Hollow. Characterized by being difficult to locate from the outside and immediately obvious to navigate once inside, built around the shadow map residents carry rather than around visible landmarks.

Relations · Lupine: deep kinship, the oldest beastkin alliance; agility and pack-force are complementary architectures, and the kinship holds across sharply divergent cultural practice. Humans: complex. Catkin are among the most frequently enslaved lineages because their physical features are considered aesthetically appealing by human buyers, and this history shapes every cross-species interaction in ways neither party consistently acknowledges. Hobgoblins: professional respect; practitioners describe the pairing as “the most uncomfortable military alliance that consistently wins engagements.”

Historical role · The Clawbound Accord’s original function was assassination and deep infiltration; the shadow-walking architecture made them the Concord’s preferred covert operatives during the Spirit War. Current Catkin communities hold no parades for this history. They acknowledge it, the way you acknowledge something that happened and that you cannot pretend didn’t.

Known figure · Saiya, active, Scourge of Hell mercenary operations.

Lupine: Wolfkin

Pack-Soul Beastkin · Lunar Strikers

Born from the Great Hunt Pact during the First Moonfall, when wild spirits and mortals fused beneath a blood eclipse. Call them wolves who became people and you will have it wrong. They are the pact itself, given a body that runs on both.

Spiritual lineage · Fenraal, the Lunar Stalker, whose moonbound clarity and Pack-Soul allow individual Lupine to function as a singular metaphysical organism at sufficient proximity. Cooperation is the least of it. At Pack-Soul depth, they think together.

Soul architecture

In the blood · Their souls oscillate with the lunar cycle, and the Core answers by reflex, triggering Aetheric bursts through physical reflex rather than cognitive calculation. They feel the technique happen before they decide to use it.

Untrained lean · Body 60% / Spirit 40%. Among those who walk the Spirit Path, it is distributed across the pack as a shared architecture, and no one Lupine holds all of it.

Isolation Decay produces Crystal compression rather than fracture, a slow fading that reads as emotional shutdown before it reads as spiritual damage. Lupine who have been isolated long enough describe feeling “too quiet for too long.” The recovery requires pack proximity, and no physician can substitute.

Acute fracture risk at Stage V if isolated. Splintering requires the soul to crack open to become larger, and the Lupine soul architecture is communal, resisting individual expansion. In isolation, Stage V tends to produce collapse rather than growth.

Stage VII stall: the soul needs to perceive itself from outside, but a Lupine’s self is partly constituted by their pack. Perceiving the self requires first separating the individual architecture from the shared one, which the Crystal resists.

Resilience Integrity degrades under isolation with no damage to the Crystal at all, because the Crystal is not a closed system for Lupine: it is designed to be partly constituted by pack-proximity.

Metaphysical identity

Wellsprings · Verdantia primary: life as continuous communal process, pack as ecosystem. Luminalis, moonlight being the light that hides, primary secondary: their power expression genuinely varies with lunar phase. Anima Spirare accessible through bond-formation overlap. Somnalis accessible at full-moon peak, for dream-pack communion during Howls of Mourning. Judicium and Fixatio suppressed: the reactive architecture reads structured stillness as threat rather than discipline.

Glyphs · Strong: Ye Unity (the Pack-Soul’s primary phoneme), Ka Cycle, Va Moment, Ma Memory. Backlash: Ko Order, Al Edge. Structure and boundary phonemes produce the Aetheric equivalent of a wall appearing inside a field that was never designed to contain one.

Kinpulse falls outside the formal glyph-chain systems. It is emotional Aether transmission without phonemic structure. Experienced practitioners can translate Kinpulse states into approximate glyph expressions, but the translation always loses something.

Overgrowth risk · Bloodmoon Corruption. During Lunar Inversions the oscillating soul-lattice destabilizes and high-stage Lupine can enter Involuntary Berserk states in which complex glyph-casting fails but raw physical output spikes catastrophically.

Domain law. Pack-Hunt Domains. The territory’s spatial geometry becomes a hunt-field: distance between predator and prey within the Domain is subject to Kinpulse law. Entities trying to create separation from a pack inside the Domain find the separation collapsing; the hunt-geometry reasserts.

Governing principle: the distance you think exists between you and us is not the distance that exists.

Culture and record

Kinbinding · Pack members cut their palms and press them together under a full moon, creating a permanent Aetheric tether. The tether carries no words, and it does not transmit thoughts. It transmits weight: the sense of another soul’s presence, its state, its direction. The closest thing Lupine culture has to a sacred act, and treated as one.

Howls of Mourning · A communal vocal ritual recording the life-echoes of the fallen into local ley-lines. The pack’s history is encoded in the land. Walking through old Lupine territory, practitioners with sufficient Attunement hear faint resonances in the ground, with neither words nor images in them: the emotional signature of lives that were lived here.

Alpha-Binding · Linking individual Soul Crystals to a single Alpha’s Crystal architecture, creating a collective metaphysical organism. The Alpha accepts responsibility for the pack’s coherence in exchange for the pack’s amplified output. Call that leadership and a Lupine will correct you: it is load-bearing.

Settlements · Lumis Village · Greyfang Crossing · the Shrine of Nine Howls. Positioned relative to ley-line nodes and lunar sight-lines rather than resource proximity. The strategic logic of their positioning is not immediately legible to other races.

Relations · Elves: deep distrust rooted in the First Betrayal, a historical event in which Elven Accord operatives used Lupine pack-bond architecture against them during a treaty negotiation. The details are in the Lupine oral tradition and not in any Accord archive. Humans: the most frequently hunted beastkin lineage where the Labor Provision permits wild beastkin clearance; their speed makes them difficult to capture, and their Isolation Decay makes extended pursuit more effective than it should be.

Historical role · The Moonwake Accord. Lupine packs guarded collapsing Wellspring sites during the Verdant Siege, allowing the Western Realms to stabilize. They received formal recognition from the Concord. Formal Accord membership did not come with it. The distinction was understood.

Ursine: Bearkin

Living Fortifications · Mountain-Anchored

Formed during the Age of Glacial Memory when the Titan of Endurance drew mountain-spirits into mortal flesh during a confluence of Monolithion and Verdantia eruptions. People expected a warrior race. They got a people who understand load-bearing.

Spiritual lineage · Orruvak, the Unmoved Mountain, a being who watched civilizations build and collapse across the hillside it occupied and never once considered that the appropriate response to either was to move. Bearkin carry this as a constitutional truth: some things should stay.

Soul architecture

In the blood · The specific weight of things that refuse to end. Their souls are old-growth heavy.

Untrained lean · Body 70% / Spirit 30%. The Spirit side shows through environmental attunement and the Stillness Trial discipline: the spiritual practice of holding while everything around you fails is both a combat technique and a soul architecture.

Crystal fracture pattern · Subtle-Blindness amplification. The Crystal resists abstract and illusory architecture so fundamentally that exposure to high-tier Illusion or Dream workings produces confusion fractures, a specific Crystal disorientation with no physical damage in it.

They cannot be tricked into reaching for a wall that isn’t there, because their Crystal does not process “wall that isn’t there” as a possible category.

Progression · Slow, thorough, through all stages. No rapid acceleration. No sudden fractures. Each transition takes roughly one and a half times the species average, because the Crystal is denser and requires more Essence pressure to restructure, and weakness has nothing to do with it. Stall potential at Stage VI Glory, whose burning-bright expression conflicts with a constitutional preference for not broadcasting. Dissonance expression: Geological Stall. The Crystal enters absolute stillness that can last years, where another people’s would shatter. Bearkin Dissonance survivors are indistinguishable from sleep to outside observation. They are not asleep.

Dexterity Evasion floors consistently: the design intent is to make damage insufficient, and avoiding it never entered the plan.

Metaphysical identity

Wellsprings · Monolithion primary, native. Verdantia strong secondary, the living system’s version of the mountain’s permanence. Basilithe accessible. Oneirion, Eidolyn and Hypnather rejected: Subtle-Blindness; contact produces confusion fractures.

Glyphs · Strong: Ir Endurance, Tar Weight, Ka Cycle, Ky Structure. Their glyph-chains are the most materially stable in any beastkin tradition: Ir-Tar-Ky chains persist without maintenance for decades. The Bearkin are the only beastkin lineage whose inscribed workings outlast the practitioner who made them without Aetheric renewal. Backlash: El Illusion, No Negation. Their soul is too grounded for negation.

Overgrowth risk · Weight-Lock. Over-investment in Vitality and Resilience Traits can develop Crystal density so extreme that Aetheric flexibility is suppressed, leaving a person capable of surviving almost anything and unable to do almost anything.

Domain law. Earthen Domains. The territory becomes materially denser, invisibly, physically and Aetherically. Objects weigh more. Force requirements increase. High-speed techniques require more Essence inside the Domain than outside it.

Governing principle: mass as sovereign law. Things should stay where they are. Kinetic disruption costs more. Defensive endurance costs less.

Culture and record

Stillness Trials · Meditative duels where two Bearkin stand within a collapsing Domain or under sustained pressure; the first to break their stance loses. Physical endurance decides very little. The question is whether the practitioner can hold their Crystal’s architecture coherent while everything around them is telling the Crystal that change is warranted. The winners are not always the strongest Bearkin. They are always the most settled ones.

Weight-Binding · Tethering the soul to a specific mountain or glade, creating a permanent Aetheric anchor. Weight-Bound Bearkin pull Aether from bonded territory within range and refine it to Essence as other practitioners do, and develop responsibility for that territory’s Aetheric health. What the mountain feels, they feel. They do not describe this as a burden.

Settlements · Korr Orvun · Glade of the Slumbering Root · Ulg-Vorakar. Among the oldest continuously occupied locations in the world, the same sites in many cases since the Age of Glacial Memory. The architecture is stone and has outlasted the practitioners who built it.

Relations · Marrowkin: honor-bound kinship; forge-culture and endurance-culture share enough foundational philosophy that cooperation is natural rather than negotiated. Lupine: protective regard: Lupine move; Bearkin stay, and neither lineage pretends the complementarity is accidental. Humans: their size reads as threatening to those who have not spent time with them. They are the most dangerous-looking and least aggressive of the major beastkin lineages, which creates consistent mismatches in initial encounter framing.

The Siege of the Seventh Fold. Bearkin held the final line for three days while the Domain around them collapsed, allowing other races to escape. The hold required sustained Stillness Trial discipline at Stage V+ from every participant. None survived intact.

The surviving Bearkin lineages consider this the defining event in their historical record and the reason the Accord’s treatment of them is an ongoing institutional insult.

Annually, Bearkin communities observe a three-day silence in memory. They call the silence recognition, and they will not have it called grief.

Fox-Spirit: Mishiro-Kin

Spirit-Adjacent Beastkin · Aetheric Nose

First Eon origin that the Guild Accord’s taxonomy system has never cleanly handled. Fox-spirit beastkin predate the formal beastkin classification and sit at the border where the Fae taxonomy also has a partial claim. The Mishiro lineage has been in contested classification since the First Eon, when the earliest practitioners demonstrated Somnalis attunement at birth that no formal harmonization ritual could account for.

Every tavern from the Salt Road to the sea calls trained sensing the nose (the Guild says sensing, the Moto kehai), and Mishiro-kin find this very funny. The rest of the continent is talking in metaphor. They are not.

Spiritual lineage · Classified under beastkin taxonomy for Accord purposes. True lineage: Somnalis-adjacent Fae of unknown direct ancestor. The Mishiro family maintains that the answer to who made your people is “we were always here,” which is not a satisfying answer to archivists and is probably correct.

Soul architecture

In the blood · Warmth, the specific quality of choosing openness as a position, and a tidal memory of felt things. Their souls are permeable in a way that other beastkin souls are not: they receive environmental Aether rather than just projecting into it.

Untrained lean · Spirit 78% / Fate 18% / Body 4%. Do not look for a combat lineage here.

Fox-spirit Crystal fracture is rare and typically associated with sustained olfactory overload: exposure to extreme Aetheric event residue without adequate processing time. The Crystal’s porosity, which makes the nose function, also makes it vulnerable to saturation. Practitioners in high-Aether environments need periodic Somnalis-integration sleep to process accumulated intake.

Stage III is the stage most affected by captivity and environmental suppression. Fox-spirit Crystal requires conditions of reasonable safety, or the kind of danger that pushes outward. Inward-pressing danger, meaning captivity, sustained threat, isolation from sensory richness, stalls Stage III at the threshold.

Metaphysical identity

Wellsprings · Somnalis native: opens without harmonization ritual. It is their home frequency; they dream with resolution and accuracy that formal practitioners require decades of work to approach. Anima Spirare develops in sustained relationships: the architecture of choosing to remain beside someone builds this Wellspring access naturally over time. Verdantia accessible via lineage. Manganthra peripheral: changes chosen or survived leave traces.

Glyphs · Strong: Ma Memory (the nose’s deepest phoneme; every Fox-Spirit technique is Ma-authorized), Zo Echo, Sa Silence, Ye Unity. Backlash: Uur Disruption, Fi Passion. These produce metabolic spike, sharp input overload that temporarily saturates the olfactory architecture.

Their glyph expression is ambient, with nothing inscriptive about it. Fox-Spirit workings look less like formal technique and more like the world cooperating.

The practitioner forces nothing; they remind the environment of what it already was.

Traits · Awakening brings the nose’s first Aetheric layer becoming consciously distinguishable from biological scent, and fox-fire, a minor ambient cold-amber luminescence, palm-sized, three to five minutes at Stage III. At Stage V, fox-fire becomes significantly less minor. The Stage V crack-open expands what it can do, moving it from ambient light into directed Aetheric expression.

Overgrowth risk · Olfactory saturation in high-Aether events. Processing failures express as temporal confusion: the practitioner loses sequence, unsure whether they are smelling the current state of a space or its state from hours ago.

Domain law. Memory Domains, rare, Stage VI+. The territory holds recent Aetheric events as accessible residue: footstep patterns persist visibly, voice-echoes hang in the air, significant emotional events leave readable impressions on surfaces. The Domain judges nothing it records, and it records everything.

Governing principle: the present moment is transparent, and its history is visible to those who know how to look. Within the territory, deception leaves residue. No formal proceeding will admit that residue as evidence. It is information that cannot be unfelt.

Culture and record

The Name-Keeping Fox-spirit beastkin place significant weight on names as the last thing that cannot be physically removed. The practice of speaking one’s name aloud in the presence of a witness when circumstances have taken everything else is documented in Mishiro oral tradition as the act of remaining.

The Waiting Fox-spirit patience has nothing passive in it. It is the accumulated practice of a people who have learned to hold their position and their perception long enough for the world to reveal what it actually is. The fox waits. The thing-that-seems-to-be eventually reveals itself as what-it-is. Most fox-spirit wisdom traditions begin here and build outward.

Bond tending Significant relationships have literal Aetheric consequences. Practitioners tend their significant bonds the way they tend their nose: regularly, with attention, without forcing the process.

Settlements Nomadic-adjacent, driven by Wellspring quality and Somnalis accessibility. Mishiro communities are small and mobile, a pattern that predates the Labor Provision and has continued regardless of it.

Fox-spirit beastkin appear in the formal historical record almost exclusively in Labor Provision documentation, as property rather than as actors. This is an accurate reflection of how the Accord’s record was compiled, and a poor one of what actually occurred.

The Mishiro lineage’s actual historical record exists in oral tradition and in the nose’s archive, in the Aetheric memory of territory that remembers what happened there. It is not accessible to the formal historical apparatus. This may be intentional.

Known figure · Yoko Mishiro, active, traveling with Sodoku and Emira Moto.

Serpentkin: Scalebond

Ancestral Memory-Bearers · Coil-Pattern Holders

Second Eon, Dissolution Period. Formed near the Scorchline Wellsprings when memory-essence from collapsing realms etched itself into scaled biological bodies specifically shaped for metaphysical erosion resistance. They did not evolve into their function. Their function was the condition of their creation.

Spiritual lineage · Zseraxil the Coil, ancient guardian of the Anamnesis site called Ancestral Echoes. Their lineage grants native connection to the Coiling Memory of the world: the principle that nothing is truly gone, that the echo of a thing persists in the Aetheric record longer than the thing itself.

Soul architecture

In the blood · The mirror before anything. Their souls function as Aetheric mirrors, capable of reflecting and storing complex historical data within the Core. What they touch, they remember. What they remember, they can use.

Untrained lean · Spirit 65% / Fate 25% / Body 10%. The Fate side shows through historical Domain formation: territories where Serpentkin have practiced become living archives.

Their Crystal is architecturally unusual: memory imprints accumulate as structural layers rather than as stored data. An old Serpentkin Crystal, viewed with sufficient Gnosis, looks like sedimentary rock, each layer a period of their life, compacted into the substrate.

Stall potential at Stage VIII. Transcendence requires genuine change, and the Coiling Memory architecture is oriented toward preservation. The Crystal resists restructuring because it contains too much it doesn’t want to lose.

Sub-stat notes · Gnosis Retention is definitive: accumulated Crystal-layer memory that does not degrade, an archive growing continuously from birth. Harmonics Memory is elevated above most races’ maximum, making them uniquely effective at Wellspring work in damaged zones: they remember what the Wellspring site was before it was damaged. Against truly novel situations they are more cautious than other races, because novelty has less precedent to cross-reference. And Dexterity Finesse: a Serpentkin does not hit harder than necessary, because “harder than necessary” wastes the Coiling Memory’s evidence of what sufficient force looks like.

Metaphysical identity

Wellsprings · Anamnesis primary: recovered memory, historical resonance, temporal depth. Mirithane strong secondary. Somnalis accessible, used for Coiling Memory integration work. Solfatara accessible through temperature-sync architecture, and the Titan Vohrin’s cold ground workable through opposite-temperature regulation: both thermal extremes available, moderate temperatures actually their weakest environmental range.

Glyphs · Strong: Ma Memory, Zo Echo, Ka Cycle, En Lattice, Len Line. Their glyph chains are among the most temporally stable in the Continuum: Ma-Zo-En chains persist without degradation because the Crystal substrate feeds them passive Essence from its accumulated memory layers.

Backlash · Uur Disruption, No Negation. The Coiling Memory cannot process negation of pattern: the architecture is built on preservation, and negation workings produce a category error where backlash would be, a gap in the Crystal’s filing logic where the working should have landed.

Traits · Awakening brings Chronos-Vision (seeing after-images of prior Aetheric events in physical space), the first memory-layer deposition, and scale-resonance at significant historical sites. Ascension moves through personal memory → ancestral memory → Coiling Memory access. At high stages, Serpentkin stop accessing their own memory and start accessing the world’s.

Domain law. Memory Domains: passive accumulation of event residue. The territory becomes a living archive. At Stage VII+, the Domain can be queried intentionally: what happened here, in what sequence, by whom.

Governing principle: the past is present. What occurred in the territory is part of the territory’s current Aetheric composition. The law is passive and passes no judgment.

Culture and record

The Coil-Rite · A coming-of-age ritual in which the young practitioner meditates within an Anamnesis Wellspring zone, allowing the Crystal to receive its first deep memory-layer deposit. The layer contains the combined Aetheric signature of the lineage’s history: pattern-memory, the shape of what the lineage is, with no one individual’s recollection in it.

Memory-weaving · Practitioners can share Crystal layers with willing recipients through sustained physical contact and synchronized breathing. The recipient receives the emotional and Aetheric texture of the shared period and none of the content. Used for historical transmission and for grief processing.

Settlements · Coilspire Den · Stoneglass Hollow · the Pale Fang Archipelago (disputed). Positioned at historically significant sites: Wellspring ruins, event-dense ley-line intersections, anywhere the Coiling Memory is accessible.

Relations · Marrowkin: mutual respect grounded in shared commitment to preservation, material preservation and Aetheric preservation as parallel projects. Elves: scholarly cooperation, productive and slightly territorial. Humans: viewed as people who do not understand what is being lost when they destroy things, with no contempt in it: a patient, ongoing observation of a civilization that consistently fails to consult available historical data before repeating documented mistakes.

The Dissolution Period’s surviving historical record is partially the Serpentkin’s contribution: their Crystal-layer memory of events that no formal archive preserved.

The Accord’s historical division treats this data as anecdotal because its source is living memory rather than inscribed record.

The Serpentkin find this classification clarifying.
