Forms

Forms

Nov 28, 202633 min read

Big Maud Skerritt sold herring on the Skerry quay, and she threw them, flat and hard, at sixty paces, at customers who were slow with the coin. A herring from Maud would crack a tankard in a man’s hand and leave a silver bruise that smelled for a week. When a Guild assessor came to ask which hall’s token she carried, she knocked his hat into the harbor, and the Accord convened a public hearing to establish what, in law, she was doing. Eight Measurewrights (the Guild’s sworn measurers) sat on it. The bookies of Skerry opened the book before the benches were full: Vectra at two to one, Arts at three, Manifestus at twelve for the romantics, and forty to one on Mechanica, the forbidden one, which the fish-merchants’ guild was paying somebody to argue for. Four days, three fistfights among the bench, one Measurewright in tears, and a demonstration that took the presiding examiner’s coif off on the fifth herring.

They entered her under Arts, with a dissent. Vectra’s backers rioted in a small way. Maud paid forty silver in fees, in herring, and raised her prices.

I have told that story to every apprentice I have ever had, because a table of this kind looks like a law of nature and it was written by people like those eight. The register they were fighting over had twenty-six headings. The trade has since folded them into the twelve below, and under these Maud is a Strike, with a dissent for Shape, and the bookies would still open. Most of what the old headings said was sound, so it has been kept here under the Form it belongs to, and the old names go on being used wherever they earn their keep. All of it has been fought over by someone with money riding on the answer.

Forms sort workings, the things a person does with power. They do not sort people or their trades. A chant, a rune, a tag and a Draft are all done by whoever has leveled the skill, under whatever guild will have them.

A home is a qualifier and never a lock. It works the way a Wellspring’s kind does. Any Path can take up any Form, and each will do it in its own layer’s terms: a Body cutter’s ward stops the bottle, a Spirit cutter’s is taken for a wall, and a Fate cutter’s sets a term on her own share of the odds. A Form is cheapest at home. Away from home the work is clumsier and dearer than it looks in the manual: yield (η, the share of the spend that arrives, as a distillation has its yield) falls, and the lost share is paid in the worker’s own coin, heat for a Body hand, hollowing for a Spirit one, a heavier knot for a Fate one. The rung it reads is still whichever one the art’s Path line picked. The same fighter routinely has two techniques under two Forms, and a bookie will quote you both.

Each Form below keeps what the old register knew about it.

Strike

Structured, discipline-based styles blending movement, form, and the flow of power: controlled Flux (the flow of what a soul holds through the body, which is the Charge, raw Aether packed in the Shell, for a Body fighter, and Essence for anyone else), martial shaping, embodied Aether expression. Strike is often a practitioner’s first structured magic regardless of eventual Path, since the truth held in the Essence Core (the Crystal’s inner layer) and the expression of the Aether Shell (its outer layer, which turns power into force) already speak the same language here. A clerk who says Corporis means the case where the body is the conduit and cannot be separated from the technique.

You have seen it as: Resonant Forms · the Iron Bison · Nadi · a herring at sixty paces.

Shape

Direct control of a working in real time, the technique being the act. The old heading Vectra covered motion, direction, velocity and spatial gravitation, and it is still what trained mouths call anything that is fundamentally about where force goes. Shape climbs Body Mastery rung by rung: matter as an object, through contact, by recognition, by law, as a whole system. What it spends on the Body Path is raw Aether (mana), drawn in, packed in the Shell and driven out through it, and the lost share burns in the Shell’s furnace and comes off as heat.

A trade feeds the climb. On a material that a Body fighter’s trade skill knows at Journeyman or better, they work one rung up the Material Force: contact reaches recognition, and so on up. A smith reads steel deeper than she reads anything else, a mason stone, a butcher the joints of whatever is in front of him. The lift applies to that material and no other, and it never carries anyone past the Body Mastery ceiling of their Stage. Marta Kell, on the Skills page, is the case the examiners quote.

You have seen it as: Gravity Folding · Aetherstep · Spatial Collapse · the Material Force.

Read

Reading and sensing through a lens. Mnemata reads backward: memorycraft and echo work, dream records, ancestral fields, temporal imprints and historical echo-law, the practiced discipline behind Lattice Memory and Crystalline Memory, extended outward to other souls’ records. The Wellsprings of remembrance and depth (Anamnesis, Phreatis, Somnalis) are its most natural partners.

Auguria reads sideways, and it is the reading of outcome weight, causal load, and probability distribution: what is leaning, with what will happen left to happen. Mnemata reads backward into what a thing was; Auguria reads sideways into what a thing is being pulled toward. The answer must be received by a Core capable of holding it without resolving it into narrative, which is the failure mode: an Auguria reading that arrives already interpreted has been interpreted by the reader.

Auguria is the licit, open end of the Plane of Fate, because it only looks. Moving the weights it reads is a Pull, and it is another matter.

Aetherica is the old name for the measuring trade, the Measurewrights’ own: Flux, Aether Units, Yield (Efficiency in the older rolls), Density, Coherence. Its readings are often right about how much and never about why. A practitioner of it might never cast in their life, instead ensuring everyone else’s workings are measured against the same scale. It is also the shelf that keeps the window, the soul reporting its numbers to its owner, unseen by anyone else: the window gives its bearer numbers, ranks, percentages, bars and Level, Aetherica holds the EU, AU/s and yield behind them, and its scholars argue about who built the thing.

You have seen it as: Outcome Reading · Tide-Sense · the crow’s sight (Yukari) · water-scrying practices with no register entry · Dreamfolding · Echo Archives · Solution.

Write

Glyphica is the Parun-based language of law, the syntax by which the Continuum (the whole lawful order of reality) recognizes and authorizes transformation, explicit in sigils and scripts or implicit in internalized chains. It governs the Four Glyph Roles: Authorization, Boundary, Direction, Sealing. It is the Essence Core’s intention made legible. A practitioner with sloppy internalized chains still casts, and what comes out is slightly different from what was intended: a quiet, accumulating error.

Scriptia is the clerk’s word for cutting it into something. Every cut or prepared sentence files here. A rune goes on working in its material after the cutter has gone home, and a tag is prepared ahead, fueled per tag and fired only by a practitioner; both are skills anyone with a woken Crystal can level. The home is Spirit, and it is Fate only for writing on Fate itself, as a written blessing does. What a glyph is and what a fault in one does are on the Master Glyph Index.

You have seen it as: Wordform Chains · runes and cut wards · tags · Parunic Script Weaving · the Spirit Rune Arts.

Rite

Formal rituals built on Wellspring harmonics, Realm laws, and Parunic syntax. A typical sequence: Intention → Space → Circle → Offering → Invocation → Catalyst → Seal. Ritus anchors are temporary and ceremonial. A rite is performed, not built, and the Essence Core’s posture during the rite matters more than any glyph’s placement. It is also the ancestor of every field-capable discipline that later internalized what the circle used to externalize. Turgia is the clerk’s word for the kind that leaves a lasting change behind it.

Transitus is the old name for the crossing of a body, soul, or object between planes, and for the making and holding of the apertures through which crossing occurs. Shape moves a thing to somewhere else in the world. Transitus moves it out of the world it was in, across the Veil’s inner fold. A crossing requires three things at once and fails if any is missing: a body that survives arrival, a Core that stays coherent through the transition, and a claim on the far side that has agreed to receive it. Uninvited crossings resolve as the receiving plane sees fit.

You have seen it as: Rite of Hollow Union · Pale Root Binding · Choir of Severance · Transference Crossing · Gate-Work · Veil-Thinning.

Make

Animatria is the shaping and projection of consciousness into constructs, fields, or avatars. An Animatria construct is a small detached fragment of the caster’s Essence Core given temporary independent operation, closer to a deliberately-shaped Essence Shard than a Domain. This is why Animatria fails completely in Silence Realms (regions where no lawful working can be authorized): the construct’s continuity depends on a connection back to its source, which Harmonic Silence severs. The Spirit Forge does the same job in ore, bone or glyph-cut stone.

Crystalia is the engineering, forging, and refinement of Soul Crystals and related lattices, the practiced discipline behind the Crystalia Constraint: harmonization with three relevant Wellsprings, certified understanding of the subject’s Path and Wellsprings, sufficient Temperance to survive exposure to another soul’s Essence Core. Its object is the Core, and its method is physical: forging and tempering done with hands and instruments on a lattice that happens to also be a soul.

The discipline’s hard limit is that nucleation cannot be manufactured at the bench. A Crystalia practitioner can widen conductivity, repair a fracture, and prepare a lattice to receive a melt cleanly. With hands and instruments they cannot supply a seed, because a true seed is a law the subject survived into existence and no instrument makes one. Everything the trade sells past that point is a Draft under another name, a seed out of a bottle, and the Accord’s halls distinguish the two on paper and nowhere else.

A Master Work is the far end of Make: a piece that does what its Grade and material should not be able to, made once at the end of a lifetime, always Unique or Mythic, and named.

You have seen it as: Eidolon Crafting · Spiritchain · Fractal Alignment · Core Rebinding · Tempered Latticework.

Sing

Magic of tone, vibration, and resonance. Harmonia sits on two Paths because tone originates in the Essence Core, what the soul means, but only becomes Harmonia once it travels outward to affect other Crystals, by way of the Attraction Layer, what the soul reaches. Mismatched Resonant Tone produces Echo Fracture (the failure state of tone-work). A chant files here, or under Write when the sentence matters more than the note, and a chant stops when the speaker does.

Synergia is emergent resonance from multiple souls, Wellsprings, or Domains interacting, birthing shared Temperance fields and collective intent. A Synergia field is several Soul Crystals briefly sharing one Essence Core, Shell, and Layer, which is why souls harmonized to the water Wellsprings anchor these fields most easily.

Concordia is the apex: aligning multiple Aether bodies, Wellsprings, and Domains into a single coherent field, Synergia and Harmonia scaled up and made durable rather than momentary. It requires both homes: Spirit-side precision to align many Essence Cores without flattening any, and Fate-side reach to hold that alignment as a lasting Domain rather than a passing chord. A successful Concordia working is functionally a Major Shared Realm (a Domain that has outlived its founders and become a place)‘s founding moment, performed deliberately, and it is what people reach for to heal a damaged Wellspring vein or begin closing a Hollowing (a region’s slow loss of the capacity for lawful working).

You have seen it as: Veilsong · Lunar Chorus · Frequency Collapse · Affinity Fields · Severance Choirs · Guild Choirs · Concord Domains · Nexus Formation · the Harmonic Arts.

Brew

Alchemical procedures channeling a Wellspring’s process through Parun glyphs (signs in the root script of law). Drafts are formulaic: Aether + Essence + Vessel + Glyphica = Reaction. Though Drafts touch all three Soul Crystal layers, the decisive variable is how cleanly the Essence Core’s intention survives the journey into the crucible, and hands alone can pour one, so a Crystal that never woke can level the skill. Conjunction is the bench operation that weds prepared laws, Essences or Traits in a vessel, and it requires strict compatibility: opposed laws need mediation glyphs, and failing that the Rupture Event follows. A clerk’s Conjunctia means one practitioner’s working on two lenses at once, which needs no heading of its own.

Brew is also where the money lives. A Draft or a blessing can supply a Catalyst, the change a Crystal needs before it passes a Threshold, so Stages can simply be bought; the great houses hoard the top Drafts for that reason. A monster’s core renders into Essence Drafts used for tempering, with Creature-Sourced potency and the risk of Identity Contamination, and a Crystal cut from a dead practitioner makes the strongest Draft of all and the worst crime in every realm. A breakthrough Draft follows the Path of the soul it is brewed for: Salt-based for a Body-led soul, Core stock for a Spirit-led one, and for a Fate-led one the Layer reagent, the dearest of the three and the most criminal. A Fate Essence Draft made on that reagent is a Master Work, a piece no ordinary bench can make: extremely hard, and very coveted. There is no fight Draft for Fate at all, since a Fate fighter’s reserve is Essence and Core stock refills it.

You have seen it as: Coagulation · Distillation · Sublimation · monster-core tempering Drafts · Apotheosis Fusions · Absolution Weaves.

Call

Invocation by recognition and correspondence: Titans, Archons (the fourteen great powers that embody cosmic law), World Spirits, lesser entities, Realms. Vocatia never dominates. It negotiates shared resonance, the practice-form of Invocation and Consent. A Vocatia working addressed to an unwilling higher being is, at the level the Continuum reads intention, an attempted Usurpation, regardless of correct surface syntax. Successful Vocatia is always an act of the Current of Recognition, never Refusal. Nothing is pulled down, which is why it can be learned in a yard at all.

You have seen it as: Titan Echo · Archon Descent · Concord Summons · the Spirit Summoning Arts.

Bind

Claustra is the binding, sealing, and lawful imprisonment of a thing against its own nature: souls, entities, Wellspring processes, Phenomena mid-event, and other practitioners’ workings. Where Write supplies the Sealing role as one of four, Claustra is the discipline of making that role carry the whole weight of a working indefinitely. The seal’s syntax is Soul Plane precision while its duration is a Fate-side claim that must be renewed by something. A Claustra working that loses its anchor opens, all of it, at once. It is the home of the Vow Clause: a vow is a seal a practitioner sets on themselves, and it is the only Claustra working whose anchor is the bearer’s own continued behavior.

Votia is the old name for workings whose operative currency is a declared and irrevocable price: an offering made before the effect, a restriction accepted permanently, a thing given up so that a law will answer more readily than the practitioner’s Temperance should permit. What is being altered is the Essence Core’s posture rather than its output. A Votia working makes a practitioner stronger by making them narrower, and the Continuum answers narrowness with power.

The sacrifice arts are Votia at its far end, where the thing given up cannot be given twice: an eye, a hand, a year of life, a name. They stand outside the ordinary accounts the way a blessing does. A sacrifice opens what the practitioner’s gates have not opened, and what it lends is not counted on the cap of two steps over the sustainable figure, because the soul is not lifting itself: it has paid something else to do the lifting. That is the whole of the good news. It works once, for the one working it was offered for. The price is ruinous, taken from the body on the spot and written on the Ledger besides, where it goes on being collected after the wound has closed, and no hall I know of will teach one to a student it expects to see again.

The Vow Clause, stated mechanically. A vow declares a permanent restriction and the Core adopts it as a standing law. Tempering Coherence measures the alignment between a practitioner’s stated law and their lived behavior, and it is the structural prerequisite for Threshold eligibility. A vow kept is therefore a direct purchase of Coherence by conduct rather than by expenditure, which is why vows are common in every hall and camp, and commonest in the populations that cannot buy Drafts: soldiers, hedge-workers, the orders, the poor.

It is the cheapest advancement mechanism in the world and it is priced in the rest of your life.

A broken vow lands at once by its own terms, and repeated denial of the truth behind it makes a Shear Break. The Core has been running a law the bearer has now denied, and the law does not negotiate its own withdrawal. This is why the orders treat a lapsed vow as a medical emergency rather than a disciplinary one, and why the disciplinary response follows only if the practitioner survives.

A vow is small Fate, a person binding their own probability, and anyone can make one. It spends the soul’s own share and pulls nothing down, so a vow kept leaves no knot. Spoken, it shows in the bearer’s window with the bargain and its terms, and what it buys counts toward the two-step cap like any other boost of one’s own. See Vows and Oaths.

You have seen it as: Dragon Binding · Null-Anchors · The Veil’s standing closures · Oath-Seals · Gisei · Sacrament and Oblation · Disclosure practices.

Claim

Domain Weaving is the deliberate reshaping of local Continuum behavior so that a chosen law becomes easier to sustain within a defined space. The practitioner does not enforce the law directly; they alter what the region is willing to do cheaply. A Domain is a standing negotiation with the Plane of Fate rather than an application of force. It persists exactly as long as the Attraction Layer keeps recognizing the claim, which is why a Domain fails all at once rather than fraying. Every Domain is a claim by the Layer, on every Path, and it needs no Fate Mastery and pulls no thread. Anyone at Stage VIII can have one, held by a Second Release or, on Fate, by the One, their own concept made real, and money cannot buy it: a sect teaches it, or the fighter finds it. Concordia aligns many existing fields into one; Domain Weaving builds a single field from a single soul’s claim.

Silentia is the removal of magical possibility from a region, a body, or a working. Counterspelling and shielding are other trades. This is the deliberate production of an absence in which lawful transformation cannot be authorized at all. Mechanica removes recognition and substitutes extraction. Silentia removes recognition and substitutes nothing, which is why Silence Realms sever Animatria constructs from their source and why a soul held inside one reports the sensation as loneliness rather than weakness. It is the counterplay Form: every other entry here describes something being done, and Silentia describes the conditions under which none of them work.

You have seen it as: Aspect Sovereignty · Standing Claim · Law-Bias Fields · Harmonic Silence · Null Zones · Aether Suppression · the Hollow’s quieter effects · Talos.

Pull

A pull moves the weights Auguria reads, to work outside what reality already knows and to control the fate of a thing. At one end it is a Fate Path fighter’s single lean on the odds, rare, ruinous and often a failure. At the far end it is a Fate Art, and that is rare, mostly by bloodline and considered incredibly overpowered. A pull comes down from above through the Veil’s outer face, and it always reads Fate Mastery on top of whatever else is being paid. What it spends is Thread, which is never held: it is pulled for the one working and paid for out of the soul’s own share of fate, and every pull leaves a knot that the world collects later, sized to how far the pull stood from likely. It moves only what already has a way of happening. The Works of Hataraki are inherited grips on it, and a grip lifts Fate Mastery past the second rung, where every soul without one stops, and lowers the chance of failure, and never the price or the knot; the edit types of the Ketsumyōgan are others, and a personal grip can be won, once, though only a Work or a blessing lifts the rung.

You have seen it as: the rotten pile at Akagawa · Danshi’s cut · an involuntary Fate-pull that took somebody somewhere they did not ask to go.

Seam Arts

A ward is a wall, and a wall stands on one side of something. Ask the cutter which side, before you pay. Proverb of the Ward-Cutters’ Company, Sallow Quay

Two of the twelve Forms have two homes. Brew sits on the Body and the Spirit, and Sing on the Spirit and Fate. For nearly everyone who practices them that is a filing matter: a Draft is poured at a bench, a chant is sung in a choir stall, and both are trades and skills that anyone may level, under whatever guild will have them. Nothing in this section is about those people.

A Seam Art is a combat art whose Form crosses two Paths and is worked where the planes themselves join. The Veil has two such places. Its inner fold runs between the Physical World and the Soul Plane, and a Brew worked there is one working that is matter and meaning together. Its outer face is where the lower realities meet Fate, and a Sing worked there carries a tone out of the Core and along the bonds a soul holds on its Layer. Where the fold and the face come together, the trade says seam, and the name has stuck to the arts.

Why they are feared. Every counter in the manuals stands on one plane. Plate, Durability and a Body ward stop what arrives as matter. A soul-ward stops what arrives as meaning. Soundness, a kept oath and reaching the puller answer Fate. A Seam Art arrives as two of these at once and belongs wholly to neither, so the defense that stops one half lets the other through, and a fighter who has only ever drilled against one road finds that the drill has nothing to say. A Measurewright asked to grade one will ask which road’s Mastery to read it on, and the honest answer is both, each half on its own.

Why they are rare. They are unstable, and the instability is in the seam and no fault of the practitioner. A working held across two planes has to stay true on both, and the Veil is thick in one street and thin in the next. On thick ground the halves come apart and the art does half its work at full price. On thin ground it does more than was asked. It demands two Paths walked in earnest, with all that costs in years, and its failures are its own: a Brew at the fold that curdles does so in the brewer’s body and Core together, and a Sing at the face that slips its key rings back along the singer’s own bonds first, since those are the nearest. Most who attempt one are dead or ruined before the art has a name, and the halls do not teach them. They are found, or inherited, or taken off the dead.

Two that are still spoken of. Neither has been assayed by anyone who came back to file the paper, so what follows is casework and report.

The Red Vintage. Odo Sacheverell kept a wine shop in Harrowmouth and killed eleven sworn duelists across nine years, every one of them better than he was on any measure the Accord keeps. He brewed in the wound. Each cut he made he fixed as a substance in the opponent’s own blood, a ferment that was part poison and part grief, so that the body sickened and the soul soured together, and a man warded against venom died of despair with his veins clean while a man warded in the soul died of the venom. The surgeons who opened his victims found the blood gone thick and dark as lees. He was taken in the end by a fishwife’s son with a boathook who gave him no time to cut, which is the counter the casebook recommends, and by then a third of his own blood had turned, by the examiners’ estimate, from the brews that had curdled in him over the years.

The Kin-Knell. A sung art out of the hill chantries, credited to a cantor the registers call only the Widower of Low Tessaly. He sang one sustained tone at one person, and it did nothing to them that anyone could see. It went out along what bound them. A captain’s sworn company dropped their shields forty paces behind him. A mother three valleys off sat down in her kitchen and did not get up that day. The Concord’s casework holds that the tone was an ordinary soul-strike, which a soul-ward on the target stops perfectly well, and that this is of no use at all, because the target was never where it landed. Its known answers are to have no bonds, which is the answer of a certain kind of villain, or to kill the singer in the first breath. The Widower’s own name is said to sit wrong in the mouths of those who knew him, and nobody now alive will say for certain what it was.

Villains favor these arts for the plain reason that they punish the prepared, and a hall that learns one is at large in its district generally posts the bounty before it posts the warning.

Mechanica, the Crime

Mechanica is no Form, because it is any of them done as theft. It covers forbidden constructs bypassing Soul Crystals, Wellsprings, or Glyphic Syntax. Mechanica coerces transformation without lawful Wellspring participation, consuming the resonance around it and damaging the Continuum. Every Form involves recognition: of Wellsprings, Domains, other souls. Mechanica is what remains when recognition is removed and replaced with extraction: Conjunction’s compatibility deleted, Glyphica’s authorization skipped, Drafts performed without consent. Statute XXVI, Mechanica Irreversibility, exists because this is the one practice that leaves scars the Continuum doesn’t forget. The long account is Mechanica: Extraction Without Recognition.

You have seen it as: Soul Splicing · Aether Inversion · Trait Collapse Engines, and a very great many bad nights in the cellars of very respectable houses.

What No Form Covers

Forms sort what somebody does. These are done by nobody in particular, or they are what the doing is done with.

Phenomena are large-scale anomalies where Aether law bends under overload: semi-autonomous, self-sustaining events marking major law stress, the Singularity of Statute XIV. Once triggered, Phenomena are no longer cast. They have outrun any Attraction Layer, which is why Counter-Phenomena are so dangerous: the trigger has an author, but the Phenomenon itself does not. Echo Collapse, Worldbirth, Aetherquake and Memory Storms are the ones that get into songs.

Manifestus is the old word for short-lived Wellspring emissions and flares triggered by high emotional density or sudden pressure, reflecting current Temperance and Essence posture. An involuntary Trait preview: Overtone Bloom given external form. The trigger is always a spike in the Core and the expression is a flare that briefly exists physically. Early-Temperance souls experience this far more, and it is what Trait Awakening looks like from outside. Maud’s backers at twelve to one were arguing she could not help it.

Traits are standing laws carried in the soul’s own material and expressed through the Crystal once there is a Crystal to express them: Primary, Secondary, Inherited, Passive and Compound Reflections.

Traits are not encoded by Temperance. Primary and Inherited Traits are woven into a soul’s original Essence, the rest are forged by what the soul lives through, they run in the unwoken whose Crystal will never open, and awakening arms what was already there rather than granting anything new. What Temperance does is the reverse of encoding. At a Threshold the Crystal melts, and Traits are the only structures that survive the heat, because a law the Continuum has accepted as reliable does not stop being reliable at temperature. The new lattice cools around them. This is why Traits govern advancement rather than merely decorating it.

A Trait is held in the Core, and its most legible expression is almost always physical, and the expression is literal rather than figurative. A Trait biases the Aether Shell’s conductivity permanently, and because the Shell is the Material-Plane layer the bias is laid down in tissue: mineral into bone, altered myelination, rebuilt vasculature, a resting core temperature off the population mean. A body is a Trait record and a corpse is a better one. So the Tempering that melts the Soul Crystal down and rebuilds each layer also leaves the body with its Traits written into it. A Trait can be leveled and refined like a skill. Its level rises with use, grows with overall Level and skill, and shows on its bearer’s sheet; the law itself stays the law it was.

The Wellsprings are the Lens line and nobody’s Form. The Sixty are the nouns; everything else is a way of using them as verbs. Temperance governs how much of each process a soul can bear, and a Wellspring defines what law and emotional grammar are available.

The Guild’s Naming Table

A clerk’s form has a box for this, and the clerk needs one word. The root is the Affinity (the face of a lens’s idea a person works: fire, not Pyreveil, gives Pyromancy its root). The suffix says what the worker is doing with it, and it places them before they have finished introducing themselves. Nothing here is a law of the world, and three separate halls have tried to make it one.

Nobody has ever coined a suffix for Call, Bind, Claim or Pull that stuck. Those trades have other names, mostly unfriendly ones, and the people who do them have rarely filled in the form.
