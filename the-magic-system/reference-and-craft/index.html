Reference and Craft

Reference and Craft

Nov 28, 20261 min read

There is a dent in the oak table of the Guild’s reading room at Altherion, left by two Measurewrights (the Guild’s sworn measurers) and one lectern in the Duel of the Two Ruptures, and these are the pages they were fighting over. Consulted rather than read. The Field Manual is the compilers’ own: how a working gets written down, with what magic is left to the rest of the shelf.

  • Forms The five lines every art states, the twelve Forms with their home Paths, Big Maud, the Guild’s naming table and where the old headings went. Consulted, not read.
  • The Lexicon of Magic — Master Terminology Every term, defined, windows, classes, monster cores, titles and blessings included. Consulted, not read.
  • The Working Vocabulary — Trade Jargon and Its Metaphysical Extensions The trade words of lamp-wardens, assayers and glasshouses, carried over into the metaphysical case.
  • The Practitioner’s Field Manual — The Forge The compilers’ own. How a working gets written down rather than what it is, with the sheet, the class and the climb, and the design notes behind the working trades.
