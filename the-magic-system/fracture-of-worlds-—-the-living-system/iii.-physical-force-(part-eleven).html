III. Physical Force (Part Eleven)

III. Physical Force (Part Eleven)

Nov 28, 202634 min read

The Continuum does not distinguish between the fist and the meteor. It measures the pressure that arrives, and it measures what survives.

Two women took the Hwangsan Gate in one afternoon, and the Southern houses are still fighting about which of them did it. The Seo women, an aunt and her niece, hired out as a pair, Hye-rin with a sword and Bo-ra with her fists, in matching persimmon coats, the same figure against both their names on the Guild roll, and a strike that graded A on the wall-reckoners’ table. Bo-ra went first. She set her heel in a cart rut, her aura flared the orange of her coat from the shoulder down, and she punched the curtain wall.

Forty paces of masonry came down in a wave of yellow dust with the garrison still standing on it, and a Measurewright watching from the siege line got a mouthful of it. Hye-rin strolled through the dust, told her niece she had missed a bit, and drew one line through the iron portcullis and the oak doors behind it, so clean that the halves stood a moment longer and the gate captain was still shouting orders when the top half slid. The minstrels gave the day to the sword. The sutlers in the camp paid on the fist. Bo-ra’s arm had come back with the wall’s answer in it, the wrist and both bones of the forearm, a month in splints, because every push is pushed back and the Charge only pays part of that bill. And the Measurewright, spitting grit, worked the whole thing out in charcoal on a wagon board and announced that the two blows had carried the same energy within a rounding error, whereupon both women tried to hit him.

That wagon board is this page.

What Force of Hand Is

Force of Hand, which the older registers call Physical Force, is the umbrella measure of every destructive, resistive or manipulative output a character can bring to bear on matter, by bodily action, by reinforced technique, or by a working driven out through the Shell. Five facets: Strike Force, the EU a single attack delivers; Durability, the capacity to absorb, deflect or endure what comes in without structural failure; Lifting Strength, sustained force applied against weight or structural resistance; Range, the spatial envelope across which force stays whole; and Aura Pressure, the passive force a character’s presence exerts on their surroundings.

Every Grade on this page reads off EU delivered, which is the EU spent times the yield (η), the share of what is spent that reaches the world, as a distillation has its yield. The thresholds are the ones the trade has always used. The Measurewrights reckon them in joules, newtons and tons of TNT besides, at one EU to the megajoule, and their columns stand beside each table as the guild’s conversion: often right about how much, and never the reading. Older copies of this Part say yield for a blow’s whole energy; the word now belongs to η alone, and this Part says output or EU delivered where it means the blow.

Force of Hand is not separate from Aetheric power. It is its most immediate showing. Every soul works under what the Measurewrights still call the Aether-Physics Fusion Rule, and what the benches put more plainly: a working changes the worker, and the world gives way as far as that soul is truly changed, without overturning any of the habits the world settled in the Physical Epochs. Stone keeps its strength, a fist still has to meet the wall, and the wall still answers. When a practitioner punches through a castle wall, the wall breaks because the fist carried more than masonry was ever made to bear, and the Measurewright will say the kinetic energy concentrated in the fist exceeded the tensile and compressive strength of the masonry, which is right about how much. That the blow was driven by the Charge, raw Aether packed in the Shell, rather than by muscle alone changes nothing about what the stone does once it is struck. The Continuum measures what arrived. It does not ask where it came from.

A note on the word Essence in this Part. The older copies call the engine of every blow Essence. A Body Path fighter refines nothing: what the Shell holds and drives out is the Charge, shaped there by the lens, and its lost share burns in the Shell’s furnace as heat. Force that breaks matter leaves by the Shell whoever sends it, because the Shell is the only door into matter, so a soul on another road who wants a wall down pays for it through their own Shell at their own Body Mastery. Where a line below says Essence of a blow, read the reserve. Every figure stands as written.

What does differentiate is delivery mechanism. A punch delivers force through physical contact across a finite impact area. A Sage Art (a working fed by the Wellspring Aether pooled in the ground underfoot) technique delivers force through Aetheric projection across a volumetric zone. A Domain-grade expression delivers force through ground that has taken the practitioner’s own law in place of the world’s. The EU delivered may be identical. The tactical application is not.

The Dual-Law Requirement

Every expression of Force of Hand must satisfy two criteria at once.

True to the world’s habits. The blow must have a source, a line, a point of application and a measurable force. Weight, momentum, inertia and leverage go on applying to the striker and to the thing struck even when the drive is the Charge rather than muscle, because the world gives way to a changed soul without dropping its habits. A punch the guild reckons at three giganewtons still comes back up the arm that threw it, taken by reinforced bone density and Vitality-governed tendon architecture. A thrown boulder still flies the way thrown boulders fly.

Aetherically coherent. The Soul Crystal must structurally support the output. A character whose Ardency Sub-Stats have not reached the step required for a given output experiences Overchannel (forcing more output than the Crystal can safely sustain) events, Backlash, or outright technique collapse when attempting it. The body may be capable of the motion. The Crystal may not be capable of the cost.

The benchmarks below therefore represent the structurally sustainable ceiling. Characters can exceed them through Overchannel or crisis, and an Overchannel counts toward the boost cap, two steps on the hidden scale, like any other self-boost. The cost is Crystal Fracture risk, Residual Strain, and potential permanent Sub-Stat degradation.

Strike Force

Strike Force is the EU a single attack delivers. The Measurewrights reckon it two ways besides, in newtons for the peak contact force at the moment of impact and in joules for the whole of what arrived, and both of their readings matter, because steel and bone keep the world’s habits. A sword thrust concentrates little into an extremely small contact area, producing high local pressure and deep penetration. A body slam spreads an enormous blow across a broad contact zone, producing catastrophic structural damage without penetration. The Continuum records both.

The guild’s conversions run one EU to the megajoule and one ton of TNT to 4.184 × 10⁹ joules. Their TNT column describes destructive capacity, not that every attack produces an explosion. A sword strike with City Block-level output cuts. It does not detonate. What it could destroy and what it does destroy are questions of delivery, not capacity.

The Somatic-Aetheric Split. In a blow grading Hollow or F, Strike Force is predominantly somatic. By E the ratio is roughly sixty percent Aetheric, forty percent somatic. From C up the somatic contribution is structurally negligible: the body is the delivery frame, the reserve is the engine. By the time a fighter’s blows grade S the body has been so thoroughly rebuilt by saturation that the distinction between muscle and Aether ceases to be anatomically meaningful. The practitioner’s bicep does not contract harder. Their Shell packs the Charge into the limb’s contact zone so densely that the guild reckons the force in giganewtons, because the furnace in the Shell is doing the work and the muscle is only the frame it works through.

The Focused-Yield Principle, as the trade still names it from the days when yield meant a blow’s whole energy. Strike Force measures what is delivered, the EU spent times the yield, never the whole of the reserve expended. A practitioner whose strike grades A, with Ardency Compression a step higher, can focus their output into a contact area smaller than a coin, producing local pressures orders of magnitude above the rest of their reckoning. This is why a sword cut grading A bisects materials a blunt strike of the same Grade only cracks. The EU delivered is identical. The pressure distribution, which is a matter of steel and stone, is not.

Lifting Strength

Lifting Strength is a continuous output metric: how much mass can be held motionless against gravity, or how much structural resistance overcome through sustained application rather than impact.

Governed primarily by Vitality, particularly Fortitude and Constitution, with secondary contribution from Tempering Yield, which determines how much of what the body spends it can hold as sustained effort. Body Path practitioners receive a multiplicative bonus at all Grades because they work through the Shell, the one door into matter, where weight is the world’s own habit and nothing has to be translated.

Gravitational Sovereignty. Once a lift grades S, Lifting Strength stops being mechanical. A Body Path practitioner lifting at that Grade does not lift a mountain by gripping it and pushing. They pack the Charge beneath the mass as a floor the mountain’s weight must answer to, and the weight is borne by the soul’s own authority over matter rather than by muscle: the mountain gives way exactly as far as the lifter has truly become a thing that bears mountains. The Measurewrights describe it as a counter-gravitational lattice redistributing load vectors across the Aetheric architecture until the mountain’s gravitational potential energy is opposed, and their figures for the load are good. Conventional leverage mechanics, which require a fulcrum, a moment arm and a contact surface, cease to apply above roughly ten million tonnes.

The Binding Strength Corollary. Lifting Strength also governs resistance to metaphysical displacement: gravity wells, telekinetic pulls, spatial compression, dimensional anchoring, soul-yank techniques. A character whose ceiling is 500,000 tonnes cannot be forcibly displaced by any effect applying less than the weight of that load, which the guild reckons at roughly 4.9 × 10⁹ newtons sustained. High-Vitality Body Path practitioners are functionally immune to knockback from lower-Tier opponents. They are not resisting the technique. Their passive Essence architecture is heavier than the technique’s displacement budget. All of that concerns where the body ends up. What a Spirit working does to the soul inside it is not on this table.

Durability

Durability is the total EU of destruction a character can absorb, deflect, distribute or endure without structural failure of body, Aether Shell or Soul Crystal. It is a composite of four layered systems.

Layer One: Physical Hardness. Raw material resistance of the body: bone density, muscle fiber tensile strength, skin cohesion, organ integrity as enhanced by Essence saturation. The last line of defense. Governed by Vitality, particularly Constitution, Fortitude and Regeneration.

Layer Two: Aether Shell Shielding. The active Essence field surrounding every woken practitioner, buffering between incoming force and the body, worn as a coat of the practitioner’s own aura. Density governed by Resilience, particularly Integrity, Hardening and Fortification. Held evenly, the Shell absorbs and redistributes what comes in across its entire surface before residual force reaches the body, and a Shell rated at five million EU (5 TJ by the guild’s conversion) absorbs that much across all concurrent attacks before anything reaches flesh. It can be thickened where a blow is expected, at the price of leaving the rest thin, and reading where it went is half of reading the fighter. It is held, not owned: asleep, drugged or taken by surprise, a practitioner keeps the passive hardness of Layer One and loses this layer entirely. “Essence field” is the older wording. As it is read now, the coat that buffers a body is a Shell coat, raw Aether held in the Shell at its wearer’s Body Mastery, whichever road the wearer leads on. A Spirit coat is Essence and wards the soul, not the hide, and a Fate coat is the claim worn as presence, which carries Pressure and settles a room and absorbs nothing in this column.

Layer Three: Technique-Active Defenses. Wards, barriers, Domain-grade exclusion fields actively maintained or deployed. Not included in baseline benchmarks because they are technique-dependent and Essence-costly. A Fixatio-anchored (anchored on Fixatio, the Wellspring of binding) barrier with a fifty-million-EU ceiling (50 TJ as the guild reckons it) adds that on top of baseline, but costs Essence to maintain and can be shattered independently.

Layer Four: Domain Resistance. From Stage VIII, practitioners with active Domains gain a layer derived from Domain coherence. Within their own Domain, local law favors their survival. Not quantified in EU, because it absorbs nothing. It is ground holding another law than the attacker’s, and it degrades the coherence of incoming attacks, cutting their yield before they reach the Shell.

An aside on Layer Two, because it is the most famous art in the world and the most quarreled over. Every culture learned it, and none will use another’s word. On a Guild roll it is Aether Shell Shielding, and in most mouths it is the coat. The Moto say matoi, the wearing, and hold that a coat anyone can see is badly worn. A Kharven rider calls it the hide, and thinks the Moto are showing off by hiding it. The Mahuo say gabot, armor, and teach it before they let a student touch a sword. The lineage halls say hùtǐ, guarding the body, and keep a commentary older than any living elder on how thick is vulgar. The figures below apply to all of them, which no school has ever been pleased to hear.

Why a bolt still kills. The ceilings above read the whole stack with the coat up and the blow seen. Take the coat away, by sleep, by drugging or by surprise, and Layer One stands alone, and Layer One is no line on this table. It turns a fist, a thrown stone and a glancing edge, more surely the higher the Vitality, and it buys time from a bad hit, since saturated flesh closes and saturated bone holds. But a point concentrates what it carries. A steel-bowed arbalest’s bolt of about 440 J, stopped inside five centimeters, pushes about 8,800 newtons, and on a square millimeter of point that is near nine gigapascals, well past any bone. So a bolt nobody saw coming goes into a high-Stage fighter as it goes into anyone, and the throat, the eye and the roof of the mouth have the least to say about it. A bolt seen is a contest on the scale: every missile carries a fixed threat Grade by its kind (sling stone, hunting arrow, war-bow shaft, crossbow bolt, ballista bolt), a worked missile taking its maker’s Grade instead, and the target’s speed meets it on the same scale before the coat does.

The Asymmetry Principle. Durability ceilings are deliberately lower than Strike Force ceilings at every Grade. This is architecture, not oversight. A world where every character could perfectly tank every attack from their own rank would produce stagnation, endless exchanges with no consequence. The offset ensures a committed, well-placed strike from a peer produces meaningful damage if it lands cleanly. Technique, positioning, timing and tactical intelligence exist precisely because raw Durability is not sufficient to ignore raw Strike Force from a peer.

The ratio varies. At F through D on the guard’s Grade, Durability sits at roughly seventy to eighty percent of Strike Force output, so a clean peer hit penetrates but is not catastrophic. At A through S, the ratio narrows to sixty to seventy percent, reflecting denser Shell architecture but also more lethal focused technique. At SSS and above it widens again to roughly fifty percent, because the gap in EU between a clean hit and survival is so vast that the Crystal’s priority shifts from absorption to avoidance, Domain manipulation and reality-grade countermeasures.

Range and Force Coherence

Every expression of force has a coherence envelope: the maximum distance from the body at which it keeps its full destructive output. Beyond that, it degrades, at a rate set by delivery mechanism.

Contact Force has zero range beyond the point of contact. A punch delivers its full output at the knuckle surface and nowhere else. The shock of it can carry through air and stone and extend the effective damage radius, as any blow’s shock does, but it thins as it spreads, losing roughly seventy-five percent of its force per doubling of distance from impact, which the Measurewrights write as the inverse square law.

Projected Force degrades according to the practitioner’s Ardency Cascade Sub-Stat and the Wellspring used. A tightly gathered Coagulatio-based (shaped through Coagulatio, a Wellspring that gathers and densifies) force lance keeps ninety percent of its output across its full effective range. A broadly dispersed Pyreveil-based (shaped through Pyreveil, a fire Wellspring) detonation wave keeps only forty percent of its peak at the edge of its blast radius. Range benchmarks below are the maximum distance at which at least fifty percent of peak Strike Force is delivered. Projected force that breaks matter is a Shell working at any distance and reads Body Mastery, whether it comes from a high Body fighter, a Sage on Wild ground or anyone else. A Spirit worker’s thrown force keeps the same range column and lands on souls only.

Range Without Force

Not every reach is a strike. A practitioner also has to name a thing before he can set a limit on it, hold a limit at a distance from his own body, and keep a volume of ground under his own law rather than somebody else’s. Those three reaches are not the range above and are not governed by it. A working that expresses no force is not bound by the coherence envelope of one that does. They have their own ladder, and it keys to Grade the same way.

Addressing range is the greatest distance at which a practitioner can name a thing precisely enough to impose a boundary on it. Anchor reach is the greatest distance between the boundary he sets and the practitioner, or the ink, blood, voice or hand that anchors him. Authority radius is the radius inside which his own law is the operating law: a Domain’s edge, a ward’s jurisdiction, a Seal’s writ.

The three are one field read at three thresholds, and the thresholds differ by a hundredfold each. A boundary must stand a hundredfold above the background to be imposed at all. The background itself is where a law stops being the operating one. A name is narrow and coherent, so it is read a hundredfold beneath the background, the way a faint tone is picked out of a loud room by somebody who knows which note to listen for. A presence carries less the further it has to carry, and by the Measurewrights’ rule, that a field’s strength falls with the square of distance, each hundredfold step in threshold is a tenfold step in radius. Anchor reach, authority radius and addressing range stand as one to ten to a hundred. The middle rung is the Passive Pressure Field of the table above, which is precisely the distance at which a practitioner’s field still stands above ordinary ground.

Ground changes all three together. Each falls as the square root of the local Aetheric Density, against the reference of ordinary Ambient ground, because a voice is harder to hear in a crowded room, and thick Aether is a crowded room. The square root is the Measurewrights’ rule of thumb, and it has not yet been wrong about how much. That is about ten times further on Aether-thin ground, an eighteenth at Active Concentration (ground near a Wellspring site, thick with Aether), a thousandth at a Veil-Thin Nexus, and about a five-millionth inside a Wellspring Core (a site where a Wellspring’s Aether pools, in common speech, mostly found at the heart of a dungeon). Residue still ringing counts in the same sum, which is why fought-over ground is short ground for hours afterwards. The ground a practitioner would choose for output is the worst ground he could choose for reach, and every veteran knows one of those two facts.

One measure runs the other way, for one road. A Fate Path fighter lives on Range and on the radius of his Domain, and rich Aetheric ground crowds both by the root rule above exactly as it crowds anyone’s. Thread Weight, the measure of how much has been decided on a piece of ground, extends them: on heavy ground his Range and his Domain’s radius run longer than the Density alone allows. The two are read together, and a battlefield that is both rich and heavy takes with one hand and gives with the other (The Three Grounds).

A working target is ten times easier to name. Anything standing a hundredfold above the background is addressed at ten times the range, and anything performing a working at all stands there. Suppression is the same fact run backwards, and it is paid for continuously rather than once.

Below Refraction the anchor is not the body. A working below Stage VII needs voice, hand, ink or blood, and anchor reach is measured from the anchor rather than from the man. This is the whole commercial case for a drawn circle and an inscribed wall. They move the origin to where the boundary is needed, and a good one is worth more than a step on the scale.

The crossover. Projected force range and addressing range cross at the B row. Below it a practitioner can name what he cannot hit. From there upward he can hit what he cannot name, and every doctrine of scouting, spotting and signal lives in that gap.

The failure modes, which are not gentle. Past addressing range a name does not single its target out. Two men are one address, and the working lands on both of them or on the wrong one. Past anchor reach the boundary sets and slips. Past authority radius nothing of the practitioner’s is operating at all, and a Seal sworn on authority he does not hold there closes anyway, with the difference deducted from the speaker.

Aura Pressure as Force of Hand

Aura Pressure is the passive force a character’s metaphysical presence exerts on everything in proximity. Not a technique. Not activated or deactivated. The ambient consequence of a Soul Crystal existing at a given density within a given Temperance Stage. It increases monotonically with Temperance advancement and Dominion, and cannot be reduced below baseline without active suppression.

Between the woken it is read on the Stage gap, with Dominion deciding inside the gap. It shows in the room first: smoke, frost, lamp flames, animals. Then it reaches the bodies. One Stage up bends attention. Two brings sweat and nausea, and the lower party’s Aether Shell begins consuming Essence to resist the ambient load. Three or more drops people, and the Shell of anyone still standing takes passive structural damage simply by being present. A gap of a whole Crystal look changes the weather.

The unwoken take it by the fighter’s rank, because they have no Stage to set against it.

From an Initiate or an Apprentice, Aura Pressure is perceptual rather than physical. The unwoken feel dread, instinctive deference, mild sensory distortion. Psychological and spiritual, no measurable physical force.

From a Journeyman, weight enters the room and measurable physical effects begin. Air density increases within the field. Loose objects vibrate. Surfaces crack under sustained exposure. The ground compresses. Effects scale with the gap between the practitioner’s Pressure and whatever is absorbing it.

From an Expert an unwoken body stops being able to stand under it. An Expert standing within ten meters of a porter exerts a pressure the guild reckons as several hundred kilograms of distributed weight. The porter’s knees bend and their breathing compresses. From a Master whose Pressure has climbed high, the effects on the ground become geological. Stone cracks. Water compresses. Air becomes optically dense. Footsteps leave indentations not from body mass but because Aura pressure exceeds the soil’s load-bearing capacity.

Governed primarily by Dominion, through its Pressure Sub-Stat, with Harmonics Projection setting how far the field carries. Body Path Aura is physically heavier but spatially compact. Fate Path Aura is lighter per square meter but extends across far larger volumes. Spirit Path Aura is perceptually intense, targeting cognition and emotional stability rather than physical structure, and exerts less raw gravitational-analogue force.

An aura’s look comes in layers: the Wellspring a soul has tempered on longest sets the base color, clouded and smoky at the low Stages and clearing as the Stage rises, any others add texture and sound, and a class or a big Trait adds a shape. Pressure is felt in the room first, in the lamps and the knees and the dog going under the table, and then the window reports it to the owner of the knees: [Pressure: heavy, above your Stage]. A stronger Gnosis Perception reads a weaker Resilience Ward. Everything else is an estimate said aloud, and people can hide their pressure or fake it, which has won more tavern brawls than it has lost and ended a few fakers very quickly.

Everyone knows this art and nobody agrees on its manners. A Guild examiner writes Pressure. A southern tavern says a man is leaning on the room, and a Kharven soldier calls it the low sky and thinks hiding it is a kind of lie. The Moto say atsu and consider leaking it indoors an insult to the host. Mahuo halls say nureum, the pressing-down, and the lineage halls wēiyā, which they will tell you is a matter of virtue and which feels the same on the knees. The Marrowkin enter it in the Tally as a load, and argue about who should pay for it.

Path Modifiers to Force of Hand

Path modifiers apply after Temperance and the step are calculated. The magnitude widens with distance from the Physical Plane. Because they come after, they never move a blow’s Grade and never count on the boost cap, and the same goes for a Wellspring’s lens.

Body Path. Direct multiplicative bonus to Strike Force, Durability and Lifting Strength, from +5% at early Temperance Stages to +20% at Stage XII and above. The shortest road and the most immediately reliable. Body Path practitioners at equivalent figures and Stage consistently out-hit, out-tank and out-lift their counterparts by margins that widen with advancement. Trade-off: reduced bonuses to Range and Aura Pressure, so low down they must close distance to leverage the advantage. Those figures are the force table only. High on the Path a Body fighter commands matter by recognition and by law, and their reach grows as far as there is matter to read, still needing existing substance and paying in recoil through body and Crystal.

Spirit Path. Moderate bonus to Strike Force delivered through projection, +5% to +25%, but no bonus and in some configurations a slight penalty to physically delivered Strike Force. They hit hardest at range, and projected force retains coherence across greater distances. That projected force is force on souls and on souls only: it knocks a soul out of true, lays dread on a mind or wounds a Core, it is spent in Essence through the Core, and it marks no matter. The bonus is read in the table’s own units as a measure of what arrives, and it is answered by Resilience Integrity and Anchoring, never by the Durability column. A Spirit worker who wants a stone moved moves it through their own Shell, as a Body working at their own Body Mastery and with no bonus from this line. Durability bonus negligible, replaced by enhanced precision in defensive technique deployment. They survive through correct response rather than raw absorption.

Fate Path. Smallest direct bonus to Strike Force and Durability, largest bonus to Range, Aura Pressure, and the interaction between Force of Hand and Domain authority. Less raw contact damage than a Body Path counterpart at the same figures, but projected force degrades less over distance, Aura extends across larger volumes, and their Domain amplifies all Force of Hand expressions within its territory by a factor scaling with Dominion Radius and Gravity: +10% while the lower of the two stands below 401, +25% from 401, +40% from 551, inside the line only. Once those two stand at 401 and up the compound Domain-amplified advantage can exceed Body Path’s raw output advantage, but only within their own Domain. Outside it, Body Path remains king. These Range, Aura and Domain figures are what a Fate fighter lives on from day to day. A real pull of Fate adds nothing to any figure here: a guard slips or a strap gives, and an ordinary cut at an ordinary EU rating lands where it had no business landing, at a ruinous cost in reserve and often not at all, and landed or failed the pull leaves a knot the world collects from the puller afterward. The far end of the Path, where the true Fate Arts live, works on what has to be so before a blow can land, and its gates are permissions rather than output, as The Three Paths sets out.

Two and three Paths. These bonuses are written for a soul on one Path with a clean seat. A fighter on two Paths takes three-quarters of each Path’s bonus, and a fighter on three takes half of each, for life: time closes the gap in the gates and never this one. So the Body Path’s +20% at Stage XII is +15% on two Paths and +10% on three, and the rest of the table follows the same way, the Fate Domain’s amplification included.

What this table does not say. This Part measures force, so the Body Path looks its best on it, the way a mason looks his best in a quarry. A Spirit working that goes past the Shell is never read against the Durability column, since armor and Durability do not cover the soul, and a Fate fighter who has spoiled the footing never has to stand in front of the Strike column at all. None of the three is the better road, and The Three Paths sets out how each beats each.

Force Interaction Rules

The Differential. The single most decisive factor. A strike a full Grade above the guard it meets wins a direct exchange without meaningful contest; the gap in what arrives is too large for technique or positioning to overcome. Two Grades above is one-sided destruction. Three Grades above means the one receiving it is not participating in a fight. They are experiencing an event.

Within-Step Resolution. Inside the same step, exchanges resolve by technique quality, tactical intelligence, positioning and Sub-Stat architecture. A character at 275 delivers about forty-four times the EU of a character at 176, and by the guild’s reckoning roughly five times the peak force in newtons. Both strikes grade B. The differential is real and significant but not automatically decisive, because technique architecture, Wellspring affinity and Path bonuses can compress or invert the raw stat advantage.

The Sharp-Blunt Asymmetry. A swordsman whose cut grades F, delivering 0.2 MN through an edge contacting roughly one square millimeter produces local pressure of 200 GPa, exceeding the compressive strength of virtually all natural and most Essence-reinforced materials at that Grade. The same 0.2 MN through a fist contacting one hundred square centimeters produces 20 MPa, enough to crack stone but not bisect steel. The Continuum tracks EU delivered for the Grade comparison, and local pressure, which is a matter of edges and stone and the world’s old habits, for what the blow does to the material. The ratio is ten thousand to one at any force, which is why a swordsman’s B-Grade cut parts what a brawler’s B-Grade blow can only dent, and why the brawler shatters structures the swordsman can only slice.

Wellspring-Typed Damage. Force delivered through Wellspring-attuned technique carries that Wellspring’s signature. Pyreveil adds its burning. Fractura adds the breaking point a thing already carries. Vohrin, a Titan’s power worked at Wellspring level and no member of the Sixty, adds thermal extraction. These are not counted in the Strike Force benchmark because they are Wellspring-dependent, but they represent real additional damage that can push what arrives above the raw EU rating. The Path decides how it arrives and what it arrives on: the same Pyreveil burns flesh on contact from a Body Path fist, and thrown from a Spirit Path hand it burns what a soul means and holds and leaves the coat unscorched, so it adds nothing to an EU rating. A Spirit Wellspring adds no such damage on any Path, because it gives no fire and changes what the person can bear instead, and that shows on the Durability side of a fight if it shows here at all. A Fractura-enhanced strike targeting a pre-identified fault line in the target’s Aether Shell can produce Shell failure at outputs thirty to fifty percent below the Shell’s rated ceiling, because Fractura’s idea is that everything has a place where it will go, and a blow laid on a place already found asks the Shell to do only what it was always going to do. The Measurewrights say the strike applies force at the exact frequency and vector that maximizes structural degradation, and their thirty to fifty percent is right.

Force of Hand at the Boundaries

The Apotheosis Threshold. The Zenith rows in these tables sit beside the Grade ladder, not on it: each is keyed to a Stage, where every row above it is keyed to a stat range. At Zenith, Stage XIV, Force of Hand is no longer assessed by conventional metrics. Revelation, Stage XV, keeps its EX+ Grade label, but its force goes unmeasured in the same way; the EX+ row marks where the lettered ladder ends, not a figure a Revelation practitioner is measured by. A punch from a Zenith-level practitioner does not deliver a quantifiable number of EU. It delivers an event the local law must accommodate. Every rating on this page assumes a blow is a finite quantity meeting matter on the world’s old habits; a Zenith soul has become so wholly its own law that the ground under the blow takes that law as its own for as long as the blow lasts. The force is real. The meeting is no longer standard.

The Hollow Floor. Hollow-Grade output is indistinguishable from ordinary mortal capacity, below sixty joules by the guild’s reckoning, a normal human fist at under twelve and a half meters per second. The System tracks a soul here all the same: a sealed Crystal keeps a Level and a few trade skills on a small window, and only Essence, Mastery and a class wait on the Crystal opening.

Overchannel Spikes. The benchmarks above are sustainable output. Overchannel can temporarily spike Strike Force and Lifting Strength by two to five times the sustainable ceiling, depending on the Ardency Overchannel Sub-Stat and the character’s tolerance for Crystal Fracture risk. From the top of a Grade that lands inside the next Grade up on every row, so it reads as one step and spends one of the two the cap allows. Every Overchannel event deposits structural damage that must be healed through rest, alchemical treatment or Temperance advancement. A character who regularly Overchannels accumulates fracture density that eventually becomes permanent. Some fractures become architectural features. Most become liabilities.

Every EU was earned. The Measurewrights will tell you every newton has a name.
