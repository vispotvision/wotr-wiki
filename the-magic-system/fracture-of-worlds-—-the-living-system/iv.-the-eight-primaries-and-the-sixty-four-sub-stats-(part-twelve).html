IV. The Eight Primaries and the Sixty-Four Sub-Stats (Part Twelve)

IV. The Eight Primaries and the Sixty-Four Sub-Stats (Part Twelve)

Nov 28, 202622 min read

The Crystal does not measure what you believe you are. It measures where the pressure went.

On Loaf Day at Ostler’s Cross the Guild read two wrestlers as the same Vitality to the point, and the fight hall, which believes the Guild when it suits the gate, billed it as the fairest match of the year. Bran Anvilchops had put his points into Fortitude and Absorption (two of the Sub-Stats, the finer measures beneath a Primary), and you could break a bench across him, as several had. Moll Thrice-Hanged had put the same total into Hemostasis, Regeneration and Filtration, and had earned her name in three different towns, the last of which still owes her a rope. In a ring of trampled clover under green and yellow bunting, with every carter on the East Road hollering, Bran caught her arm in the first minute and snapped it across his knee, and half the crowd paid out on the spot. Moll looked at the bone standing out of her sleeve, the color of a peeled almond, pushed it back with her other hand, and spat a mouthful of the wolfsbane somebody had put in her ale. By the time the ale-wives had made change the arm was knitting. Two hours in she had been broken nine times and he had not been broken at all, and he was slipping in his own blood from a split over the eye that would not close, gone pale under the sunburn, while she grinned at him with two teeth that had not been there when the bell rang. He dropped at sundown, undefeated in every particular but the last. Their registers still read the same. Ostler’s Cross now pays a clerk to copy Sub-Stats onto the door of the fight hall, and the Guild charges double for the reading.

A Primary Stat is the combined total of its eight Sub-Stats. Points are spent on Sub-Stats only; a Primary is never purchased and holds no value of its own. The Temperance ceiling and the Path gates bind the Sub-Stat. The Primary is the arithmetic afterward, and an assessor reads it off the mean of its eight, or of Dominion’s seven counted entries, set against the hidden scale. No letter is ever put on a person: a normal look at the owner’s window shows the eight Primaries with their totals; a Sub-Stat appears when it is used or when it moves, and the full set remains to study. Life spends most of the points, by what the person does, and a few are the owner’s to place at each Stage.

Primary Stats are the broad categories. Sub-Stats are the grain. Two characters at identical Primary totals can be radically different practitioners because their distributions tell different stories. One may have Vitality concentrated in Fortitude and Absorption, producing a frame that refuses to break but bleeds badly and heals slowly. Another may have the same total across Hemostasis, Regeneration and Filtration, producing a body that breaks readily, reconstructs itself and shrugs off poison. The Primary says they are equally durable. The Sub-Stats say they are durable in completely different ways.

Consolidated from one hundred and twenty to sixty-four. The original architecture carried fifteen Sub-Stats per Primary, of which a substantial share were restatements of each other under different names. Dominion Density and Dominion Stability both measured how hard the law is to break. Vitality Contuition and Vitality Filtration both measured poison resistance. Gnosis Sapience and Gnosis Fluency both measured Parunic (Parun, the written language of magical law) construction. Those duplicates are merged, and the merged entry carries the senior name.

Where an old name appears on an existing sheet, read it as the entry that absorbed it. Each entry below names, in italics, the old Sub-Stats it absorbed.

I. Vitality

The measured structural integrity of the physical vessel.

Vitality governs how the body resists, absorbs and persists against destructive forces across both physical and metaphysical vectors. It does not measure fighting capacity. It measures the capacity to remain a coherent physical organism while fighting capacity is expressed through other stats. A character with high Vitality and nothing else does not win fights. They survive them.

Fortitude Resistance to blunt trauma, impact force and structural breakdown, across bone, joint and connective tissue. Whether a strike folds the frame, snaps the limb or dislocates the shoulder. The first number consulted when a clean hit lands and the question is whether the recipient is still standing. Absorbs Ossification and Tenacity.

Absorption Capacity to dissipate incoming force laterally rather than receive the full transfer inward. Where Fortitude decides whether the body breaks, Absorption decides whether the body moves.

Constitution The raw pool of physical health, and resistance to attrition: exhaustion, oxygen deprivation, starvation, environmental grind over a long engagement. Why one fighter is still swinging ten minutes in while the other is breathing through their mouth. Absorbs Endurance.

Hemostasis Speed and stability of wound closure and blood retention. The difference between a wound that bleeds out in minutes and one that seals in seconds. Operates during combat, not after.

Regeneration Rate of physical restoration outside active combat, and the degree to which healed wounds come back structurally reinforced rather than weakened. Not a healing working; the body’s own reconstruction speed, and whether it learns. Absorbs Scarring.

Filtration Resistance to toxins, venoms, alchemical corrosives, corrupted Essence bleed and Aetheric contamination. The body’s capacity to recognize hostile intrusion at the cellular and Essence-lattice level and reject it before systemic degradation. Absorbs Contuition.

Tolerance Resistance to environmental vectors the body must simply refuse: extreme temperature, gravity distortion, spatial compression, Essence-induced weight amplification, and the absence of Aether in Wellspring-dead zones. A character with high Tolerance does not feel comfortable in a furnace. Their tissues refuse to denature at the rate physics demands. Absorbs Gravitas and Nullity.

Threshold The degree to which the body filters or suppresses pain signals during combat. High Threshold does not eliminate pain. It prevents pain from translating into functional degradation.

II. Ardency

The measured output potential of a soul through the layer in use: raw Aether through the Shell on the Body Path, Essence through the Core on the Spirit Path, Thread through the Attraction Layer on the Fate Path.

Ardency governs not what the body can do unaided, but what a soul can put out through the layer it leans on. The three roads spend three different things. A Body fighter’s Ardency is raw Aether (mana, in the plainer books), drawn in, packed unrefined in the Shell as the Charge, shaped there by the Wellspring’s lens and driven out through the Shell. A Spirit worker’s is Essence (spiritual energy), Aether refined and stamped in the Crystal and spent through the Core. A Fate puller’s is Thread, pulled down through the Attraction Layer and paid for out of the soul’s own share. The letter is read the same way on all three, and a soul that walks two roads uses the one Ardency for both. A practitioner with low Ardency and high Vitality is a wall. High Ardency and low Vitality is a trebuchet. What separates a weapon from a practitioner is having both.

Flux — Raw destructive and constructive output of active expressions. The damage ceiling. How much force the working carries when it arrives. Flux is the same number on every Path, but it does not do the same work on every job: the Mastery of the Path in use sets the yield (η, the share of a spend that arrives as effect, as a distillation has its yield). A Spirit master with a great Flux who turns it on matter works it through his Body Mastery, which on a road he has only studied reads two rungs behind and never past II, and gets that poor rung for his trouble, and most of that great Flux burns off in the Shell’s furnace as heat on his own skin. The Measurewrights’ way of putting it is that Flux says how much you can throw and Mastery says how much of it lands.

Alacrity — Casting speed, cooldown compression and reflexive casting window availability. The tempo axis.

Depth — How deeply the caster synchronizes with an active Wellspring, how unmistakably the working carries that Wellspring’s qualitative character, and whether two Wellspring qualities can be held in one expression without resonance conflict. Whether you are borrowing from a Wellspring or speaking its language. Absorbs Saturation and Duality.

Penetration — Capacity to pierce defensive fields, Soul Crystal wards, metaphysical barriers and worked armor, where a strike is read on this figure against the piece’s own Grade and proof; plain armor fights by its kind. High Flux with low Penetration hits like a padded wall.

Compression — On the Body Path, the mastery stat: how cleanly a fighter works Aether unrefined in the Shell. The Charge is raw, and raw Aether handled badly is loud and hot, the furnace roaring for little, so a Body fighter’s Compression is the difference between a blow thrown with steam pouring off the shoulders and the same blow thrown with a dry back. On the Spirit Path it keeps its old sense, the degree to which Essence is packed before release. Either way it buys the same three things: the same destruction for less cost and with less signature, since a compressed working is quieter in the Aether and harder to identify before it lands. Absorbs Radiance.

Cascade — Reach. How wide a working spreads when spread is intended, and how far it travels before attenuation degrades its structure. High Cascade covers battlefields. Low Cascade covers opponents. Absorbs Spectral.

Overchannel — Maximum temporary output achievable by pulling beyond sustainable thresholds, the minimum investment required for a significant destructive release, and how much of a failed or interrupted working’s rebound comes back as usable output rather than damage. How far past safe before the engine eats itself. Absorbs Detonation and Conversion.

Density — Structural weight and coherence of anything a working leaves standing: inscriptions, active glyph-chains, summoned constructs, bound spirits. Whether your ward outlasts the siege and whether your summon is a suggestion or a participant. Absorbs Inscription.

III. Gnosis

The architecture of the mind across both cognitive and metaphysical axes.

Gnosis is the stat of understanding. Not wisdom, which is a product of experience and Temperance, but the raw cognitive and perceptual machinery that lets a soul process what it encounters at the speed and resolution required to act. Whether they act correctly on what they see is a question for other stats.

Perception — Sensory acuity and Aetheric threat detection. Awareness of displacement, Veil distortion, concealed entities and incoming force vectors before they resolve, and accurate modeling of the surrounding geometry including distorted architecture and Domain interiors. Absorbs Cartography.

An aside, since this entry and Empathy and Sense are between them the famous art of sensing, known in every tavern and named differently in each. A Guild examiner splits it across the three. A Kharven soldier asks whether you can smell a man’s stack, by which he means how much the man is carrying. The Moto speak of kehai, the sign of someone there, and think it rude to read a guest before the tea. The Mahuo say nunchi, and the lineage halls shénshí, spirit-sense, with diagrams. Each school will prove to you that its way is the true one, and each has lost someone to a Feint. The one hard law under the quarrel is this: a stronger Gnosis Perception reads a weaker Resilience Ward. Everything else is an estimate said aloud, and people can hide it or fake it.

Acuity Tactical cognition and real-time processing of multi-vector combat environments. Positioning, sequence recognition, adaptive strategy, and the speed at which an unfamiliar pattern is internalized mid-fight. Absorbs Adaptation.

Forecast Combat sequence forecasting from kinetic, spatial and Aetheric pattern recognition. Tells you what is about to happen. Whether you can do anything about it is a Dexterity problem.

Fluency Precision and vocabulary depth in the Parunic inscription language, structural logic of glyph-chain construction, and sensitivity to Command Weight and spoken oath-structures as force rather than sound. The difference between a working that does what you meant and one that does what you said. Absorbs Sapience and Resonance.

Retention Clarity and accessibility of the Crystalline Memory structure under pressure, and the number of complete working structures held loaded in active cognitive memory without external inscription. Absorbs Memorium.

Analysis Reading Essence signatures, Crystal fractures, Wellspring composition and corruption grade from limited observational data, on contact or at proximity, and reading matter the same way: the load a beam is carrying, the grain of a blade, the flaw in a casting, what an ore or an alloy is made of. A Body fighter’s Analysis is how they know where a wall will go before they hit it, and a healed soul wound shows on it as a pale place in the Core. The diagnostic eye. Absorbs Diagnosis.

Cognition Capacity to engage law-level phenomena, Archonic expressions and higher-dimensional constructs without perceptual fragmentation, and resistance to glamours, false spatial constructs and psychic misdirection. Whether a Principle-level truth enlightens you or shatters you. Absorbs Warding.

Vigilance Passive detection of approaching Temperance ceilings, Essence overspend proximity and the warnings that precede Dissonance (the eleventh Stage, where power and identity clash) events. The stat that reads your own architecture rather than the room.

IV. Dexterity

The body’s capacity to occupy, vacate and redirect across spatial geometry with precision and efficiency.

Not how strong the body is; how correctly it uses the space it occupies. A high-Dexterity fighter does not hit harder. They hit where the opening is, arrive before the defense closes and leave before the counter launches.

Celerity Raw movement speed across physical and Aetheric displacement, and maximum acceleration over short distances from standing or braced. Travel and the first step’s lethality. Absorbs Burst.

Reflex Speed of involuntary response to sensory or Aetheric threat data. What moves the body before the mind has finished deciding.

Finesse Precision and accuracy of technique, with the empty hand and with an implement. Edge control, draw speed, footwork integration, and altering the path of incoming force through timed contact rather than absorption. What separates a person holding a sword from a swordsman. Absorbs Dexterity Acuity and Deflection.

Economy Maximum positional advantage from minimum motion, balance and orientation during non-grounded states, and the rate at which stance and positional integrity are restored after a miss or a hit. How long the vulnerability window lasts. Absorbs Equilibrium and Recovery.

Evasion Passive tendency to occupy positions that conflict with incoming attack vectors, separate from conscious reaction. The body drifts toward safety the way water finds the lowest point.

Grapple Close-range physical control, joint manipulation, leverage and transition speed. Dexterity at zero distance, where the arena is the opponent’s body.

Feint Depth and believability of deceptive movement, and suppression of kinetic noise, Aura spill and sound output. How many layers of false commitment before a genuine technique releases, and whether the approach was heard at all. Absorbs Silence.

Sequence Number of distinct technical actions executable within a single engagement window without mechanical degradation, including separate simultaneous actions with different limbs. How many clean strikes fit inside one breath. Absorbs Independence.

V. Harmonics

The degree to which a soul’s frequency coheres with the ambient metaphysical substrate and other living resonances.

Not charisma in the human sense, but the raw degree to which a soul’s frequency is legible to, compatible with and influential upon the frequencies around it. A character with high Harmonics does not necessarily speak well. They resonate well. The Continuum hears them more clearly than it hears others.

Attunement Affinity and synchronization strength with bound Wellsprings, retention of previously encountered frequencies for re-attunement without re-contact, and the clarity with which a working reflects the soul that authored it. Reduces the cost of drawing Aether in, increases maximum invocation depth. The difference between borrowing power and being trusted with it. Absorbs Memory and Fidelity.

Projection Weight, suppressive reach and range of the passive resonance field. How far the soul’s voice carries when it is not trying to speak. Your Aura arrives in the room before you do. Absorbs Harmonics Density.

Breadth Total number of Wellsprings maintainable in active harmonization simultaneously, and the capacity to hold several of them inside a single working as layered effect profiles. The difference between a note and a symphony. Absorbs Chord.

Stability Consistency of resonance output under environmental pressure, emotional volatility, hostile interference or Wellspring-absent ground, and the speed of re-establishing it after a break. Prevents Harmonic Drift. How long you are deaf to the Wellsprings after someone drowns out your note. Absorbs Recovery and Voidance.

Empathy Passive sensitivity to emotional and Essence states within Aura range. You feel the room change before anyone has spoken.

Synergy The degree to which ambient Wellspring activity feeds the character’s Harmonic state and the Harmonic field synchronizes with and amplifies allied resonances. You fight stronger on rich ground and allies hit harder standing near you. Absorbs Confluence.

Axis Structural integrity of established Spirit Axes; how much Essence flows across bonded relational connections. Bonds carrying metaphysical weight, not just emotional weight.

Suppression Consciously compressing or masking the resonance field to reduce detectability and conceal Temperance tier. The muffle on the soul’s bell, and a skill that tires the user for as long as it is held.

VI. Resilience

The structural coherence of the Soul Crystal against forces that target existence itself.

Where Vitality protects the body, Resilience protects the soul. Low on the scale the distinction barely matters, because most threats are physical. High on it it is the only thing that matters, because the threats that kill Masters are conceptual intrusions, reality-overwriting effects, Domain inversions and forces that target the right of a thing to continue existing.

Integrity The Crystal’s resistance to fracture, Dissonance collapse and catastrophic resonance failure under overspending or hostile pressure, and the degree to which prior fractures have been processed into structural reinforcement. The foundation every other Sub-Stat rests on. Wounds that become walls. Absorbs Scarring.

Ward Defense against law-breaking, reality-overwriting and Domain-erasure forces, including techniques aimed at collapsing, inverting or redirecting the character’s own Domain. What stands between a character and an effect that says they do not exist. Absorbs Severance.

Persistence Resistance to forced cessation, obliteration and conceptual erasure, and to Nospheric intrusion, Oblivion-class pressure and the corrosive qualities of inverted or annihilation-aspected Wellsprings. The soul’s refusal to stop being when something with sufficient authority tells it to. Absorbs Resilience Nullity.

Anchoring Resistance to dissolution, corruption, possession and the Mirage Identity threshold, and to memory erasure, false implantation and Anamnesis-hostile intrusion. Whether identity holds its shape and its continuity under pressure. Absorbs Coherence.

Fortification Compressing incoming hostile Essence and redirecting it through internal structure rather than absorbing it as damage, and resistance to self-generated blowback from Overchannel rebound and interrupted high-tier workings. Converting a wound, or a mistake, into something the Crystal can metabolize. Absorbs Insulation.

Hardening Resistance to hostile resonance imposition and forced re-attunement, and passive rejection of Erebus-class contamination, possession vectors and gradual metaphysical degradation. The Crystal’s refusal to vibrate at someone else’s frequency. Absorbs Immunity.

Oath Structural durability of active vows, contracts and Fate-anchor oaths under attempts to sever, invert or weaponize them.

Continuity Resistance to spatial displacement, forced dimensional transit and Domain-class intrusion, and to sequence disruption, localized timeline alteration and temporal dislocation targeting causal continuity. Absorbs Dimensional.

VII. Tempering

The potential axis. The measure of how far a soul can go and how cleanly it gets there.

Every other Primary measures what a character is right now. Tempering measures the structural quality of the vessel that will carry them to what they are next. High Tempering does not mean more power. It means a cleaner, deeper, more structurally sound container for whatever power is accumulated. The Primary is named for the act: to temper is to melt the Soul Crystal, which is the soul itself, down and rebuild each layer, refining Essence and making it stronger while the body gains Traits. These eight entries say how well the vessel takes that.

Clarity Structural cleanliness and internal density of the Crystal’s architecture. Governs output noise, precision degradation at high output, Essence waste, and resistance to Essence pressing outward into flesh and bone faster than Temperance can shape it. Low Clarity produces Overgrowth. Absorbs Tempering Compression and Resistance.

Overflow Maximum sustainable output before Backlash, Dissonance or Essence-burn triggers. The red line. Everything past it costs structural integrity.

Maturity Integration of prior stage trauma, resolution of internal contradictions, the speed at which fractures and failed transitions are metabolized into architecture rather than persistent damage, the rate at which transition conditions are recognized, and the efficiency with which bloodline traits and ancestral Geasa activate cleanly rather than ambush. The primary Sub-Stat consulted when evaluating Threshold readiness. Absorbs Acceleration, Processing and Inheritance.

Coherence Alignment between chosen Path and lived metaphysical behavior. Dissonance between stated Path and actual expression degrades Tempering efficiency. The structural prerequisite for Threshold eligibility. The Crystal’s internal honesty assay.

Tempering Yield Raw volume of Essence produced per Temperance Stage relative to universal baseline, the efficiency of converting ambient Wellspring activity into usable internal Essence, and the gap between generation and availability. On a Body-led soul read Charge for Essence throughout: the same figure says how fast the Shell packs raw Aether. Reload speed for the soul. Absorbs Conversion and Latency.

Ceiling Theoretical maximum Stage achievable given bloodline, Crystal architecture and Wellspring compatibility. Structural potential rather than current attainment.

Capacity Maximum number of Wellspring harmonizations the Crystal can hold without resonance conflict. The Crystal’s bandwidth for metaphysical languages.

Longevity Resistance to accelerated metaphysical aging and erosion from high-tier operation, dimensional pressure and extended exposure to absolute-scale forces. How long the soul remains structurally viable at the ceiling it has reached.

VIII. Dominion

The degree of structural command a soul exercises over local metaphysical law.

Low on the scale, Dominion is mild ambient influence, the instinctive way a strong presence bends conversations and adjusts posture. High on it, it is literal reality modification: the character’s inner law becoming local law, overriding the world’s ambient habits within their territory. Every Archon was, before they were an Archon, a soul whose Dominion reached the point where the Continuum stopped treating their will as a request and began treating it as legislation.

Radius — Active reach of the Domain when formed, the speed at which its boundary extends during active formation, and the capacity to push a partial expression outward as an influence field short of full formation. How far your word carries, how fast it gets there, and what it can do before it has committed. Absorbs Expansion and Dominion Projection.

Sovereignty — Control over local geometry, distance behavior and trajectory interference, and the maximum complexity of law imposable through the Domain. Whether the rules inside your territory are simple physical rules or encoded conceptual truths. Absorbs Reach.

Sense — Perceiving and interacting with higher-order phenomena: dimensional seams, conceptual laws, Archon-tier pressures, Veil fold proximity, Realm boundaries, and the passive detection of tears and unstable junctions in spatial fabric. Where the seams are and where reality is thin. Absorbs Dominion Acuity and Rift. Renamed from Acuity to end the collision with Gnosis Acuity.

Gravity — The structural weight of the Domain’s law and its resistance to collapse, hostile inversion or dissolution, in calm ground and in a hurricane. Your law is heavy. It does not move when someone pushes it, and it does not dissolve because the Realm is hostile. Absorbs Dominion Density and Dominion Anchoring. Renamed from Stability to end the collision with Harmonics Stability.

Pressure — Oppressive environmental weight generated by concentrated will. Passive suppression of weaker Aetheric expressions, resolving within Dominion range. The weight of your presence when you stop being polite about it.

Command — The degree to which spoken intent carries metaphysical authority, causing ambient Aether to reorganize, oaths to register with the Continuum, and subordinate spiritual entities to respond. The voice of sovereignty made audible.

Fate — Integration with the Plane of Fate, the control of Attraction Force and Obsession Force and of the workings of reality itself. Governs reach and stability of the Attraction Layer, how far a soul can bend probability, covenant-binding capacity, and the mechanical weight of sworn oaths and of the blessings a soul can give. How loudly the Continuum hears you when you make a promise, and how far it lets you rewrite what was going to happen. It is what pushes on the rare occasion a Fate fighter moves what a blow or a march depends on, and the target’s Persistence and Continuity are what push back.

Throne — Latent compatibility between accumulated doctrine and the Hollow Thrones of defunct Archons. Not a measure of power. A measure of destiny. Throne carries no Temperance ceiling and is excluded from the Dominion total and the Dominion mean, since its floor sits above every ceiling in the Stage table and including it would distort the Primary beyond use. Dominion’s mean is the total divided by seven. It is carried as its own line on the character sheet.
