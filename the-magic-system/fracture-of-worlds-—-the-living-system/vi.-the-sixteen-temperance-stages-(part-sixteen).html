VI. The Sixteen Temperance Stages (Part Sixteen)

VI. The Sixteen Temperance Stages (Part Sixteen)

Nov 17, 202612 min read

“You call Temperance a buff. It is a confession of how much you’ve survived.”

The Ruan Hall keeps its festival gongs for Lantern Night, and they have rung out of season exactly once. Ruan Ziyan, a physician of the hall, fifty-one years old and at Stage IV, known for a foul temper and good stitches, was lighting the third row of lanterns on the quay when a Well-spawn (a creature born where a Wellspring’s Aether pools) the height of the granary came up out of the canal, its mask the green of old bronze, and every outer disciple in sight remembered an errand. She went at it with a lamp pole. She held it for as long as it took the bridge to clear, and then it took her left arm off at the elbow with something like a shrug, and she went down on her knees among the broken paper and the burning oil with nine rows of ancestral tablets watching through the hall doors. Those nearest heard her Crystal go, crack after crack along every grudge she had ever kept. Her aura, a modest apple green all her life, split into three colors at once and roared up past the eaves, and every gong in the hall went off by itself.

She stood up laughing and sobbing, called the thing the son of a dead turnip in full archaic grammar, and broke its mask with the pole. The arm stayed gone, and for most of a year she wept at the sight of soup and could not be trusted with a needle, and the hall entered the night in its book under a title nine words long. A Guild roll writes all of that as Stage V, Splintering. Below are all sixteen under the tidy headings the Guild gives them, and a physician of the Ruan Hall has some notes on the word tidy.

Each Stage is a qualitative transformation in the architecture of the Crystal (the Soul Crystal, which is the soul itself). The Stages are structural reorganizations the Crystal undergoes when the pressure of accumulated experience exceeds the architecture’s current capacity: tempering melts it down and rebuilds each layer, refines Essence and makes it stronger, and the body gains Traits. That pressure is brought to the break by three roads, crisis, seclusion before the Threshold, or tribulation, and most practitioners walk some of each; the Catalyst named under each Stage is the moment the break is met, whichever road led there. A lived breakthrough passes through the four stages of the Great Work in its own Path’s terms, nigredo, albedo, citrinitas and rubedo, the soul broken down, purified, woken and fixed through the layer it leans on, which is what Ruan Ziyan was doing on her knees in the burning oil. A Draft or a blessing can supply that Catalyst instead, and then the Stage has simply been bought. A bought Stage opens the door and no more: a Release must still be found and mastered by fighting, and sects teach what money cannot buy (Part Eight has the terms). Each Stage alters how every stat behaves, not by adding numerical bonuses but by changing what the numbers are structurally permitted to mean.

The first fourteen constitute canonical mortal progression, Murmuring through Zenith. Stages XV and XVI, Revelation and Apex, extend beyond the canonical ceiling into the Apotheosis approach (the climb from mortal soul toward becoming an Archon).

Inner Refinement, Stages I through VII

Stage I — Murmuring (Tier 1 · Initiate)

The Crystal has opened. Essence (the soul’s own substance, spent as power) stirs but cannot be directed. The practitioner feels power as noise, a hum beneath the skin that responds to emotion but not to intent. All Sub-Stats (the sixty-four finer stats beneath the eight Primaries) present but volatile. Output unreliable and heavily mood-dependent. Max Grade E. Ceiling 50. (Max Grade: the highest letter rank of output; Ceiling: the highest stat value.) The world has noticed you. You have not yet noticed the world noticing.

Stage II — Welling (Tier 2 · Apprentice)

Essence rises and spills before it can be contained. The practitioner feels the direction of their power but cannot control its volume. Techniques misfire regularly. The Aether Shell (the soul’s outer layer, which passes power out into the world) is forming but porous. Max Grade D. Ceiling 100. Catalyst (the event that sets off the breakthrough): first genuine contact with the Aether of a Wellspring (one of the sixty governing ideas that transform how a soul uses Aether, each an alchemical and a spiritual transformation with its own type of Aether) under personal pressure.

Stage III — Ascension (Tier 3 · Journeyman)

First true awakening. The Path (the layer of the Crystal a soul has begun to lean on) becomes visible. The Crystal begins expressing a coherent identity rather than reactive noise. Techniques can be constructed deliberately. The practitioner transitions from surviving their power to directing it. Max Grade C. Ceiling 175. Catalyst: first technique developed independently from instruction.

Stage IV — Flourishing (Tier 4 · Adept)

Power blooms. Identity stabilizes. The resonance signature becomes recognizable and consistent. Techniques cohere into a personal style rather than isolated expressions. The first genuine Spirit Axis bonds (bonds with another soul that carry real weight) become mechanically possible. The First Release (the one concept taken into a transformed body) opens from this Stage, to be found and mastered by fighting. Max Grade B. Ceiling 275. Catalyst: formation of the first genuine Spirit Axis bond.

Stage V — Splintering (Tier 5 · Expert)

The soul cracks open to become something larger. The first crisis Stage. The architecture has reached the limit of its initial configuration and must break to grow. Characters who pass through emerge with deeper but less stable power. Characters who fail fracture and may lose Sub-Stat progress. Max Grade B; allocation stops at 275. Ceiling 350, the band from 276 to 350 the instability zone, reachable only under strain. Catalyst: surviving contact with an entity or force more than two full Tier Grades above the character’s current ceiling. The Crystal fractures along emotional lines.

Stage VI — Glory (Tier 5 · Expert)

Peak of the inner phase. The self burns bright and is noticed. The Aura’s weight on a room, felt Stage by Stage all the way up, can no longer be mistaken for anything else. Techniques carry the weight of identity rather than improvisation. The Crystal has processed Splintering’s fractures into features. The three class ladders (Aether, Essence and Thread Class, each ranking how cleanly one layer of the soul passes power through it) can first be read, the soul makes its first offer of a named Class, and the Echo field forms. Max Grade A. Ceiling 400. Catalyst: willingly accepting genuine soul-cost to preserve something valued above the character’s own structural integrity.

Stage VII — Refraction (Tier 5 · Expert)

Dual sight. The soul sees itself from outside. The practitioner begins perceiving their own Crystal architecture as an object of study rather than an unconscious medium. Domain (a space where a soul’s inner law becomes local reality) seeds can be called and held from here, through the Second Release that opens at this Stage. Gnosis (the Primary stat for perception and understanding) Sub-Stats undergo a qualitative jump as self-awareness extends to metaphysical structure. Projected fields are coherent but still depend on sustained presence. Traits (a belief hardened into a personal law of the soul) externalize. Max Grade A; allocation stops at 400, with late push toward S entry. Ceiling 475, the band from 401 reachable only under strain. Catalyst: first Domain seed activation, a one-time flicker of the person’s own Trait at the Threshold, which needs no Release.

Outer Influence, Stages VIII through XIV

Stage VIII — Transcendence (Tier 6 · Master)

The world is read as symbol. The practitioner resolves a core internal contradiction documented in the Crystal’s fracture record, a wound carried as structural damage and consciously processed into architecture. A true Domain can form from here, grown by living and called aloud, a piece of the lattice that accepts the soul’s signature. Techniques begin channeling forces larger than the individual. Ardency (the stat for raw power output) and Harmonics (the stat for pulling on outside Aether) undergo a qualitative shift as external power sources become structurally integrated. Max Grade S. Ceiling 550.

Stage IX — Invocation (Tier 6 · Master)

The practitioner calls on Wellsprings, Titans or Archonic law (the law of the Archons, the world’s fourteen great powers) as co-authors of their expression. Techniques are no longer purely personal. The Third Release, open from this Stage, consolidates the Domain into a Realm capable of holding other Domains. Pacts, artifacts and law begin to imprint on terrain. Max Grade S; allocation stops at 550, with late push toward SS entry. Ceiling 625, the band from 551 reachable only under strain. Catalyst: a first flicker of a Realm at the Threshold, once, on the same terms as the seed.

Stage X — Realization (Tier 6 · Master)

Truth as lived consequence. The world answers back. The Domain becomes a reality the Continuum (the whole fabric of reality) must accommodate rather than merely permit. Emotional and moral stability begins having direct environmental consequences. Max Grade SS. Ceiling 725. Catalyst: Domain achieving Continuum accommodation, the moment reality stops resisting the character’s inner law and starts adapting to it.

Stage XI — Dissonance (Tier 7 · Grandmaster)

Power and identity clash. The second crisis Stage and the most dangerous. The Crystal has accumulated enough pressure to threaten its own coherence. Characters who pass through emerge with Crystals fractured but denser than clean advancement could produce. Tempering Overflow, Ardency Overchannel and Resilience Persistence can only reach their true ceiling in characters who have passed through Dissonance. The fractures are not damage. They are architectural features. Passive miracles leak. Regions behave differently around the character. Max Grade SS; allocation stops at 725, with fracture risk above 700. Ceiling 750, structurally unstable, the band from 726 reachable only under strain. Catalyst: deliberately entering contact with a force capable of ending the character’s existence and remaining coherent long enough to choose how to meet it.

Stage XII — Emanation (Tier 7 · Grandmaster)

Emergence from Dissonance. The Crystal is denser and cleaner than before entry. Fractures have become features. The practitioner radiates outward. Domains form naturally around them. Presence alters ambient metaphysical conditions without deliberate activation. Traits become local law. Dominion (the stat for presence and pressure on others) undergoes its most significant jump. The character transitions from projecting power to embodying it. Max Grade SSS. Ceiling 950.

Stage XIII — Principality (Tier 8 · Archmaster)

A local force of nature. First World Spirit threshold. Domain coherence reaches the level where the Continuum formally registers a law the character embodies. Their doctrine becomes a recognized structural feature of local reality. Biomes carry their doctrine. The Crystal self-writes. Dominion Sovereignty and Command undergo their final qualitative transformation. The character is no longer merely powerful. They are legislating. Max Grade X. Ceiling 1,200.

Stage XIV — Zenith (Tier 8 · Archmaster)

Furthest reach of canonical mortal coherence. Full World Spirit. The accumulated doctrine becomes recognizable to a Hollow Throne, and the Throne resonates with them. Zenith is not a breakthrough. It is recognition, and recognition from a Hollow Throne carries weight no amount of power alone can replicate. A purse can buy the Catalyst here as for any Stage, and the Throne must still recognize the buyer, so money buys the attempt and not the seat. Reality acclimates to the character. Story becomes physics.

Zenith does not end. It is the state of a soul that has reached the upper boundary of mortal architecture and remains there, pressing against what it has not yet become. Speed, attack output and all conventional metrics require metaphysical recalibration to even register. The character does not fight. They exist, and their existence has consequences that other characters survive or fail to survive. Max Grade EX. Ceiling 1,500.

Beyond Canon, Stages XV and XVI

Stage XV — Revelation (Tier 9 · Paragon)

Genuine exchange with an Archon or Titan at the level of participant rather than witness or casualty. The Crystal has become dense enough to hold Archon-grade pressure without collapsing. All Sub-Stats gain access to their EX+ ceiling. The first step of the Apotheosis approach, where the distinction between mortal soul and Principle begins to dissolve.

At Revelation, output is no longer measurable against mortal-scale benchmarks. Techniques do not merely carry force. They carry precedent. A Revelation-stage working does not just happen. It becomes something that has always been possible, a retroactive edit to local metaphysical grammar. Max Grade EX+. Ceiling 2,200.

Stage XVI — Apex (Tier 9 · Paragon)

Holding a Principle in the Soul Crystal without fracturing. The accumulated doctrine has reached a density where the Crystal can temporarily contain a truth normally reserved for beings that have completed Apotheosis.

Apex is perpetual. It does not end. It is the state of a soul pressing against what it has not yet become, with enough structural coherence to press back. No formal upper ceiling beneath Apotheosis. The character is not waiting to become an Archon. They are demonstrating whether they should. Catalyst: holding a Principle in the Crystal, even momentarily, even imperfectly. Recognition from a Hollow Throne that has not merely resonated but responded.
