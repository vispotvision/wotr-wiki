VII. Aether Class, Paths and the Lens, Aether Flow (Parts Seventeen–Nineteen)

VII. Aether Class, Paths and the Lens, Aether Flow (Parts Seventeen–Nineteen)

Nov 17, 202632 min read

Nobody at the Saltgate Cod Fair had ever seen a line like it, because the bookies had never been asked to price a coin toss with fists. Sissel and Gunnild Scarbrow were twin sisters out of the Tar Street Bellows, a fire school with yellow-and-black colors and a song about its own bad manners, and they stood at the seventh of the sixteen Stages (the rungs of the soul’s refinement), both of them. The Guild’s Measurewright (an assayer of souls and workings) had read them that morning in front of a paying crowd and called out two sheets that matched to the last figure. Even money, then, on the strangest card of the summer. Sissel went first, because Sissel always went first: she planted her boots, filled her chest and threw a fire the color of a ripe persimmon, a great roaring sheaf of it that came with a fistful of sparks and a stink of scorched hair and set the Chandlers’ Guild’s bunting alight twenty paces off. The crowd howled with joy. Gunnild answered from the hip with a thread of white flame no wider than a bootlace and no louder than a kettle, which crossed the ring without lighting anything at all until it reached the oak post behind her sister’s shoulder, and the post fell over in two pieces.

It took three exchanges. Sissel finished flat on her back in a smoking yellow shirt, short one tooth and the ends of both braids, laughing so hard the surgeon could not get a hand on her, while the Tar Street end sang the whole of the bad-manners song at her sister and everyone with money on her went looking for whoever had read those sheets. The sheets had been right. They gave what each sister could put forward. A third of what Sissel put forward had gone into bunting and noise on its way out of her, and Gunnild had lost about one part in twelve. On the Saltgate quays they call a clean delivery a clear throat and a leaking one a wet throat, and they will tell you which you have for the price of a drink. Each sister had known for years what her own window said, Bonfire Herald on a Muridic Shell for one and Wick-Trimmer on a Resonant Shell for the other, and neither had thought it the bookies’ business. The Guild’s word for the whole of it is Aether Class, and it opens this Part.

Part Seventeen — The Aether Class System

“Power is not what you hold, but how cleanly you pass the infinite through the finite.”

The Class the Soul Is Offered

At thresholds the soul is offered a named class that fits how the person has lived. A gate-sergeant is offered one thing and the woman who robbed him another, and the name arrives in the window in the owner’s own tongue. A class gives abilities, shapes growth and evolves by how it is lived, each time the person’s leading ladder rises, so the same Wick-Trimmer may come out a rung later under a name nobody else has ever been offered. The one wild concept stays the person’s own: the class is what the soul makes of a life, and the concept, soup or debt or doors, is what the person goes on to do with it. Crews squabble over class names and taverns rank them. Measurewrights will tell you for free that the offer proves the Continuum reads biography, and for a fee that it proves no such thing.

The first offer comes at Glory, Stage VI, where the Shell first reads on the coil; Stages I through V are unclassed. The class has no ladder of its own. Three ladders stand beside it, one to each layer of the Crystal, and the first of them, Aether Class, is the Shell’s.

What Aether Class Measures

Temperance (the soul’s inner refinement, climbed in sixteen Stages) measures refinement of the Essence Core (the soul’s innermost layer and the center of its identity) on the Soul Plane (the layer of existence where law becomes felt truth). Tier Grade measures raw output magnitude. Aether Class measures one thing only: how cleanly the Aether Shell conducts what it holds outward into form. It is the Shell’s transparency, and so how strong a person’s influence on the physical world is. When the Essence Core speaks, how much of what it says actually arrives? The older copies have the Shell conducting Essence. What a Shell holds and drives out is the Charge, raw Aether packed there unrefined, and the Core’s part in a blow is to decide it. The measure itself has not moved.

Two characters at identical stat values and Temperance Stage can have wildly different combat output if one is Class III Resonant and the other Class I Muridic. The stats are the same. The delivery is not.

Scholars of a certain age still score a Shell by a sum they call the Aether Index, and it is a fine way to pass a winter, since nothing on any sheet has ever read it.

The Tiers of Aether Class

Aether Ø, The Dormant. The unawakened Shell. Sealed, unresponsive, nearly silent. The soul dreams without memory and moves through the world without leaving lasting metaphysical trace. Precedes Initiate entirely. Corresponds to the Seed Crystal. No stat output above Hollow Grade (the lowest output rank, below F) is possible.

Class I, The Muridic. The awakening Shell, flickering with first resonance. The Charge leaks unevenly into space. Emotion distorts weather and mood. Will echoes unpredictably. The bearer may appear unstable when their lattice is simply learning to speak. Glory, Stage VI, Faceted Crystal, where Aether Class first emerges; Stages I through V are unclassed, and the coil (the instrument that assays a soul) reads no Class. Roughly thirty to forty percent of the yield (η) lost to Shell interference.

Class II, The Harmonic. The attuned Shell begins to hold rhythm. Essence and Aether find a recurring pattern. Simple spellwork stabilizes. Wellspring (one of the sixty governing ideas that transform how a soul uses Aether, each with its own type of Aether and its own law) whispers find purchase. Bonds form without tearing the self. Harmony is structured tension that no longer shatters. Early Refraction, Clear Crystal. Fifteen to twenty-five percent loss.

Class III, The Resonant. The conductor Shell. Essence and Aether exchange freely. Power moves even in silence. Magic ceases to feel like performance and begins to feel like breathing. Dual-casting, Synergia (power born when several souls or forces resonate together) and complex techniques awaken naturally. Presence becomes as important as any incantation. Late Refraction, Clear Crystal. Five to ten percent loss. The first Class at which stat values translate into combat output at near-full fidelity.

Class IV, The Luminous. The lucid Shell thins. Light and thought draw close. Wellsprings respond with minimal prompting. Aether behavior mirrors the bearer’s emotional and moral alignment with unnerving precision. No single corresponding Crystal Tier; a Shell-specific peak: the Body Path’s first rung of law, and it also turns up in Spirit Path souls whose Shell clarified faster than their Core or Layer. A Luminous soul may test as Resonant or even Fire Crystal overall while their Shell has simply gotten there first. Yield loss negligible; output precision exceeds baseline stat values by a factor depending on Sub-Stat architecture.

Class V, The Radiant. The world-bending Shell. Aether bends toward the bearer. Currents curve around their presence. They can stabilize Realmgates, hold Domains (a space where a soul’s inner law becomes local reality) against collapse and speak Parun (the tongue law is written in) motifs into matter. The line between intention and environment blurs; the Shell’s clarity exerts topological pressure on its surroundings. Early Master, Fire Crystal, where the first true Domain forms. Stat output begins exceeding what the raw numbers suggest, because the Shell is amplifying rather than merely conducting.

Class VI, The Voidic. The inverted Shell. Polarity reverses. Instead of emitting, the Shell devours, remembers and reflects. Voidic conductors channel absence, silence and negation without erasing themselves, and interface most directly with Nihiloth, Vantabriel and the other Wellsprings of absence, and with Titanic depths. Lateral rather than sequential; it can appear alongside Resonant, Luminous or Radiant Shells rather than strictly after them. Most often in Split-Shell Entities and souls deep in those harmonizations. Their Ardency does not deal damage in the conventional sense. It removes things.

Class Ω, The Absolute. The threshold Shell. The Aether Shell dissolves as a distinct boundary. Being and Continuum interpenetrate. Every fragment of the self conducts law with minimal deviation. Paragon, Absolute Crystal. Archons (the world’s fourteen great powers), Titans and World Spirits who have crossed the Apotheosis Threshold exist at this clarity. Stat output is no longer meaningful as a separate measurement, because the practitioner and their output are the same event.

The Stage each Class names is the typical path, not a lock. A Shell whose Class sits above the ladder for its Stage stands as recorded; Luminous (IV) stands in line, and Voidic (VI) stays lateral. Read the Stages above as ceilings opening. The Body Path’s command of matter rides the same rungs: I as an object, II through contact, III by recognition, IV and V by law, Ω as a whole system.

Essence Class and Thread Class

The Shell is one layer of three, and the other two have ladders of their own, read from Glory as it is. Essence Class is the Essence Core, influence on spiritual reality: souls, spirits, the dead, what a thing means. Thread Class is the Attraction Layer, how much of Fate a soul can pull down and hold. VI on each is lateral, an inverted way of holding that power.

Each ladder sets the yield of workings through its own layer. The Core authors everything, and the layer it lands through sets the rung: a blow through the Shell reads the Aether rung, a working authored in the Core reads the Essence rung, and a pull reads the Thread rung. Matter reads Aether, a soul or a meaning reads Essence, and a condition reads Thread. An art’s Path line picks the ladder, and a true pull always takes Thread on top. The Three Paths has the list by working and by trade. Thread is the steep one: most souls stay Ø for life, its numeral gives a yield two rungs below the same numeral elsewhere, and it opens no earlier than Glory unless a bloodline or a blessing gives a grip. “Below the table” at Thread I and II does not mean a pull cannot be made. It can, at a terrible rate: about a tenth of the spend arrives at I and about a fifth at II, so a pull on those rungs takes a huge share of the reserve and often fails, and it leaves its knot either way.

The Stage sets a ladder’s ceiling, and practice moves a person up to it. A bought Stage opens the ceiling and not the rung.

A Walked second Path’s ladder runs a rung behind until mastered, and a Studied ladder runs two behind and stops at II, or at I for Thread. Thread has no Stage climb: it moves by a grip from blood or a blessing, plus decades, and a sworn fighter who keeps vows reads I or II at most. The Fate Path’s Stage is another matter and does climb: Fate Path Stage rises with the soul’s Stage like the Stage of any Path, and it is the Path Stage the Fate gates are read against, so only the Thread rung waits on grip and decades. Anyone at Thread Ø evolves their class on whichever ladder reads highest, and a Fate-led soul does the same at any rung, its class evolving on whichever of its three ladders stands highest. A window reads all three on one line, [Aether IV · Essence II · Thread Ø]. The Soul Crystal Tier of Part Twenty-One stays as the reading of what the Crystal has grown into. The full table, what moves each rung, what each rung of the two new ladders can do and what a pull costs at each are set out in Classes and the Three Ladders.

Aether Class Progression

The Shell’s rung ascends through refinement, not conquest, up to the ceiling its Stage allows. Each step removes interference: ego noise, denial, unprocessed fracture, clinging to false narratives. Each time the leading ladder climbs, the named class evolves by how it has been lived, and the window shows the new name beside the new rung.

Temperance Refinement: gradual clarity through discipline and self-confrontation. The most common and safest path. The Core refines and the Shell learns to match.

Wellspring Baptism: deep immersion in a Wellspring’s Aether where it pools, forcibly realigning the Shell’s resonance. Potent, perilous, often irreversible.

Aether Forge Rites: Mechanica (forbidden working that takes power without lawful consent) and ritual that widen conductivity or restructure the Shell directly, sometimes at the cost of memory, autonomy or Essence stability.

Higher Resonance Infusion: alignment with Archonic principle, Titanic depth, or world-bearing law that rewrites the Shell from above.

Variants and Anomalies

Rot-Born Conductors channel Aether through damaged or corrupted Shells. Output can be immense, but each working corrodes the lattice further. Every stat expression carries a hidden cost in Crystal integrity.

Synthetic Aetherics bear engineered or grafted Shells. They rise quickly through lower Classes yet suffer accelerated decay, instability or identity drift. Their Aether Class can outpace their Core’s actual Temperance by a dangerous margin.

Split-Shell Entities possess dual polarity, luminous and voidic channels braided through one soul. Capable of rare paradoxical feats, prone to fracture, inversion or self-annihilation. A Split-Shell soul may register as Luminous and Voidic simultaneously.

Interaction with the Stat System

A ladder’s rung is a multiplier on stat expression for workings through that layer. Raw values determine ceiling. The rung determines how much of that ceiling translates into observable output.

A character at B-Grade Ardency Flux with a Class II Harmonic Shell produces roughly seventy-five to eighty-five percent of their theoretical maximum damage. The same character at the same value with a Class V Radiant Shell produces one hundred percent or more.

The interaction is not linear. II to III produces the largest single improvement in stat-to-output fidelity. V to VI produces the most dramatic qualitative shift, from conventional force expression to inverted and negation-based expression. The jump to Ω produces output the stat system can no longer meaningfully track.

Part Eighteen — Paths, the Lens and the Kinds

“The element does not choose you. You temper yourself on it, and it remembers you.”

Older books sort souls by an inborn bias toward one element or another. The trade has given that up. Nobody is a fire soul: there are only people who have tempered themselves on a Wellspring of fire. What gives a blow its character is the Wellspring a soul has tempered on and the Path it comes out by, and both of those can be read off a life. Which Wellsprings sit easily with a soul follows from how each one’s kind sits with the Path. How a Primary Trait (a belief hardened into a personal law of the soul, the first and quietest of them) forms is a matter of origin, first imprint and the lived Path, by Part Twenty. Where a class is going is a matter of how the person has lived, by Part Seventeen.

The Essence Core is the center of identity and the Crystal is the soul, so Essence is the soul’s own substance, and spending it is spending yourself. That sentence belongs to the Spirit Path, and to vows, which spend the soul’s share. Spending Charge is spending the world, and the body pays for the handling. The Core authors on every road: on the Body Path it decides and the Shell does the work. And because the Shell is the only door into matter, a Spirit worker’s thrown force lands on souls (a soul knocked out of true, dread laid on a mind, a Core wounded) and marks no matter; one who wants a stone moved moves it through their own Shell, at their own Aether Class.

One Path, Two or Three

A soul has one life’s time to spend. A Path on a card is Mastered, Walked (at least thirty percent of a person’s practice) or Studied. Anyone may study any Path, which gives knowledge and small uses and opens no gates. The percentages on a card are the record of time spent. Each road on a card also carries a Path Stage, the highest Stage whose gates that Path has opened. Mastered means Path Stage equals soul Stage, read again at each Threshold. The card reads Body VII Mastered · Fate VI Walked 35%. On Fate, practice is counted in vows kept and bonds held.

Three Paths is rare and looked down on by nearly everyone. Force modifiers and the lens apply after the Tier is read, so they never move a Grade’s letter and never count on the two-Grade boost cap.

The Kinds of Wellspring

Every one of the Sixty has a kind, and the kind names its home plane. A kind is a qualifier and never a lock: any Path can use any Wellspring.

Wild (33) are at home in the Physical World: transformations of matter, things you can point at, which a practitioner shapes what they spend around. Spirit (15) are at home on the Soul Plane. They are purely spiritual, their ground looks ordinary, they usually do little or nothing to the physical world and they give a fighter no fire to throw, showing instead in what a person can bear, know, keep or let go. Wellsprings of Fate (6) are at home on the Plane of Fate and work on bonds, debts, names and odds; on their ground luck goes strange, oaths bite, and strangers keep meeting. Medium (6) are at home on all three. The sort itself is in Part Thirteen.

The Lens

A Wellspring is the lens a soul bases its tempering on, a lens on magic already potent, and it shapes what each Path spends: raw Aether in the Shell, Essence in the Core, the claim on the Layer, where the Thread pulled through the claim takes its shape. The idea stays and the target changes. Take Pyreveil, a Wild Wellspring. On the Body Path the heat lives in the tissue and the blow burns on contact, and what it burns is matter. On the Spirit Path it is thrown, and it burns what a soul means and holds, a grief, a grudge, an attachment, whatever in it is false, and leaves the coat unscorched. On the Fate Path it is a claim that burns its way through: it burns chances, what a thing could have been, and rivalries and oaths catch around the person who holds it. Absolution, a Spirit Wellspring, gives no fire on any of them: on the Body Path old injuries stop costing what they cost, at home it lets the old poison out of a soul, and on the Fate Path a held grudge can be let go, and the thing it was pulling on stops pulling.

At its home Path a Wellspring harmonizes cleanly and can even double its effect, starting small and reaching double only with years of mastery. Away from home it works in that layer’s terms, at lower-quality resonance and higher cost, and half again is the most anyone has got out of it. On two Paths the lens is read per working, by the layer the working goes out through. No stat points change hands either way. The number stays where it was, and the Body Path fighter on Pyreveil simply burns twice as much like herself, once the years have been put in. Young fighters who claim the doubling are generally claiming it to a bookie.

Wellspring, Path and the Stat System

Neither a Wellspring nor a Path adds stat points. They change the qualitative character of stat expression. Two characters with identical Ardency Flux at S-Grade produce radically different combat output if one has tempered on Pyreveil and the other on Nihiloth. The Pyreveil practitioner’s Flux burns, ruptures and catalyzes. The Nihiloth practitioner’s Flux ends, quiets and unmakes. The EU rating may be identical. The experience of being hit by either is not. The Path changes it again, since the same Flux may arrive by the fist, by a line thrown across a yard at the soul and at nothing else, or by way of a claim on the thing struck.

How a Wellspring’s kind sits with the Path determines compatibility, which determines which Sub-Stats receive qualitative enhancement through harmonization. A Body Path fighter harmonizing with Pyreveil receives stronger Ardency and Vitality enhancement than a Spirit Path fighter attempting the same. The Spirit Path fighter’s Crystal does not reject Pyreveil, but the lattice works harder to integrate it, producing lower-quality resonance at higher cost.

Harmonization

The lock happens at the Shell, where Aether enters. The tie is held on the Layer. The lens shapes what the lead layer works: raw Aether in the Shell for Body, Essence in the Core for Spirit, the claim on the Layer for Fate. A person’s base hue comes from the lens tempered on longest, and the rest add texture. What each Wellspring favors, hates and stands contrary to is on its entry in The Sixty Wellsprings.

Part Nineteen — Aether Flow and Essence Economy

“The soul does not cast from infinite reserves. It casts from what it has earned, compressed, and dared to spend.”

The Three Routes

The Part keeps its old name, and the name is now two-thirds of the matter. A reserve leaves a soul by one of three routes, and the figures below count the first two in one unit and price the third.

One reserve, one number: a Body-led soul holds most of its EU as Charge and a Spirit-led soul as Essence, a Fate-led soul holds Essence too, refining what it draws as a Spirit soul does and paying its pulls and its vows out of that, a soul on both holds a share of each (Charge 70% · Essence 30%), and the two never trade back. Tier Grade, Bands and the Aether Shell sets the routes out at length and The Tell and the Knot has the third one’s bill.

The Resource System

EU (Essence Units): the raw reserve. Total volume of usable power the soul holds at any moment, Charge in the Shell and Essence in the Core counted as one figure; the unit kept its name when the Charge was let into it. Set by Level and capped by Stage (below), governed primarily by Tempering Yield. Maximum EU is the structural ceiling. Operating below ten percent EU produces Starvation (Essence Starvation in the older registers, a name now kept for the Spirit and Fate roads, whose reserve is Essence; a Body fighter’s is Charge Starvation): reduced stat expression, Shell degradation, Trait flickering. Then each road sends its own bill. A Body fighter’s Shell starts drawing its Charge out of the flesh, and the skin splits in fine lines. A Spirit worker’s Core frays, names first and then memory, since Essence is the soul’s own stamped substance. A Fate worker’s reserve is Essence as well, so her Core frays the same way, with the Fate tells on top: luck curdling, then her name sitting wrong in other mouths, and at the bottom every knot due at once.

Flux Density (EU/g): the compression ratio, how much power is packed per unit of Crystal mass. Higher means greater output from the same reserve, and it is the same number on every Path. Governed by Tempering Clarity and Ardency Compression. This separates a practitioner who burns through their reserve in three techniques from one who fights for an hour on the same pool.

AU/s (Aether Units per second): the output rate, how much of the reserve is spent into active expression per second at full output. Governed by Ardency Flux, Ardency Alacrity and Tempering Yield. Formula: AU/s = Flux Density × η.

Yield (η): the share of a spend that arrives as intended effect, as a distillation has its yield; the Measurewrights call it efficiency. At η 0.50 through the Shell, half of every expenditure burns in the Shell’s furnace as heat, with noise and structural bleed; the same figure through the Core is paid as hollowing, and through the Layer it stands as a knot. At η 0.90, ninety percent converts to clean output. A Spirit master’s great Flux turned on matter goes through a Shell he never trained, at that Shell’s yield, and mostly burns off as heat on his own skin. The single most important variable in sustained combat, because for the same delivered effect it determines how long the reserve lasts. Governed by the class ladder of the layer in use, Tempering Clarity and Ardency Compression: the ladder sets the band and the other two the place inside it. From Glory that ladder’s rung sets it, Aether Class through the Shell, Essence Class through the Core and Thread Class for a pull, and the unclassed read their Stage’s typical figure below.

What the owner sees. None of these figures appears in a window. The window shows the reserve as a percentage and a bar, [Reserve: 41%], and leaves EU, AU/s and yield to the assay sheet and the slate. Body tells come first anyway: at half the breath shortens, at a quarter the color thins, at a tenth the Traits begin to stutter. Each road adds its own on top. A Body fighter runs hot and then cracks: heat-haze on the skin at half, steam and cramps at a quarter, the skin splitting in fine lines at a tenth. A Spirit worker runs hollow: the colors near them go flat, then a cold and a slipped word, then names and memories. A Fate worker runs unlucky: small luck curdles nearby, then things go wrong around them, then their own name sits wrong in other mouths. The Three Paths has the table.

The Turn, and What a Unit Is Worth

One combat turn is 6 seconds. Every duration the system states in combat turns is read through that figure: a working held three turns is held 18 seconds, a field that stands eight turns stands 48. The fourteen-day turn of older papers is another thing that shares the name.

One AU is one EU, and a rate is read in AU/s. Spent is AU/s, delivered is AU/s × η, and the difference burns in the Shell’s furnace as heat, with sound and structural bleed, when the spend went through the Shell. The Measurewrights reckon one EU at one megajoule, so by their conversion 1 AU/s is 1 MW. Through the Core the same difference is paid in the Core, unit for unit, and warms nothing; through the Layer it is owed as a knot. A reserve of E EU spent at R AU/s empties in E ÷ R seconds.

Yield counts twice, on purpose: once in AU/s, for how much the layer in use can move, and once in delivery, for what survives the trip. So at full output a higher yield empties the reserve faster, because the layer moves more, and for the same delivered effect it lasts longer, because less is lost on the way. Part Twenty-Three works the figures.

The EU Band by Temperance Stage

A reserve is set by Level and capped by Stage, by the law of reserves in Part Twenty-Three: log₁₀ EU = 3.4202 + 0.012812 × Level. A Stage’s practitioners usually stand in its Band’s (a span of a hundred Levels) cluster of Levels, and that cluster read through the law is the Stage’s working band. The benchmark inside each band is its midpoint in decades, the geometric mean of floor and ceiling. The gate ceiling is the reserve at the Level the Stage cannot pass without its next Threshold (the breakthrough that opens the next Stage), and it is hard where the working band is only typical.

The AU/s Progression

A reserve becomes a rate the moment a turn has a length. A Stage’s AU/s band is its working band spread across one turn, and the benchmark rate is the benchmark reserve spent inside a single turn. That is the plainest statement of what full output costs: open at your Stage’s benchmark rate and you have 6 seconds of it, and nothing after. Sustained work therefore runs far below the benchmark, and the ten percent Starvation floor is reachable inside one exchange by anyone who forgets it.

Essence Recovery

Passive Recovery: the baseline drawing-in of ambient Aether without active effort, packed raw in the Shell as Charge or refined in the Crystal into Essence, in the shares the soul’s Paths have set. Governed by Tempering Yield and Harmonics Synergy. Faster near active Wellspring veins, slower in depleted or hostile environments, negligible in Silence Realms (places where the Aether is dead). Rich ground fills a Crystal faster and thin ground starves it, which is why the scholar’s Aetheric Density (how thickly a place is charged with Aether) is the first thing a veteran asks about a new town, usually in rougher words.

Active Recovery: deliberate absorption through meditation, Wellspring communion or alchemical treatment. Significantly faster, but requires the character to stop fighting and concentrate. An alchemical treatment quickens the draw from the ground and the Wellspring, and a fight Draft (a dose brewed for battle) pours power straight back into a reserve, taking its price in the crash that follows. It follows the drinker’s road. Core stock feeds the Core with Essence that arrives unstamped, and the crash is the stamping. A Body fighter’s Draft is a Salt, raw Aether that packs straight into the Shell, goes down like a swallowed burn and leaves cramps and fever. No Draft is brewed for the Fate road: a Fate worker’s reserve is Essence, so Core stock refills it. Governed by Harmonics Attunement and Tempering Yield.

Crisis Recovery: surges triggered by soul-cost events, Threshold completions or genuine emotional breakthroughs during combat. Not reliable and cannot be manufactured. The Crystal’s reward for genuine contact with consequence, the same principle underlying the XP system.
