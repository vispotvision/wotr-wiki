The Counted Speech — A Speaker's Grammar of Parun

The Counted Speech — A Speaker's Grammar of Parun

Nov 28, 202618 min read

A Speaker’s Grammar of Parun, with the Lexicon and the Spoken Hands

Compiled for the Guild Accord Division of Alchemetrica · companion volume to The Descent of Parun

Twenty-five cells · one constant · three lawful short sentences

“The Descent taught you fourteen ways to write it. This teaches you why almost nobody says it.”

Opening line of the Vaulted Ring recitation primer, unattributed.

When the east wall came down at Dunmere Gate, Sergeant Ebba Thorn was in the gatehouse with a spear butt coming at her mouth and the whole room full of burning thatch. She took the butt, spat two teeth across the floor and bellowed the field sentence, AT PEN MA RA, in the voice she used for mules: eight and twenty-one and thirteen and twenty-three, sixty-five, sealed.

The air of that room ran inward toward her, every lick of flame and rag of smoke hauled back off the door like a dog on a chain, and it stayed that way till first light while the garrison in their yellow coats came up the stair past her, cheering. Beside her stood a licensed Cant speaker in a very clean tabard, who needed something nobody had composed for him in advance, composed it, and came out three short of the sum. Nothing sealed it. It ran down the gatehouse stair and it is, the locals will tell you, still a bad stair. The sum is why Thorn’s name got a song and his got a footnote, and this volume is the sum.

What Is Actually Being Done

Parun has no mother tongue and no register below the legal one, and the reason is arithmetic rather than culture.

A spoken sentence is a path traced across the same lattice the written hands trace, performed with the mouth instead of the chisel. The sounds are the cells.

To speak the sentence is to walk the square out loud.

This produces a language that cannot be babbled, cannot be sung to a child, cannot be used to gossip, and cannot be spoken quickly by anyone who has not made the count automatic.

It can be shouted across a breach by a woman who has done it ten thousand times, and there are perhaps four hundred such people alive.

The count is a skill like any other: anyone with a woken Crystal can level it, it rises with use, and it shows as a Grade on the sheet. Sergeant Thorn never held a title in anything, and her soul has been telling her for years what her Parun stands at, which is more than the Vaulted Ring has ever done for her.

The Twenty-Five

Five vowels and twenty consonants, one to each cell, keyed to the cell's value and not to its position.

The Lattice

The five-by-five kamea of Mars. Every row, every column and both diagonals total sixty-five.

Thirteen sits at the center and is the only cell that does not move under any of the fourteen Archonic stations.

Which is why the dental nasal appears in more Parun words than any other sound, and why a speaker who cannot produce it cannot speak the language at all.

The whole square totals three hundred and twenty-five. This number matters later and is best not reached.

The Three Laws

I · A sound may not repeat inside a word.

A traced path cannot revisit a cell it has already occupied. This caps a word at twenty-five sounds and makes reduplication structurally impossible.

It is the reason Parun has no lullabies, no chants, no drinking songs and no baby-talk. You cannot say a sound twice for comfort. The language has no mechanism for it. What the choirs do is another thing, held in resonance, and the singing has its own account.

II · The first sound declares the slot.

Values 1–5 open a Warrant. 6–11 a Ground. 12–17 a Vector. 18–25 a Seal.

A listener therefore knows a slot has been skipped before the speaker has finished the word, which in a discipline where a skipped slot means no working at the moment you needed one is the single most useful thing the language does.

III · The sentence must total sixty-five.

Add the values of every sound in all four words. A lawful sentence comes to sixty-five, or to a whole multiple of it. Nothing else seals.

This law binds the mouth and only the mouth. A spoken sentence has nothing else to close on. A cut one is closed by its sealing stroke and held by what it is cut into, and it answers to the Seal and the stone and not to the sum.

This is the entire difficulty and the entire reason the world writes Parun rather than speaking it, because a speaker composing live is performing mental arithmetic while making a legal claim under pressure, and the arithmetic is not forgiving.

The practical consequence: Parun is grotesquely rich in near-synonyms of differing value. A speaker does not choose a word because it is the right word. He chooses it because it is the right word that makes the sum close.

The slots are the four roles of the working trades, in the mouth. Notice what is missing from all of them, including every sentence on this page: the Wellspring. Nobody says it. It is named by harmonization under the Warrant, read off the Attraction Layer of the soul behind the sentence, so at pen ma ra in Sergeant Thorn’s mouth and in a Pyreveil speaker’s are the same words doing different things. A written form from the Glyph Index can fill a slot where a plain word would, and the Index shows one in a sentence on each of the three Paths.

A Warrant that overstates standing does not fail loudly. It seals, and the difference between the standing claimed and the standing held is deducted from the speaker at the moment the Seal closes.

Practitioners who habitually over-warrant are identifiable across a room by their hands.

That deduction governs a Warrant spoken in Parun. Vocatia summons by recognition and keeps its own law: a summoner who claims a standing he lacks gets nothing.

A Warrant answers the question Irath’s station reads, by what right, and when two souls contest a working it is weighed as Dominion Fate (the soul’s weight in Fate). Those are three names for one idea on three shelves.

What a Seal can reach

A Seal spends the speaker’s own share of fate. It sets a term, and it moves only what already has a way of happening. It never pulls a thread of Fate, so anyone may speak a Seal with Fate unread (no rung at all of the Mastery that measures a soul’s reach into Fate), and wolyeth may wait a hundred years on the last of a line without killing anybody.

A spoken term is carried by the speaker. The Seal sets it, the speaker’s own Layer anchors it for as long as it runs, and the speaker is the substrate that caps it, which is why first light is a long way off for a tired sergeant. See how long a working lasts.

A Fourth Seal needs a grip. A sentence that ends a bloodline or a building is past what any soul’s own share will buy, so it takes a grip on Fate, by blood or blessing, or a Warrant that carries one. A Warrant word grants nothing: it names a grip somebody holds. Said without one, the sum closes and the difference between standing claimed and standing held comes out of the speaker at the Seal, and most of the Ring’s register ends there.

Failure

FaultWhat happens
Under the sumNothing sealed it. The working runs and does not stop. This is the fault that kills bystanders
Over the sumIt seals early. The difference is taken out of the speaker, in the body, at the moment of closing
Slot skippedNo seal, no working, no cost. The commonest fault and the only harmless one. The Valorin bank whole formulae this way, one word short, against the day they need them
Slot misorderedSeals inverted. The Vector runs from the Ground into the Warrant, which means into the speaker
Sound repeatedThe path crosses itself. The word is simply not a word

The rule across every trade is the same. A slot left wholly empty does nothing, and a sentence that fires with a bad limit runs.

A practitioner who has been over the sum is legible afterward. Depending on where the count went wrong the deduction comes out of the hands, the hearing, the teeth or the years, and the Vaulted Ring keeps a register of which faults take which, compiled from people who survived them.

The Spoken Hands

The same six pressures the Descent uses, transposed from hand to mouth. Speech produces far fewer varieties, because a mouth that cannot hold the count does not produce a dialect. It produces a people who write instead.

VarietyCharacter
Root Parun the Counted SpeechThe reference variety. All twenty-five held distinct, the count carried silently, the sentence composed live. Taught at the Vaulted Ring and in three other places. A Root speaker can compose a Doubled Seal in the time it takes to draw breath, and this is the specific skill the Accord licenses Understood by every trained Parunist. Composed live by almost none of them
The Vaulted Cant Guild Accord · the memorized handWhat most licensed practitioners actually speak. Lawful sentences learned whole, by rote, in blocks of eleven, deployed without any count being performed at the time. It works, it is fast, and it fails absolutely the moment a situation requires a sentence nobody has pre-composed. Cant speakers are visibly the same people as Root speakers until the moment they are not, distinguishable only by asking for something new
Cold Parun Echo Elf · the counted-aloud handThe Echo Elves externalize the arithmetic. One sound to one beat, metronomic, the count audible in the rhythm rather than held in the head, exhausting to listen to and effectively immune to error. Their treaties sum correctly in the mouth and on the page and have never once needed renegotiation. They regard the second fact as sufficient answer to the first Corrected mid-sentence by any listener who is also counting
Inverted Parun Drow · the spoken cipherEvery sound replaced by its complement, twenty-six minus the value. An outsider follows easily, confidently, at speed, and understands wrongly. The arithmetic constrains it savagely: a complemented sentence only seals when its total sound-count is divisible by five, so a Drow speaker composes to a length before composing to a meaning. None of the three Single Seals is invertible: all three run to nine sounds. There is therefore no such thing as a small Drow working, which is a fact about their reputation usually attributed to temperament
Mirror-Speech Deepkin · the hand that is not saidThey say every cell the word does not touch and leave the word itself unsaid, so the meaning is the silence in the middle of a great deal of noise. The sums hold always, because the complement of four words against the full square comes to nineteen sixty-fives without fail. The cost is length. A Deepkin sentence is nineteen times the sentence it means, which is why they correspond in Mirror-Sign and speak it only where a working must not be detected. Nothing was inscribed and nothing was said, so there is nothing for a ward to find
Swamp Parun Verdant Orc · the unbroken handThe reed never lifts and neither does the breath. Four slots on one exhalation, no pause admitted anywhere, which makes lung capacity the hard limit on how large a working a Verdant speaker can attempt. A Third Seal spoken on one breath is a physical feat before it is anything else, and Verdant practitioners are built accordingly
Gravetongue Ironblood · the hand that abandoned the countThe exception, and the reason it is the exception. Its meaning is not carried in the sounds and therefore not in the sums, which is why a transcription is inert. Whatever Gravetongue is doing, it is not walking the square, and the Ironblood have never claimed otherwise. The Vaulted Ring’s position is that Gravetongue is not Parun. The Ironblood position is that Parun is a way of counting and Gravetongue is a way of being made of something, and that the Accord’s confusion is its own affair

Settled Positions

QuestionSettled position
The latticeMars at 5×5. Twenty-five phonemes, constant sixty-five. Venus at 7×7 would give forty-nine phonemes and a constant of a hundred and seventy-five, which no mouth carries. The lattice is not an aesthetic decision. It is the reason the language can be spoken at all
The three Single SealsFixed at three, permanently. The lexicon may grow; the Single Seal count does not. This is the Vaulted Ring’s oldest standing finding and the one piece of Parun scholarship nobody has ever attempted to overturn, because every practitioner alive learned all three as a child and none of them wants a fourth
GravetongueNot Parun. It does not walk the square, its meaning is carried in a physical resonance rather than in sounds, and the inert transcriptions follow directly. Structurally it is a chant with no notation. The Vaulted Ring and the Ironblood are both correct and have been arguing past each other for six hundred years
The Total Seal, 325Not barred. Simply fatal, for a reason internal to the arithmetic. The whole square includes cell thirteen, the still cell, the one that does not move under any of the fourteen Archonic stations. A sentence using every cell therefore warrants under all fourteen at once, and no living person holds that standing. The difference between standing claimed and standing held is deducted at the Seal. At the Total the difference is everything the speaker is. The Accord’s position that no attested lexicon reaches 325 is fortunate is a polite way of saying the thing has killed everyone who got close, and the Ring’s register of over-sum deductions ends its list at the fourth level for that reason
Moto standingEntered in the table above. Their Warrant class is delegated sight, which the lexicon has no word for and no value for.
