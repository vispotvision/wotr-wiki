The Foundations

The Foundations

Nov 28, 20262 min read

What a working is, the laws it conditions, and the boundaries every working is written in.

Dob Threlkeld, hedge-worker of Cobb’s Wharf, once bet his mule that he could set a wet hay bale alight in a rainstorm, and he won, the whole bale going up at once with the rain still falling on it, and then lost the mule, the bale, the barn and his good name in three parishes when the fire went on keeping the habits fire has always kept after he had stopped looking at it. This shelf exists so that you can lose your mule for better reasons.

  • The Physical Account: Two Sets of Books What a working is, in two complete descriptions of every event: the soul’s, which is the lens and says why, and the Measurewrights’ physics, their own theory, often right about how much and never about why; and where the power actually comes from.
  • The Sixty Wellsprings The sixty alchemical and spiritual transformations, each a governing idea that changes how a soul uses Aether, a lens on magic already potent, each with its own operation at the bench and its own flaw, and each explained by the Measurewrights, in their own account, as a law of natural philosophy. Read the hub, then whichever kind you need.
  • The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms The limits set on those laws, where each one stops.
  • The Spell Index Every attested working, filed by shelf, with its glyphs, Wellspring, operation, cost and counter.
  • The Master Codex The canon workbook, sheet by sheet, and where each sheet lives on this wiki.
  • The Descent of Parun — The Fourteen Hands The fourteen ways of writing a boundary down, and what each one changes. Its child, The Counted Speech, is why almost nobody says it aloud.

This shelf is the second in The Magic System, after First Steps. Next shelf: The Soul and the Climb.
