Wild Wellsprings

Wild Wellsprings

Nov 28, 2026116 min read

Thirty-three of the Sixty, at home in the Physical World · the kind you can point at

The people of Pennyhithe will tell you about the Aldgate sisters whether you ask or not. Prudence Aldgate heated the public baths on Fish Lane with her bare hands for thirty years, a copper a tub, two if you wanted it hot enough to lift the grease, and she turned away anyone who owed her, the reeve’s wife included, and once, gloriously, the reeve. Her sister Bess kept the family’s grudges. On the night of the Herring Fair she walked down into the customs cellar under the tollhouse with a list of names and her whole body lit the white-orange of a smith’s best heat, and the crowd up on the quay heard a flame-front turn into something else.

The tollhouse roof came down in the harbor with the tollkeeper’s best hat still on its peg. The quay cheered, and the minstrels had a song by morning. The song leaves out that the shock came back up the cellar along its own path, with Bess standing at the origin of it, and that she has been deaf in both ears and short three fingers ever since. Prudence’s tubs ran on Pyraeon and Bess’s fingers went to Cataclysm, one smolder and one detonation out of the same orange fire, and a Measurewright (a certified assessor of workings) who says so in a Pennyhithe tavern should expect to be asked which sister he is calling gentle.

What Wild means · A Wild Wellspring is a thing in the world: fire in the ground, stone that moves, lightning, a solution crystallizing out of itself on the bench. Its home is the Physical World, Aegor’s ground, and its Aether (mana, the raw stuff of magic) shows up as a physical phenomenon or an alchemical transformation. The stone was there and the water was there, and the Aether reshapes them, moves them, burns them. A site of one of these alters the landscape where anyone can see it, so the surveyor’s body is a reliable instrument and his environment is not.

The lens · A soul tempered on one of these carries that thing in everything it does: a little at first, and after years of mastery on the Body Path, its home, up to double, so that the same fist at the same Grade lands with twice its idea in it, the same shoulder drives twice as much of what it has become into what it meets, and the same Fortitude behaves twice as much like the thing it has decided to be. Any Path can take any of them. Away from home half again is the most anyone has got out of one, at a higher cost and with a rougher result. The bath-house trade will tell you it shows in the water, anyone who has bought a stone Draft and then tried to dance will explain, and that is how a bridge gets knocked down badly. The full account of the lens is on the hub.

Each Wellspring below opens on what it is. The idea is the concept it lays over a soul’s Aether, and The turn, where the trade has fixed one, is the moment that idea comes clear to someone working inside it. Kind names its home plane, and never locks it. Titan is the Titan whose law it is one showing of, Hue the color it gives an aura, Ground what it favors and hates underfoot, and Contrary the Wellsprings whose laws fight it in one soul. On the Body, On the Spirit and On Fate say where the same idea lands on each Path. On the Body it shapes raw Aether packed in the Aether Shell (the layer of the soul that meets matter, and the only door into it) and works on matter, with no Essence spent and the Shell’s furnace burning off as heat. On the Spirit it shapes Essence (spiritual energy) spent through the Essence Core (the layer that holds identity and authors what a working is) and works on souls and on what a thing means and holds, never on matter, at a price paid in the Core as hollowing. On Fate it shapes the claim on the Attraction Layer (a soul’s own share of fate, its bonds, oaths, claims and odds), and the Thread pulled through that claim takes its shape, and works on what a thing could have been, and every pull leaves a knot the world collects later. Spirit is the transformation it works on the soul, one road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage), and Alchemy the principle it stands for at the bench. Then come Stat Effect, what shifts on the sheet, Glyph, the forms the Index seats on it, and Failure, how it goes wrong, from the flaw its idea cannot hold. Operation, after Titan, names the bench operation the idea performs, the one a working’s Codex line carries. Last on each entry stands The Measurewrights’ account, Analogue and Mechanism: that guild’s physics of what the type of Aether does to a Soul Crystal, often right about how much and never about why. Entries run in alphabetical order.

Abython · The Abyssal Pulse

Folk name the Abyssal Pulse (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea Peace with the abyss. Matching the pressure in place of fighting it, so that the soul can walk where everyone else suffocates.

Kind Wild

Titan Zar’thuun: annihilation as peace, the dark of Abythron met on its own terms.

Operation Dissolution: the soul’s difference from the deep is loosed into it until nothing is left between them for the deep to crush.

Hue Near-black, because the soul has matched itself to the dark of the deep and keeps no light of its own to set against it.

Ground Favors great masses of stone, heights and brittle old rock or rock already under load, places where the world has long pressed hardest and a soul has the most to make peace with; hates silt and loose sand, a drop with nothing under it, and soft even ground, which press on nothing.

Contrary Thaloré (pressure felt in full against pressure matched away); Chthonica (gripping against floating free of net stress); Petralon (bearing the load against having no load to bear).

On the Body Flesh stops holding a difference against the world, so a crushing blow finds less to crush and Aether-starved ground does not suffocate the fighter who walks onto it. The same body hurried back into a rich hall comes apart in ways a diver would recognize.

On the Spirit The soul matches whatever is pressed on it, and a rival’s Aura Pressure (the felt weight of another’s power) meets nothing pushing back and little to grip. The trouble comes when it is time to throw something, since a Core with no difference in it has very little to push with.

On Fate Presence drops to exactly what the room already has, so the holder can stand unremarked in a hall full of people hunting them. Stretching that over a column, so that it passes through a hostile province with no sentry or rumor finding a difference to catch on, is the work of every waking hour of the march, and the return to ordinary company takes the holder the way a fast ascent takes a diver. The knot it leaves is a difference owed: on some later day the holder is the one thing in the room that everybody notices.

Spirit Temperance through void.

Alchemy The balanced vessel: pressure inside matched to pressure outside, so the glass has nothing to resist. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Resilience Persistence, Harmonics Stability, Vitality Tolerance. The character operates where other practitioners suffocate.

Glyph None attested in the Index (see Where the Registers Disagree on the hub).

Failure Peace with the abyss is a match and never a defense: the soul holds nothing of its own against the deep, so it cannot be hurried back out of it. Return to ordinary Aetheric density too fast and the matched interior has no time to match the room it has come into, and comes out of solution all at once, which is as unpleasant in the soul as it is in a diver. The Measurewrights call it rapid ascent.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Pressure equalization. Neutral buoyancy and the vanishing of net stress.

Mechanism · A vessel at depth, they say, survives by matching the water, never by resisting it, because net stress is a difference and a difference of zero loads nothing. By their reckoning Abython trains the Shell to equalize rather than oppose, which is why its practitioners work in Aether-absent environments that suffocate everyone else: they have stopped holding pressure in and stopped maintaining a differential at all. Why a soul would want peace with the abyss in the first place is not a question their gauges were built to answer.

Anima Spirare · The Soul’s Breath

Folk name the Soul’s Breath (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea The breath of the whole. A body that remembers being whole and answers a small hurt with everything it has.

Kind Wild

Titan Omnis: to breathe is briefly to echo him, and this is a whole body doing it at once.

Operation Fermentation: the whole body is quickened round a small hurt, and the wound is answered with all of its own life.

Hue Bright arterial red, because the whole body’s blood comes running to the smallest hurt.

Ground Favors thick living growth, mild weather and ground close to blood, places where bodies have long answered a hurt with everything they had; hates barren ground, bitter cold and Aether-thin soil, where nothing is alive enough to answer.

Contrary Mortalis (a body refusing the gate against a soul walking to it).

On the Body A small cut draws an answer from the whole body, blood and breath arriving in a flood exactly where they are wanted, so wounds seal under the fighter’s own pulse and the last round is breathed as easily as the first. Lose the signal that says where, and the flood goes everywhere.

On the Spirit A working carries a memory of wholeness and presses it on what it touches, so a split ward, a scattered mind or a soul wounded in the Core is told what it used to be, loudly, and answers as well as it can. It closes no flesh, which is the Shell’s work, it hollows the one who sends it, and it works best on what was once alive.

On Fate Pull one bond and all of them wake: hurt one of a crew and the others feel it under the breastbone, and in the rare great working a flank struck at dawn finds the reserve already on the road with nobody able to say who sent it. It costs blood from every vein at once and cannot easily be called off, so a feint against one picket has been known to turn out the whole army and leave nothing to close the real wound, and the knot falls due the same way, some small hurt of the holder’s own answered later by far too much.

Spirit Temperance through vitality. The confessors’ reading: remembering wholeness, wounds closing because the body remembers being whole.

Alchemy The quickening: the steady blood-warmth of the Bed, in which a charge stays alive. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Vitality Hemostasis, Regeneration, Harmonics Synergy. The body pulses with living rhythm. Wounds close because the body remembers being whole.

Glyph [Leth] Blood, [Dr] Vitality, [Bst] Beast; attested on two more.

Failure The whole body answers a small hurt with everything it has, and it has no way to answer with less. Lose the sense of where the hurt is and the whole answers everywhere at once, spending its own blood and breath on every vein until nothing is left to close the wound that started it. The Measurewrights call it disseminated activation.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · The coagulation cascade and cooperative oxygen binding.

Mechanism · Hemostasis, they say, is an amplifying cascade and never one event, in which each activated factor activates many of the next, so a small injury signal produces a large and precisely localized response. Layered under it, by their reckoning, hemoglobin’s cooperative binding means the fourth oxygen loads far more readily than the first, and Anima Spirare imposes both patterns: amplification where it is needed, and a delivery curve that steepens with demand. It is a fine account of a barber’s arm and says nothing at all about why the body remembers being whole.

The story they tell.

Bertil Ashgrove kept a barber’s chair on Mill Gate with a red-striped pole and a painted board that read TWO COPPERS A WOUND, NO CREDIT FOR COLORS. The day the Millrace Boys met the Hammers of Saint Orrin on the bridge over a disputed bout, he walked into the middle of it with his sleeves rolled and arterial red brightening every vein in both arms, and he closed fifty-three wounds in an hour on both sides, each wet pulse sealing a gash, taking his two coppers each time and flatly refusing the two men who had stiffed him at the last fair. The fifty-fourth wound was a nick on his own thumb. By then the cascade had nothing left to tell it where to stop, it ran through him everywhere at once, and that one small nick bled for three days while both clubs, in the only agreement they have ever reached, took turns holding his arm up and paying for ice.

Ascensio · The Ladder of Ascent

Folk name the Ladder of Ascent (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea The ladder. The whole height climbed by steps, with no single step large enough to break you.

Kind Wild

Titan Atla’zon: which way is down, and the height paid for in his coin, one step at a time.

Operation Distillation: the same work passed up again and again by degrees, until the whole height is reached and no single pass has broken the glass.

Hue Steel gray with pulley-lines, because the height is taken by rope and tackle, each line one step that will bear the load, and never by one heave.

Ground Favors towers, cliffs, high walls and brittle old stone or stone already under load, places where people have long climbed by steps and every step had to bear them; hates silt and loose sand, a drop with no step in it, and soft even ground with nowhere to climb.

Contrary Exuroth (the ordeal against the road with no step large enough to break you); Sublimatio (every step climbed against no steps at all).

On the Body The body takes its load in steps. A fighter climbs a long fight by small increases of force, each one inside what the joints will bear, and arrives at a blow that would have torn them apart had they opened with it.

On the Spirit A working is authored in stages, each inscription standing on the last, until a long prepared piece reaches a height no single casting could survive. Anyone who interrupts it on the fourth step has saved themselves a great deal of trouble.

On Fate It hardly moves the odds on any given day, and thirty years of days is another matter: one more captain owing the right man, one more village out of forage, until the porter’s nephew is dining with the harbor court and the enemy’s army is a day short of everything. A whole war climbed this way is a life’s masterwork and its knots are deferred and stack, so the holder arrives at the top owing every step at once, and in a single fight it buys no more than a slight edge by the fourteenth exchange.

Spirit Temperance through rising.

Alchemy The degrees of fire: one heat reached by steps, where a fool reaches it at a stroke and loses the glass. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Tempering Maturity, Harmonics Attunement. Stage transitions become smoother and less violent.

Glyph None attested in the Index (see Where the Registers Disagree on the hub).

Failure A ladder makes the height bearable and never makes it lower: every step is still owed, only spread out along the climb. Practitioners mistake the absence of crisis for the absence of cost and arrive at the next Stage owing the difference. The Measurewrights call it deferred load.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Mechanical advantage. Path-independence of gravitational potential energy.

Mechanism · Lifting a mass to a height, they say, costs the same total work by any route, but the inclined plane trades distance for peak force and so the same climb can be made without ever exceeding what the structure can bear. In their account Ascensio applies that to Threshold transitions, spreading the required change across a longer path so the Crystal never sees the peak stress that a direct ascent imposes.

Basilithe · The Stone Will

Folk name the Stone Will (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea The stone will. One note the soul holds that nobody can retune.

Kind Wild

Titan Atla’zon: permanence, one posture held because he knelt first.

Operation Coagulation: the soul is fixed on one note and set there past any retuning.

Hue Ochre-gold with quartz-clear lines, because the soul has become a standard, the gold every weight is read against and the clear line of a note that will not be moved.

Ground Favors ore-rich rock, worked stone and deep foundations, places that have long held one shape without being moved; hates open water, high peaks and decks and wagons that will not keep still.

Contrary Materia Primordia (every form against the one note); Transmutatio (one note nobody can retune against the same thing said in another key).

On the Body The Crystal’s stiffness comes down into the bones and the body refuses to be shaken, stunned or struck out of rhythm, with shockwaves passing through a stance that holds its own pitch. Every so often somebody finds that pitch with a hammer, and the fighter rings like a bell and comes apart.

On the Spirit The soul holds one note against any working that would retune it, so compulsion, false memory and imposed law slide off, and so does any new idea. Thrown, it is a dead pitch that stops an enemy’s chant from resolving, and that is about all.

On Fate A soul that cannot be retuned is a standard for other people: merchants trust their weights, and a wavering line drifts toward their pitch without noticing, which is the whole of the lens on most days. Holding that note against an enemy’s arranged panic is a contest that can crack the holder’s Crystal if lost, and an enemy who finds the one pressure that matches it brings down the man and the line together. A note held by pulling leaves a knot, which falls due on the one day that wanted bending.

Spirit Temperance through permanence. The confessors’ reading: constancy, the soul’s frequency refusing retuning.

Alchemy The Standard: the one fixed measure every other weight is read against. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Resilience Hardening, Harmonics Stability. The soul’s frequency locks and refuses retuning.

Glyph [Grv] Gravity; attested on one more.

Failure A soul that holds one note can never change it, even to get out of the way. When some outside rhythm happens to sing that very note, the soul cannot be moved off it and takes the whole of it, and rings like a bell until it comes apart. The Measurewrights call it resonant catastrophe.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Natural frequency and stiffness-to-mass ratio. Quartz oscillator stability.

Mechanism · A body’s resonant frequency, they say, is fixed by its stiffness and its mass, and a high-stiffness low-loss structure holds that frequency against forcing so reliably that a Measurewright with a cut quartz on a string will set her water clock by it. By their reckoning Basilithe raises the Crystal’s stiffness until its resonance simply will not be driven off, which is a defense against every technique that works by imposing an external rhythm. Her clock keeps excellent time, and has never once told her what the note is for.

Cataclysm · The Shattered Law

Folk name the Blast (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea Upheaval. The moment a fire stops being a fire and becomes a blast, a change in kind as well as size, and the soul that has learned to call that moment.

Kind Wild

Titan Valmir: collapse as the moment of transition, the instant a thing changes in kind.

Operation Calcination: the burning is driven past itself until it goes over all at once into something else in kind.

Hue White-orange flash with a pale ring, because the change comes in one instant, and all it leaves is the ring where the fire went over into something else.

Ground Favors kilns and forges, mills and mines whose air is thick enough to catch, and hot springs, places where fire has long waited on the edge of going over into something worse; hates lukewarm places where nothing is hotter than anything else, cold that has soaked through everything, and air that will not feed a flame.

Contrary Solfatara (never building against everything built going at once); Lethegraal (upheaval against settling on its own schedule).

On the Body Given confinement behind it, a set stance or a wall at the enemy’s back, a blow stops being a push and goes over into a blast, and the pressure crosses the target faster than the target can flinch.

On the Spirit A working sent at a soul can change in kind partway across, leaving as a pressure and arriving as a rupture in the Core, a dread that had been building for a year going over all at once into collapse. It breaks no wall and raises no dust, and the people it arrives on rarely get to learn what it was meant to be.

On Fate Slow quarrels near this person can be brought past their point all at once, so a garrison’s ten-year feud and a month of short rations turn into a mutiny in one night, and a Domain (a region where the world runs on a practitioner’s terms) widens by whole streets as everyone picks a side. It wants confinement, it comes off perhaps once in a war, and what comes back along the same path finds the holder standing at the origin with their ears and fingers at stake, and the knot is some quarrel of the holder’s own going over, later, in one night.

Spirit Temperance through upheaval.

Alchemy The vessel’s violent end taken as a law: the moment a burning charge stops burning and becomes a blast. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Ardency Cascade, Ardency Overchannel, Dominion Radius. Area expressions become wider and more structurally coherent.

Glyph [Thn] Thunder, [Eith] Flux, [Wy] Motion; attested on five more.

Failure Upheaval needs something to hold it in before it can go over, and once it goes it does not know which side of the wall it was called from. In confinement the blast comes back along its own path, and the practitioner standing where it began takes the blast and its return together. The Measurewrights call it reflected overpressure.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Deflagration-to-detonation transition. Blast scaling under the cube-root law.

Mechanism · A subsonic flame front, they say, that meets sufficient confinement and turbulence accelerates until the reaction couples to a shock wave and begins propagating faster than sound in the unreacted medium, so that the mechanism changes kind where a lesser one would only change degree. They hold that Cataclysm makes that transition available to what the soul spends, and that blast radius thereafter scales with the cube root of yield, which is why the Wellspring’s area effects widen coherently rather than merely spilling. Their tables for the radius are the best in the trade, and none of them says what it is in a soul that learns to call the moment.

Chthonica · The Root Deep

Folk name the Root (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea The root. Holding on by touching everything, so that whatever you stand on becomes yours to stand on.

Kind Wild

Titan Atla’zon: adhesion, the reason anything stays where it was put.

Operation Conjunction: the soul and whatever it stands on are joined at every point where they touch, until each holds the other.

Hue Dark iron-oxide, because the soul takes the color of the ground it holds to, rust and red earth.

Ground Favors ore-rich rock, worked stone and deep foundations, places where things have long held on by touching everything around them; hates open water, high peaks and anything moving underfoot, where there is nothing steady to take hold of.

Contrary Cymorath (every way open against holding to what is underfoot); Oneirion (a place with no ground against nothing but ground); Abython (gripping against floating free of net stress).

On the Body The Shell takes hold of whatever bears it with a countless fine grip, so a fighter stands on a sheer wall or a bucking deck as if it were a parlor floor and cannot be thrown off either, every blow going down into what they stand on. A wet deck or a fouled boot drops them with no warning at all.

On the Spirit From the Core it roots a working to the place, and a ward done this way sits in the stone the way a tooth sits in a jaw. Dull to throw and a fine thing to leave behind, and Spirit Path scholars on this lens tend to stop traveling.

On Fate The person and the ground become each other’s claim, so a family on its own land has a hold that bailiffs have failed to break, and a Domain raised there comes up out of the floor with its roots already in. A lifetime on one valley can turn it against an invader, the wagons miring in the one soft lane and the wells going sour in the week they are needed, while each pull draws the holder down with the water and leaves its knot in the same soil, to come up later under their own cart, and moved to a new country they are slow to know what to do with their hands.

Spirit Temperance through grounding. The confessors’ reading: rootedness.

Alchemy Lute, the humble seal: it holds because it touches everything. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Vitality Absorption, Dexterity Economy, Dominion Gravity. The practitioner roots to whatever surface they stand on.

Glyph [Sed] Sediment; attested on three more.

Failure The root holds by touching everything, so it holds nothing it cannot touch. Put wet, grease or filth between the soul and its ground and the grip lets go, all of it at once, without warning and with no halfway. The Measurewrights call it contamination.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Static friction and molecular adhesion. Interlocking asperity; setal van der Waals contact.

Mechanism · Grip owes nothing to weight, they say. A gecko holds to glass through billions of hair-scale contacts each contributing an intermolecular attraction too small to measure alone, and by their reckoning Chthonica multiplies the Shell’s contact area with whatever surface bears it, converting proximity into anchorage, which is why its practitioners root to sheer stone and moving decks with equal indifference.

Cinerion · The Ash Veil

Folk name the Ash (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea What the fire leaves. Ash taken as treasure: every spent thing feeds the next thing, and the soul learns to live on its own aftermath.

Kind Wild

Titan Prometheus: what his fire leaves, which he has never once counted as waste.

Operation Calcination: the spent thing is burned down to its ash, and the ash is kept as treasure to feed what comes next.

Hue Gray-orange, embers showing through ash, because what the fire left is kept and the heat still lives in it.

Ground Favors burned-out ground, kilns, forges and hot springs, places where fire has long been at work and left its ash behind; hates lukewarm places where nothing has ever burned, cold that has soaked through everything, and air that will not feed a flame.

Contrary Sublimare (treasure in what stays behind against treasure in what rises).

On the Body The Shell eats its own leavings, turning waste heat and burned-off Charge into second wind, so the practitioner looks worst and feeds best at the end of a long fight.

On the Spirit What a working leaves behind feeds the next one, and the practitioner can read a thing by its residue, getting from a burned letter some of what it said.

On Fate Lost causes settle on the holder like ash in a low place, and after a long enough defeat a fighter can pull on what has been spent, so the failed feint of an hour ago becomes the place the opponent steps wrong. A beaten commander who lives on this may make the burned bridge and the dead men’s debts delay a pursuit by a week, at the cost of whatever ruin they had left to draw on, and the gift fouls and stops on the day they have spent the most. The knot of each pull is paid as one more loss, a little later, which the lens then gets to eat.

Spirit Temperance through aftermath.

Alchemy The law Calcination draws on, the Burning of the bench: strong dry heat drives off what is volatile and leaves what is fixed.

Stat Effect Strengthens Tempering Yield, Harmonics Stability. The residue of destruction becomes recovery material.

Glyph None attested in the Index (see Where the Registers Disagree on the hub).

Failure Every spent thing feeds the next, and the soul cannot refuse anything it has spent. Past a point the ash heaps up faster than it can feed anything and smothers the fire it was kept to feed, and the practitioner’s recovery fails exactly when they have spent the most. The Measurewrights call it fouling.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Pyrolytic char and its catalytic residue. Particulate optical depth.

Mechanism · People call what survives a fire waste, the guild says, and people are wrong. Char is a high-surface-area carbon scaffold that catalyzes further reaction, and settled ash returns potassium and phosphorus to the ground that fed the fire. By their reckoning Cinerion retunes the Shell to metabolize its own combustion products, harvesting from expenditure what other practitioners simply vent, and the suspended residue also scatters incident light and Essence-sight alike. On this one the benches and the guild agree about the ash and part company over why anyone would love it.

Coagula · The Unifying Pulse

Folk name the Mending (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea Gathering the broken. Scattered pieces come back into one body without ever melting, keeping the shape they had while broken, the flesh first and then the soul that wears it.

Kind Wild

Titan Gaia: material harmony, scattered matter returned to one body.

Operation Coagulation: the scattered pieces are gathered and set back into one body without ever being melted down.

Hue Amber-gray, because the broken pieces come back still wearing the gray of their breaking, held together in amber.

Ground Favors ore-rich rock, worked stone and deep foundations, places where broken stone has long been gathered up and set back into walls; hates open water, high peaks and moving decks, where nothing scattered comes back together.

Contrary Dissolution (the bench’s oldest pair, unbinding against gathering); Fractura (gathering the broken against finding where it breaks).

On the Body Broken bone and split muscle draw back together under the Shell with no melting stage and no pause, each piece keeping the shape it had while broken, so a fighter who has been smashed gets up crooked and whole. The great Coagula brawlers look like sacks of walnuts and are extremely hard to finish.

On the Spirit In the Core the same law does the old inward work: a soul split by a bad Threshold or a bad decade gathers up its own pieces, and what it sends holds together over distance because no part of it will consent to leave. Cast on another’s scattered soul it is dear and brief, and the sender’s own Core pays, in pieces that come back to the wrong places.

On Fate Scattered things come to the same table, and feuding cousins and lost partners find themselves in one town with this person somehow always in the doorway. The great working of it, a broken army’s stragglers finding the same crossroads on the same night, leaves a knot the holder is years paying off in partings of their own, and the reassembled line keeps every seam where it was, which is the first place a sharp enemy hits.

Spirit Temperance through merging. The confessors’ reading: the Soul Plane’s unifying force, a soul gathering itself back together.

Alchemy The personal twin of Coagulatio: gathering without melting, a person’s own Setting. Basalt takes it.

Coagula is a person’s gathering, flesh and soul together, and a few fighters at great cost have thrown the same current outward over a room or the ground underfoot. It lasts as long as they hold it. Matter gathered for good belongs to Coagulatio.

Stat Effect · Strengthens Resilience Integrity, Tempering Coherence. The Crystal becomes harder to split.

Glyph · None primary. Attested on [Krn] Crown and [Wy] Motion.

Failure · Gathering without melting keeps everything the broken pieces were, the gaps between them included. The places where they never quite met stay in the mended body and the mended soul, and they are exactly where the next break will start. The Measurewrights call it residual porosity.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Tenebra · The Hidden Shadow

For seven years there was a price on the head of Hanne Mott and nobody could describe the head. Heralds cried the bounty up from four hundred silver to two thousand, the minstrels made The Woman Who Is Not There, which gets her height and her number of legs wrong, and bookies took odds on whether she existed. Titan Prometheus: the first lesson of the teacher of Transmutation, that nothing is lost when a thing changes its form.

On the Body A missed fist or a failed block is nothing lost, and the Shell says it over in another form, so the stumble becomes the next step and the broken punch comes back through the other hand.

On the Spirit A working gone wrong can be restated from the Core in another basis, the same quantity said another way, and a thing described from far enough round starts to answer to the new description.

On Fate A broken oath or a bad debt can be carried over into other terms by a patient holder, and in a desperate hour so can a lost exchange, what was given up in the last pass turning up as the other fighter’s wrong step in the next, the knot being the same loss owed again later in terms the holder did not choose. Carrying a lost battle over into a won road, the routed wing turning out to be why the enemy’s supply has gone astray, is a masterwork few attempt twice, because a holder who loses track of the terms they started in does not come back as anything recognizable.

Hue Near-black with a retained faint glow, because it keeps everything it has taken and gives none of it back, save the warmth of all it carries, which cannot help showing.

On the Spirit Thrown from the Core, a working on this lens reflects nothing and reports nothing. Whatever it takes joins the pile, and a ward it swallows becomes one more thing the caster must carry for life.

Calling the whole weight down at once, so that a general who has beaten them five times looks up on the sixth field and finds all five defeats drawn up against him under one banner, is done once if at all, and the holder has to carry everything it brings, the knot with the rest.

Verdantia · The Green Pulse

The idea Growth without end. A self with no final size, which feeds on simply being out in the world. It comes through accumulation rather than rupture, one good day on top of another.

Kind Wild

Titan Florwyn: growth itself, her breath in every green thing.

Operation Fermentation: the soul is fed a little at a time from the world it stands in, and grows on that feeding without end.

Hue Chlorophyll green, because it is the color of everything that grows and has not yet stopped.

Ground Favors thick living growth, mild weather and ground close to blood, places where life has long gone on adding to itself; hates barren ground, bitter cold and Aether-thin soil, where nothing grows.

Contrary Monolithion (the finished monument against the self with no final size); Mortalis (more life against the boundary of life).

On the Body The body never settles on a final size and recovers simply by being outdoors, so a long fight on open ground tends to leave the Verdantia fighter bigger at the end than at the start. Keep them in a cellar and they grow long, pale and useless.

On the Spirit An inscription keeps growing after its author has walked away, and a line written on a wall in spring has put out new clauses by autumn. Nobody has found a way to make it grow in the direction they wrote.

On Fate A name spreads the way a root system does, quietly and in every direction, until the holder has cousins and tenants in towns they never visited, and after decades an invading army may find itself buying its bread and hiring its guides from all of them. A Domain (a region where the world runs on a practitioner’s terms) raised on this lens keeps creeping outward for as long as it is held, at a growing cost that neighbors resent and the holder, thinning out at the center, eventually cannot feed. A pull along those roots leaves a knot that goes on growing until the world collects it.

Spirit Temperance through growth.

Alchemy The Feeding: growth given a little at a time and never all at once. (the trade’s reading)

Glyph [El] Life, [Eld] Elderwood, [WL] Wildlaw, [Blm] Bloom, [Flo] Bloom-Root; attested on seventeen more.

Failure A self with no final size cannot stop growing, even when the world has stopped feeding it. Shut away from the open air it grows on anyway, all length and no substance, until it cannot hold itself up. The Measurewrights call it etiolation.

Every gardener in the realm could have told them, and did, for a fee.

Outside the Sixty · Vohrin · The Stillwater Core

This entry is the register’s one exception, and it is marked as one. The Sixty are fixed, and Vohrin is not among them. It is the power of a Titan, the Prime Cryotheic of Niflheim, worked directly at Wellspring level, where the other thirteen Titans reach a soul by way of the Sixty. The note sits on this page because a cold that stills is still a thing you can point at in the physical world, and because fighters keep writing Vohrin into their entries as one of the sixty, and somebody has to tell them what they have written.

Folk name the Still Cold (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea The cold that stills. Prometheus burns what is false, Zar’thuun unmakes what is excess, and Vohrin stills what is left so it can be built upon. Cold here is no fire of its own. It is a deficit, heat let go of and held as a discipline.

Kind Wild

Titan Vohrin itself: stillness after the end, the settling of motion and thought once everything else is done, which the Measurewrights call entropy’s peace. No Wellspring stands between this power and its Titan.

Operation Coagulation, in the old bench’s sense of congelation: what still moves is stilled and set where it stands, so that it can be built upon.

Hue Ember-orange banked down nearly to brown, under a skin of rime, because the fire is never put out, only banked and held; the cold that shows is what it has let go of. The blue belongs to the ice.

Ground Favors cold air, deep ice and the long northern winters, places where everything has long since settled and gone still; hates warm air, where a stilling costs many times as much, and ground already at rest, where there is nothing left to still.

Contrary Heat or motion forced back in faster than it can be stilled, which is why Pyreveil and its like are the standard answer. It stands outside the Sixty, so its contrary is read from its idea and no entry names it back.

On the Body The blow carries stillness into the wound, heat let go of rather than thrown, and the holder’s own blood runs slow and steady under it.

On the Spirit Projected from the Core, the cold stills what it reaches and holds a thing at what it already is, and a hurrying thought or a half-finished working simply stops where it stood.

On Fate What is held stays held: a bond stops moving in either direction, and with the whole of a life’s discipline a charge can lose its reasons halfway across the field, or a war about to happen can sit in camp through the last of the good weather. The result has only been put off, the knot waits with it and thaws on the same day, and whoever is holding it off grows steadily colder in the fingers and then in the purpose.

Two levels One power, worked two ways. The frost arts of the hunters and the northern schools work it at Wellspring level or as a Titan-derived current, each at the Stage floor its own entry states. The Titan-Keyed Stillwater Rite works it at the Titan’s level and is gated at Revelation.

Where it pools A cold site is a Titan-touched site. Surveyors and renderers read a Vohrin gallery the way they read a Core, with the same care and the same rope, and it does not go on any list of the Sixty’s sites.

Stat Effect None of record, since it has no row in Part Thirteen. In use, Vitality Tolerance answers for the cold.

Glyph No primary of its own that the register accepts. Its arts write in common forms such as [Vor] Return and [Cro] Cycle, and the Codex’s frost rows, [FrTh] FrostThrone and [ObsG] Obsidian-Glacier, still stand under Cymorath’s name (see the Cymorath entry).

Failure A stilling has no end of its own. Whatever is held still has to go on being held, and past what the holder can carry the stillness settles in them instead: the fingers, the lungs, then the mind, with purpose going before pain. A stillness that is only held is also broken by heat or motion forced back in faster than it can be stilled, which is how flame ends the Stillwater Rite. The Measurewrights call it cold soak.

Signature The aura is ember-orange banked down nearly to brown under a skin of rime. The blue that people remember is the ice, and the aura is not blue. Sound is a long creak of settling ice and then the sound of the room falling away; the air smells scrubbed and then of nothing; heat leaves the skin toward the working. The practitioner’s lungs ache and small purposes slip. The tell is frost running out from the feet, with the breath stopping in the air before it has gone anywhere.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Heat extraction. Conduction down a held gradient and the minimum work of a heat pump, which climbs steeply as the cold end falls; at the Titan’s level, the zero-flux condition of a body that neither emits nor absorbs.

Mechanism · Cold is never emitted, they say. A region goes cold only when energy leaves it, so by their reckoning the Shell takes heat out along a gradient that already exists and has to put it somewhere: the lungs, the air behind the practitioner, the bank of a field. In cold air the heat is cheap to move and in warm air it costs many times as much, and in thermal equilibrium there is nothing to pump at all; at contact the ordered cold crystallizes the working’s path through a wound. Their figures for the warm-air price are good. They have never said where the heat goes at the Titan’s level, where nothing leaves at all.
