Medium Wellsprings

Medium Wellsprings

Nov 28, 202626 min read

Six of the Sixty, at home on all three planes · the kind that works on you while you work it

Jory Quill of Long Sallow found water for a living, a penny a well and a penny more to tell you what the well remembered, with a copper-green sheen in his palms and dew beading on the bucket rope before he had touched it. Two villages hired him in the same dry June over the one spring between them, and he stood in the shallows in front of both with the water standing up round his shins like a dog that knows its name, took a mouthful, and told them whose great-grandfather had poisoned it and what he had been paid. The water moved in the ground that afternoon, so that the spring came up on the other bank. The old debt came due that same week in three marriages and a boundary stone. The two villages have been fighting in the happiest way ever since, with a joint fair and a rule about knives. Jory went home knowing a lullaby in a language nobody in the county speaks, and he cries when he hums it, and he will tell you for free that he has no idea whose it is. What goes into that water does not come out again, and for that half-hour he was in it.

What Medium means · A Medium Wellspring is at home on all three planes and still crosses the Veil, operating in every direction at once at the threshold where one plane’s law softens into another’s. No one element leads it: all six are at home on every plane. The practitioner reshapes the thing and is reshaped by it in the same act, and the observer is altered by the observation, so neither body nor Crystal gives a stable reference point. A strong Medium site looks to a visitor like illness or hallucination.

The lens · A soul tempered on one of these has the whole idea in everything it does, a little at first and after years of mastery up to double, and because all three Paths are home it can reach double on whichever one the soul walks. That sounds like a bargain, and the trade will gladly sell it to you as one. It costs nothing extra, and it goes both ways. Everything a Medium lens does to the world it is also doing to you, so a Medium practitioner of thirty years is a person with a great deal of company. The full account of the lens is on the hub.

Each Wellspring below opens on what it is. The idea is the concept it lays over a soul’s Aether, and The turn, where the trade has fixed one, is the moment that idea comes clear to someone working inside it. Kind names its home plane, and never locks it. Titan is the Titan whose law it is one showing of, Hue the color it gives an aura, Ground what it favors and hates underfoot, and Contrary the Wellsprings whose laws fight it in one soul. On the Body, On the Spirit and On Fate say where the same idea lands on each Path. On the Body it shapes raw Aether packed in the Aether Shell (the layer of the soul that meets matter, and the only door into it) and works on matter, with no Essence spent and the Shell’s furnace burning off as heat. On the Spirit it shapes Essence (spiritual energy) spent through the Essence Core (the layer that holds identity and authors what a working is) and works on souls and on what a thing means and holds, never on matter, at a price paid in the Core as hollowing. On Fate it shapes the claim on the Attraction Layer (a soul’s own share of fate, its bonds, oaths, claims and odds), so that the Thread pulled through that claim takes its shape, and it works on what a thing could have been, and every pull leaves a knot the world collects later. Spirit is the transformation it works on the soul, one road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage), and Alchemy the principle it stands for at the bench. Then come Stat Effect, what shifts on the sheet, Glyph, the forms the Index seats on it, and Failure, how it goes wrong, from the flaw its idea cannot hold. Operation, after Titan, names the bench operation the idea performs, the one a working’s Codex line carries. Last on each entry stands The Measurewrights’ account, Analogue and Mechanism: that guild’s physics of what the type of Aether does to a Soul Crystal, often right about how much and never about why. Entries run in alphabetical order.

Anamnesis · The Wellspring of Remembrance

Folk name the Remembering (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea Remembrance. Everything keeps its past, and a soul under this lens can touch a thing and read it.

Kind Medium

Titan Chronaeus: the debris of events compacted into layers, and a soul that can read them.

Operation Separation: the past a thing has kept is parted from it and read, as the residue is parted from the vessel that held it.

Hue Faded violet, because everything it reads is already past, and the color has the age of the thing on it.

Ground Favors darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground and ruins, places where little new has happened for a long time and the past lies undisturbed for the reading; hates saturated Aether, crowd density and active Domains, where the present is so loud that nothing old can be heard under it.

Contrary Letheveil (forgetting on purpose against everything keeping its past); Sublimatio (the record kept against the lower state left behind).

On the Body A palm on a spear shaft gives back the grip that wore it and the last blow it took, and a hand on a broken wall gives back the blow that broke it, in enough detail for the practitioner’s own shoulders to repeat it. The flesh under this lens forgets nothing it has ever met, so an old fighter has a lifetime of falls and parries standing ready in the joints.

On the Spirit In the Core it reads a soul as a fired pot reads its kiln: what this person has committed to, what was done to them, how long ago and at what dose. Inscribed on a thing, it makes that thing’s history part of what the thing is, which is how an old sword comes to know its trade.

On Fate Every oath, debt and death leaves a record on the Attraction Layer and this lens reads it on contact, so a handshake can say who else this hand has sworn to, and a sword taken from a dead man can say which guard he always dropped. With a palm on a fortress’s footing it finds the winter the mortar was skimped and the oldest claim on that ground, and calling either one in, so that the wall remembers what it is owed, takes a lifetime’s skill and a body’s worth of blood. Courts pay very well for the reading and then try to have the witness quietly sealed, and a careless pull erases the very record it was pulling on. The knot is a record of the holder’s own, called in against them when they had forgotten it was kept.

Spirit Temperance through awareness. The confessors’ reading: remembrance, which preserves charged records of lived commitment, and more than mere memory.

Alchemy The fired pot’s testimony: the residue in a vessel says everything it has ever held. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Gnosis Retention, Gnosis Analysis in reading past trauma, Resilience Anchoring. The practitioner reads history through contact with objects, places, and beings.

Glyph [Ma] Recall, [AnMa] Anamnesis, [Lei] Binding, [VaM] Moment, [Mn] Mnemonic, [EchoS] Echo-Scar; attested on nine more.

Failure Everything keeps its past, and the reading becomes part of that past the moment it is made. A careless hand laid on the record writes itself over what it came to read, so a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of taking it. The Measurewrights call it thermal resetting.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why. Anamnesis is an instrument, and anyone calling it a miracle has not looked hard at a fired pot.

Analogue · Remanent magnetization and thermoluminescent dosimetry.

Mechanism · Basalt, they point out, records the direction of the field it cooled in and holds that record for geological time, and fired ceramic accumulates trapped electrons in proportion to the radiation dose it has absorbed since its last firing, and releases them as light when heated. Objects, by their reckoning, genuinely store their history in physically readable form, and so, each in its own layer, do a body, a soul and a sworn word, which is their reason this Wellspring is at home reading any of them. Their instruments can read the dose a pot has taken; none of them has ever read whose hand wore the spear.

Aurevane · The Wellspring of Light

Folk name the True Light (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea Light that does not flatter. What you see grows truer, and what you send out arrives exactly as you meant it.

Kind Medium

Titan Omnis: he carries light down into matter, and this is that light as it arrives.

Operation Sublimation: what is seen and what is sent are raised clear of everything that would cloud them, until only the true thing is left to arrive.

Hue Hard-edged white-blue, because a light that does not flatter leaves no soft edge for anything to hide in.

Ground Favors dry air, ground veined with metal that carries a light along, and a clear line of sight, places where nothing stands between a thing and the eye that looks at it; hates iron-caged interiors, rooms walled in heavy iron and dense fog, where the light is shut in or smeared before it can arrive.

Contrary Tenebra (light that arrives as sent against light that never comes back); Vantabriel (emission against absorption); Eclipseron (seeing the thing against reading the hole it leaves).

On the Body The eye resolves a fight down to the grain of a wrist, and what the limb sets out to do arrives with no slop in it, every ounce landing on the span of skin it was meant for.

On the Spirit What leaves the Core goes out whole and arrives exactly as it was meant, and whatever stands in that light shows what it is without flattery.

On Fate A word or an oath sent under this lens arrives with its whole meaning intact, and presence carries clear across a square or a field, which is most of what its fighters do with it. Drawn tighter it can close the space between an order given and an order received, so a signal reads true through the smoke at the far wing, or for one breath the single shaft loosed at a gap in the harness meets nothing to spoil it. That breath costs a fair part of the reserve and leaves the eyes bleeding and a knot behind it, some message of the holder’s own that later arrives garbled, and since all of it shines, the enemy’s first crossbow of the morning always knows where to look.

Spirit Temperance through illumination.

Alchemy The clear light of the watched vessel: the color that tells the worker the truth about the charge. (the trade’s reading) Iron conducts it.

Stat Effect Strengthens Gnosis Perception, Vigilance, Harmonics Attunement. What you see becomes truer. What you express becomes more accurate.

Glyph None attested in the Index (see Where the Registers Disagree on the hub).

Failure Light that does not flatter shows the one who carries it as plainly as anything else, so Aurevane practitioners are the easiest people in any engagement to locate. Pushed too hard it will not soften even for its own way out: what is sent leaves through the worker’s own eyes as true and hard as everything else it touches, burns the road it leaves by, and the eyes go first. The Measurewrights call it glare.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Optical resolution and coherence. The Rayleigh criterion; phase-locked emission.

Mechanism · Two points, they say, are resolvable only when their diffraction patterns separate, and coherent light holds a fixed phase relationship across its whole front, which is what separates a true beam from a bright lamp. By their reckoning Aurevane raises both figures at once: what the practitioner perceives resolves finer, and what they express leaves the Shell in phase, meaning the output matches the intent with almost no distortion introduced in transit. A stroke, a meaning and a sworn word can each arrive true or arrive smeared, which in their account is why this lens is at home on all three Paths. Why the light will not flatter a liar is not a question their lenses have been ground to answer.

Fixatio · The Binding Flame

Folk name the Knot (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea Commitment. What is bound here stays bound, because the way back has stopped existing.

Kind Medium

Titan Atla’zon: he decides whether Essence stays inside the shape it was poured into, and here it does.

Operation Coagulation: what is bound is set past melting, and the way back is gone.

Hue Amber resin, because what it binds is held like a fly in amber, exactly as it was when the setting came.

Ground Favors mineral-rich rock, worked stone and deep foundation, things that were set once and have stayed set for longer than anyone remembers; hates open water, high altitude and moving platforms, where nothing holds still long enough to be bound.

Contrary Manganthra (the go-between that stays out of the product against the bond nobody can leave); Somnalis (every branch held open against the way back closed); Dissolution (the bond let go against the bond past going back).

On the Body The cure goes into the flesh: a grip closed under this lens does not open until told, a set bone holds through a fall, and a stance once taken has to be cut out of the ground. A fighter who sets a wound wrong carries the mistake to the grave.

On the Spirit Written from the Core, an inscription is cured into what a thing is, so a seal stays sealed and a line of characters on a door is a fact about that door until the door is gone. Every sealing school in the four quarters trains on it, and each of them has one room it cannot open.

On Fate An oath under this lens has no way back in it, and the sworn thing is treated as already done: claims hold across generations, bonds hold past deaths, and the terms of a Domain (a region where the world runs on a practitioner’s terms) set as hard as law. A captain who binds her company to a breach can take every road off it out of the day’s reckoning, and a duelist who binds one cut to one man can reduce the fight to a question of when, and either may stake the rest of a life on it. The knot of such a binding cannot be shifted either, and falls due as one road of the holder’s own closed for good. Few try it more than once, because a binding placed in error stays placed, which is why the old marriage contracts take a week to read and why some garrisons are still holding ground nobody wants.

Spirit Temperance through permanence. The confessors’ reading: commitment, in which what is held stays held.

Alchemy One of the six Wellsprings the bench names its works after: it anchors and seals, and every Sealing chain on the bench runs under its law. The Bench files a Fixatio Draft as a Coagulation. Basalt takes it.

Stat Effect Strengthens Vitality Fortitude, Resilience Anchoring. What is held by Fixatio stays held.

Glyph [Tp] Topology; attested on twelve more.

Failure Commitment cannot tell a true binding from a mistaken one, because the way back is closed for both alike. Wanting it undone does not undo it, and a Fixatio binding placed in error is a permanent feature of whatever it was placed on. The Measurewrights call it irreversibility.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Covalent cross-linking. Thermoset cure.

Mechanism · A thermoplastic, they say, can be melted and reformed indefinitely, while a thermoset, once cured, has formed covalent bridges across its whole network and will char before it flows. By their reckoning Fixatio performs the cure: what it holds is held by the fact that the alternative configuration no longer exists as a reachable state, and force has nothing to do with it. A tendon, an inscription and a sworn word are all networks of a kind in their telling and all can be bridged past undoing, which is why the trade has never agreed on which road this Wellspring belongs to. Their tables of cure times are the best in the four quarters, and their own sealing school still has its one room.

Luminalis · The Hidden Light

Folk name the Lamp (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea The hidden light. Seeing what hides and hiding what shows, one act run in two directions.

Kind Medium

Titan Nysera: the line between what is seen and what is there, drawn in light.

Operation Separation: what is seen is parted from what is there, so the hidden can be drawn into view and the shown put out of it.

Hue Near-white with ultraviolet edges, because what it sees and what it hides both lie at the rim of sight, just past where the eye can follow.

Ground Favors quartz-bearing rock, twilight and moonlit ground, still water, glass and any clean boundary where a look can be turned back; hates flat glare with no shadow in it, smoke and blown dust that smear every look before it lands, and open country at noon where nothing has an edge to hide behind.

Contrary Penanceflare (the light that cannot hide against the light that hides what it shows); Mirithane (hiding what shows against the inside and outside made one surface).

On the Body The eye reaches past what plain sight shows and the Shell turns a look aside at its surface, so the fighter sees the warmth of a man behind a screen, and the man’s eyes slide off the place where the fighter is standing.

On the Spirit The soul sees what hides and hides what it shows, and one working run in either direction will turn up a concealed Trait or bury its maker’s from anyone reading.

On Fate A bond or a claim can be kept out of every reckoning and produced at the right moment, and secret loyalties show to the holder like a second color on the people in a room. At great cost the reason a blow is going to land can be kept off the other fighter’s reading until it has landed, and perhaps once in a long career the enemy’s count of its friends is simply wrong, because the captain bound at midnight twenty years ago shows no color until the morning he opens the gate. Holding a thread that far out of sight is a lifetime’s labor, and it splits the holder so that their own later workings arrive out of step with themselves. Its knot is something the holder needed to see, kept from them on the day it matters.

Spirit Temperance through perception.

Alchemy The hidden tincture: the color present in the charge that no eye reads until the right light finds it. (the trade’s reading) Quartz amplifies it.

Stat Effect Strengthens Gnosis Perception, Dexterity Feint. The practitioner sees what hides and learns to hide what they reveal.

Glyph [Lum] Light, [Ie] Perception, [Sar] Reflection, [Vei] Depth, [Oc] Oculus, [Dn] Dawn; attested on nine more.

Failure Seeing and hiding are one act run two ways, and a soul that keeps both running at once begins to run two ways itself. Its own output splits along the seam between what it shows and what it hides, the two halves going out at different paces, and complex workings arrive out of step with themselves. The Measurewrights call it birefringence.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · The non-visible spectrum and polarization. Total internal reflection.

Mechanism · Most of the spectrum, they say, is not available to the eye, and most of what matters is happening there. By their reckoning Luminalis extends perception past the visible band and gives the Shell a polarizing behavior: incident observation is filtered out at the boundary rather than absorbed, and light that strikes past the critical angle does not leave at all. Seeing and hiding turn out, in their account, to be the same optical operation run in opposite directions, and a body, a soul and a claim each have a surface that a look can be turned from, which is their reason this lens is at home on all three Paths. Their optics have nothing to say about a captain who shows no color for twenty years.

Mortalis · The Black Gate

Folk name the Black Gate (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea The black gate. Ending looked at squarely and understood as a passage with its own law, by a soul that has stopped fearing it and has started, a little, to cool.

Kind Medium

Titan Valmir: the Veil Threshold and the concept of completion, looked at squarely.

Operation Dissolution: a thing’s hold on what it is gets loosed, lawfully and in order, until it comes apart at its proper end.

Hue Venous black-red, the color of blood that has stopped hurrying, because it is life looked at from the side of its ending.

Ground Favors thick living growth, mild country and the nearness of blood, where there is always something on its way to its end; hates sterile ground, deep cold and Essence-depleted soil, where nothing lives and so nothing passes.

Contrary Terranova (every wound answered with new ground against the ending understood); Anima Spirare (a body refusing the gate against a soul walking to it); Verdantia (more life against the boundary of life).

On the Body The body learns its own ending as a known road, so a Mortalis fighter goes on working, cool and unhurried, well past the wound that should have dropped them. A blow from that hand tells flesh it may stop spending itself, and flesh is often relieved to hear it.

On the Spirit The Core looks at ending squarely and can author it, withdrawing a thing’s hold on what it is, lawfully and in order: a ward lapses, a ghost goes on its way, a curse finds it has reached its term. It keeps nothing and nobody afterward.

On Fate It works the last term of every tie, so the holder feels a bond, a line or a life come near its end and can see a dying oath out with honor, which is most of why they are sent for. Hurrying an end is done by few: a wound that would have killed by morning called in before the exchange is over, or in a lifetime’s one great working a broken wing that might have lasted till dusk learning at noon that it has already ended. Endings belong to Archons and the great principles before they belong to anyone, and the holder who has reached for one comes back a little cooler and does not warm again, with a knot that ends something of their own before its time.

Spirit Temperance through surrender. The confessors’ reading: dissolution, threshold, completion and the lawful withdrawal of presence. It governs passage and keeps nobody in storage.

Alchemy The Blacking: the charge gone black in the Rot, at the point where a matter stops holding itself together. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Resilience Persistence, Gnosis Cognition. The soul engages ending as a structural event rather than a catastrophe.

Glyph None primary. Attested on nine, among them [Au] Death, [Ob] Obol and [SoulDr] Soul-Drift.

Failure A soul that has stopped fearing the gate has also stopped holding out against it, and the fear was part of what kept it on this side. Stay near the ending long enough and the worker drifts toward it, cooling a little at a time without noticing. The Measurewrights call it equilibration.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · The non-equilibrium steady state, and the thermodynamic boundary of its collapse.

Mechanism · A living body, they say, is a structure held far from equilibrium by continuous expenditure. Death in their account adds no event to that structure: it is the cessation of the expenditure and the return to equilibrium that follows without needing a cause. By their reckoning Mortalis lets the practitioner engage that boundary as a described physical transition rather than as a catastrophe, which is a stranger and colder comfort than it sounds. The boundary runs through every layer of a soul alike, they hold, since flesh, identity and bond all end by the same law, and that is their reason this lens is at home on all three Paths. The guild still buries its own dead with the same prayers as everyone else.

Phreatis · The Waters of Memory

Two readings, one entry. The same Wellspring is read two ways because memory itself is. On the one reading it is the keeping of what was lived, the path in a soul worn deeper each time it is walked. On the other it is deep water: rain reaching the well decades after it fell, and returning what entered it, poison included. The Measurewrights give each reading a physics of its own, at the foot of the entry.

Folk name the Waters of Memory (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea Memory as deep water. What is lived sinks in, is built into you, and comes back long after the weather has changed.

Kind Medium

Titan Sylorin: the memory the current carries, which reaches the well decades after it fell.

Operation Dissolution: what is lived is taken down into the deep water and held there in solution, to be given back whole long after.

Hue Copper-green, the green old water leaves on a copper basin, because memory works on a soul the way water works on copper, slowly and for good.

Ground Favors standing or moving water, damp air, springs and wells where deep water is pushed up into the light, and thick living growth, all places where what sinks in comes back; hates arid terrain, the thin air of the heights, close still air that never turns over, and sterile ground, where nothing sinks in and so nothing returns.

Contrary Letheveil (what sinks in and returns against what is cut loose).

On the Body What the body has lived sinks into the tissue and comes back when needed, so a Phreatis fighter’s old drills surface whole in the hands decades after the last lesson.

On the Spirit The practitioner reads and draws what has sunk into water and into a soul, and a working can call up what a place or person has long since stopped remembering.

On Fate Nothing stays buried: old ties, debts and promises rise on their own schedule the way a spring does, and the holder can feel them coming. At the price of a heavy bite out of the reserve and a nosebleed that can last a day, one can be drawn up early, the lesson the other fighter’s first master beat into them wrong surfacing in the exact exchange where it will cost them. Doing the same to a war, the poisoned treaty coming due or the well sunk a hundred years ago going bad the summer an army camps on it, is the work of a whole life, and the knot is whatever else is down there, which comes up with it, the holder’s own old business included.

Spirit Temperance through reflection. The confessors’ reading: memory, what is lived built into the structure.

Alchemy The remembering menstruum: a solvent that keeps the trace of everything ever dissolved in it. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Gnosis Retention, Harmonics Attunement, Resilience Anchoring. Memory becomes architecturally reinforced. Identical effect on either reading.

Glyph [Ap] Aperture; attested on two more.

Failure Deep water does not ask what is sinking into it. Whatever is lived goes down and is built in, and nothing can be fished back out of it later on its own, so a falsified memory comes back up as readily as a true one. The Measurewrights call it contamination of the aquifer.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Long-term potentiation on the one reading. Aquifer recharge and isotopic dating on the other.

Mechanism · Synaptic potentiation, they say, records experience by physically strengthening a connection that fired, while groundwater carries an isotopic signature of the rain that fell decades before it reached the well. By their reckoning Phreatis holds both: memory as reinforced structure, and memory as a slow reservoir that returns what entered it long after the weather has changed. Flesh, soul and sworn word each take in what is lived and give it back later, which in their account is why this lens is at home on all three Paths. Neither of their readings can say whose lullaby Jory Quill brought home.
