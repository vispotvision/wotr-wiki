Spirit Wellsprings

Spirit Wellsprings

Nov 28, 202656 min read

Fifteen of the Sixty, at home on the Soul Plane · the kind with nothing to point at

Dame Hesketh Rook, the Mallet of Copper Row, fought nine summers in the Tollgate brackets on the strength of hating her father, and the whole quarter came down in yellow ribbons to watch her do it. At fifty she sat down with a confessor to settle a bet and got up three days later harmonized to Absolution, with no glow on her, no heat in her hands and nothing anyone in the stands could point to. A Hot Tun brawler threw his best at her in the next final, and she took it on a shoulder that had been broken twice and had stopped remembering either time, and laughed, and won. The bookies paid out. The minstrels complained that there was nothing to sing about. A Guild surveyor went over the ring with his instruments for an afternoon and reported an entirely normal ring. That night she took a jug up to the old man’s grave with half the quarter creeping along behind her to hear what she would say, and what she said was that he would have liked the second round. It was some weeks before she noticed that the best punch of her life, the one with his face on it, had gone with the rest, and she has been offering good money for it ever since.

What Spirit means · A Spirit Wellspring is a change in a soul: forgiveness, forgetting, a failure owned. Its home is the Soul Plane, Ashura’s reality, and its Aether (mana, the raw stuff of magic) works on the soul and nowhere else. There is nothing to point at and it gives its bearer no fire to throw. It usually does little or nothing to the physical world, though an edge case will always find a Measurewright (a certified assessor of workings): a Thaloré grief can be felt across a room, and the argument over whether that counts is older than the Guild. The landscape of a Spirit site looks entirely normal, so the surveyor’s environment is a reliable instrument and the surveyor is not. An analogue on one of these entries is the scholars’ picture of what happens in the soul, and no one has yet watched a forgiveness anneal.

The lens · A soul tempered on one of these changes what it can bear, know, keep or let go: a little at first, and after years of mastery on the Spirit Path, its home, up to double, so that the same Grade carries twice the weight of the idea into everyone who has to deal with it. Any Path can take any of them. On the Body Path a Spirit lens still gives no fire and changes what the flesh can stand. Away from home half again is the most anyone has got out of one, at a higher cost and with a rougher result, and a rough result here is a hole in your own childhood. The full account of the lens is on the hub.

Each Wellspring below opens on what it is. The idea is the concept it lays over a soul’s Aether, and The turn, where the trade has fixed one, is the moment that idea comes clear to someone working inside it. Kind names its home plane, and never locks it. Titan is the Titan whose law it is one showing of, Hue the color it gives an aura, Ground what it favors and hates underfoot, and Contrary the Wellsprings whose laws fight it in one soul. On the Body, On the Spirit and On Fate say where the same idea lands on each Path. On the Body it shapes raw Aether packed in the Aether Shell (the layer of the soul that meets matter, and the only door into it) and works on matter, with no Essence spent and the Shell’s furnace burning off as heat. On the Spirit it shapes Essence (spiritual energy) spent through the Essence Core (the layer that holds identity and authors what a working is) and works on souls and on what a thing means and holds, never on matter, at a price paid in the Core as hollowing. On Fate it shapes the claim on the Attraction Layer (a soul’s own share of fate, its bonds, oaths, claims and odds), and the Thread pulled through that claim takes its shape, and works on what a thing could have been, and every pull leaves a knot the world collects later. Spirit is the transformation it works on the soul, one road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage), and Alchemy the principle it stands for at the bench. Then come Stat Effect, what shifts on the sheet, Glyph, the forms the Index seats on it, and Failure, how it goes wrong, from the flaw its idea cannot hold. Operation, after Titan, names the bench operation the idea performs, the one a working’s Codex line carries. Last on each entry stands The Measurewrights’ account, Analogue and Mechanism: that guild’s physics of what the type of Aether does to a Soul Crystal, often right about how much and never about why. Entries run in alphabetical order.

Absolution · The Cleansing Current

Folk name the Pardon (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea Forgiveness. A soul’s history stays and its shape stays, and the old poison stored in them is let out.

Kind Spirit

Titan Dazkar: restitution completed, the debt let go once proportion has been met.

Operation Separation: the old poison is parted from the history that stored it, and the history is left whole.

Hue Polished steel, bright and unclouded, because the poison has been let out and the shape that held it is left whole with nothing locked under the shine.

Ground Favors old pilgrim roads, shrines and the quiet ground of a feud settled; hates fresh battlefields and any ground under a live grudge. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with nothing in the world for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to the ground’s own measures, Aetheric Density and, on ground like this, Echo Depth, which runs high here and cheapens a Spirit working.

Contrary Exuroth (the quench against the anneal); Tarturon (the debt held against the debt let go); Penance (the anneal against the hammer).

On the Body Old injuries stop costing what they cost: the knee keeps its scar and its bad angle and no longer charges the fighter for them. There is no fire and no glow, only a body that has stopped carrying its history.

On the Spirit At home it lets the old poison out of a soul, one’s own or a willing other’s. The memory and the shape it left stay exactly where they were, and what they had been leaking into everything else stops.

On Fate A debt or grudge can be let go and the thing it was pulling on stops pulling, and at the end of long confession a levied host held in the line by an old wrong may wake one morning with no very clear reason to charge. It takes all the holder’s strength to absolve what was never theirs to forgive, it fails more often than not and leaves a knot either way, some old grudge against the holder waking up elsewhere, and whether the men feel the line go slack or merely forget to form up is an old quarrel among confessors.

Spirit Temperance through forgiveness.

Alchemy The gentle reheat: the strain let out of worked metal and its shape left alone. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Tempering Clarity, Harmonics Attunement, Resilience Fortification. Old fractures lose their toxicity.

Glyph [Grs] Grace; attested on five more.

Failure Not every old hurt is poison: some of what a soul stores in its history is what holds it shut, and forgiveness, once it is running, cannot tell the two apart. Let out more than the poison and the soul loses the hold it had been quietly relying on to stay closed, and the old fractures it had carried safely open. The Measurewrights call it over-relaxation.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Stress-relief annealing. Removal of residual stress without change of form.

Mechanism · A worked component, they say, carries locked-in stresses from its own manufacture, invisible, dimensionally irrelevant, and entirely capable of causing failure under a load the part should have carried, and heat treatment relaxes them while leaving the shape untouched. By their reckoning Absolution does exactly this to the old fractures in a soul: the history stays, the geometry stays, and the stored energy that made the history dangerous is released. Reports of it easing anything outside a soul, a cracked bell or an unwilling stranger, are rare and argued over. Where the released energy goes, their ledgers have never said.

Abyntheus · The Outer Pulse

Folk name the Vastness (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea Vastness. The great scale read off the small vessel, a mortal soul stretched to take in things far too large for it.

Kind Spirit

Titan Ilgrimm: the unknown met at a scale that keeps knowledge humble, and breaks those it does not humble.

Operation Sublimation: the small soul is raised toward the great scale and brought back down still holding it.

Hue Deep naval blue, slow-pulsing, the color of open sea and night sky, because the soul has been stretched over the great distance and keeps the slow time of things far too large to hurry.

Ground Favors high open ground, sea horizons and clear night sky; hates iron-caged interiors and deep shafts. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with nothing in the world for the ground to act on, it fills a reserve from Aetheric Density like any other, and in a fight it answers to Echo Depth, the weight of memory a place holds.

Contrary Coagulatio (a soul stretched over the great distance against a soul settled round one grain).

On the Body It lends the arm nothing. It gives a frame that can stand under things too big to look at, so the fighter keeps their feet and their breakfast when a far higher Stage’s Pressure or the sky over a Well comes down on them.

On the Spirit The Core’s aperture widens past what a mortal head was built to hold, and a Principle or an Archon’s working becomes something the practitioner can read and, at some cost to their sanity, engage.

On Fate The holder feels very long ties, the bond laid down before they were born or the debt that runs to the far side of the world, and soldiers near them bear what others break under, having been made to feel how small one field is inside what is coming. A tug on one of those ties, which might lean a whole people’s old hatred on somebody’s border, is the labor of decades, and the longest threads are apt to belong to Archons and the great principles, who may look down to see who is pulling. The knot on a thread that long is sized to match.

Spirit Temperance through vastness.

Alchemy The Emerald Principle at the bench, as above, so below: the great scale read off the small vessel. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Dominion Sovereignty, Sense, Gnosis Cognition. The conceptual ceiling expands to engage Archon-tier and Principle-level phenomena.

Glyph [Ab] Abyss, [Abys] Deep, [Let] Tide; attested on three more.

Failure The small vessel can hold the great scale only while it still knows it is small. Stretched too long between the near end and the far one, the soul loses its sense of how the two fit together, and what comes back to it is noise the practitioner is nonetheless convinced is a signal. The Measurewrights call it baseline decoherence.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Very-long-wavelength propagation and interferometry. Resolution from baseline, not aperture.

Mechanism · Long wavelengths, they explain, travel enormous distances, penetrate what shorter ones cannot, and carry the oldest signals in existence. Resolution at that scale comes from separating two small instruments by a continental baseline and reconciling their phase, and a larger instrument would not help. They hold that Abyntheus expands the conceptual aperture of the Essence Core by the same trick, letting a mortal Crystal engage phenomena whose scale should render them illegible. All of it happens in the soul. It throws nothing and moves nothing, and the reports of far-off bells heard and floors felt to tilt around an old Abyntheus master are argued over at every Measurewright’s dinner.

Catharsis · The Wellspring of Release

Folk name the Letting Go (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea Letting go. A load set down so completely that the setting down spends it, and the soul comes out on the other side lighter and changed.

Kind Spirit

Titan Sylorin: Soul Reforging, a load of trauma spent down in the change of state.

Operation Dissolution: the hard thing held in the soul is loosed into the change of state and is gone into it.

Hue Pale rose-gold, the flush of a face after the weeping is done, warm and spent.

Ground Favors bathhouses, hot springs and confessionals, where people have long come to set things down; hates barracks and courts, where nobody dares. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with nothing in the world for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to the ground’s own measures, Aetheric Density and, on ground like this, Echo Depth, which runs high here and cheapens a Spirit working.

Contrary Pyraeon (keeping the heat for life against setting the load down); Vantabriel (setting it all down against carrying it all).

On the Body It gives a fighter nothing to throw. Terror, exhaustion and the shaking after a kill go out of the limbs in one long breath, and hands that could not close an hour ago close.

On the Spirit Grief and rage held in the Core are spent by the letting go of them and take the old order of the soul along, so the person who stands up afterward is calmer and honestly somewhat different from the one who sat down.

On Fate With everything the holder has, a bond or a load can be put down so completely that the line goes slack, and once in a long while a beaten company under such a captain sets down its terror all at once and stands up again with nothing left to rout from. Turned across a field it is rarer and stranger, the men opposite losing their hatred in the hour they needed it, and the holder comes out of it unable to say for a season what they themselves were fighting for, and is handed, later, a load that somebody else set down, which is the knot.

Spirit Temperance through letting go: release, purgation, true clearing. The Heresiology’s warning stands beside it, that nothing is purified by being merely emptied.

Alchemy Purgation: the matter gives up its burden in the change of state itself. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Harmonics Empathy, Tempering Maturity, Resilience Fortification. Emotional weight converts into structural clarity.

Glyph [Xr] Excision, [Sv] Sever; attested on three more.

Failure Nothing can be let go that has not first been admitted. A soul on this lens that will not say what it is carrying cannot set it down, so the load goes on gathering past the hour it should have gone, and when the admission finally comes it comes all at once and takes everything near it down with the load. The Measurewrights call it supercooling.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Latent heat of phase change. Enthalpy absorbed at constant temperature.

Mechanism · Melting ice at zero degrees, they point out, absorbs three hundred and thirty-four joules per gram and the temperature does not move a fraction, the whole of it going into breaking the ordered structure. They read Catharsis as the Core doing the same with an emotional load: routing it into a change of state instead of into output, so that the pressure is spent in the change and the practitioner arrives calmer and differently made. The ice is a comparison and all of this happens in the soul: nothing outside the practitioner changes by a degree, unless you count the chill reported in a few confessionals, which the guild is still arguing about.

Contrition · The Broken Flame

Folk name the Hard Lesson (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea A failure owned. Only what a soul truly got wrong and truly survived turns into armor for it.

Kind Spirit

Titan Dazkar: karmic return taken up willingly, the thing done coming back as armor.

Operation Fermentation: a failure truly lived through is left to work in the soul until the poison of it turns into armor against itself.

Hue Dull red with a sting, because a failure owned is a wound kept, healed over and still smarting wherever it is touched.

Ground Favors plague graves, hospices and ground where a whole people failed and lived; hates sterile ground and places that have never been tried. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with nothing in the world for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to the ground’s own measures, Aetheric Density and, on ground like this, Echo Depth, which runs high here and cheapens a Spirit working.

Contrary Lethegraal (no hand choosing what falls against a failure owned by name); Sublimatio (a failure kept as armor against a lower self left behind).

On the Body The body learns to bear what it has already failed against, and the tremor that took a fighter’s hand in their first rout does not come a second time, because the same fear met again is answered at once. A rehearsed failure teaches the flesh nothing, and neither does anyone else’s.

On the Spirit A real failure, owned without excuse, is written into the Core as a standing answer, so the deceit or the despair that broke a soul once finds it already armed. An excuse voids the record, and the Core knows an excuse when it has made one.

On Fate An admitted fault loses its hold, so a captain who has stood in front of her company and named her own rout has taken away a crack that a Fate fighter would have pried at, and the next oath sworn over it tends to keep. It has to be paid for by failing in earnest and surviving it, and such a person is famously hard to blackmail and seldom beaten the same way twice.

Spirit Temperance through acceptance. The confessors’ reading: a failure owned and survived.

Alchemy The dose: a poison survived at its measure, and the body keeps the receipt. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Tempering Maturity, Resilience Integrity. Past failures become structural reinforcement.

Glyph None attested in the Index (see Where the Registers Disagree on the hub).

Failure A failure owned becomes armor against that failure, and a soul that owns its failures too thoroughly stops telling the failure apart from itself. The armor spreads past what it was raised against, and the practitioner begins answering their own tissue as though it were the original insult. The Measurewrights call it autoimmunity.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Immunological memory and hormesis. Sub-lethal exposure producing adaptive benefit.

Mechanism · A pathogen survived once, they note, is answered faster and harder the second time because the response was recorded, and a sub-lethal dose of a stressor frequently leaves an organism measurably more capable than it was. Their reading is that Contrition converts failure into that record. On their account the mechanism requires the failure to have been survived, and it requires it to have been genuine, because a rehearsed one produces no antibody. The record is kept in the soul, and reports of it reaching further, a body shrugging off the very poison its owner once confessed to, are few and argued over. How the Core tells a rehearsed failure from a real one is a question they answer by changing the subject.

Eclipseron · The Shadowed Star

Folk name the Absence (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea Reading what is missing. The shape of an absence taken as knowledge of the thing that cast it. This lens has the truest claim of the Sixty to reading what fails to be there. People call that darkness and are being romantic: it is a system described by its absences, which is a harder and less romantic thing than its practitioners generally admit.

Kind Spirit

Titan Ilgrimm: Shadow as Revelation, truth glimpsed sideways.

Operation Distillation: what was driven off is read from the dead head left behind in the retort, the absence drawn down into knowledge of the thing that cast it.

Hue Red-violet with a dark disc, because the lens is read by what is missing, and the disc is the absence at its center that tells the rest of the light what cast it.

Ground Favors Veil-thin zones, surveyed ground and the sites of old observatories; hates warp scarring, contested Domains and unmapped terrain. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with nothing in the world for the ground to act on, it fills a reserve from Aetheric Density like any other, and in a fight it answers to Echo Depth, the weight of memory a place holds.

Contrary Aurevane (seeing the thing against reading the hole it leaves).

On the Body The body learns to bear what has been taken from it, with no fire or force added: a missing finger, a deaf ear or a dead leg stops throwing the fighter, whose limbs now know the exact shape of what is gone and work around it.

On the Spirit The practitioner reads a soul by what it withholds, and derives from the shape of a silence, or of a memory cut out with a clean edge, the thing that used to be there.

On Fate The holder reads the shape of what is missing, the oath left out of the contract or the column that ought to be on the ridge by now, and that reading takes nothing but a long sad habit. To act on it is the hard thing: after years of grief spent learning how, a company under this person can be made to close on the shape of its dead, so the gap torn in the line at noon never opens in the men beside it, and the holder carries the whole absence afterward, with a knot that falls due as one more gap, opened where they were not looking.

Spirit Temperance through eclipse.

Alchemy The dead head read: what stayed behind in the retort tells you what left it. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Gnosis Analysis, Harmonics Suppression, Dominion Sense. The practitioner reads reality by what is missing from it.

Glyph [Oth] Path, [Ph] Phase; attested on five more.

Failure An absence is knowledge only if something cast it, and the idea cannot tell a gap left by a thing from a gap that was always empty. A practitioner committed to reading what is missing will eventually construct an object out of a gap that had nothing behind it. The Measurewrights call it false transit.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Occultation and transit photometry. The information carried by absorption lines.

Mechanism · A hidden planet, they say, is found by the dip in its star’s brightness long before anyone sees it, and a spectrum’s dark lines carry more composition data than its bright continuum. By their reckoning Eclipseron tunes the perception of the Essence Core to the negative: the practitioner reads what has been blocked, subtracted, or withheld in a soul, and derives from the shape of the absence the shape of the thing casting it. It casts no shadow and dims no lamp, and whether a master can read a sealed box the same way, from the hole it leaves in a room, is claimed by some and argued over by the rest.

Hypnather · The Sleep Crown

Folk name the Sleep Crown (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea The sleep crown. Change that comes only at rest, the soul setting itself in order in the dark while nothing is asked of it.

Kind Spirit

Titan Nysera: the sleep that passes under her veil, the soul setting itself in order where nothing real is asked of it.

Operation Distillation: the soul is passed through the dark again and again while it rests, each pass leaving its order a little purer than the last.

Hue Glassy violet, the clear still violet of a dark room at rest, because nothing stirs on the surface while the soul sets itself in order underneath.

Ground Favors darkness, silence, sickrooms and old dormitories; hates crowd density and any place with an alarm bell. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with nothing in the world for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to the ground’s own measures, Aetheric Density and, on ground like this, Echo Depth, which runs high here and cheapens a Spirit working.

Contrary Ignivale (no gap between wanting and doing against change that comes only at rest); Tarturon (the soul at rest against a debt that will not let it lie down).

On the Body The body does its mending asleep and does it thoroughly, a single night’s rest doing the work of many, and none of it works while the eyes are open. Its fighters come to the field new every morning and are finished after a few nights without it.

On the Spirit At home the Core sets itself in order in the dark while nothing is asked of it, and the person who lay down is not quite the one who wakes. The rare art that lays that quiet on someone else in sight is really laying it on their soul, the sect masters say, with the body merely following, and nobody has settled the matter since the only witness is asleep.

On Fate A sleeper’s oaths hold and their enemies lose the thread of them, and a commander who will give up waking for it, sometimes for weeks, may see a camp rest when it ought to lie awake and a night attack come down the wrong valley, for a knot that falls due as a night’s rest the holder badly needed and does not get. The old rule against waking anyone under it is older than the Guild, because what stops partway sets worse than it began.

Spirit Temperance through rest.

Alchemy The Standing: nothing is done and nothing may be done, and the thing goes on ordering itself in the dark. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Vitality Regeneration on sleep cycles, Tempering Yield, Gnosis Retention. Recovery during rest becomes dramatically more efficient.

Glyph None primary. Attested on [Vel] Mind, [Vl] Veil, [AnMa] Anamnesis and [Ora] Truth.

Failure Change that comes only at rest belongs to the rest, and the soul has no say in when it is finished. Break it off partway, by waking or by force, and the order it was setting stops where it stood, worse ordered than before it began. The Measurewrights call it interrupted relaxation.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Structural relaxation. Physical aging of a glass toward its equilibrium configuration.

Mechanism · A glass, by their account, is a liquid that stopped moving before it finished arranging itself, and left undisturbed it continues to relax toward the ordered state it never reached, slowly and only while quiescent. They say Hypnather grants the Crystal that behavior. The reorganization happens during genuine rest and cannot be forced by ordinary means, which is why, they say, Hypnather practitioners sleep like the dead and wake structurally different. A few arts do force it, pinning the quiet true on command in the practitioner or in a body held in sight, so that the target’s motion slows and its outline smooths as though sleep had come down all at once. On their figures the physics is nearly free and the Essence is not, and a relaxation forced is a relaxation that ends in the Failure above.

Lethegraal · The Cup of Forgetting

Folk name the Cup (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea The cup. Time’s mercy hurried along: old pain settles on its own schedule, and no hand chooses what falls.

The turn Accepting that something is over, and meaning it.

Kind Spirit

Titan Chronaeus: pace, every disturbance settled in its own time. His Era-Forge’s Toll is written in this Wellspring.

Operation Dissolution: old pain is loosened into the soul on its own schedule until nothing of it is left but plain structure that holds weight.

Hue Gray-violet, slow ebb, because old pain settling on its own schedule gives up its color the way a bruise does, a little each day and never by anyone’s choosing.

Ground Favors old battlefields grown over, ruins and quiet graveyards; hates saturated Aether, crowd density and fresh wounds. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with nothing in the world for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to the ground’s own measures, Aetheric Density and, on ground like this, Echo Depth, which runs high here and cheapens a Spirit working.

Contrary Cataclysm (upheaval against settling on its own schedule); Penance (letting time take the old pain against going back to it on purpose); Contrition (no hand choosing what falls against a failure owned by name).

On the Body The same wounds close on the same schedule, and the terror stored in them drains early, so a man burned half to death can stand beside a cook fire by spring with no faster healing to show for it.

On the Spirit Under this cup the soul digests what it has been choking on, by its own clock and nobody’s choice, and old grief goes down into the Core as plain structure that holds weight. Physicians prescribe it for that reason, and nothing about it reaches anyone but the one holding the cup, unless you count the family who wonder why she has stopped crying.

On Fate Time wears bonds as well, and by pouring themselves out a holder can hurry it along, so the rage an enemy army carried out of its burned capital may have gone to mere complaint by the second week of the march. It is slow, draining work that nothing can aim, its knot least of all, and its holders have lost sieges to their own side’s forgiveness when a vow to die on the wall went slack on the morning it fell due.

Spirit Temperance through quiet. The confessors’ reading: time, old pain digested into neutral architecture.

Alchemy The settling: the charge left to come to rest on its own schedule, with no hand choosing what falls. (the trade’s reading)

Stat Effect Strengthens Tempering Maturity, Harmonics Stability. Trauma metabolizes faster. The Crystal digests old pain into neutral architecture.

Glyph [Cro] Cycle, [Len] Line, [StSl] Still-Sleep; attested on eight more.

Failure The cup can hurry time’s mercy and cannot choose where the settling stops, because no hand chooses what falls. Hurry it too far and the old pain does not come to rest but swings past it on every cycle, and the practitioner rocks back and forth rather than settling. The Measurewrights call it under-damping.

The Measurewrights’ account. This is the guild’s theory, often right about how much and never about why.

Analogue · Relaxation time. The characteristic constant with which a perturbed system returns to equilibrium.

Mechanism · Every disturbed system settles, they say, and the only question is how long. By their reckoning Lethegraal shortens the time constant. Nothing is removed and nothing is chosen; the same trauma metabolizes down to neutral architecture on a schedule the practitioner is not otherwise able to influence, which is why the Wellspring is prescribed and Letheveil is merely permitted. What set the schedule in the first place is not in their tables.

Letheveil · The Waters of Forgetting

Folk name the Forgetting (what carters, soldiers and the commons call it; each culture may add its own beside it).

The idea Forgetting on purpose. Learning which memories hold up the house and which ones are chains, and cutting the chains. It surfaces when denial has started to cost more than acceptance.

Kind Spirit

Titan Sylorin: what the river takes off a soul on the way to its next shape.

Operation Separation: the memories that are chains are parted from the ones that hold up the house, and skimmed off.

Hue Black-violet with color drained from the edges, because what is forgotten on purpose goes from the rim first, the chains skimmed away and the dark center left to hold the house up.

Ground Favors darkness, silence, ruins and still black water; hates crowd density and active Domains. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with nothing in the world for the ground to act on, it fills a reserve from Aetheric Density like any other, and in a fight it answers to Echo Depth, the weight of memory a place holds.

Contrary Phreatis (what sinks in and returns against what is cut loose); Anamnesis (forgetting on purpose against everything keeping its past); Redemption (cutting the wound out of the story against building the self around it).

On the Body The flesh keeps its own memories, flinches and old breaks among them, and this lens lets the body put down the ones it no longer needs, so the shoulder forgets the spear that ruined it and the knee forgets to buckle at the old angle. No strength arrives with it, and a hand that has forgotten the wrong scar will walk straight back onto the blade that made it.

On the Spirit At home it goes into the Core with a skimmer and takes off the memories that are chains, one’s own or a willing other’s, and what is left runs lighter and truer. Done to the unwilling it is the crime the seal-courts were built for, and whether it can be done at a distance is argued mostly by people who cannot remember.

On Fate Forgetting a debt does not strike it off, as a great many people have been surprised to learn, but with a great effort the holder’s own end of the line can be cut, and a company that has cut its end of a rout marches back onto the same field with no memory in its knees of how that morning went. The holder pays in their own childhood, piece by piece, and what the rout cost is still owed, with the knot added, when the collector reaches the door.
