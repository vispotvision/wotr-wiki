The Ladder and the Draft — Temperance, Advancement and Essence Drafts

The Ladder and the Draft — Temperance, Advancement and Essence Drafts

Nov 17, 202626 min read

The most expensive goose in the history of alchemy belonged to a chandler at Marrow Fair. Corvin Hathersage, nineteen years old and heir to the Hathersage wool money, had been told by three instructors that he had the floor for Splintering and none of the rest of it, so he hired the great stage at the autumn brackets, sold the seats at a silver mark a head, put on a gold sash and drank eleven hundred marks’ worth of breakthrough Draft in front of six thousand people, with a herald crying the price of every swallow. It worked, the way they work. His aura went from charcoal with a rumor of blue in it to a cracking web of arc-blue, every hair in the front four rows stood up, and the whole fairground watched his Crystal go to the melt. At exactly that moment Nob Pye, a stable-hand retained by his rival and possessed of a sense of occasion, let the goose out.

It crossed the great stage at a dead run, neck out and wings wide, screaming like a rusted gate, and it took him by the gold sash.

Whatever is in a Crystal at the moment of reorganization is built into what comes out. Hathersage arrived at Stage V before six thousand witnesses, a genuine Expert, with a lattice organized around the purest terror of his young life. He went on to kill nine men in formal duels, and he killed them superbly, with the Stage he had paid for and not one Release to his name. He could not cross a farmyard. Geese knew, somehow, and followed him from county to county in long honking lines, and the family spent a fair part of the wool money on crossbowmen. The instructors tell it as a joke. The apothecaries sold out of that batch before the fair closed.

The Stage-by-Stage account lives in The Sixteen Stages, with each Stage given four ways: the Codex (the Accord’s master register of laws and figures) ledger line, what it is from inside the person it is happening to, what it looks like from outside, and what the folk call it. This page does not repeat it.

What this page does is join the ladder to the rest of the system: what changes in each Crystal layer (the three layers of the Soul Crystal, which is the soul itself, made a working thing) as a soul climbs, what an Essence Draft is, what money does to advancement, and why the most profitable crime in every realm exists.

One sentence governs everything below. A Threshold requires a quantitative floor and a Catalyst Condition, and both can be bought.

I · The two keys

Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage) is subtraction. The Soul Crystal is literally the soul, and to temper it is to melt it down and rebuild it, layer by layer. Each pass tempers every layer in its own coin. It packs the Shell tighter, so the Shell can draw in, hold and drive out more raw Charge, and cleaner; it refines the Core’s Essence and makes that Essence stronger; and it firms the Layer’s claim, though no pass moves a Thread rung, which waits on grip and decades. The layer a soul leans on is rebuilt first and hardest, so a Body soul’s melt begins in the Shell, a Spirit soul’s in the Core and a Fate soul’s in the Layer. The body comes out of it gaining Traits, as The Trait System explains under What a Trait Is. Each Stage is a structural reorganization the Crystal undergoes when accumulated pressure exceeds its current architecture, and no Stage has ever been paid out as a reward for banked experience. A Threshold opens only when both keys turn.

The quantitative floor. Sufficient pressure inside the current Stage. Measurable, purchasable, and the half of the equation every hall’s assayers are trained to read.

The Catalyst Condition. A specific quality of experience the Crystal requires before it will authorize the transition. A practitioner can sit quantitatively eligible for months of in-world time and never pass a Threshold, because the Catalyst has not occurred. The Sixteen Stages names the Catalyst for each rung, and that list is the lived road: the accident survived, the bond held, the wound made load-bearing. It gives each one in three forms, under The Climb by Path, because the Crystal makes one demand at each floor and the demand arrives in the layer the soul leans on: a Body soul meets it in the flesh and in matter, a Spirit soul in a soul or a meaning, a Fate soul in a bond, an oath or the odds. Met on the lived road, it carries the soul through the four stages of the Great Work in its lead Path’s own terms, nigredo, albedo, citrinitas and rubedo: the lead layer broken down, purified, woken and fixed. A soul on two Paths meets its lead Path’s Catalyst and is asked for no second trial. A hitter who waits for a grief to do what only a load could will wait a long time, and the halls keep lists of them.

Seclusion is kept on the Path’s own ground. The long guarded sitting before a Threshold wants ground that feeds the layer being rebuilt. A Body soul secludes on Aetheric Density, where the air itself is thick with what the Shell packs. A Spirit soul secludes in Echo Depth, among what has been felt and meant in a place for a long time. A Fate soul secludes under Thread Weight, where a great deal has been sworn, owed or decided. The Three Grounds has all three, and a sect’s seclusion caves tell you which road the sect teaches before anyone opens a door.

The lived road is one way to turn the second key. A Draft or a blessing will also turn it. Stages can simply be bought, a purse can buy the Catalyst for any rung of the ladder, and the wealthy are stronger because they are wealthy. At Zenith a Hollow Throne must still recognize the buyer, so up there money buys the attempt and not the seat. The instructors who taught for two centuries that the second key could not be drunk were teaching a comfort, frequently on a house’s retainer, and every apothecary in the bracket towns was making a living off the difference.

A soul is assembled out of what happened to it and what it could afford, and the rich get to choose the order.

So the great houses hoard the top Drafts the way other people hoard grain before a siege, and they have fought more wars over a vault than they will ever admit to. The poor rise by three roads: dungeon luck, the crew that comes up with a core nobody expected and the nerve to keep it; guild patronage, a hall that stakes a promising hand against years of commissions; and blessings, from anything strong enough to give one and bored or fond enough to bother. A commoner holding a big monster core is the single most frightening sight in any lord’s county, and lords have burned villages on the rumor of one.

What a bought Stage buys, and what it does not

A bought Stage opens the door and no more. The Stage is real: the stat ceiling lifts, the Band gate clears, the color brightens, and the coil (the brass assay instrument a practitioner stands inside) reads the Stage the same however it was crossed, though it reads the seat differently, as The Sixteen Stages sets out in its appendix. Everything past the door still has to be got the long way.

A rich Stage IX may have no Release at all, while a poor fighter at the same Stage has all three, and the second one tends to win. Sects teach what money cannot buy: Releases, Domains, techniques, skills and the rites for their Wellspring, along with real fights, a crew and a name, which is why a duke’s son in a gold sash will still go on his knees at a mountain gate and scrub floors for an elder who is rude to him. What he is scrubbing for depends on his road, since a Release and a Domain are three different things on the three Paths, and Releases and Domains by Path sets them side by side.

II · The arc, in five movements

Summary only. The full treatment is in The Sixteen Stages. The Catalysts named here are the lived road.

Band I · Stages I to IV, the first part of Inner Refinement. Murmuring, Welling, Ascension, Flourishing. Better than nine souls in ten will live and die inside this Band, and everything the world thinks it knows about magic it knows from watching people who never left it. The Crystal opens, spills, finds direction, and stabilizes into an identity.

The first crisis · Stage V. Splintering. The Crystal fractures after surviving contact with something more than two full Grades above its ceiling, and the fracture runs through the layer the soul leans on. A Body soul’s breaks run through the Shell and are carried in the flesh. A Spirit soul’s run through the Core, along emotional lines, which is the form the old books give for everyone because Spirit workers wrote the old books. A Fate soul’s run through the Layer, along its bonds and its luck. On every road the break follows whatever the soul was already carrying badly. Survival is the whole requirement, and defeating the thing adds nothing.

Consolidation · Stages VI to VII. Glory and Refraction. The fractures become features, the aura starts generating environmental effects, and then the soul turns and looks at itself from outside. Refraction is where a practitioner first reads their own fracture record as an object of study.

Outer Influence · Stages VIII to X. Transcendence, Invocation, Realization. The first true Domain (a region where the world runs on the practitioner’s terms) forms from a wound consciously made load-bearing. Then the practitioner stops working alone. Then the Continuum (the lawful fabric of reality) stops resisting and starts accommodating, and the direction of that accommodation never reverses back.

Mythic and Absolute · Stages XI to XIV. Dissonance, Emanation, Principality, Zenith. The chosen approach to annihilation, what radiates from a soul that came back, formal registration of a law, and finally recognition by a Hollow Throne. Zenith is conferred, and no breakthrough reaches it: a purse may buy the attempt, and the Throne still decides, as The Sixteen Stages, Appendix B has it.

Beyond the canon · Stages XV to XVI. Revelation and Apex. Archon-participant territory and then a perpetual state with no ceiling beneath Apotheosis.

III · What climbs, layer by layer

The ladder is usually read as one number going up. It is three architectures changing at different rates, and they do not change together.

Harmonization runs across all three, and it is said once on The Three Paths: the lock happens at the Shell, where Aether enters, the tie is held on the Layer, and the lens shapes what the lead layer works: raw Aether in the Shell for a Body fighter, Essence in the Core for a Spirit one, the claim on the Layer for a Fate one.

The table is one soul read evenly, and nobody is. The layer a soul leans on, which is all a Path is, climbs ahead of the other two, and the assay slip shows it. A Body fighter’s Shell reaches each row’s column first: it packs and holds more Charge than her Core can yet make sense of, and her Aether Class sits at the Stage’s ceiling while her Essence Class trails it. A Spirit worker’s Core is the column that arrives early, readable to itself a Stage before the Shell has learned to say anything clearly, which is why so many of them are clumsy with a door. A Fate soul’s Layer column does not move with the rows at all past what the table gives everyone: the Domain and the claim come with the Stage, but the Thread rung comes by grip and decades, so the third column of a Fate soul’s slip is the one that surprises the clerk. Everything else about a Fate soul climbs like anyone’s. Its Fate Path Stage rises with the soul’s Stage and opens the Fate gates on schedule, and its reserve is Essence, refined in the Core as a Spirit worker’s is, because Thread is never stored and is only pulled one working at a time. Its Core column therefore runs closer behind the Layer than a hitter would guess. A soul walking two Paths shows two columns ahead and one behind, and the gap is where a careful opponent aims.

The architectural note on Dissonance. Souls that pass through Stage XI rather than around it arrive at Emanation fractured and denser than the clean path produces. Overflow, Overchannel and Persistence reach their true ceiling in no other way. Those fractures count as structure. Anyone who tells an ambitious practitioner otherwise is either being kind or has an interest in them not trying.

IV · Essence Drafts

Aether + Essence + Vessel + Glyphica (the glyph-writing that shapes a working) = Reaction. A Draft is a working stabilized into a substance, and the substance came from somewhere.

TypeSourcePotencyRisk
Creature-SourcedMagical creatures, monster cores above allHighestIdentity Contamination
Material-SourcedHigh-density mineralsModerateClean but weaker
EnvironmentalAffinity-saturated immersionLow per unit timeNear zero
SyntheticAlchemically blendedVariable and uniqueHighest. Internal reaction.

Where the material comes from. Every substance in the ledger carries a Resonance and a Wellspring, with the kind that names its home, alongside its Bearing and Holding grades. A dragon’s scale is no generic reagent: it carries a specific Wellspring resonance, it renders at a specific grade, and that grade begins falling from the moment the animal stops. The whole beast trade is an argument with decomposition, which is why the northern runners are the profitable ones and why some rendering houses are sited beside a glacier for no other reason.

Monster cores. A monster’s core is in a way its Soul Crystal, and it is worth more the stronger the monster was. This is the lawful Crystal Draw of The Standing Index, the Creature-Sourced stock the whole trade turns on. Monsters gain Levels by killing, each other included, so an old one has done a great deal of tempering of its own, and a crew that brings up the core of a famous beast with a bounty on it has often made more in an afternoon than its home village will see in a generation. An alchemist renders the core down, taking the Essence off it into Essence Drafts, and those go overwhelmingly to one use, which is tempering: a practitioner takes the course, goes to the melt, and comes out with each layer tempered in its own coin, the lead layer first, and a body that has gained by it. The Traits fed this way are the ordinary ones in the ordinary Reflections, see The Trait System, and they rise on the owner’s window like anything else that has been worked.

What stock does to whom. A course is matched three ways, and a good apothecary asks all three before she takes your money. The lens: stock from the Wellspring you temper on goes in clean, and foreign stock tempers just as well and adds carry (what a soul keeps of where its stock came from), so a Pyreveil fighter drinking wolf for a season is going to notice wolf. The layer: every monster’s stone is mostly Core, whatever the beast was, so every stone renders mostly to Core stock and feeds the Core. Tempering rebuilds each layer, so a course for the Shell is made from the Salt ash of the render, the part the bench reads as body, and a Body fighter drinks more of it and a Spirit fighter less. A Body monster’s stone parts heaviest in that ash, because the Charge the beast packed all its life set into the stone, and a renderer will pay over the odds for the core of anything that killed with its weight. The third layer: shaved Layer is the black-market Fate reagent. An ordinary stone has none of it. Only a Fate monster’s stone grows a thin skin of Layer, and no bench can tell that skin from what a dresser takes off a Crystal, the richest of which comes off a dead person’s. It firms a Layer’s claim in tempering and it is the base of the Fate breakthrough Draft and of the Fate Essence Draft, which is why nobody who owns one discusses its provenance. It refills nothing. A Fate worker’s reserve is Essence, and Core stock fills it like a Spirit worker’s.

The raw core. A core can be swallowed or broken raw, which skips the alchemist’s fee and delivers everything it holds at once. Most who try it die, and a good share of the rest come out wrong: a cooper who growls at his wife and cannot say why, a girl who has stopped sleeping and started hunting. The few who come out whole are the reason it goes on happening, and every one of them is a story told in a cheap tavern to somebody with nothing left to lose.

Identity Contamination is the risk that makes Creature-Sourced Drafts both the most potent and the most carefully counted stock in any hall’s cellar, since nothing else tempers as fast and nobody with a career to protect takes it without keeping tally of the doses. The Essence taken carries the identity it came from, and an Essence Core that absorbs enough of it stops being able to say cleanly which convictions are its own. The failure mode leaves the practitioner alive, with an Attunement (how the soul tunes itself to what it works) that has quietly stopped matching their Coherence (how closely its stated Path matches how it lives), and they are the last to notice.

The great crime. A Crystal can be cut from a dead practitioner and rendered, and it makes the strongest Draft of all, because no monster that ever lived tempered as deliberately as a person who spent a lifetime at it. It is the worst crime in every realm, the most profitable, and the reason Index Grade IV stock in circulation has never balanced against what the lawful sources can produce: some of those “monster cores” came out of people. Every realm hangs for it. Every realm has at least one great family whose fortunes improved abruptly after a war.

Master Work Drafts. A Master Work is a piece that does what its Grade and material should not be able to, made once at the end of a lifetime, always Unique or Mythic, and named. The trade’s own Master Works are Drafts, and the most coveted of them is a Fate Essence Draft, which bottles the manipulation of possibility itself: the very flow and idea of a thing, set in a vial. It is extremely hard to make, there are perhaps a handful in the world at any time, and people who could afford a county have instead spent their lives trying to get near one.

Fight Drafts. Everything above works on the ladder. The Drafts a fighter carries on the belt work on the reserve: a fight Draft takes a beat to get down, refills reserve, and leaves a crash that pays for it. It follows the road of whoever drinks it. A Spirit worker’s is Core stock: what pours in is Essence with nobody’s stamp on it, and the crash is the Crystal stamping it afterward, all at once. A Body fighter’s is a Salt: raw Aether in a fraction that packs straight into the Shell, which hits like a burn going down and leaves a crash of cramps and fever in place of the stamping. A Fate worker drinks Core stock, the same bottle a Spirit worker carries, since her pulls and her vows are paid from Essence, and there is no Fate fight Draft on any lawful shelf or any other: Thread cannot be bottled for the belt because it cannot be held. Drafts are graded by the Stage they serve and priced about threefold a Stage, and counterfeits are common enough that bracket crowds keep a chant for the man who has just swallowed a mouthful of chalk at the worst moment of his life.

V · What a Draft does to advancement

A Draft can supply the quantitative floor, a seed, and at the breakthrough grades the Catalyst itself. A blessing can supply a Catalyst too.

What no vial has ever supplied is control over what comes out. Stats forced to stay above their sustainable Grade by alchemical augmentation, bloodline exploitation or external metaphysical imposition generate Crystal Fracture Events at an exponentially increasing rate until the stat collapses or the Threshold is passed through crisis. That is the lasting forced stat. A boost for the length of a fight passes the Stage’s ceiling lawfully inside the two-Grade cap, read from the sustainable Grade on the card, and see Stacking and Spending for that.

So, in the matter of advancement, a Draft does four things, and the trade will sell you any of them with a straight face.

The everyday use. A course of Drafts fed into honest tempering, Salts for the Shell and Core stock for the Core in the measure the road wants. Each layer is tempered in its own coin, the lead layer first, the body gains, a Trait climbs a level or two, and the practitioner is ahead of the neighbor who could not pay. This is what most Drafts are for, it is the backbone of every apothecary’s year, and most of the world means nothing else by the word.

The honest shortcut. A practitioner who has met their Catalyst but sits below the floor can be brought to the floor. Legitimate, licensed, expensive, and it shortens the wait.

The breakthrough Draft. A practitioner who has not met their Catalyst is handed one in liquid form, and the Crystal goes to the melt on schedule. It is poured by Path, because the Catalyst it stands in for is met in the lead layer. A Body breakthrough Draft is built on Salt, a Spirit one on Core stock, and a Fate one on the Layer reagent, which makes the Fate Draft the dearest of the three at every Stage and the most criminal, there being almost no lawful Layer to make it from. A Draft poured for the wrong road takes a soul to the melt and asks the wrong layer the question, and the bench word for what comes out is slag. The Stage is real. Whatever the Catalyst would have taught is simply absent, and so is every Release that fighting would have found along the way, which is the whole of the difference between Corvin Hathersage and the people who wanted him dead. The price climbs about threefold a Stage on the shelf and then leaves the shelf altogether, and at the top grades these Drafts are held in house vaults, given as dowries and used as hostages.

The dishonest use. A practitioner with no Catalyst and no money for the real article can be pushed above their sustainable grade anyway, on Ladder Dust and its cousins. The Crystal will hold the numbers for a while. Then it begins fracturing, and the fracture rate compounds, and it ends one of two ways: the stat collapses, or the Threshold is forced through crisis and the practitioner arrives at the next Stage having been reorganized around whatever was in the Crystal at that moment. Whatever is in the Crystal at the moment of reorganization is built into what comes out. Somebody who forced Splintering while afraid is a permanently different practitioner from somebody who met it having decided something.

Blessings. A blessing grants a power out of a person’s natural reach, Level included, and it can turn the second key as a Draft does. A blessed Level still stops at a Band gate like any other, unless the same blessing carries the Stage’s Catalyst as well, and a Grade held up by a blessing is the giver’s load until the giver lets go. It appears on the sheet as its own line, and some people have lived for years under a line that read as a gift. Its own page has the givers, the terms and the way one comes off.

What this means for the world. The trade in advancement is enormous, and it is the shape of the world. A Stage on a token tells you what somebody’s Crystal can hold; it tells you nothing about whether they ever had to. Everybody who has spent their life in the halls learns to ask the follow-up question, usually with a practice sword.

VI · Why the moment of reorganization decides everything

The mechanism under the sentences above, stated in the vocabulary of the forge rather than the vocabulary of doctrine.

A Threshold is a melt. The Crystal is carried to the point where its lattice stops holding its shape, and it is allowed to cool into a new one, layer upon layer. A melt does not cool into order on its own. Something has to survive the heat for the new lattice to grow around, and in a Soul Crystal that something is Traits.

A Trait is a law the Continuum has already accepted as reliable, and a law does not stop being reliable at temperature. This is the only property in the Crystal with that behavior, and it is why Traits decide the quality of an advancement that strain merely made possible. Strain gets a soul to the melt. Nucleation decides what the melt produces.

The consequences follow without further argument.

A seeded melt cools dense, deliberate and clean. Clarity and Compression come out ahead of where they went in. This is the clean seat.

A contradicted melt cools around seeds that disagree with each other, and the lattice records the disagreement permanently. This is the rough seat, and it is why rough-seated practitioners are unpredictable rather than weak.

An unseeded melt cools amorphous. The Stage does not advance, Clarity and Compression fall, and the weakest Trait commonly dissolves without reforming. The halls call this a Dead Set, a seat that never took. Everyone else calls it slag. Recovery is slow, partial, and the practitioner is usually the last person in the hall to accept that it happened.

This is what a Draft is actually doing. A Draft supplies an artificial seed. It gives the cooling lattice a law to grow around that the soul did not survive into existence, which is why a Draft genuinely improves the quality of an advancement and why the improvement is not the practitioner’s own. In Creature-Sourced Drafts the borrowed law arrives with the identity it was rendered from, and that is Identity Contamination stated as a structural mechanism rather than a warning.

A Draft can supply the floor, a seed and a Catalyst. A rich melt is therefore the cleanest melt money can arrange, and it is cooled around somebody else’s convictions.

VII · Tempering, and what a Draft actually touches

Tempering is the stat of becoming. A high-Tempering practitioner may hold no more power than the next person, and holds it in a cleaner, deeper, structurally sounder container.

The Sub-Stats a Draft plausibly reaches: Clarity (refinement of Essence already held) and Tempering Yield (volume produced per Stage, ambient Aether absorbed rather than passed through, and the gap between generation and availability). This is where a course of Essence Drafts does its daily work. A Salt reaches a third, Compression (how cleanly the Shell works the raw Charge it holds), which is the Body fighter’s half of the same daily work, and Core stock does not touch it.

The Sub-Stats no Draft has ever reached: Coherence, the alignment between a practitioner’s stated Path and their lived behavior, which on the lived road is the structural prerequisite for Threshold eligibility, and Maturity, the degree to which prior trauma has been genuinely integrated, which is the primary Sub-Stat the Crystal consults when evaluating readiness.

Coherence is the Crystal’s internal honest scale, and it cannot be drunk. A breakthrough Draft carries a soul over the Threshold without consulting that scale, and the soul arrives on the far side reading exactly what it read before. That is the mechanical reason a bought Stage opens the door and no more, stated in the vocabulary of the stat framework rather than in the vocabulary of doctrine.
