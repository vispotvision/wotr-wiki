Power in Use

Nov 28, 20261 min read

At Sallow Bridge, Dunstan Pike spent his whole reserve on the first swing, in glorious orange, in front of the betting crowd, and was then beaten by a man eating a pear. These pages are how power is put into the body, stacked, spent, carried in tags and beaten, so that it need not be you.

  • Reinforcement The coat of one’s own aura over the body’s base, what it costs, and when it fails.
  • Stacking and Spending How boosts stack to the cap, how the reserve runs down, how the body tells on it and how the window reports it.
  • Runes and Tags Workings cut into objects ahead of time: who fuels them, who fires them, who cuts them.
  • The Working Trades Drafts, runes, tags, chants and the spoken arts: what each medium can do that the others cannot, what outlives its maker, and what the guilds fight about.
  • Counterplay: What Beats a Practitioner What beats a practitioner, and in what order the decision is made.
