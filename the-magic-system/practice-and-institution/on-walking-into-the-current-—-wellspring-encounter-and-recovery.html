On Walking Into the Current — Wellspring Encounter and Recovery

On Walking Into the Current — Wellspring Encounter and Recovery

Nov 17, 202620 min read

A Field Register of Wellspring Encounter, Survival, and Recovery · Second Edition

Guild of Measurewrights, under the Hexagonal Oath, in concurrence with the Research & Archives Division and the Voyager-Era Survey Corps (Retired)

The current was always running. The question was never whether you would find it. The question was whether you would notice before it found you.

Voyager-era survey maxim, attributed to the third Chainward expedition

Surveyor-Major Osric Tallant of the Chainward service was explaining the correct way to pitch a tent, at Cold Kettle Hollow, in full sun, when a bolt of bottle-green lightning left his right hand in the middle of a sentence and took the top off a pine. He looked at the pine. He told the party it had been a demonstration. By nightfall he had given six more demonstrations, each larger than the last, the scorched trees smoking around the camp in a ring and his coat, which had been a respectable plum for twenty years, gone green to the shoulder, and he was happier than anyone had ever seen him, explaining at the top of his voice that a man at last understood his own gifts. His second, a clever young Magister, went forward to read the source, and that is the last the record has of her. It was the porter who ended it. Wat Nettle had carried the tent poles for nineteen seasons, he had noticed that the Major’s nose was bleeding and that the Major had not, and at dawn he knocked him down with a tent pole, tied him over a mule, and walked him out of the valley by voice, saying out loud at every step what was actually there: that’s a rock, sir, that’s a birch, that’s your own hand. Tallant lost two fingers and kept the harmonization. He mourned that valley for the rest of his life, and he wrote the preface to the first edition of this register, and he never once named the porter.

The Sixty Wellsprings, with their encounter types and density scale, are written for practitioners who understand the system and are approaching concentrations deliberately. Most Wellspring encounters in unmapped territory are not deliberate. They are accidents.

The surveyor steps across a threshold they did not see, and the first indication that they have entered a concentration is the concentration’s effect on their body or their mind or both.

This register is written for the accident.

A warning to the confident reader. Confidence is a Spirit-type early symptom. If you are reading this document and feel certain you would recognize the onset, you are describing exactly the state in which surveyors fail to recognize it.

A note on words, for the new reader. A Wellspring represents an alchemical and a spiritual transformation: it is the governing idea that changes how a soul uses Aether, a lens set on magic already potent, and the scholars’ account of it is sixty different types of Aether that affect the Soul Crystal (the soul itself) differently. A concentration is a place where one of them pools, thick enough to act on whoever is standing in it, and surveyors call the way it moves through such a place the current. Rich ground fills a practitioner faster, and this page is what happens when the ground is richer than you are. Most concentrations of any size have a dungeon around them or will shortly, and monsters move across the whole Inner World, so an encounter in the field rarely stays the only thing happening to you.

A harmonization caught by accident is as real as one got at a school’s rites, out of a Draft, from a blessing or by birth. It starts small, like any of them, and it reaches double only with years of mastery on its home Path, and half again at most away from it. Surveyors who come out convinced they have doubled overnight are running a Spirit-type symptom.

The Onset Profiles

What each encounter type does to the body in the first thirty seconds.

Wild-Type Onset

The body knows first. The feet report a surface that does not match what the eyes see. The weight of the pack shifts. A tool in the belt resonates at a frequency it should not carry. Metal tastes wrong against the teeth. The air pressure changes without altitude change. Sound arrives at the wrong speed, or does not arrive at all from directions where it should.

The Crystal is unaffected. Gnosis reads normally. The Shell is stable. This is the critical diagnostic: if your own internal architecture feels normal and the world feels wrong, the concentration is Wild-type.

Response time is generous. Wild-type onset is perceptible before it is dangerous, because the physical anomalies are legible to the untrained body. You do not need Gnosis training to notice that rocks are floating. You need functioning eyes and the willingness to believe what the eyes report.

The common failure is rationalization. The surveyor sees an anomaly and explains it away, because the human inclination when the ground behaves strangely is to assume the ground is normal and the perception is wrong.

In a Wild-type concentration the perception is correct and the ground is lying. Trust the body.

Stay anyway and the Crystal stops being unaffected. A Wild Wellspring is a thing in the world, and a soul that lingers beside it starts shaping its Essence around that thing, which is how a harmonization is caught here: slowly, with plenty of warning, and with everyone in the party able to see what you are becoming.

Spirit-Type Onset

The Crystal knows first, and the Crystal does not report to the conscious mind at the speed the conscious mind requires.

The first symptom is physiological, and it comes before anything you could call a perception. Thermal change in the hands or face with no external heat source. A sudden emotional shift, grief or elation or rage, that does not connect to any stimulus you can identify. An involuntary discharge, a technique half-forming in the Shell without the practitioner having decided to cast it. Nosebleed. The headache behind the eyes specific to Gnosis overload. The taste of your own Essence in the back of the throat, which most practitioners describe as metallic and which experienced surveyors describe as the taste of the Wellspring itself.

The landscape is unchanged. Trees are trees. Stone is stone. Sky is sky. Nothing in the environment is wrong. The instruments read normal.

You are the instrument that is reading wrong, and you frequently will not recognize this, because the Crystal’s activation feels, from the inside, like your own decision.

This is the critical danger. A Spirit-type concentration makes no attack. It authors.

The practitioner begins producing effects they believe are their own, because the Crystal is the origin of all their effects and the Crystal is the thing being activated.

The fire does not fall from the sky. The fire comes from the practitioner’s own Shell, and the practitioner, feeling the fire leave their body, concludes that they chose to produce it. A spiritual Wellspring has no fire to give. What leaves the Shell is the person’s own Essence, shaped by whatever they have already tempered on and driven out by a soul that is being changed.

Response time is narrow. The window between onset and commitment (the point at which the Crystal has been retuned enough that you can no longer distinguish your native architecture from the concentration’s influence) is measured in seconds at Active Concentration and is functionally zero at Veil-Thin and above.

The surveyor who hesitates during a Spirit-type onset to confirm their reading is the surveyor who has already been retuned.

The common failure is self-trust. By the time they suspect the effects are not their own, the effects have been running long enough that the distinction is no longer available to them. The Crystal that would make the distinction is the Crystal that has been compromised.

Fate-Type Onset

Nothing reports at all. The landscape looks normal and the instruments read normal. The feet agree with the eyes, Gnosis reads clean, the hands are the temperature they were, and there is no taste in the throat. The first thirty seconds of a Fate-type encounter are the most comfortable thirty seconds in this register.

What changes is how the week goes. Luck goes strange: a mule that has never gone lame goes lame on the one morning it matters, and the same man draws the short straw three times running. Oaths bite: a cook who swore at breakfast he would sooner lose a thumb than carry that kettle another league is short a thumb by the following noon, and nobody can quite say how. Strangers keep meeting: the peddler on the ridge turns out to be owed money by the guide’s brother, and the woman at the next ford knew the peddler. And the surveyor’s plans keep working out, in ways the surveyor did not plan.

Neither the body nor the Crystal’s working layers have anything to tell you, because this current works on the Attraction Layer, the part of a soul that holds its bonds, oaths, claims and odds.

The critical diagnostic is repetition. If your body feels normal, your Crystal feels normal, the world looks normal and you are on your third coincidence of the day, the concentration is Fate-type.

A Fate-type concentration makes no attack and authors nothing. It collects.

Response time is long and useless. You have days, sometimes weeks, and almost nobody spends them, because a strange run of luck is a story you tell at dinner and no one has ever withdrawn a survey over a good story.

The common failure is the lucky streak. The surveyor whose plans keep coming off is the last person in the party who wants to leave, and the debts come due after they have made camp on the strength of it. Stay anyway and what you carry out will not show as a new color in the Shell. It will show as a bond you did not have when you walked in, to someone you have not met yet.

Medium-Type Onset

Both report simultaneously, and neither report is trustworthy.

The stream is flowing the wrong direction and childhood memories are surfacing uninvited. The light is bending around a clearing and Gnosis is suddenly reading things it has never been able to read. The air pressure has dropped and the emotional state has shifted.

Both the environmental changes and the internal changes are real, and you cannot determine which is causing which, because both are being driven by one current that crosses the Veil threshold every cycle.

The stream flows wrong because the current is altering local law. The memories surface because the same current is activating the Crystal’s Anamnesis architecture.

Both are symptoms of one cause operating on two planes, and you have no instrument that reads both planes at once unless you are a Ketsumyōgan bearer or a Transference-trained Bifold Perception specialist.

A Medium current is at home on the Plane of Fate as well, so there is often a third report coming, days late, in the form described above.

Response time is the worst of the four. The body says the world is wrong. The Crystal says the body is wrong. Both are correct. The surveyor inside a Medium-type concentration at Active density has lost their reference frame, and restoring it requires an external observer standing outside the effect radius.

The common failure is confusion mistaken for illness. They sit down. They wait for the symptoms to pass. The symptoms do not pass, because they are harmonization in progress and no illness at all, and sitting down inside the concentration gives the current more time to complete the process.

The six Medium grounds

A Medium site runs one law, and that law works on the ground and on the delver’s Crystal along the same line. A site never mixes two. Each of the six reads this way:

The cost of standing in one is the Medium row of the Tier Ladders. The way out is the one above: an observer or an anchor outside the radius, which the law cannot reach, gives the delver a fixed point to read against.

The Exposure Curve

What happens after the first thirty seconds. You have not withdrawn. You are still inside.

Active Concentration · involuntary harmonization. See the encounter types and density scale in The Sixty Wellsprings for compatible and incompatible outcomes.

Veil-Thin Nexus · planar bleed.

  • Bifold Perception onset: the Soul Plane perceived simultaneously with the Physical. You have not been trained for this. Objects appear as structure and soul-signature at once; people as body and Crystal at once. The thinning is not selective, and Gnosis is not built for the volume.
  • Involuntary Soul Drift: your position on the Soul Plane dissociates from your position on the Physical. Practitioners describe it as being in two places at once while knowing that both places are real.
  • Echo Beast manifestation: the concentration’s consequence, rather than its product. Unprocessed grief, rage and trauma given temporary autonomous form by the inter-planar conditions the concentration has created.

Wellspring Core · law replacement.

A warning about the word. In common speech any worked site is a Core, meaning a dungeon somebody cleared and now holds, and porters will tell you cheerfully that they have sat beside a dozen. On this register a Wellspring Core means the innermost and densest class of concentration, and the two usages have killed people who heard the wrong one.

Everything inside the Core obeys the Wellspring’s law, including the surveyor, and their previous architecture persists only as a structural memory that the Core’s law is not interested in consulting.

Every surveyor recovered from Core exposure describes it identically: they felt no change happening to them. They felt like they had always been the thing the Core was making them.

The change was experienced as truth. The recovery, paradoxically, is experienced as loss.

Recovery Protocols

Wild-type · Withdraw. The body is reliable. Walk in the direction that feels most normal; anomalies decrease with distance from the core. When the ground behaves correctly and the tools stop resonating, you are out. Then run a standard Wellspring inventory: if a new harmonization has appeared, it was acquired during exposure.

Spirit-type · Withdrawal requires external assistance. The Crystal that would tell you which direction is safe is the Crystal that has been retuned. A Spirit-exposed surveyor self-navigating is navigating with a compass that has been re-magnetized.

The external observer talks the exposed surveyor out by voice, confirming each step against observable reality.

With no observer available: move in the direction that feels least comfortable, because the concentration produces comfort toward the core and discomfort toward the exit. This is unreliable. It is better than the alternative, which is staying.

Post-exit assessment requires a Gnosis specialist. The self-report cannot be trusted, because the Crystal that would produce the self-report has been altered. An external reader performs a full inventory and compares against the pre-deployment baseline.

Fate-type · Withdraw, and do not take leaving for being out. Distance ends the pull and does nothing about what has already been bound. Leave by the route that has been going badly, since the current favors the road toward its center. On the way out swear nothing, promise nothing and accept no gift, favor or courtesy you would have to repay.

The external reader works from the log and the baseline, and the question is simple: what does this person owe, and whom, that they did not owe at the gate? Anything on the list that did not come in with them came out of the ground.

A bond caught this way is a real bond, and it does not lift because the Guild has filed it. Some are paid off, some are outlived, and a few run on after the surveyor stops believing in them. This is normal. It is also why the older surveyors do not take lifts from strangers for a year afterward.

Medium-type · Treat as Spirit-type until proven otherwise. The unique complication is memory contamination: Phreatis or Anamnesis content can introduce memory material the surveyor experiences as their own. Post-exit they may report memories of events that did not happen to them, or fail to report events that did. Disentangling requires a Mnemata specialist and is frequently incomplete.

Core · Transference-assisted re-anchoring by a specialist outside the radius. Requires Stage IX or above; no field surveyor carries the training.

What the field surveyor needs to know: it works, and it is slow. The surveyor who comes back will describe it as losing something they valued. What they valued was the Core’s law, which felt, while they were inside it, more true than their own identity. They will mourn the law. This is normal. It is also temporary, provided the re-anchoring was performed competently.

The Unawakened in the Field

Everything above assumes the reader has a woken Crystal. Nine people in ten standing inside a concentration do not.

A survey party is a practitioner or two and eight other people. Porters, guides, mule-handlers, the cook. Settlements sit downwind of concentrations for generations without anyone in them ever being screened. A current does not check for a Crystal before it acts, and the register has been written for a decade as though it did.

An unwoken soul’s Shell is sealed and cannot phase-lock, so no harmonization occurs. This is the whole of the good news. The Essence Core is present, furnished, and fully exposed.

Wild-type. Identical in every respect, and the unawakened are frequently better at it. The body is the instrument, the instrument is unimpaired, and a porter has no Gnosis reading to argue with when the ground starts lying. Experienced survey leads have known for years that the man who says this is wrong first is usually the one carrying the tent poles, and the published curriculum does not mention it.

Spirit-type. The onset is physiological and it arrives on schedule. Thermal change in the hands, an emotional shift with no stimulus behind it, nosebleed, the taste in the back of the throat. What does not arrive is the involuntary discharge, because the sealed Shell cannot express it.

This is worse, and in one specific way. A practitioner in a Spirit-type onset vents. The load leaves through the Shell as a half-formed working, badly, expensively, and it leaves.

An unawakened soul has no exit. The current drives the Essence Core and the Essence Core has nowhere to put what it is being given. The load stays, and it goes on staying after the party has withdrawn and made camp and gone home.

The recorded presentation is a porter who was cheerful on the outbound leg and is never cheerful again. It reads as ordinary hardship and it is filed as ordinary hardship.

Fate-type. Identical, and this is the one current a sealed Shell does nothing about. Every soul has an Attraction Layer, the unwoken included, and a porter’s oaths are as good as a Magister’s. Nobody in the party will connect the thumb to the kettle. A village on Fate ground is a place with a reputation: marriages that arrange themselves, a family whose debts always find them, and a market where nobody has gone back on a handshake in living memory because of what happened to the last man.

Medium-type. Fully applicable and fully undocumented. Anamnesis and Phreatis content acts on the Essence Core, and the Essence Core is what an unawakened soul has. Memory contamination in an unawakened bearer is indistinguishable from confabulation to every physician who will ever examine them.

Long exposure. Residence at Active Concentration or above, sustained over seasons rather than hours, produces a Trait by Wellspring Convergence. It is real, it is lawful, it is heritable, and it is in a body nobody will screen. Villages downwind of a concentration produce a family that has been strange for six generations, and the strangeness is a Wellspring’s law running quietly in people who have no word for any of this.

Recovery. There is none written. Transference-assisted re-anchoring assumes a Shell to anchor. No protocol exists for an unawakened soul returned from a Wellspring Core, and the Guild has never commissioned one, because the recovery ledger records practitioners and the practitioners are the ones the Guild is liable for.

The hole in Protocol IV. Pre-deployment baselines are taken for team members with woken Crystals. Nobody has ever taken one for a porter.

Which means that for the entire Voyager Era there has been no instrument, anywhere, capable of establishing what a survey did to the people carrying it. Nobody conspired at this. It is a filing category that was never opened, and it has been doing the work of a conspiracy for a hundred and forty years.

Standing Field Protocols

#Protocol
IAssume Spirit-type until proven otherwise. The cost of treating a Wild site as Spirit is unnecessary caution. The cost of treating a Spirit site as Wild is a surveyor whose Crystal has been forcibly rewritten before they identified the perimeter
IIMonitor the surveyor, not the landscape. A surveyor trained to watch the ground will miss Spirit-type and Medium-type onset. A surveyor trained to watch their own body will not. Neither of them will catch a Fate-type onset, which is what Protocol IX is for
IIIDeploy in pairs with one member outside the estimated effect radius. The interior surveyor reports what they experience. The observer confirms or contradicts. The contradiction is the diagnosis
IVCarry pre-deployment Crystal baselines for every team member. Without a baseline the assessor cannot distinguish native architecture from the concentration’s additions. Taken before entering unmapped territory, not after the first day’s march
VDo not carry Object Wellsprings into Site Wellsprings. A surveyor wearing a forged ward and stepping into an Active Concentration is asking two Wellspring laws to share a Shell that was built for one
VIWithdrawal is no retreat. Marking the perimeter from safe distance completes the survey. The Guild has buried enough surveyors who decided that the interior documentation could not wait
VIIReport Medium-type sites as priority. A surveyor who identifies one and reports location, density and dominant Wellspring has done more for the survey than a surveyor who maps the interior of a Wild-type site that was going to be found anyway
VIIIWatch the unawakened members of the party. They will not report onset, because they have no vocabulary for it and no reason to think it applies to them. A porter who stops talking is a reading. Baseline them before departure by any means available, even if it is only a note on how they were
IXKeep a dated written log on every site that reads normal: who was met, what was sworn, what fell out well and what fell out badly. A Fate-type site reads as nothing on every instrument and reads plainly on paper. Three coincidences in a day is a reading
XSwear nothing on ground that has not been typed: no oaths, no wagers, no promises, no courtesies that could be taken for one. The oath sworn lightly on Fate-type ground is the one that holds
