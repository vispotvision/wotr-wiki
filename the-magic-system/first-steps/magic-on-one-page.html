Magic on One Page

Nov 17, 20267 min read

For a guest who has just sat down at the table and has five minutes. Every bold word has a page of its own when you want it.

“Three roads, one purse, and the bill always comes.”

What the ferrymen at Aske tell anyone who asks how a soul fights.

On the night the Cooper’s Arms at Harl Cross burned, three people walked out of it whom nobody in the yard had met. The first came through the wall, steam pouring off her shoulders and the bricks glowing where her hands had been. The second came out of the door, cold as a cellar, and the colors of the lanterns went flat as she passed. The third simply walked out after them with soot on his boots and nothing on him at all, and the burning roof beam that should have taken his head off chose that moment to fall the other way. Everyone in the yard could tell you which one was the dangerous one. They all named a different person. This page is why.

Aether

Aether is what foreigners call mana: the stuff the whole world is made of, left over from before the world was split into its planes. It lies thick in some places and thin in others, and only a living body can pull it in. Nobody, at any Stage, works Aether out in the world without drawing it into themselves first. Where you stand matters, because rich ground fills you faster.

The Soul Crystal and its three layers

Your soul is the Soul Crystal, not a metaphor for it. It has three layers, one to each plane of the world, and everyone has all three:

  • the Aether Shell, on the outside, the only door your soul has into matter;
  • the Essence Core, in the middle, the seat of who you are, your memory and your will;
  • the Attraction Layer, outermost and strangest, your own small share of Fate.

The Core decides everything you do. The layer it goes out through decides what it can touch.

Three Paths

Every soul leans on one layer, and that lean is its Path. Each Path spends something different and pays a different bill.

So the woman through the wall was Body, the woman out of the door was Spirit, and the man the roof missed was Fate. Fighters call the styles Aether, Essence and Thread Manipulation. A Spirit worker cannot move a stone except through her own Shell. A Body fighter cannot touch your soul. A Fate worker cannot win a straight exchange, which is why he never offers one. No Path is better than the others; each beats one and loses to another, and people who can afford the decades walk two.

Wellsprings

A Wellspring is the idea a soul tunes itself to, and it gives a fighter’s power its shape, color, sound and smell. There are exactly sixty. Fire is one; so are frost, debt, sleep and mercy. Any Path can use any Wellspring, and it does the same idea to a different target: fire on the Body Path burns wood, on the Spirit Path it burns a grudge, and on the Fate Path it burns the chances a thing had. Magic here is alchemy worked on yourself: the idea changes the one who works it, and the world gives way as far as that soul has truly changed, so what burns in the crucible burns in the one who tends it.

Stages, Levels and the climb

A soul climbs sixteen Stages, from Murmuring at the bottom to Apex at the top, in five Bands and the Apotheosis Approach beyond them. Level rises with danger overcome. A Stage sets how high everything else can go, and each crossing melts the Crystal and rebuilds it, the layer you lean on first and hardest, so your lead layer always climbs ahead of the other two. What a Stage asks of you to cross it comes in that layer’s terms: a Body soul is tried in the flesh, a Spirit soul in a grief or a truth, a Fate soul in a vow or the odds. A Release (your one idea transforming your body) can be found from Stages IV, VII and IX, and it has to be found and mastered by fighting. A Domain (the world around you obeying it) can stand from Stage VIII. The Soul Crystal grows as you climb, from Seed in the unwoken through Clouded, Faceted, Clear, Fire, Flawless and Starred to Absolute, and you can see it in the aura: smoky low down, clear in the middle, bending light at the top. Stages can be bought with money, in a Draft poured for your road. A bought Stage opens the door and nothing more.

Stats and windows

Your soul reports its numbers to you, and only you, in windows: plain lines that show up after a blow, a gain or a wound.

[Charge: 41% ▓▓▓▓░░░░░░]
[Wound: two ribs cracked, left. No full breath, no hard twist.]
[Skill: Staff, D to C]

A sheet shows Eight Primaries with letter Grades, F at the bottom and S and above at the top, and the Sub-Stats under them when they move. Skills rise with use and anyone can have them: a baker has Baking. Traits are standing laws of your soul and have their own levels. A contest is decided by the Grade of the stat in use, then by who read the other better, then by what each has left to spend, then by the ground.

The three ladders

From Stage VI, Glory, an assay reads three class ladders, one per layer, from Ø to Ω: Aether Class for the Shell, Essence Class for the Core and Thread Class for the Layer. Each says how cleanly power goes out through that layer, so how much of what you spend arrives and how much is lost. Most people read Ø on Thread their whole lives. At the same time the soul offers you a Class, a name that fits how you have lived, with a small trick of its own. The offer comes through the layer you lean on, so it usually sounds like your Path.

Gear

Plain gear is what it is. Worked gear (runed, Draft-tempered or cut from monsters) multiplies your Sub-Stats, a helm at Perception ×1.15, and costs a little reserve to keep lit. All your gear together caps any one Sub-Stat at ×1.5, and none of it lifts you past your Stage. So money makes a strong fighter terrifying and a weak one only better dressed. Everyone looks at your kit, and everyone says what it is worth.

What it all costs

Power is finite and the spending shows. At half your reserve your breath shortens; at a quarter your hands shake; at a tenth your aura flickers and your Trait stutters. Below a tenth is what the halls call Starvation. On top of that each Path tires its own way, and a trained eye reads your Path from how you flag. Wounds stay until someone treats them, soul wounds included, and the rich pay to walk off what cripples the poor. The dead stay dead.
