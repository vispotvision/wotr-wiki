First Steps

Nov 17, 20263 min read

At the Lammas fair in Harrow Cross a goose-plucker named Tib Oakshott climbed onto a cider barrel and bet a fairground brawler his whole year’s feathers that he could name every part of the man’s art out loud, blow by blow, while it was being thrown at him. He lost a tooth and most of one ear and won four sacks of silver, and the Guild has been writing to him ever since. I cannot promise you the silver. I can promise you the names, and the numbers your own soul will show you in a window (its report to you, in your own tongue, which nobody else can see).

The newcomer’s road. Read these five first, in this order, and everything after them will make sense. This shelf opens the one reading order on The Magic System page, and Start Here keeps the short routes.

  • Magic on One Page The whole system on one screen, for a guest with five minutes: Aether, the Crystal’s three layers, the three Paths and what each spends, Wellsprings, Stages, stats and windows, the ladders and gear.
  • How Magic Works The whole system in brief, for a newcomer: the soul and its windows, the three Paths and what each one spends (Charge, Essence or Thread), Wellsprings as lenses, Traits and skills with their levels, classes and the three ladders, Stages for sale, monster cores and dungeons, guild titles, gear, Fate and blessings.
  • The Measures at a Glance Every number a practitioner, a monster or a piece of gear is read by, what it decides, and one fighter read on all of them, window included.
  • One Exchange, Annotated A single exchange between two fighters, with every rule that decided it marked and each fighter’s windows shown as a worked example.
  • The Core Vocabulary The words. The first three pages explain each one as it comes; this is where they are kept, and it stays open beside everything after.

Five pages from the next shelves answer the questions a newcomer asks first, usually the morning after a first bout. Take them as the need comes.

  • The Three Paths What each road works, where it is held, what the lost share becomes and what it costs, and how to tell a hitter from a thrower by the way they tire.
  • Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell Its section on the three routes, Charge, Essence and Thread, and why a soul still has one reserve and one number.
  • Soul Wounds and the Menders Why your armor did not help, what you have lost, and what it costs to have it closed.
  • The Tell and the Knot How to see a pull on a thread coming, how long you have, and what the world takes back from the puller afterward.
  • Releases and Domains by Path What a Release and a Domain do on each of the three roads.
