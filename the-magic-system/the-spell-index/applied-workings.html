Abyssal Sway

Applied · [Ab] Abyss · Flourishing and above

WellspringAbyntheus (Spirit)
Second WellspringDissolution
OperationSublimation
TemperanceFlourishing (Adept) and above

Effect. Deep-pressure column that equalizes incoming force.

How it works.

  • The idea. Vastness. The great scale read off the small vessel, a mortal soul stretched to take in things far too large for it. Dissolution’s idea rides with it (unbinding, what no longer serves is allowed to come apart on its own, and keeping anything at all becomes the work).
  • The operation. Sublimation. The small soul is raised toward the great scale and brought back down still holding it.
  • In the worker. Held to Flourishing or past, the idea is set as law by [Ab] Abyss, and the world gives way that far: deep-pressure column that equalizes incoming force.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus overpressure rebounds.
  • Flaw. The small vessel can hold the great scale only while it still knows it is small. Stretched too long between the near end and the far one, the soul loses its sense of how the two fit together, and what comes back to it is noise the practitioner is nonetheless convinced is a signal.
  • Counter. [RenB] Boundary refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Coagulatio.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Very-long-wavelength propagation and interferometry.

Cost and backlash. Overpressure rebounds

Counterplay. [RenB] Boundary
