Root Invocations

Root Invocations

Nov 17, 202630 min read

The fourteen root invocations, one to each Archon: the working an Archon’s own law makes when it is called by its glyphs.

Part of The Spell Index. 14 entries. Each gives the working’s glyphs and least Temperance, its Wellspring and operation, what sets it off and what it does, how it works by its idea, and what it costs and what answers it.

Each Archon’s law and court are on The Fourteen Archons pages and in the Archon Register.

Covenant of the Instant

Root Invocation · [VaM] Moment + [Cro] Cycle · Refraction and above

WellspringAnamnesis (Medium)
Second WellspringLethegraal
OperationSeparation
TemperanceRefraction and above

Trigger. Requires a genuinely singular moment — a decision point, an impact, a single spoken word — not a duration.

Effect. Isolates a single instant from causal drift, letting the caster act inside a held moment while the surrounding world continues at its normal pace.

How it works.

  • The idea. Remembrance. Everything keeps its past, and a soul under this lens can touch a thing and read it. Lethegraal’s idea rides with it (the cup, time’s mercy hurried along: old pain settles on its own schedule, and no hand chooses what falls).
  • The operation. Separation. The past a thing has kept is parted from it and read, as the residue is parted from the vessel that held it.
  • In the worker. Held to Refraction or past, the idea is set as law by [VaM] Moment + [Cro] Cycle, and the world gives way that far: isolates a single instant from causal drift, letting the caster act inside a held moment while the surrounding world continues at its normal pace.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus the caster’s own metabolism and Aether Shell still run at pocket-time, meaning three seconds outside can cost 24-36 seconds of internal fatigue.
  • Flaw. Everything keeps its past, and the reading becomes part of that past the moment it is made. A careless hand laid on the record writes itself over what it came to read, so a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of taking it.
  • Counter. [Uur] Disruption refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Letheveil or Sublimatio.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Remanent magnetization and thermoluminescent dosimetry.

In numbers. Subjective time inside the pocket runs roughly 8-12x normal; duration caps at three real-time seconds regardless of Temperance.

The target. Everything outside the pocket sees no time pass at all: to anyone watching, the caster simply didn’t move.

Cost and backlash. The caster’s own metabolism and Aether Shell still run at pocket-time, meaning three seconds outside can cost 24-36 seconds of internal fatigue.

Counterplay. [Uur] Disruption refuses the held moment its claim from outside, snapping the caster back to normal time mid-action, often mid-motion, which is dangerous.

Last Rites of Uurgath

Root Invocation · [Uur] Disruption + [Vorn] End · Dissonance and above

WellspringNihiloth (Wild)
Second WellspringDissolution
OperationDissolution
TemperanceDissonance and above

Trigger. Target structure/curse/ward must already be past its functional lifespan — the glyph will not act on something still load-bearing.

Effect. Grants a structure, ward, or curse lawful permission to complete its own decay in a single stroke rather than lingering; the target’s Essence is left raw and ready for creation anew.

How it works.

  • The idea. The hollow law. Power by negation: the rule itself unmade, and what stands in the gap. Dissolution’s idea rides with it (unbinding, what no longer serves is allowed to come apart on its own, and keeping anything at all becomes the work).
  • The operation. Dissolution. The rule itself is loosed until nothing of it is left, and the gap is left standing where it was.
  • In the worker. Held to Dissonance or past, the idea is set as law by [Uur] Disruption + [Vorn] End, and the world gives way that far: grants a structure, ward, or curse lawful permission to complete its own decay in a single stroke rather than lingering; the target’s Essence is left raw and ready for creation anew.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus if no clean target remains when the barrier drops, the unmaking still has to finish on something, and it finishes on the caster’s own Aether Shell.
  • Flaw. A rule unmade is unmade for everyone, and the idea has no way to spare its maker. Everything the practitioner brought inside is subject to the same gap, including the parts of themselves they were relying on.
  • Counter. [El] Life refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Eidolyn, Oblivara or Monolithion.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Destructive interference and the null field.

In numbers. Collapse completes in under two seconds regardless of the structure’s original size, since the barrier removal is binary, not gradual.

The target. The target doesn’t fight the collapse; per Uurgath’s law it recognizes its own decay as true and stops resisting it structurally.

Cost and backlash. If no clean target remains when the barrier drops, the unmaking still has to finish on something, and it finishes on the caster’s own Aether Shell.

Counterplay. Restoring the missing activation barrier (a Thalen-glyph reinforcement, or simply propping the structure with fresh materials) before the glyph completes stops the cascade.

The Chord That Remembers Its Shape

Root Invocation · [Se] Song + [Ar] Accord · Invocation and above

WellspringJudicium (Wild)
Second WellspringMirithane
OperationSeparation
TemperanceInvocation and above

Trigger. Requires at least three other souls already producing audible or Essence-resonant output in the same field.

Effect. Synchronizes the Essence frequencies of every soul in a region into a single unified chord, stabilizing a Domain’s tone directly through Selhar’s own law.

How it works.

  • The idea. Truth told apart. Discernment that knows what a thing is made of, and whether it is lying, from the mark it cannot help leaving. Mirithane’s idea rides with it (the honest mirror, the inside and the outside made one surface).
  • The operation. Separation. A thing is parted from what it claims to be, the true told apart from the false by the mark it cannot help leaving.
  • In the worker. Held to Invocation or past, the idea is set as law by [Se] Song + [Ar] Accord, and the world gives way that far: Synchronizes the Essence frequencies of every soul in a region into a single unified chord, stabilizing a Domain’s tone directly through Selhar’s own law.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus forcing synchronization on unwilling participants produces measurable resistance-feedback into the caster, proportional to how many refuse.
  • Flaw. Discernment tells everything apart and cannot choose to leave anything untold. Off guard, every object in view reports what it is made of at once, and the reader loses the power to put any of it first. Pushed past the point where a reading only reads, telling a thing apart becomes taking it apart: the read starts to pass judgment, which can turn away force or at the very top end a life, and since it has no way to look at one thing only, it takes the reader’s own memory in the same stroke.
  • Counter. [Uur] Disruption refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Tenebra or Eidolyn.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Emission and absorption spectroscopy.

In numbers. Full lock achieves within roughly ten seconds for a small group, longer for larger fields; Selhar’s own Rite of Choral Concord reportedly took hours across three full Domains.

The target. Synchronized participants report a felt sense of unified purpose and reduced internal conflict for the working’s duration.

Cost and backlash. Forcing synchronization on unwilling participants produces measurable resistance-feedback into the caster, proportional to how many refuse.

Counterplay. [Uur] Disruption introduces a deliberately unmatched frequency into the field, and because the lock is only as strong as its weakest coupling, one true dissonance can decouple the whole chord.

The Heartflare Given Shape

Root Invocation · [Ve] Desire + [Asa] Creation · Invocation and above

WellspringIgnivale (Wild)
Second WellspringExuroth
OperationCalcination
TemperanceInvocation and above

Trigger. Requires an unfulfilled want the caster has carried long enough to have a stable emotional ‘shape’ — a fresh whim won’t work.

Effect. Brings into brief physical existence something that has never been, shaped entirely by the intensity of the caster’s longing.

How it works.

  • The idea. The spark. The smallest beginning that goes on to pay for itself, so that the gap between wanting a thing and doing it closes to almost nothing. Exuroth’s idea rides with it (the trial by fire, power comes only through ordeal, plunged and reheated and plunged again, until the soul holds a hardness no gentle road could have given it).
  • The operation. Calcination. One point of fire is set to the work at the first degree, and the burning is left to pay its own way.
  • In the worker. Held to Invocation or past, the idea is set as law by [Ve] Desire + [Asa] Creation, and the world gives way that far: brings into brief physical existence something that has never been, shaped entirely by the intensity of the caster’s longing.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus the created thing remains emotionally ‘hooked’ to the caster; distance or neglect causes visible caster distress, like a phantom-limb sensation.
  • Flaw. With the gap between wanting and doing closed, the soul has no moment left in which to decide. Any stray feeling in the room is beginning enough, and the working fires without the practitioner having decided to fire it.
  • Counter. [Ur] Balance refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Pyraeon or Hypnather.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Minimum ignition energy and the activation barrier.

In numbers. Object stability lasts roughly one hour per point of sustained emotional intensity.

The target. The created object/effect behaves exactly as the caster envisioned it, down to fine detail the caster didn’t consciously specify but unconsciously wanted.

Cost and backlash. The created thing remains emotionally ‘hooked’ to the caster; distance or neglect causes visible caster distress, like a phantom-limb sensation.

Counterplay. [Ur] Balance sets an equal and opposite want beside it, and two wants cannot both take shape in one place, so one usually unmakes the other or both fail.

The Hexagonal Oath

Root Invocation · [Th] Foundation + [En] Lattice · Invocation and above

WellspringPetralon (Wild)
Second WellspringMateria Primordia
OperationCoagulation
TemperanceInvocation and above

Trigger. Caster must trace the full six-sided boundary of the intended Domain by hand or by marked stake before invoking.

Effect. Declares the fundamental geometry of a Domain — fixing which angles, proportions, and forms may take root within it for the duration of the working.

How it works.

  • The idea. The foundation. To become what other things stand on: load taken, carried and sent down, at the price of slowly settling. Breaking, holding and rising are one law seen from three positions, and this lens treats a soul as a loaded structure, asking the only question a master mason ever asks: where the load goes, and what happens at the place it concentrates. Materia Primordia’s idea rides with it (the first substance, one self and many forms, and a soul that finds it can become its own opposite and still be itself).
  • The operation. Coagulation. The soul is fixed as the foundation, set to bear and never to rise.
  • In the worker. Held to Invocation or past, the idea is set as law by [Th] Foundation + [En] Lattice, and the world gives way that far: declares the fundamental geometry of a Domain — fixing which angles, proportions, and forms may take root within it for the duration of the working.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus the caster’s own body must hold the geometric template in mind continuously during the initial trace; a lapse mid-trace corrupts the whole boundary.
  • Flaw. A foundation bears whatever presses down on it, and nothing ever asked it to hold on. Pull on it, the one load it does not answer, and it does not give gracefully. It parts.
  • Counter. [Uur] Disruption refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Cymorath or Abython.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Compressive load path and creep.

In numbers. Holds indefinitely once set, but only within the traced boundary — extending the boundary later requires re-tracing and re-casting.

The target. Anything built or altered inside the Domain that violates the fixed geometry meets immediate, hard mechanical resistance, as if pushing against solid stone.

Cost and backlash. The caster’s own body must hold the geometric template in mind continuously during the initial trace; a lapse mid-trace corrupts the whole boundary.

Counterplay. [Uur] Disruption introduced at a boundary seam (a corner, a gap in the original trace) unravels the constraint from that single weak point outward.

The Mask That Loves You Back

Root Invocation · [Ka] Dream + [Vl] Veil · Invocation and above

WellspringOneirion (Spirit)
Second WellspringSomnalis
OperationSublimation
TemperanceInvocation and above

Trigger. Requires physical proximity to a sleeping or meditating subject, or the caster’s own trance state for a solo dreamscape.

Effect. Constructs a habitable dreamscape following the grammar of the subconscious rather than waking law — convincing enough that an observer’s own heart confirms it as real.

How it works.

  • The idea. Dream as a place. Work done in the space of what can be thought, reaching a soul where no line of sight runs, because no soul was ever in the room. Somnalis’s idea rides with it (living among what might be, the near possibilities of the Plane of Fate held in view at once, each with its odds on it, and a soul at ease in the not knowing).
  • The operation. Sublimation. The working is lifted off every bench into the space of what can be thought, and comes down again in a soul.
  • In the worker. Held to Invocation or past, the idea is set as law by [Ka] Dream + [Vl] Veil, and the world gives way that far: constructs a habitable dreamscape following the grammar of the subconscious rather than waking law — convincing enough that an observer’s own heart confirms it as real.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus the dreamscape draws its ‘realism’ partly from the caster’s own subconscious material, meaning the caster’s private fears/desires can leak into scenes they didn’t intend to build.
  • Flaw. A dream is a place with no ground under it, and nothing done there knows where it is in a room. A working that was true in the space of what can be thought lands nowhere in particular when it has to come down into a real one.
  • Counter. [Ie] Perception refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Mirithane or Chthonica.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Configuration space.

In numbers. Dreamscape stability holds as long as the caster maintains active attention; unattended sections begin degrading into incoherence within minutes.

The target. Occupants perceive the dreamscape as fully real, including pain, temperature, and consequence, until and unless something breaks the illusion’s internal consistency.

Cost and backlash. The dreamscape draws its ‘realism’ partly from the caster’s own subconscious material, meaning the caster’s private fears/desires can leak into scenes they didn’t intend to build.

Counterplay. [Ie] Perception lets an occupant compare the dreamscape against a remembered true sensation, and that single inconsistency unravels the whole construct outward from the point of doubt.

The Prism That Does Not Blink

Root Invocation · [Ie] Perception + [Vei] Depth · Invocation and above

WellspringLuminalis (Medium)
Second WellspringOneirion
OperationSeparation
TemperanceInvocation and above

Trigger. Caster must already know the immediate act whose consequences they want traced — the glyph traces forward and backward from a known point, it can’t search blindly.

Effect. Reveals the full causal chain connecting an act to its ultimate consequence, read directly rather than inferred.

How it works.

  • The idea. The hidden light. Seeing what hides and hiding what shows, one act run in two directions. Oneirion’s idea rides with it (dream as a place, work done in the space of what can be thought, reaching a soul where no line of sight runs, because no soul was ever in the room).
  • The operation. Separation. What is seen is parted from what is there, so the hidden can be drawn into view and the shown put out of it.
  • In the worker. Held to Invocation or past, the idea is set as law by [Ie] Perception + [Vei] Depth, and the world gives way that far: reveals the full causal chain connecting an act to its ultimate consequence, read directly rather than inferred.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus perceiving a full causal chain costs proportional cognitive load; casters routinely report the revealed information as unshakeable once seen, a genuine memory burden going forward.
  • Flaw. Seeing and hiding are one act run two ways, and a soul that keeps both running at once begins to run two ways itself. Its own output splits along the seam between what it shows and what it hides, the two halves going out at different paces, and complex workings arrive out of step with themselves.
  • Counter. [Vl] Veil refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Penanceflare or Mirithane.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. The non-visible spectrum and polarization.

In numbers. Chain length revealed scales with Stage; Invocation-tier casters see roughly a season’s worth of downstream consequence, higher Stages see years.

The target. The revealed chain appears as an overlaid, navigable structure in the caster’s perception — not a vision, a legible diagram.

Cost and backlash. Perceiving a full causal chain costs proportional cognitive load; casters routinely report the revealed information as unshakeable once seen, a genuine memory burden going forward.

Counterplay. [Vl] Veil doesn’t block the trace, it multiplies false junctions into the diagram, making the true chain indistinguishable from decoys.

The Still Gate’s Mercy

Root Invocation · [Au] Death + [Vor] Return · Transcendence and above

WellspringMortalis (Medium)
Second WellspringSomnalis
OperationDissolution
TemperanceTranscendence and above

Trigger. Subject must be actively dying, with the passage already beginning; the glyph opens a threshold, it can’t create death.

Effect. Opens a lawful, brief threshold at the Still Gate, letting a dying soul pass without becoming a wandering Echo.

How it works.

  • The idea. The black gate. Ending looked at squarely and understood as a passage with its own law, by a soul that has stopped fearing it and has started, a little, to cool. Somnalis’s idea rides with it (living among what might be, the near possibilities of the Plane of Fate held in view at once, each with its odds on it, and a soul at ease in the not knowing).
  • The operation. Dissolution. A thing’s hold on what it is gets loosed, lawfully and in order, until it comes apart at its proper end.
  • In the worker. Held to Transcendence or past, the idea is set as law by [Au] Death + [Vor] Return, and the world gives way that far: opens a lawful, brief threshold at the Still Gate, letting a dying soul pass without becoming a wandering Echo.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus the caster absorbs a fraction of the passage’s grief-charge directly into their own Attraction Layer, a real emotional cost that compounds with repeated use.
  • Flaw. A soul that has stopped fearing the gate has also stopped holding out against it, and the fear was part of what kept it on this side. Stay near the ending long enough and the worker drifts toward it, cooling a little at a time without noticing.
  • Counter. [Wy] Motion refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Terranova, Anima Spirare or Verdantia.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. The non-equilibrium steady state, and the thermodynamic boundary of its collapse.

In numbers. Threshold stays open for roughly ninety seconds — enough for a natural death’s actual biological passage window.

The target. The passing soul experiences the transition as gentle and complete rather than abrupt or contested.

Cost and backlash. The caster absorbs a fraction of the passage’s grief-charge directly into their own Attraction Layer, a real emotional cost that compounds with repeated use.

Counterplay. [Wy] Motion agitates the threshold before it seals, risking an incomplete passage that leaves a partial Echo behind.

The Storm That Remembers Not

Root Invocation · [Wy] Motion + [Eith] Flux · Invocation and above

WellspringCataclysm (Wild)
Second WellspringTerranova
OperationCalcination
TemperanceInvocation and above

Trigger. Must be cast directly against an identifiably stagnant current — a dead Wellspring flow, a stalled front, a frozen conflict; it has nothing to act on otherwise.

Effect. Shatters a stagnation ward or dead current wholesale, setting flow, motion or growth going again on a civilizational scale.

How it works.

  • The idea. Upheaval. The moment a fire stops being a fire and becomes a blast, a change in kind as well as size, and the soul that has learned to call that moment. Terranova’s idea rides with it (the living earth, mending by growing, every wound answered with new ground).
  • The operation. Calcination. The burning is driven past itself until it goes over all at once into something else in kind.
  • In the worker. Held to Invocation or past, the idea is set as law by [Wy] Motion + [Eith] Flux, and the world gives way that far: shatters a stagnation ward or dead current wholesale, setting flow, motion or growth going again on a civilizational scale.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus the first push comes from the caster’s own reserve, a stagnant current made to remember it can run; casters report acute exhaustion equivalent to running a literal marathon.
  • Flaw. Upheaval needs something to hold it in before it can go over, and once it goes it does not know which side of the wall it was called from. In confinement the blast comes back along its own path, and the practitioner standing where it began takes the blast and its return together.
  • Counter. [Au] Death refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Solfatara or Lethegraal.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Deflagration-to-detonation transition.

In numbers. Restarted flow reaches roughly 60-80% of the current’s original pre-stagnation throughput within the first hour.

The target. Everything downstream of the restarted current feels the surge as sudden, disorienting abundance or force after prolonged absence.

Cost and backlash. The first push comes from the caster’s own reserve, a stagnant current made to remember it can run; casters report acute exhaustion equivalent to running a literal marathon.

Counterplay. [Au] Death lays stillness back on the current’s source, and if it lands before the flow has taken hold again the current settles back into stagnation.

The Unreachable Made Near

Root Invocation · [Zh] Space + [Oth] Path · Invocation and above

WellspringEclipseron (Spirit)
Second WellspringTransmutatio
OperationDistillation
TemperanceInvocation and above

Trigger. Both endpoints must be points the caster has physically stood at some point in their life.

Effect. Folds two points once separated by a cosmic gulf into true adjacency for the duration of the working.

How it works.

  • The idea. Reading what is missing. The shape of an absence taken as knowledge of the thing that cast it. This lens has the truest claim of the Sixty to reading what fails to be there. People call that darkness and are being romantic: it is a system described by its absences, which is a harder and less romantic thing than its practitioners generally admit. Transmutatio’s idea rides with it (change without loss, the same thing said another way, so that what looked like a failure turns out to be fuel in an inconvenient form).
  • The operation. Distillation. What was driven off is read from the dead head left behind in the retort, the absence drawn down into knowledge of the thing that cast it.
  • In the worker. Held to Invocation or past, the idea is set as law by [Zh] Space + [Oth] Path, and the world gives way that far: folds two points once separated by a cosmic gulf into true adjacency for the duration of the working.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus folding space this way strains the caster’s own spatial sense afterward — mild disorientation and misjudged distances for hours following.
  • Flaw. An absence is knowledge only if something cast it, and the idea cannot tell a gap left by a thing from a gap that was always empty. A practitioner committed to reading what is missing will eventually construct an object out of a gap that had nothing behind it.
  • Counter. [Ma] Recall refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Aurevane.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Occultation and transit photometry.

In numbers. Fold radius scales with caster Temperance; Stage IX manages roughly city-block distances, Stage XII+ manages cross-Realm distances.

The target. Anything crossing the fold experiences a seamless single step; anything watching from outside sees the two locations’ edges visibly overlap for the fold’s duration.

Cost and backlash. Folding space this way strains the caster’s own spatial sense afterward — mild disorientation and misjudged distances for hours following.

Counterplay. [Ma] Recall interrupts by reminding the fold which distances are ‘true,’ collapsing it back to the correct, unfolded separation — sometimes stranding anything mid-crossing.

The Wheel That Turns Again

Root Invocation · [El] Life + [Vael] Renewal · Invocation and above

WellspringVerdantia (Wild)
Second WellspringRebirthine
OperationFermentation
TemperanceInvocation and above

Trigger. Requires soil, tissue, or Essence-substrate that is dead but not erased — Uurgath’s true erasure leaves nothing for this glyph to act on.

Effect. Reconstitutes an entire dead or sterilized ecosystem from barren earth, turning what died there straight into fresh growth.

How it works.

  • The idea. Growth without end. A self with no final size, which feeds on simply being out in the world. It comes through accumulation rather than rupture, one good day on top of another. Rebirthine’s idea rides with it (the cycle, loss taken as the first step of the next building, what is broken down paying for what comes up).
  • The operation. Fermentation. The soul is fed a little at a time from the world it stands in, and grows on that feeding without end.
  • In the worker. Held to Invocation or past, the idea is set as law by [El] Life + [Vael] Renewal, and the world gives way that far: reconstitutes an entire dead or sterilized ecosystem from barren earth, turning what died there straight into fresh growth.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus sustained casting draws directly on the caster’s own vitality; overdrawn casters show symptoms identical to severe malnutrition afterward.
  • Flaw. A self with no final size cannot stop growing, even when the world has stopped feeding it. Shut away from the open air it grows on anyway, all length and no substance, until it cannot hold itself up.
  • Counter. [Uur] Disruption refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Monolithion or Mortalis.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Carbon fixation and indeterminate meristematic growth.

In numbers. Visible regrowth begins within minutes; full ecosystem reconstitution over the following season, per Elyndra’s own Verdant Exhalation precedent.

The target. Regrowth spreads outward from the cast point at a rate the caster can consciously throttle, at least initially.

Cost and backlash. Sustained casting draws directly on the caster’s own vitality; overdrawn casters show symptoms identical to severe malnutrition afterward.

Counterplay. [Uur] Disruption reintroduces the original sterilizing condition at the growth’s leading edge, and uncapped growth left unchecked can outrun the caster’s ability to throttle it at all.
