Titan-Keyed Arts

Titan-Keyed Arts

Nov 17, 202626 min read

The fourteen arts keyed to a Titan’s law, each the Titan’s ground worked at a size a soul can carry.

Part of The Spell Index. 14 entries. Each gives the working’s glyphs and least Temperance, its Wellspring and operation, what sets it off and what it does, how it works by its idea, and what it costs and what answers it.

The Titans themselves, and the fuller story of each art, are on The Fourteen Titans pages.

Flesh Remembers Stone

Titan-keyed art · [Th] Foundation + [Ma] Recall · Zenith and above

WellspringMateria Primordia (Wild)
OperationConjunction
TemperanceZenith and above

Trigger. Requires direct skin contact with the target tissue, bone, or soil.

Effect. Crystallizes living Essence into Soul-Crystal-grade matter on contact, letting the caster reforge flesh, bone, or soil directly into stable lattice, as Gaia’s own Wellspring does beneath the world’s crust.

How it works.

  • The idea. The first substance. One self and many forms, and a soul that finds it can become its own opposite and still be itself.
  • The operation. Conjunction. The one substance is wed to its own opposite and goes on being itself, the marriage the bench calls the first matter.
  • In the worker. Held to Zenith or past, the idea is set as law by [Th] Foundation + [Ma] Recall, and the world gives way that far: crystallizes living Essence into Soul-Crystal-grade matter on contact, letting the caster reforge flesh, bone, or soil directly into stable lattice, as Gaia’s own Wellspring does beneath the world’s crust.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus Caster’s hands take the heaviest exposure; repeated use produces genuine mineral deposits under the caster’s own skin over a career.
  • Flaw. One self can wear many forms, but only one at a time, and it has to remember the others to come back to them. Under enough pressure it forgets, and the practitioner keeps for good whichever form they were wearing when the pressure came.
  • Counter. [Uur] Disruption refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Oblivara or Basilithe.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Allotropy.

In numbers. Crystallization front advances roughly one centimeter per second along tissue, faster through soil and loose stone.

The target. Affected matter takes on visible mineral sheen and rigidity while retaining its original shape, per Gaia’s own manifestation.

Cost and backlash. Caster’s hands take the heaviest exposure; repeated use produces genuine mineral deposits under the caster’s own skin over a career.

Counterplay. [Uur] Disruption unmakes the stone before it has finished setting, and flesh forced back out of half-made stone is its own kind of trauma.

The Bent Knee’s Burden

Titan-keyed art · [Th] Foundation + [Len] Line · Zenith and above

WellspringPetralon (Wild)
OperationCoagulation
TemperanceZenith and above

Trigger. Cast only within a Domain whose weight and order are already visibly failing (a sagging structure, a buckling timeline).

Effect. Locks a Domain’s weight and causal sequence to Atla’zon’s own kneeling burden, granting immense structural and temporal stability at the cost of freezing all forward motion within the field.

How it works.

  • The idea. The foundation. To become what other things stand on: load taken, carried and sent down, at the price of slowly settling. Breaking, holding and rising are one law seen from three positions, and this lens treats a soul as a loaded structure, asking the only question a master mason ever asks: where the load goes, and what happens at the place it concentrates.
  • The operation. Coagulation. The soul is fixed as the foundation, set to bear and never to rise.
  • In the worker. Held to Zenith or past, the idea is set as law by [Th] Foundation + [Len] Line, and the world gives way that far: locks a Domain’s weight and causal sequence to Atla’zon’s own kneeling burden, granting immense structural and temporal stability at the cost of freezing all forward motion within the field.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus the caster’s own joints and breath take on Atla’zon’s characteristic strain — genuine physical burden, not metaphor, for hours after release.
  • Flaw. A foundation bears whatever presses down on it, and nothing ever asked it to hold on. Pull on it, the one load it does not answer, and it does not give gracefully. It parts.
  • Counter. [Wy] Motion refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Cymorath or Abython.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Compressive load path and creep.

In numbers. Field-wide time dilation caps at roughly 0.3x normal flow rate for the duration; structural load tolerance roughly doubles.

The target. Anything inside the field moves and ages measurably slower relative to the outside; falling objects behave as though gravity itself thickened.

Cost and backlash. The caster’s own joints and breath take on Atla’zon’s characteristic strain — genuine physical burden, not metaphor, for hours after release.

Counterplay. [Wy] Motion brought in at the field’s edge runs inward as a wave of motion given back, and the locked burden lets go all at once rather than gradually.

The Canyon’s Patience

Titan-keyed art · [Uur] Disruption + [Vorn] End · Zenith and above

WellspringFractura (Wild)
OperationSeparation
TemperanceZenith and above

Trigger. Target must already be structurally or morally compromised — the glyph guides an existing fault line, it does not create one.

Effect. Guides a controlled, purposeful collapse through a target structure or curse, ensuring the fall clears space rather than merely destroying, as Valmir intends.

How it works.

  • The idea. The breaking point. Everything has a place where it will go, and this lens shows it to you, in bridges, in friends and in your own left wrist.
  • The operation. Separation. A thing is parted along the one place where it was always going to part.
  • In the worker. Held to Zenith or past, the idea is set as law by [Uur] Disruption + [Vorn] End, and the world gives way that far: guides a controlled, purposeful collapse through a target structure or curse, ensuring the fall clears space rather than merely destroying, as Valmir intends.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus if the caster’s own resentments toward the target are unresolved, the collapse tends to ‘choose’ a more destructive trajectory than intended.
  • Flaw. The lens shows where everything will go, and it cannot leave its bearer out of everything. Under strain a Fractura practitioner reads their own breaking points with perfect clarity, at the worst possible moment.
  • Counter. [Th] Foundation refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Orrenthal, Coagula or Redemption.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Linear elastic fracture mechanics.

In numbers. Collapse trajectory can be bent up to roughly 30 degrees from the structure’s natural failure line before it resists further steering.

The target. The structure falls along the chosen path, sparing anything outside the redirected collapse zone.

Cost and backlash. If the caster’s own resentments toward the target are unresolved, the collapse tends to ‘choose’ a more destructive trajectory than intended.

Counterplay. [Th] Foundation reinforces the natural fault line before the redirect takes hold, refusing the bend and forcing the original, unsteered collapse instead.

WellspringNihiloth (Wild)
OperationDissolution
TemperanceRevelation and above

Trigger. Cast only on a region already showing measurable Essence overcharge (visible saturation, unstable ambient resonance).

Effect. Cleanses a region of overaccumulated Essence by absorbing it into inverted null-space, the way Zar’thuun’s own Wellspring holds absence in shape.

How it works.

  • The idea. The hollow law. Power by negation: the rule itself unmade, and what stands in the gap.
  • The operation. Dissolution. The rule itself is loosed until nothing of it is left, and the gap is left standing where it was.
  • In the worker. Held to Revelation or past, the idea is set as law by [Thru] Hollow + [Au] Death, and the world gives way that far: cleanses a region of overaccumulated Essence by absorbing it into inverted null-space, the way Zar’thuun’s own Wellspring holds absence in shape.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus prolonged proximity erases small, low-significance memories from everyone nearby along with the excess Essence — the mercy and the erasure are mechanically the same process.
  • Flaw. A rule unmade is unmade for everyone, and the idea has no way to spare its maker. Everything the practitioner brought inside is subject to the same gap, including the parts of themselves they were relying on.
  • Counter. [El] Life refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Eidolyn, Oblivara or Monolithion.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Destructive interference and the null field.

In numbers. Absorption capacity scales with the null-field’s radius, typically clearing a 10-15 meter overcharge zone per casting.

The target. The overcharged region returns to stable baseline Essence density almost immediately.

Cost and backlash. Prolonged proximity erases small, low-significance memories from everyone nearby along with the excess Essence — the mercy and the erasure are mechanically the same process.

Counterplay. [El] Life reintroduces active Essence generation into the field faster than the sink can absorb it, overwhelming the null-field’s capacity.

The Era-Forge’s Toll

Titan-keyed art · [Cro] Cycle + [Len] Line · Zenith and above

WellspringLethegraal (Spirit)
OperationDissolution
TemperanceZenith and above

Trigger. Requires a conflict or era that has been genuinely stalled — not merely slow, but structurally unable to resolve on its own.

Effect. Compacts unresolved tension in a Domain into forced historical resolution, ending a stalled conflict or stagnant era by grinding it forward through sheer accumulated consequence, per Chronaeus’s law.

How it works.

  • The idea. The cup. Time’s mercy hurried along: old pain settles on its own schedule, and no hand chooses what falls.
  • The operation. Dissolution. Old pain is loosened into the soul on its own schedule until nothing of it is left but plain structure that holds weight.
  • In the worker. Held to Zenith or past, the idea is set as law by [Cro] Cycle + [Len] Line, and the world gives way that far: compacts unresolved tension in a Domain into forced historical resolution, ending a stalled conflict or stagnant era by grinding it forward through sheer accumulated consequence, per Chronaeus’s law.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus the acceleration doesn’t discriminate meaning from suffering; innocents living through the compressed era age and suffer at the same forced rate as the resolution itself.
  • Flaw. The cup can hurry time’s mercy and cannot choose where the settling stops, because no hand chooses what falls. Hurry it too far and the old pain does not come to rest but swings past it on every cycle, and the practitioner rocks back and forth rather than settling.
  • Counter. [Ser] Renewal refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Cataclysm, Penance or Contrition.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Relaxation time.

In numbers. Compression ratio is typically 10:1 to 50:1 — a decade of stalled tension can resolve in roughly a season.

The target. The stalled conflict or era reaches a genuine resolution point, for good or ill, rather than continuing indefinitely.

Cost and backlash. The acceleration doesn’t discriminate meaning from suffering; innocents living through the compressed era age and suffer at the same forced rate as the resolution itself.

Counterplay. [Ser] Renewal introduces a genuinely new growth-vector into the compressed timeline, diffusing the stored pressure into gradual change instead of a single forced toll.

  • The Measurewrights’ line. Ensemble forecasting.

OperationSeparation

Trigger. Requires total ambient silence already present or actively enforced before the glyph can take hold.

Cost and backlash. The caster is not exempted by the silence they enforced; their own fear surfaces in the same beat as everyone else’s.

Counterplay. [Se] Song reintroduces harmonic noise into the silence, and the beam cannot form without a genuinely quiet field to strobe through.

The Mirrored Scale

Titan-keyed art · [Ur] Balance + [Sel] Measure · Zenith and above

WellspringJudicium (Wild)
OperationSeparation
TemperanceZenith and above

Trigger. Requires an actual signed, sworn, or spoken commitment already in effect — the glyph mirrors real reciprocity, it cannot invent an obligation.

Effect. Mirrors all incoming Essence back upon its source in exact kind — the metaphysical basis of karmic reciprocity, made instantaneous through Dazkar’s own Wellspring.

How it works.

  • The idea. Truth told apart. Discernment that knows what a thing is made of, and whether it is lying, from the mark it cannot help leaving.
  • The operation. Separation. A thing is parted from what it claims to be, the true told apart from the false by the mark it cannot help leaving.
  • In the worker. Held to Zenith or past, the idea is set as law by [Ur] Balance + [Sel] Measure, and the world gives way that far: mirrors all incoming Essence back upon its source in exact kind — the metaphysical basis of karmic reciprocity, made instantaneous through Dazkar’s own Wellspring.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus the caster is bound by the same mirror for the working’s duration — any cruelty in how they cast it returns to them first, before it ever reaches the target.
  • Flaw. Discernment tells everything apart and cannot choose to leave anything untold. Off guard, every object in view reports what it is made of at once, and the reader loses the power to put any of it first. Pushed past the point where a reading only reads, telling a thing apart becomes taking it apart: the read starts to pass judgment, which can turn away force or at the very top end a life, and since it has no way to look at one thing only, it takes the reader’s own memory in the same stroke.
  • Counter. [Ve] Desire refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Tenebra or Eidolyn.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Emission and absorption spectroscopy.

In numbers. Reflection is exact to within the same Stage-band the caster operates in; no amplification or attenuation.

The target. Whoever breaks the commitment receives back precisely the Essence-cost of the harm they caused, undiminished.

Cost and backlash. The caster is bound by the same mirror for the working’s duration — any cruelty in how they cast it returns to them first, before it ever reaches the target.

Counterplay. [Ve] Desire introduces a new, unmirrored want into the field, breaking the closed reflective loop the working depends on.

WellspringExuroth (Wild)
OperationCalcination
TemperanceZenith and above

Trigger. Requires the caster to name, honestly, one thing about the target fragment/artifact that is false or unworthy.

Effect. Burns away everything false or unworthy in a soul fragment or artifact, refining what remains into pure, usable Essence vapor, by Prometheus’s law that what is false must burn so that what is true can be refined out of it.

How it works.

  • The idea. The trial by fire. Power comes only through ordeal, plunged and reheated and plunged again, until the soul holds a hardness no gentle road could have given it.
  • The operation. Calcination. The soul is burned, plunged and burned again, until only the hardness that lives through the fire is left.
  • In the worker. Held to Zenith or past, the idea is set as law by [Ra] Flame + [Asa] Creation, and the world gives way that far: burns away everything false or unworthy in a soul fragment or artifact, refining what remains into pure, usable Essence vapor, by Prometheus’s law that what is false must burn so that what is true can be refined out of it.

  • Flaw. The trial gives hardness only to a soul that lives through it, and the idea cannot tell an ordeal from a ruin. Plunge again too soon, without the rest between, and the soul comes out hard, brittle and split along the lines of its own trials.
  • Counter. [Vael] Renewal refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Absolution or Ascensio.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Quench and temper.

In numbers. Refinement removes roughly the bottom 20-40% of a fragment’s Essence-purity, proportional to how much falsity the caster correctly identified.

The target. What remains afterward is measurably more stable and ‘honest’ by every Harmonics Fidelity metric.

Cost and backlash. The process cannot distinguish sentiment from falsehood with full precision — genuinely valued memories attached to the false part burn away too.

Counterplay. [Vael] Renewal reintroduces new growth into the fragment mid-burn, diluting the purity threshold and letting false material survive alongside true.

Counterplay. [Ra] Flame brings want and heat back into the stillness, and once the stillness is broken the target falls back into normal (and often catastrophic) time.
