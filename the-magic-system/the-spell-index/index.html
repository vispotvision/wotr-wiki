The Spell Index

The Spell Index

Nov 17, 20266 min read

Every attested working in the Master Codex, 109 of them, filed by kind

A working is a Wellspring’s idea, set as law by its glyphs and held by a soul tempered far enough to hold it. This index files every attested one the same way: the glyphs it is written in, the least Temperance that can hold it, the Wellspring whose idea it runs on and the operation that idea performs, what sets it off, what it does, how it works, what it costs the worker and what answers it.

The idea and its operation come from the Sixty; the glyphs from the Master Glyph Index. The Measurewrights’ line at the foot of each account is the guild’s own reckoning, often right about how much and never about why.

How to read an entry

  • The line under the name gives the shelf, the glyphs the working is written in and the least Temperance that can hold it.
  • Wellspring and operation say which idea the working runs on and what that idea does at the bench.
  • Trigger is what has to be true before the working will take. Effect is what it does.
  • How it works is the working by its idea: the idea, the operation, what happens in the worker, the price in the self, the flaw, the counter, and last the Measurewrights’ line.
  • In numbers, the target, cost and backlash and counterplay follow.
