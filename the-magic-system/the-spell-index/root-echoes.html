Root Echoes

Root Echoes

Nov 28, 202623 min read

Applied workings that echo a root invocation, the root’s shape carried down into common use.

Part of The Spell Index. 18 entries. Each gives the working’s glyphs and least Temperance, its Wellspring and operation, what sets it off and what it does, how it works by its idea, and what it costs and what answers it.

Axis Lock

Root echo · [Ur] Balance · Refraction and above

WellspringFixatio (Medium)
Second WellspringJudicium
OperationCoagulation
TemperanceRefraction and above

Effect. Freezes one outcome branch while verdict is cast.

How it works.

  • The idea. Commitment. What is bound here stays bound, because the way back has stopped existing. Judicium’s idea rides with it (truth told apart, discernment that knows what a thing is made of, and whether it is lying, from the mark it cannot help leaving).
  • The operation. Coagulation. What is bound is set past melting, and the way back is gone.
  • In the worker. Held to Refraction or past, the idea is set as law by [Ur] Balance, and the world gives way that far: freezes one outcome branch while verdict is cast.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus backlash if verdict delayed.
  • Flaw. Commitment cannot tell a true binding from a mistaken one, because the way back is closed for both alike. Wanting it undone does not undo it, and a Fixatio binding placed in error is a permanent feature of whatever it was placed on.
  • Counter. [Wy] Motion refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Manganthra, Somnalis or Dissolution.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Covalent cross-linking.

Cost and backlash. Backlash if verdict delayed

Counterplay. [Wy] Motion

Effect. Explosive burst of speed inside a slowed-time bubble for surgical strikes.

Lethegraal’s idea rides with it (the cup, time’s mercy hurried along: old pain settles on its own schedule, and no hand chooses what falls).

  • The Measurewrights’ line. Deflagration-to-detonation transition.

Cost and backlash. Temporal lag; rebound fatigue

  • The Measurewrights’ line. Destructive interference and the null field.

Cost and backlash. Backlash emptiness; despair

Counterplay. [Vael] Renewal

Judged Path

Root echo · [Ur] Balance · Flourishing and above

WellspringJudicium (Wild)
Second WellspringThaloré
OperationSeparation
TemperanceFlourishing and above

Effect. Creates a route that equalizes threat on both sides, averting ambush bias.

How it works.

  • The idea. Truth told apart. Discernment that knows what a thing is made of, and whether it is lying, from the mark it cannot help leaving. Thaloré’s idea rides with it (depth of feeling, emotion given its true weight in the soul, so that a grief can press on every soul in a whole room. A soul under this lens takes the shape of what holds it, and is likelier than most to fall into Synergia (Crystals briefly sharing one working) and Echo Bonds).
  • The operation. Separation. A thing is parted from what it claims to be, the true told apart from the false by the mark it cannot help leaving.
  • In the worker. Held to Flourishing or past, the idea is set as law by [Ur] Balance, and the world gives way that far: creates a route that equalizes threat on both sides, averting ambush bias.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus Rigid routing under chaos.
  • Flaw. Discernment tells everything apart and cannot choose to leave anything untold. Off guard, every object in view reports what it is made of at once, and the reader loses the power to put any of it first. Pushed past the point where a reading only reads, telling a thing apart becomes taking it apart: the read starts to pass judgment, which can turn away force or at the very top end a life, and since it has no way to look at one thing only, it takes the reader’s own memory in the same stroke.
  • Counter. [Rk] Rupture refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Tenebra or Eidolyn.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. Emission and absorption spectroscopy.

Cost and backlash. Rigid routing under chaos

Counterplay. [Rk] Rupture

Lightward Path

Root echo · [Lum] Light + [Oth] Path · Welling and above

WellspringLuminalis (Medium)
Second WellspringEclipseron
OperationSeparation
TemperanceWelling and above

Effect. Lights a safe route through darkness or illusion, visible only to the caster and those they choose to share it with.

How it works.

  • The idea. The hidden light. Seeing what hides and hiding what shows, one act run in two directions. Eclipseron’s idea rides with it (reading what is missing, the shape of an absence taken as knowledge of the thing that cast it. This lens has the truest claim of the Sixty to reading what fails to be there. People call that darkness and are being romantic: it is a system described by its absences, which is a harder and less romantic thing than its practitioners generally admit).
  • The operation. Separation. What is seen is parted from what is there, so the hidden can be drawn into view and the shown put out of it.
  • In the worker. Held to Welling or past, the idea is set as law by [Lum] Light + [Oth] Path, and the world gives way that far: lights a safe route through darkness or illusion, visible only to the caster and those they choose to share it with.
  • Price in the self. Reserve, plus the light attracts anything that hunts by sight as readily as it guides.
  • Flaw. Seeing and hiding are one act run two ways, and a soul that keeps both running at once begins to run two ways itself. Its own output splits along the seam between what it shows and what it hides, the two halves going out at different paces, and complex workings arrive out of step with themselves.
  • Counter. [Vl] Veil refuses its claim before it settles; the contrary idea is Penanceflare or Mirithane.
  • The Measurewrights’ line. The non-visible spectrum and polarization.

Cost and backlash. The light attracts anything that hunts by sight as readily as it guides.

Counterplay. [Vl] Veil
